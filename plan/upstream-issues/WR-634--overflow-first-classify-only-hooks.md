#### WR-634. Blends of several edges that overflow across cliffs are built together, with no way to order them
- **Severity:** Missing feature (wrong result for its callers)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet executive)
- **Location:** the fork's `source/SMLib/inc/SmFilletExecutive.h` (`SetOverflowFirst`, `SetClassifyOnly`, `GetOverflowEdges`), `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletExecutive.cpp` (`SmFilletExecutive::CreateFilletBrep`, the rail classification of the cliff overflow) and `source/SMLib/inc/SmFilletGeom.h` (`SM_FILERR_OVERFLOW_FIRST`), tangency-labs/smlib@241f7ba (branch `replay/1.0.1`).
- **What happens:** rounds of several edges where some overflow their faces across cliffs (WR-631) are blended together. R21 on a 20 cube's edge with R5 on the corner's other two edges fails in the fork before 241f7ba (1001, "Corner Geometry Computation Failure" at the convex N x N corner): the R21 takes the whole of both faces the R5 edges lie on, and the corner of three rounds has no geometry. The executive had no way to tell a caller which blends overflow so that it could build those first.
- **Trigger:** `[21, 5, 5]` on a 20 cube's three edges at a corner, the R21 on one of them.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#960). 241f7ba adds executive hooks that stop `CreateFilletBrep` right after the rails are classified, building nothing, with `SM_FILERR_OVERFLOW_FIRST` and the edges whose blends overflow: `SetClassifyOnly` always, `SetOverflowFirst` when blends of several edges overflow and none of the cliff overflow's refusals applies. The ordering itself (classify each edge alone, blend those that overflow on their own first, then what is left of the others) is in the fork's C API, outside SMLib, and is not ported.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run of the fork was made; not reachable in the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`), which has no cliff overflow.
- **Rust port:** `FilletSession::set_overflow_first`, `FilletSession::set_classify_only` and `FilletSession::overflow_edges` (`blend/fillet_executive/session/overflow_first.rs`), the stop in `FilletExecutive::refuse_blends_larger_than_faces` (`blend/fillet_executive/p3.rs`), `FilletErrorType::OverflowFirst` (11; the fork's code is one past its `SM_FILERR_CORNER_UNSUPPORTED`). No `UpstreamFixes` field: both settings are per session and off by default, so a session that does not set them is unchanged; they act only with `fillet_refuse_rails_leaving_faces` and `fillet_cliff_overflow`, where the port runs the fork's rail classification.

  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr634_overflow_first.rs`; with the ordering emulated by the hooks, `[21, 5, 5]` gives the R21's alone, 6107.529369332 against the closed form 6107.529369550 (the fork: 6107.529369565), the R5 edges gone with their faces.
