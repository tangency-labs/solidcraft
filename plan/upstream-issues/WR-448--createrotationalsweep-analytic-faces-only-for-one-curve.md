#### WR-448. `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateRotationalSweep` makes analytic faces only for a one-curve profile
- **Severity:** Wrong result (correct shape, non-analytic faces: later operations lose the analytic paths).
- **Area:** Surfaces and volumes
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp`, `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateRotationalSweep`: the whole turn (the active `USE_NEW_ANALYTICS` branch) calls `sm_CreateAnalyticRotationalSweep` only for a profile of one curve and gives every other curve an `SmSurfOfRevolution`; a partial turn sweeps with `SmRotationalSweepGeometry`. `source/SMLib/src/SmRotationalSweepGeometry.cpp`, `EdgeSweepHigher`: the B-spline surface of `sm_CreateRotSrf`, then `SmBSplineSurface::CopyAndAddAnalytics`, whose `SmCylinder` and `SmTorus` recognition is commented out and whose `SmCone` and `SmSphere` copies are parameterised differently from the B-spline the face's trims are made on.
- **What happens:** a revolved profile of more than one curve has no cylinder, cone, sphere or torus faces: a drilled hole with its drill point, a dome on its disc and a torus from two half circles get surfaces of revolution (whole turn) or B-spline surfaces (partial turn).
- **Trigger:** `SmApiCreateRotationalSweep` of a drilled hole's profile (a line parallel to the axis, then one from its end to a point on the axis), 360 or 90 degrees. Also every partial `CreateCone`, `CreateSphere` and `CreateTorus`, which sweep their profiles. tangency-labs/solidcore#545 (SolidCraft).
- **How found:** issue #545.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run here; the port shows it (whole turn: `SurfOfRevolution` faces for the drilled hole).
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib 0b143f7 (branch `port-revolve-analytic`) sweeps each profile curve to its analytic surface, bounded to the curve and the turn. That commit is not public; the port's fix follows the issue's description.
- **Rust port:** `sweep::analytic_revolution` builds the cylinder (line parallel to the axis), cone (another line not perpendicular to it), sphere (an arc centred on the axis) or torus (an arc off it), the curve in one half-plane through the axis, laid out as `sm_CreateRotSrf`'s B-spline (u the turn, v the curve from its start) and only used when its corners, start tangent and sense of turn agree with that layout. With the opt-in `Kernel::fixes.revolve_analytic_profile_faces` (off by default: upstream's surfaces, as `c9c5979`), `create_rotational_sweep` uses it before the `SurfOfRevolution` for each curve and `create_partial_rotational_sweep` sweeps with `RotationalSweepGeometry::set_analytic_revolutions`, under which `edge_sweep_higher` uses it instead of `CopyAndAddAnalytics`, with the same loop. Every difftest runs with the fix off and so keeps comparing upstream's surfaces. The analytic surface's natural boundary must match the B-spline's (same degenerate sides, lengths), so a surface whose iso circles differ keeps the B-spline (before #899, a full minor circle not starting at tube angle 0, whose iso circle collapsed); tube ranges stay inside [-360, 360]. See `plan/findings/sweep.rotational_sweep_geometry--analytic-faces-for-every-profile-curve-545.md`.
