Updates: WR-663

#### WR-663 (update, 2026-10-31). The remaining SmCurve and SmBSplineCurve sites

The rest of tangency-labs/smlib 8311f8e (`SmCurve.cpp`) and 8843967 (`SmBSplineCurve.cpp`) is ported under the same opt-in field, `Kernel::fixes.query_statuses_pass_on` (default `false`, upstream's behaviour). With the flag off every site keeps its own ignore policy. The `SmSurface.cpp` sites (21dcf69) are a separate change.

- **Passed on (`SER`):**
  - `curve/curve/p1.rs`: `sm_RefineCCSolutions` (the local intersections and both `Inversion`s; an `Inversion` of a value outside the solution interval is `SM_ERR`), the degenerate `ApproximateCurve`'s `EditParameterization`, `ClassifyIntersectingRangeSolution`, `SmCPGlobalSolver::LocalSolve` (continuity, end tangents, angles) and `GlobalPointSolve`'s `SolveIt`.
  - `curve/curve/p2.rs`: `CreateBySplitCurve`'s trims, `SurfaceMaxDistanceBetween`'s samples and `sm_FlushCrv`'s arc `EditParameterization`.
  - `curve/curve/p3.rs`: `ComputeCrvsNormal` (its evaluations, and a zero normal becomes the status instead of `(0, 0, 1)`), `FindParameterAtArcLength` (knots, intervals, span lengths), `LocalPointSolve`'s found point, the `SmCCTangentIntENFO::StaticEvaluate` switch of the curve/curve evaluators and the projected `LocalCurveSolve`'s check evaluation.
  - `curve/curve/p4.rs`: `ApproximatePolarBox` and the property evaluator's unitizations.
  - `curve/curve/p5.rs`: `Length`'s end points, `LocalPropertyAnalysis` (found points, angle), the normal evaluator's surface derivatives and unitizations.
  - `curve/curve/p6.rs`: `CalculateBoundingBox` (samples, sub-interval, box, basis), `CurveMaxDistanceBetween`'s knots, `DeformToEndPointTargets`' drops and `ConvertTo2D`. A target that does not drop onto the surface is `SM_ERR`, and the UV curve is converted before it is replaced, so a failure leaves it unchanged.
  - `curve/curve/p7.rs`: `SmCCIGlobalSolver::LocalSolve` (box projections, the intersection test box, span end points, continuities, end-point drops) and `GlobalPropertyAnalysis` (angle, evaluations, frame).
  - `curve/curve/p8.rs`: `GlobalCurveIntersect` (refinement, interval trims, the combine and cull evaluations, range classification). Upstream's consistency check of consecutive point solutions only warns, so the port leaves it out. With the flag its evaluations are made and a failure is returned, as in the fork.
  - `curve/curve/p9.rs`: `SmCCGlobalSolver::LocalSolve` (end points, end solves, cylinder guesses, `LocalCurveSolve`), `GlobalCurveSolve` (boxes, both `SolveIt`s), the tight boxes' evaluations and `IsOnPlane` (frame, box).
  - `nurbs/b_spline_curve/p2.rs`, `p3.rs`: `CreateAnalyticCurve`'s `InsertOneKnot`, `CreateSimpleOffset`'s frame and `SubdivideAtDiscontinuity`'s trim. `nurbs/b_spline_surface_n_lib/p2.rs` repeats `SmCurve::ApproximateCurve` over a tangent field, so its degenerate `EditParameterization` and `sm_FlushCrv` follow too.
- **Answers from what was computed:** `IsArc`, `IsLine`, `IsLinear`, `IsPlanar`, `IsPeriodic`, `IsOnPositiveSideOfPlanes` and the seam test of curve/curve solutions are `FALSE`. `IsDomainBoundary` is `SM_SP_NEITHER`. `HasPlaneCrossings` and the property solver's branch culling are `TRUE`. `FixChainCurvesList` links nothing to a curve that cannot be evaluated. It ends a chain at a curve that cannot be reversed and leaves that curve in the list. `CrvsCleanup` keeps a curve it cannot evaluate. `CrvsFixGaps` neither closes nor counts a gap it cannot measure or set. `RemoveDuplicateCurves` does not call such a curve a duplicate. `SmBSplineCurve::IsDegenerate` is `FALSE` without a NURB.
- **No change needed:** `GetEnds` already gives the undefined point for an end that cannot be evaluated. `sm_UnitizeTangent`, `smgu_PointProjectToPlane` and `SmPolarBox::AddVector3d` always succeed. In the port, these sites already return the status (Rust `?`): `MakeUnitOrthoVectors` in the boxes, `SetCanonical` of the projected curves, `Intersect` of the solver intervals, the knots in `SurfaceMaxDistanceBetween` and `sm_FindTestParams`, `CCWAngleBetween` in `SmCurvePropertyGS::FindBestSubdivisionNode` and `LocalSolve`, and the B-spline calls that need the NURB. The fork's `(void)` sites stay dropped: `GlobalPropertyAnalysis`'s `EvaluateGeometric` and comb unitizations, and `GlobalCurveIntersect`'s span `Intersect`.
- **Not ported (no port of the function):** `RemoveCoincidentSections`, `ApproximateConstrainedCurve`, `ConvertIvlFromSTEPToNURBS`, `ConvertIvlFromNURBSToSTEP`, `AreCoPlanar`, `AreOnPlane`, `OutputGraphics`, the `Draw*` functions, `WriteToFile`, `ReadFromFile` and their array forms, `Dump`. Also unported: `SmBSplineCurve`'s `HasNullSegments`, `GetNullSegments`, `HasSameParameterization`, `CreateSectionCurve`, `SnapInsideInterval`, `Simplify`, `GetGrevilleAbscissae`, `InsertKnots`, and the analytic-line branch of `JoinWith`.
- **Left out:** the `SmBSplineCurve` methods whose port takes no `Kernel`, so the flag cannot be read there: `CalculateBoundingBox`, `IsLine` (`GetControlPointsPointer`; the port already fails there), `AssertNoPeriodicJump`, `GetGrevilleAbscissa`, `CreateByJoining`, `CreateConicSegment`, `CreateMirrorCurve`, `EditEndPoint`, `MatchWeights`, `ReverseParameterization`, `EvaluateCurvature` and `FairBlendCurveDerivatives`. Their dropped statuses either cannot fail after the calls before them succeeded (the NURB exists, the basis is orthonormal) or already fail in the port. `SmBSplineCurve::AssertValid`'s reworked tests 5/6 and 11-14 and `HasInternalPole` (its knots exist once the NURB does) are also left out. The base `IsLine` on a B-spline (`is_line_base`) is left out because its evaluation cannot fail once `GetEnds` found the NURB.
- **Confirmed:** by injected-failure tests (a B-spline without its NURB, or a Hermite curve, which cannot be reversed), each run with the flag on and off. The pinned C++ drops these statuses, so with the flag off the port matches it.
