#### WR-343. `SmSurfOfRevolution::CreateOffsetSurface` misses the axis crossing on the far side of the frame origin
- **Severity:** Wrong result (the offset surface reaches past the axis; a shell leaves a lamina circle round the pole and fails with `SM_ERR`)
- **Area:** Surface (offset)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmSurfOfRevolution.cpp`, `SmSurfOfRevolution::CreateOffsetSurface`, "Trim the offset curve with the axis of rev": the axis segment is `CreateLineSegment(origin, origin + dHeight * zAxis)`; on one crossing it keeps "the largest portion" (`dParam < sDomain.GetMid()`), and the endpoint test `sDomain.GetMin() != dParam || sDomain.GetMax() != dParam` is always true.
- **What happens:** the segment runs from the frame's origin along +z only, so an offset generator that crosses the axis on the origin's other side is not cut: it is revolved past the axis (a negative radius). Where it is cut, the longer part is kept, which need not be the part on the generatrix's side.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft rig shell/4567, a vase revolved about y from a fit-point spline (frame origin at y = 0.3686, z axis (0, -1, 0)), shelled 2 mm inward: the offset generator crosses the axis at y = 2.003, outside the segment; left untrimmed it was revolved 0.1156 past the axis, leaving a lamina circle round the pole; the offset is not a closed solid and `ShellBrep` returns 1001.
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig on the SMLib fork (tangency-labs/solidcore#973); gdb on the fork's C++ build.
- **Confirmed:** on the fork's C++ build: the vase rebuilt from its six cubic spans, revolved about ±y; the bottom's inward offset ends on the axis (1e-15 from it) at y = 2.003349024 against the exact 2.003349018. Before the fix, about -y, it stopped 0.116 from the axis.
- **Fix (fork commit "SMLib: a revolved face's offset is trimmed at the axis on either side of the frame's origin"):** the segment spans the offset curve's whole extent along the axis (its box corners' axial range, widened by the old height on both sides); the endpoint test is `&&`; the part kept is the one whose mid point has the larger radius along the frame's x axis.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.revolution_offset_axis_both_sides` (default `false`, upstream behaviour), read in `SurfOfRevolution::create_offset_surface` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/surf_of_revolution/p2.rs`). Test `issue_973_offset_cut_at_axis_below_origin`. No difftest case compares the opt-in path (the runners use the default kernel). Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#973.
