#### WR-671. The offset's edges and vertices drop the original's own tolerances, so its gaps fail the check
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Offset
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp`, `SmOffsetExecutive::BuildOffsetOfFaceuse` (the offset face's edges and vertices are made from the face's UV curves at the offset B-rep's zone tolerance) and `SmOffsetExecutive::MapEdgeuses` (relates each offset edge use to its original, nothing more).
- **What happens:** each offset face is built from copies of the original face's UV trim curves, so its edges and vertices are new entities at the B-rep's zone tolerance. An original edge or vertex whose own tolerance is larger than the B-rep's loses it: only where `SmEdgeuse::SetUVTrimCurve` later passes a larger value does an edge grow (in the case below the two arc edges reach twice the B-rep's, the two line edges stay at it, and every vertex takes its arc edge's). `SmBrep::ValidateAndUpdateTolerances` then holds each offset vertex's edge gap to half that vertex's tolerance, so gaps that are within the original's own zone are reported as Bad Gaps.
- **Trigger:** `offset_brep_simple` (extended, merged) of the trimmed saddle B-spline sheet `fixtures/topology.assert_valid/data/issue2111_patch_sheet.smb` (B-rep tolerance 2.0767e-5, edges 4.1533e-5, vertices 8.3066e-5). At d = 0.75 the offset's vertices are at 4.1533e-5 and `check_brep(Level2)` reports `SmBrep::Test #0/#2 Bad Gap: Edge/Vertex gap across Edgeuse ...` of 3.71e-5, 2.39e-5 and 3.13e-5 against 2.0767e-5; at -0.75 four (2.47e-5 to 3.93e-5), at 1.0 one (3.11e-5 against 2.96e-5). Every gap is under half the original vertices' tolerance (4.15e-5).
- **How found:** SolidCraft's thicken of a filled B-spline patch (tangency-labs/solidcore#2111), reduced to the fixture above; traced with the tolerance writes logged.
- **Confirmed:** yes, the pinned C++ gives the same lines (difftest `topology.assert_valid`, `copy_offset_patch_sheet.json`, byte-identical; `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2111_offset_patch_sheet_gaps.rs`).
- **Fix (opt-in):** `Kernel::fixes.offset_edges_keep_source_tolerance`. When `MapEdgeuses` matches an offset edge use to an original one, the offset edge is raised to the original edge's tolerance and its vertices to the larger of the original edge's vertices' tolerances (`SetTolerance(.., bUpdateOnlyIfLarger = TRUE)`, so nothing shrinks). The later steps that compare against the edge's tolerance (`ReplaceEdgeCurves`) then use the original's zone, and the merge carries the tolerances into the result. The offsets by +-0.75, 1.0 and +-1.5 then check clean, with the default's area to 1e-6. Not fixed by it: at d = 0.5 the offset's own vertex/edge gaps are 6.0e-5 to 6.8e-5, beyond even the original's tolerances; the default fails `ValidateGeometry` on two edges there, and with the flag `AssertValid` runs and reports those gaps.
