#### WR-471. A revolved arc whose centre is within tolerance of the axis makes a sphere (or torus) with no pole
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Sweeps
- **Location:** the SolidCraft fork's analytic faces of a rotational sweep (tangency-labs/smlib 0b143f7, `SmRotationalSweepGeometry::AnalyticSurface`, sphere branch `dMajor <= dEps`); Rust `candidate` in `crates/solidcore/src/sweep/analytic_revolution.rs`. Not in pinned `c9c5979`, which makes analytic faces only for a one-curve profile.
- **What happens:** the arc's centre is off the axis by less than the B-rep tolerance but more than the capped fit tolerance. The fork's C++ builds the sphere from the arc's own radius moved onto the axis and takes the latitudes about the off-axis centre: the sphere misses both ends of the arc by about the offset, and an end on the axis lands short of the pole by a parallel of radius about sqrt(2 dEps R), while the sweep's topology (`sm_CheckEdgeVerticesOnAxis`) treats that end as on the axis. The face's loop runs up the seam to a pole the surface does not have, and tessellation refuses it ("boundary winds around the surface"). The Rust port does not take the sphere branch at all: it builds a spindle torus of major radius c on the arc's own circle, 5e-7 off the sphere of the profile.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft rig shell/4847, step 6: a quarter disc, (0,0,0)-(20,0,0), an arc, (0,0,20)-(0,0,0), turned a whole turn about Z. The arc comes from a sketch through (20,0), a rounded (14.142136, 14.142136) and (0,20): centre (c, 0, c), c = 1.2847e-6, radius 19.99999871533725.
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig.
- **Confirmed:** yes on the fork's C++ build (issue #1123: pole vertex (1.2644e-6, 0, 20) before; (1.2e-15, 0, 20), rim at r = 20, volume 16755.160821 against 16755.160819 after). In Rust, the dome of the same profile strays 5.3e-7 from the radius-20 sphere before, 3.6e-15 after.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.revolve_arc_centre_near_axis_is_sphere` (`UpstreamFixes`, default `false`; needs `revolve_analytic_profile_faces`). An arc centred within the caller's tolerance of the axis is the sphere centred on the axis through the arc's two ends (the fork's fix: height from equal distance to both ends, latitudes `atan2(height, distance from the axis)`). Test `issue_1123_arc_centred_within_tolerance_of_the_axis_is_a_sphere_with_its_pole_on_it` (`crates/solidcore/src/sweep/api_brep.rs`) runs the profile above with the field off (torus, as before) and on (sphere through the corners, pole on the axis, closed solid, volume within 1e-6 of 2/3 pi 20^3).
