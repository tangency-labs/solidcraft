#### WR-609. A fillet's trace runs over base domains extended by half their size, so a blend wider than that is clamped at the extended border
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blend (fillet solver)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp`, `SmFilletSolver::SetupOffsetExtension` (`:4193`, `sSize = m_dSurfaceExtensionFactor * rDomain.GetSize()`); `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletStandardSolver.cpp`, `SmConstantDistanceFS::LoadInitialValues` (`:1839`) and `SmVariableRadiusFS::LoadInitialValues` (`:2897`), the same extension of the other rail's base domain.
- **What happens:** the trace and its rail solves search each base surface's natural domain extended by the solver's extension factor (0.5) times its size. A rail can lie `r / tan(t/2)` from the edge (`r` the radius or offset, `t` the angle between the faces' planes); a variable round's law also grows on past the edge's ends, where the trace runs on. A blend whose rails lie beyond the extended domain is clamped at its border, and the fillet surface fitted over the whole trace comes out wrong all along the edge, or the trace ends early.
- **Trigger:** a variable round 2 -> 11 on the 20 long top edge of a 10 x 20 x 30 box (the top 10 wide) was 0.037 mm off its circular sections; 2 -> 25 and steeper ended the trace short or folded it (the fork's commit message). The variable round 2 -> 10 on that box, its law reaching 30 where the trace ends, fits the default reach and comes out at 5822.58649 either way.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#960, the cliff overflow, which builds blends wider than their faces). The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@a9277fd (rev `68fb9162`), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run of the fork was made. The Rust port follows the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`) with the flag off.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_trace_reaches_rails`, `CreateFilletBrep` runs the fork's `sm_ExtendTraceToItsRails` for each solver before the corners (`blend/fillet_executive/session/trace_reach.rs`, through `BrepCreationAccess::creation_extend_trace_to_its_rails` in `blend/fillet_executive/p3.rs`): when the factor's extension of a base surface is shorter in 3D than `(2 / tan(t/2) + 1) r` (`t` the smallest angle between the faces' normals at seven points of the edge, at least one degree; `r` the largest radius over the trace, 65 samples of a variable round's law), it sets the solver's trace reach to that, with the parameter directions of each base surface that run across the edge. `FilletSolver::domain_extension` (`GetDomainExtension`, `blend/fillet_solver/trace_reach.rs`) then extends those directions at least that far in 3D, in `SetupOffsetExtension` and both `LoadInitialValues`; with no reach it is the factor's extension, bit for bit.

  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr609_trace_reaches_rails.rs` (the box's 2 -> 10 round: reach 90 across each face's u with the flag, none without, the volume 5822.58649 both ways, deterministic) and the unit tests in `blend/fillet_solver/trace_reach.rs`.
