#### WR-405. A hole touching its outline at a corner is built outside the face
- **Severity:** Wrong result (wrong solid; the check passes it)
- **Area:** Surface / planar faces from curves
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmSurface.cpp`, `SmSurface::CreatePlaneWith3DCurves`, and `SmBrep::CreateFacesWithCurves`: `OrderCurvesIntoLoops` accepts loops that touch at a vertex (`SmTools::FindFirstPointWithinTolToPnt`: "We allow this to accomodate two loops that touch at a vertex") and `FindOuterLoops` nests the hole in the outline, but `CreateFacesWithCurves` places each loop by classifying its start vertex against the faces made so far. The hole starts at the touching corner, which is on the outline and in no face, so it is built in the surface's big face outside the outline and kept as a face.
- **What happens:** a 10 x 6 plate with a triangular hole whose corner touches the outline at (0, 3), extruded 2 up (`SmApiCreateLinearSweep`), gives 12 faces: a 2 mm triangular prism of material with the plate's faces as a sheet around it. Building the hole as a second loop of the plate would not help: `SmFace` tests 32, 38 and 39 refuse coincident vertices and loops sharing a vertex.
- **Trigger:** planar faces (`CreatePlanarFacesWith3DCurves`, caps of linear sweeps) from an outline and a hole whose loops share a vertex.
- **How found:** reported by jyviko (tangency-labs/solidcore#892); fixed in the SolidCraft fork (1f2b0c4, `SmTrimmingTools::JoinTouchingLoops`).
- **Confirmed:** no (read at c9c5979 `source/SMLib/src/SmTools.cpp:324` and `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp:3439`; reported and fixed in the SolidCraft fork, not independently reproduced)
- **Fix:** after `FindOuterLoops`, within each outer loop's set, while a curve of one loop starts where a curve of a later loop starts, splice the later loop into the earlier one there (the earlier loop up to that vertex, the later loop round from it, the rest of the earlier loop). The face is one loop through the corner twice, as SMLib's booleans leave such a face.
- **Rust port:** `crates/solidcore/src/surface/surface/p2.rs` (`join_touching_loops`, called by `Surface::create_plane_with_3d_curves`), opt-in `Kernel::fixes.join_touching_loops` (off by default: upstream's separate loops). Test: `issue_892_hole_touching_outline_is_one_loop` in the same file (volume 118, ten faces).
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#892.
