Updates: WR-26

#### WR-26 (update, 2026-10-31). Unconditional zero-fill of all higher-order derivative slots in `HermiteCurve::evaluate` (`#2163`)

- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmHermiteCurve.cpp:299`-`303` (`SmHermiteCurve::Evaluate`, def. `:283`).
- **Defect in pinned `v1.0.1`:** `SmHermiteCurve::Evaluate` zeroes derivatives beyond the cubic third derivative with `for (ULONG i=4; i<lNumDerivatives; i++)` instead of `i <= lNumDerivatives`. Because `aPointAndDerivatives` has `lNumDerivatives + 1` entries (`0..=lNumDerivatives`), every call with `lNumDerivatives >= 4` leaves `aPointAndDerivatives[lNumDerivatives]` untouched — retaining `SM_UNDEF_DOUBLE` on a default-constructed `SmVector3d` array, uninitialised stack/heap bytes on raw buffers, or stale data when a caller reuses an evaluation buffer across curve types.
- **Resolution in the Rust port (unconditional, `#2163`):** Under `AGENTS.md` §4b, reading or returning uninitialised/stale caller buffer slots is an unconditional memory-hygiene/UB defect rather than an opt-in `UpstreamFixes` behaviour change. `HermiteCurve::evaluate` (`crates/solidcore/src/curve/hermite_curve.rs:303`) allocates `vec![Vec3::ZERO; num_derivs.saturating_add(1)]` and populates slots `0..=3`, so every derivative slot `4..=num_derivs` — including `out[num_derivs]` — is unconditionally `Vec3::ZERO` (likewise through `Curve::evaluate` and `Curve::evaluate_into` in `crates/solidcore/src/curve/curve/p2.rs`, even when `evaluate_into` starts from a `[Vec3::UNDEF; N]` slice). The module-level `Deviations` note in `hermite_curve.rs` now cites `WR-26` directly.
- **Difftest compatibility:** `harness/cpp/shim/curve_hermite_curve.cpp:164` pre-initialises `std::vector<SmVector3d> pd((size_t)n + 1, SmVector3d(0.0, 0.0, 0.0))` before calling `SmHermiteCurve::Evaluate`, so `curve.hermite_curve` remains green with bit-identical comparisons.
- **Regression tests (`curve::hermite_curve::tests`):**
  - `wr26_evaluate_zeroes_all_higher_derivative_slots_including_last`: checks a full 3D cubic (`c`, `d` non-zero on all axes) across `t` in `{0.0, 0.35, 0.7, 1.0}`, `num_derivs` in `{4, 5, 6, 10}`, and `non_zero_tangents` in `{false, true}` (plus a zero-start-tangent cubic at `t = 0.0` with `non_zero_tangents = true`), verifying `out.len() == num_derivs + 1`, exact power-basis values in `0..=3`, and `Vec3::ZERO` in every slot `4..=num_derivs` including `out[num_derivs]`.
  - `wr26_curve_dispatch_preserves_zero_last_slot`: verifies `Curve::from(h).evaluate(&Kernel::default(), 0.4, 5, true, false)` and `Curve::evaluate_into` into `[Vec3::UNDEF; 6]` both yield `Vec3::ZERO` in `out[4]` and `out[5]`.
