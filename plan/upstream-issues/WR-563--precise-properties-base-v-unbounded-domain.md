#### WR-563. `SmFace::ComputePreciseProperties` starts every iso-u integral at the face UV domain's lowest v, so a face unbounded in v loses its accuracy (1.0.1)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Mass properties
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFace.cpp:6223` at `3fd5b95` (`SmODEAreaIFEO sAreaEval(*this->GetSurface(),*pBSC,GetUVDomain().GetMin().y, ...)`), `SmFace::ComputePreciseProperties` (def. `:6144`); `:6073`-`:6079` (`dStep = sPV[0].y - m_dMinV`, `RungeKuttaODEIntegrate(dY,m_dMinV, sPV[0].y, ...)`), `SmODEAreaIFEO::Evaluate` (def. `:6042`).
- **What happens:** the face's properties are a loop integral along each UV trim curve of the integral along the iso-u line from `m_dMinV`, the face UV domain's lowest v, up to the trim point. Any base gives the same loop integral (another base adds a function of u alone, whose integral round a closed loop is zero), but on a face whose UV domain is the surface's unbounded one (`±INFINITE_PARAMETER` = ±1234567 in v) every inner integral runs about 1.2e6 parameter units below the face. Each is then a large term, its error bounded only relative to the per-component scale, and the area is the small difference of large terms.
- **Trigger:** `ComputePreciseProperties` of a disk of radius 0.5 on an `SmPlane` at (100, 200, 300) whose UV domain is (-1, -1234567)..(1, 1234567) (`CreateFacesWithCurves` with one `SmCircle`), accuracy 1e-6, `PPF_AREA | PPF_AREA_FIRST_MOMENTS`, origin (100.37, 200.21, 300.41): area 0.7857377377804369 against π/4 = 0.7853981633974483 (4.3e-4 relative) and first moment y -209.77691650390625 against -0.16493361431346 (the same face with the domain bounded to (-1, 1) in v: 0.7853981649071202 and -0.16493361681648178). `SmApiFaceComputeCentroid` of the faces of `SmApiCreateCylinder` (origin (100, 200, 300), radius r, height 2.4 r) with each face's v domain set to ±1234567, accuracy 1e-6: side area off by +1.7% (r 5), -43% (r 0.5), +485% (r 0.05); cap areas off by 2e-4 to 7e-4; centroids off by up to about r.
- **How found:** tangency-labs/solidcore#1556 (STEP imports through surface kinds whose domain stays open, such as `SurfOfExtrusion`; SolidCraft modelling with the C++ library saw faces at up to 2.1 times their area until the domains were bounded).
- **Confirmed:** the C++ reference build gives the disk numbers above (difftest `mass.integrator`, `fixtures/mass.integrator/issue1556_unbounded_v.json`, at parity with the Rust port's default); the cylinder numbers are the Rust port's default (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1556_unbounded_v_face_properties.rs` with the flag off).
- **Fix (opt-in, not the default):** `Kernel::fixes.precise_properties_trim_base_v` starts the inner integrals at the lowest v of the union of the oriented trim curves' bounding boxes (as `CalculateUVDomainFromUVTrimCurves` builds the trim domain), kept no lower than the face UV domain's lowest v; with no trims, the domain's. With it on, every face of the cylinders above holds 2e-6 of its exact area and 1e-6 r of its exact centroid at accuracy 1e-6 (the test above with the flag on). The fix is independent of WR-494's `precise_properties_split_at_surface_knots`, which hides the error on the cylinder sides but not on planar caps.
