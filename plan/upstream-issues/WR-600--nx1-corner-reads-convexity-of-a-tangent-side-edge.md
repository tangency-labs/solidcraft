#### WR-600. An Nx1 fillet corner reads a convexity off a tangent side edge, so the two ends of one arc differ (status 1001)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletNx1Corner::MakeCornerTopology`, where `bSideEdgeSameCvxty[]` is set from `same_convexity_edges()` (before the tangent side-edge skip).
- **What happens:** at each end of a round, the corner asks `same_convexity_edges()` whether each side edge has the filleted edge's convexity. When a side edge's faces meet tangentially at the vertex (G1), `IsConvexRadialSector` reads only the sign of the rounding in the geometry. With a sketch solver's residues, the two ends of one concave arc read differently: in the fork's gdb run, "same" at the back end and "opposite" at the front. So the front corner was not built as the mixed-convexity corner the back end is, and the round failed with 1001, "Corner Edge Computation Failure".
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft rig case `fillets/report-fillet-concave-arc-bar-top-into-cylinder-solver-residues`. A stadium bar's top meets a cylinder its round end lies on, with the cylinder as one face, so the vertices at the arc's ends have 3 edges. The bar's sides run tangent into the cylinder, and the concave arc where the top meets the cylinder is rounded r 2.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft. The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@7992ec7 ("fillets: an Nx1 corner's tangent side edge is of the filleted edge's convexity"), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** no. The Rust port did not reproduce the "opposite" reading. On bars x 0..20 with R 5 (axis-aligned with residues up to 3e-10, and rotated by 10 to 90 degrees), `same_convexity_edges` read every tangent side edge "same", and the round failed with 1001 at both ends for a reason this record does not cover. The pinned C++ has the same code, but the fork's case geometry is not available here.
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@7992ec7. A side edge tangential at the vertex (`sm_CheckTangentialEdgeuseAtVertex`) has no convexity and is never of the opposite one, so `bSideEdgeSameCvxty` becomes `TRUE` for it. WR-593 (tangency-labs/smlib@1ff7353) reads edges reached past such an edge in the same way.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. `Kernel::fixes.fillet_nx1_tangent_side_edge_same_convexity` ports the change in `tangent_sides_same_convexity`, which `FilletCorner::make_nx1_corner_topology` calls after `same_convexity_edges`. It does not depend on WR-457's flag. Test: `wr_600_tangent_side_edge_is_of_the_filleted_convexity` in `blend/fillet_corner/topology.rs`, on the peg's vertex (20, -5, 10).
