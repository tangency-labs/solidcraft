#### WR-407. Two walls swept along planar arcs that share their planes fail to join (1006), and a coincident face can be merged onto a B-spline continued across a fold
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Intersection
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp:5112`, `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateCurveSweep` (the `sPathPtAndDir[1].MakeUnitOrthoVectors` frame), and `source/SMLib/src/SmSurface.cpp:8263`, `SmSurface::CoverCoincidentSurface` (the `sOrigDomain.Union(sNewDomain, sUpdateDomain)` / `AdjustSTEPUVDomain` step). The sweep's own frame is built again in `source/SMLib/src/SmBSplineSurfaceNLib.cpp:5806` (`SmBSplineSurface::CreateSweepSurface`, `Z0` from `MakeUnitOrthoVectors` of the trajectory's start tangent).
- **What happens:** `CreateCurveSweep` turns the path so that a frame built from the start tangent's `MakeUnitOrthoVectors` becomes the global one, and the sweep (`N_CreateSweepScale`, `Z0` = global z) carries the profile on a frame that starts there and is computed point to point. Along a planar path the plane's normal is the one direction that frame keeps; for a start tangent along +y `MakeUnitOrthoVectors` puts z in the path's plane instead, so the path does not come out in the frame's XY plane and a wall swept along a half circle gets a flat top whose control points are spread 2.8e-5 mm about its plane (the modelling tolerance is 1e-5). Two such walls that share their top and bottom planes then fail their union with `SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER` (1006) from `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint`: the spurious vertices on one wall's top split its edges, and an edge definition finds no vertex at its end. With exactly planar tops the union completes, but it merges the coincident tops through `CoverCoincidentSurface` onto one B-spline *continued past its natural domain* to cover the other (a planar one up to a hundred times its size). That continuation is not one sheet: the straight lines of a swept planar sector run on through the sector's centre and back over it. Points of the other face are then dropped onto the wrong sheet, the merged face's UV domain spans the fold, and the face rebuilt on it gets UV trims far from its edges (UV gaps, the wrong volume).
- **Trigger:** wall A, a 12 x 60 rectangle swept along a half circle of radius 50 (r 44..56 over 0..180 degrees), and wall B, the same along a half circle of radius 58 turned 90 degrees (r 52..64 over 90..270), joined by `SmApiBooleanUnion`; the walls overlap over a quarter turn and share the planes z = 0 and z = 60. On the base the union returns 1006; with the sweep-frame half alone it completes wrongly (volume 157833 for 223933 with UV trim gaps).
- **How found:** SolidCraft's kernel regression rig, case `tests/kernel-cases/sweeps/844-join-two-overlapping-swept-arc-walls` (solidcraft#844), reported as tangency-labs/solidcore#434.
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the C++ build: the pinned `c9c5979` fails the union with 1006, and both halves are needed. Fixed on the fork by tangency-labs/smlib `1b6f5fa` (the frame starts on the plane's normal; a B-spline covers past its domain only where its normal neither vanishes nor turns back between neighbouring samples of a 21 x 21 grid over the domain the merge uses). The fork's result is one valid solid of volume 223932.59 against the closed form 223932.72.
- **Rust port:** both halves are carried as the documented deviation "SolidCraft change, tangency-labs/smlib 1b6f5fa" (`primitive/primitive_creation/p3/geometry.rs`, `surface/surface/p3.rs`); the case is `fixtures/solidcraft/434-two-overlapping-swept-arc-walls.json` and the divergence it causes in `surface.surface_p3` is in `plan/findings/surface.surface_p3--cover-refuses-a-folded-continuation.md`.
