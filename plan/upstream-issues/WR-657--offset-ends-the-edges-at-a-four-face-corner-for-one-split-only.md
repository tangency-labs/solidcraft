#### WR-657. A solid offset ends the edges at a corner of four plane faces for one of its two splits only, so two chamfers meeting at a corner break the inset and the shells
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a shell returns success with no volume and loop gaps, or with loop gaps; an offset returns no closed solid or is refused)
- **Area:** Offsets and shells
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp`, `SmOffsetExecutive::DoExtendedOffset` (def. `:1105`): the edge offsets end each edge at a corner where four faces meet without regard to how the corner splits, and nothing between them and `SmOffsetExecutive::PiecewiseMerge` (def. `:2362`) gives the corner the edge it becomes.
- **What happens:** at a convex corner where four plane faces meet, the four offset planes in general no longer meet in one point (they do in the original). The corner becomes an edge between two faces across it from each other (`a` and `c`), from the point where `a`, `b` and `c` meet to the one where `c`, `d` and `a` meet; which pair it is depends on the offset (outward or inward, which face is open). Each offset face is built as its own sheet, and the offset ends that face's two edges at the corner at the same pair of those points whichever way the corner splits. For one split (an outset of two chamfers meeting at a corner) the ends are right and only the two faces that gain the edge are left with a loop gap; for the other (an inset, or a shell open at one of the four faces) both points lie outside the offset solid.
  - Shell 1 inward open at a wall at the corner: success with 10 faces, no closed solid and volume 0 (`SmApiBrepCheck`: loop gaps of sqrt(2)).
  - Shell 1 outward open at that wall: success at the right volume (2152.620538), with loop gaps of sqrt(2) and check failures.
  - With the upright edge under the corner chamfered too (a third chamfer and a corner triangle: corners of four faces at each of the triangle's ends), an offset by 1 is a success with loop gaps and volume 0, and by -1 is refused (1001); most shells are one or the other.
  - Every edge chamfered: both offsets and all 52 shells (each face open, 1 each way) are refused (1001, `SmMerge::MakeFaceuse` and `offset_brep`).
- **Trigger:** a 30 x 20 x 10 box (`SmApiCreateBox`) with edges 0 and 9 chamfered 2 (`SmApiFilletEdges`, a linear section): edge 0 is the top's edge at x = 30, edge 9 the top's edge at y = 0, meeting at (30, 0, 10). Shell 1 inward open at the wall x = 30, or 1 outward. Then edge 8 (the upright edge at x = 30, y = 0) chamfered too, and every edge chamfered.
- **How found:** the private ordinary-operation matrix (chamfered boxes, `kbox:30:20:10:2:0.9`, `0.9.8` and `cbox`), with the port at its defaults and with every opt-in.
- **Confirmed:** on the pinned C++ build (v1.0.1, 3fd5b95), the matrix's C++ runner: the inward shell open at x = 30 returns `status 1000` with 10 faces, 23 edges, not manifold, volume 0 and 4 check failures; the outward one 15 faces, 34 edges, volume 2152.620538 and 5 check failures, as the port does.
- **Related:** WR-578 (`Kernel::fixes.offset_closes_loop_gaps`) closes a loop's gap with the straight edge from the gap's end to the face's vertex. That closes the outward split's gaps (the outward shell above comes out sound) but not the inward one, whose vertex is outside the offset solid: with that flag alone the inward shells are refused (`SM_ERR`), and an inset of the three-chamfer box or of the fully chamfered box is a success with no closed solid and volume 0 (`offset_brep` has no counterpart of the flag's refusal in `shell_brep`). #1795 and WR-617 are of the same family (an offset makes two faces meet that did not) but not this mechanism: an edge between curved faces whose offsets stop meeting along one curve (`MateEdges`), which this fix does not reach.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.offset_splits_corner_vertices` and `offset_closes_loop_gaps` (the new flag has no effect without it), `close_loop_gaps` first runs `split_corner_vertices` (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/step_gaps.rs`):
  - For each vertex of the original with four edges and four plane faces, a convex corner (each edge runs inside every face's plane, the normals turned against the sum of the edges' directions), it finds the split: the pair of faces across the corner for which both meeting points lie inside the fourth face's offset plane (by more than ten times the tolerance; none where the four planes still meet).
  - In each offset face at the corner it moves the two edges' ends at the corner to their own points of the split along their lines (an edge on face `b` to `a b c`, the others to `c d a`), and the face's vertex there to the nearer. `close_loop_gaps` then closes `a`'s and `c`'s loops with the straight edge between the two points, as for WR-578.
  - All corners are planned before any edit, as an edge between two split corners moves at both ends. A corner one of whose edges would vanish (its ends meet or pass one another: more changes there than one new edge, as where a corner triangle is offset clear of the body) is left as WR-578 leaves it, and the rest are planned again without it.
  - An offset (`offset_brep`) or a shell of a manifold solid that is no closed manifold solid is refused (`SM_ERR`) instead of returned.

  `tests/wr_657_offset_splits_corner_vertices.rs` checks the volumes against the closed forms (each body is convex: the volume of its faces' half-spaces moved by the offset, an open face staying, computed apart from the kernel) to 1e-9; the port lands within 1e-12. The two-chamfer box shelled 1 inward open at x = 30 is 1806.1797017478 (upstream: volume 0), outward 2152.6205383942 with no loop gap; the three-chamfer box offsets by +-1 are 8238.5322279774 and 3979.9694554144; the fully chamfered box offsets are 7654.8483019533 and 3831.7556433148. In the probe behind the test, every shell of the two- and three-chamfer boxes and 40 of the fully chamfered box's 52 shells (both offsets of each) come out sound at their closed forms; with the flags forced off five of the seven tests fail.
- **Open (2026-10-31):**
  - Inward shells open at a chamfer: on the fully chamfered box the cavity meets the open chamfer along a line only (it is 1 in from both its sides, the chamfer's width), so the result, at the right volume, is no manifold solid; the opt-in refuses those 12 (`SM_ERR`). On the two- and three-chamfer boxes the shells open at a chamfer (or at the corner triangle's neighbours) are refused by WR-578's `close_loop_gap` with `SM_ERR_OFFSET_EDGE_VANISHES` (1043): the edges of the open face's neighbours run past the end of their edge there, a change of topology neither fix makes.
  - Only corners of exactly four plane faces are split. A corner of five or more faces, or with a curved face, is left to WR-578.
