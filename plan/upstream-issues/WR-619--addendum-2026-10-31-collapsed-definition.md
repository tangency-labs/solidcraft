Updates: WR-619

#### WR-619 (update, 2026-10-31). Why the strip union deletes A's bottom piece: a definition imprinted as a closed edge on one vertex
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp`, `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint` (its `GetFinalVertex` calls for the definition's two ends) and `SmProtoEdge::Resolve` (the self-loop test); the deletion follows in `SmMerge::ManifoldBoolean`'s radial-sector classification (`SmEdge::FindRadialSector` / `RadialSectorClassify`, `source/SMLib/src/SmEdge.cpp:1789`, `:1050`).
- **What happens:** on each of the coplanar bottoms (z = 0) and tops (z = 4), the intersection makes a proto edge from one ProtoVertex back to itself, holding two edge definitions. `Resolve` keeps it (its first definition's curve, 1e-4 long, is longer than twice its tolerance, 1e-5; WR-306's test does not apply, its ends finding different vertex definitions). At the imprint, one definition gets two distinct Vertices in each Brep and is a proper edge; the other, a speck (a line 5e-6 long, every point within 2.6e-6 of the Vertex, whose tolerance is 1.25e-5), gets the same Vertex for both its ends in each Brep, so it is made a closed edge from that Vertex back to itself (in B, edge 26 inside B's side face F2). At that edge B's two faces form a flat (180°) radial sector, and A's coplanar bottom sliver piece, at 90° to both, is classified inside B and deleted (the second leak of WR-619). The two engines agree call for call (gdb: the same radial-sector answers, RSC result codes 0/1, and the same STEP 6 delete list).
- **Possible upstream cause:** `GetFinalVertex` picks each end's Vertex per Brep among the ProtoVertex's vertex definitions; both ends of the collapsed definition pick one Vertex. Changing that choice would change vertex picking for every definition, so the opt-in below does not.
- **How found:** gdb on both sides (step-off frames, the B-rep's edges around the closed edge, the proto edges at `Resolve` and the final vertices at the imprint).
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With the opt-in `Kernel::fixes.imprint_skips_collapsed_definition` (own design, an extension beyond the fork, not a fork fix; it turns on no other flag, in particular neither WR-306's `degenerate_proto_edge_in_one_vertex` nor WR-214's `proto_edge_self_loop_on_vertex_degenerate`), `imprint_edge_definition` makes no edge for a definition when all hold: its ends are one ProtoVertex; they land on one Vertex in each Brep; its curve is a speck on those Vertices (9 samples, ends included, all within each Brep's Vertex tolerance and within the EdgeDefinition's own tolerance, `speck_on_vertex`); and another definition of its ProtoEdge gets two distinct Vertices in each Brep, so the ProtoEdge is not lost (`collapsed_beside_sibling` in `topology/proto_topology/p2.rs`). The speck carries no geometry its Vertex does not have. Otherwise nothing changes.

**Which strips it fixes.** On the fixture's strips (`wr614_coincident_strip.json`) the speck is a line d long, from y = 20 to y = 20 - d at B's corner. This was traced 5e-6 apart. 1e-5 and 1.5e-5 apart it is inferred from the vertex positions and tolerances at the skip (gdb), and agrees with the 1.246e-5 Level-2 gap 1.5e-5 apart. The opt-in skips it 5e-6 and 1e-5 apart: the union 5e-6 apart becomes one solid of both cubes' volume; the difference 5e-6 and 1e-5 apart becomes box A as it was, 6 faces and manifold (upstream: 8 faces, non-manifold, a sheet); the intersection there becomes empty (upstream: two sheets). The union 1e-5 apart still fails with `SM_ERR` (WR-614). 2e-6 apart nothing changes. 1.5e-5 apart the speck reaches 1.25e-5 from Brep 1's Vertex, past the definition's tolerance of 1e-5, so the opt-in leaves it, and all three results are upstream's. 1e-5 apart the speck reaches exactly 1e-5, the definition's tolerance (and Brep 1's Vertex tolerance), and is skipped only because the test is `<=`. That gap sits exactly on the bound, so a one-ULP change in the operands could flip it. 5e-6 apart it is well inside: it reaches 5e-6 from Brep 1's Vertex and 2.5e-6 from Brep 2's, against 1e-5.

**Tried and dropped.** Without the definition-tolerance bound, the skip also fires 1.5e-5 apart, and the union there fails the Level-2 check: its Edges end 1.25e-5 from the Vertex, past half the Vertex tolerance (7.5e-6; `SmBrep.cpp:33600`). Two changes were tried. (a) Fitting the Vertex to its Edges afterwards, with WR-306's fit (marking the ProtoVertex absorbed; the fork's 03e7bf1 `UpdateVertexEdgeIntervals`): this moves 3D Edge ends but not the UV trims. The difference then failed with `SmTrimmingTools` Edge/Edge gaps of 2e-4 against 2e-5, and the 1.5e-5 union with `SmFace` "Face has Coincident Edges", so it was dropped. Do not retry it without moving the trims too. (b) Bounding the speck by the definition's tolerance as well, which is the rule above. Half the Vertex tolerance, the Level-2 bound, was considered and not used: 1e-5 apart the speck reaches 1e-5, so that bound would leave 1e-5 unfixed, and 5e-6 apart it is met only with equality.

`crates/solidcore/tests/wr_619_imprint_skips_collapsed_definition.rs` checks:
- the strip union 5e-6 apart: one solid region, manifold, the volume of both cubes, no closed edge on a speck, both operands and the result valid;
- the difference 1e-5 and 5e-6 apart: box A, valid, its volume;
- the intersection 1e-5 and 5e-6 apart: empty;
- that 1.5e-5 apart the union, difference and intersection equal those without the opt-in (shape, volume and Level-2 failures);
- that a genuine closed edge on a vertex (a cylinder's circle on a box's top, radius 1 and 1e-3) and an ordinary union of two boxes on one plane are unchanged;
- and it records the 1e-5 union.
