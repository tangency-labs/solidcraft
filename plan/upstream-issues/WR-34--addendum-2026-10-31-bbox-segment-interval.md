- **Details:** `SmCompositeCurve::CalculateBoundingBox` (`source/SMLib/src/SmCompositeCurve.cpp:414-450`) has two coupled parameter-interval defects relative to `SmCompositeCurve::GetNaturalInterval` (`:467-495`) and `SmCompositeCurve::MakeCompositeNurb` / `SmBSplineCurve::CreateByJoining`:
  1. `sIvlOffset` starts at `0.0` and translates each segment's natural interval `[a_i, b_i]` by `sum_{j < i} (b_j - a_j)` to `[a_i + sum_{j < i} L_j, b_i + sum_{j < i} L_j]`, which neither anchors subsequent segments after `b_0` when `a_0 != 0` nor strips `a_i` when `a_i != 0` (for example, segments on `[5, 7]` and `[10, 13]` have composite `GetNaturalInterval` `[5, 10]`, yet `CalculateBoundingBox` shifts segment 1 to `[12, 15]`, completely missing it over `[5, 10]`).
  2. The intersected composite sub-interval `sTgtIvl` is passed directly to `m_aCurves[ii].GetCurve()->CalculateBoundingBox(sTgtIvl, ...)` without mapping back into `[a_i, b_i]` or reversing within `[a_i, b_i]` when `!m_aCurves[ii].GetSameSense()`, causing `SmBSplineCurve` segments to clamp to wrong knot spans and `SmLine` segments to extrapolate outside their domain.
- **Rust port (#2135):** Default (`Kernel::fixes.composite_curve_bbox_segment_intervals == false`) preserves pinned SMLib behaviour. With `Kernel::fixes.composite_curve_bbox_segment_intervals = true`, `CompositeCurve::calculate_bounding_box` stacks each valid segment's composite span end to end starting at the first valid segment's `natural_interval().min()` (`[a_0, b_0]`, then `[comp_start_i, comp_start_i + (b_i - a_i)]`) and maps each non-empty overlap `[t_lo, t_hi]` with `interval` back onto `[a_i, b_i]` (`[a_i + (t_lo - comp_start_i), a_i + (t_hi - comp_start_i)]` when `same_sense`, or `[b_i - (t_hi - comp_start_i), b_i - (t_lo - comp_start_i)]` when `!same_sense`). Scope note: segment `calculate_bounding_box` calls and box unioning (including `PolarBox` tangent sense on `same_sense == false` segments and `PolarBox::union_assign`) are otherwise unchanged.
