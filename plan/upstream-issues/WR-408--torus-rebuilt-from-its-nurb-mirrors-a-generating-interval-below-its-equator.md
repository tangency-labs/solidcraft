#### WR-408. `SmTorus::RebuildSTEPFromNURBParameters` mirrors a generating interval below the equator
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a torus face's analytic surface is the wrong half of the tube)
- **Area:** Surfaces / torus
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmTorus.cpp`, `SmTorus::RebuildSTEPFromNURBParameters`, and `IsNurbSurfaceTorus` (not ported): the generating circle's end angles come from `AngleBetween`, which is unsigned (0 to 180 degrees).
- **What happens:** an interval below the torus's equator, or one past 180 degrees, comes out mirrored. For example -90..90 (the outer half of the tube) becomes 90..450 (the inner half). `SmFace::SwapUV` rebuilds the torus this way when a sweep orients its faces, so a partial revolve of an arc running the other way round fails the check (`SmSurfOfRevolution` #2, `SmTorus` #2, and #5 on a horn torus).
- **Trigger:** rebuild the torus of a face whose generating interval starts below the equator, e.g. a half disc whose arc runs from -90 to 90 degrees, revolved a quarter, half or three-quarter turn.
- **How found:** reported by jyviko (tangency-labs/solidcore#1112, SolidCraft rig sweeps/4007). Fixed in the SolidCraft fork ("a torus rebuilt from its NURB keeps a generating interval below its equator", `sm_TorusGenCurveInterval`).
- **Confirmed:** no (read at c9c5979 `source/SMLib/src/SmTorus.cpp:1162-1163`; reported and fixed in the SolidCraft fork, not independently reproduced; shown by the faithful port, test `issue_1112_rebuilt_torus_keeps_an_interval_below_its_equator` with the opt-in off)
- **Fix:** measure both angles signed, counter-clockwise from the circle's X axis toward the torus's axis (as `EvaluateSTEP` measures V), over a whole turn. For an inside-out torus, take the interval from the NURB's ends in reverse order. Snap angles within `SM_EFF_ZERO_DEG` of 0, and lift the end a turn when it is not above the start.
- **Rust port:** `crates/solidcore/src/surface/torus.rs` (`torus_gen_curve_interval`, used by `Torus::rebuild_step_from_nurb_parameters`), opt-in `Kernel::fixes.torus_signed_gen_curve_interval`. It is off by default, which keeps upstream's unsigned angles, so the `surface.torus` difftest keeps matching the C++. `IsNurbSurfaceTorus` is not ported, so it has no counterpart. Test: `issue_1112_rebuilt_torus_keeps_an_interval_below_its_equator` in the same file. A quarter torus over v -90..90 rebuilds to 270..450, the same points.
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#1112.
