#### WR-511. `SmSurfOfExtrusion::AssertValid` test 8 point-solves its origin with an unscaled `SM_EFF_ZERO`, so a valid extrusion at coordinates of about 10 fails "origin is a point on the generating curve"
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Surfaces and volumes
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmSurfOfExtrusion.cpp:4154` (origin-check comment; the `GlobalPointSolve` at `:4156`, test-8 report at `:4164`), `SmSurfOfExtrusion::AssertValid` (def. `:4049`).
- **Pinned reference:** SMLib v1.0.1, commit `3fd5b95`.
- **What happens:** Test 8 calls `m_pGenCurve->GlobalPointSolve(..., SM_SO_INTERSECT, m_vOrigin, SM_EFF_ZERO, ...)` and reports the origin missing when the solve returns no solution. At coordinates of about 10 the solve resolves an origin that was evaluated on the curve to a few 1e-12, past the unscaled 1e-12, so a valid extrusion is refused. The issue's correction is a coordinate-aware zero with its own factor, `SM_EFF_ZERO * (1 + |origin|max)`; the neighbouring point checks use different tolerances (test 5's `dTol` uses factor 100, test 6 uses `SM_EFF_ZERO_SQRT`, tests 9/11 use `SM_EFF_ZERO` on distances).
- **Trigger:** `SmSurfOfExtrusion::AssertValid` on an extrusion of a quadratic Bezier at coordinates of about 10 (a text outline's letter), whose origin was evaluated on the generating curve.
- **How found:** issue tangency-labs/solidcore#1508 (SolidCraft text-outline sweep; fork tangency-labs/smlib#31). Read during porting. Upstream's real-extrusion `surface.surface_assert_valid` harness covers the override, but the issue's exact trigger is not one of its fixtures. The port's regression test `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1508_extrusion_origin_scaled_zero.rs` exercises the issue's scale with an intentional 5e-12 out-of-plane residual, both without and with a made NURB; the latter also runs the base, STEP/NURB and generator-parameterization checks at levels 0, 1 and 2, and the STEP/NURB and parameterization checks also at `LEVEL_GIVEN`.
- **Confirmed:** no. The issue's exact test-8 case at coordinates of about 10 was not generated for the C++ comparison, so the defect report itself is not confirmed; the issue's capi `sweeps.cpp` test is the reported confirmation. The regression's 5e-12 residual is synthetic, not the exact original valid-text-outline/C++ trigger.

The fork's correction is `SM_EFF_ZERO * (1.0 + m_vOrigin.GetMaxDimension())`. The port keeps the pinned C++ result by default and applies it only with the opt-in, default-off `Kernel::fixes.extrusion_origin_scaled_zero` (`crates/solidcore/src/kernel.rs`), so the default path follows the pinned reference and difftest tolerances are unchanged.
