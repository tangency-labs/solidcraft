#### WR-631. A convex blend whose rail leaves its face across a cliff cannot be built
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blend (fillet solver)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletExecutive.cpp`, `SmFilletExecutive::CreateFilletBrep` and `DoFilleting`; `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletNx1Corner::CalcCornerEdgeGeom`.
- **What happens:** a round or chamfer that removes material can be wider than a face it rolls on, its rail leaving that face between the blend's ends across a non-tangent edge (a cliff: an opening's rim, the body's outline). SMLib traces it, then trims and merges fillet faces that run off their faces: the result is not a closed solid, or the corners fail. SolidCraft's opt-in check (WR-302) refuses every such blend.
- **Trigger:** a round on a box edge wider than the face beside it (a 5 x 5 bar's edge r10), a round across a hole's rim, a pocket's rim chamfer wider than the pocket is deep (SolidCraft #1080, #1074, #1466, #1467).
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#960). The fork builds such a blend on its faces' continuations, closes it into the region it removes and subtracts that from the body (tangency-labs/smlib@8ba4667, `SmFilletCliffOverflow.cpp`, rev `68fb9162`), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run of the fork was made. The Rust port follows the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`) with the flag off.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_cliff_overflow` and `fillet_refuse_rails_leaving_faces`, `CreateFilletBrep` makes the fork's choice (`FilletExecutive::choose_cliff_overflow`, `blend/fillet_executive/p3.rs`): `RailsFitTheirFaces` also tells whether a rail rolls over a tangent edge and whether a rail that leaves its face runs on into the body (`IsConcaveEdge`, `sm_InsideMaterial`, `blend/fillet_solver/p5/rails_fit.rs`); a concave overflow, a round running into the material, an overflow with another blend concave, with a rollover, or with the global merge on, are refused with `FilletErrorType::NoRoom` and the fork's messages; otherwise `FilletExecutive::cliff_overflow` is set and the rollover classification is skipped. `SM_FILERR_NOT_EXACT` is `FilletErrorType::NotExact` (10; the fork's 9). The open ends, the cut at them, the removal region and the subtraction are not ported yet: an accepted overflow is still refused with the WR-302 message.

  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr631_cliff_overflow_choice.rs` (a stepped bar: a convex round over a cliff is an overflow; a round into the wall beyond a step is refused and a chamfer there overflows; a concave overflow is refused; a blend that fits is none).
