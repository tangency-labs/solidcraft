Updates: UB-242

#### UB-242 (update, 2026-10-31). A MakeEdgeInFace that finds no vertex sector edits nothing, so upstream's continuation is defined
- **Location:** as UB-242; `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint` (`source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp:1462` for Brep1, `:1538` for Brep2) calling `SmBrep::MakeEdgeInFace` (`source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp:13770`), whose two `SmFace::FindVertexSector` calls and `NER(pStartVU)` / `NER(pEndVU)` (`:13845-13873`) come before `MakeEdgeInFaceWithVU`, the first edit.
- **What happens:** `FindVertexSector` is const, so a `MakeEdgeInFace` that finds no sector returns `SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER` (1006) with its B-rep untouched. When this happens in Brep2 after Brep1's `MakeEdgeInFace` succeeded, Brep1's new edge is whole: `Imprint` only forgets the curves Brep1 now owns and deletes the copy. No vertex use is freed, so the UB-242 hazard does not arise. Upstream `IntersectInsertRelate` goes on, and that continuation is defined.
- **Trigger:** `DoDefeature` of face 2 of `Defeature/defillet.smb` (`blend.feature_executive` case `single_face_defillet_2`). Extending face 8 XORs its extended B-spline surface with a copy of the face. The extension folds over itself (for example, UV (0, 1.0) and (0, -2.955) are the same point), so the intersection UV curves sit on the other preimage of the face's vertices. Eight edge-definition imprints then find no sector. The same failure appears in `single_face_fillet1019_5`, `_6`, `rb_33_defillet_8` and `single_loop_circle_defillet_2`.
- **How found:** temporary probes on both sides. A `-Wl,--wrap` of `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint` showed the same eight 1006 statuses as the port. After the imprint, the C++ (whose `IntersectInsertRelate` returns `SM_SUCCESS`) and the port have the same topology and UV trim curves. The port then stopped, so its XOR kept 7 faces where the C++ has 10.
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the C++ build.
- **Rust port:** `ImprintOutcome::failure_edited` no longer counts an edge-definition imprint whose failing `MakeEdgeInFace` stopped at its `FindVertexSector` checks (`topology::brep::p5::is_no_vertex_sector`, operation `NO_VERTEX_SECTOR_OP`). The port then goes on as upstream. The `boolean.topology_intersector_p2` shim's copy decides the same case with the same const `FindVertexSector` calls before the imprint (`ed_fails_unedited`). Failures inside `MakeEdgeInFaceWithVU`, and failed vertex-definition imprints and glues, still stop as before.
- **Parked cases:** the two `offset.offset_executive_p2` cases parked with `cpp_defect = "UB-242"` (`issue1528_box_cylinder_api_shell_2_volume`, `_first`) are this case. Their second `MakeEdgeInFace` stops at `FindVertexSector` (the 2026-10-30 addendum). Both now run, go on as upstream and match the C++, so the parked entries are removed. `boolean.api_brep`'s `ub175_after_editing_imprint_failure`, previously rejected as UB-242, also runs and matches now.
