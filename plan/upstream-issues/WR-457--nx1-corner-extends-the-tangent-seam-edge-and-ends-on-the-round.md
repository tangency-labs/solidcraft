#### WR-457. An Nx1 fillet corner takes a tangent side edge as its side edge and picks the round as the end face (status 1001)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletNx1Corner::MakeCornerTopology` (about `:6040`-`:6300`), and `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`, `SmFilletEdge::CalcEdgeOnExtendedEdge` (about `:7649`).
- **What happens:** a side edge of the corner whose two faces meet tangentially at the vertex (a flat top running smoothly onto a round) is treated as a real same-convexity side edge. The corner extends it and takes the round as the end face, which the fillet never meets, so the blend fails with status 1001. The end face should be the face beyond the tangent seam, reached by skipping the tangent edge round the vertex, and the extended edge is where the side face meets that end face's extension, which `CalcEdgeOnExtendedEdge` cannot represent (it intersects the extended edge's own two faces).
- **Trigger:** an r 2 round on the straight edge between a stadium bar's flat top and a slot floor, each end of the edge on the seam where the top runs tangent into the rounded end (tangency-labs/solidcore#418, SolidCraft rig case 3369, `fixtures/solidcraft/3369-fillet-edge-on-tangent-seam.json`).
- **How found:** SolidCraft's fillets and chamfers on SMLib (rig area `fillets`, ladder tiers 07/08).
- **Confirmed:** yes, the Rust port refuses it too (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_418_fillet_edge_on_tangent_seam.rs`).
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@d5ebcb3: such a side edge is skipped round the vertex (when the edge past it has the filleted edge's convexity and the other side edge the opposite one), giving the standard 3x1 mixed-convexity corner with the slot wall as end face; `CalcEdgeOnExtendedEdge` intersects the side face with the end face's extension when the side face is not one of the edge's own faces.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_nx1_tangent_side_edge` (`FilletCorner::make_nx1_corner_topology`, `FilletBrep::calc_edge_on_extended_edge`) it ports the fork's change; the round then removes (4 - pi) x 6 from the bar, as the analytic volume.
