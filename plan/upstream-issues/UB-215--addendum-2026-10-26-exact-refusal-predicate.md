#### UB-215 (addendum, 2026-10-26). The exact refusal predicate for partial cones and tori
- **Severity:** Crash / undefined behaviour
- **Area:** Topology
- **Location:** as UB-215. The read is reached only when `SweepEdgeHigh`'s degenerate-surface test (`IsDegenerateCurve() || IsDegeneratePoint()`, `SM_ERR_INVALID_INPUT`) lets the face through, `MakeFaceWithCurves` deletes the profile curve's swept copy (`ApproximateLength(ivl, 5) < SM_ZONE_TOL_3D`) and still returns `SM_SUCCESS`.
- **What happens:** the face surface (`CreateSurfOfRevolution`) is the profile curve turned rigidly, so at `(u, v)` `|Sv|` is the curve's speed (`L` for a line, `6.5073 * minor` at most for the torus' quadratic minor circle at the sampled parameters) and `|Su|` is the distance from the axis times the sweep circle's speed. `SmSurface::IsDegenerateCurve` refuses when `|Su|` or `|Sv|` is at most `max(1e-6, SM_EFF_ZERO * (1 + max dimension of the profile curve's ends))` at all 13 samples (corners, midlines, diagonals, centre); `sm_IsNSrfDegenerate` refuses when the control net's box is at most `max(1e-6, 1000 * SM_EFF_ZERO * (1 + its max dimension))` along every axis, which from about 1e4 away refuses every line in the band and 1,000 away decides at `1.001e-6`. The sweep circle is `N_CreateCircArc`'s: `N_ConicData` turns a sweep below `1e-6` degrees, or a negative one, into a (nearly) closed circle, so a `1e-9` degree turn gets a large `|Su|`, passes the test and reads the freed curve, while a turn of `0.01` degrees is refused. `SmRotationalSweepGeometry`'s constructor asserts (debug only) a turn above `1e-4` degrees.
- **Trigger:** native boundaries (refused below, freed read above), bisected to 12 digits: cone top line `1e-6` at unit size (90, 359, -30 and `1e-7` degrees, base and side lines alike), `5.73685662283e-6` at 10 degrees and `1.93185165258e-6` at 30 (`|Su|`), `1.00100004374e-6` at `(1000, -700, 300)` (net box); torus minor radius `1.53673519e-7` at major radii `0.01` and `1` (four turns), and at major radius `0` `4.936e-7` at 359 degrees and `3.970e-7` at -30 (`|Su|`). The predicate matched all 27 within `2e-7` relative. A torus minor circle between about `6.3e-7` and the zone tolerance is mostly deleted with `MakeFaceWithCurves` failing (`SM_ERR`), so no read follows; this case is not modelled.
- **How found:** native probes of the `primitive.primitive_creation_p2` shim, one process per input, with a scratch `SmTopologySweep.cpp` that reports per swept face the refusal or whether `pOrigEdgeCurve` survived a successful `MakeFaceWithCurves`. 1,500 random cones and tori (origins up to `1e6`, turns from `1e-10` to 359 degrees and negative): the predicate predicted every refusal and every freed read.
- **Confirmed:** yes. The FFI guard evaluates the predicate with a margin of `1e-6` relative plus `1e-13` times the coordinates' size; on the 1,500 probes it rejects every freed read and accepts 195 cones and 250 tori the earlier guards rejected. `ub215_exact_guard_accepted.json` compares 30 of them against C++.
