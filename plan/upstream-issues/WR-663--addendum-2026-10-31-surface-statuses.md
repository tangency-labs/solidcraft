#### WR-663 (update, 2026-10-31). The remaining `SmSurface.cpp` statuses and predicates

The rest of the fork's `SmSurface.cpp` handling (tangency-labs/smlib 21dcf69, and the `SmSurface.cpp` part of 1612a7d) is now ported under the same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.query_statuses_pass_on`. With the flag off every site keeps the policy it had.

- **Passed on (`SER`):** the evaluations, boxes, solves, trims, interval edits, knot reads and `Unitize`s that `ApproximateSurface`, `CalculateTightBoundingBox`, `CoincidenceCheck` (its boxes, boundary trims, corner, seam and shape evaluations, and `smsurf_CheckShapeMatch`'s normal sections), `CoverCoincidentSurface` (its point evaluations, `Intersect`, `AdjustSTEPUVDomain` and the callers' `RemoveUVTrimCurves`), `ComputeParamDirection`, `ComputeInwardVector`, `CreateSilhouetteCurves` (a split that cannot be trimmed fails), `CreateIsoParametricCurve`, `CreateBlendSurface`, `CalculateContinuities`, `FindTessellationSplits`, `FindDegenParamForDirection`, `FindFilletMinMaxRadii`, `InvertPointNearSingularity`, `IntersectSurfaceBoundaries`, `LocalSurfaceSurfaceIntersect`, `LocalSurfaceIntersect`, `LocalPlaneSurfaceIntersect`, `LocalPointSolve_0`, `DropPoint`, `DropPointsToTangentPlane`, `EvaluateNormal`, `GlobalLineIntersect`, `GlobalCurveIntersect`, `SimpleCoincidenceChecker`, `ClassifyIntersectingRangeSolution`, `sm_GlobalCurveIntersectCombineSolutions`, `sm_ClassifyCurveSurfaceXSectPoint`, `sm_FilterSolution`, `sm_LoadGuesses`, `sm_CheckSeamDrop`, `smsurf_IsPointOnNormal`, `SmSurfaceEval::EvalNormal`/`EvalNormalSection`, `SmDerivSurfDefinition::GetSampleValues` (surface boundary curve) and the solvers' evaluators (`SmCSNormalizeENFO`, `SmSSNormalizeENFO`, `SmFindSingularParamWithGivenTangentDirEFO`: a `Unitize` there fails the evaluation) drop.
- **Soft answers:** `sm_AssertTestSurface4` fails when the continuities cannot be computed and `sm_AssertTestSurface6` when the boundaries cannot be built or compared; `IsDegeneratePoint`, `IsDegenerateCurve` (the `SmSurface` bodies), `IsFlatCorner`, `IsPeriodic`, `IsSingularity` and the two convergence tests are `FALSE` without their evaluations; `sm_GoodDropResult` does not show such a drop good; `GetSingularities` takes a boundary that cannot be evaluated as not singular; `ApproxDerivativeLengths` averages the samples that evaluate (`SM_UNDEF_DOUBLE` when none does); the four `IdenticalSolutions` of the surface solvers and the surface/surface one give `SM_SP_DIFFPARAMETERS` without the points; `SmLSIGlobalSolver::BranchMayContainAnswers` is `TRUE` without the box intersection; the CS, CSI, LSI, SP and SS `LocalSolve`s treat a branch outside the domain or interval of interest as no work; `sm_ExtractIsoCurve` gives no curve when the iso curve cannot be trimmed, reversed or reparameterized (1612a7d); `GetDomainWithContinuity` is an uninitialised domain without the knots (1612a7d).
- **Already as the fork on main:** the pole points and normals of `GetSingularities` and the corner points of `GetFlatCorners` stay undefined when they cannot be evaluated; the extra-guess classifications of the LSI and CSI `LocalSolve`s add a guess only when it succeeded; `SmDropCurveFail` copies cannot fail; `sm_GetOriginalSurface` (158cbe7) already returns an error for a node without a surface cache (`SM_ERR`, where the fork has `SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER`); the fork's `SER` sites that are already `?` in the port (for example `CreateCanonical`, `CalculateFilletRadius`'s derivatives, `smsurf_IsPointOnNormal` in `LocalPointSolve_0`, `FindUVCurveIvlInDomain`'s interval).
- **Cannot fail where they stand:** the `Unitize`s after a length test (`EvalNormal`, the `EvaluateNormal` cross product over the normal limit, `smsurf_DropVectors`' normal and its `SetSTEPCanonical` frame, `SmSSPIntersectENFO` and `SmSSINormalizeENFO`'s normals, `GetSampleValues`' surface normal) and `CalculateBoundingBox`'s `SetBasis` of the unit axes, so they are left as they are.
- **No port to change:** `ApproximateConstrainedSurface`, `ApproximateSurfaceLeastSqs`, `ComputeLocalCurveFrame`, `CreateArcOnSurf`, `CreateBoundaryCrvOnSurf`, `AdjustPeriodicityUVPoints`, `IsPlanarPoint`, `IsOnUVBoundary`, `SmSurface::HasPlaneCrossings`, `PointBasedSurfaceIntersect` (3D point version), `GetSampleValues` (from a base curve), `WriteToFile`, `WriteArrayToFile`, `ReadFromFile`, `ReadArrayFromFile`, `OutputGraphics` and the drawing functions.
- **Not in this file:** the B-spline overrides that the analytic surfaces' `IsDegenerateCurve`/`IsDegeneratePoint` dispatch to (`SmBSplineSurface.cpp`) are unchanged.
- **Regression tests:** `surface::surface::p1::tests::issue_2009_unevaluable_surface_is_not_shown_degenerate_or_a_flat_corner`, `issue_2009_unevaluable_surface_is_not_shown_periodic`, `issue_2009_unevaluable_param_direction_is_passed_on` (coverage only), `surface::surface::p3::tests::issue_2009_unevaluable_tight_box_corners_are_passed_on`, `surface::surface::p7::tests::issue_2009_unevaluable_samples_give_no_derivative_lengths`, `issue_2009_unevaluable_points_are_not_shown_converged`.
