#### WR-498. `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint`'s end fit (WR-452, WR-453) pulls an edge end off its own vertex to the mean of a proto vertex that resolves into several vertices
- **Severity:** Wrong result (edge ends up to 8.06e-5 off their vertices, loop "Edge/Edge Gap" over 2e-5; short intersection edges lost)
- **Area:** Booleans / proto topology
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp`, `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint`, the end-fit block the SolidCraft fork added (tangency-labs/smlib b43876f, a8caccd). Not in upstream SMLib: the defect is in those fork commits, which WR-452 and WR-453 port.
- **What happens:** the end fit moves (WR-452) or continues (WR-453) each end of an intersection curve towards its proto vertex's position. A proto vertex collects every vertex definition within the capture distance (0.01) and `Resolve` may make several vertices of it; its position is then their mean. A curve that already ended exactly on its own vertex is pulled to that mean, and the edges between the vertices of that proto vertex are lost.
- **Trigger:** the union of two crossing pleated loft skins (SolidCraft rig booleans/4623; also fillets/1488, a slab window joined with a nudged pleated loft, tangency-labs/solidcore#470). With both imprint flags on, solidcore's union has 992 edges and 608 vertices and an edge end 8.061e-5 off its vertex; the C++ fork's has 1096 edges and 712 vertices, every end within 1.206e-5 (all `UpstreamFixes` flags on).
- **How found:** SolidCraft kernel regression rig (tangency-labs/solidcore#1480); fixed in the SolidCraft fork, tangency-labs/smlib ffaf4e8 (0b1b945 on the 5afca53 line).
- **Confirmed:** yes against the C++ fork (#1480: the fork's union of the same operands, 1096 / 712 / 1.206e-5, is what the Rust port gives with the fix and every flag on); upstream SMLib has no end fit and is not affected.
- **Fix:** the target is the proto vertex's position only when every vertex definition in it was imprinted onto the same final vertex in each B-rep as the edge's end. Otherwise the target is the end's final vertex in B-rep 1 when its final vertices in the two B-reps lie within the edge definition's tolerance, and the end is left alone if not.
- **Rust port:** `crates/solidcore/src/topology/proto_topology/p2/imprint_ends.rs` (`fit_edge_def_ends_to_proto_vertices`, which now takes the end's final vertices from `imprint_edge_definition`). Folded into the existing opt-in flags `Kernel::fixes.imprint_trims_edge_to_proto_vertex` and `Kernel::fixes.imprint_extends_edge_to_proto_vertex`, since ffaf4e8 corrects exactly the fork commits they carry; off by default (upstream). Test: `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1480_pleated_loft_union_edge_ends.rs`.
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#1480.
