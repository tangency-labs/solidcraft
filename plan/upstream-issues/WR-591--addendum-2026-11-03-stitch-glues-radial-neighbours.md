Updates: WR-591

#### WR-591 (addendum 2026-11-03, second). The 9 orders without a solid region come from the stitch's edge pairing; the stitch now glues radial neighbours (#2506)

- **Cause traced.** Not the region building but the edge gluing before it. `create_linear_sweep` (one repetition, the analytic path) builds the eight side planes and the two caps as loose faces and calls `StitchAndOrient`. Four side faces end on the vertical line through the corner (0, 3), so the stitch finds four coincident lamina edges there. `SmStitch::DoStitching`'s edge gluing (`Stitch::glue_edges_at_vertex`, `crates/solidcore/src/heal/stitch/p2.rs`) glues the first pair that matches geometrically in the vertex's edge order and checks only the curves' direction. Seen from above the faces leave the line at 90° (upper wall), 14° (upper hole face), −14° (lower hole face) and −90° (lower wall). In rotations 4, 6 and 7 of every variant the first match is the upper wall with the lower hole face, which leaves the lower wall with the upper hole face: the two glued edges cross round the line, each face's outer side carries on into the next face's inner side, and all 20 face uses form one shell. `GlueEdgesGeneral`'s `TestClosureSplitRegion` then never finds a closed side, so the body keeps one void region. The 15 orders that work glue the two walls and the two hole faces.
- **Fork.** `SmStitch::DoStitching`'s edge gluing in the SMLib fork (v1.1.0-alpha, a18bbe5) is v1.0.1's apart from status passing and the nested-solid region setting, and its `CreateLinearSweep` stitches loose faces the same way, so there is nothing to port. Its C++ result for the 9 orders is not measured.
- **Fix (not upstream, the default, no flag):** where more than two lamina edges lie on one curve, the edge gluing skips a pair that has another of them between its faces on both sides round the curve, judged by each face's binormal (its direction into the face) at the compared point (`RadialGlue`). Pairs of faces next to each other round the curve never cross. The check applies only where each face there meets the curve once (Rossignac and Cardoze's Matchmaker, ACM Solid Modeling 1999, pairs a non-manifold edge's faces by their order round it); a face meeting it twice, a seam or a sliver within the tolerance, leaves the gluing upstream's (`plan/findings/heal.stitch_p2--radial-pairing-faces-meeting-the-curve-twice.md`). Where only two lamina edges meet, nothing changes.
- **Cases it moves:** the WR-591 capped sweep (`loops_go_on_round_the_vertex` on), rotations 4, 6 and 7 of each of the 3 variants (9 orders): before, ten faces, every edge manifold, one void region, volume 0; now the plate less the hole, volume 118, a manifold solid with two regions, `Level2` clean. The 15 other orders glue the same edges as before. `difftest --green` at the base `eff55aeed` and with the fix gives the same outcome in every case of all 280 units (591 949 cases).
- **Test:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr591_touching_loops_sweep_any_order.rs`: `wr591_on_sweep_is_the_plate_less_the_hole_in_every_order` (all 24 orders) replaces `wr591_on_no_solid_region_known_failing` and fails without the fix in the 9 orders; `crates/solidcore/src/heal/stitch/p2.rs` adds `stitch_glues_a_pair_past_curves_that_only_touch_its_mid_point` and `radial_glue_leaves_a_face_meeting_the_curve_twice_to_the_gluing`.
