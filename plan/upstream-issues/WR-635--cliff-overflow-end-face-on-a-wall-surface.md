#### WR-635. A cliff overflow ending on a corner's end face on a wall's surface is refused
- **Severity:** Wrong result (refusal)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet executive)
- **Location:** the fork's `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCliffOverflow.cpp`, `SmFilletExecutive::CloseRemovalRegion`, `sm_DistanceToSurface` and `sm_CoveringSurface` (tangency-labs/smlib@8ba4667).
- **What happens:** at each end of a sloped basin's front rim edge chamfered past the rim (the leg on the top running off the 1 mm rim over the slab's front face), the pocket's corner is an Nx1 corner of mixed convexity. It ends the chamfer on a face of the sheet on the end wall's surface extended back past the vertex over the rim. That face's lamina edge along the pocket's corner edge lies on the end wall's surface and the front wall's both, and `CloseRemovalRegion` took any second surface as ambiguous: `SM_FILERR_NO_ROOM`, "a cliff overflow's fillet boundary lies on the surfaces of two faces". The top's chain then ends at the rim's vertex itself, which only a side edge could close, and a planar B-spline wall (a loft's side) was taken to stop at its domain.
- **Trigger:** solidcraft#1080's sloped basin: a 100 x 60 x 30 slab less a degree-1 loft (planar B-spline walls), its front rim edge chamfered 2, 3 or 4.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#1080, #960). Fixed in the fork by tangency-labs/smlib@eddf0ed (branch `replay/1.0.1`): a lamina edge's host is never the surface its own sheet face lies on (`sm_LiesOnSurface`); a chain ending at a filleted edges' vertex of its host closes there; a planar surface of any kind continues as its plane (`sm_PlaneOf`, for the distance and the covering surface).
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run of the fork was made; not reachable in the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`), which has no cliff overflow. The port refused the same case with the same comment before the change.
- **Rust port:** follows eddf0ed under `Kernel::fixes.fillet_cliff_overflow` (the refinement of that construction, as the fork has it with no switch of its own): `plane_of`, `lies_on_surface`, the host test in `lamina_hosts` and the vertex end in `side_end`, `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_cliff_overflow.rs`.

  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr635_636_cliff_overflow_closing.rs` (`a_sloped_basin_rim_chamfer_past_the_slab_front_is_subtracted`: upright and leaning end walls, rims 1 and 0.5, chamfers 2, 3 and 4, against the basin less the chamfer's section clipped to the slab, integrated exactly, to 1e-8; refused without the flag).
