#### WR-680. `SmSurface::DropCurve` fits the UV trim of a plane section of a cylinder, 2e-6 to 3e-5 off its exact edge
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Intersection
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmSurfaceDropLib.cpp`, `SmSurface::DropCurve` (the base body `SmCylinder` inherits; its start point drop at `:4310`-`4316`, then the span trace): every curve on a surface other than a plane is traced span by span and fitted with a piecewise cubic Hermite within the drop tolerance. A boolean's plane/cylinder edge reaches it through `SmEdgeuse::CreateUVTrimCurve` (`SmCone::IntersectWithPlane` gives the exact ellipse with no UV curves), a fillet's rail through the fillet geometry's drops.
- **What happens:** the trim of an edge that is an exact plane section of a cylinder face (an ellipse, as the boolean and the exact rounds of WR-669 make them) is a fit: lifted onto the cylinder it is 2e-6 to 3.4e-5 off its own edge, above `approx_tol_3d` (5e-6) and up to 3x the zone tolerance (1e-5), while the plane face's trims of the same edges are exact (2e-14). `face_area` and `brep_volume` integrate over the trims, so the cylinder face's area is off by up to 2e-5 mm² (~1e-7 relative) and the volume by up to 8e-10 relative. Such a section has an exact UV curve: on a rational quadratic segment of the cylinder's circle, `s / (1 - s)` of a point is a ratio of affine functions of it (its barycentric coordinates in the segment's control triangle), so a rational curve on the cylinder has a rational UV curve in its own parameter.
- **Trigger:** issue #2109: a 40 x 40 x 20 box (top face z = 0) united with an r3 cylinder through the origin tilted 15, 30 or 45 degrees about x. The cylinder's trims of the rim are 9.7e-6, 8.5e-6 and 2.3e-6 off it (nearest point); the peg's side area at 15 degrees is 4.8e-6 below `2 pi R 15`. With the rim rounded r1 under `fillet_exact_round_classes`, the rails' trims on the peg are 4.4e-6 to 3.4e-5 off and the side area is up to 2.1e-5 off `2 pi R (15 - 1 / cos tau)`.
- **How found:** the absolute `VOLUME_TOL = 2e-4` that PR #2102 needed in `crates/solidcore/tests/wr669_exact_conic_rounds.rs`, traced in issue #2109.
- **Confirmed:** yes. The issue's four tilts as a scratch `boolean.api_brep` measured fixture gave `PASS cases=4 passed=4 max_rel_err=0e0` against the C++ reference (the volume and all nine face areas bit-identical), so the C++ builds the same trims.
- **Rust port (opt-in):** `Kernel::fixes.cylinder_plane_section_trims_exact` (`UpstreamFixes`, default `false`; tangency-labs/solidcore#2109). `Surface::drop_curve_base` (`crates/solidcore/src/intersect/surface_drop_lib/p3.rs`) first tries `exact_uv_curve` (`crates/solidcore/src/intersect/surface_drop_lib/cylinder_section.rs`): for a curve of a B-spline kind on a cylinder, its UV curve over the common denominator `d(t) W(t)`, a rational Bezier curve of twice the curve's degree on each piece between the curve's knots and its crossings of the circle's segment joints. It is kept only when it lifts onto the curve within `1e-10` of the model's size at sampled parameters, stays in the domain, does not cross the seam and is not an iso-parameter line (which `DropIsoCurve` already gives exactly); anything else is traced as upstream. With the field on, the trims above are rational quartics (rational of degree 8 on the round's quartic rails) 4e-15 to 1.1e-14 off their edges, the peg's side area is within the integration's accuracy (1e-12 relative) of the closed form and the volume within 1.4e-15 relative. Test: `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2109_cylinder_plane_section_trims.rs`.
