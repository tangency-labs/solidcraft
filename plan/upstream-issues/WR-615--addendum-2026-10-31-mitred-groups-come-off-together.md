Updates: WR-615

#### WR-615 addendum (2026-10-31): mitred groups on a moved face come off together
- **What changed:** the mitred-group removal in `OffsetFaces` step 3 is now ported (fork tangency-labs/smlib a96ce9f, `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetFaces.cpp`, the group block before the "could not be taken off" refusals; unchanged up to 2fc375f). Before, such a group was refused as `OffsetFacesRefusal::Unsupported` ("... SmFeatureExecutive (SM_RB_EXTEND_SURFACE), which is not ported"). The block takes each connected group of blends left after the one-by-one removals, provided every face round the group is a plane. It marks the edges there before (not the group's own) `s_lBefore`, then calls `SmFeatureExecutive::AddFeature(faces, SM_RB_EXTEND_SURFACE, SM_RB_FROM_OTHER_BREP)` and `DoDefeature` (`blend::feature_executive`, #1851). It refuses unless `GetBaseWasApplied`, then runs `RemoveTopologicalEdgesAndVertices` keeping the marked edges. It refuses unless the result passes `AssertValid` (level 2) and is a manifold solid. Finally it marks each blend's sharp edge where its two support faces meet, refusing if one is missing. The fork's three refusals in the block are ported with its reasons.
- **Deviation:** the fork's "taken in afresh" copy (`SmBrep` copy constructor, `RemoveAllTopology`, `MergeBrep`) is not made. The arena B-rep's cached topology fields are refreshed instead and its ids are kept (module header of `crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_faces.rs`).
- **Trigger:** #1060, SolidCraft rig 4492. A 40 x 30 x 10 box has its four top edges rounded r 3, and the top is offset +19. The fork (2fc375f) gives 10 faces, 20 edges and 12 vertices, with V 34539.95627294846 and A 6274.637126053606. The port now gives the same topology and agrees to 1e-9 relative, with flags off and with SolidCraft's preset.
- **Rust port:** `OffsetFacesExecutive::take_off`. This is still a fork-only API, not a `Kernel::fixes` field. Test: `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1060_offset_mitred_rounded_box_top.rs`.
