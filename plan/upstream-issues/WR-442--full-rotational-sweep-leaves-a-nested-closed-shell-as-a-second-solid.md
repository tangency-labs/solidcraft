#### WR-442. A full rotational sweep leaves a closed shell nested in another as a second solid lump, not a void
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Topology
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp`, `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateRotationalSweep(r3DCurves, crRotAxisBasePt, crRotAxisDir)` (def. `:2565`; the 360-degree dispatch from the angle overload is `:2870`), reached from `SmApiCreateRotationalSweep` (`source/SM_API/src/SmApiBrep.cpp:1254`). Each curve becomes its own face (`CreateFaceFromSurface`, `:2728`) and the faces are joined only by `SmBrep::StitchAndOrient()` (`:2756`; `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp:26661`), whose `OrientTrimmedSurfaces` (`SmBrep.cpp:24120`) leaves a face that already bounds two regions as it is, its infinite-region face use outward.
- **What happens:** when the profile is a closed loop whose two edges lie on the axis (a half ring, revolving into a hollow sphere), those edges sweep to nothing and the two arcs become two separate closed sphere faces. Each closed face bounds the infinite region on one side and a region of its own on the other, and nothing tests whether one closed shell lies inside the other. The result has three regions: the infinite region holding both spheres' outer face uses, and two solid regions, the ball r 40 and the ball r 18.5 nested in it. It is two overlapping solid balls, not a solid shell with a void: `SmApiBrepComputeVolume` gives `4/3*pi*(40^3 + 18.5^3)` = 294604.42 instead of `4/3*pi*(40^3 - 18.5^3)` = 241560.73. The function's notes say the region must be the infinite region ("limitation of SmStitch"), but the call succeeds and returns this body.
- **Consequence downstream:** a Boolean takes the inner ball as a body of its own. Union with the box 8..20 x -4..4 x -4..4, which crosses the inner sphere (`SmApiBooleanUnion(box, shell)`), gives 268082.57 (the solid ball r 40: box, inner ball and outer ball unioned). Running `SmBrep::SetRegionIsVoidFlagsForNestedSolids()` on that result (the WR-265 fix's step) marks the region inner-ball-plus-box a void and gives 241446.07, which is 768 (the whole box, 12 x 8 x 8) short of the right 242214.07: the void becomes the inner ball united with the box instead of the inner ball less the box. The same union with the shell built as `SmApiBooleanDifference(sphere 40, sphere 18.5)` (infinite, solid and void regions, the void's shell inside the solid region) gives 242214.069, right without any fix.
- **Trigger:** `SmApiCreateRotationalSweep` of the XY profile line (18.5,0)-(40,0), `SmApiCreateArc((0,0,0), 40, 0, 180)`, line (-40,0)-(-18.5,0), `SmApiCreateArc((0,0,0), 18.5, 0, 180)` about the X axis through the origin, 360 degrees, capped. SolidCraft case `451-hollow-shell-cut/block_crossing_inner_sphere_join` (`fixtures/solidcraft/451-hollow-shell-cut.json`).
- **How found:** SolidCraft case 451 (solidcore's SolidCraft runner), then an `SM_API` program against the pinned reference build, logging each region's void flag and shells.
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the pinned `c9c5979` reference build (x86-64 Linux, g++, `-ffp-contract=off`): the revolve's three regions as above, and the union volumes 268082.5726750750 (as built) and 241446.0667553804 (after `SetRegionIsVoidFlagsForNestedSolids`). The Rust port agrees to every printed digit in both modes (268082.572675075 by default, 241446.06675538042 with `Kernel::fixes.boolean_outer_shell_first`).
- **Rust port:** `PrimitiveCreation::create_rotational_sweep` (`crates/solidcore/src/primitive/primitive_creation/p3/sweeps.rs`) follows upstream. A fix would have to find the closed shells left in the infinite region, test which lies inside which, move each nested shell's outer face uses into the enclosing region and make its own region a void (flipping the faces where the orientation then disagrees). Nothing upstream does this for a sweep result. `SetRegionIsVoidFlagsForNestedSolids` only resets void flags and shell order and moves no shell, and `SmShell::IsInnerShellOf` is unported.
