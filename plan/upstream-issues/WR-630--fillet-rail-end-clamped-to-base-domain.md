#### WR-630. `CalcRailIntEdgeuse` evaluates a rail's end on its base surface clamped to the surface's domain, so a rail end just past a side edge's end lands on the edge's vertex
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blend (fillet solver)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`, `SmFilletVertex::CalcRailIntEdgeuse` (def. `:1739`): `SER( pSurf1->EvaluatePoint( sUV, sPnt ) )` (`:2039`) evaluates the solve's UV point on the base surface, which a B-spline surface clamps to its natural domain.
- **What happens:** `RailEdgeuseIntersect` solves over the base surface's extended domain, so the rail's end can lie past the face. Clamped, the evaluated point moves back onto the domain's border: for a rail meeting a side edge's line just past the edge's end, onto the edge's end vertex, off the rail by about the square of that distance over twice the radius, under ten times the tolerance. `SmFilletNx1Corner::CalcCornerEdgeGeom` then takes the rail as meeting the side edge and builds an end rib that is not on the fillet.
- **Trigger:** a cliff overflow (#960): a 40 x 20 x 10 box's edge rounded r10.02 to r10.07 on its 10 high face (SolidCraft #1467). It needs the side edge run on along its line (WR-610) for the solve to find the rail's end past the edge.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#960, #1467). The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@3f22e97 (rev `68fb9162`), which is not merged upstream. The same commit makes `CalcCornerEdgeGeom` take a rail end off its side edge (by more than the tolerance) as missing it, inside the cliff overflow's branch of that function.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run of the fork was made. The Rust port follows the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`) with the flag off.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_rail_end_on_continued_surface`, the rail end is evaluated with out-of-bounds evaluation on, in place, as the fork's `SmTemporaryChangeValue` on `GetOutOfBoundsEnabled()` does (`blend/fillet_geom/p2.rs`, `calc_rail_int_edgeuse_with_solvers`). The `CalcCornerEdgeGeom` half of the commit sits inside the cliff overflow's branch, which is not ported yet (#960): it is not part of this flag.

  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr630_rail_end_on_continued_surface.rs` (the 40 x 20 x 10 box's edge r10.05 with `fillet_trace_reaches_rails`: the rail ends on the front face sit at z = 0 without the flag, at z = -0.05 with it).
