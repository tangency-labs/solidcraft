#### WR-494. `SmFace::ComputePreciseProperties` steps across the surface's knots, so curved faces miss the requested accuracy (1.0.1)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Mass properties
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFace.cpp:6233` at `3fd5b95` (`SER(pBSC->GetKnots(sKnots, NULL, &sIvl));`, the outer integration's pieces), `SmFace::ComputePreciseProperties` (def. `:6144`); `:6078` (`SER(sIntegrator.RungeKuttaODEIntegrate(dY,m_dMinV, sPV[0].y, ...))`, the inner iso-u integral), `SmODEAreaIFEO::Evaluate` (def. `:6042`).
- **What happens:** the face's properties are a double integral: along each UV trim curve (the outer adaptive Runge-Kutta run) of the integral along the iso-u line from the face's lowest v (the inner run, `SmODEAreaIFEO`). 1.0.1 splits the outer run at the trim curve's own knots only. The integrand's derivatives also jump where the trim curve crosses a knot line `u = k` of a B-spline surface, and the inner integrand's where the iso-u line crosses a v knot. A NURB cylinder, cone, sphere or torus has a rational circle's double knots in both directions. A Runge-Kutta step across one is outside the method's error model: its error estimate does not bound its error, so the accuracy asked for does not hold.
- **Trigger:** `SmApiFaceComputeArea` of the cylindrical face of `SmApiCreateCylinder` (origin (1, 2, -3), radius 5, height 12) at accuracy 1e-8: 376.99109865933315 against 2πrh = 376.99111843077515 (5.2e-8 relative). `SmApiBrepComputeArea` at 1e-8: cylinder r5 h12 3.7e-8, sphere r7 1.2e-8, cone r6 h9 4.2e-8, frustum r6..2 h9 3.5e-8, torus R10 r3 6.0e-9 off their closed forms. `SmApiBrepComputeMassProperties` of a torus R10 r3 at (1, -1, 0.5): Ixx about the centre 98819.4138975709 against V (R²/2 + 5r²/8) = 98819.4140659072 (1.7e-9).
- **How found:** wiring solidcore into SolidCraft as a second kernel (tangency-labs/solidcore#1435): the SolidCraft fork of SMLib gives 1.5e-10 (cylinder area) and 1.8e-11 (torus inertia) for the same call sequence.
- **Confirmed:** the Rust port's default (difftest-gated parity with the C++ reference) reproduces the numbers above exactly (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1435_precise_properties_split_at_knots.rs` with the flag off); the SolidCraft fork measured the same defect in its C++ build ("at 1e-8 a cylinder's area 5.6e-8", tangency-labs/smlib@08d9a14) and fixed it there.
- **Fix (opt-in, not the default):** `Kernel::fixes.precise_properties_split_at_surface_knots` ports the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@08d9a14. The outer run integrates each trim curve piece by piece between its knots and its crossings of the surface's u knot lines (`sm_TrimCurveBreaks` -> `topology::face::p2::trim_curve_breaks`: a sign change of `u(t) - k` between `4 (degree + 1)` points of each knot span, then bisection; breaks the integrator cannot step between merged), skipping an empty piece. Each inner iso-u integral is split at the surface's v knots inside `(min v, v(t))` (`sm_SurfaceKnotsInside` -> `topology::face::p2::surface_knots_inside`), each piece from a half-length first step, the pieces summed. A surface not of the B-spline kind has no knots. With it on, the cases above hold 1e-9 of their closed forms at accuracy 1e-8 (the test above with the flag on).
