#### WR-644. A bevel at a face's concave corner whose rails meet the corner edge apart is cut by a surface that ends there
- **Severity:** Wrong result (failure)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletNx2Corner::AdjustTopology` (the bevel split of the side vertex), `SmFilletNx2Corner::CreateCornerPatch`, `SmFilletCorner::CalcCornerGeom`.
- **What happens:** two blends bevelled (mitred) at a vertex of three edges where the common face's corner between them is concave and whose walls lean differently (a sloped basin's rim) meet the side edge at two places. `AdjustTopology` splits the side vertex as for a convex bevel, where the cut lies on the wall itself: it cuts the larger blend by the side wall's own surface, which ends at the side edge, and `SmFilletEdge::CalcFilletIntSideFace` fails; with that surface extended, the smaller rail runs past its face and `TestRollOver` fails on it. The fillet fails with status 1001 (fillet error 3).
- **Trigger:** a 100 x 60 x 30 slab less a basin lofted from 20..80 x 1..50 at the top to 30..70 x 20..40 at z = 10, its front and right rim edges (or all four) rounded r 0.5, with the corners mitred (WR-642, or a corner set to bevel).
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#960; its case `1080-fillet-sloped-basin-1-mm-rim-r0-5-4-edges` and the 5835 chamfers). The fork's tangency-labs/smlib@2223108 ("a mitre whose rails meet the corner edge apart fills the gap past it") builds the topology `AdjustConcaveCornerTopology` builds at a swept corner of mixed convexity (`m_bBevelGapPatch`): each rail ends where it meets the side edge, the larger blend's cut and the smaller rail's extension run on the side wall's extended surface to where the mitre's curve meets them, and `CreateCornerPatch` fills only that gap with a patch on the extended surface.
- **Confirmed:** the port reproduces the 1001 failure with the mitre on and this off; no C++ run of the fork was made. At the pin (`3fd5b95`) the corner is swept unless the caller sets it to bevel.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_mitre_gap_patch` (`blend/fillet_corner/p3.rs`: `adjust_topology_nx2_with`, `calc_corner_geom_with`, `create_corner_patch_nx2` / `_with`; `FilletCorner::bevel_gap_patch`). Its own flag rather than WR-642's: it also changes the pinned path for a corner the caller sets to bevel (`SetBevelCorner`), without WR-642.
