Updates: UB-242

#### UB-242 (update, 2026-10-31). A failing GlueCoincidentBrepVertices no longer stops the port
- **Location:** as UB-242; `SmTopologyIntersector::ImprintIntersections` (`source/SMLib/src/SmTopologyIntersector.cpp:2387`) records the status of `SmProtoTopologyManager::GlueCoincidentBrepVertices` (`source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp:5902`), which runs after every vertex- and edge-definition imprint. `IntersectInsertRelate` then goes on to `SetRelationships` (`:1264`).
- **What happens:** When every imprint worked and only the glue fails, upstream goes on with the glued B-reps. The port treated the failing glue as a half-edited B-rep (`ImprintOutcome::failure_edited`) and stopped there as for UB-242. The `IntersectInsertRelate` then failed where the C++ succeeds.
- **Trigger:** a census over the full difftest gate (280 units) with the glue status logged: no case in a green unit has a failing glue. Three `blend.feature_executive` cases have one: `single_face_fillet1019_2`, `fillet_each_torii_fillet_b`, and a probe-only step case on source face 3 of `single_face_fillet1019_2`. All three fail in a `DeleteEdge` of a non-manifold lamina edge (1001) after every imprint worked.
- **How found:** temporary probes on both sides (not committed) checked each body (`SmBrep::AssertValid`, failure kinds and counts) before and after every glue, paired by face, edge and vertex counts. Only the C++'s first check of each state is compared, because that first check caches gaps and tolerances on the live body. In `single_face_fillet1019_2` and its step case, each snapshot matches the C++, including both sides of the failing glue. In `fillet_each_torii_fillet_b` the bodies already differ before the failing glue (primary body faces/edges/vertices 43/100/58 in the port, 43/101/59 in C++). That difference comes from the merge's earlier imprints, not from the glue, and is a separate open item.
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the C++ build: the glue returns the same failure on both sides, and C++ `IntersectInsertRelate` goes on.
- **Rust port:** `TopologyIntersector::imprint_intersections_reporting_edits` still records the glue's status as `eRetStat`, but a failing glue no longer sets `ImprintOutcome::failure_edited`. `intersect_insert_relate` then goes on as upstream. Failed vertex-definition imprints, and edge-definition imprints that fail at or after their first edit, still stop as before (`boolean.topology_intersector_p2` cases `gen0111` and `gen0498` stop on both sides). The `boolean.topology_intersector_p2` shim's copy of `ImprintIntersections` makes the same change. Regression test: `crates/solidcore/tests/defeature_glue_failure_goes_on.rs`. The SolidCraft fork's af8db37 still stops on any failing `ImprintIntersections`, glue included; the port's default follows the pinned v1.0.1 here.
