#### WR-452. `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint` makes an edge over its curve's whole interval, past a proto vertex that lies inside the curve
- **Severity:** Wrong result (SmFace test #34, "Face has partially Coincident Edges"; a vertex gap only a larger tolerance covers)
- **Area:** Booleans / proto topology
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp`, `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint`, which passes `m_pXSectInterval` and the curves to `MakeEdgeInFace` unchanged.
- **What happens:** where a thread groove's flank meets a shaft's end face, the flank/plane intersection curve crosses the shaft's rim at a grazing angle (3.3 degrees for M33x3.5), so where it crosses is only known along the rim to the tolerance over that angle's sine. The proto vertex there comes from another intersection (the flank/cylinder helix meeting the rim) and lies on the flank/plane curve about 2e-5 inside its end. The edge runs past its vertex and lies along the rim, within tolerance, for that distance: two edges of one face coincident over a range.
- **Trigger:** a cylinder minus a screw-swept thread groove, the groove running a pitch past each end (SolidCraft rig cases 1250, 2193 and 2209, tangency-labs/solidcore#435; also #410).
- **How found:** reported by the SolidCraft kernel regression rig (tangency-labs/solidcore#435, #410); fixed in the SolidCraft fork, tangency-labs/smlib b43876f.
- **Confirmed:** no (read at c9c5979 `source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp`; the Rust port shows the gap: an M44x1 groove on 4 mm leaves an edge end 9.8e-6 from its vertex and an M33x3.5 one on 14 mm 6.8e-6, over half the vertex tolerance of 1e-5; the C++ build was not run)
- **Fix:** before imprinting, each end of the definition's interval moves to the curve's point nearest its proto vertex when that point lies inside the interval, within the definition's tolerance and nearer than the end; the UV curves are trimmed with it, on copies. The 3d curve keeps its extent.
- **Rust port:** `crates/solidcore/src/topology/proto_topology/p2/imprint_ends.rs` (`trim_end_to_proto_vertex`, called from `imprint_edge_definition`). Opt-in `Kernel::fixes.imprint_trims_edge_to_proto_vertex`; off by default, which keeps upstream's imprint, so the proto-topology difftest keeps matching the C++. Tests: `issue_435_m44x1_edges_end_at_their_vertices` and `issue_435_m33x3_5_edges_end_at_their_vertices` in `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_410_435_thread_groove.rs` (SmFace test #34 is not ported; they measure the edge-end/vertex gaps it comes from).
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#435.
