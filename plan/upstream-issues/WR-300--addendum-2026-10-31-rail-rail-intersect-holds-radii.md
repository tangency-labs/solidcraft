Updates: WR-300

#### WR-300 (update, 2026-10-31). `RailRailIntersect` holds the variable radii at every corner too (the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@465b62b)

- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp`, `SmFilletSolver::RailRailIntersect`. Its two `SmTemporaryRadiusChange`s hold both rounds' radius laws at their end values wherever the function is called: from `SmFilletVertex::CalcRailIntRail` and from `sm_CreateCubicRailRailInterpolant` (`SmFilletEdge::CalcRailRailInterpolation`, `SmFilletGeom.cpp`).
- **What happens:** the hold is only right at a corner calculated as constant radius (`SmFilletCorner::IsCalcAsConstRad`). There `CalcCornerVertGeom` then maps each law so that it ends at the rail end found. At any other corner the rails' meeting point is found at the laws' end radii, short of the edges' ends, where the laws are smaller. Two rounds 0.5 -> 2 on a 10 box's top edges, meeting at (10, 10, 10) with the vertical edge there not rounded, had their rail end at (10, 8, 8) with radius 2, where the law gives 1.7. The trace from that end, with its spans held to the law (`fillet_variable_span_follows_fillet`, WR-646, fork 4438859), misses it by about half a step at each step and fails with 1001. This is the same rule as the record's retry (fork 1d06159), which already holds the radius only where the first search does.
- **Fix:** the record's flag `Kernel::fixes.fillet_retry_radius_as_first` now also covers this. `FilletSolver::rail_rail_intersect` takes the fork's `bHoldEndRadii`. Its callers (`calc_rail_int_rail`, and `calc_rail_rail_interpolation` through `create_cubic_rail_rail_interpolant`) pass `!fillet_retry_radius_as_first || corner.is_calc_as_const_rad()`, so with the flag off the radii are held as upstream holds them.
- **Test:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr300_variable_rounds_meet_at_law_radii.rs`. With the flag, the two rounds remove more than one round alone, `(1 - pi/4) 17.5 = 3.7555`, and less than 6.8226, which is the fork's check. The four top edges of `TestSmVariableRadiusFillet` remove between two and four rounds' worth. Without the flag both fail with 1001, and on main before this change they failed with the flag on too.
