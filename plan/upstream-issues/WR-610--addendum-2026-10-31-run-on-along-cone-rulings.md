Updates: WR-610

The SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@779cb9b extends `RailEdgeuseIntersect`'s run-on of a straight side edge along its line from planes to cylinders and cones: a side edge whose UV curve is a line there is a ruling, along which the surface continues as itself. A disc's top rim rounded past its 2 mm wall (solidcraft#190, R13 x 2 rounded R2.5) failed at its closed corner (error 3, status 1001): the wall's rail runs round the cylinder 0.5 below the bottom, and the corner's vertex is where it meets the seam's line there. The Rust port follows it under the same flag, `Kernel::fixes.fillet_trace_reaches_rails` (`run_side_edge_on_along_its_line` in `blend/fillet_solver/p2.rs`). The commit's second half, evaluating the UV curve out of bounds along its line while it runs on, is already `fillet_run_on_bspline_trims` (WR-654; a line trim never clamps). Regression test: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr610_779cb9b_rail_meets_seam_line_past_its_end.rs`.
