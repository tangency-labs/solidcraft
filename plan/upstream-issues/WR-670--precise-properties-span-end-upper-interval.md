#### WR-670. With WR-494's span-wise iso-u integrals, `SmODEAreaIFEO` evaluates each piece's end in the knot span above it, so faces spanning many non-C2 knots take about 5x the work (fork 08d9a14)
- **Severity:** Performance (results agree within the requested accuracy)
- **Area:** Mass properties
- **Location:** the SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@08d9a14, `source/SMLib/src/SmFace.cpp`, `SmODEAreaIFEO::Evaluate` (the loop over `sBreaks` calling `RungeKuttaODEIntegrate(dPieceY, dA, dB, ...)`) and `SmODEIsoCurveLengthIFEO::Evaluate` (`m_crSurface.EvaluateSimple(sUVPnt,1,1,TRUE,TRUE,TRUE,sPV[0])`). Rust: `topology::face::p2::OdeArea::evaluate` under `UpstreamFixes::precise_properties_split_at_surface_knots` (WR-494).
- **What happens:** WR-494 splits each inner iso-u integral at the surface's v knots so no Runge-Kutta step crosses a derivative jump. Each piece `[dA, dB]` is then integrated with the iso-curve integrand, which evaluates the surface with `bVFromLeft = TRUE`: at a knot that is the upper interval (`SmSurface::EvaluateSimple`: "TRUE=Use upper interval"). The piece's last point, `dB`, is a knot, so it is evaluated in the next span. Where the surface's v derivatives jump at the knot, the integrand jumps at the piece's last point, the step's error estimate sees the jump, and the steps shrink toward every knot. Results stay within accuracy; the cost grows.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcore#2091: 36 blades lofted through G2-not-C2 sections (19 cubic Bezier segments a section, each on its own unit knot span) unioned with a revolved disk, with `geometric_g2_continuity` (WR-380) so each blade side is one face across the 19 spans, and SolidCraft's fixes. `brep_volume` at 1e-6: 26.7 s with 3.9M inner evaluations a blade face, against 0.48 s for the same model with its faces split at the knots (the flag off). One unit span `[18, 19]` of an iso-u line took about 160 evaluations; the point at v = 19 gave area density 488.8 where the span's limit is 443.9.
- **How found:** profiling #2091 (counters around `OdeArea::evaluate` and `OdeIsoCurveLength::evaluate`; dumping one slow piece's evaluations).
- **Confirmed:** the Rust port of 08d9a14 (difftest has no fork build); the fork's source at its current head still evaluates with `TRUE` there.
- **Fix (opt-in, not the default):** `Kernel::fixes.precise_properties_span_end_from_below` evaluates each piece's end `dB` from the span below it (`bVFromLeft = FALSE`), every other point as before (also for thick faces' offset surfaces). On the #2091 model the volume takes 5.8 s (4.6x faster), 3538233.161144 against 3538233.178915 without it (the split model: 3538233.208098); the split model is unchanged. The rest of the gap is inherent: each iso-u line runs from the face's lowest v across every span below the trim point (about 10 spans a line on the merged face, against one on a split face), with per-piece cost now equal to a split face's. Regression test: `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2091_precise_volume_spans.rs`. No effect without `precise_properties_split_at_surface_knots`.
