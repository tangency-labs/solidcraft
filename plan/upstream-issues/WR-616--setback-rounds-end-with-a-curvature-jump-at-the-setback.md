#### WR-616. A set-back corner's rounds end at their circular section, so a curvature-continuous corner fill cannot meet them or the faces at the hole's corners
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a shape limitation, no failure)
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletExecutive.cpp`, `SmFilletExecutive::CreateFilletBrep` (each round keeps its own circular cross-section up to the setback); `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`, `SmFilletEdge::CalcRailRailInterpolation` (the face curve is the conic of `sm_InterpolateEndptsAndTangents`; the G2/G3 fairing branch after `else if (TRUE/*eFSGType == SM_FSG_CIRCULAR*/)` is unreachable).
- **What happens:** at a set-back corner (`SmFilletCorner::m_dSetBackDist`) each round ends at its circular section, curvature 1/r across, where the planar face it meets has curvature 0; and the curve on the face that joins the two contact lines leaves them with curvature about 0.4 per mm. At each corner of the hole these two mismatches meet, so no curvature-continuous fill can agree with both the round and the face there. SMLib has no setting that ends the rounds curvature-continuously.
- **Trigger:** a 40 mm box, the three edges at one corner rounded r = 8 and set back 12 (SolidCraft ladder 07/015, `corner: g2`): the worst curvature jump on the hole's face curves is 0.16-0.27 at the hole's corners, under 3e-4 elsewhere; a G2 fill shows a V-shaped pinch in reflection stripes where a round's section meets a plane face.
- **How found:** SolidCraft's G2 corner fill, tangency-labs/solidcore#1376.
- **Confirmed:** the Rust port matches the C++ by default (no difftest change); the fork (tangency-labs/smlib `e74bbd3`, capi `d262e08`, tests `56f6e81`) adds `SmFilletCorner::SetEaseDist` and `SmFilletExecutive::EaseSetBackSections`, `0.0` (none) by default.
- **Fix:** none in the C++ at the pin.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. `FilletSession::set_corner_ease(corner, ease)` (per corner, with a setback; `FilletCorner::ease_dist`) eases each round, over `ease` before the setback, from its own surface into the `G2` blend-curve surface at thirds on the same rails by the law `w = 10 s^3 - 15 s^4 + 6 s^5`, exactly in homogeneous Bernstein form (`blend/fillet_executive/session/ease.rs`), and at that corner `calc_rail_rail_interpolation` takes the faired G2 interpolant of the rails' ends. Regression test: `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1376_setback_ease.rs` (ease 4 on the fork's box: cross curvature 1/8 up to the ease, about 1e-29 at the setback, rising between; worst curvature at the hole's corners 9.9e-9, the fork reports 8.7e-9; ease 0 and default: the pinned body).
