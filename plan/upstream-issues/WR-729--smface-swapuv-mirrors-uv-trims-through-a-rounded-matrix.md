#### WR-729. `SmFace::SwapUV` mirrors the UV trims through a rounded matrix, so a swapped face's pcurves end an ulp past its domain
- **Severity:** Wrong result (accuracy: pcurve ends outside the face's parameter domain)
- **Area:** Topology (face reversal)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFace.cpp:13983`, `SmFace::SwapUV` (the mirror at `:13990-13993`, used at `:14032`). Rust: `crates/solidcore/src/topology/face/p1.rs`, `swap_uv_notifying` (the mirror placement and the `create_mirror_curve` arms).
- **What happens:** `SwapUV` "transposes" each UV trim by mirroring it about the plane through the origin whose x axis is `(1, 1, 0)` unitised (`CreateMirrorCurve`, `N_CrvTransform`). The unit vector is rounded (`0.7071067811865476`), so the mirror matrix `I - 2nn^T` has `-2.2e-16` on its diagonal and `1 + 2.2e-16` off it, where the transpose has 0 and 1. A rational trim is also divided by its weights and re-weighted. Every coordinate of a swapped face's trims moves by up to an ulp, and a trim end on a domain bound lands past it: `0` becomes `-2.2e-16`, `1` becomes `1.0000000000000002`. `SmBrep::StitchAndOrient` swaps every face it turns over, so this reaches every primitive, boolean, fillet and sweep result that orients faces. A tessellator that expects the ends in the domain (closed at its bounds) to rounding sees them outside it (tangency-labs/solidcore#2479, found in a SolidCraft review).
- **Trigger:** `SmApiCreateBox` of a unit box at the origin. The three faces `StitchAndOrient` swaps (faces 0, 2 and 5) each have two trim ends at `v = -2.220446049250313e-16` on the domain `[0, 1]`; before the stitch every trim is in its domain. A 10 x 20 x 30 box at (-5, 3, 7) also has six such ends.
- **How found:** a test-only scan (`crates/solidcore/tests/support/pcurve_domain_scan.rs`) of every face's loops, each trim's end points against its face surface's natural UV domain (`GetNaturalUVDomain`). It was first run, through temporary instrumentation of `Brep::refresh_cached_topology` (which every public operation calls before it returns), over every body the whole solidcore test suite builds: booleans with cylinders, spheres, cones and tori through seams, fillets and chamfers on planar and curved faces, linear, rotational, curve and screw sweeps, offsets, shells, local operations, splits and the SolidCraft files. It found 32,113 out-of-domain ends. With the exact transpose forced on everywhere 2,554 remain; they come from other producers, not fixed here:
  - **SmLine's end overshoot, 1 to 4 ulps.** Most of the rest, mainly on plane faces of boolean, offset, sweep and fillet results. `SmLine` evaluates `point + t * scale * vector` with a unitised `vector` (Rust `crates/solidcore/src/curve/line.rs:793`), so a bounded line misses its end point by ulps. In the box intersection of `issue_1462_boolean_attributes`, an imprinted edge line ends at z = 10.000000000000005 where its vertex is at 10. `SmBrep::MakeEdgeInFace` drops that line onto the face (`drop_edge_curve_in_face`, `crates/solidcore/src/topology/brep/p9.rs`), and the trim ends at u = 10.000000000000005 on `[0, 10]`. Changing how lines evaluate would change every line in the kernel, so this is left for its own issue.
  - **The old chamfer's faces, 1 ulp.** `fillet_edges` with cross-section 0 on a box's top edges leaves four trim ends at `v = 1.0000000000000002`, with or without the fix. That code is being replaced by the generic blend (AGENTS.md §4b), so it is not fixed here.
  - **Tolerance-level gaps, 1e-15 to 1.5e-6.** A few offset, shell and steep-chamfer results whose faces' domains and trims disagree, mostly in the cases their tests pin as broken without a flag. These are not rounding and are out of scope.
  - **ASCII reload.** `wr655_ascii_doubles_round_trip`'s file reloaded without its fix (WR-655).
- **Confirmed:** in the Rust port, `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2479_pcurve_ends_in_domain.rs`. The C++ shares the defect: the Rust is a line-for-line port of `SwapUV`'s mirror (same placement, same `N_CrvTransform`), and the difftest's primitive and boolean units, which match the C++ within 1e-9, carry the same rounded trims. The pinned C++ was not run on the scan itself. Ends outside the domain per case (without / with the fix): unit box 6 / 0, offset box 6 / 0, box minus a cylinder through it 6 / 0, box minus a sphere across its seam 6 / 0, box minus a cone 6 / 0, box minus a torus 6 / 0, filleted box bottom 10 / 0, chamfered box top 8 / 4 (old chamfer, above). The cylinder, cone, sphere, torus, cylinder union a crescent across its seam, filleted cylinder rim, extruded circle and revolved rectangle have none either way.
- **Rust port (opt-in):** `Kernel::fixes.face_swap_uv_transposes_trims` (`UpstreamFixes`, default `false`). `swap_uv` transposes each trim by swapping its NURB control points' `x` and `y` exactly (`BSplineCurve::create_transposed_curve`, `nurbs::crv_nurb::Curve::swap_xy`), making a 2D NURB 3D as the mirror does. The fix is solidcore-native, with no fork commit. It changes results beyond bits: with it forced on, 11 tests that pin upstream or fork values bit for bit change. One test that turns on every flag excludes it: `fork_smlib104_setback_corner.rs`'s `all_fixes` pins the fork's volumes bit for bit (1e-14), and with this flag the concave corner without `fillet_blend_corner` moves from 7015.147079173456 to 7015.147079129933 (6e-12 relative). Every other test that turns on every flag passes with it.
