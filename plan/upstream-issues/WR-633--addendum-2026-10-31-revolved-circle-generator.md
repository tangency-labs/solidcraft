Updates: WR-633

The SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@43fd6b6 extends the continuation to a surface of revolution whose generator is an arc of one circle (`sm_GeneratorIsCircle`: every generator span a rational quadratic arc of one centre and radius). SMLib's concave round of a circular edge is such a surface; continued by its tangent (G1R), a round traced past its rim solved its rail on a surface that was no longer the round's (1.2 degrees off at the seam in SolidCraft rig fillets/1207). The generator is now carried on as a torus's is, about its own centre, over the arc's own angle. The Rust port follows it under the same flag, `Kernel::fixes.offset_extension_as_itself` (`generator_is_circle`, `continues_as_itself` in `surface/offset_surface.rs`). Regression test: `wr633_43fd6b6_revolved_circle_continues_as_its_circle` (a semicircle r 2 revolved a quarter turn, offset 0.5: on its tube to 1e-9 past every end with the flag, more than 1e-3 off without).
