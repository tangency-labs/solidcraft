#### WR-646. `SmFilletIntersector::ComputeNextPoint` accepts a variable round's span whose rails cut across the radius law between its traced points
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blend (fillet intersector)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletIntersector.cpp`, `SmFilletIntersector::ComputeNextPoint`, after the test-point loop (`:1036`). A check against the fillet solved inside the span is commented out there ("does nothing in practice").
- **What happens:** the span is accepted when the Hermite rails between two traced points lie on the base surfaces within `GetThisApproxTol3d` and the tangent turns less than the angle tolerance. On a plane every uv curve lies on its face, so that test passes any span. For a variable radius the step therefore grows to the angle limit, and the rails interpolate straight across the radius law between the points. The round removes the wrong volume and its surface leaves the law's sections.
- **Trigger:** a 20 mm cube's edge y = z = 20 rounded with the cubic Bezier law 6, 6, 1, 1. The volume should be 8000 - (1 - pi/4) * 20 * 107/7 = 7934.39315. Upstream gives 7934.38335, short by 1.5e-4 of the volume removed, and the law's 45-degree section points lie up to 6.1e-4 off the surface. In SolidCraft's case, a 40 x 20 x 15 wedge whose top front edge tapers from 13.5 to 0, the fork measured 787 mm3 removed where the law's sections take 777, with the surface up to 0.107 mm off.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#1989). The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@4438859. Where `OffsetRadiiCanChange()`, the fillet is solved at the span's five test shares (a step of that share from the span's start). The span is rejected where a solution's contact lies farther than the tolerance from its rail (`LocalPointSolve` minimise, from the share as guess).
- **Confirmed:** in the Rust port (default = the pinned C++). No C++ run of the fork was made.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. `Kernel::fixes.fillet_variable_span_follows_fillet` ports the fork's check in `compute_next_point_on` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_intersector.rs`). The intersector's `FilletSolverOps` gains `offset_radii_can_change`, which each solver forwards to its own `OffsetRadiiCanChange`.

  Test: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr646_variable_round_follows_law.rs`. With the flag, the volume is 7934.38963 (5.4e-5 of the volume removed, within the fork's 1e-4) and the surface lies 2.0e-4 from the law's sections. Without it, upstream's 1.5e-4 and 6.1e-4. The flag-on test fails without the change.
