#### WR-645. Rounding a drafted pocket's rim and corner edges leaves a corner patch's edge off the corner round (0.845 at R3, 10 degrees)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** the corner of three edges of mixed convexity in `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp` (two convex rim rounds and one concave corner round meeting at the pocket's top corner); the function that places the patch's boundary is not traced yet.
- **What happens:** `SmApiFilletEdges` with radius R on the 4 rim edges and the 4 corner edges of a pocket whose walls are drafted (lean out from the floor) succeeds, but at three of the four top corners the edge between the B-spline corner patch and the corner round lies off the round by about 0.28 R at 10 degrees: its tolerance and its two vertices' are raised to that gap (0.844781 at R3). `SmBrep::ValidatePointers` then fails test #59 (edge tolerance relative to the B-rep's) three times and test #82 (vertex tolerance) six times, so SMLib's own check refuses the result. The gap grows with the radius and the draft (R3: 0.173 at 2 degrees, 0.845 at 10, 1.561 at 20; R1 at 10 degrees: 0.282). With vertical walls (no draft) every corner is exact. Which corner comes out right depends on how the pocket was built (the face order).
- **Trigger:** an 80 x 80 x 40 block minus the pocket whose floor square (side `s = 60 - 30 tan 10 = 54.710191`) is at z = 25 and whose walls lean out 10 degrees to the top (built as the box z 25..45 intersected with four boxes each turned 10 degrees about a line through a floor edge, so the walls meet at corner edges), then `SmApiFilletEdges(brep, {4 rim edges at z = 40, 4 corner edges}, 3.0, 1, 1, 1.0)`. The bad edges run across the corners at z = 37.521, e.g. (13.392, 69.563) to (10.437, 66.608).
- **How found:** the model of tangency-labs/solidcore#968 (a SolidCraft regression case), while writing its regression test (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_968_drafted_pocket_floor_cut.rs`, which does not check the rounded body for this reason).
- **Confirmed:** yes. A standalone SM_API driver linked against the pinned reference libraries (v1.0.1, `3fd5b95`, the prebuilt `bin/cpp`) gives 23 faces, 52 edges, volume 206417.544076, the same three edges with tolerance 0.844781 and the same 9 failed `ValidatePointers` reports. The Rust port gives the same body.
- **Rust port:** ported as is (same result); no opt-in fix yet.
