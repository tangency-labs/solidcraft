#### WR-458. `SmOffsetSurface` builds its extension beyond the base surface differently depending on which points were evaluated first, so two copies of one offset disagree (#419)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Surfaces and fillets
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetSurface.cpp`, `sm_ExtendSurface` (static), called by `SmOffsetSurface::EvaluatePoint` and `EvaluatePlainOrSimple`.
- **What happens:** an offset surface evaluated past its base's domain extends the base with NLib's G1R extension, made to reach one domain size past the first point evaluated out there, then extended again from that extension for the next point. An extension's shape depends on its length and on how the other sides were extended, so two copies of one offset surface (the fillet solver's, on which `RailEdgeuseIntersect` finds a round's end points, and the one the trace runs on) disagree at the same parameters beyond the base. The end points then lie on neither offset surface (0.03 to 0.07 mm off) and the trace fails with 1006, then 1001.
- **Trigger:** an R4 round on a notch rim where a half cylinder r5 meets a B-spline ramp extruded along x (SolidCraft rig case 4143). In isolation: a half cylinder r5 offset by 4, evaluated beyond both ends of its arc, straight at two far points on one copy and after two nearer points on the other, differs by 7.2e-4.
- **How found:** SolidCraft routing its fillets through SMLib (tangency-labs/solidcore#419).
- **Confirmed:** yes, by the fork's capi test in `capi/tests/ops_kernel.cpp` (tangency-labs/smlib@2d7c7ed); in the port, unit test `issue_419_offset_extension_does_not_depend_on_evaluation_order` (gap 7.2e-4 by default, 0 with the flag). The full round is not run in Rust.
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@2d7c7ed always extends the base surface, every side that can grow at once (not past a singularity or along a period), each by the base domain's size times the least power of two that reaches the point and never less than the extension it replaces (`sm_ExtendToward` does one NLib extension).
- **Rust port:** upstream behaviour by default. With `Kernel::fixes.offset_extension_from_base`, `extend_from_base` in `crates/solidcore/src/surface/offset_surface.rs` does the same. It changes every offset extension, so it stays opt-in.
