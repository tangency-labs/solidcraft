Updates: WR-669

#### WR-669 (update, 2026-10-31). Rounds on a plane-conic spine are the exact rational surface of the ball's arcs
- **What changes:** with `Kernel::fixes.fillet_exact_round_classes`, the `Conic` class (a plane with a cylinder or cone at any other angle) and the `ConicPair` class (two cylinders or cones whose offsets touch one ball the same way round) are no longer traced. They are built by `blend::fillet_exact::rational::calc_rational_spine_fillet_geom`, the fork's `SmFilletSolver::CalcRationalSpineFilletGeom`. The spine is the plane's section of the other quadric (`sm_PlaneSection`: ellipse, hyperbola or parabola) cut into rational quadratic segments, each reparametrised so that its weights are 1 at the joints. Over each segment the contacts and the ball's arc between them are Bernstein polynomials (`sm_ContactOf`, `sm_RoundRows`): weights `2G`, `G` and `DaDb` with `G = DaDb (1 + cos)`, or the line between the contacts for a chamfer. A segment is halved (6 times at most) while any weight is not positive. A plane with a cylinder gives degrees (4, 2). Before the round is kept, its rails are checked on their faces and every sampled point is checked at the radius from the spine, both within the body's tolerance. The rails are the v = min and v = max isoparameter curves, and the spine is stored as the centre line.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@0061c41 (the builder, dispatched for `SM_BS_CONIC`) and tangency-labs/smlib@1b5f1e7 (`SM_BS_CONIC_PAIR` dispatched to it). The span direction is 0061c41's, the edge's direction at the trace's first point. Tracking: tangency-labs/solidcore#960.
- **Not ported yet:** the builder's nodal-quartic arm (`sm_NodalQuartic`, `sm_PowerToBernstein`), which tangency-labs/smlib@dda3b5d reworks. dda3b5d also changes the conic span's direction to the trace's order. The `NodalQuartic` class is still traced.
- **Confirmed:** in the Rust port (`crates/solidcore/tests/wr669_exact_conic_rounds.rs`). The fork's C++ was not run here.
  - A plate with an r3 peg tilted 30 degrees, rim rounded r1 (`Conic`). Two rational round faces. Every sampled point is within 1e-12 of radius 1 from the closed-form spine ellipse (z = 1 on the r4 cylinder). The round's area matches a spectral quadrature of the canal surface to 1e-9 (difference 1.4e-10 on 36.3). The added volume matches the divergence theorem to 2e-4. The rest of the volume error comes from the neighbouring faces' fitted UV trims: the peg's face area is 2e-5 off, and the traced round is 1e-3 off.
  - An r3 pole and a 45 degree cone tilted 15 degrees, both tangent to the r3 ball at the origin, their concave conic rounded r1 (`ConicPair`). Same checks: within 1e-12 of the spine, area to 1e-9 (6e-12 on 15.5), volume to 2e-4.
  - Without the flag, or with the arm traced, both rounds are fitted non-rational B-splines.
- **Deviations:** see the header of `blend/fillet_exact/rational.rs`. A non-finite span, or one needing more than 1e5 segments, is `SM_ERR`; the fork's count conversion there is undefined. The surface, rails and spine are built first and stored together.
