#### WR-528. `SmTopologyIntersector::CloseIntersectionLoop` relates two different edges that only share their end vertices
- **Severity:** Wrong result (an edge 37.9 off its face, a UV trim of two points)
- **Area:** Booleans
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmTopologyIntersector.cpp`, `SmTopologyIntersector::CloseIntersectionLoop` (def. `:6108`), the sole-common-edge branch (`:6185`-`:6230`).
- **What happens:** When an unmated edge of one body runs between two vertices that are mated to vertices of the other body, and exactly one edge of the other body connects those mates, the function drops the edge's midpoint onto that edge (`DropPoint`, `SM_SO_MINIMIZE`). If the drop succeeds, it relates the two edges as one. A closest-point drop always succeeds, so any two edges between the same pair of vertices get related, however far apart they run.
- **Trigger:** a crescent prism standing against a cylinder, with its r 10 wall on the cylinder's wall and its corners on the rim, united with the cylinder. The rim splits at the crescent's corners. With the cylinder's seam inside the crescent, the rim's long arc is the sole edge between the corners. That arc is related to the crescent's r 6 arc, and the r 6 wall is built on the rim's circle.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft. The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@e881cdb ("boolean: closing an intersection loop relates two edges only when one lies on the other"), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run was made here. The Rust port reproduces the branch line for line at the pin (`3fd5b95`, usd-brep v1.0.1).
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.close_loop_relates_only_coincident_edges` (`TopologyIntersector::close_intersection_loop`), the edges are related only when the drop distance is at most `max(2 * dMaxDist, the two edges' tolerances)`; otherwise the function goes on to the coincidence intersection as before. `tests/wr_528_crescent_on_rim_join.rs` joins the prism with the cylinder, once across the cylinder's seam and once clear of it. It checks that the result passes the level-2 check and has the exact volume.
