#### WR-524. A 3x2 mixed-convexity fillet corner whose unfilleted edge is concave takes an L-shaped box's rail roles (status 1001)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFillet3x2MixedConvexityCorner::MakeCornerTopology` (`:4755`-`:4897`).
- **What happens:** the corner always takes the concave fillet's rail to cross the corner's unfilleted edge (`SM_FV_RAIL_X_EDGEUSE`) and the convex fillet's rail to cross that edge's continuation past the vertex (`SM_FV_RAIL_X_EXTENDED_EDGEUSE`), with the face between the unfilleted edge and the concave edge extended. That is right in an L-shaped box, where the unfilleted edge is convex and runs up from the vertex. Where it is concave (a boss standing over a bar's side face: the boss's line on that face runs down from the vertex) the convex fillet's rail is the one that crosses it; the corner patch's boundary runs off to the far side of the boss, the tangent field's ends do not match, and filleting fails with status 1001, fillet error 3 (`SM_FILERR_VERTEX_PROBLEM`, "Corner Geometry Computation Failure").
- **Trigger:** a bar 100 x 30 x 10 (y -15..15) joined with a boss r 20, height 20, on the origin; the bar's top front edge and the boss's concave base arc over the bar (both halves) rounded r 1 or r 2 together, meeting at (13.229, -15, 10) (tangency-labs/solidcore#1475, SolidCraft rig case fillets/1207 and its variants).
- **How found:** SolidCraft's fillets on SMLib (rig area `fillets`).
- **Confirmed:** yes, the Rust port fails the same way by default (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1475_mixed_corner_concave_side_edge.rs`, on the C++ union's body).
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@1255993: the roles follow the unfilleted edge's convexity. When it is a convex radial sector (`IsConvexRadialSector(5)`), the fillet the corner names convex takes the crossing role (its rail crosses the unfilleted edge, its cross section runs from there to the rail/rail vertex, and the face it shares with the unfilleted edge is the one extended) and the other the extended role; otherwise the roles are as before. An L-shaped box is unchanged. The fork resolves the in-between edge use and its convexity before the solvers.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. `Kernel::fixes.fillet_3x2_mixed_concave_side_edge` (`FilletCorner::make_3x2_mixed_corner_topology`) ports the fork's change; the r 2 round then builds one manifold solid (F 11, E 25, V 16) of volume 49710.950, against the issue's estimate 49711.025 that leaves out the corner patch.
- **Difftest:** `blend.fillet_executive_p3` keeps gating the default path: 17 of its cases (`fixtures/blend.fillet_executive_p3/corner_3x2.json`, two-box unions with a stepped or overhanging box, and two `stubs_round3.json` cases) reach this corner by default and pass. With the field forced on, 14 of them build a different result (volume 0.2-0.6 % off the unforked C++), as their unfilleted edge takes the swapped roles too (the unforked C++ fails on them with 1001).
- **Fork confirmation:** with the field forced on, the pinned C++ with tangency-labs/smlib@1255993 applied to `SmFilletCorner.cpp` matches the port on all 612 compared `blend.fillet_executive_p3` cases with `max_rel_err=0`. That includes the 14 that move: there the unforked C++ fails with status 1001, while the patched C++ and the Rust port both build the round. In those cases `IsConvexRadialSector(5)` of the unfilleted edge use is TRUE on both sides, so the fork's change applies to them as well.
