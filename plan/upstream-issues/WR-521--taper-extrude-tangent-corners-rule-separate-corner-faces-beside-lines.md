#### WR-521. `CreateTaperExtrude`'s tangent (linear-extension) corners rule separate corner faces even beside lines, and never refuse a corner
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Primitive creation / taper extrude
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp`, `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateTaperExtrude` with `SM_OC_LINEAR_EXTENSION` (the `CreateTrimmedOffsets` corner lines, each ruled to the corner by `sm_CreateRuledSurfaceFromOffsetCurve`).
- **What happens:** a corner piece is its own face ruled to the corner even beside a line, whose tangent plane is its own wall: two lines meet through two flat triangles, so a rectangle drafted outward has 14 faces instead of 6. A wall that closes before the far end is not detected (WR-520's strip builds a ridge), and a half disc drafted outward does not close (WR-402).
- **Trigger:** `SmApiCreateTaperExtrude` of the rectangle (0,0)-(20,0)-(20,10)-(0,10), height 10, draft -10 (outward), both caps, no fillet: 14 faces. The strip of WR-520 with the same rule: a 5-face solid.
- **How found:** reported from SolidCraft (tangency-labs/solidcore#1449); fixed in the SolidCraft fork (tangency-labs/smlib e8f23e0, "the tangent draft corner continues lines' and arcs' walls as their tangent planes").
- **Confirmed:** by the port's regression test (14 faces and SMLib's 5-face strip with the field off); not compared on the C++ reference (fork-only change).
- **Fix:** for a profile of lines and circular arcs with a sharp corner, the tangent rule runs through WR-520's construction with the walls continued as their tangent planes: a line's wall continues as itself, an arc's end gets a flat piece along its tangent where the corner opens a gap. Lines meet at a corner edge; a corner that cannot be closed is refused. Profiles with other curves, and fillet corners, are unchanged.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.taper_tangent_corner_planes` (off by default: SMLib's offset method, so `primitive.primitive_creation_p2` and `sweep.api_brep`, which compare taper extrudes and draft sweeps, keep matching the C++), read in `primitive/primitive_creation/p2/taper.rs` and built by `taper_walls::curvature_keeping_walls` with its tangent step. A refusal is `SM_ERR_INVALID_INPUT` from `create_taper_extrude` and `TaperExtrude::Refused` from `create_taper_extrude_with_corner`. Tests: `with_the_fix_the_strip_is_refused_where_its_short_wall_closes`, `a_drafted_rectangle_has_six_faces_with_the_fix`, `a_half_disc_keeps_its_cone_or_gets_tangent_planes` (6 faces, volume 6555.9139 as WR-402's fix), `without_the_fix_the_strip_is_smlibs_ridge` (flag off).
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#1449 (with WR-520).
