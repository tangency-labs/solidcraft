The issue's own case (#1467) end to end through the cliff overflow, and where the earlier tests do not reach the placement this record fixes.

- **Both halves of the fork's fix are in the port.** tangency-labs/smlib@3f22e97 changes two functions. `CalcRailIntEdgeuse`'s evaluation on the surface continued is `Kernel::fixes.fillet_rail_end_on_continued_surface` (this record, #1876). `CalcCornerEdgeGeom`'s test that a rail end off its side edge by more than the tolerance misses the edge is in the cliff overflow's branch of that function, `FilletCorner::drop_end_rib` under `fixes.fillet_cliff_overflow` (#1905). The record's "not ported yet" for that half is out of date.
- **Case:** #1467's 40 x 20 x 10 box, a top edge along x (y = 0 or y = 20) rounded r on its 10 high face, with `fillet_refuse_rails_leaving_faces`, `fillet_trace_reaches_rails`, `fillet_run_on_bspline_trims` and `fillet_cliff_overflow`.
  - With this flag, on either edge, r10.05 to r12 build a manifold solid of the closed-form volume (the box less the round's corner region clipped to it; relative error at most 1.6e-10). The rail ends on the 10 high face sit at z = 10 - r, on the rails.
  - Without it, on either edge, r10.01 and r10.02 fail with error 4 ("Fillet Surface Computation Failure: Fillet Face Creation Error") and r10.03 to r10.08 with error 3 ("Fillet Corner Error: Corner Edge Computation Failure"). The rail end is put on the side edge's bottom vertex, off the fillet by (r - 10)^2 / 2r (2.0e-5 at r10.02, 1.24e-4 at r10.05, 3.2e-4 at r10.08), under ten times the corner's tolerance (4e-4), and 0 from the side edge, so the end rib is kept, off the fillet. From r10.09 (4.0e-4) the gap test alone takes it as a miss and the box builds without the flag too.
- **Not reached by `wr631_cliff_overflow_construction`'s #1467 case.** That test rounds the back edge (y = 20) without `fillet_run_on_bspline_trims`. The side edges' UV trims on that face are exact degree-1 B-splines, which `RailEdgeuseIntersect`'s run-on does not continue without WR-654's flag: no rail end is found there, the fillet vertex is `SM_FV_NO_INT_RAIL_EU` before `CalcRailIntEdgeuse` would place it, and the case builds the same with or without this flag. On the front edge (y = 0) the trims are lines, and the flags of #960 alone reach the placement.
- **Still refused, not by this record's code:** r10.01 to r10.04, with this flag, on either edge, are refused at the subtraction (`NoRoomReason::CliffSubtractionLeavesNoSolid`, WR-631 addendum of 2026-10-31). The rail ends are placed on the rails and miss their side edges as at r10.05. The removal region is a closed manifold solid of the closed-form volume 40 r^2 (1 - pi/4) (861.84440935 at r10.02, against 861.84440943), but the Boolean difference from the box returns a body that is not a manifold solid (6 faces at r10.02, 8 at r10.04). There the round's face meets the box's bottom face (r - 10)^2 / 2r from its edge, 5.0e-6 at r10.01 to 8.0e-5 at r10.04, under ten times the box's tolerance (1e-5). At r10.05 that distance is 1.24e-4 and the subtraction succeeds.
