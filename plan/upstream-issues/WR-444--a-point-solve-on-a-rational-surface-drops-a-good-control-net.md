#### WR-444. A point solve on a rational surface drops a good control-net guess for a control point on the wrong part of the surface
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Surfaces and volumes
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmSurfacePointSolve.cpp`, `SmSurfPtGlobalSolver::FindPolygonGuess` (the control-net triangle guess at `:785`-`:838`, the nearest-control-point guess after it, the output at `:998`-`:1020`). Reached from `SetPointClassParameters` -> `ComputePointParameters` -> `GlobalPointSolve`.
- **What happens:** on a rational surface the control-net triangle guess is marked suspicious. When it lies farther from the point than the control spacing / 100, `FindPolygonGuess` discards it for the control point nearest to the point, and the two are never compared on the surface. When the control net is coarser than the gaps between distant parts of the surface, the nearest control point is on the wrong part, the solve converges there and finds no point (`SM_ERR` 1001).
- **Trigger:** the end (62.158, 43.731, 73.426) of a seam intersection curve between an r 76 cylinder and a round-wire coil (radius 2.5 centred at r 75.5, pitch 10, 8 turns from z 10, tolerance 1e-3; its walls are rational): the control points lie about 16 mm apart along a turn while the next turn is 5 mm above, so the nearest control point is on the next turn. The triangle guess was 0.585 off (right turn), the control point 5.41, the grid 12.6. Four of six coil start angles (0, -10, -35 and 90 degrees) fail with 1001.
- **How found:** SolidCraft rig `booleans/4505`, traced through the point solve (tangency-labs/solidcore#1164).
- **Confirmed:** yes (reported with the fix and a closed-form volume check in #1164).
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib keeps the suspicious triangle guess as a candidate and returns whichever of it and the control-point guess is nearer on the surface (`CalcFuncVal`); it stays suspicious, so the grid search still runs, and the directed maximize is unchanged.
- **Rust port:** `SurfPtGlobalSolver::find_polygon_guess` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/surface_point_solve/p2.rs`) behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.point_solve_keeps_triangle_guess` it keeps the suspicious triangle guess and returns the nearer one on the surface, as the fork does. Test `a_point_solve_finds_the_point_on_stacked_runs_with_the_fix`: a rational strip of four runs stacked 5 apart (control points 20 to 40 apart along a run), where `global_point_solve` (intersect) finds no solution for a point on the first run without the fix and finds it with the fix; over 400 points along the strip the fix never loses a point upstream finds.
