#### WR-45 (update, 2026-10-31). Opt-in chain-rule third-derivative terms in `smvol_LiftThirdDerivative` (#2165)

- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmVolume.cpp:9851-9975` (surface overload of `smvol_LiftThirdDerivative`, specifically `:9926` in `rDuuv` and `:9948` in `rDuvv`), `source/SMLib/src/SmVolume.cpp:10126-10401` (volume overload of `smvol_LiftThirdDerivative`, specifically `:10217` in `rDuuv`, `:10239` in `rDuuw`, `:10261` in `rDuvv`, `:10295-10315` in `rDuww`, `:10352` in `rDvvw`, and `:10359-10379` in `rDvww`), and `source/SMLib/src/SmVolume.cpp:10406-10449` (`smvol_ConcatEvals`); Rust `crates/solidcore/src/surface/volume/p1.rs` (`lift_third_derivative_srf_opt_in`, `lift_third_derivative_vol_opt_in`, `concat_evals_opt_in`).
- **Evidence:** In the pinned v1.0.1 C++ source, the surface overload multiplies `(crSv.x * crSu.z^2 + 2 * crSv.z * crSu.x * crSu.z)` in `rDuuv` (`:9926`) and `(crSu.x * crSv.z^2 + 2 * crSu.z * crSv.x * crSv.z)` in `rDuvv` (`:9948`) by `crDxxz` instead of `crDxzz` (whereas `rDuuu` at `:9905` and `rDvvv` at `:9969` already use `crDxzz`). The volume overload repeats the same `crDxxz`-for-`crDxzz` substitution in `rDuuv` (`:10217`), `rDuuw` (`:10239`), `rDuvv` (`:10261`), `rDuww` (`:10310`), `rDvvw` (`:10352`), and `rDvww` (`:10374`), and additionally builds `rDuww` (`:10295-10315`) from `crFuv` and `crFv` instead of `crFuw` and `crFw`, and `rDvww` (`:10359-10379`) from `crFvv` and `crFv` instead of `crFvw` and `crFw` (`rDuuu`, `rDuvw`, `rDvvv`, and `rDwww` are already exact, as is the curve overload). Central finite differences of `lift_second_derivative_srf` and `lift_second_derivative_vol` confirm the multivariable chain-rule formulas and diverge macroscopically under the pinned expressions whenever `Dxxz != Dxzz` or `Fuw`/`Fw` differ from `Fuv`/`Fv`. This numerical reproduction is Rust-only; it does not newly confirm the defect through a C++ execution.
- **Rust opt-in:** `Kernel::fixes.volume_lift_third_derivative_terms`, default `false` for pinned C++ parity. Existing `lift_third_derivative_srf`, `lift_third_derivative_vol`, and `concat_evals` keep their signatures and default (`false`) behaviour; `lift_third_derivative_srf_opt_in`, `lift_third_derivative_vol_opt_in`, and `concat_evals_opt_in` apply the exact chain-rule terms when `exact_terms` is `true`.
- **Production reachability:** `SrfInVolume::evaluate` passes `k.fixes.volume_lift_third_derivative_terms` directly to `lift_third_derivative_srf_opt_in` and already forwards `fixes: k.fixes` in `EvalOptions` when evaluating its volume (`CrvInVolume::evaluate` likewise forwards `fixes: k.fixes`). `Volume::evaluate` (orient and next-map concatenation), `Volume::map` (inverse-orient, orient, and next-map concatenation), `BendVolume::evaluate`, `TwistVolume::evaluate`, and `UnbendVolume::evaluate` pass `opts.fixes.volume_lift_third_derivative_terms` to `concat_evals_opt_in`, preserving `EvalOptions::fixes` across arbitrarily nested next maps.
- **Regression:** Unit tests in `crates/solidcore/src/surface/volume/p1.rs` (`wr45_lift_third_derivative_srf_matches_finite_differences`, `wr45_lift_third_derivative_vol_matches_finite_differences`, and `wr45_compound_volume_and_srf_in_volume_end_to_end`) verify default (`false`) parity, exact agreement with central finite differences when `volume_lift_third_derivative_terms = true` across polynomial maps, compounded `TwistVolume`/`BendVolume`/`UnbendVolume`/oriented `Transform` volumes (`evaluate` and `map`), `SrfInVolume` (tilted `Plane` and curved `Sphere`, parameter-space and in-space), and `CrvInVolume`.
