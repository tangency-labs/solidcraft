#### Updates: WR-80 (2026-10-29). Cause traced; ported as an opt-in fix

- **Cause (from the SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@0dbe681):** `TraceCurve()` returns `SM_SUCCESS` with an empty `m_vCurvePoints` when its start point is a tangent or coincidence point (or a bounded point) that already lies on a found curve. It also leaves `m_bCurveIsClosed` as the previous trace left it (`FALSE`). `DoIntersection()` and `FindInteriorCurves()` take that for an open curve to trace in the other direction and call `ReverseCurveDirection()` on the empty list, which fails the whole intersection with `SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER`. `DoPointIntersection()` already checks for points first. In the fork's corpus run, `c2n_quartic_contact_parabola` goes from ERROR to WRONG TOPOLOGY (it completes; its duplicate tangent traces are WR-76), and `y23_t2_ex5_two_loops` goes from ERROR to INCOMPLETE.
- **Rust port:** `Kernel::fixes.ssi_no_reverse_of_empty_trace` (off by default) adds the "something was traced" condition in front of both reverse traces: `SurfaceIntersector::do_intersection_in` (`intersect/surface_intersector/p5.rs`) and `find_interior_curves` (`p4.rs`).
- **Kind:** a result-changing fix (a failure on valid input), so it follows the opt-in rule. Off by default, the port matches the pinned C++.
- **Test:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr_80_ssi_empty_trace_not_reversed.rs` uses the corpus case `c2n_quartic_contact_parabola` (jyviko/ssi-corpus `b04c8a6`). It expects `SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER` with the flag off, and with the flag on a completed intersection with at least one curve.
