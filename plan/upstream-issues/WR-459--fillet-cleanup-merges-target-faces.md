#### WR-459. The clean-up after filleting merges the target's own faces
- **Severity:** Wrong result (an invalid body returned as a success)
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletExecutive.cpp` (`DoFilleting`, the `BooleanPostProcess(TRUE)` call after `RemoveTopoEdges`), `source/SMLib/src/SmMerge.cpp` (`SmMerge::BooleanPostProcess`, the edge loop at about line 857).
- **What happens:** after the fillet faces are merged into the target, `BooleanPostProcess` runs with `bCheckAllEdges` true and removes every topological edge of the body, the ones between the target's own faces too, not only the fillet's. Faces of the target that lie on coincident surfaces are merged into one face on one of those surfaces. On a sphere in six patches (each on its own sphere surface, as an importer hands over a cube projected onto the sphere) cut flat, an R1 round on the rim merges the five patches into one face whose surface no patch's parameterization can carry: loops of the wrong orientation (`SmTrimmingTools` #3) and rails up to half a millimetre off their surfaces (`SmBrep` #59), with status success.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft rig case 2752: a sphere r 2 in six patches cut flat at z = 1, its rim rounded R1 (tangency-labs/solidcore#423). A single-face sphere does not show it. The Rust test (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_423_fillet_cleanup_keeps_faces.rs`) shows only the mechanism, an edge between the target's own coincident faces removed by the round; it was not run against any C++ build, and the sphere's six-patch import is not built in Rust.
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig on the SMLib fork (tangency-labs/solidcore#423).
- **Confirmed:** no in this repository (read at c9c5979; the fork's replay of the dump gives 26.925789 against 7 pi + pi^2 / 2 = 26.925950 after the fix).
- **Fix:** the fork (tangency-labs/smlib@9ddf4a8) records the target's edges and faces at the start of `DoFilleting` and `BooleanPostProcess` skips each edge in the first set and each edge whose faces are all in the second.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_cleanup_keeps_target_faces` (default `false`, upstream behaviour): `FilletSession::do_filleting` records the target's edges and faces, `CleanupRelations::kept_edges`/`kept_faces` carry them to `boolean_post_process` in `crates/solidcore/src/boolean/merge/p1.rs`. Arena ids are never reused, so the recorded ids name the same entities after the merge. Part of tangency-labs/solidcore#423.
