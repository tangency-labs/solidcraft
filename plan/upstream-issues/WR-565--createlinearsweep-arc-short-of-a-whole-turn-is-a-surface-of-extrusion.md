#### WR-565. `CreateLinearSweep` makes an arc short of a whole turn a surface of extrusion, not a cylinder
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Sweeps
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp:1345` (v1.0.1, `3fd5b95`), `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateLinearSweep` (curve overload, def. `:1197`); the same test at `:1676` in `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateLinearSweepSimple` (def. `:1554`).
- **What happens:** a profile curve that `IsArc` finds circular becomes an `SmCylinder` only when its sweep angle is within `dTol` of 360 degrees. Any shorter arc swept square to its plane becomes an `SmSurfOfExtrusion`, which exports as a B-spline surface although it lies on a cylinder. The code says so: "This can be extended to create partial cylinders".
- **Trigger:** `SmApiCreateLinearSweep` of a quarter disc of radius 4 about (4, 4, 0) (the arc from (0, 4, 0) to (4, 0, 0) and its two radii), 50 along +Z: an `SmSurfOfExtrusion` and four `SmPlane`s. An extruded L section with a modelled bend (arcs of radius 4 and 2 about (4, 4)) has two such faces, so a sheet-metal convert that picks a point on the bend finds no cylinder there ("no edge of the body at the bend point").
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig (sheet-metal/1092, sheet-metal/4328), issue #1477.
- **Confirmed:** no. The pinned source takes only a whole circle (`:1345`). The `primitive.primitive_creation_p1` open-arc cases (`linear_arc_*`, `linear_analytic_arc_*`) agree with the C++, but their records (face domains and sample points) do not show the surface kind.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.linear_sweep_arc_is_cylinder` (`UpstreamFixes`, default `false`: upstream's surface of extrusion) in `PrimitiveCreation::create_linear_sweep` (`crates/solidcore/src/primitive/primitive_creation/p1/primitives.rs`, helper `arc_sweep_cylinder`). With it, an arc short of a whole turn that passes the whole circle's cap-offset test (swept square to its plane) is the cylinder of the arc's frame and radius. The frame is turned about its Z axis to the arc's start or, for a sweep against the arc's normal, to its end and then flipped about its X axis, and the cylinder is bounded with `AdjustSTEPUVDomain(0, 0, sweep angle, distance)`. This follows the SolidCraft fork's `ArcSweepCylinder` (tangency-labs/smlib 12918e9) as #1477 describes it; the fork's code was not read. A whole circle keeps upstream's cylinder, and a sweep that is not square stays a surface of extrusion. `CreateLinearSweepSimple` has no Rust port (no plan unit owns it), so only the curve `CreateLinearSweep` changes. Every difftest runs with the field off. Test `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1477_arc_sweep_cylinder.rs`: the issue's quarter disc with the field off (as before) and on (a cylinder of radius 4 about x = 4, y = 4, domain 0..90 by 0..50), a sweep against the normal, a circle over 180..270 and a NURBS arc, the extruded L (10 faces, 24 edges, 16 vertices, two cylinders), a sweep not square to the arc, and a whole circle. Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#1477.
