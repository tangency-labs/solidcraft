#### WR-380. `smgu_EvaluateCurveContinuity` judges G2 by the parameterization and compares a normal with itself
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a continuity classification: G2 reported across a curvature flip, or refused across a reparameterized smooth join)
- **Area:** Geometry utilities
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmGeomUtility.cpp`, `smgu_EvaluateCurveContinuity`, the G2 block: `dCurvature1 = aVals1[2].Length()`, `dCurvature2 = aVals2[2].Length()` (the raw second derivatives, not the `aFrenet1/2[2]` the function computes just before), `sNormal1.AngleBetween(sNormal1, dAngRad)` (the first normal against itself), and `SM_IS_ZERO_TO_TOL(dCurvatureGap, SM_EFF_ZERO_SQRT/10.0)` (an absolute gap).
- **What happens:** the normal-angle test can never fail, so two sides with equal second-derivative lengths and opposite normals are G2. The curvature gap compares second-derivative lengths, which scale with the parameter speed, so the same circle traced at twice the speed (|P''| = 4 against 1) is not G2, and an absolute 1e-7 gap ignores the curvature's size.
- **Trigger:** any caller that classifies continuity at a join: `SmCurve::EvaluateContinuity` (`CalculateContinuities` at B-spline knots) and the surface closure check's boundary continuity (`SmSurface::IsClosed` with continuity wanted). E.g. tangents (1,0,0) both sides with second derivatives (0,1,0) and (0,-1,0) are `C1G2`; (1,0,0)/(0,1,0) against (2,0,0)/(0,4,0) (one circle) is only `G1`.
- **How found:** source reading (tangency-labs/solidcore#933, #838, jyviko's status sweep and a helical pipe sweep investigation).
- **Confirmed:** source only (the port reproduces upstream bit for bit with the default).
- **Fix:** take the curvature vectors from `smgu_FrenetFromDerivatives` (`P' x P'' x P' / |P'|^4`, length `|P' x P''| / |P'|^3` along the normal), compare their lengths relative to the larger (`SM_EFF_ZERO_SQRT/10 * (1 + max)`), and measure the angle between the two normals.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `ContinuityOptions::geometric_g2` on the new `geom::geom_utility::p2::evaluate_curve_continuity_with` (`evaluate_curve_continuity` keeps upstream's test), set from `Kernel::fixes.geometric_g2_continuity` (default `false`) by `curve::curve::p2`'s `EvaluateContinuity` and `surface::surface::p7::base_is_closed`. Test `issue_933_geometric_g2_continuity` in `crates/solidcore/src/geom/geom_utility/p2.rs`. With the default, `geom.geom_utility_p2` (which calls `evaluate_curve_continuity` directly) and every unit reaching the two callers compare as before. Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#933 and tangency-labs/solidcore#838.
