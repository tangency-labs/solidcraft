#### WR-566 addendum (2026-10-31). The faces either side of a closed wall are one face
Updates: WR-566

- **What changed:** with the step wall closed to nothing and deleted, the face move (`SmOffsetFacesExecutive::OffsetFaces`, `offset::offset_faces`) left an edge between the moved top and the top that stayed, two faces on one plane: 7 faces where the 40 x 30 x 20 box (low top up 10) or 40 x 30 x 10 box (high top down 10) has 6. The volumes were already right (24000, 12000). Tracked as tangency-labs/solidcore#1978.
- **Fork fix:** tangency-labs/smlib 9504880, `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetFaces.cpp`. After the local operation, the edges between a face on a moved surface and a face that stayed, which `SmEdge::IsTopological` finds removable, are removed (`RemoveTopologicalEdgesAndVertices`, every other edge and vertex on the keep lists), with the vertices at their ends where those join two edges on one curve. A face the body splits on purpose stays split.
- **Port:** the same opt-in, `Kernel::fixes.local_operation_closes_walls_to_nothing`, now also does this in `OffsetFacesExecutive::move_faces` (`join_across_closed_walls`). The fork's surface mark is replaced by "a moved face (`MARK_MOVED`) lying on a new surface", because the port decides "on a new surface" geometrically and the face that stayed lies on the same plane. The "moved no face" refusal is unchanged.
- **Which path shows it:** the face move, `OffsetFacesExecutive::offset_faces(k, b, &[top], d)`, which runs `move_faces` (`FaceuseOffset` of the top by `|d|` along its outward normal, then `local_operation_simple`). The repro moves the low top with d = +10 and the high top with d = -10. Flags: `local_operation_step_walls`, `local_operation_closes_walls_to_nothing`, `local_operation_offset_status`. The plain `SmBrep::LocalOperation` call that `tests/wr_566_local_operation_closes_a_wall.rs` makes also leaves 7 faces in solidcore: checked on this branch with a throwaway probe of that test's `move_top`, which gave 7 faces for both moves, and this fix does not touch that path. SolidCraft's replace face (rig case shell/cov-replace-face-step-onto-the-higher-face) already reports 6 faces and 24000 on main without this fix. So the merge there comes from a step SolidCraft runs after the local operation, outside solidcore and not inspected here. It is not a later step in solidcore's local operation, so that rig case is no evidence for this fix.
- **Test:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr_648_offset_faces_closed_wall_join.rs` (6 faces and 24000 / 12000 with the flag; 7 faces before the fix; 1043 without the flag). The direct `SmBrep::LocalOperation` path is unchanged: the fork changes only the face move.
