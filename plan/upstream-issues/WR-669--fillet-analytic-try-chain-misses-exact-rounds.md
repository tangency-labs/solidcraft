#### WR-669. `CalcFilletGeom` decides whether a constant round is exact by a try-and-fall-through chain gated on the edge's shape, which misses rounds on spheres and the corner ball
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a round that is a surface of revolution comes out traced and fitted, and a corner-ball round fails)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet solver)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp`, `SmFilletSolver::CalcFilletGeom`. It calls `CanMakeAnalytic`, then `sm_TestAnalyticSurface` (planes and cones only), then `CalcCylinderFilletGeom` (`IsLine` of the edge) and then `CalcTorusFilletGeom` (`IsArc` of the edge). Each one falls through to the trace when it fails. `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`, `SmFilletGeom::ReCalcFilletGeom`, tries `CalcConeFilletGeom` and then `CalcTorusFilletGeom`.
- **What happens:** whether a round is exact follows from its faces alone. The faces are natural quadrics, and the curve the ball's centre runs on (the spine) depends only on their kinds and placement. A line spine gives a cylinder, a circle about a common axis gives the section turned about that axis, and a point gives a piece of the ball. Upstream instead asks the edge whether it is a line or an arc, and admits only planes and cones as faces. So a round between a sphere and a coaxial cylinder is traced and fitted. The corner ball (two cylinders as wide as the ball, axes meeting) is never tried (WR-420 added it behind its own flag). The torus frame also comes from `IsArc` of the edge rather than from the faces' axis.
- **Trigger:** an r5 ball unioned with a coaxial r2 cylinder, its concave rim rounded r1: the round is fitted, not revolved. The #416 tray (bottom edges R2, then vertical edges R2) fails with 1001 at the corner ball.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@b5f2867 (one classification of the spine from the faces, `SmFilletExact.cpp`) and tangency-labs/smlib@7ac037e (the try-chain deleted; line, circle and point rounds built by class). Tracking: tangency-labs/solidcore#2085.
- **Confirmed:** in the Rust port (`crates/solidcore/tests/wr669_exact_round_classes.rs`). The fork's C++ was not run here.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_exact_round_classes`, the classification replaces the chain at both call sites, and a builder's failure becomes an error rather than a trace. The classification is `blend::fillet_exact::classify_exact_blend` / `classify_blend_spine` and the dispatch is `calc_exact_fillet_geom`. The call sites are `fillet_solver::p4::c3_exact_by_class` and `FilletBrep::re_calc_fillet_geom` (where a line round is rebuilt as its cylinder, not the cone). The builders are the cylinder and torus bodies without their `IsLine`/`IsArc` gates (`FilletSolver::calc_line_spine_fillet_geom`, `calc_circle_spine_fillet_geom`, with the frame from the class axis) and the corner ball (`calc_sphere_fillet_geom`, regardless of `fillet_corner_ball_patch`).
  The builders' own fork-fix flags still apply on their own (WR-421/572/581/422/592/568/541). The fork's behaviour is all of them on. A chamfer round a circle is traced unless `fillet_chamfer_cone_on_arc` is on.
  The conic, conic-pair and nodal-quartic classes are classified but traced. Their exact rational builder (fork 0061c41, 1b5f1e7, dda3b5d) extends this flag later, as addenda to this record.
  Tests: `wr669_exact_round_classes.rs`. On the ball and peg, the round is one surface of revolution whose points lie within 1e-12 of the ball's radius from the spine circle, and it is traced without the flag. On the tray, the corner balls are built with only this flag, with the closed-form volume to 1e-7, and the default fails with 1001. A plate's boss foot (circle class) and plate edge (line class) give the closed-form volume to 1e-9 and agree with the chain. The classification itself has unit tests in `blend::fillet_exact::tests`.
