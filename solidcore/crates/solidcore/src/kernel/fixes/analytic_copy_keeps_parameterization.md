`SmBSplineSurface::CopyAndAddAnalytics` keeps an exact copy where the recognised analytic surface does not give the same points at the same parameters (7 x 7 grid), so a face split by `MakeEdgeInFaceWithVU` keeps trims that still lie on its edges (WR-450, #387; the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@7af1987 (was 276b509)). With it, `SmBrep::Mirror` also keeps each face's UV domain, which `SetSurface` resets to the mirrored surface's natural domain: the mirrored surface keeps the face's parameters.
