`SmBrep::RayFire` ([`crate::topology::brep::p2::ray_fire`]) and `SmShell::RayFire` ([`crate::topology::shell::ray_fire`]) select the nearest ray intersection strictly by `solution_value` (preferring the lowest-dimensional entity within the tie tolerance and breaking ties by smaller `solution_value`) instead of falling through to `start.param(0) < best.start.param(0)`. Upstream shifts `param(0)` to the surface `u` parameter on face hits (and leaves it as curve `t` on edge hits or `0.0` on vertex hits), so any farther hit along the ray with a smaller `param(0)` overwrote the true nearest hit and inverted point-in-solid / disjoint-shell classification (e.g., classifying a body sitting in a pocket as inside the surrounding solid). Also iterates every edge of an unclassified primary face in `classify_remaining` (`boolean::merge::p4`) instead of retrying `edges[0]`.
