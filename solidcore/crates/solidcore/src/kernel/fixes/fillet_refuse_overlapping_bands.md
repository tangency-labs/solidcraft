`create_fillet_brep` refuses blends whose bands on a face they share overlap away from any corner that trims them (a rail of one inside the band between the other's filleted edge and its rail: both vertical edges of a box end rounded past half its width), with `FilletErrorType::NoRoom`, where upstream builds a body that is not closed (WR-542; the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@c0d74ee, `BandsClearOtherRails`). A rail is sampled only between its end vertices, where the corners have placed them: the rails are not trimmed yet and run on past their corners' mitres, so a run-on crossing another blend's band refused a valid round (WR-649, #1984; the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@d548305). Not the fork's (#18178): where two kept rails on the face they share cross (their curves' intersection within the solvers' tolerance), the pieces the crossings cut the rail into are each judged at their middle by the same band test, so a short overlap the samples step over is refused too (a ring's outer chamfer and its hole's chamfers, whose corner mitres reach a few hundredths past the outer chamfer's rail at the ring's narrowest places), and rails that only touch are not. Not the fork's (#18563): a sample whose foot on the other blend's run is an end of that blend's own edge (not a closed one) is judged too where the other rail's kept part runs on past that end (a round running out onto a face beyond its edge's end, as onto the sphere that trims a block's corner): a sample whose nearest point of that rail lies on the run-on is inside the band where it lies on the end's side of the rail, in the direction of the band's section at the edge's end and short of that section's width, so neighbouring rounds whose rails cross there are refused. That direction is the run-on's own only where the run-on is straight on a plane; an end on another edge of a tangent chain is still beside the band. The check also runs with this fix off where a round of the blend, taken as apart by the tangent rollover, rolls over a smooth edge onto a face another round of the blend stands on (`FilletExecutive::rolled_beside_band`, set in `fillet_executive/session/tangent_rollover.rs`): the check is the only thing that compares that round's band with the rolled rail, so without it an overlap there went unrefused until the fillet failed later with a message that did not name it (#19242).
