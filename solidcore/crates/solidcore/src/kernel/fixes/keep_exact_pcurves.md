`SmBrep::CreateFaceInRegionFromSurface` passes the natural iso-line pcurves of the face it makes on its surface's parameter rectangle (`CreateNaturalUVTrimCurves`) to `SmBrep::MakeFaceWithCurves` with `pbOptCrvOnSurf` set, so they are the face's edge uses' trims, where upstream drops them and projects each edge's curve again later, a fit; reprojecting a closed section at a collapsed horn rail can choose a different singular parameter.
