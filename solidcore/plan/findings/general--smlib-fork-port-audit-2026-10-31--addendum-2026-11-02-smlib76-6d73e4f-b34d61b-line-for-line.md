# General: addendum (2026-11-02): smlib#76's 6d73e4f and b34d61b checked line for line against their ports

Updates: plan/findings/general--smlib-fork-port-audit-2026-10-31.md (with its addendum `--addendum-2026-11-01-smlib76-68f6913-6ca3405-line-for-line.md`, which did the same for the pair's other two replayed commits)

tangency-labs/smlib#76 (merged into the fork's `replay/1.0.1` as cd47efd; solidcore#2243) carries three fillet commits that solidcore had already ported from their twins, or retires with them: 6d73e4f (twin 856b54e, WR-600, #2099), 0cc48bd (twin 4ee7d3a; it takes a87c502 back out, WR-681) and b34d61b (twin cd47efd, #2130). This addendum checks the two ported ones line for line (solidcore#2248). 0cc48bd is ported by that PR (`plan/upstream-issues/WR-681--addendum-2026-11-01-retired-by-0cc48bd.md`).

## The twins are the same trees

Each smlib#76 commit has the same git tree as its `replay/1.0.1` twin: 6d73e4f and 856b54e (c7828691), 0cc48bd and 4ee7d3a (9291aac1), b34d61b and cd47efd (724e5369); so do 9bebf34/de3a8c9 and a87c502/261b3f9. GitHub's patches for 6d73e4f/856b54e and b34d61b/cd47efd are byte-identical apart from the `From <sha>` line. So the C++ the ports were made from is the C++ of smlib#76, and the check is of the Rust against it.

## 6d73e4f: no difference

Every piece is under `Kernel::fixes.fillet_nx1_tangent_side_edge_same_convexity` (WR-600's flag), where the fork has it unconditionally.

- **`sm_IsTangentSeamAtVertex` → `is_tangent_seam_at_vertex`** (`blend/fillet_corner/topology.rs`). Same order: the tangential test at the vertex, then `sm_TestG1Continuity` of the filleted edge and the side edge (a continuation is no seam), then the side edge's nearer end, then for the edge use and its radial `EvaluateBinormal` with the uv direction, the face's surface, `EvaluateDirectionalDerivs` to order 2, the speed test `< SM_EFF_ZERO`, the normal curvature `N . D2 / |D1|^2`, and the comparison `|k0 - k1| > SM_EFF_ZERO_SQRT (1 + |k0| + |k1|)`. Every failed evaluation answers `false`, as the fork's `return FALSE`; only `Unported` and a cancel propagate (`or_false`, the module's rule). `sm_TestG1Continuity`'s errors in the port are the null curves upstream dereferences.
- **`SmFilletNx1Corner::MakeCornerTopology`** (`topology.rs`, `topology/nx1_end.rs`):
  - `bTangentSide[kk]` is set inside the same tangential test that sets `bSideEdgeSameCvxty[kk]`, side 0 then side 1 (`tangent_sides_same_convexity`);
  - the skip round the vertex starts with `bTangentSide[kk] ||` and clears `bTangentSide[kk]` when it reaches a corner edge past the tangent edge;
  - at a mixed corner on a side of the filleted convexity, the rail vertex is `SM_FV_RAIL_X_EDGEUSE` with `SetTangentEnd(TRUE)` at a tangent side, `SM_FV_RAIL_X_EXTENDED_EDGEUSE` otherwise, and the end face at a tangent side is the corner edge use's face, or its radial's when that is a fillet side face;
  - the end face's extension is `SM_CT_CINFINITY` when the corner is mixed and either side is a tangent side, `SM_CT_G1` otherwise;
  - each cap edge is `SM_FE_ON_EXTEND_EDGE` (original edge the extended one) when its rail vertex is an extended rail end or, at a mixed corner, its side is a tangent side.
- **`m_bTangentEnd`** → `FilletVertex::tangent_end`, `false` from the constructor (`blend/fillet_geom/p1.rs`).
- **`CalcFilletIntSideFace`** (`blend/fillet_geom/p5.rs`): an end rib between two tangent ends is `SM_ERR` (returned before the failure branch, as `SER_MSG`); the section's contact order is taken before the rib and its error returned at once (`SER`); the rib runs from the tangent end to the other vertex, reversed when the edge's end vertex is the tangent end; a failed rib or a missing end surface (`NER(pEnd)` inside the rib) takes the same failure branch as a failed intersection (`SM_FIL_SURF_INT_FAILURE`, the corner's error record). With `fillet_gn_section_generator` the order comes from the G^n section (`gn_section_contact_order`, WR-685, the fork's later 11ea2d9); that is not part of 6d73e4f.
- **`CalcEdgeOnExtendedEdge`'s seam test**: after the face count check and before WR-457's past-a-tangent-side-edge branch, on the edge's first edge use, at the corner's filleted vertex, with the corner's tangency tolerance in degrees. The port runs the test on a scratch copy of the target, because `EvaluateBinormal` may make a missing UV trim (a cached member upstream); the answer is the same.
- **`CalcEdgeOnTangentSeam` → `calc_edge_on_tangent_seam`**: the `SM_CAST_PTR(SmBSplineCurve, ..)` kinds (B-spline, line, ellipse and circle, hyperbola, parabola; `SmConic` and the other `SmCurve`s give the null pointer), the reach as the larger of the vertices' distances to the nearer edge end, the trim to the edge's interval (virtual, notifying) and the two `SM_CT_CINFINITY` extensions by twice the reach at the start and then the end (`CreateExtendedCurve` is not virtual: the B-spline data), the tolerance `max(edge tolerance, corner's approximation tolerance)`, the drops over the natural interval with `SM_FE_END_PNT_DROP_FAILURE`, the trim to `[min, max]` and the reversal when the start vertex's parameter is the larger.
- **`RailEdgeuseIntersect`'s run-on** (`blend/fillet_solver/p2.rs`): the tangent seam exception, the tangential test of the side edge use at the side edge's corner vertex with the solver's tangency tolerance in degrees, keeps the run-on past both ends. It sits inside WR-643's far-end-only code, so it acts only with `fillet_run_on_past_far_end_only` on too (recorded in WR-600's 2026-10-31 addendum).
- `sm_SectionContactOrder` → `section_contact_order` and `sm_TangentEndRibPoint` → `tangent_end_rib_point` (`blend/fillet_geom/tangent_end_rib.rs`): same switch, same Newton (40 steps, the residual test from the second step, the determinant test, the three Cramer updates in the same order), the end surface's point returned.

## b34d61b: no difference

`sm_TangentEndRib` at b34d61b against `tangent_end_rib`: the natural domains, the four drops and their `dApproxTol` refusals in the same order, the section found across u then v with `SM_EFF_ZERO_SQRT max(1, length)`, the refusal of ends on one section, `s ~ t^2` for odd k, the zero-speed end conditions for odd k past 1 (the fillet surface's and end surface's first derivatives at the tangent end, `2 (s1 - s0)` along the section, the normal equations with their `SM_EFF_ZERO a c` singularity test and the `SM_EFF_ZERO_SQRT max(1, |X''|)` tangent-plane test), the interpolation (`CreateInterpolatingCurve` degree 3 at the stations with a zero first and the given second start derivative, the second control point tested against the first and set to it; `InterpolatePoints` at the stations otherwise), the stations from 8 to 1024, the chord then linear guesses, the last station on the other end, the other-end refusal, the midpoint test of the three curves at `t`, the halving of off spans in reverse order for the zero-speed rib and the doubling otherwise, and the final refusal. The port's added guards (`checked_add` on the order, `.get()` on the arrays) cannot fire.

### The end surface's drop has no face context: not a difference

`tangent_end_rib` drops the rib's ends onto the end surface without a face context (`drop_point::<NoObject>`), while the intersection branch of `calc_fillet_int_side_face` passes the face's services when the end surface is the face's own (`extended: false`), so that a global point solve on a face's surface reads the face's trimmed cache as upstream's `DropPoint` does through the surface's owner. A tangent end never hands the rib a face's own surface:

- at a tangent side `MakeCornerTopology` sets `pExtendEdge`, so `bDoExtend` holds and the end face's extended surface is registered (`AddExtendedOriginalSurface`; if the face had one already, that one stays);
- `m_bExtendedSurfacePatch` starts `TRUE` and is cleared only in the multi-neighbour branch, which needs `pEndFace == NULL`; at a tangent side the end face is the face across the seam;
- so `CalcFilletIntSideFace` takes `GetExtendedSurface(GetOriginalFace())`, never `pFace->GetSurface()`, and that surface is a copy no face owns (`CopyAndAddAnalytics`; the port's `copy_and_add_analytics` makes a new `Surface` without owner).

A run with the rib's end surface printed (local, not committed) showed the extended surface, owned by no face, for each of the 28 end ribs built by `fork_856b54e_tangent_end.rs`, `fork_cd47efd_tangent_end_rib_sqrt_s.rs` and the thirteen cases of `fork_9bebf34_tangent_end_cases.rs`. Nothing to fix.
