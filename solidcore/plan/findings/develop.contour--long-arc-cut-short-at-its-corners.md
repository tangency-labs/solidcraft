# develop.contour: a long arc cut short at both its corners came back a whole turn too long (2026-11-26)

- **Unit:** the contour offset (`develop::contour`, `offset_profile` behind `offset_contour` and `offset_closed_contour`; no goldens unit).
- **Input:** the closed profile of the test `a_long_arc_cut_short_by_its_corners_keeps_less_than_a_turn`: a circle of radius 10 about the origin cut by the chord y = −6, run counter-clockwise (the long arc from (8, −6) round to (−8, −6), sweep π + 2·atan2(6, 8) = 4.43, then the chord back), moved 7.875 inside. The moved circle has radius 2.125 and the moved chord is y = 1.875; they cross at (±1, 1.875), so the arc's new ends are at atan2(1.875, 1) and atan2(1.875, −1), 0.98 apart.
- **Values:** sweep 7.263 before the change (0.98 and a whole turn), 0.98 after.

![Two panels of the same profile and view. Left, the result before the change: the moved arc drawn as a whole circle plus the short arc, sweep 7.26. Right, after: the short arc over the top between the two crossings, sweep 0.98, with the start's move of +1.72 and the end's move of −1.72 marked on their rays from the centre.](develop.contour--long-arc-cut-short-at-its-corners.svg)

*Look at the end moves: each is less than half a turn, but taken together they are more, so wrapping them as one added a turn.*

- **Cause:** at a sharp corner an arc's end moves to the crossing of the shifted curves nearest the corner, so each end moves less than half a turn along its circle (here the start +1.72, the end −1.72). The old line wrapped the difference of the new and drawn sweeps, `end − begin − sweep` (here 0.98 − 4.43 = −3.45), into one half turn; that difference is the sum of both moves and can pass half a turn while neither move does, and then a turn was added (4.43 − 3.45 + 2π = 7.26). The new line wraps each end's move on its own: 4.43 − 1.72 − 1.72 = 0.98.
- **Where it differs:** only at an arc with a sharp end whose two moves add to more than half a turn, which a closed loop meets where a long arc is cut much shorter (a circle cut by a chord). An arc without a sharp end has both moves exactly 0 and keeps its sweep bit for bit. No existing test or golden moved: at a6366fb3eb the `solidcore` and `solidcore-ops` suites (no existing test edited) and all 283 goldens units passed.
- **Read:** Patrikalakis, Maekawa and Cho, *Shape Interrogation for Computer Aided Design and Manufacturing*, §11.2.2 to §11.2.4 (planar offset curves, their self-intersections and approximations). §11.2.4 gives the offset of Tiller and Hanson ("Offsets of Two-Dimensional Profiles", IEEE Computer Graphics and Applications 4(9), 1984; read there and in its abstract, not in full): offset each leg, intersect consecutive legs to find the new vertices; self-intersections are removed after, as their own step.
- **How the construction follows it:** the contour offset is that construction with lines and arcs for legs: a line's offset is a line, an arc's is an arc on the same centre with its radius changed, consecutive pieces meet where the offsets cross (the crossing nearest the corner, which is what bounds each end's move to less than half a turn), and loops the moved profile makes are left to the caller to check as a region. Where two pieces meet turning less than `smooth`, they meet on the common normal instead.
