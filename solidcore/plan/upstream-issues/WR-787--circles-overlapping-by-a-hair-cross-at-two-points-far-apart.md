#### WR-787. Circles and parallel cylinders overlapping by a hair cross at two points far apart, while circles that far apart touch
- **Severity:** Wrong result (two holes a hair less than tangent are cut in two lines and points that bound a strip thinner than the tolerance; the cut fails in the merge or comes out not a solid)
- **Area:** Geometry utility / curves (ellipse) / surfaces (cone)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmGeomUtility.cpp`, `smgu_CircleCoPlanarCircleIntersect`: the tangent test `if(dCCDist > dRadius1+dRadius2-dZero || ...)` with `dZero = SM_EFF_ZERO*(1+dRadius1+dRadius2)`, while circles up to `dTolerance` apart pass the `None` test before it and are taken as tangent. Its callers `SmEllipse::IntersectWithEllipse` (coplanar circles), `SmCone::IntersectWithCone` (parallel cylinders) and the curve/surface intersection classifier (osculating circles) use its result as the candidate points and lines, and to decide between tangent and near-tangent.
- **What happens:** two circles that overlap past touching by δ cross at two points `2√(2δ r1 r2 / (r1 ± r2))` apart, far beyond δ, while the circles stay within δ of each other between them. Below the tolerance a gap of δ is one tangent point, but an overlap of δ above about 1e-12 is two crossings: for two radius-2.5 circles overlapping by 2.3e-9 they lie 1.5e-4 apart. Two parallel holes a hair less than tangent are then cut in two wall lines and two points on each rim, and the strip between them, thinner than the tolerance, cannot be placed: `SmMerge::MakeFaceuse` fails (`SM_ERR`), or the result is not a solid.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#15429: a multi-hole whose sketch solver left two drill centres 4.9999999977 apart for 5 mm holes (overlap 2.3e-9). Plain primitives: a plate with a through hole of radius 2.5 cut by a parallel cylinder of radius 2.5 whose axis is 5 − δ away fails for δ from 1e-9 to 1.99e-5 (it builds from 2.01e-5); the two cylinders' union fails at 1e-6.
- **How found:** SolidCraft issue triage (tangency-labs/solidcraft#15429).
- **Confirmed:** no. The pinned C++ is not built on this machine. The port reproduces it on main: `parallel_holes_a_hair_less_than_tangent_cut_as_touching` (`crates/solidcore-ops/tests/boolean.rs`) fails at δ = 1e-9 without the fix.
- **Rust port:** `circle_coplanar_circle_intersect_in_contact` (`crates/solidcore/src/geom/geom_utility/p1.rs`) keeps the near-miss band (`tolerance`) apart from a contact tolerance: an overlap below the contact tolerance (or upstream's zero, when larger) is tangent, at the point midway between the two circles' nearest points on the line of centres, on the side the smaller overlap names; nested circles within the contact tolerance of each other all round keep their crossings. `circle_coplanar_circle_intersect` passes zero and keeps upstream's band. Both take the side from the smaller overlap: upstream takes the inside formula whenever the centres are nearer than the radii's sum by more than `SM_EFF_ZERO·(1 + |c1|)`, so circles overlapping from outside by between that and `SM_EFF_ZERO·(1 + r1 + r2)` got a tangent point off by the smaller radius. On the boolean's path every deciding site passes `contact_tol_3d` (`geom/tol.rs`, the system zone tolerance, 1e-5 by default: the touching point midway lies within the approximation tolerance of both operands; at twice it, through holes overlapping by 1.02e-5 to 1.99e-5 came out a valid solid that kept the second tool whole, as a void): the parallel-cylinder branch of `Cone::intersect_with_cone` when both surfaces bound faces, `Ellipse::intersect_with_ellipse` for an edge against a face's intersection curve (`intersect/curve_class/p9.rs`), and the curve/surface classifier's osculating circles (`classify_curve_surface_xsect_point_with`, `surface/surface/p4.rs`) when it has an edge and a face. Bare curves and surfaces (the classifier's thousandth-of-the-radius band, the ray tracer, the curve/curve guess) keep zero. Deciding only the walls, or the walls and the rims, did not fix the cut: the plate's case still came out not a solid at δ = 1e-9, and with three different tolerances the walls gave two lines where the rims gave one point for δ between them. Tests: `circles_overlapping_below_the_contact_tolerance_touch` (unit), `parallel_holes_a_hair_less_than_tangent_cut_as_touching` (`crates/solidcore-ops/tests/boolean.rs`, through holes to δ = 9.9e-6, and two crossings at 2.5e-5). Not covered: overlaps between the zone and the intersection tolerance (still not a solid), parallel cones (the drill points, met by the marching intersection; from δ = 1e-8 they add an edge or two to a valid solid) and coplanar faces bounded by such circles (refused at δ = 1e-6 on primitives).
