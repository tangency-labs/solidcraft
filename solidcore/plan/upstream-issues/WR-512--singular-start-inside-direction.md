#### WR-512. `SmAdvSurfaceIntersector::ComputeSingularityPoint` starts a curve at a singular point along a direction that need not point into either domain
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Intersection
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmAdvSurfaceIntersector.cpp`, `SmAdvSurfaceIntersector::ComputeSingularityPoint`: the start-point branch with a previous point and at least one inside direction (`:831`-`:854`), after `rTsectPnt.CrvDeriv() = aSingDirections[0]` (`:799`).
- **What happens:** At a start point where the two surfaces are tangent, the function collects the singular directions and the subset that points into both surfaces' domains. With a previous direction it computes `dAngleMin` from `aSingDirections[lInsideIndex[0]]`, the first inside direction, but the output still holds `aSingDirections[0]`, the first direction of all. The later-angle search only assigns the output when a later inside direction is strictly closer. So where the first inside direction is the nearest one, or the only one, the curve starts along `aSingDirections[0]`, a direction that need not point into either domain. In the reported case a round whose end lies at a singular point of its intersection is traced square to its end and comes out as a single point, and the fillet fails with status 1001.
- **Trigger:** A plate 41 1/6 x 21 joined with the corner of a radius 21 cylinder, 10 high, whose arc wall runs out into the back face in a knife edge (SMLib boxes and a cylinder). Round the top front edge R2, then the arc wall's top edge R2: the second round ends on the front round where both are tangent to the top face, a start point with four directions out of it; the first of them runs along the top face square to the round's end. Also a 20 mm cube with an r3 round on its top back edge and the end face's top edge rounded r1 into the round's end arc.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft (issue tangency-labs/solidcore#1522) and read during porting; the fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib#52 ("SSI: a curve starting at a singular point starts along the inside direction nearest the given one"), not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** no. The Rust port's default path reproduces the C++ source line for line at the pin (`3fd5b95`, usd-brep v1.0.1), but no C++ run of either trigger model was made here; the fork's fix is not in the pinned v1.0.1.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.ssi_singular_start_inside_direction` (`SurfaceIntersector::adv_compute_singularity_point`, snapshotted by `SurfaceIntersector::new` when the intersector is built), the output starts at `aSingDirections[lInsideIndex[0]]` before the later-angle search, which still replaces it with a strictly closer later inside direction; the angle computations, their order and every tolerance are unchanged. The only-inside and nearest-first-inside cases are covered by `singular_start_uses_the_only_inside_direction_when_fixed` and `singular_start_uses_the_nearest_first_inside_direction_when_fixed` in `crates/solidcore/src/intersect/adv_surface_intersector.rs`; `singular_start_still_takes_a_later_inside_direction_when_fixed` keeps the later-best update and `singular_start_default_is_unchanged_when_the_first_direction_is_inside` the unchanged default path. The model-level fillet reproductions above were not run.
