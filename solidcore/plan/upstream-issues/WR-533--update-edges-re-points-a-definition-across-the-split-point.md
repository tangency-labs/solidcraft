#### WR-533. `UpdateEdges` re-points a coincident edge definition that runs across the split point to one piece, and the imprint finds no vertex for its ends
- **Severity:** Wrong result (the boolean fails, `SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER` 1006, on valid input)
- **Area:** Booleans
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp`, `SmProtoTopologyManager::UpdateEdges` (def. `:5023`): the loop over edge definitions on the split edge. Only definitions that start or end at the calling proto vertex are trimmed there; the rest are moved whole to the piece under their midpoint.
- **What happens:** When an edge is split, an edge definition coincident with it that runs across the split point is moved, as a whole, to the piece under its midpoint. That piece's vertices are not the definition's ends, and `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint` finds no vertex for them.
- **Trigger:** a block with rounded corners and a 1 x 1 lip swept along its rim, flush with it, united. The sweep's B-spline edge lies on the block's rim arc within tolerance and crosses it twice, 0.05 apart, at the arc's middle. The union splits the arc at those points and fails with 1006.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft (boolean_regression `lip_swept_flush_round_a_corner_joins`). The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@f11d0ac ("boolean: a coincident edge definition across a split point is split with its edge"), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run was made here. The Rust port's `update_edges` follows the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`).
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.boolean_splits_definition_across_split`, `ProtoTopologyManager::update_edges` handles a definition on the split edge whose ends both lie farther than the cross-section tolerance from the split point. If the split point drops onto the definition's curve within that tolerance and strictly inside its interval, the definition's proto edge is split there (`proto_edge_split`). The upper part's definitions are appended to the list and classified in their turn. `update_edges_splits_a_definition_across_the_split_point` (in `topology/proto_topology/p2.rs`) checks that, with the fix, each part ends up on its own piece, and that without it the whole definition stays on one edge.
