#### WR-613. A fillet corner patch takes the mean twist where a face side meets a blend side, so it crosses the face beside that side
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmBSplineSurfaceNLib.cpp:4092`, `sm_CreateCoonsSrf` (def. `:3813`), the four corner twists `T00`, `T10`, `T11`, `T01`. Reached from `SmFilletCorner::CreateCornerPatch` (`source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp:1955`) through `CreateCornerBlend` and `CreateCoonsPatch`.
- **What happens:** the bicubic Coons patch sets each corner's twist to the mean of the two cross-boundary derivative curves' twists there. A side meets its cross-boundary derivative only when the corner twists are the other sides' own. At a fillet corner where one side lies on a face of the body and the other on a round, the two twists differ, because each twist's part along the normal is its own surface's curvature on the two boundary tangents. The mean tilts the patch's cross derivative off the face's tangent plane along that side, so the patch, tangent to the face at the side, crosses it just inside.
- **Trigger:** a bar running tangent into a cylinder, its top meeting the wall in a concave arc. The arc and the bar's front top edge are rounded together at r (one fillet call, a 3x2 mixed-convexity corner). The corner patch stands past the front face plane by 0.0193 / 0.0374 / 0.0602 / 0.0900 / 0.128 / 0.174 / 0.230 at r = 1..7 (SolidCraft issue #1836). At that corner the round side's twist is (0, 5.657, 0) and the wall side's 0; the mean tilts the cross derivative by up to 3.9 degrees.
- **How found:** SolidCraft issue #1836 (jyviko). The port's source is the issue's diff of fork commit tangency-labs/smlib@7fd9fb0, which is not yet published (on no fork ref, so not yet merged in the fork's main).
- **Confirmed:** by the port's unit test below (the issue's part is not reproduced). By default (pinned behaviour) the corners take the mean twist.
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@7fd9fb0. `CreateCornerPatch` marks the ordered edges that have an original face (`lFaceSides`, bit k for edge k). `CreateCornerBlend` maps them to the Coons order (1 left, 2 right, 4 bottom, 8 top) and `CreateCoonsPatch` passes them to `sm_CreateCoonsSrf`. There `sm_FaceSideTwist` gives a corner between exactly one face side and a blend side the blend side's twist. Corners between two face sides or two blend sides keep the mean. The issue reports a second, smaller crossing at r 5..7 (0.0016 to 0.034), from the cross derivative's size on the convex round's side. It is not fixed here.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. `Kernel::fixes.corner_patch_face_side_twist` sets the marks in `blend::fillet_corner::p3::FilletCorner::create_corner_patch_default_with`. They pass through `BSplineSurface::create_corner_blend_on_faces` and `BSplineSurface::create_coons_patch_on_faces` to `nurbs::b_spline_surface_n_lib::p1::create_coons_srf` (`face_side_twist`). Without the flag the marks are 0, so every caller builds upstream's patch.
- **Tests:**
  - `nurbs::b_spline_surface_n_lib::p1::tests::wr_613_face_sides_meet_their_cross_derivative`: a unit square whose left and right sides are a plane's (no twist) and whose bottom and top are a blend's (twist (0, 0, 1)). With the plane's sides marked, the patch's u derivative along them matches their cross derivative to 1e-12. With no marks it misses by 0.048.
  - No whole-fillet test: the issue's part is not reproduced here. WR-593's peg with the concave arc and the front top edge rounded fails with status 1001 under every combination of the nearby fillet opt-ins, and #1475's bar and boss (built with `fillet_3x2_mixed_concave_side_edge`) gives the same body with the flag on or off.
- **Difftest:** no change on the default path.
