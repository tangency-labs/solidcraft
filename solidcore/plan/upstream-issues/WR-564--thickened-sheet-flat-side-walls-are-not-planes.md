#### WR-564. Thickening a sheet builds its flat side walls as ruled surfaces, so a tube's end rings are not planes
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Offsets and shells
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp`, `SmOffsetExecutive::ShellBrep` (def. `:191`), sheet branch (`bCreateOffsetSolid && !bInputIsManifoldSolid`, `:285`). The walls come from `BuildSideWallOfEdgeuse` (def. `:2915`), which calls `SmBrep::CreateEdgeEdgeBlend`, and nothing after that puts a flat wall onto its plane.
- **What happens:** each side wall is the ruled surface between a lamina edge and its offset. Where the edge and its offset lie in one plane the wall is flat, but it is not an `SmPlane`. Between a circle and its offset about the same axis, the wall is an `SmSurfOfRevolution` of the line between them. Between a line and an offset of a different length, it is a bilinear patch. Any caller that reads the body sees a curved surface where a plane is. The volume is still correct.
- **Trigger:** one `SmCylinder` face (r 10, 20 high, from `CreateFaceFromSurface`) thickened by `ShellBrep` with `bCreateOffsetSolid`, by +2 or -2. The result has 4 faces: two cylinders (r 12 and r 10, or r 10 and r 8) and two `SmSurfOfRevolution` end rings.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft (tangency-labs/solidcore#1599). The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib#62 (commit d384e86, "thicken: a flat side wall is on its plane, a cylinder's end rings among them"), not merged upstream. It extends the fork's #978 change (5448b9a), which put a wall onto its plane only when the wall was about to merge with a neighbour.
- **Confirmed:** yes. The C++ build gives 4 faces with no plane among them, and so does the Rust default: cases `static_shell_thicken_cylinder_sheet_*` of `offset.offset_executive_p2` (fixture `thicken_cylinder_sheet.json`).
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.thicken_planar_side_walls`, `ShellBrep`'s sheet branch puts every wall that is planar (`IsPlanar(SM_EFF_ZERO)`) onto an exact plane, visiting walls in `GetFaces` order. This is the fork's `sm_PutPlanarFaceOnItsPlane`: `ReplaceSurface` remakes the UV trims. Then each edge of the face that was a seam of its old surface (`SmEdge::IsSeam`, checked before the replacement) is deleted with `DeleteEdge`. On the plane that seam would be a slit through the ring, so deleting it splits the ring's one loop into its outer and inner circles. Coplanar walls are still merged only with `ShellOptions::merge_sheet_side_walls` (#978). `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1599_thicken_planar_side_walls.rs` thickens the cylinder sheet by +2 and -2. With the flag on, each result is 2 `SmPlane` rings with 2 loops each plus 2 cylinders, it is a manifold solid, and its volume is π(R² − r²)·20 (880π and 720π). With the flag off, the result is the upstream one.
