#### WR-340. `SmOffsetExecutive::ShellBrep` leaves a thickened sheet's coplanar side walls as separate faces
- **Severity:** Wrong result (extra faces; topology only, the body is valid)
- **Area:** Offset
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp`, `SmOffsetExecutive::ShellBrep`, the sheet branch (`bCreateOffsetSolid` on a non-manifold input): `MergeBrep` of the sheet into the offset, then `StitchFaces`.
- **What happens:** the side walls are built one per lamina edge (`BuildSideWallOfEdgeuse`). Where two consecutive lamina edges and their offsets lie on one line, their walls are two faces on one plane, split along the edge from the sheet's vertex to its offset. SMLib's other operations remove the topological edges they make (booleans in `SmMerge::BooleanPostProcess`, fillets, a solid's shell through its boolean difference); the sheet branch has nothing equivalent.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft rig shell/5326, an L sheet thickened 2 either way: 10 faces where 8 are expected. Two squares side by side thickened 1: 10 faces where 8 are expected.
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig on the SMLib fork (tangency-labs/solidcore#978).
- **Confirmed:** on the fork's C++ build (the L sheet gives 8 faces, volumes 1920 and 2080 to 1e-12; a two-face sheet keeps its split: 8 faces, volume 400).
- **Fix (fork commit "SMLib: a thickened sheet's coplanar side walls are one face"):** after the stitch, every topological edge between two walls (the non-sheet faces along the sheet's boundary, tracked through `MergeBrep`'s sheet-to-copy map) is removed with `RemoveTopologicalEdgesAndVertices`, keeping the other topological edges and every topological vertex. Each wall it bounds first goes onto an `SmPlane` (`sm_PutPlanarFaceOnItsPlane`): a trapezoid wall is a bilinear patch, and merging it as such gave fitted trims (area 96.00004).
- **Rust port:** opt-in `ShellOptions::merge_sheet_side_walls` (default `false`, upstream behaviour), in `OffsetExecutive::shell_brep` (`merge_sheet_side_walls`, `put_planar_face_on_its_plane` in `crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p2.rs`). Test `issue_978_thickened_sheet_coplanar_walls_are_one_face`. No difftest case compares the opt-in path (the `offset.offset_executive_p2` runner passes `false`). Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#978.
