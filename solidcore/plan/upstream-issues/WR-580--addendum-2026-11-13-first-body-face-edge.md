Updates: WR-580

#### WR-580 (addendum 2026-11-13): the first body's face needs the same reuse

- **What happens:** `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint`'s Brep1 branch for a definition whose first source is a face (`source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp:1436`, `MakeEdgeInFace` at `:1462`) makes a new edge in that face in the same way as the Brep2 branch (`:1529`, `:1541`), also where the curve runs along an edge the face already has.
- **Trigger:** a bar joined to a round hub, a Union with the hub first (models in mm, tolerance 2e-5). The hub is a cylinder of radius 11; the bar's end is concave on a cylinder 2.5e-5 smaller whose axis is 3.14e-5 off, so over the bar's width (12 or 17) the two cylinders stay within 1e-5 of each other and the merge takes the two faces as coincident; the two top faces lie in one plane. Where the bar's end arc runs inside the hub's top face, within tolerance of the hub's circle, the imprint makes a second hub edge beside the circle. The hub's circle piece stays unmated, and the coincident-surface repair (`MergeCoincidentSurfaces`, `merge_face_edges` in `boolean/topology_intersector/p3.rs`) copies it into the bar's cylinder face: a loop of zero area (`SmTrimmingTools` tests 3 and 4) and a body that is not a manifold solid.
- **Rust port:** with `Kernel::fixes.imprint_reuses_coincident_face_edge`, `ProtoTopologyManager::imprint_edge_definition` asks for a coincident edge of the face in both branches (`ProtoTopologyManager::coincident_face_edge`); an edge found for the first body's face becomes the definition's Brep1 edge and no edge is made.
- **Tests:** `a_bar_joined_to_a_hub_on_nearly_the_same_cylinder_is_one_solid` (SolidCraft, `crates/smlib/src/core/boolean_tests.rs`): at both widths one valid manifold solid of 7 faces whose volume is the closed form; without the reuse in the first body the same Union fails `check` with the zero-area loop.
