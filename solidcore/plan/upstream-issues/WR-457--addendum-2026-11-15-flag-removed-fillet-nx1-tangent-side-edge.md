Updates: WR-457

#### WR-457 (update, 2026-11-15). `fillet_nx1_tangent_side_edge` is removed

`FilletCorner::make_nx1_corner_topology` now always skips a side edge whose faces meet tangentially at the corner's vertex (and passes, once, the filleted edge's opposite-convexity G1 continuation, WR-499), and `CalcEdgeOnExtendedEdge` always intersects the side face with the end face's extension when the side face is not one of the edge's own faces, as tangency-labs/smlib@ba7f68e and @420e47d. The fix is unconditional and the flag is gone; SolidCraft already ran with it on. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::fillet_nx1_tangent_side_edge`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json`, `issue_2260`'s flag list and the goldens name map, the fixture opt-in of `fixtures/solidcraft/3369-fillet-edge-on-tangent-seam.json`, the flag-off branches, and every test setting of it.

- **Results:** 30 `blend.fillet_executive_p3` rows go from upstream's status 1001 to a built body: the box unions of `stubs_round3.json` (`stubs3-overhang-union-nx1-multi-neighbour-end-face-*`, `stubs3-union-overhang-*`) and two of `corner_3x2.json` (`corner3x2-union-overhang-corner-edges6-12-r1`, `-6-13-r1`). Each is certified by the goldens test `blend_fillet_executive_p3::end_to_end::wr457_certificates`: check_brep Level 2 clean, one closed manifold solid, and the volume enclosed (`enclose_volume`) in an interval no wider than `1e-7 * 2600`; the golden row records that body (its counts, and a volume within 1e-4 of the enclosure). For the 21 single-round cases the enclosure holds the closed form (the union's volume, changed by `r^2 (1 - pi/4)` along the edge's length), and for the 4 cases with two concave rounds meeting at a convex corner it holds `2600 + A L + (pi/2) A d` (`A = r^2 (1 - pi/4)`, `d = r (10 - 3 pi) / (12 - 3 pi)`, Pappus for the corner's quarter turn). The other 5 two-edge cases, which mix a convex and a concave round, are certified by validity and the enclosure only. The kernel rig runs with SolidCraft's preset, where the flag was already on.
- **Tests:** `tests/issue_418_fillet_edge_on_tangent_seam.rs`, `tests/issue_1474_nx1_corner_tangent_round_end.rs`, `tests/wr_468_overhanging_boss_fork_parity.rs`, `tests/wr_593_nx1_tangent_corner_edge.rs`. Removed with the flag: issue 418's refusal check and off/on comparison (the off-seam control, now `off_seam_round_holds_its_closed_form`, asserts a manifold solid and its closed-form volume to 1e-3), and issue 1474's status-1001 check. wr_593's `residue_round_fails_by_default`, which pinned the status-1001 refusal without the skip, becomes `residue_round_builds_by_default`: the round builds, check_brep Level 2 is clean, the body is one closed manifold solid, its volume enclosure lies between the peg's volume and the round section's full half-turn sweep, and it holds the exact-geometry round's volume to 1e-6.
