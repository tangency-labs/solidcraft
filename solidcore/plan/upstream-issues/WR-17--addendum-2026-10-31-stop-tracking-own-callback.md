#### WR-17 (update, 2026-10-31). Opt-in own-callback check in `SmTrackTopologyChanges::StopTracking`

- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmTrackTopologyChanges.cpp:580`-`595` (`SmTrackTopologyChanges::StopTracking`, def. `:557`; `:512` at the older `c9c5979` pin) vs `source/SMLib/inc/SmTrackTopologyChanges.h:385` (`~SmTrackTopologyChanges`) and `SmTrackTopologyChanges::IsTracking` (`:542`-`550`).
- **Defect:** `StopTracking` checks `if (m_pLastNotifyCallback == NULL)` instead of `if (IsTracking())` (which `~SmTrackTopologyChanges` uses). Because `m_pLastNotifyCallback` starts as `NULL` and is never updated after `StartTracking`, `m_pLastNotifyCallback == NULL` holds in two non-tracking states:
  1. A tracker constructed with `bStartNow = FALSE` that has never called `StartTracking`.
  2. A tracker that started on an empty context (`m_pLastNotifyCallback == NULL`) whose callback was subsequently replaced in `SmContext::m_pSysNotifyCallback` by another caller before `StopTracking` ran.
  In both states `StopTracking` wipes out the foreign callback (`SetSysNotifyCallback(NULL)`) and runs post-edit `RecordDependencies` on a tracker that was not observing the completed edit.
- **Fix (`Kernel::fixes.stop_tracking_checks_own_callback`, #2149):** Default `false` preserves pinned v1.0.1 C++ behaviour in `TrackTopologyChanges::new`, `stop_tracking`, and `stop_tracking_with_brep` (including difftest fixture `track_stop_clears_foreign` in `topology.topology`). When enabled — via `TrackTopologyChanges::new_with_kernel(k, ...)` or `TrackTopologyChanges::set_stop_checks_own_callback(true)` — `stop_tracking` clears `Context::sys_notify_callback` only when `self.is_tracking(...)` holds, and `stop_tracking_with_brep` captures `was_tracking = self.is_tracking(context.as_deref())` before `stop_tracking` and runs `TopologyChangeLists::record_dependencies` only when `was_tracking` is `true`.
- **Wired callers:** `heal::heal_data::p3::tracked_edit` constructs its edit tracker with `TrackTopologyChanges::new_with_kernel(k, ...)`, and `heal::heal_data::p3::capture_changes` likewise checks `callback.tracker() == 1` before clearing `k.context`'s system notify callback when `k.fixes.stop_tracking_checks_own_callback` is on.
- **Regression tests (`topology::topology::tests`):**
  - `wr17_unstarted_tracker_preserves_foreign_callback`: tracker A constructed with `start_now = false` wipes out tracker B's callback on `stop_tracking` under default settings, whereas opting in leaves B's callback (and custom `NotifyCallback`s) installed and receiving notifications across both `stop_tracking` and `stop_tracking_with_brep`.
  - `wr17_replaced_callback_preserved_on_stop`: tracker A starts on an empty context, records an `AddToBrep` vertex event, and is replaced by a foreign callback (both a custom `NotifyCallback` and another `TopologyChangeCallback`); default `stop_tracking_with_brep` clears the foreign callback and expands connected edge/face dependencies on A, whereas opting in (via `new_with_kernel` and `set_stop_checks_own_callback`) preserves the foreign callback and skips post-edit `record_dependencies` on A.
  - `wr17_active_tracker_still_clears_and_records_dependencies`: under `stop_tracking_checks_own_callback = true`, an active tracker clears its own callback and records connected edge/face dependencies identically to upstream, and nested trackers (`last_notify_callback == true`) still leave the outer tracker active under both default and opt-in modes.
  - `wr17_flag_lookup`: verifies `Kernel::default().fixes.stop_tracking_checks_own_callback == false`, `UpstreamFixes::flag_mut`, and `UpstreamFixes::flags`.
