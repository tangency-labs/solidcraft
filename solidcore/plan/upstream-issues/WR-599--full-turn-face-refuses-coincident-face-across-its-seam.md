#### WR-599. A full-turn face does not take in a coincident face that lies across its seam
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Booleans
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmSurface.cpp`, `SmSurface::CoverCoincidentSurface` (def. `:7964`): the boundary walk returns as soon as `sm_CrossedSeamOrPole` (def. `:7780`) reports a crossing (`:8228`). `source/SMLib/src/SmMerge.cpp`, `SmMerge::BooleanPostProcess` (def. `:827`), which merges the two faces after `DeleteEdge` (`:1143`) and never completes the merged face's seam.
- **What happens:** a cover fails whenever the boundary walk of the covered face steps across this surface's seam, also when this surface's domain already closes on itself in that direction. The two coincident faces stay apart with the edge between them. A later fillet meeting that edge finds a 4-edge vertex SMLib does not fillet (status 1001).
- **Trigger:** the SolidCraft test-peg part. A bar 40 x 16 x 10 with a half-round end of radius 8 (a linear sweep of two lines, the half arc from -90 to 90 degrees and a line), and a cylinder r 8 x 10 standing on its top about the half-round's axis, joined. The cylinder's wall is one full-turn face whose seam (angle 0) runs down the middle of the half-round, which lies on the same cylinder. The union keeps the two walls apart: 8 faces.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft. The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@16bf1cb ("merge: a full-turn face takes in a coincident face across its seam, the seam run on through it"), not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** yes, in the Rust port, which matches upstream by default: the union gives 8 faces with no vertex on the bar's seam line.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.cover_wraps_across_closed_seam`, `CoverCoincidentSurface` first tests whether this surface is closed in u and in v over its natural domain (`IsClosed`). A step that `sm_CrossedSeamOrPole` flags is a failure only if it jumps more than 0.4 of the domain in a direction that is not closed, or ends at a pole. Otherwise the crossed direction is noted, and after the union of the domains the update domain and the covered domain keep this surface's original extent in that direction (its period). `BooleanPostProcess` then runs `HasSeamProblem` on each merged face after its UV domain is set. When a seam is crossed or missing, it runs `SplitAtSeam` with the optional arguments left out, which adds the missing piece of the seam and splits the edges that cross it. `crates/solidcore/tests/fork_16bf1cb_full_turn_cover_across_seam.rs` builds the peg. With the flag on it has 7 faces, the bar's bottom arc is split at (40, 0, 0) by the seam, and the volume is unchanged. With the flag off the result is the upstream one: 8 faces and no such vertex.
