Updates: WR-450, WR-361

#### WR-450 (update, 2026-11-23). `analytic_copy_keeps_parameterization` is removed

`BSplineSurface::copy_and_add_analytics` now always keeps an exact copy of the B-spline where its recognised analytic surface does not give the B-spline's points at the same parameters (the 7 x 7 grid check, the fork's `sm_SameParameterization`). Where it does, the analytic is kept as before. The check evaluates a clone of the analytic, so the analytic it returns is the one recognised, unevaluated, as on main. An analytic such as a surface of extrusion builds its B-spline on first evaluation, and that alone moved a row. The flag is gone, and so is WR-361's restaging in `MakeEdgeInFaceWithVU`: it dropped all of the new face's UV trims whenever the copy was of another kind. In its place, `drop_trims_off_domain` drops only the trims whose certified box over the edge's interval leaves the source surface's natural domain. The copy keeps the source's parameterisation there only, and the dropped trims are made again lazily. A seam split of a cone had kept a trim reaching u = 1.125 on [0, 1], 0.765 off its edge; regression test `wr450_rows::seam_cone_split_drops_trims_off_the_domain`. A kept trim inside the domain still rests on the copy check's 7 x 7 grid for input outside the goldens. That residual is open; see the last item. SolidCraft had no preset line or `fixes.json` entry for it. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::analytic_copy_keeps_parameterization`, its doc, `drop_face_trims` in `topology/brep/p9.rs`, the WR-493 refit seam's refusal of the flag and every test setting of it.

- **check_brep, #17790:** the Test #4 rise from 8 to 39 reported there no longer reproduces on main: later edge and vertex tolerance work (an edge's tolerance covers its edge/face gap, #18325's exact foot, #19295) removed it. With the flag removed, the `corpus.parity` check counts of `blade_hub_1/2/3`, `cliff_1/2`, `sphere_plane` and `revolution_mold` stay 0. `fillet_ts` goes from 4 to 1 and the `boolean_trees` `brep[5]` count goes from 5 to 0.
- **Results:** 402 golden rows in 38 units move. No row changes its error, deferred status or `topology` field. 312 rows change only float values. 90 also change integer fields (`ints`), per unit: `brep_p10` 30, `brep_p6` 24, `api_files` 14, `fillet_executive_p3` 8 (`result_topology`), `feature_executive` 5 (graph digests), `assert_valid` 3 (the max-gap face), `brep_cutting` 2, `corpus.parity` 2 (check counts), `brep_local_operation` 1 and `fillet_executive_p1` 1. `goldens/src/wr450_rows.rs` reruns each of them with main's two choices put back through the test-only replay seam (`Seam::AnalyticCopy`, `Seam::SplitTrims`, feature `wr57-replay`). Each then agrees with main's golden line under the goldens' own rule (`golden::diff` with the unit's floors; the committed `wr450_rows/main.jsonl` holds main's lines, the same at 1b210ef4b0, e5199bcb17 and 97c3343fb9). At least one call's choice differs from main's, and each run as is gives its committed golden line. Classes: 359 `copy` (only exact copies), 10 `copy+trims` (an exact copy on a split face whose trims main also dropped), 33 `trims` (the analytic is kept and the new face keeps its trims).
- **Tests:** `nurbs::b_spline_surface::p2::tests::rational_half_cylinder_copy_keeps_parameterization` (the recognised analytic is off by more than 1e-3; the copy is the exact surface); `tests/wr_450_box_minus_rational_cylinder.rs` (the record's boolean is valid on a default kernel); the `wr450_rows` replay.
- **Kept trims, certified:** `wr450_rows::kept_trims_are_certified` takes every UV trim that the `trims` and `copy+trims` rows' B-reps hold and main's do not. For each, it proves a bound on the distance between the edge and the trim's image on the committed face surface, over the whole edge interval, within the edge's tolerance. The bounds come from `certify::trim_tolerance`: the kernel's `trim_image_gap_bound`, plus `crv_on_surf_trim_gap_bound` and `clamped_trim_gap_bound`, in outward-rounded intervals. So no moved row rests on the grid. On cone and cylinder faces, the residual between the analytic's evaluation and its net is not enclosed (#20643, pre-existing).
- **Open:** in library code, whether a trim inside the domain is kept is still decided by the 7 x 7 grid (`same_parameterization`), so for general input it is a sampled acceptance. Main dropped every such trim instead. Gating each kept trim on a proved bound (`trim_image_gap_bound` within the edge's tolerance, else drop) is the follow-up.

Its edges 19 and 25 carry gaps of 4.3e-4 and 7.3e-5; every other edge has the same gap as in the file. Why the strips do not tighten is unproved. Every face is on its model surface within 5.1e-13.
