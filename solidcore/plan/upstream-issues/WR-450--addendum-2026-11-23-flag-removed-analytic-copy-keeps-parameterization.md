Updates: WR-450, WR-361

#### WR-450 (update, 2026-11-23). `analytic_copy_keeps_parameterization` is removed

`BSplineSurface::copy_and_add_analytics` now always keeps an exact copy of the B-spline where its recognised analytic surface does not give the B-spline's points at the same parameters (the 7 x 7 grid check, the fork's `sm_SameParameterization`). Where it does, the analytic is kept as before. The check evaluates a clone of the analytic, so the analytic it returns is the one recognised, unevaluated, as on main. An analytic such as a surface of extrusion builds its B-spline on first evaluation, and that alone moved a row. The flag is gone, and so is WR-361's restaging in `MakeEdgeInFaceWithVU`: it dropped the new face's UV trims whenever the copy was of another kind, which only the flag-off path needed. SolidCraft had no preset line or `fixes.json` entry for it. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::analytic_copy_keeps_parameterization`, its doc, `drop_face_trims` in `topology/brep/p9.rs`, the WR-493 refit seam's refusal of the flag and every test setting of it.

- **check_brep, #17790:** the Test #4 rise from 8 to 39 reported there no longer reproduces on main: later edge and vertex tolerance work (an edge's tolerance covers its edge/face gap, #18325's exact foot, #19295) removed it. With the flag removed, the `corpus.parity` check counts of `blade_hub_1/2/3`, `cliff_1/2`, `sphere_plane` and `revolution_mold` stay 0. `fillet_ts` goes from 4 to 1 and the `boolean_trees` `brep[5]` count goes from 5 to 0.
- **Results:** 402 golden rows in 38 units move. No row changes its error, deferred status or `topology` field. 312 rows change only float values. 90 also change integer fields (`ints`), per unit: `brep_p10` 30, `brep_p6` 24, `api_files` 14, `fillet_executive_p3` 8 (`result_topology`), `feature_executive` 5 (graph digests), `assert_valid` 3 (the max-gap face), `brep_cutting` 2, `corpus.parity` 2 (check counts), `brep_local_operation` 1 and `fillet_executive_p1` 1. `goldens/src/wr450_rows.rs` reruns each of them with main's two choices put back through the test-only replay seam (`Seam::AnalyticCopy`, `Seam::SplitTrims`, feature `wr57-replay`). Each then agrees with main's golden line under the goldens' own rule (`golden::diff` with the unit's floors; the committed `wr450_rows/main.jsonl` holds main's lines, the same at 1b210ef4b0 and e5199bcb17). At least one call's choice differs from main's, and each run as is gives its committed golden line. Classes: 359 `copy` (only exact copies), 10 `copy+trims` (an exact copy on a split face whose trims main also dropped), 33 `trims` (the analytic is kept and the new face keeps its trims).
- **Tests:** `nurbs::b_spline_surface::p2::tests::rational_half_cylinder_copy_keeps_parameterization` (the recognised analytic is off by more than 1e-3; the copy is the exact surface); `tests/wr_450_box_minus_rational_cylinder.rs` (the record's boolean is valid on a default kernel); the `wr450_rows` replay.
