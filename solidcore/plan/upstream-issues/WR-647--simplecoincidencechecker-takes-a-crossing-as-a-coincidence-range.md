#### WR-647. `SimpleCoincidenceChecker` takes two curves that cross at a grazing angle as a coincidence range
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a union is non-manifold, or comes back with no solid region and volume 0, and is reported as success)
- **Area:** Curve/curve intersection (Booleans)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmCurve.cpp`, `SmCurve::SimpleCoincidenceChecker` (the pre `SM_TOLERANT_XSECT` version, def. `:19417`), the coincidence return after `CurveMaxDistanceBetween` (`:19690`-`:19728`); reached from the span pairs of `SmCCIGlobalSolver`.
- **What happens:** the checker drops the two span pairs' ends onto the other curve. When the drops lie within tolerance and the largest gap between them is under tolerance, it returns a `SM_ST_RANGE_OF_VALUES` coincidence between the first and last drops. It does not check which side of each other the curves are on. Two equal circles a little apart graze within tolerance over a stretch, and they cross inside it. The range ends wherever the subdivision cut the spans, and the range becomes a tiny edge in the boolean.
- **Trigger:** the union of a ring (a capped linear sweep of the circle r 5 about (0, 10, 0), z 0..2, minus the same of r 3) with a half-annulus prism (a capped linear sweep of the outline r 4..5 about (e, 10, 2) in y >= 10, 18 long along z). At e = 1e-4 the result is not manifold (SmTrimmingTools #0/#2/#3/#4: a zero-area loop on a 5e-5 edge shared by four faces). At e = 1e-3 it has volume 0 (vertex/face gaps of 5e-4). The union is valid at e = 0 and 0.01. The same operands built as cylinder and box booleans (the #480 test) do not reach this path.
- **How found:** tangency-labs/smlib@c2e3d9d (SolidCraft rig case booleans/2928, reduced); tangency-labs/solidcore#1979. Reproduced in the port on origin/main. A probe showed the new branch is taken once per failing offset, at a span pair from parameter 0.5 to 0.50007 (1e-3) and 0.500007 (1e-4).
- **Confirmed:** yes, by the fork on the C++ build (before its fix: e = 1e-4 non-manifold, e = 1e-3 not a solid; after: valid at 0, 1e-4, 1e-3 and 0.01, V 354.99997). In the port, the default reproduces the same failures.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. The opt-in `Kernel::fixes.simple_coincidence_crossing_is_point` ports the fork's fix in `Curve::simple_coincidence_checker` (`curve/curve/p6.rs`). Where both end separations are longer than `SM_EFF_ZERO * (1 + max end coordinate)` and point to opposite sides (negative dot product), it returns the one crossing `local_curve_intersect` finds in the range. The search starts where the separation, taken as linear between the ends, vanishes. It returns `SM_ERR` when no crossing is found. `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1979_ring_lip_crossing_not_coincidence.rs` checks both settings.
