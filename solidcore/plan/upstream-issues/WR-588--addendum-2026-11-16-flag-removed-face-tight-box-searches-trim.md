#### WR-588 (update, 2026-11-16). `face_tight_box_searches_trim` is removed

`SmFace::CalculateTightBoundingBox` (`query::face::p3::calculate_tight_bounding_box_into`) now always searches a trimmed face's own extremes inside its trim (`face_directed_max`, with WR-651's knot division and tangency-labs/smlib@95bf434's face-only points). The copy of the surface's global solve over the face's domain is gone, and so is the flag. Removed: the field, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260_gn_pipe_refined_range_end.rs`, and every test setting of it. The tests' upstream halves (the global-solve box of a trimmed face, which missed the face's own peak) are dropped.

One defect of the search is fixed with it, because it is the only path now. The search passes over a monotone cell because the face's edges hold its extreme. A face with no edge (made over a UV domain smaller than the tolerance, or trimmed by a lone vertex) breaks that premise. With no vertex either, the box of its edges was empty, and its inverted bounds came back as points: the face boxed to ±1e20 on every axis. A planar face returned that empty box before the search. Such a face's box is now its domain's bound, unioned with any vertex, taken as the search bounds a cell it leaves unsolved: the control-point hull of the subpatch plus its rounding, else the loose box (tests `tight_box_of_a_face_without_edges_is_its_surfaces_box`, `tight_box_of_a_planar_face_without_edges_is_its_planes_box`).

- **Results:** 28 golden rows move, in `query.face_p3` (21), `query.face_p1` (4) and `query.brep` (3). Every box that grows reaches a face point the old box missed. A face that had no edges now gets its subpatch's hull instead of an empty box. The rest are rounding-scale or come from points outside the face. The PR that removes the flag has the row-by-row table.
- **Tests:** `query::face::p3` (`tight_box_finds_a_trimmed_faces_peak_when_the_surface_peaks_outside`, `tight_box_with_the_peak_inside_the_trim_agrees_with_upstream`, `tight_box_of_an_analytic_cylinder_face_is_the_same_with_the_search`, `tight_box_of_a_face_without_edges_is_its_surfaces_box`), `issue_1799_tight_box_divides_at_knots.rs`, `fork_95bf434_rounded_corner_tight_box.rs`.
