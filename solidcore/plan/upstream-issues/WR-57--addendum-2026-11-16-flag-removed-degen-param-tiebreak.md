Updates: WR-57

#### WR-57 (update, 2026-11-16). `degen_param_tiebreak_uses_actual_param` is removed; the guesses are parameters, the input's first

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246). Turned on alone, the flag's mapped tie-break (`s_ivl.evaluate(f)`) turned goldens `surface.surface_p7` gen0435 from converged into `SM_ERR`: it picked sample 0, and upstream starts the Newton solve at `sSIvl.Evaluate(f)`, which for `f = 0` is the domain boundary, there a collapsed corner (`|S_v|` 3.3e-11) where the tangent across the singular boundary is undefined. Upstream measures each guess at the pulled-in sample `sample_domain.evaluate(f)` (`SmSurface.cpp:6873`-`:6900`) but compares, starts and falls back at a different point.

`Surface::find_degen_param_for_direction` (`surface/surface/p7.rs`) now works with the parameters where the alignment is measured: the near-equal tie-break compares them with `dS`, both Newton solves start at them, and an unconverged answer is the guess itself. The input parameter `dS` is the first near-best guess when it lies in the interval and its tangent is defined, since of nearly equal guesses the one nearest the input is kept and the input is nearest itself. That part is needed: where the alignment is the same over a stretch of parameters, the Newton solve stops at its start. With only the samples as guesses, a pole inversion there returns a sample rather than the input, even when the input is already an answer. Goldens `topology.brep_mirror` mat0076 then hung: each inversion of its drop curve jumped 0.86 in `v`, so every span failed and was subdivided down to the 1/8000 floor. The solve finds where the alignment is stationary, or a kink in it, so a new start can end at a minimum or past a kink downhill: from the samples, goldens `surface.surface_p7` gen2112 moved from a maximum (alignment 0.463) to a minimum (0.335), and gen0415 converged to an alignment of -0.29. A solve whose answer is aligned worse than its start by more than the function's near-equal tolerance `fTol` (2/50), or whose tangent is undefined there, now counts as not converged; upstream accepts any answer. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::degen_param_tiebreak_uses_actual_param`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, and the flag test.

- **Tests:** `wr57_sphere_pole_seam_tiebreak_on_non_unit_and_unit_domains` and `wr57_nurbs_degenerate_patch_shifted_domains_and_both_singular_directions` keep their fixed-path assertions on the default kernel; their upstream-path halves are removed. The sphere's angular-domain roots are checked against the solve's stopping test, `|F| < 1e-9` with the closed form `F(u) = -sin(u w) w`. `wr57_newton_starts_at_the_sample_not_the_collapsed_corner` is gen0435's case: it fails (not converged) with the solve started at `s_ivl.evaluate(f)`. `wr57_plateau_keeps_the_input_parameter` checks that an input on gen0435's plateau comes back unchanged, which fails without the input guess.
- **Results:** the pull request's inventory lists every moved goldens row with its class and evidence.
