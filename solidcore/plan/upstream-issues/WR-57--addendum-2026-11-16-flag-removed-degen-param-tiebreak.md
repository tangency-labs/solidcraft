Updates: WR-57

#### WR-57 (update, 2026-11-16). `degen_param_tiebreak_uses_actual_param` is removed; the guesses are parameters, the input's first

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246). Turned on alone, the flag's mapped tie-break (`s_ivl.evaluate(f)`) turned goldens `surface.surface_p7` gen0435 from converged into `SM_ERR`: it picked sample 0, and upstream starts the Newton solve at `sSIvl.Evaluate(f)`, which for `f = 0` is the domain boundary, there a collapsed corner (`|S_v|` 3.3e-11) where the tangent across the singular boundary is undefined. Upstream measures each guess at the pulled-in sample `sample_domain.evaluate(f)` (`SmSurface.cpp:6873`-`:6900`) but compares, starts and falls back at a different point.

`Surface::find_degen_param_for_direction` (`surface/surface/p7.rs`) now works with the parameters where the alignment is measured: the near-equal tie-break compares them with `dS`, both Newton solves start at them, and an unconverged answer is the guess itself. The input parameter `dS` is the first near-best guess when it lies in the interval and its tangent is defined, since of nearly equal guesses the one nearest the input is kept and the input is nearest itself. That part is needed: where the alignment is the same over a stretch of parameters, the Newton solve stops at its start. With only the samples as guesses, a pole inversion there returns a sample rather than the input, even when the input is already an answer. Goldens `topology.brep_mirror` mat0076 then hung: each inversion of its drop curve jumped 0.86 in `v`, so every span failed and was subdivided down to the 1/8000 floor. The solve finds where the alignment is stationary, or a kink in it, so a new start can end at a minimum or past a kink downhill: from the samples, goldens `surface.surface_p7` gen2112 moved from a maximum (alignment 0.463) to a minimum (0.335), and gen0415 converged to an alignment of -0.29. An answer now counts as converged only where it is a local maximum of the alignment on the closed interval, checked with the solve's own `F = A'` and `F' = A''`: an end where the alignment grows outward, a stationary point (`|F| < tol`) with `F' <= SM_EFF_ZERO`, polished by one Newton step, or a knot where the one-sided slopes change from `+` to `-` (a kink, where the solve's bisection stops beside the knot). Where the surface is closed in the singular direction (`IsClosed` over the natural domain) the ends meet at the seam, so an end where the alignment grows outward is a maximum only when it also grows toward the seam at the other end (a crease); otherwise the solve restarts at the other end and its answer is checked like any other. Without this, `wr_550_projection_through_sphere_poles` failed: its sphere's maximum lies 0.00176 past the seam, the end u = 0 was accepted, and the pole trace took its derivatives off the curve's meridian. A maximum less aligned than the best sample is not the largest one: then the best sample's solve, and the end where the best sample is an end sample, are tried too, and the most aligned maximum is kept. Upstream accepts any answer the solve reports found. `fTol` stays only in upstream's near-equal sample tie-break. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::degen_param_tiebreak_uses_actual_param`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, and the flag test.


The correct maximum exposed a defect in its one consumer, `Surface::invert_point_near_singularity` (`surface/surface/p8.rs`, upstream `InvertPointNearSingularity`). That method assumes the boundary at the guess is a collapsed pole and drops the test point onto the iso curve at the degenerate parameter without checking the result. Where the boundary is not collapsed, that iso curve can lie far from the test point: in goldens `surface_p8` gen1191 the boundary `u = 1` runs from 4.0 to 36085 away from the point, the search converges at the end `v = -0.652`, and the drop lands 35505.9 away, while the guess is 1395.2 away. Upstream reports that as found, and the point-solve stepper ends its iteration there. Main returned `SM_ERR` (1001) only because the old search stopped elsewhere. The method now refuses an answer whose certified distance from the test point is provably greater than the guess's (`SM_ERR`), unless the answer is the guess's own point within rounding (16 ulps of the largest coordinate; the certified distances do not bound the evaluations' rounding). A margin in the distance comparison instead (of `tol`, or of 16 ulps) let an answer tie its guess and end the stepper early: goldens `surface_p4` thin0064 then ended 2.7e-7 farther than main at `tol` 1e-9. The stepper then takes its normal Newton step from the guess. This change is unconditional: no answer proven farther from the point than the guess is the closest point.
