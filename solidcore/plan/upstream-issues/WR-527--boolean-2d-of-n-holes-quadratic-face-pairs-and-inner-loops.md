#### WR-527. A 2D boolean of a sheet with n holes takes time in n^2: every face pair is intersected and every inner loop classified against each new face
- **Severity:** hang (quadratic time in an input-derived count).
- **Area:** Booleans
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmTopologyIntersector.cpp`, `SmTopologyIntersector::IntersectTopology` (def. `:1580`), the face pair loop (`:1736`); `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp`, `SmBrep::MakeEdgeInFaceWithVU` (def. `:12474`), the inner loop transfer (`:13263`).
- **What happens:** `IntersectTopology` checks every face of one operand against every face of the other, and both of its checks see whole surfaces rather than faces. `Boolean2D` grows each operand's plane to cover the whole sheet, so on a perforated sheet every face pair passes the coincidence check and classifies its edges on the other face (`IntersectWithEllipse` for each pair of holes). Cutting the holes from the plate then imprints each hole as a new face, and `MakeEdgeInFaceWithVU` point-classifies every inner loop of the plate against that face. Both costs are quadratic in the number of holes. The results are right but slow: SolidCraft's sheet of a thousand holes ran for more than 20 minutes.
- **Trigger:** a plate with 64 or 256 r 2 holes, the disks united in balanced rounds and then cut from the plate with `SmApiBoolean2d`. The 256-hole case takes about 14 times as long as the 64-hole case.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft (tangency-labs/solidcore#1526, #1527). The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@60b1c6a ("boolean2d: faces apart are not intersected, and loops apart are not classified"), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ timing run was made here. The Rust port reproduces both loops line for line at the pin (`3fd5b95`, usd-brep v1.0.1).
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. `Kernel::fixes.boolean_skips_faces_apart` enables two skips:
  - `TopologyIntersector::intersect_topology` computes each face's box once (`face_calculate_bounding_box`, not tight). After the existing-SSI check, it skips any pair whose boxes are disjoint by the intersection tolerance. A box that fails or has negative volume is unbounded, so it rules nothing out.
  - `redistribute_inner_loops` (`MakeEdgeInFaceWithVU`) handles both faces lying on planes. It computes the union of the new face's outer-loop edge boxes, each grown by its edge tolerance, and leaves in place, without classifying, any inner loop whose test point is outside that box at 100 times the point tolerance. If an edge has no curve or no box, the skip is off.

  These skips are not provably bit-identical to upstream. A skipped pair no longer makes `FaceIntersect`'s transient curves, and errors that upstream would have ignored there no longer happen, so the fix is opt-in. `tests/issue_1526_boolean_2d_faces_apart.rs` cuts a 4 x 4 grid of holes and checks that the result has the same faces with and without the fix and keeps its exact area.
