Updates: WR-736

#### WR-736 (update, 2026-11-18). A lamina arc's two ends are taken on one turn of its continued curve

`ExtendTrimEdges` continues an edge use's UV trim curve ten of its lengths each way (`SmOffsetGeometryCreation.cpp`, `ExtendTrimEdges`, the `10.0 * sIvl.GetLength()` extensions with `SM_G1G2`) and lifts what lies in the extended surface's domain. The continuation of a circular arc is its circle: a quarter circle on a plane that holds the whole circle is lifted as that circle five times over, on the parameters -10 to 11 where the edge itself runs from 0 to 1.

The vertex-image trim (this record's fix) solves the vertex's image onto that curve and asks for every solution. On such a curve there is one for each turn, all of them one point: for the image of the end at parameter 1 of the 30 x 40 plate's r 18 corner, the parameters 1.0000011, 4.9627, 8.9394, -6.9394 and -2.9627. `trim_extended_edge_ends` then took, as upstream's search does, the candidate nearest the base vertex. They lie 1.0000000000000364, 1.0000000000006906, ... from it (the offset distance, to the lift's rounding), so the turn taken was a matter of rounding: it changed with the radius, the thickness and the side of the offset.

- An end on another turn than the other end gives the edge the rest of its circle (the interval -2.9627 to 0: the other three quarters), or the circle several times over (-7.9154 to 1).
- Both have the edge's own two end points, so `ReplaceEdgeCurves` took its matching-ends shortcut and kept the old curve, upstream and here until WR-796: the wrong interval was never used.
- Since WR-796 the shortcut asks that the old curve lie on the new one between its ends. The other three quarters of the circle do not hold it, so they replaced the corner's quarter, and the offset failed in `do_extended_offset` (1001). The circle several times over holds it, and was still passed over.

Not upstream's defect alone: the candidate search is upstream's, the solutions on every turn come from this record's solve, and upstream's shortcut hides the interval. Not run against the C++.

- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#18468: an L-shaped prism whose arm's end is rounded 18 at both caps, its concave edge rounded 22. SolidCraft builds that round by thickening the arm's face in the notch, a plane face with two quarter-circle corners, by half the thickness to either side; the thicken failed with 1001. Reduced: the 30 x 40 plate in y = 0 with two corners rounded 16, 17 or 18, thickened by 2 or 104, to one side or both. Of those twelve, the one with r 17, by 2, to both sides fails first in an unoptimised build on aarch64 (macOS); which fail depends on rounding. Through SolidCraft's command line, the rounded end face of a 30 x 25 x 40 block copied as a sheet and thickened fails for 37 of 98 combinations of radius (8 to 19), thickness (0.5 to 104) and side.
- **Fix:** always on, in `trim_extended_edge_ends` (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_geometry_creation.rs`), for an open lamina edge whose vertex image has more than one solution. The point taken is unchanged: the solution nearest the base vertex. Where that solution is a passage of the image (within ten approximation tolerances of it, the distance that already decides "the same point" in this search), the image's other passages are that point again, and only one stays a candidate (`passages_on_one_turn`): the one nearest, along the curve, to where the edge's middle drops onto it. An open edge's end is nearer its own middle than the same end a turn on, so both ends come out on the middle's turn. The middle's drop is itself passed once a turn; the passage taken is the one farthest from both ends of the curve (`middle_passage`), so its turn is not cut short, and since `ExtendTrimEdges` continues the edge as far each way it is the turn the edge is on. Curve parameters are compared only with each other, never with the UV curve's: an iso curve lifted with the surface's own parameters is treated alike. Solutions that are no passage of the image stay as they are; a closed edge, whose one vertex is both its ends, is unchanged.
- **Not covered:** the other candidates of the same search (a neighbour's offset surface cutting the curve, a mated neighbour's vertex, a UV crossing) are also found once a turn on such a curve and are still told apart by their distance to the base vertex. No case that reaches them is known.
- **Goldens:** no row moves (`--all --goldens` against the same tree without the fix, per case). A first version that compared the solutions with the UV curve's own interval moved `gen0545` of `offset.offset_geometry_creation`: there the lifted curve is an iso curve with the surface's parameters and the image lies 5 off it, so its 125 solutions are no passages. That is why only passages of the image are thinned, and only against other parameters of the same curve.
- **Tests:** `a_plate_with_round_corners_thickens_to_its_prism` (`crates/solidcore-ops/src/offset/tests.rs`): the twelve plates above thicken to the prism of their outline, `(l h - 2 (1 - pi/4) r^2) t` to 1e-9, in 8 faces, 18 edges and 12 vertices. Without the fix it fails at r 17, by 2, to both sides (1001). `a_continued_arcs_ends_are_taken_on_one_turn` (unit test beside the fix): on a quarter circle continued ten lengths each way, the two ends come out on the turn of whichever passage stands for the middle, and the middle on the quarter's own. SolidCraft's `a_concave_round_runs_on_past_both_rounded_corners_of_its_face` (`crates/kernel/src/blend.rs`) builds the issue's round.
