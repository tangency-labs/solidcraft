Updates: WR-495

#### WR-495 (update, 2026-11-23). `offset_analytic_mate_edges` is removed; the exact analytic mate is always used

`OffsetGeometryCreation::mate_edges` (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_geometry_creation.rs`) now always replaces a marched piece that lies on an exact line or conic of its two analytic surfaces with that curve, and the flag is gone. Removed: the field, its `fixes` doc file, SolidCraft's preset line, the tests' flag toggles and the flag-off test halves (`issue_1061`'s flag-off test is deleted; `issue_1453` keeps only the exact-edge assertions).

Turning the swap on everywhere exposed seven defects. Each is fixed where it arises:

- **Exact curves swapped for other exact curves.** Pieces that `clip_exact_curves_with_context` already cut as lines or conics are kept. Swapping them for differently anchored lines made the three pyramid bodies in `topology::brep::local_operation`'s tests unsound (Check 14010).
- **Lines sized by an unbounded plane.** The exact curves are sought only over the marched pieces' UV box, grown by a quarter each way, with a plane's copy cut to that box. A face given a new plane has a domain of about 1.2e6, and `IntersectWithPlane` sizes its line by the planes' boxes, so the line's ends were off both planes by that size times the unit direction's rounding (3.4e-11 in the tilted-box test), where the marched piece lies on both to 1e-15.
- **A crease cut of no length** (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/curve_on_surfaces.rs`, `cut_at_creases`). A crease crossing whose parameter rounded onto a segment's end, while its fraction stayed below 1, left a zero-length piece that no halving could prove. That made `curve_on_surfaces` refuse the exact ellipse on the tilted cylinder's offset (`issue_15282`, edge 25), so `MateEdges` kept the marched curve and the result failed the check. Such a crossing now moves that end's point onto the crease line, as a crossing whose fraction rounds to the end already did. The cause was not the curve's interval: the ellipse's interval lies inside its domain. The exact ellipse's edge carries the offset's own B-rep tolerance (0.001339890721639), the tolerance of every edge of the inward offset and the floor of the outward one; the swap does not raise it.
- **An exact piece running against the marched curve.** `exact_piece` returns a curve in the direction the surface intersection gives it. On `problemShell4` face 2 (offset, extend), the exact circle ran counter-clockwise against the clockwise marched ring, so its arcs ran the other way and the face lost its short seam edge. `run_as` now reverses a piece whose tangent at the middle of its interval points against the marched curve's. `mate_edges` and `make_edges_on_analytics_exact` share it.
- **A coincidence over the whole curve taken as a cut.** `trim_extended_edge_ends` treated a `RangeOfValues` solution that covers the whole curve as a cut, and snapped the trim end to the seam. This happens when the circle lies on the neighbour's extended surface. Such a solution marks no end, so it is now skipped (`covers_curve`). With both fixes, `problemShell4` face 2 is sound with main's topology: 3 faces, 5 edges, 4 vertices.
- **A UV line trim raised by twice its edge's tolerance** (`crates/solidcore/src/topology/edgeuse/p3.rs`, `trim_raise_distance`). The exact mate between two planes attaches a UV `Line` trim. `trim_deviation_bound` gives a line no enclosure, so the raise fell back to upstream's 2x and doubled the edge tolerance (`attributed_box_face0_in`, 1e-5 to 2e-5). A line's raise is now its NURB plus evaluation slack bound (`line_trim_bound`, the bound `raised_trim_bound` already used), about 7.8e-9 there, and the row matches main again.
- **Two planes at a small angle** (`exact_piece`). A point within `tol` of two planes whose normals make the angle `φ` can be up to `tol / min(sin(φ/2), cos(φ/2))` from their line, and that bound is sharp. In `a_draft_moves_a_face` (a wall drafted 5° against a wall leaning 5°, normals 10° apart), the marched hinge end is 8.7e-7 from each plane but 1.0000000014e-5 from the line, with `tol` 1e-5. The marched piece was kept, and the local operation's body failed its check (Bad Gap 16011). When both analytic surfaces are planes, their line is now also taken where each of the piece's three points is within `tol` of both planes. This uses a certified point-to-plane bound with `coincident_strip`'s rounding model. The line is exact at any angle, and the caller still drops it on both extended surfaces within `tol`. Other surface pairs keep the strict distance test.

- **Tests:** `offset::offset_geometry_creation::tests::a_range_covering_the_whole_curve_is_not_a_cut` and `an_exact_circle_running_backwards_is_reversed`, `issue_1453_shelled_cylinder_exact_edges`, `issue_1442_offset_walls_touch`, `issue_1061_moved_boss_top_extends_cylinder`, `issue_15282_tilt_cyl_mate_pair` (`tilt_cyl_fixture_mate_is_the_exact_ellipse`: the mate is the closed-form ellipse of the fixture's cylinder and plane, within a derived rounding allowance, and is certified on both surfaces), `offset::curve_on_surfaces::tests::a_crease_crossing_that_rounds_onto_the_end_moves_the_end`, and the three pyramid tests in `topology::brep::local_operation`, `offset::offset_geometry_creation::tests::a_piece_on_two_planes_at_a_small_angle_takes_their_line`, and SolidCraft's `core::offset_tests::a_draft_moves_a_face`.
- **Goldens:** 167 rows move: 2 in `blend.fillet_executive_p3`, 109 in `offset.offset_executive_p2` and 56 in `topology.brep_local_operation`. No row is added or removed, and none fails. Each moved row is replayed in `goldens::wr495_rows`, which checks it against main's facts. #21301 lists the rows by class. `line_trim_bound` also lowers trim tolerances by 1e-5 each (from about 2e-5) in 13 of the 56 `brep_local_operation` rows: gen0004, gen0025, gen0043, gen0065 and gen0079, whose geometry blocks' sum and abs_sum drop by 1e-5 per lowered tolerance (2e-5 to 6e-5), and fc0008, fc0025, gen0000, gen0021, gen0031, gen0041, gen0059 and gen0070, whose drop is within the golden compare's tolerance and whose lines are written again from the run. The trim-tolerance ledger (`goldens::certify::trim_tolerance`) certifies each of these 13 rows' moves on its edge or vertex. It also lowers 15 values in gen0068, whose blocks (abs_sum 7e21) hide them, so no golden line moves; its operand-trim row lists the 15 values and certifies them there, and it has no ledger row. The ledger's three `offset.offset_executive_p2` rows, the shelled cylinders' vertex-box moves, are now WR-495's moves, so they and the `vertex-box` class are removed. The following leave the ledger the same way: gen0051's vertex 12 and gen0055's vertex 19, which the exact mate raises by 2.1e-16 and 7e-18; fc0025's edge 67, whose tolerance is now the exact mate's alone (its operand row replays without it); and the 4v fillet shell's vertex rounding entry 78 (`trim_tolerance_other.tsv`), which the exact edges drop.
