#### WR-499. An Nx1 fillet corner takes the filleted edge's opposite-convexity continuation as its side edge (status 1001)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletNx1Corner::MakeCornerTopology` (about `:6040`-`:6300`).
- **What happens:** at a corner where the filleted edge runs on tangentially across the vertex as an edge of the opposite convexity (the rim of a cylinder's overhang continuing the concave arc where the cylinder meets a block's top), the corner takes the edges next to the filleted edge round the vertex as its side edges. Both have the opposite convexity, so the corner caps the round from the rail ends on them, through the original vertex. The rail on the cylinder runs alongside the rim and never meets it (`SM_FV_NO_INT_RAIL_EU`), and `SmFilletEdge::CalcEdgeOnEdge` fails (1007, then 1001, "Corner Edge Computation Failure").
- **Trigger:** a block 40 x 30 x 5 with a cylinder r 10.5 standing on its top, its axis on the block's front edge, and the concave arc where the cylinder meets the top rounded R 2 (tangency-labs/solidcore#1474, SolidCraft rig case fillets/4174; 4365 and 4369 end the same way against a boss).
- **How found:** SolidCraft's fillets on SMLib (rig area `fillets`).
- **Confirmed:** yes, the Rust port refuses it too, with WR-457's `fillet_nx1_tangent_side_edge` on or off (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1474_nx1_corner_tangent_round_end.rs`).
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@420e47d: WR-457's tangent side-edge skip also passes, once, the filleted edge's G1 continuation of the opposite convexity (reached past any tangent edges, such as the cylinder's seam). The edge past it, of the filleted edge's convexity, is the side edge extended; the end face is the face between it and the other side edge (the front face), and the side face is cut where the end face's extension meets it (WR-457's `CalcEdgeOnExtendedEdge` side-face case).
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_nx1_opposite_continuation_side_edge` and WR-457's `Kernel::fixes.fillet_nx1_tangent_side_edge` both on (`FilletCorner::make_nx1_corner_topology`) it ports the fork's change; the round then ends in the front face's plane and adds Pappus' volume over the half turn. It is a separate field rather than an extension of `fillet_nx1_tangent_side_edge` so that enabling WR-457 keeps its recorded results; the new field has no effect without it, as the fork's change lives inside that skip loop.
