Updates: WR-646

#### WR-646 (update, 2026-11-22). `fillet_variable_span_follows_fillet` is removed: a variable round's span always follows its fillet

`SmFilletIntersector::ComputeNextPoint` (`compute_next_point_on`, `blend/fillet_intersector.rs`) now always runs the fork's check (tangency-labs/smlib@4438859) where the solver's offsets change along the trace (`offset_radii_can_change`): the fillet is solved at the span's five test shares and the span is rejected where a solution's contact lies farther than the approximation tolerance from its Hermite rail. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::fillet_variable_span_follows_fillet`, its doc file, its names in the SolidCraft fix lists of `issue_2260` and `issue_18166`, and every test setting of it. SolidCraft ran with it on already, so only the default kernel's results change.

- **Results that change:** seven `blend.fillet_executive_p3` law rows, volume only; status and topology are unchanged. Removed volume against the reference of each row (`wr646_variable_round_follows_law`), old and new:
  - `law-cube20-e0-deg2-smooth-4-2-4` and `-smooth-g2` (one face): 47.8344114 and 47.8340622, against the balls' quadrature 47.8339303 (4.8e-4 and 1.3e-4 off).
  - `law-cube20-e0-deg3-asymmetric` and `law-cube20-e7-deg3-asymmetric`: 46.3569502 and 46.3571359, against 46.3572014 (2.5e-4 and 6.6e-5 off).
  - `law-box10x20x30-e0-deg2-edge-map-sub-range` and `-reversed`: 13.0801043 and 13.0805354, against 13.0805832 (4.8e-4 and 4.8e-5 off).
  - `law-cube20-e0-deg2-smooth-xsect-linear`: 112.0517107 and 112.0006987, against the closed form 112 (each section is the chord between the ball's feet, `int r^2 / 2 ds`).
- **The references.** The ball rows' reference is the balls' closed-form quadrature (`envelope_removed`: exact for a cylinder and a linear law's cone, converged per row at twice the panels and samples to 1e-7). It is a converged quadrature, not an interval bound. Each row's round is held to its reference within T x the envelope's area (T = 4 x the edge tolerance) with and without the check; the check moves every row 3.6 to 10 times closer to it. The linear section's 112 is exact; without the check the round is 5.2e-2 off it, past T x the chord surface's area (3.8e-3).
- **Certified so far.** `wr646_certificates` (goldens, one test per row) certifies on each finished body that the golden's volume and mass values are the body's own, inside its `enclose_volume` and mass-property enclosures (no definition spread: each round is a bare B-spline net, `bare_blend_faces`, so `round_definition_rows`' definition bands do not apply). That shows consistency, not correctness. For the linear section, the 112 closed form refutes main's value. For the six ball rows, both main's and the new values lie within the bound of the converged quadrature, so the old values are not refuted, only farther off. An interval bound on the balls' quadrature is the remaining step.
- **Tests:** the flag-off halves are gone: `wr300_variable_rounds_meet_at_law_radii` runs each case on the default kernel only, `wr646`'s former flag-off cube case is `variable_round_on_planes_holds_its_balls`, and `fork_64e5632`'s fork kernel is the default kernel. New: `a_linear_section_round_removes_its_chords_volume`, which fails without the check.
