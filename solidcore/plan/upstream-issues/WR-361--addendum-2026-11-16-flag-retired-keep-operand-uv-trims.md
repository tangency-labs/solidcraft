Updates: WR-361

#### WR-361 (update, 2026-11-16). `keep_operand_uv_trims` is removed; the operand's exact trims are always kept

A face a Boolean takes over from its second operand now always gets copies of the operand edge uses' UV trims (`copy_uv_trim_curves_of_other_face` in `crates/solidcore/src/boolean/merge/p2.rs`), and the flag is gone. Removed: the field, SolidCraft's preset line, its `fixes.json` entry, its profile-list names and the tests' flag-off halves.

Turning the copy on everywhere exposed three defects the re-projected trims had hidden. Each is fixed where the bad trim is made, not in the copy:

- **A domain side taken for a seam** (`crates/solidcore/src/topology/brep/p9.rs`, `MakeEdgeInFaceWithVU`). Upstream moves the other edge use's trim to the opposite side of the domain whenever the trim lies on a domain boundary (`SmCurve::IsDomainBoundary`, `SmBrep.cpp:12853`-`12935`). On an open side (a plane face's edge) that puts the trim on the wrong edge: in `boolean_api::an_arc_grazing_a_coplanar_box_face_joins` a sliver along the box face x = 10 got the z = 0 trim for its z = 10 edge (a gap of 10). The trim now moves only where `Surface::is_closed` says the surface closes across that direction (`seam_if_closed`).
- **Knot insertion off an iso-line** (`crates/solidcore/src/nurbs/nurbs_crv.rs`, `crv_split` and `crv_trim`). `alf*u + (1 - alf)*u` rounds an ulp off `u`: the cylinder's seam trim u = 6 pi cut at v = 1 (alf = 1/22) ended at 18.849555921538762, an ulp past the domain (`issue_2479_pcurve_ends_in_domain`). Knot insertion now combines control points with `CPoint::affine2`, which keeps a coordinate the two points share exactly; that is the exact value of the affine combination.
- **Stale trims after a re-parameterising split** (`p9.rs`, the separating cut). Without WR-450's fix (`analytic_copy_keeps_parameterization` off, the default), the new face of a split B-spline face gets the recognised analytic in its own parameterisation while the moved edge uses keep trims made on the B-spline: 5.59 off their edges in `wr_450_box_minus_rational_cylinder`. Those trims are now dropped when the copy changes kind (`drop_face_trims`); they are the lazy caches of the edges' curves on the face and are made again when next asked for. With that, the flag-off half of WR-450's boolean is valid at the closed-form volume, so the test asserts that for both halves.

The copy itself is gated so a copied trim is exact by construction, not by samples:

- only onto an edge `MergeEdge` copied from the operand's own edge (`Relations::copied_edges`). An edge the intersection made or related carries the intersector's UV curve, an approximation that can lie farther from the exact curve than the trim dropped from the edge (a torus cut by a box's planes: 9.6e-7 off the plane against 7.1e-8), so it keeps `MakeFace`'s trim. The exception is a UV curve that ends at a singularity of the surface (`Surface::is_singularity` at `EFF_ZERO`, singular in some direction; a sphere's pole). The surface maps a whole side of its domain to that point, so the drop finds no parameter there and its trim gets coincident control points and a cusp, while the intersector's trace ran into the pole at its own parameter. That trim is copied (`solidcore-ops` `boolean::a_ball_with_its_poles_on_a_rolls_face_and_its_seam_turned_slightly_off_is_cut`);
- only when the new edge's curve is the operand edge's by definition (`Curve::same_definition`: the same kind and IEEE-equal defining data; a closed curve run the other way round, a re-fit or NaN data refuse), over the same interval;
- only onto an edge at least as tolerant as the operand's (a trim lies on its edge only within that tolerance);
- never for a line edge on a plane (`Plane::drop_curve` is exact there, so `MakeFace`'s line trim is kept);
- only when the surface copy keeps the operand surface's parameterisation (not a B-spline recognised as an analytic in its own parameterisation).

`CPoint::affine2` returns the plain combination unchanged when its weight is not finite, so NaN control points stay NaN.
