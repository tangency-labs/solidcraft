#### WR-741. `SmEllipse::AssertValid` tests 1 and 2 ignore the rounding the analytic form carries, so a near-degenerate ellipse fails them
- **Severity:** Wrong result (an exact result refused)
- **Area:** Curves
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmEllipse.cpp`, `SmEllipse::AssertValid`: at five STEP samples, test 1 compares the NURB point of the converted parameter with the STEP point, and test 2 inverts the NURB point onto the analytic form. Both use `dScaledZero = SM_EFF_ZERO * 100.0 * (1.0 + sNurbPoint.GetMaxDimension())`.
- **What happens:** the analytic form, `centre + rx cos(t) X + ry sin(t) Y`, can be evaluated only to about `max(rx, ry)` times an ulp of `t` (the angle in degrees, converted to radians). The tolerance scales with the point's coordinates, not with the radii. An ellipse whose radius is far larger than its points' coordinates therefore fails both tests on rounding alone: its NURB and analytic forms are each exact to rounding, yet they differ by more than the tolerance.
- **Trigger:** a plane that nearly holds a cylinder's axis. SolidCraft's enclosure part `periph_case_R` (tangency-labs/solidcore#2539) cuts its case body, and #2539's reduction `e3` cuts a cone, with `t24`: a box clipped by a slanted half-space and notched by a cylinder of radius 8 whose axis lies along the slanted face to 4.5e-7 rad. The face meets the notch in an ellipse of radii 1.8e7 and 8 over 2.3e-4 degrees, which is a line to 6e-11 over its length. One ulp of `t` at 90 degrees moves its analytic point by about 4e-9. Measured: `e3`'s test 2 at 3.97e-9 against 3.81e-9, and `periph_case_R` minus `t24` at tests 1 and 2, 4.64e-9 against 1.7e-9. Both results are one closed solid.
- **Confirmed:** yes, by the C++ fork (tangency-labs/smlib, through SolidCraft's stand): it refuses `e3`'s cut at test 2, offset 3.9721306e-9 (#2539).
- **How found:** by SolidCraft's 50-part stand (#2539). The assembly's two kernels refused different operands of the part's last cut, depending on the last bits of the notch cylinder's net (traced on #2618).
- **Construction change:** tests 1 and 2 also allow the rounding the STEP point carries beyond the point term, derived from its evaluation as WR-737 derives a net's: `gamma_11 max(|rx|, |ry|) (1 + |t|)`, `t` the angle in radians and `gamma_n = n u / (1 - n u)` from `nurbs::math`. An angle off by `d` moves the STEP point by up to `max(|rx|, |ry|) d`.
  - **The angle's roundings, relative to `|t|`: 11 on test 2's chain, the longer.** `atan2` (within an ulp, 2), `+ 2 pi` (2), `* (180 / pi)` (3), the periodic map into the domain (1), and `t * pi / 180` back (3). Test 1's STEP point has the last 3. Its NURB point is found from the STEP point: the conversion keeps it within `SM_EFF_ZERO (1 + |p|)`, or inverts it onto the NURB.
  - **The evaluation's roundings, relative to `max(|rx|, |ry|)`: at most 10.** `cos` or `sin` (within an ulp, 2), the products with the radius and the axis (2), and the two sums (6). The sums round the axis terms, at most `sqrt(2) max r`, and `o`, at most that beyond `|p|`; `|p|`'s share is the point term's.
  - **For #2539's ellipse** (radius 1.79e7, `t` 1.5708) the bound is 5.6e-8, against the 4.6e-9 the rounding reaches.
  - **On an ordinary ellipse it is negligible.** While `max(rx, ry) <= 1 + |p|` it is below 1e-4 of the point term, e.g. 2.5e-14 on radii 8 and 5. It exceeds the point term only where `max r (1 + |t|) > 8.2e4 (1 + |p|)`.
  - **UV ellipses.** The check also serves 2D ellipses on a surface's parameter plane; they take the same bound in parameter units.
- **Difference from the C++:** solidcore accepts an ellipse whose NURB and STEP points differ by more than upstream's `100 SM_EFF_ZERO (1 + |p|)` and by no more than that plus the bound; upstream refuses it on its own rounding, as it refuses `e3` (above). In the old C++ comparison, 27 of `curve.ellipse_p1`'s 2527 generated cases moved, each only in the tolerance a failing report records (larger by the bound, 7.3e-16 to 2.3e-14) with the same refusal at the same distance. No golden moves: `goldens --all --goldens` passes 283 of 283 units unchanged.
- **Rust port:** `curve::ellipse::p1`, `Ellipse::assert_valid` (unit `curve.ellipse_p1`), by default: no flag (#2647's policy; solidcore-native, no fork commit).
  - **Tests:**
    - `curve::ellipse::p1::tests::a_near_line_ellipse_passes_with_the_rounding_its_radii_carry` rebuilds the cut's ellipse from its data. At one of the check's samples its NURB and STEP points lie farther apart than upstream's tolerance, and the check passes it.     - `crates/solidcore/tests/wr579_near_coincident_tube_ends_join.rs` (`wr741_*`) checks SolidCraft's records: `t24` alone, `e3`'s cone minus `t24`, and `periph_case_R` below z = -14 minus `t24`.
