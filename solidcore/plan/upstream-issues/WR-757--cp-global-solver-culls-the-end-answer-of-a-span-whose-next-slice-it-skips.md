#### WR-757. `SmCPGlobalSolver::LocalSolve` culls a span's end answer as "in the next segment" while it skips that segment as zero length, so a point whose foot is at an interval end a rounding past a knot gets no solution
- **Severity:** Wrong result (no solution where the closest point is the interval's end; a round's guess on its other rail fails with status 1001, `Fillet Corner Error: Unable to Compute Geometry`)
- **Area:** Curve (`SmCurve::GlobalPointSolve`, minimise)
- **Location:** at the pin `3fd5b95`: `source/SMLib/src/SmCurve.cpp:10980-10981` (a span's end continuity is made discontinuous only when `sBezIvl.GetMax() >= m_crInterval.GetMax()` / `GetMin() <= GetMin()`, exact), `:10989` (the next span's slice of the interval is dropped when `SM_ARE_SAME(max, min)`), and `:11391-11410` (a solution at a span end whose continuity is G1 or better is culled as one "the next segment" will find).
- **What happens:** an interval ending `e` past a knot, with `e` within `SM_ARE_SAME`'s scaled zero (1e-12 and below on parameters near 0.6; measured at 2e-14 and 1e-12), leaves the span before the knot with a G1 end, so its end answer is culled. The next span's slice of the interval is `e` long, so it is skipped as zero length. Neither span gives the answer, and `GlobalPointSolve` returns no solution. With `e` at 1e-9, or the interval ending on the knot, or `e` negative, the end answer is kept. The result flips on rounding noise of the interval's end.
- **Trigger:** a chain of straight cubic Bezier spans (triple interior knots): a 26 mm span, a span 4.8e-4 mm long on each side of it, on the same line; minimise the distance from a point 1 mm to the side of the long span, its foot 1e-4 mm past the span's end, over the interval of an edge on it (the knots, each 2e-14 outward). Upstream returns no solution; the closest point of the interval is its end.
- **How found:** solidcore#2656: after a round's surfaces are kept as their definitions, the guess of the next round on its other rail has its foot at the end of the rail's edge, and the edge's interval is a rounding past the knot; the fillet fails with status 1001.
- **Confirmed:** by reading the C++ at the pin (the lines above); the Rust port repeats the three tests line by line, and `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2656_point_solve_at_interval_end.rs` reproduces it on the port without the fix. Not run against the C++ (no C++ build here).
- **Rust port:** the "interval ends at this span's end (start)" test also accepts `are_same`, the tolerance that skips the next slice (`curve/curve/p1.rs`, `CpGlobalSolver::local_solve_branch`). No flag: nothing moves in the goldens (283 units, the same 83 failing before and after).
