#### WR-716. Chamfers meeting at a vertex: the mitre's edges are marched, the facet is a Coons patch, and four chamfers are refused with no reason
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blend (fillet corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp:1269` and `:1445`, `SmFilletCorner::Create` (def. `:1129`): a corner where every blended edge use has a linear section is a chamfer corner, skips the Ball, the Setback and the concavity test, and is always `SmFilletConvexNxNCorner` with `SetChamfer(TRUE)`, convex and concave alike. `SmFilletConvexNxNCorner::MakeCornerTopology` (def. `:5642`) in bevel mode refuses any count but three (`:5724`, `SER(SM_ERR)`, "Will not handle other cases for now"). `SmFilletCorner::MakeAnalyticCornerSurface` (def. `:3379`) builds the non-bevel corner face through `sm_CreatePlanarSurface` (`:833`, called at `:3508`). `source/SM_API/src/SmApiFillets.cpp:95` sets every corner of `SmApiChamferFillet` to bevel.
- **What happens:** where every edge at a vertex is chamfered and every chamfer is a plane between two planes, the corner is closed exactly by the chamfer planes, but the pinned corner does not build it that way.
  - **Bevel (the mitre):** the three edges from the rails' crossings X_i to the chamfers' common point M are general surface/surface intersections of the chamfer surfaces (`CalcFilletIntFillet`, `sm_SrfSrfIntersection`), not the planes' exact lines. Four or more chamfers are refused with `SM_ERR` and no reason or size.
  - **Not bevel (the facet):** the corner face goes through the corner's vertices: an `SmPlane` for three, a bilinear B-spline for four (a plane only if the four are coplanar), refused for more. Where the chamfers are unequal, the corner face can have four sides and is then not a plane. For a 40 x 30 x 20 box with the edges at its (40, 30, 20) corner chamfered 2, 3 and 4 through `fillet_edges_per_edge`, the result has 10 faces and volume 23640.833333. The closed forms are 23645.25 for the mitre (9 faces) and 23644.0 for the facet (10 faces). The corner face's vertices (4, 2, 0), (3, 4, 0), (0, 4, 3), (4, 0, 2), in the corner frame (u, v, w inward), are not coplanar (measured in tangency-labs/solidcore#2345).
  - **A mitre point outside the corner** (one chamfer much wider than the others) is not detected. Neither branch names a size that builds.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/smlib#89's brief and its inventory (65 chamfer corners in the fork's suite, all N = 3 convex mitres), ported as tangency-labs/solidcore#2345.
- **How found:** porting the generic blend's corner events (`docs/generic-blend.md` §3.2) from the fork's brief.
- **Confirmed:** in solidcore, `crates/solidcore/tests/wr697_ball_corner.rs` (`without_the_flag_the_chamfer_corner_is_the_pinned_patch` pins today's corner with the flag off; `per_edge_linear_chamfers_at_a_corner_are_the_mitre` runs `fillet_edges_per_edge` with linear sections on the 2/3/4 corner: 10 faces, 23640.833333, a four-sided B-spline corner face whose fourth corner is off the plane of the other three, without the flag; the mitre's 9 faces and 23645.25 with it).
- **Rust port:** `Kernel::fixes.fillet_chamfer_corner` (off by default): the blend corner's chamfer event, `crate::blend::blend_corner::chamfer`, an own design from the brief with no fork code. The corner type is the session's (`FilletSession::set_chamfer_corner`, `BlendChamferCorner`).
  - **Mitre** (the default): M from the 3x3 solve. The edges X_i-M are exact lines with exact line pcurves on both chamfers, and there is no corner face.
  - **Facet:** the plane through the X_i, oriented out of the material. Its edges are the chamfers' end sections, exact lines on both faces.
  - **Corner patch** (the owner's answer 3 on #2345): where four or more chamfers' crossings are not in one plane, the corner is a patch, whichever type was asked. The chamfers stop at their crossings and n bilinear quadrilaterals round the crossings' centroid C close the skew polygon of their end sections: quadrilateral m between C, the middle of the side before X_m, X_m and the middle of the side after it, G0 to each other along the straight spokes from C and to each chamfer along its end section (a chamfer corner is G0, as its chamfers are). At four sides they are the quarters of the crossings' own bilinear patch (the hyperbolic paraboloid through them) and are that one face; past four, no one smooth patch can carry the straight end sections (one polynomial over a polygon has one value where two sides' lines meet, and past four sides non-adjacent sides' lines meet), so they are n faces.
  - **Definition:** the corner keeps what defines it as plain data (`ChamferCornerDefinition`): the ring's chamfers (edge, width, plane), the X_i, M at a mitre, the facet's normal at a facet, and the patch (the crossings' centroid and their order round it) at a corner patch. It is what the corner-definition type of tangency-labs/solidcore#2488 is to take.
  - **Classification and validity** use sign tests on linear forms at the part-scale rounding gate. The event declines (and the pinned corner builds) where an edge is not straight, a face not a plane, a chamfer not of constant width, or the chamfers of mixed convexity.
  - **Refusals:**
    - A mitre point outside the corner is refused with `FilletErrorType::NoRoom`, naming the largest size of the too-wide chamfer that keeps M inside, less the fit margin, as the refusal's fit. The facet is refused there too.
    - Four or more chamfers whose crossings lie in one plane build the facet; their mitre is refused, naming why.
    - A corner patch is refused where a crossing is not on the kept side of a chamfer not through it. It never stands past a face or into a chamfer: every corner of every quadrilateral lies in the crossings' convex hull, which lies in the corner.
    - Chamfer planes so nearly coplanar (a very flat corner) that the points where they meet are not determined to the part's rounding are refused with `FilletErrorType::CornerUnsupported`, naming that: the 3x3 solves' first-order rounding bound, `u (s + |y|) sum_k |c_k| / |D|`, is above the gate.

  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr697_ball_corner.rs`, closed forms only: the 20 cube's corners as mitres and facets; `unequal_chamfers_at_a_corner` (2/3/4) as the mitre (M = (2.5, 1.5, 0.5), 24000 - 354.75) and the facet (10u + 9v + 4w = 48, 1.25 more removed); a concave pocket-floor corner both ways; a mitre point outside the corner, refused with its fit, which then builds; an N = 4 pyramid apex; the 2/3/4 corner's definition; a low three-face apex, refused at slope 1/100 and an exact mitre at 5/12.

- **Retirement (the flag is for the port in progress only):**
  - At parity the flag becomes the default and is removed, and the pinned 3x3 corner's chamfer branch is deleted with it: `SetChamfer`'s route to `SmFilletConvexNxNCorner` for chamfer corners, its bevel topology and marched mitre edges, and `MakeAnalyticCornerSurface`'s chamfer corner faces (the plane through three vertices and the bilinear patch through four).
  - Parity here means every chamfer corner that branch builds today is either built by the event or refused, naming why. The event still declines where a chamfer edge is not straight, a face is not a plane, a chamfer is not of constant width, or the chamfers are of mixed convexity; those cases keep the pinned branch until they are covered.
  - WR-254 (`analytic_corner_failure_is_false`) overlaps: its `SER` / `NER` exits in `MakeAnalyticCornerSurface` include the chamfer faces'. Those exits retire with the chamfer branch. The flag stays for the corners that are not chamfers (the sphere, the surface of revolution, the variable-radius corner).
