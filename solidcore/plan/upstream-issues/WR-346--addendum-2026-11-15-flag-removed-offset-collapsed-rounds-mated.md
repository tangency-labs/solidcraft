Updates: WR-346

#### WR-346 (update, 2026-11-15). `offset_collapsed_rounds_mated` is removed

An extended inset face whose offset collapses (a convex round thinner than the wall) gets no offset face, and the faces beside it are mated across it in `do_extended_offset`, as at a sharp edge. The fix is now unconditional and the flag is gone; SolidCraft already ran with it on. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::offset_collapsed_rounds_mated`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260_gn_pipe_refined_range_end.rs`, and every test setting of it.

A collapsed face the mating cannot bridge is now refused as `OffsetFailure::Collapses { face, distance, radius }` (`SM_ERR`), naming the face, the distance and a radius the face attains (sampled by a grid and a pattern search, not a certified minimum; only reported, the refusal rests on the collapse test: the one `proved_singular` finds over the face displaced along its NURB normal by the signed distance its collapse test used, or, where it finds none, `|distance|`, an upper bound within the tolerance on the face's least radius and not one it is known to attain), where the flag's path returned a bare `SolidError::Upstream` 1034. An empty success is not returned there: an unbridged collapsed face proves only that this face cannot be built, not that the whole offset is empty.

- **Results:** three `offset.offset_executive_p2` goldens rows move, all insets past a primitive's own radius (goldens test `offset_offset_executive_p2::tests::wr346_collapsed_insets_are_accounted_for`):
  - `extended_sphere_inset_past_radius` (unit sphere, inset 1.5): 1001 becomes 1000 with an empty body (one region, no faces). The inset set of a ball of radius 1 by 1.5 is empty. Its `ownership` goes from `[0, 1, 0]` to `[0, 1, 1]`: the result origin is now a new allocation where there was no result.
  - `member_cylinder_extend_inset_at_radius` and `member_cylinder_extend_inset_past_radius` (r 1, h 4, inset 1 and 1.5): still refused with 1001, now typed as `Collapses` with radius 1 (`offset_executive::p2::tests::wr346_unbridged_collapsed_wall_is_refused_as_collapses`). What moved is the executive's offset held at the refusal: it holds the two caps' offsets built before the wall was found unbridged (planes with vertices at `z = d` and `z = 4 - d`), where it was empty.
- **Tests:** `tests/wr567_revolution_round_offset_collapses.rs`, `tests/issue_1945_fork_1207_step4.rs` and the ignored `issue_397_inset_rounds_thinner_than_wall_collapse` in `offset_executive/p2.rs` now run on the default kernel.
