Updates: WR-41

#### WR-41 (update, 2026-11-14). `plane_reparameterize_moves_origin` is removed

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, batch S-plane). `Plane::reparameterize` (`surface/plane/p1.rs`) always sets the origin to upstream's `sOriginMax`, the point that keeps the old domain's upper corner at the new domain's upper corner, so the analytic plane and its NURB stay one surface. `Plane::reparameterize_opt_in` and `Plane::reparameterize_with_kernel` are gone; `Surface::reparameterize` and `Plane::is_nurb_surface_plane` call `Plane::reparameterize`. A reparameterisation onto the same domain keeps the origin exactly, as upstream's release build does (it never sets `sOriginMax`), instead of recomputing it through the upper corner with rounding.

- **Tests:** the `wr41_*` unit tests in `surface/plane/p1.rs` run on the default path: the moved origin against closed forms (`(26/3, 17.75, 30)` and `(4.5, 4.25, 5)`), analytic against NURB points across the new domain, a huge-scale plane (origin ~1e7, moved ~2e6) on its NURB within 4e-15 relative, and a new test that an identity reparameterisation leaves the plane bit for bit. The test that pinned the old divergence is gone.
- **Results:** on the library's default kernel (SolidCraft's preset had the flag on), 24 goldens rows of `surface.plane_p1` move; no other unit moves. All 24 change their record length; in 5 of them the block's weighted sum also flips between `-inf` and `NaN` (gen0065, gen0891, gen1006, gen1105 to `NaN`, gen0329 to `-inf`), since the record holds both infinities and their positions move with the length. Each is a generated program with a `Reparameterize` onto a new domain, after which the origin moves by 0.92 to 8.6e6 (the huge-scale planes). `goldens` test `surface_plane_p1::reparam_origin_tests::reparameterized_planes_stay_on_their_nurb` replays every such program: after each reparameterisation onto a new domain, the analytic plane agrees with its NURB at three domain corners (both are affine in (u, v), so that is agreement everywhere) within `1e-14 * (1 + size)`, and each of the 24 rows is among them with an origin move above 0.5. Upstream's kept origin translates the analytic plane by the whole move (0.92 to 8.6e6). The later drops, line intersections and trims therefore run on the plane its NURB describes, and their solution counts change. The other 117 programs change only values the unit's block sums cannot show (their records hold infinities).
