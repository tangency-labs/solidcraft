#### WR-453. `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint` makes an edge that stops short of a proto vertex lying just past its curve's end
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a vertex gap over the vertex's tolerance, SmBrep vertex/edge gap check; the result is valid only when the gap is absorbed into a grown tolerance)
- **Area:** Booleans / proto topology
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp`, `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint` (the same grazing rim crossing as WR-452, the other way round).
- **What happens:** in an internal thread (a nut minus a screw-swept groove) a flank's intersection curve with the bore meets the bore's rim at about 3 degrees; its end, traced to the face boundary, lies 3.1e-5 along the rim before the proto vertex that the rim/flank intersection made. The edge stops short of its vertex and the gap goes into the vertex's tolerance (6.2e-5 against the body's 1e-5).
- **Trigger:** a nut (a disc with a bore) minus a screw-swept thread groove (SolidCraft rig internal M10x1.5, tangency-labs/solidcore#410).
- **How found:** reported by the SolidCraft kernel regression rig (tangency-labs/solidcore#410); fixed in the SolidCraft fork, tangency-labs/smlib a8caccd (`SmEdgeDefinition::ExtendEndToProtoVertex`).
- **Confirmed:** no (read at c9c5979; the Rust port reaches the extension in a generated thread cut but not the fork's exact internal case, which its helical sweep differs from; the C++ build was not run)
- **Fix:** when the proto vertex lies ahead of the interval's end along the tangent (within a twentieth of the interval) and the interval ends where its curves do, the 3d curve and both UV curves are continued exactly, each by the polynomial of its own end span (the span made one Bezier segment by knot insertion, then de Casteljau at a parameter past 1, in homogeneous coordinates; no control points are added), to the parameter where the 3d curve passes nearest the proto vertex. That point must be within tolerance and nearer than the old end, and each UV curve's new end must lie on its face within tolerance; otherwise nothing changes. Not ported: the fork's `SmBrep::ValidateAndUpdateTolerances` change (a308a4d, half-tolerance gaps) and settling every operation result's tolerances (bf7c272), because that function is not in the Rust port.
- **Rust port:** `crates/solidcore/src/topology/proto_topology/p2/imprint_ends.rs` (`extend_end_to_proto_vertex`, `extended_curve`). Opt-in `Kernel::fixes.imprint_extends_edge_to_proto_vertex`; off by default. Test: `extended_curve_continues_the_end_span_polynomial` in the same file (the continuation is the old curve over its interval and the Taylor polynomial of its end beyond it, at either end).
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#410.
