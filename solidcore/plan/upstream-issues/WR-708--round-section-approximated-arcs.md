#### WR-708. The executive's circular rounds are built from approximated arcs, so a round's end section is up to 1.6e-3 off the exact section of a round it joins
- **Severity:** Wrong result (accuracy)
- **Area:** Fillets (sections)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletExecutive.cpp`, `SmFilletExecutive::SetFilletParameters` (each `SM_FSG_CIRCULAR` generator is made with `bApproximateArcs` TRUE), and `SmFilletCorner::MakeConstantRadiusFillet` (`source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, its circular generator likewise).
- **What happens:** a circular round's sections are polynomial arcs within 0.05% of the radius (1e-3 relative for the corners' rounds), not the ball's circle. Where a B-spline round continues into an analytic round (a torus), the edge between them takes the torus's exact section, and the B-spline round's own end section is up to 1.6e-3 off it at r 2. Upstream hides this: `SmFace::ShrinkGeometry` drops the fillet face's trim curves and projects the edge's curve again, within the body's tolerance. Where the trims are kept (`face_trim_curves_kept_where_exact`, WR-700, and `MakeFaceBrep` keeping the fillet's own trims under `fillet_blend_corner`, WR-697), the round's trim lies on its own approximated section, off the edge, and `check_brep` reports bad gaps of 1.2e-3 to 2.5e-3.
- **Trigger:** the fork's tangent peg (capi `tangent_peg(0)`: a bar 40 x 16 x 10 with a half-disc end R 8 about (40, 8), joined with a disc R 8 about the same centre from z = 10 to 20). Round the front top edge r 2, then the concave arc where the top meets the cylinder together with the front round's end on the cylinder, r 2 (capi `peg_rounds_in_turn(2, 2, true)`). The joint section from (32, 2, 10) to (33.6, 3.2, 12) is the circle of radius 2 about (32, 2, 12).
- **How found:** diagnosing `fork_9bebf34_tangent_end_cases`, whose four rounds-in-turn cases failed `check_brep` with WR-700 and WR-697 on together (smlib#79 steps 4a and 4b). The fork's tangency-labs/smlib@523bc25 makes the exact circle the default.
- **Confirmed:** the fork, built locally (SMLib and SM_API, the harness's flags) and run on the trigger and the other three rounds-in-turn cases: at 523bc25's parent 58d03ef the round's trim along the joint is 1.642e-3 off its edge (2.463e-3 and 1.232e-3 at r 1.5 / 3, the same as the port without the flag). At 523bc25 and at 5e8c9c1 it is 1.6e-13 off, and the volume goes from 9399.385589120 to 9399.383293173. The port with the flag: volume 9399.383292102, and the round's surface matches 5e8c9c1's to 1e-13 at the points compared. The pinned C++ was not run on the trigger: the port's flag-off path is the pinned generator, at difftest parity.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_exact_arc_sections`, off by default. With it on, the executive's circular generators (`SetFilletParameters`) and `MakeConstantRadiusFillet`'s are made with `bApproximateArcs` FALSE. An explicit request for approximated arcs (`SmApiConstantRadiusFillet`'s cross-section type 1) keeps them. The fork builds its circle with its `SmGnSectionFSG` order-1 section (3aae1c4, not ported). The port uses the pinned `SmCircularCrossSectionFSG`'s exact rational arcs, so the values above agree with the fork's to the precision shown, not bit for bit.
- **Variable rounds:** the executive's variable circular rounds take the exact circle too, as in the fork. Run locally on `wr685_variable_round_touch_circle`'s cases (SmFilletExecutive as `SmApiVariableRadiusFillet` runs it), the fork at 5e8c9c1 and the port with every fix give the same volumes to the last digit printed: the linear round on its cone 11956.222433581395 (the closed form's 11956.2224267359 to 5.7e-10, where the approximated arcs were 2.1e-7 off), and the five cliff overflows. The cubic law's round is 5957.716621492882 at the fork, 2.2e-13 off here. At 58d03ef the fork gives the approximated arcs' values, the port's with this flag off. The tests that model the fork before d01d7b0 (no G^n section, `fork_ea59e9c_section_orders`' variable mark) keep this flag off with it.
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr708_exact_arc_sections.rs`; the variable rounds in `crates/solidcore/tests/wr685_variable_round_touch_circle.rs`.
