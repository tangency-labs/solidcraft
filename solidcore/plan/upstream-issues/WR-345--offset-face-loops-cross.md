#### WR-345. `SmOffsetExecutive::DoExtendedOffset` builds offset faces whose loops cross
- **Severity:** Wrong result (the shell fails: `SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER` in the merge's imprint, 1001 on stock)
- **Area:** Offset
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp`, `SmOffsetExecutive::DoExtendedOffset`: each loop of an offset face is built from its edges' offsets (`BuildOffsetOfEdgeuses`) and trimmed only where its own consecutive edges meet, then `PiecewiseMerge` runs.
- **What happens:** nothing trims one loop of a face against another. When an inward wall is thicker than the gap between a hole and the face edge beside it, the hole's offset loop grows past the outer loop's and crosses it; the face fails `SmFace` test #38 (Intersecting Face Loops). The piecewise merge cannot imprint it: `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint` loses an edge's end vertex (`SmProtoTopology.cpp:1615`, `SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER`); mended there, the loops share vertices (test #39) and the merged offset encloses no region.
- **Trigger:** a 40 x 40 x 10 plate with a 4 mm through hole at (4.5, 20), shelled 1.5 inward with the top open: 1006. SolidCraft ladder 09/011, 10/008 and 11/004.
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig on the SMLib fork (tangency-labs/solidcore#854); gdb on the fork's C++ build.
- **Confirmed:** on the fork's C++ build, volumes against the closed form, results valid at check level 2: hole crossing one side 4554.583397 vs 4554.583419 (5e-9); crossing both sides (the floor in two faces) 1771.623909 vs 1771.623923 (8e-9); clear of the side (control) unchanged; an offset that only touches the side fails with `SM_ERR_BAD_INTERSECTIONS`.
- **Fix (fork commit "SMLib: an offset face whose loops cross is rebuilt from the stretches that bound it"):** `SmOffsetExecutive::ResolveCrossingLoops`, before `PiecewiseMerge`: the crossings are found in UV as test #38 finds them, each loop is cut there, the outer-loop stretches outside every hole and the hole stretches inside the outer loop and outside the other holes are kept (`SmLoop::ContainsUVPoint`), chained at the crossings, and the face is rebuilt with `MakeFaceWithCurves` (split with `MakeFaceFromLoopOnFace` + `SplitDisconnectedShells` when it comes apart); attributes and the offset maps carry over. Loops meeting at a loop vertex, running along one another, three loops through a point, a stretch neither inside nor outside, and an ambiguous continuation fail with `SM_ERR_BAD_INTERSECTIONS`.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.offset_resolve_crossing_loops` (default `false`, upstream behaviour), read in `OffsetExecutive::do_extended_offset` (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p2.rs`); the routine is `crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/crossing_loops.rs` (`Relation::firsts` / `remove_pair` added in `geom/tol.rs`). Deviation: `SmBrep::MakeFaceFromLoopOnFace` is not ported, so a face that comes apart is made one `MakeFaceWithCurves` per outer loop, and one that comes apart around a hole is not resolved (`SM_ERR_BAD_INTERSECTIONS`). Test `issue_854_offset_face_crossing_loops_resolved` (the plate above). No difftest case compares the opt-in path. Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#854 (the grazing triple-point tangency that still fails 09/011 at 1001 is a separate defect).
