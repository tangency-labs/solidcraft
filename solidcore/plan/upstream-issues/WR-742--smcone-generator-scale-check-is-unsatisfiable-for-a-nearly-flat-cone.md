#### WR-742. `SmCone::AssertValid` test 5 (generator line scale against `1/cos(semi-angle)`) refuses every cone whose half angle is within about 0.01 degrees of 90
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a valid cone is refused, so a revolve is refused)
- **Area:** Surfaces / cones
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmCone.cpp`, `AssertValid`, "Bad Cone GenCurve LineScale"; the Rust check is `assert_valid.rs`, `generator_scale_tolerance`.
- **What happens:** the cone keeps its semi-angle in degrees, and the test compares the generator's line scale with `1/cos(semi_angle_deg * pi/180)` within `(1 + scale) * SM_EFF_ZERO`. For a half angle `a` near 90 degrees the rounding of `a` itself (a few ulps, 4.4e-16 rad) is divided by `cos(a)` twice over: the error of `1/cos` is `sin/cos^2 * 4.4e-16`, relative `4.4e-16 / cos`. That passes the relative 1e-12 only while `cos > 4e-4`. The line scale itself, `length / height`, is right to 1e-16 relative.
- **Trigger:** a revolve of a profile line a few 1e-4 degrees off perpendicular to the axis (a ring of radial width 2.65 and height 1.3e-5 to 2.1e-5: half angle 89.9997 degrees, scale 1.3e5 to 2.0e5). Measured offsets 2.2e-6 to 8.6e-6 against tolerances 1.3e-7 to 2.0e-7 (an e-bike battery pack design in SolidCraft: a rubber grommet, three faces).
- **How found:** SolidCraft reported "SMLib's check refuses the result: 3 failed tests" for the revolve.
- **Confirmed:** no, the pinned C++ is not run for it; the formula is identical in the port.
- **Rust port:** the tolerance adds the propagated rounding of the angle, `sin/cos^2 * 4 * EPSILON * |a|`, to the existing term. A gentle cone keeps its tolerance to within 1e-16; a scale off by 1e-6 of itself is still refused. When the tolerance reaches the scale it checks (e.g. radial width 2.65e10 over height 1e-6, where the angle rounds to 90 degrees and the tolerance is about 23 times the scale), the stored angle no longer fixes even the magnitude of `1/cos` and any line scale would pass, so test 5 refuses: the angle cannot represent that cone. A consistent cone at 89.999999 degrees (tolerance about 8e-8 of the scale) is compared normally. Tests: `nearly_flat_cone_passes_the_generator_scale_check`, `generator_scale_tolerance_still_refuses_a_wrong_scale`, `cone_whose_angle_rounds_to_flat_is_refused` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/surface/assert_valid.rs`).
