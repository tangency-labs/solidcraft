Updates: WR-695

#### WR-695 (update, 2026-11-16). `fillet_overflow_trim_at_creases` is removed

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246). A cliff overflow, a round with WR-674 section ends (WR-706) and an open G1 setback corner are always trimmed at their creases in place (`session/trim_at_creases.rs`); the subtraction of the region the blends remove is deleted, as smlib#79 step 6 deletes it in the fork. SolidCraft's preset already had the flag on.

Removed:
- the field `UpstreamFixes::fillet_overflow_trim_at_creases`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entry in `fixes.json`, the fixture opt-ins (`190-round-as-tall-as-its-wall.json`, `293-cube-corner-rounds-as-wide-as-faces.json`) and every test setting of it;
- the flag-off path: `FilletSession::subtract_removal_region` (`session/cliff_overflow.rs`), the session's `body_application_attributes` / `replace_application_attributes` (used only by its Boolean), the trim's refusal of a section end without the flag, and the subtraction's closing construction in `blend/fillet_cliff_overflow.rs` that nothing else reaches (`close_removal_region`, `lamina_hosts`, `lamina_chains`, `closing_face`, `close_round_loop`, `close_band`, `close_section_ended_round`, `covering_surface`, `lies_on_surface` and their private helpers).

- **Results:** SolidCraft's preset already had the flag on, so SolidCraft's results do not change. The goldens ran the default kernel (flag off): two `blend.fillet_executive_p3` rows reached the subtraction and now take the trim, `big-radius-box_diff_cyl_big_rad-r28-edge12` and `issue_2020_mitred_put_back_d9_full`. Their status, V/E/F counts (13/20/9, 8/12/6) and mass rows are unchanged. Only the representation records move: entity order and the faces' UV domains (the trim keeps the built surfaces' domains, such as a cone's `[0,1]²` instead of the Boolean's `[0,0.0455]×[0,1]` and a top plane's `[-68,108]×[-68,98]` instead of `[0.76,29.24]×[0.76,39.24]`), so the samples at domain fractions move. Old and new bodies were compared (MEASURED): `check_brep` Level2 is clean on both, the surface kinds per face are the same, the vertex sets match within 1.5e-14, and volume, centroid and inertia agree to 1e-12 relative (area to 2.6e-10 at the integrator's 1e-8 accuracy).
- **Tests:** the tests that ran both paths keep their trimmed half, with the same expected values: `wr695_overflow_trim_at_creases.rs` (the six bodies' closed forms; the #17673 variable round now pins the face, edge and vertex counts the subtraction built, which it compared against while the subtraction was the flag-off path), `issue_2715_refused_fillet_keeps_geometry.rs` (the subtraction's refusal case goes with the subtraction; the trim's and the kept features' stay). `NoRoomReason::CliffSubtractionLeavesNoSolid` and `CliffBoundaryBranches` are no longer built; they stay in the public enum.
