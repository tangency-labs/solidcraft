Updates: WR-695

#### WR-695 (update, 2026-11-16). `fillet_overflow_trim_at_creases` is removed

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246). A cliff overflow, a round with WR-674 section ends (WR-706) and an open G1 setback corner are always trimmed at their creases in place (`session/trim_at_creases.rs`); the subtraction of the region the blends remove is deleted, as smlib#79 step 6 deletes it in the fork. SolidCraft's preset already had the flag on.

Removed:
- the field `UpstreamFixes::fillet_overflow_trim_at_creases`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entry in `fixes.json`, the fixture opt-ins (`190-round-as-tall-as-its-wall.json`, `293-cube-corner-rounds-as-wide-as-faces.json`) and every test setting of it;
- the flag-off path: `FilletSession::subtract_removal_region` (`session/cliff_overflow.rs`), the session's `body_application_attributes` / `replace_application_attributes` (used only by its Boolean), the trim's refusal of a section end without the flag, and the subtraction's closing construction in `blend/fillet_cliff_overflow.rs` that nothing else reaches (`close_removal_region`, `lamina_hosts`, `lamina_chains`, `closing_face`, `close_round_loop`, `close_band`, `close_section_ended_round`, `covering_surface`, `lies_on_surface` and their private helpers).

- **Results:** SolidCraft's preset already had the flag on, so SolidCraft's results do not change. `Kernel::default()` results do change wherever a cliff overflow is reached: the WR-631 construction tests (now the trim; #1467's r 10.01 and 10.02 build instead of being refused), the default-kernel cases of `293-cube-corner-rounds-as-wide-as-faces.json` (refused by the trim) and two `blend.fillet_executive_p3` goldens rows, `big-radius-box_diff_cyl_big_rad-r28-edge12` and `issue_2020_mitred_put_back_d9_full`.
  - Both rows keep their status and V/E/F counts (13/20/9, 8/12/6). Their mass rows (integrator accuracy 1e-4) agree within the goldens' 1e-9 relative rule: largest moves are area 2.6e-10 (big radius) and volume 6.1e-12, centroid z 3.1e-11 and products 3.7e-11 (`issue_2020`). The topology and geometry records move: entity order, and each face keeps its built surface's UV domain instead of the Boolean's (a cone's `[0,1]^2` instead of `[0,0.0455]x[0,1]`; a top plane's `[-68,108]x[-68,98]` instead of `[0.76,29.24]x[0.76,39.24]`), so the samples at domain fractions move.
  - `issue_2020_mitred_put_back_d9_full` is PROVEN by `issue_2020_mitred_put_back_trimmed_at_creases.rs`: its 8 vertices and volume against the closed form and `check_brep` Level2.
  - `big-radius-box_diff_cyl_big_rad-r28-edge12` (a box less a cylinder, a round past the cylinder's rim) is MEASURED against the old body: both clean at `check_brep` Level2, the same surface kind per face, vertex sets within 1.5e-14.
- **Tests:** the tests that ran both paths keep their trimmed half, with the same expected values: `wr695_overflow_trim_at_creases.rs` (the six bodies' closed forms; the #17673 variable round now pins the face, edge and vertex counts the subtraction built, which it compared against while the subtraction was the flag-off path), `issue_2715_refused_fillet_keeps_geometry.rs` (the subtraction's refusal case goes with the subtraction; the trim's and the kept features' stay). `wr631_cliff_overflow_construction.rs`'s `an_overflow_whose_subtraction_leaves_no_solid_is_refused` is `a_round_a_hair_past_its_wall_builds_its_closed_form`: r 10.01 and 10.02 must build a manifold solid of the closed-form volume (1e-8), where the subtraction was allowed to refuse (`CliffSubtractionLeavesNoSolid`); its `_is_subtracted` tests are `_is_trimmed`, as are `wr635_636_cliff_overflow_closing.rs`'s. `NoRoomReason::CliffSubtractionLeavesNoSolid` and `CliffBoundaryBranches` are no longer built; they stay in the public enum.
