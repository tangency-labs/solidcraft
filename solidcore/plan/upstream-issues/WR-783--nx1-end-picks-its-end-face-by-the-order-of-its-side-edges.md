#### WR-783. A round's End beside two tangent faces picks its end face by the order of its side edges: one side builds, its mirror image comes out open
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Fillets and blends
- **Location:** `SmFilletNx1Corner::MakeCornerTopology` (`source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, the tail the port has as `blend::fillet_corner::topology::nx1_end::finish_nx1_end`): where no face holds both side edges and both are of the other convexity ("both are cliff edges, just pick one"), the end face is the first side edge's face. The fork's clean-up keep (WR-459, tangency-labs/smlib@1ccd461) then keeps every edge of the fillet's input by its id (`m_sPostProcessKeptEdges`).
- **What happens:**
  - A concave round ends at a vertex with four edges: its own, two sharp convex side edges, and a smooth edge between the two end-side faces, which meet tangentially there (a bore's flat wall and its round bottom). The round's end cap lies in the flat wall's plane, which holds both side edges; the round bottom holds only one.
  - Which side edge comes first round the vertex flips between a part and its mirror image. Where the round bottom's side comes first, the cap is laid on the cylinder, the corner's gap check finds the cylinder's extension off the other side edge, and the body is left with a three-edge hole at the end: not a closed solid.
  - With the end face picked as the face whose surface holds the other side edge, both sides build the same cap. The fork's keep then still differs: the vertical side edge is split at the round's rail, and the piece beside the cap keeps the input edge's id on one side and gets a new id on the other, so one side keeps a coplanar edge between the cap and the flat wall (28 faces, 75 edges, 49 vertices) and the other merges them (27, 73, 48).
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#15768, test11.sc from #15641 up to Extrude9: the concave ledge edge at (-15, 30, 33) rounded r 0.5 returns an open body; its mirror at (15, 30, 33) builds, V = V0 + 6 * 0.25 (1 - pi / 4) = 43914.279834127 mm³.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft (Brett Lawson's reproduction on #15641).
- **Confirmed:** in the port, by tracing both ends: the end rib's face is the bore plane on the right and the cylinder on the left; after the end-face fix, the clean-up keeps edge 66 (`kept=true`) on the left where it removes edge 73 on the right.
- **Fix in solidcore:** the end face is the side face whose surface holds the other side edge (`face_holds_edge`, the coincidence check the gap check runs); the clean-up keeps an edge between two of the input's faces, decided by the faces, never by the edge's id. Both rig cases `fillets/15768-fillet-ledge-concave-edge-beside-bore-r0-5` and its mirror build 27, 73, 48 at the closed-form volume.
