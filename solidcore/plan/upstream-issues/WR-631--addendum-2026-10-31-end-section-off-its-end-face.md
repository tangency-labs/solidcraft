Updates: WR-631

The SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@a307480 extends the cliff overflow's choice in `CreateFilletBrep` to a fillet whose end section runs off its end face (`SmFilletSolver::EndSectionsFitTheirFaces`, new). At an Nx1 end where the corner takes the end face as it is (both side edges of the filleted edge's convexity, neither tangent), the end section is the fillet's and the end face's surfaces' exact intersection between the rails' crossings of their side edges (`sm_SrfSrfIntersection`), classified against the end face. A section that runs off the face across a concave edge of it (under something standing on it) means the blend overflows its end face, and it is built as a cliff overflow is: cut by the end face's surface continued, the removed material subtracted. SolidCraft rig case: an 80 x 50 x 30 box shelled 2 open at the top, a 1 x 2 lip on the inner half of its rim, its outer corner edge chamfered 3: the Nx1 corner's end rib left its face and the chamfer came back unsewn (not a closed solid); now the lip stays over the chamfer, V0 - 135 = 22473.

The Rust port follows it under the same flag, `Kernel::fixes.fillet_cliff_overflow` (`FilletSolver::end_sections_fit_their_faces_with_ops` in `blend/fillet_solver/p5/end_sections_fit.rs`, called from `FilletExecutive::choose_cliff_overflow` through `SolverGeometryAccess::geometry_end_sections_fit`; the free section curve is classified through the new `FilletClassObject::FreeCurve` handle). Regression test: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr631_a307480_end_section_off_its_end_face.rs`.
