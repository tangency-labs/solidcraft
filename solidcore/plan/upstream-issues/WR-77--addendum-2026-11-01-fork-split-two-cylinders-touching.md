Updates: WR-77

#### WR-77 (update, 2026-11-01). The fork's split at singular crossings, ported; two cylinders touching inside at one point unite soundly (#2110)
- **What changes:** with `Kernel::fixes.ssi_split_curves_at_singular_points`, `SmSurfaceIntersector::DoIntersection` ends with the fork's `sm_SplitCurvesAtSingularPoints` (after `AddTangentNodes`, skipped for a self intersection): each crossing or near-tangent B-spline curve that is not degenerate has `s(t) = |n1 x n2|` sampled 8 times per unique knot span (plus the last knot); each sharp local minimum is refined by golden section (at most 80 steps, down to `SM_EFF_ZERO (1 + |a|)`), and the 3D curve and both UV curves are split by `CreateBySplitCurve` where `s < 1e-6`, `s` rises above `1e-3` again both since the previous split (or the start) and after the point, and the point is more than 10 approximation tolerances from both ends. Splits run from the last down; the tails are appended with the curve's type and deviation. Port: `crates/solidcore/src/intersect/surface_intersector/singular_splits.rs`, line by line from the fork.
- **How found:** tangency-labs/solidcore#2110 (SolidCraft's coverage case of a round between cylinders touching at a point, which failed before the round). The fork's tangency-labs/smlib@3c9d1d3 (replayed on 1.0.1 as 1ff9264; the same at 7ac037e, which SolidCraft reports builds the case).
- **Trigger:** A, a cylinder R 10 along x through (y, z) = (0, -10), x -20..20; B, R 5 along y through (x, z) = (0, -5), y -20..20; union. They touch inside at the origin, where the intersection's two loops round B meet at a node.
- **Confirmed:** at the default fixes the Rust port matches the pinned C++ (difftest `issue2110_*`, `fixtures/boolean.api_brep/issue_2110_tangent_cylinders.json`: status and level-2 check; for the issue's placement, A at 135 and B at 225 degrees, status and result counts only, because the pinned C++ crashes with SIGSEGV checking that result, likely UB-253). Upstream's result depends on where the seams lie: with a seam through the origin the union is sound; otherwise it fails 1001 in `MakeFaceuse`, returns A alone (B dropped, a body that checks clean), or returns a body failing `SmFace` test #29 (Face crosses Seam) and `SmEdgeuse` test #7 (Bad UVTrimCurve). With the flag, all 64 seam placements tried (45 degrees apart on each) unite to a manifold solid that checks clean at level 2, within 4e-7 of the closed-form volume 5000 pi - 1270.66448382136 = 14437.2987841276 (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2110_tangent_cylinders_union.rs`). The fork's C++ was not run here.
- **Interaction:** with `bspline_trim_preserves_uv_spans` (WR-441, solidcore-only) also on, 2 of the 64 placements (A's seam at 270 degrees, B's at 135 or 225) fail `SmBSplineCurve` test #15 (Bad Repeated CPts) in the check (likewise with every flag on; bisecting the flags with A at 270 and B at 135 leaves this one). With the split alone they check clean. Not addressed here.
- **Relation to #1304:** that open PR adds a split with the same purpose written from the issue text; it differs from the fork's (sampling, rise test, normals, refinement, splitting, statuses), as tangency-labs/solidcore#393's 2026-10-31 comment lists. This port follows the fork.
