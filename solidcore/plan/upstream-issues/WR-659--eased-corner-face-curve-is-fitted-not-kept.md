#### WR-659. An eased corner's patch is bounded by fitted iso-curves, not its face curves' own halves
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a corner hole's edges are degree-6 fits, not the curvature-continuous face curves)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet corner)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletCorner::MakeFaceBrep`, the N-sided patch branch. Each patch face is bounded by its surface's four natural iso-curves. The fork's fix, tangency-labs/smlib@4702889 (branch `replay/1.0.1`), adds the static `sm_FaceCurveHalfAlong`.
- **What happens:** at a set-back corner whose rounds ease into their curvature-continuous sections (the fork's `SmFilletCorner::m_dEaseDist`, `e74bbd3`), each face curve of the hole is one quintic span, G2 to both contact lines (`SmFilletEdge::CalcRailRailInterpolation`). Such a face curve lies on a face between two rounds' contact lines and is split at its middle. `N_FillNSidedHole` splits each side of the hole at the middle of its parameter and interpolates the halves by its surfaces only to tolerance, in the degree and knots of their cross-derivative fields. `MakeFaceBrep` then puts those iso-curves on the faces, so a face curve half came back degree 6 over 7 spans (13 points), 1e-8 off curvature-continuous with its contact line.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's G2 corner on a 60 x 40 x 20 box, its three edges at (60, 40, 20) rounded r 5, set back 8 to 15 and eased 0.5 to 5 (`smlib_blend_setback_open_eased`).
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#960); the fork's capi test `corner_setback_eased_face_curves_are_quintics`.
- **Confirmed:** in the fork, whose worst curvature at the hole's corners falls from 8.7e-9 to 5e-15 with the fix. Not reachable in the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`), which has no corner ease.
- **Fix (fork 4702889):** at a corner with an ease, each side of a patch face that lies on one of the corner's face curves (an `SM_FE_RAIL_RAIL_INTERPOLATION` edge with a B-spline curve) becomes that curve's own half. The half is a copy of the curve trimmed to the half its ends match (to the corner's approximation tolerance), reversed when the side runs the other way. The replacement happens before the patch face's orientation test. Corners without an ease are unchanged.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_corner_face_curve_halves` (default `false`). It is read in `FilletCorner::make_face_brep_with` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_corner/p1.rs`), which calls the private `face_curve_half_along` (the fork's `sm_FaceCurveHalfAlong` line for line; every evaluation, copy, trim and reversal returns its error). The copy has no owner, so its trim takes the system zone tolerance, as upstream's `GetZoneTol3d` of an unowned curve.
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr_659_eased_corner_face_curve_halves.rs`.
  - Off: a face curve half is degree 6.
  - On, at setback 10 and ease 3 and over setbacks {8, 10, 12} × eases {0.5, 1.25, 3}: every half is one span of degree at most 5; two halves meeting at a face curve's middle have equal curvature within 1e-9, and a half is flat at its contact line; the result is a manifold solid with sound topology.
  - No difftest case compares the opt-in path: the pinned C++ has no ease.
