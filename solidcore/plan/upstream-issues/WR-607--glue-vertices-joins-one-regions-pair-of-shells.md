#### WR-607. `SmBrep::GlueVertices` joins one region's pair of shells, leaving the other region's apart
- **Severity:** Wrong result (shells not joined, so the check reports an edge use and its radial in two shells)
- **Area:** Topology / B-rep editing
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp:9709`, `SmBrep::GlueVertices` (def. `:9596`): the search for a region both vertices are in keeps the last pair of shells it finds, and only that pair is joined.
- **What happens:** a solid's vertex is in two regions: a shell of the solid's region and one of the infinite region's. Where the two vertices' parts lie apart in both regions (a face that alone held two parts of a body together was deleted), the search finds a pair in each region but keeps one, so after the glue the other region still holds the two parts as two shells.
- **Trigger:** the fork's case, a 60 x 40 x 10 plate with a boss r 10 whose r 2 rim round is removed (`SmApiRemoveFillet` through the fork's ring removal): the boss's top is glued back to the rest at the new sharp edge's vertex, and SMLib's check reports an edge use pair in two shells. The port's test (`crates/solidcore/tests/fork_661f79c_glue_every_region.rs`): two 10 mm cubes in one B-rep, the second's solid shell moved into the first's solid region, glued at (10, 0, 0) / (20, 0, 0): the vertex is left in three shells, two in one region.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (the fork, tangency-labs/smlib 661f79c), #1060 step 3.
- **Confirmed:** no. The pinned C++ is not built for this case; the fork's commit is the evidence. The port reproduces it with the opt-in off (`upstream_glue_leaves_one_regions_pair_apart`).
- **Fix (fork):** tangency-labs/smlib 661f79c records the pair of every region both vertices are in and, after the first pair is joined as before, joins each other pair (skipping the joined pair, a shell paired with itself and a vertex shell): its children transferred with its wire edges (the caller's list, or the deleted shell's own), the emptied shell removed from its region and deleted. It also drops the fork's earlier workaround (`SmBrep::JoinShellsAtVertex`, called by its ring fillet removal, WR-605).
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.glue_vertices_joins_every_region` (default `false`, upstream's behaviour) in `glue_with_notification_identity` (`crates/solidcore/src/topology/brep/p5.rs`), the path every `glue_vertices` entry point takes. Tests: `upstream_glue_leaves_one_regions_pair_apart` (flag off: 3 shells), `glue_joins_the_pair_of_every_shared_region` (flag on: 2 shells, one per region), `cubes_in_their_own_regions_glue_the_same_with_and_without_the_fix` (one shared region: the same either way).
