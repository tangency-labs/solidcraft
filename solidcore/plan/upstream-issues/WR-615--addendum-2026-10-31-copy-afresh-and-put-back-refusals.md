#### WR-615 addendum (2026-10-31): the mitred group's copy afresh, and typed put-back refusals
- **What changed (copy afresh):** the fork's "taken in afresh" step after the mitred-group removal (`source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetFaces.cpp` at 2fc375f: `new SmBrep( *m_pBrep, NULL, NULL )`, `RemoveAllTopology`, `MergeBrep( *pWork )`) is now ported in `OffsetFacesExecutive::take_off`. It goes through the boolean merge's arena-store wrappers (`boolean::merge::p3::copy_brep` / `combine_brep`), which carry the `SmFollowedBlendMark` marks with the copy as `SM_AB_COPY` does. This removes the previous addendum's deviation. The copy renumbers the entities but keeps their order, so no result changes: #1060's test still gives the fork's numbers.
- **What changed (refusals):** when the fillet executive fails to put the blends back for any reason except room, `OffsetFacesRefusal::NotPutBack` is now recorded. The status stays the fillet's, and the reason carries the fillet's error comment. Before, these failures had no typed refusal (`None`): refusals A ("Fillet Corner Error: Corner Edge Computation Failure") and B ("Fillet Solver Error: Unable to Process Rollover") in #2020. The fork passes the status on untyped (`SER(eStat)`), so this variant is not in the fork.
- **Trigger:** #2020. The box and rounds are the same as in #1060, but the top is offset by -6.9, -7, -7.5, -8, -9 or -9.5. With SolidCraft's flags, every depth except -9 builds, with the analytic volume to 1e-6 relative.
- **Open (d = -9):** the fork gives 6 faces and V 1006.320632128887, but the port refuses with `NotPutBack`. At -9 the top is at z 1 and the rail at z -2. The side edge's run-on (WR-610/WR-643) reaches z = -factor × 1, so the rail is reachable only at the doubled extension factor 2 (the B465 retry), and then it lies exactly on the end of the interval. A solver keeps its doubled factor. One corner (40, 30) therefore finds the rail on one geometry but not the other, and `AdjustTopologyNx2` splits it into a vertex at z -391.
  - With the flags off, the pairing of geometries to corners (the corners, corner vertices, adjusted topology, solver geometry and corner edges) matches the v1.0.1 C++ on that B-rep. The cases are `issue_2020_mitred_put_back_d9_*` of `blend.fillet_executive_p3`.
  - The group removal itself matches the C++ to 0 error (`mitred_rounds_box_top_r3` of `blend.feature_executive`).
