Updates: WR-77

#### WR-77 (update, 2026-11-16). `ssi_split_curves_at_singular_points` is removed

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, group-B batch intersect). `SmSurfaceIntersector::DoIntersection` (`intersect/surface_intersector/p5.rs`) always splits the traced crossing and near-tangent curves of a non-self intersection at the singular points they pass through (`surface_intersector/singular_splits.rs`, the fork's `sm_SplitCurvesAtSingularPoints`). Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::ssi_split_curves_at_singular_points`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, and every test setting of it.

- **Tests:** `tests/issue_2110_tangent_cylinders_union.rs` runs every seam placement on the default kernel (level 2 clean, manifold, the closed-form volume); its four upstream-behaviour tests are removed. `tests/fork_099cf96_exact_class_cases.rs` no longer sets the flag for the pipes and replaces `the_pipes_join_only_with_wr77`, which pinned upstream's 1001, with `the_pipes_join`: the pipes' body builds on the default kernel and checks clean at level 2.
- **Results:** 16 goldens rows move. In the 11 `surface.surface_p2` intersection rows and the 2 `topology.brep_planar_section` torus sections, the curves are the old ones cut at their singular points: the pieces reproduce the old curves (a certified control-point bound), and the surface normals are parallel at every cut. The 3 `boolean.api_brep` `issue2110_*` unions build the bodies the regression test checks against the closed form. The pull request's class table gives the check of each row.
