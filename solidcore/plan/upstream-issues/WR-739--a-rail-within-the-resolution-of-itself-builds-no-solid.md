#### WR-739. A round or chamfer whose rail would have its ends within the part's resolution builds a body of no volume or fails with a bare 1001
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Fillets
- **Location:** `SmFilletExecutive::CreateFilletBrep`: nothing checks, before `CreateFilletCorners`, that a rail's two ends are farther apart than the vertices at them resolve.
- **What happens:** a constant ball's round or chamfer along an arc whose rail is an arc much smaller than the edge (a chamfer just short of the round it runs round: on the plane square to the round's axis its rail has radius `R - d`) has, for `R - d` within a couple of tolerances, a rail whose end vertices (each with the blend's approximation tolerance) overlap: the two ends are one vertex. The corners and the merge then build on a rail that has no length to them: a body that is no solid, or a failure deep in the corners.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcore#2546 (SolidCraft #9522): a 30 x 30 x 30 box with its vertical edges rounded R5, its bottom loop chamfered d with `fillet_chamfer_cone_on_arc` and WR-740's fix. The bottom face's corner arcs, radius 5 - d over a quarter turn, have their ends `sqrt(2) (5 - d)` apart; the blend's approximation tolerance there is 1.6e-4. Up to d = 4.9997737 the chamfer builds at the closed form; d = 4.9998 returns a body of volume 0 that `check_brep` level 2 rejects; d = 4.9999 and 4.999999 fail with 1001 in `FindFilletBrepEdge`. d = 5 builds: the arcs close to points, the cones' apexes on the rounds' axes. Past 5 the arcs' radius 5 - d is negative, each rail on the far side of its round's axis: d 5.0001 fails with 1001, and d 5.0002 to 5.1 build bodies that `check_brep` level 2 accepts but whose volume exceeds d = 5's (25161.33 at 5.0002, 25115.81 at 5.1, against 25094.40), so less is removed by a larger chamfer.
- **How found:** sweeping the #2546 band after WR-740.
- **Confirmed:** yes, against the pinned C++ in the difftest (CI run 41788948221): `blend.fillet_executive_p3` `big-rad-filleted_box-r2.0-top-edges` and `-r3.0-` (`smFilletTest.cpp:1176`: `filleted_box.smb`, a 10 cube whose vertical edges are rounded R1, its top loop rounded r 2 and 3). The C++ builds an 18-face body; the port before this change built the same one, which the difftest compared equal. Each corner face is the round's section turned about an R1 axis it crosses (rail radius `1 - r < 0`), a spindle torus through its own axis: the part of the section past the axis turns through the part before it, so each corner face crosses itself and the body bounds no solid. Up to r = 1 the round builds, its removed volume the Pappus closed form `32 a + 2 pi a (1 - c r)` (`a = r^2 (1 - pi/4)`, `c = (10 - 3 pi)/(12 - 3 pi)`) within 2e-7. The port refuses them naming 1. Both parked on this record. (The #9522 chamfers themselves fail earlier in the C++, on WR-740.)
- **Rust port:** unconditional (no flag: solidcore has detached from SMLib, owner 2026-11-04, #2647). `FilletExecutive::refuse_rails_below_resolution` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_executive/p3.rs`) refuses such a blend before the corners with `FilletErrorType::NoRoom`, `NoRoomReason::RailBelowResolution`, naming the largest size whose rails the part resolves (open; on the error, `SolidError::FilletRefused`) and, in the error info's comment, the size at which the rail closes to a point (5 on the #9522 cube, which builds). `blend::fillet_exact::rail_below_resolution` decides it in closed form: for faces that are coaxial quadrics tracing lines in the meridian half-plane (a plane square to the axis, a cylinder, a cone), the ball's centre is where their offsets meet there, each rail point its foot on its face, and both move linearly with the ball's radius `t`, so the rail's radius is `r(t) = R_e + t (r - R_e) / d` (`R_e` the edge's radius) and its ends are `C_e |r(t)| / R_e` apart (`C_e` the edge's chord). The rail is refused when that is within the resolution, two of the solver's approximation tolerances, and is not a point to rounding; the limit is where it equals the resolution, `5 - 2 x 1.6e-4 / sqrt(2) = 4.999773725830018` here, and the point size `-R_e / slope`, 5. A rail on the far side of the axis from the edge (`r(t) < 0`) is refused whatever its length (`NoRoomReason::RailPastAxis`), naming the point size, the largest that keeps the rail on the edge's side (each refusal names its own cause's limit: on the R5 and R3 rounded block of `a_ball_that_does_not_fit_is_no_room_before_the_corners` a round of 7 is refused naming 5, though the R3 round allows less). Owner ruling on #2546: such a size is refused, never collapsed onto the point case. Solidcore-native, no fork commit.
