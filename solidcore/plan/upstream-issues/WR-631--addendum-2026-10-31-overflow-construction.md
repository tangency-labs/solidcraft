Updates: WR-631

The construction of an accepted cliff overflow is ported (the rest of tangency-labs/smlib@8ba4667, rev `68fb9162`, with the Nx1 miss test of tangency-labs/smlib@3f22e97), behind the same `Kernel::fixes.fillet_cliff_overflow`. An accepted overflow builds; it is no longer refused with the WR-302 message.

- **Open ends** (`SmFilletNx1Corner::CalcCornerEdgeGeom`): in an overflow a rail end off the fillet surface by more than ten times the corner's tolerance, or found on its side edge's line past the edge's end (off the edge by more than the tolerance, 3f22e97), is a miss; both rails may miss. A miss that does not run on deletes the end rib and notes the end (`NoteOpenEnd`) instead of setting the global merge: `FilletCorner::drop_end_rib`, `blend/fillet_corner/p1.rs`; the session's `edges_calculate` records `FilletExecutive::open_ends`.
- **Subtraction** (`SmFilletExecutive::DoFilleting`, `SubtractRemovalRegion`, `sm_HoldsItsLaw`): `blend/fillet_executive/session/cliff_overflow.rs`. A variable round on planes must keep its section's middle on its surface at seven points; each open end is cut by the end face's plane (`CutFilletAtOpenEnd`); the sheet is closed (`CloseRemovalRegion`: the host test, the chain walk and the closing face are separate functions, `blend/fillet_cliff_overflow.rs`), stitched, oriented and checked to be a manifold solid, and subtracted from the target by the Boolean.
- **Not covered by the fork's 68fb9162 either:** a box edge rounded r10.02 on its 10 high face (the fillet meets the bottom face 2e-5 from its edge) builds an empty body; the fork's test uses r10.05 and up, which build.

Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr631_cliff_overflow_construction.rs` (closed-form volumes: a stepped bar's round over a step rim and chamfer under a wall; the 80 x 5 x 5 bar r9/r10/r12, #1466; the 40 x 20 x 10 box r10.05/r10.07/r12, #1467; the 10 x 20 x 30 box R12, 5384.17828518, and the variable rounds 2 -> 11, 20, 30, 50 from either end; the round over a through hole, #422, 7580.28731536; each refused without the flag; determinism).
