#### WR-505. `SmBSplineSurface::HasInternalPole` tests a corner row at a repeated knot, not the row through it
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a surface with no internal pole is reported as having one; the check fails)
- **Area:** Surfaces / B-spline surface validity
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmBSplineSurface.cpp`, `SmBSplineSurface::HasInternalPole`, its U walk: `lCPIndexU = lKntCntU - lRowCntU - 1` with `lRowCntU = iDegreeU - (sMultsU[ii] - 1)`, and its V walk: `lCPIndexV = lKntCntV - lRowCntV - 1` with `lRowCntV = iDegreeV - (sMultsV[ii] - 1)`.
- **What happens:** the row whose basis function starts at an interior knot is `lKntCntU - 1 - iDegreeU`. The code moves one row further for each repeated copy of the knot (`lRowCntU` grows as the multiplicity does), so at a double knot it tests the corner row beside the row through the knot. A horn torus's corner rows lie on its axis next to its boundary pole, so they coincide, and the check reports an internal pole the surface does not have.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcore#1515: a horn torus (major radius 5, minor radius 5, a disc of radius 5 centred 5 from the axis, revolved) whose NURB runs once round its tube from the inner pole, so the pole is the NURB's boundary and the corner rows on the axis lie beside it. SMLib's check reports an internal pole on it.
- **How found:** SolidCraft fork tangency-labs/smlib#41, reported in tangency-labs/solidcore#1515.
- **Confirmed:** no. The pinned C++ is not built on this machine, so the fork's change is the evidence. The port reproduces the report with the opt-in off: `issue_1515_horn_torus_has_no_internal_pole` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/surface/assert_valid.rs`) finds a pole.
- **Fix (fork):** tangency-labs/smlib#41 changes only the starting index, `lCPIndexU = lKntCntU - 1 - iDegreeU` and `lCPIndexV = lKntCntV - 1 - iDegreeV`; `lRowCntU`/`lRowCntV` are still used for the rows tested after it.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.internal_pole_row_through_knot` (default `false`, upstream's behaviour) in `has_internal_pole` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/surface/assert_valid.rs`), at both the U and the V starting index. Tests: `issue_1515_horn_torus_has_no_internal_pole` (the horn torus: reported with the opt-in off, not with it on) and `issue_1515_real_pole_at_a_double_knot_is_found` (a real pole at a double knot, which upstream misses and the opt-in finds).
