#### WR-754. `SmApiBoolean` fails on an operand that touches itself along a line carried as two coincident edges of two faces each
- **Severity:** Wrong result or refusal (a union filling a pocket gives a body that is not a closed solid with the default fixes, or fails in `boolean::topology_intersector::p1::relate` with 1001 under SolidCraft's fix set; SolidCraft refuses the Join)
- **Area:** Booleans / operand representation
- **Location:** `crates/solidcore/src/boolean/api_brep.rs`, `merge_boolean_by`: the operands go to the merge as given. The representation comes from the record round trip: `crates/solidcore-convert/src/brep/record.rs` `split_touching` writes an edge a shell uses more than twice as one edge per face pair, and `to_kernel` builds one kernel edge per record edge (its contract: counts and `Order` match the record).
- **What happens:** the kernel's own results carry a solid that touches itself along a line as one edge of all four faces (a hole's bottom meeting two pocket walls along a segment of their corner edge), and the merge handles that form. The same solid after a record round trip (SolidCraft's body between features) carries the line as two coincident edges of two faces each, sharing both vertices; the body passes `check(All)`, but the merge of a tool whose edge lies along that line relates to two operand edges and either builds a bad body or fails in `relate`.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#12785: a block with a hexagonal pocket through it and a 2.5 mm hole from an end face ending exactly at a pocket corner; filling the pocket with the same hexagon as a Join. The cut's own body joins; after `to_record`/`from_record` the join fails. Sonnet's stand-in with the hole ending in a flat wall (no self-touch) joins. The rc.3 rig's dumped operands (`SmApiBoolean-a.smb`, 38 edges) fail the same way on upstream main and on the pin a445bdaac; the cut's body has 37 edges.
- **How found:** SolidCraft triage of #12785; the rig's `--dumps` of the failing call, a topology listing of the dumped operand against the ops-crate stand-in (one 8-use edge versus two 4-use edges), and a probe gluing the two edges back before the union (works with the default fixes and with the imprint flags).
- **Confirmed:** yes, in the port, with the ops crate (`crates/solidcore-ops/tests/boolean.rs`, `mod pocket_with_hole_at_its_corner`: the round-trip case fails without the glue) and the rig's dumps. Not against the C++ fork: upstream SMLib has no record format and never meets this form from its own results; given such a body it would merge it as given.
- **Fix:** always on (no flag): `merge_boolean_by` first glues, in each operand, the coincident edges that share both vertices and the curve kind, whose faces are all distinct and whose curves are proved within the B-rep tolerance of each other over their whole intervals, into one edge (in one shell, or in two shells that touch along the line: the round trip leaves the self-touching solid as two shells, ShellId 0 and 1 in the test's body) (`GlueEdgesGeneral`, no gap, with region nesting), restoring the kernel's own form. The proof is a bound on the two edges' Hausdorff distance, rounding included (`coincidence_bound`): for two lines, the larger distance between their segments' ends; for two non-rational B-splines on one interval, the convex-hull bound of `nurbs::crv_deviation::certified_distance`. Any other pair, a NaN, or a bound above the tolerance is not glued, and the operand goes to the merge as given. The gluing is all or nothing: an ordinary `GlueEdgesGeneral` failure restores the operand from a snapshot and the merge runs on it as given. Edges whose faces repeat (a face on both sides of the line: the kernel's crack edges, which its check refuses glued) are left alone; the result's export splits the edge again as before. The import keeps its one-edge-per-record-edge contract.
- **Also covered:** two solids touching along an edge (`split_shared`: one copy per shell) are glued the same way, as the kernel's own union of such solids shares the edge between the shells (the convert crate's `lumps_and_solids_touching_along_an_edge_go_round`).
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#3160.
