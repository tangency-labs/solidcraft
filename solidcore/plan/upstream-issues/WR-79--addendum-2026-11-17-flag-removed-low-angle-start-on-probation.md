Updates: WR-79

#### WR-79 (update, 2026-11-17). `ssi_low_angle_start_on_probation` is removed

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, group G6). `AdvSurfaceIntersector::find_interior_curves` (`intersect/adv_surface_intersector.rs`) always traces an interior start point whose normals are within 5 degrees (guess) or 2 degrees (solution) on probation, keeping the trace only as a closed transversal loop larger than twice the tolerance, where upstream skips the point. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::ssi_low_angle_start_on_probation`, SolidCraft's preset line (which already turned it on), the goldens' SolidCraft arm, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, and the test settings in `issue_2020_offset_mitred_below_depth.rs` and `wr650_tabbed_plate_mitred_rounds.rs`. `issue_396_probation_start_points` keeps its fixed-path assertions; its flag-off run is removed, and its off/on comparison of the transversal cut becomes an assertion that the cut is one crossing loop on the true circle.

- **Results:** 9 goldens rows move (`intersect.adv_surface_intersector` 3, `intersect.surface_intersector_p5` 6), each equal to its old row but for the recorded `curve_trace_direction` state, which a rejected probation trace (traced back the other way before the loop test rejects it) leaves at `-1`: no curve, status or count changes.
- **Tests:** `crates/goldens/tests/wr76_wr79_flag_retirement_moves.rs` replays the 9 rows from the committed inventory and asserts each differs from its old row only in `+1 -> -1` values, each at a trace direction slot found by decoding the record: the state's `trace_dir` in `intersect.surface_intersector_p5`, and in `intersect.adv_surface_intersector` a `put_state` direction slot of the record walked op by op. `issue_396_probation_start_points` covers the probation path.
