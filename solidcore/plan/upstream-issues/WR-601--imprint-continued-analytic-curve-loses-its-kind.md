#### WR-601. An intersection curve continued to its proto vertex stops being a line, circle or ellipse
- **Severity:** Wrong result (an exact analytic edge becomes a plain B-spline, or, in the port, is not continued)
- **Area:** Booleans / proto topology
- **Location:** the SolidCraft fork's `sm_CreateExactlyExtendedCurve` (tangency-labs/smlib a8caccd, `source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp`), called by `SmEdgeDefinition::ExtendEndToProtoVertex` from `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint`. The pinned v1.0.1 (3fd5b95) has no continuation (WR-453); this record is a defect of that fix.
- **What happens:** the continuation takes any `SmBSplineCurve`, the analytic subclasses too, and rebuilds the continued NURB as a plain `SmBSplineCurve`. The concave arc of a union, an exact `SmCircle` from `SmCone::IntersectWithPlane` that stops 5.4e-10 short of its tangent point, comes out a B-spline. The Rust port of a8caccd continues plain B-splines only, so there a line, circle or ellipse was not continued at all and kept its gap.
- **Trigger:** the SolidCraft test-peg part: a bar's top plane meets a full-turn boss wall in a circle, cut into three pieces at the boss's tangent points with the bar's sides (tangency-labs/smlib bec8a0b, from gdb).
- **How found:** SolidCraft fork commit tangency-labs/smlib@bec8a0b.
- **Confirmed:** no (the pinned C++ has no continuation to compare; the fork reports the arc as `SmCircle` after the fix, was `SmBSplineCurve`).
- **Fix:** a continued line, circle or ellipse is made that kind again over the continued NURB (`sm_AnalyticOfContinuedNurb`): a circle or ellipse keeps its frame, radii and inside-out sense, its angular domain carried on to the new end (`remainder` of the new end's angle from the old end, which must move the domain outward and stay within 360 degrees and the maximum analytic domain); a line that is not inside out runs between the continued ends over the same interval. The new curve must have the NURB's natural interval and evaluate within `ANALYTIC_TOL_SCALE * SM_EFF_ZERO * (1 + max |coordinate|)` of the NURB at nine points, or the curve is not continued, as for a parabola or a hyperbola.
- **Rust port:** `crates/solidcore/src/topology/proto_topology/p2/imprint_ends.rs` (`extended_curve`, `analytic_of_continued_nurb`). Opt-in `Kernel::fixes.imprint_extension_keeps_analytic_curve`, which acts only with `imprint_extends_edge_to_proto_vertex`; off by default. Tests: `wr601_continued_circle_stays_a_circle`, `wr601_continued_ellipse_stays_an_ellipse`, `wr601_continued_line_stays_a_line` in the same file (flag off: not continued; flag on: the same kind, on the curve, equal to the old curve over the old interval).
