#### WR-681. An extended rail end is where the end curve leaves the fillet, which at a tangent end is past the rail: a chamfer ending there fails the corner
- **Severity:** Failure (status 1001)
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`, `SmFilletVertex::CalcRailIntExtendedEdgeuse`, which places a rail end of type `SM_FV_RAIL_X_EXTENDED_EDGEUSE` (given by `SmFilletNx1Corner::MakeCornerTopology` at a mixed-convexity corner) at the end of `sm_SrfSrfIntersection` of the fillet surface with the end face's extended surface, marched from the other rail's end with no second known point.
- **What happens:** where the side face runs tangentially into the end face along the side edge, the intersection does not leave the fillet surface at the rail. A chamfer's plane cuts the cylinder in an ellipse that touches the rail line on the side face, so the march runs on through that point to the chamfer's far side: on the fork's tangent peg the fork's a87c502 message has the rail end put at (45.29, 2, 10), on the other rail past the corner, and the cap edge between it and the corner is not found (`SM_FILERR_VERTEX_PROBLEM`, "Corner Edge Computation Failure", 1001). A round's surface touches the cylinder there, and its march stops at that point only because the two surfaces are tangent.
- **Trigger:** the SolidCraft fork's tangent peg (capi `tangent_peg`, tangency-labs/smlib@9bebf34): a bar 40 x 16 x 10 whose far end is a half disc R 8 about (40, 8), with a disc R 8 standing 10 on its top about the same axis, joined. A chamfer d2 of the front top edge (0, 0, 10) to (40, 0, 10), whose front face runs tangent into the cylinder along x = 40.
- **How found:** the fork's capi test `tangent_end_front_chamfer_d2` (smlib#76, tangency-labs/solidcore#2243).
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run of the pinned reference here (the case is not in a difftest unit). The fork's 9bebf34 records the 1001 on its `replay/1.0.1` branch; solidcore's port fails the same way with the fork's other fixes on and WR-600's tangent end off (`crates/solidcore/tests/fork_9bebf34_tangent_end_cases.rs`, `front_chamfer_ends_at_the_rail_edge_crossing`).
- **Fork fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@a87c502 (smlib#76; its twin 169adea on the fork's `smlib-tangent-ends` branch has the same diff): outside the 3x2 mixed-convexity corner, a vertex classed on a side edge use takes the solver's rail/edge crossing (`CalcRailIntEdgeuse`, as for `SM_FV_RAIL_X_EDGEUSE`; `RailEdgeuseIntersect` continues a straight side edge past its end), kept when it drops onto the end face's surface within the solver's approximation tolerance. It is passed to `sm_SrfSrfIntersection` as the second known point, and the end curve is trimmed between the two rail ends (`SM_FIL_SURF_INT_FAILURE` when either is more than ten times that tolerance off it). Otherwise the vertex is reset to unprocessed and the march decides, as before. The fork removed this again two commits later (tangency-labs/smlib@0cc48bd), once a tangent end became a rail/edge crossing of the trace with its end rib built outward (6d73e4f; WR-600's addenda here): at the fork revision SolidCraft pins (cd47efd) an extended rail end is the march's end again. The port of 0cc48bd (#2248) retires the flag below with it.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. The opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_extended_rail_end_at_side_edge_crossing` ports a87c502 in `FilletBrep::calc_rail_int_extended_edgeuse_with_context` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_geom/p4.rs`); `CalcRailIntEdgeuse` runs through the new `FilletEdgeContext::calc_rail_int_edgeuse` (the live session's solvers, or the indexed table, now taken mutably). On the peg, with every fix SolidCraft turns on except WR-600's `fillet_nx1_tangent_side_edge_same_convexity` (the fork's later tangent end), the front chamfer builds: volume 9341.798353087 at accuracy 1e-7, against the fork's 9341.798354 and the quadrature's 9341.798377; it checks clean. The front round and curvature round keep their volumes to the last bit (9383.622468040, 9393.307895663), as the fork says of its round cases. With WR-600 on too, no tangent end reaches this code: the flag changes none of the peg's front blends. Test: `crates/solidcore/tests/fork_9bebf34_tangent_end_cases.rs`, which also brings over the fork's thirteen tangent-end cases with its known-failing marks.
