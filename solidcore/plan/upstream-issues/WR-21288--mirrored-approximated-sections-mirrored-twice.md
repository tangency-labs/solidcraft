#### WR-21288. A mirrored round's approximated sections that already have the most knots are mirrored a second time on the second pass, back to the unmirrored arc
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Fillets (sections)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletStandardSolver.cpp`, `SmCircularCrossSectionFSG::CreateSurfaceFromCurves` with `m_bApproximateArcs` and `m_bMirror` (the `// When asked - mirror the cross section` block, after the `// on 2nd pass with equal gapVector radii` branch).
- **What happens:** the first pass builds each section's approximated arc, mirrors it about its rail chord and stores it, and notes the most and fewest unique knots. When they differ, the second pass takes each stored section again: a section with fewer knots is built again with the most (unmirrored) and then mirrored, but a section that already has the most knots is left as stored, mirrored already, and the mirror block mirrors it again (`kkk == 1`), back to the unmirrored arc. The skin through mixed mirrored and unmirrored sections leaves the round.
- **Trigger:** `SmApiBrep` surface-surface round between the bilinear sheets z = 0 and x = 5 (10 by 10), radii 2 and 2, tolerance 0.01, `xsect` 1, mirror and complement (goldens row `surface-surface-mirror-complement-approx` of `blend.fillet_executive_p3`). The rails run from y = 15 to y = -5. With the offset extended from the base (WR-458), the end sections' rail points sit an ulp off the inner ones', their sweep is 4.712388980384691 against 4.71238898038469, and their approximated arcs take 8 unique knots against 7, so the second pass runs and the end sections go back to the circle about x = 3, z = 2: the face is up to 13.8 off the closed form (the cylinder of radius 2 about x = 5, z = 0). The pinned extension gives every section 7 knots, one pass, and the closed form.
- **How found:** the WR-458 flag retirement (#21288) moved the row; its certificate (a Bernstein bound of the face against the closed form's cylinder) failed. Instrumenting the sections showed four identical centres and rail chords, the knot counts 8, 7, 7, 8, and the two end rows on the unmirrored circle.
- **Confirmed:** by reading the C++ (the second pass's mirror block runs for every section the equal-radii branch does not `continue` past); the pinned C++ reaches it on any mirrored approximated round whose sections' knot counts differ.
- **Rust port:** fixed unconditionally (no flag): on the second pass with equal radii, a section that already has the most knots is kept as the first pass made it (`continue`); only a section built again is mirrored. Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr21288_mirrored_sections_once.rs`; the goldens certificate `surface_surface_row_is_a_certified_definition` (3.15e-3 off the cylinder against the row's 0.01, the arcs' own approximation).
