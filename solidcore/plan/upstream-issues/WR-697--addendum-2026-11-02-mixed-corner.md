Updates: WR-697

#### WR-697 (update, 2026-11-02). A convex and a concave blend that continue each other across a tangent seam meet in a 3x2 mixed-convexity corner built across their crossed rails
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Fillets (corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletCorner::Create` (case 3 of two filleted edge uses builds `SmFillet3x2MixedConvexityCorner`) and that corner's `CreateCornerPatch`.
- **What happens:** at a vertex V of three edges, a convex and a concave blended edge can continue each other G1 on a common plane T, with the third edge a straight tangent seam between their other faces F1 and F2. An example is the tangent peg's front top edge and the concave arc where its top meets the cylinder its front face runs tangent into. The common face closes to a cusp at V, so the blends' rails on T cross at X, before the corner (for #2256, X = (40 - sqrt(32 r), r, 10)). The pinned corner is built across the crossed rails and stands out of the faces (WR-710).
- **Source:** smlib#82 (the Mixed corner). This is built in solidcore from smlib#82: solidcore-native, no fork commit. The fork's `wip/82-mixed-corner` construction (`SmBlendCornerMixed.cpp`) served as the reference and the starting point, not as a bit-for-bit target.
- **Rust port:** under the same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner`. `blend::blend_corner::mixed::classify_mixed` decides the case in `FilletCorner::create` before WR-710's refusal. The corner must have three edges at the vertex, two of them blended, of opposite convexity, continuing each other within the solvers' tangency tolerance. The third edge must be a straight tangent seam, and the blends' common face a plane. Such a corner is the blend corner's Mixed corner (`BlendCornerEvent::Mixed`); a corner that matches the shape but fails the classification builds no corner.
  - **Topology** (`FilletCorner::make_mixed_topology`) has six vertices: X, the rails' crossing on T; A and B, the rails' ends on F1 and F2; P and Q, where the wall rails cross the seam's line; and V. It has seven precomputed edges. Each blend is trimmed along the other's rail on T swept along T's normal, from X to its rail end.
  - **Geometry** (`calc_mixed_vert_geom`, `calc_mixed_edge_geom`): the corner is two faces split along the inflection X-V in T. Face L (X, A, P, V) lies with B1 and face U (X, V, Q, B) with B2. Each face is a bicubically blended Coons patch in R^4 (`blend::blend_corner::mixed::algebra`), G1 to its blend, to the walls and to the other face. For chamfers the faces are bilinear Coons patches (G0).
  - **Trace rule** (`fillet_solver::p4::c2_trace_to_edge_ends`): with the flag on, a blend's trace runs on to its edge's ends before it is extended, where the end's corner is neither blending nor degenerate. This is what makes r 5 build.
  - **Interfaces:** the Mixed branches follow the Setback interface's in the session's vertex, edge and patch steps.
- **Triggers:** #2256's STEP-reimported tangent peg (`crates/solidcore/tests/data/issue_2256_peg_step_reimported.smb`), with the front top edge and the concave arc blended together: rounds r 2, rounds r 5 and chamfers d 2.
- **Confirmed:** in solidcore, graded on geometry (the measures of the fork's capi test `mixed_rounds_together`, re-implemented on solidcore calls). Every case passes `check_brep` Level2. It has 11 faces and stays within the unblended box (min y -5.0e-15 at r 2, -2.9e-15 at r 5, -8.3e-15 at d 2), with a vertex at X within 1e-14.
  - **Continuity (rounds):** G1 across every edge in the corner's box (worst 1.1e-11 rad at r 2, 4.9e-12 at r 5) and across every interior knot line (worst crease 1.1e-10).
  - **Volume:** the volume outside the two corner boxes agrees with its closed form to 5.7e-16 relative at r 2, 1.9e-15 at r 5, and exactly at d 2.
  - **Fork comparison (information only):** the fork's d443f10 was built locally and run on the same body. Its box minima are the same to all 17 digits printed at r 5 and d 2. Its outside volumes are 7958.2564501664401 (r 2) and 6122.8803851868306 (r 5); solidcore's are 7958.2564501664492 and 6122.8803851868342. Its total volume at r 2 (1e-12) is 9402.00748737034 and solidcore's is 9402.019433942927. That 1.3e-6 relative gap lies within the corner boxes and was not investigated further.
  - **Fork chamfers:** at d 2 the fork's own measure leaves a seam of 0.785 rad (its chamfer corner is G0 by construction), and its outside volume is 4.2e-3 off the rounds' closed form, which is the wrong reference for chamfers. solidcore grades chamfers on the chamfer closed form.
  - **Without the flag:** the pinned corner is built 6.544e-3 out of the front face y = 0, or refused by WR-710's flag.
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2360_mixed_corner.rs` covers the cases above. It also has the flag-off case, `rounds_together_r2_without_blend_corner_stands_out`. The `algebra` unit tests are in `crates/solidcore/src/blend/blend_corner/mixed/algebra.rs`.
