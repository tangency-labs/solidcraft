Updates: WR-685

#### WR-685 (update, 2026-11-02). Where a variable round's radius law has a corner, the round is not the union of its balls there
- **Severity:** Wrong result (the 20 cube's edge y = z = 20 rounded by the law 4 -> 2 -> 4 removes 35.1156 in the pinned build against the union of the balls' 39.5421; the fork before its law corners, with the pieces joined from their own end circles, 39.6456)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet solver)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletStandardSolver.cpp`, `SmCircularCrossSectionFSG::CreateSurface` (the fork's `SmGnSectionFSG::CircleSurface` in `SmFilletSection.cpp`), and the fillet executive's split of a round at its radius corners (fork only: `SmFilletExecutive::NoteRadiusCornerSurfaces` and `SplitFilletFacesAtRadiusCorners`).
- **What happens:** at a corner of the radius law the ball's radius changes slope, and each side of the corner ends on the corner ball's touch circle of its own side (the envelope's `<p - c, c'> = -r r'` with that side's `r'`), both through the same two rail points. The pinned build skins one smooth surface across the corner and overshoots there (#430). The fork, before 7babe17, skinned each side on its own and joined them along one boundary row. Neither is the union of the balls: where the law dips the circle after lies ahead of the one before and the corner ball's own sphere shows between them; where it peaks the sides run past each other and the round ends where they cross.
- **Trigger:** the fork's capi section cases `variable_round_law_dip` (above, also from the other end and as a quadratic law with a corner: 39.542073 removed, nine faces), `variable_round_law_peak` (the 10 x 20 x 30 box's edge x = 10, z = 30, the law 2 -> w -> 2 from y = 0, w 2.5, 3, 3.2 and 4: 21.799882, 27.093683, 29.389966 and 39.541034 removed, eight faces) and `variable_round_law_dips` (the cube's edge, 4, 2, 4, 2 at 0, 0.25, 0.75, 1: a dip then a peak, 38.818277 removed, ten faces), all within 1e-4.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's law corners, tangency-labs/smlib 7babe17, 58d03ef and 62fcc82 (smlib#79, merged into replay/1.0.1 at 5e8c9c1). Tracking: tangency-labs/solidcore#2273, solidcore#430.
- **Confirmed:** against fork 5e8c9c1 built locally (SMLib and SM_API with the harness's flags, `-ffp-contract=off`), through a scratch driver (not committed) that links the fork's capi sources and runs its three cases: the dip 39.542055909305 removed from either end and 39.542055908618 as the quadratic (nine faces), the peaks 21.799877917178, 27.093677260694, 29.389911194516 and 39.541083296321 (eight faces), the dips 38.818141402826 (ten faces), every result sound at check level 2.
