#### WR-503. `SmFilletIntersector::ComputeNextPoint` accepts a next point far short of its step, and the rail doubles back (wrong body: the strip the round removes)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletIntersector.cpp`, `SmFilletIntersector::ComputeNextPoint`; the point comes from `SmFilletSolver::StepSolve` (`source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp`, the NewtonRaphson solve with `SetPlaneAdjusts(TRUE)` retry, about `:3020`-`:3050`).
- **What happens:** the trace steps from a crossing point by `dStepSize` and solves the sphere equations plus the plane through `CrvPos + dStepSize * CrvDeriv`. For a step that runs past the end of the filleted edge (the trace's domains are extended 50 percent) the first solve fails, the retry lets the plane move, and the solution lands on another root of the equations a few millimetres on (x = 32.6 for a 40 mm edge, 29.9 to 59.9 aimed). `ComputeNextPoint` accepts it: the Hermite UV spans built with the step's derivative lengths stay on a straight trace, so the deviation test passes, and the next point's curve parameter is `lastParameter + dStepSize`. The rail's parameter advances by 29.9 over a 2.7 mm span; the rail curve built from the spans doubles back (its tangent turns to -x at the middle of a 40 mm edge), `TrimOriginalSurfaces` then sees the binormals of both faces flipped, selects the faces outside the blend, and the flood deletes every face of the target. `DoFilleting` returns success with the strip of material the round removes (5 faces, 74 mm3 of 6600), which passes SMLib's check.
- **Trigger:** a plate whose top narrows from 20 to 2 mm over 40 mm and is 15 high (polygon (0,0) (40,0) (40,2) (0,20)), its front top edge rounded by a degree 3 radius law of 8 equal spans with control radii 3 (x9), 2.8, 1.8 (SolidCraft's taper of a fillet that does not fit; oracle design 80), the plate imported from SolidCraft's records (analytic planes, UV in mm). A knife edge: the same plate scaled by 0.5 or 2, or built by `Brep::extrude` (planes with UV in [0, 1]), or a last-but-one radius of 3, gives the rounded plate.
- **How found:** SolidCraft's oracle on solidcore (design 80, `tapered_round_keeps_the_plate` in `crates/smlib/src/ops_tests.rs` of the SolidCraft solidcore backend).
- **Confirmed:** in the Rust port only. The port of `ComputeNextPoint` and `StepSolve` matches the C++ source line for line, and no case was run against the C++ build, so whether the C++ trace takes the same step is not known.
- **Fix:** none upstream known.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_trace_rejects_short_step` (`FilletIntersector::compute_next_point`) a point that is less than half a step from the point it stepped from, on a step that was not clipped, is not a next point (the step is then halved by the caller), and the trace goes on to the end of the edge. The regression test is in SolidCraft's solidcore backend, as the repro needs the imported planes.
