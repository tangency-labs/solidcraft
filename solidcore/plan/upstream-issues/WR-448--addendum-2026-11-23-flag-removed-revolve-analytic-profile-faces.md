Updates: WR-448

#### WR-448 (update, 2026-11-23). `revolve_analytic_profile_faces` is removed

A line or circle coplanar with the axis now always sweeps a cylinder, cone, sphere or torus in place of the B-spline surface of revolution (fork tangency-labs/smlib 0b143f7, 9853e54). That holds for every profile curve and for a partial turn as well as a whole one. A one-curve profile no longer takes upstream's `sm_CreateAnalyticRotationalSweep` special case. The fix is now unconditional and the flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14425). SolidCraft already ran with it on for `create_rotational_sweep` and the partial cone, cylinder and torus primitives, the only paths that read the flag.

- **Removed:** the field `UpstreamFixes::revolve_analytic_profile_faces` and its doc, `RotationalSweepGeometry::set_analytic_revolutions` and its field (the analytic surface now follows `make_analytics` alone), the flag's gate in `create_rotational_sweep` and in the partial cone, cylinder and torus primitives, and every test setting of it.
- **New for one API:** `non_manifold_rotational_sweep` built its `RotationalSweepGeometry` without the flag (as do the `sweep.topology_sweep` goldens), so it made B-spline surfaces of revolution even in SolidCraft. It now makes the analytic surfaces too, for every caller; no SolidCraft code calls it. Its 10 moved `sweep.topology_sweep` rows are proved like the others.
- **Defaults:** for the flag's paths, the default kernel now gives the result SolidCraft has had. No valid input becomes a refusal. The two `ub215_degenerate_faces` cones whose height is below tolerance were refused before and are still refused.
- **Goldens:** 132 rows move, across `primitive.api_primitives` (97), `primitive.primitive_creation_p3` (12), `sweep.topology_sweep` (10), `sweep.rotational_sweep_geometry` (9), `sweep.api_brep` (2) and `primitive.primitive_creation_p2` (2). The surface is the same, but its parameterisation (angle by meridian) differs, so samples taken at fixed fractions of the domain land elsewhere on it. Each unit carries a test proving its moved rows against the closed form of the solid of revolution, within the entities' own tolerances:
  - `primitive.api_primitives`: the samples lie on the face carriers; the bodies are sound and closed, with the closed-form volume; the tolerances cover their gaps; and every UV trim runs between its edge's vertices.
  - `primitive.primitive_creation_p3`: the bodies are sound (solids, or a sheet for the open line); every face lies on one carrier, with its boundary in the swept sector; and the caller's editing state is restored.
  - `sweep.rotational_sweep_geometry`: the surface kind is the kernel's rule for the profile; the samples lie on the turned carrier within the sector; the 3D trims are its boundaries; the uv trims bound the domain and the loop closes; and the normal is the parameterisation's.
  - `sweep.topology_sweep`: every vertex is an input vertex or its turned image, and every edge is an input edge, its turned image or a vertex's arc.
  - `sweep.api_brep`: the sphere passes through the profile's ends.
  - `primitive.primitive_creation_p2`: the refusal carries its typed reason.
- **Shell tip:** in `tests/wr_531_shell_drilled_hole_tip.rs`, the shell's tip cap is now a sphere whose radius is fitted from its rim arcs (`create_sphere_from_arcs`), not the offset distance copied. The cap stands in for the tip vertex, so the test bounds its radius's distance from the offset distance by that vertex's tolerance.
- **Tests:** `tests/sweep_cornered.rs` (the torus test no longer ignored, and its B-spline twin now expects a torus), `tests/issue_1491_revolve_circle_seam_at_profile.rs` (the seam is at the profile with no flag set), and the revolve regression tests that used to set the flag.
