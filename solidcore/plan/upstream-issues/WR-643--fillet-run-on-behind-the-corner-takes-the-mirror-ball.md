#### WR-643. With WR-610's run-on, `RailEdgeuseIntersect` runs a straight side edge on past the corner's vertex too, where the solve can take a mirror image of the ball
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blend (fillet solver)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp`, `SmFilletSolver::RailEdgeuseIntersect` (def. `:3262`). The pinned C++ bounds the side edge use's parameter to the edge's own interval (`:3344`). The defect is in the fork's widening of that interval (WR-610, tangency-labs/smlib@6e77631), which runs the line on past both ends.
- **What happens:** behind the corner's vertex, the side edge's line lies on the far side of the filleted edge, outside the sector the blend fills, where no rail of the blend can be. Once faces continue as themselves past their ends (WR-633), the mirror image of the true ball lies there: on a knife edge, the ball that touches the curved wall's continuation past the tip. The solve took it, the rail met the side edge behind the vertex, and the end rib then found no intersection (1001).
- **Trigger:** the fork's capi test `knife_edge_rounds` (its knife-edge part, r 0.5): the back face's bottom edge, parameters 0..1 from the knife's vertex, met at t = -0.104 (mirror contact (68.647, 0, 0)) instead of t = +0.104, in the run-on interval [-1.85, 2.85].
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#960). The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@7224896 (branch `replay/1.0.1`, not an ancestor of rev `68fb9162`), which is not merged upstream: the line runs on past the end away from the filleted edge's vertex only. A side edge with both ends, or neither, on the filleted edge still runs on past both.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run of the fork was made, and the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`) has no run-on at all. In the Rust port, the knife-edge part (WR-570's, with `fillet_knife_edge_rounds`, `fillet_trace_reaches_rails`, `offset_extension_from_base` and `offset_extension_as_itself` on) reaches the same run-on interval, [-1.85, 2.85] at r 0.5. Its solve still converges to the true ball from its first guess, so the port does not show the 1001 there with the flag off.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_trace_reaches_rails` (WR-610's run-on) and `Kernel::fixes.fillet_run_on_past_far_end_only`, `run_side_edge_on_along_its_line` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_solver/p2.rs`) widens the interval past the far end only.

  Tests: `blend::fillet_solver::p2::tests::wr643_side_edge_runs_on_past_its_far_end_only` checks the loaded interval on a cube's top edges. Without the flag the side edge runs on past both ends; with it, never behind the filleted edge's vertex; a side edge that does not meet the filleted edge still runs on past both. It fails without the change. `crates/solidcore/tests/wr643_run_on_past_far_end_only.rs` checks the knife edge rounded r 0.5, 2 and 5, each mirrored, with the flag on: 7 faces, a manifold solid, and WR-570's closed-form volume to 1e-9 relative.
