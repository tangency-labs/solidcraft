#### WR-658 (update, 2026-11-16). `brep_edit_statuses_pass_on` is removed

The B-rep's own edits, `StitchAndOrient`, the offset and shell stitches and the Boolean merge, stitch and topology-intersection passes now always return the statuses SMLib drops and the fork passes on (tangency-labs/smlib 60a8a51, 4803d6d, e7ebc8d). Passing them on is the only path, and the flag is gone. Removed: the field, SolidCraft's preset line, its entry in `fixes.json`, every test setting of it, `status_passed_on`, the surface intersector's captured `edit_statuses`, `evaluate_binormal_status_ignored`, and every flag-off branch (an undefined or previous value carried on). Where the fork fails softly instead (`sm_RedistributeSeamUVTrimCurves` swaps nothing without its four edge uses, their trim curves and end points), that soft result is the only path.

One pair of fork `SER`s is not followed: `GlueEdgesGeneral`'s two region-nesting statuses (`TestClosureSplitRegion`'s) stay ignored, as upstream. They come after the edges are merged and the shells joined, so returning them reports a committed glue as failed, and a stitch with `bIgnoreProblems` then skips that glue's bookkeeping (the glued edge's tolerance is not raised to the gap it spans).

Two passed-on statuses keep upstream's result where it is a valid one (#16956 review: a row main completed keeps main's result where main's body passes the Level 2 check):

- The matrix `Transform`'s failed `AngleBetween` (row 0 × row 1 against row 2, a singular matrix or one scaling a direction by about `1e-6` or less): upstream's degenerate transform (the unit rows scaled by their lengths) is made on a standing copy in a trial kernel without callbacks and applied only when the copy passes `CheckLevel::Level2`, its geometry owners named by the body's naming scheme. Matrices that branch would not reproduce (short rows sheared against each other, a row 2 off the unit rows' cross product, zero or non-finite rows, a non-finite translation) are refused first. A planar sheet under a rank-2 map stays a valid sheet and is kept (`mat0046`, `disk.smb`); a solid's image has zero volume (row 2 zero, `det A = 0`) or shrinks to 2–4e-6 under `1e-6·I`, below the 1e-4 surface-degeneracy tolerance, so it fails the check and stays refused with the B-rep untouched.
- The topology intersector's `UpdateObjectTolerance` refusal of a finite gap and tolerance both below `SM_EFF_ZERO` (`ToleranceTooSmall`) is read as the no-op it is: upstream refuses before touching the object, and the raise asked for (only if larger, to below `SM_EFF_ZERO`) changes no tolerance by a distance the kernel tells from zero. A NaN gap's refusal is still returned. The four `boolean.topology_intersector_p1` `merged_*` rows are main's again. The stitch's same update (`SplitEdgesWithVertices`) keeps the refusal: main's bodies there fail Level 2 (2 or 3 `SmEdge::ValidateGeometry` failures from the split itself, tracked in #17675).
