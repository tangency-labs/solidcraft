#### WR-658 (update, 2026-11-16). `brep_edit_statuses_pass_on` is removed

The B-rep's own edits, `StitchAndOrient`, the offset and shell stitches and the Boolean merge, stitch and topology-intersection passes now always return the statuses SMLib drops and the fork passes on (tangency-labs/smlib 60a8a51, 4803d6d, e7ebc8d). Passing them on is the only path, and the flag is gone. Removed: the field, SolidCraft's preset line, its entry in `fixes.json`, every test setting of it, `status_passed_on`, the surface intersector's captured `edit_statuses`, `evaluate_binormal_status_ignored`, and every flag-off branch (an undefined or previous value carried on).
