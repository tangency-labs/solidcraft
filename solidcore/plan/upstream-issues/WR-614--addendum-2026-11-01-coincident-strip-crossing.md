Updates: WR-614

#### WR-614 (update, 2026-11-01). Opt-in `boolean_coincident_strip_drops_crossing` removes two-sided coincident-strip plane crossings before `ResolveIntersections`
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.boolean_coincident_strip_drops_crossing` (`TopologyIntersector::drop_crossings_in_coincident_strips` in `crates/solidcore/src/boolean/topology_intersector/coincident_strip.rs`), `IntersectInsertRelate` removes a planar face/face edge definition (and its proto edge when left empty) before `ResolveIntersections` when all hold:
  1. Both source faces `fa` and `fb` are valid `Plane` surfaces, and the crossing curve's interval length exceeds its tolerance.
  2. At least one edge-on-face `EdgeDefinition` (`(Edge(e_a), Face(fb))` connected to `fa`, or `(Face(fa), Edge(e_b))` connected to `fb`) runs straight and parallel to the crossing within `crossing.tol`, covers `[0, len]` within `crossing.tol`, lies farther from the crossing than `crossing.tol`, and has its owning face's inward binormal at `e`'s midpoint pointing toward the crossing (`binorm.dot(off) < 0`).
  3. On the opposite side of the crossing (`off2.dot(off1) < 0`), the other face ends at an edge likewise straight, parallel to, covering and facing the crossing (`binorm2.dot(off2) < 0`) within the edge/face intersection tolerance — either as a second edge-on-face `EdgeDefinition` (when `classify_edge_interval` / imported tolerances emit both) or as a boundary edge in `face_edges` whose ends and midpoint lie within `max(GetXSectTol3d(edge, other_face), crossing.tol)` of the other face's plane.
- **Why both sides and inward binormals are checked:** requiring only one edge-on-face definition in the crossing's own proto edge drops genuine exit crossings when one body continues past the crossing out of tolerance (for instance `A = [-2, 10] x [16, 20] x [0, 4]` unioned with `B` at `d = 1e-5`, where `B` emerges from `y = 20` up to `y = 20.4`; dropping that crossing destroys `B` and leaves 0 solid regions), and checking `binorm.dot(off) < 0` rejects pairs of faces that both start near the crossing and diverge away from it on the same side.
- **Verification:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr_614_coincident_strip_crossing.rs` verifies default `SM_ERR` parity, the strip fix alone matching `d = 5e-6`, `boolean_coincident_strip_drops_crossing` paired with `imprint_skips_collapsed_definition` (and under `UpstreamFixes::all()`) producing one `Level2`-valid manifold solid of volume `128` for `d = 1e-5`, `d = 5e-6`, and imported vertex/edge tolerances (`1.5e-5` / `1e-5`), difference = `A`, intersection = empty, and non-regression on protruding wider boxes and `45°` diverging boxes.
