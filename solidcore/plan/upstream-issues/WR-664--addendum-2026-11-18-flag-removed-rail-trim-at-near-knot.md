#### WR-664 (update, 2026-11-18). `fillet_rail_trim_at_near_knot` is removed

The near-knot snap is the only path and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246). `FilletBrep::trim_rail_curves` (`blend/fillet_geom/p5.rs`) moves each end parameter of a rail to the rail's next knot inside the trim when the rail between the two is no longer than the target B-rep's zone tolerance (`snap_rail_end_to_knot`), as the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@7d79866 does. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::fillet_rail_trim_at_near_knot` (its file under `crates/solidcore/src/kernel/fixes/`) and the off path; SolidCraft's profile already turned it on. Schematic: `plan/findings/blend.fillet_geom_p5--18918-rail-trim-at-near-knot/near-knot-trim.png`.

- **The zone tolerance comes from `target`.** The fork reads it as `SmTol::GetZoneTol3d(GetEdgeuse(0)->GetBrep())`, through the solver's first edge use. The port is given that B-rep as `target` (every kernel caller passes the session's target, in which the solver's edge uses live), but it still looked the edge use up and refused with 1006 ("the solver's edge use") when the solver had none. It now reads the tolerance from `target` only. With the flag on, that lookup refused 87 `blend.fillet_geom_p5` rail trims, whose solvers are built without edge uses: 73 that build on main (status 0), 13 that `set_min_max` refuses (1007, WR-661) and one 1001.
- **The end curves follow a snapped end** (not in the fork as far as its commit description says). The snap moves a rail end along the rail after the geometry's end curves were built at the vertex. When the fillet merges, the rail's UV curve is dropped again from its trimmed 3D curve, so a blend face's loop could open at that corner by up to the zone tolerance (9.6e-7 in u on `stubs4-delete-edge-sphere_diff_3cylinders-e1-r0.1`), and the volume enclosure then refused the body. Where the snap moves a rail end, each UV trim curve of the geometry that ends at the rail's vertex now ends at the snapped end. Test: `blend_fillet_executive_p3::end_to_end::wr664_near_knot_trims::*_loops_close`. Details: the finding named above.
- **Results** (both units run with the snap forced on against main's expectations, before and after the edge-use change):
  - `blend.fillet_geom_p5`: no row moves (300 cases). Test: `blend_fillet_geom_p5::tests::wr664_rail_trims_of_a_solver_without_edge_uses_build` trims the 73 rails with bare solvers.
  - `blend.fillet_executive_p3`: five rows move, each with the same status and vertex, edge and face counts: `stubs4-delete-edge-sphere_diff_3cylinders-{e1,e4}-r{0.1,0.5}` and `stubs4-delete-edge-bottle-e8-r0.1`. On main the sphere_diff rails start 9.5e-7 to 9.8e-7 short of their knot at 0.004, so each starts in a span of 9.5e-7 to 9.6e-7, below the zone tolerance 1e-5, with coincident control points; with the snap they start at the knot, in a span of 6.7e-3 to 6.9e-3. One rail end of `e1-r0.5` and `e4-r0.5` ended 2.2e-8 past a knot and is moved onto it. MEASURED: inner control points closer than 1e-6 on the rail edges fall from 8, 11, 8, 11 to 2, 4, 2, 2 (e1-r0.1, e1-r0.5, e4-r0.1, e4-r0.5), and the level-2 B-spline curve Test #15 ("Repeated CPts") from 4, 4, 4, 4 to 2, 4, 2, 2. What remains is the trace's own last span at the rails' other end, knot to knot and 9.5e-7 long before and after, which no trim at a knot lengthens (tangency-labs/solidcraft#19075). On the bottle the snap moves one end, the start of rail 0, by 3.6e-6 of rail onto a knot (MEASURED), and its first mass value moves from 33.092189857598 to 33.092189749777 (3.3e-9 relative). Test: `blend_fillet_executive_p3::end_to_end::wr664_near_knot_trims` (one per row: the counts, and every rail edge starts in a span at least the zone tolerance).
