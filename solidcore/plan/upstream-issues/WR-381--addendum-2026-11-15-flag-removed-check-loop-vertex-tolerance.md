Updates: WR-381

#### WR-381 (update, 2026-11-15). `check_loop_vertex_tolerance` is removed; `CheckLoop` and `IsClosed3d` bound a junction by its shared vertex

`check_loop` (`topology/trimming_tools.rs`) and `is_closed_3d` (`topology/loopuse.rs`) always take a junction's limit from `junction_tolerance`: the edges' cross-section limit, plus twice the zone of the vertex both ends share when each end lies within that vertex's zone plus its own edge's zone. By the triangle inequality the two ends are then at most `(r + e1) + (r + e2)` apart, so a gap inside that limit is a junction the stored tolerances already admit. An unrelated vertex, or an end outside its zones, keeps the edge-only limit. Removed: the field, SolidCraft's preset line, the rig's `fixes.json` entry, the flag-list test entry, the six SolidCraft-preset copies in tests, and `issue_476_default_keeps_the_edge_only_gap_limit`, which pinned upstream's result.

- **Defaults:** SolidCraft's preset had the flag on, so the app's results don't change. `Kernel::default()` changes: a loop whose gaps lie inside a widened shared vertex is closed.
- **Goldens:**
  - `topology.loopuse` `tol_*` (176 rows, `IsClosed3d` on `Mouse.smb`, `ellips_ts2.smb` and `toruswvy.smb`, with and without rebuilt UV trims, at edge tolerances overridden to `0`, `±1e-12`, `1e-15` and `1e-9`). Every row stays closed. A script over the full outputs checks that each closed verdict has its gap within its limit, that a limit only grows, and that each check-all row keeps its edge use and gap. Wherever old and new rows report the same junction (all check-all rows), the growth is exactly twice a vertex tolerance of the model: `new == old + 2.0 * r` holds bit for bit in all 122 such rows, with `r` read back from the files: `Mouse.smb` `1.50177e-5`, `2.18193e-3` and, with rebuilt trims, `4.00098e-3`; the other two `1e-5`. The first-gap rows (`check_all` off) report a different edge use and gap because upstream's scan stopped at the first gap over the edge-only limit (`2.02e-5` at edge use 0), where now no gap is over its limit and the scan reports the largest (`2.73e-5` at edge use 3), the value the check-all rows already reported. Measured with a temporary runner that recomputes every junction of each `IsClosed3d` loop from public APIs (2780 calls across the unit's 9013 cases): each closed verdict has every gap within its limit, and the reported gap and limit are exactly the largest gap and that junction's limit (both `0` when every gap is `0`, as upstream). Each open verdict has a gap over its limit.
  - `topology.trimming_tools` `vertex_gap_covered_eu0/1/3` and `vertex_gap_too_wide_eu0/1/3` (6 rows, the record's cases on `plane_square.smb` with edge tolerance `5.8e-6` and vertex tolerance `2.6e-5`): closure 3 becomes 0. The moved end lies `1.3e-5` or `2.61e-5` from the vertex, inside `2.6e-5 + 5.8e-6 = 3.18e-5`, so the limit is `1.16e-5 + 2 * 2.6e-5 = 6.36e-5`. The `too_wide` cases were named against the vertex tolerance alone; `issue_476_loop_gap_uses_shared_vertex_tolerance` asserts the same numbers (`2.61e-5` closed, `3.19e-5` open). `eu2` is the record's control and does not move.
- **Tests:** the in-file `issue_476_*` tests and `loopuse.rs`'s shared-vertex tests run on the default kernel; `issue_476_and_530_fixes_combine_on_a_mixed_seam_gap` now checks that the same 3D gap is closure 3 at the vertices' own tolerances.
