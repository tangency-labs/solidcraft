Updates: WR-780

#### WR-780 (update, 2026-11-15). The mitre's reference step grows with the tolerance

`CreateSweepAlongPlanarPath` steps 0.1 along each leg from a mitre corner to pick the reference points that name the sides of the cutting line. WR-780 already caps that step at half the cutting line's reach. A fixed 0.1 is also no use once the tolerance passes it. The side test (`sm_FindSideOfCurve`) calls a point within the tolerance of its foot on the cutting line undecided, and the trim fails. For a circle of radius `0.1 s` along two perpendicular legs of length `s` with tolerance `1e-5 s`, every `s` from 1e4 up failed that way. Upstream ignores the failed trims and returns an invalid body of zero volume.

- **Fix (not upstream):** `step = min(max(0.1, 100 tol), extend / 2)`, where `extend / 2 = sqrt(2) * max_dimension` of the profile. The cutting line runs through the corner along the bisector of the legs, so a point `step` along a leg lies `step * sin(beta / 2)` from it, where beta is the angle between the legs; the side test passes when `step * sin(beta / 2) > tol` (`tol` there is the larger of the sweep's and the face's tolerance). A corner is trimmed only when its legs are more than 2 degrees from parallel (`is_parallel_to(.., 2.0)`, which also skips a hairpin within 2 degrees), so `sin(beta / 2) > 0.0175`. Where `100 tol` sets the step, the reference points therefore clear the tolerance by a factor of at least 1.75 and the trim never fails. Where the reach cap wins (a profile with `max_dimension` below about `70.7 tol`), the condition is `sqrt(2) * max_dimension * sin(beta / 2) > tol`; when it fails, the side test refuses the sweep with "the point is on the curve", never a body. The floor keeps upstream's 0.1 wherever `100 tol` is smaller. (ARGUED: the derivation is in the code comment; the tests below check both regimes.)
- **Tests** (`primitive::primitive_creation::p2::planar_path`):
  - `a_mitred_elbow_builds_at_any_size`, at `s` = 1e4, 1e5 and 1e6: four faces, one manifold solid, volume `pi r^2 * 2 s` within 1e-8 relative, the accuracy the integrator is asked for (MEASURED 4.5e-11). It fails with main's constant 0.1 step ("the point is on the curve" at `s = 1e4`).
  - `a_profile_below_the_tolerance_is_refused_at_a_mitre`: a circle of radius `0.6 tol` at a right-angle corner is refused with that typed error (the cap sets the step to `sqrt(2) r`, so each point lies `r` from the line). At `5 tol`, still capped, the elbow builds with four faces.
- **Not tested, unreachable:** a corner tighter than 1.15 degrees, the bound where `100 tol` would no longer clear the tolerance. The 2-degree parallel test skips those corners before the trim (MEASURED: hairpins at 0.5 to 1.2 degrees never reach it). Such a hairpin returns the two overlapping, untrimmed tubes, as upstream does; that is outside this addendum.
- **Results:** no golden changes. The seven `primitive.primitive_creation_p2` elbows build, as main reported, but now as a valid solid.
