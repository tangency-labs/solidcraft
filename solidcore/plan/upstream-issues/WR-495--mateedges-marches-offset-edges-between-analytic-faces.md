#### WR-495. `SmOffsetGeometryCreation::MateEdges` marches every offset edge between two analytic faces on their B-spline extensions, so a shelled cylinder's floor and rim are not circles and its rim is split (#1453, #1130)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Offset and shell
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetGeometryCreation.cpp` (at 3fd5b95), `SmOffsetGeometryCreation::MateEdges`: it intersects the two faces' extended surfaces from `SmOffsetExecutive::GetCreateExtendedSurface`, which builds each as a tangent (G1R) B-spline extension (`CreateExtendedSurface(..., SM_CT_G1R)`), so `GlobalSurfaceIntersect` marches even where both faces are planes, cylinders, cones, spheres or tori.
- **What happens:** the new edge between two analytic offset faces is a B-spline approximation of the line or conic they meet on. On a cylinder r5, h10 shelled 1 inward with its top open (source tolerance 1e-5), the inner floor circle (z = 1, r 4) is a B-spline, and the inner rim (z = 10, r 4) is two arcs split at an extra vertex 1.2e-6 inside r 4: 5 faces, 7 edges, 5 vertices, where the body has 6 edges and 4 vertices, and the volume is 333.0089389 against the closed form pi 25 10 - pi 16 9 = 333.0088213 (3.5e-7 relative). A frustum r5 to r3 over h10 shelled the same way gets a marched floor and rim (volume 1.9e-7 over). On the SolidCraft fork's C++ a shelled dome's rim is a marched cubic (#1130; not reproduced in the port, `plan/findings/offset.offset_geometry_creation--issue-1130-not-reproduced-in-port.md`).
- **Trigger:** `SmApiShellBrepFull` (or `SmOffsetExecutive::ShellBrep`) of `SmApiCreateCylinder(origin 0, r 5, h 10)` at distance -1 with the top face open.
- **How found:** SolidCraft, wiring solidcore in as a second kernel (tangency-labs/solidcore#1453).
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the pinned C++ through difftest: `SmApiShellBrepFull` of the cylinder above (unit `offset.offset_executive_p2`, source tolerance 1e-5) gives the same 7 edges and 5 vertices as the port (the split rim; its vertex position differs, `plan/findings/offset.offset_executive_p2--issue-1453-cylinder-rim-split-vertex-differs.md`). At tolerance 1e-6 (case `api_shell_cylinder_r5_top_open_tol_1e6`) both sides agree. The same defect is confirmed on the fork's C++ before its fix (#1130).
- **Fix:** the SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@fddc366 ("an offset edge between two analytic faces is their exact line or conic") with its required follow-up @98ab1b0 ("an offset's analytic copy grows only along its rulings, not round a turn"). `MateEdges` also intersects analytic copies of the two face surfaces (`sm_CreateAnalyticExtension`: a plane grown all round, a cylinder or cone along its rulings up to the apex, by five times the offset distance; never round a turn, nor a sphere or torus, where the tangent extension leaves the surface). Each marched piece whose start, middle and end lie within the body's tolerance on one exact line or conic is replaced by that curve between the same ends, the way round through its middle (`sm_ExactPiece`), when it drops to exactly one UV curve on each extended surface within that tolerance; otherwise the marched piece stays. @98ab1b0 alone has no effect (it only narrows the copy @fddc366 builds), and @fddc366 alone fails shells whose cone or cylinder copies grow round a full turn (SolidCraft rig shell/3576), so both are one flag.
- **Rust port:** `Kernel::fixes.offset_analytic_mate_edges` (`create_analytic_extension`, `exact_piece` and the swap in `OffsetGeometryCreation::mate_edges`). Default off: upstream behaviour. Tests in `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1453_shelled_cylinder_exact_edges.rs`: by default the cylinder gives 7 edges and 5 vertices; with the fix 6 edges and 4 vertices, the floor and rim are circles within 1e-12 of r 4, and the volume is the closed form to 1e-9 relative (2e-15 measured); the frustum's floor and rim are circles, and its volume is the closed form to 1e-9 (2.2e-14 measured; `#[ignore]`d test, about four minutes of integration); the dome's rim stays the circle r 18. The inner seam (the inner cylinder against itself) stays the marched B-spline (on r 4 at every sample): the exact-piece pass finds no line or conic where two copies of one cylinder meet, and the fork's code takes the same path (not run on the fork's C++).
