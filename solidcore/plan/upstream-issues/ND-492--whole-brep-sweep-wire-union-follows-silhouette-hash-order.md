#### ND-492. `SmTopologySweep::DoSweep` (whole B-rep, no stitch or merge): the wire union succeeds or fails with the silhouette hash order
- **Severity:** Nondeterminism
- **Area:** Sweeps
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmTopologySweep.cpp:971` (`DoSweep`, whole-B-rep branch: `sHCR.GetSilhouetteEdges(sSilEdges)`, which returns `SmHCR::m_pSilEdges->GetAllKeys`, `SmHCR.cpp:312`, a pointer-keyed `std::unordered_map`, ND-2); the failure surfaces at `SmMerge.cpp:7077` (`MakeFaceuse`, `SER(SM_ERR)` when the B-rep mate of the edge use's vertex is neither vertex of the merged edge), reached from the final `ManifoldBoolean(SM_BO_UNION)` at `SmTopologySweep.cpp:1425`.
- **What happens:** the silhouette edges are swept in the map's iteration order, so the face order of the swept body follows it. Without stitching, the two side faces at a face vertex that a wire edge also starts at each carry their own copy of that vertex's arc (one from the original vertex, one from a copy rotated by 0 degrees, z = 3.9e-31). The union with the swept wire B-rep finds the wire's arc coincident with both: `IntersectTopology` adds an edge definition for each, in face-pair order, and `SetRelationships` relates the wire arc to the first one and ignores the `Relate` failure for the second (`SmTopologyIntersector.cpp:5329`). When the first one is the copy-vertex arc, `MakeFaceuse` later finds the B-rep mate of the wire arc's start vertex (the original vertex) on neither end of the merged edge and returns `SM_ERR`; when it is the original-vertex arc, the union succeeds.
- **Trigger:** `sc_sweep_topology_sweep_body` body 1 (prog_test `my_create_test_brep`: the `my_test_tsurf_creation` face plus a vertex loop, an edge in the face, a wire edge from face vertex (10,0,0) to (15,2,0) and a shell vertex), rotation 36 degrees about (0,-1,0) through (-10,0,0), eps 1e-4, stitching and merging off (`sweep.topology_sweep` case `nm_test_rotation_neg_y_36`). The HCR inserts the 10 silhouettes in one fixed order; libstdc++ visits the first-inserted one, (0,0,0)-(0,10,0) in scene coordinates, last in every observed process, and the union then fails.
- **How found:** the case's known mismatch (C++ `SM_ERR`, Rust success). Probes through private copies of `SmTopologySweep.cpp`, `SmTopologyIntersector.cpp`, `SmMerge.cpp`, `SmProtoTopology.cpp` and `SmHCR.cpp` linked ahead of the reference library traced the failing `SER` chain, the edge definition order and the silhouette insertion and iteration orders.
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the C++ reference (Ubuntu x86_64 prebuilt, glibc, libstdc++). Unmodified: 5450/5450 fresh forked processes return `SM_ERR` (parent heap fragmented between runs with random sizes up to 4096 bytes, `glibc.malloc.tcache_count=0`, `mmap_threshold`, `perturb`, and shuffled free lists in the child; 18 distinct silhouette orders observed, all with the first-inserted edge last). With the silhouettes reordered after `GetSilhouetteEdges` (no other change): the HCR insertion order succeeds with 1 region, 11 shells, 15 faces, 21 loops, 66 edges, 63 vertices, and the difftest record agrees with the Rust port in every value; of 60 random orders, 27 succeed and 33 fail, agreeing in 58/62 with whether (0,0,0)-(0,10,0) precedes (-5,10,0)-(0,10,0). The result therefore depends on the standard library's unspecified iteration order of a pointer-keyed map, not on the input.
- **Rust port:** `blend::hcr` keeps the silhouettes in ascending edge id order, which is the C++ insertion order, so the port is deterministic (as for ND-491). The case is NOT parked: it stays a visible known mismatch in `crates/difftest/known_mismatches/sweep.topology_sweep.toml` (no `cpp_defect`), because the evidence comes from forced-order probes, not from a verified C++ defect.
