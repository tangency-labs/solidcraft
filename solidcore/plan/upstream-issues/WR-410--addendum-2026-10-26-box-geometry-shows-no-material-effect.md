#### WR-410 addendum (2026-10-26): no box geometry shows the fix's material effect
- **What was tried:** the fork's case (rig crashes/4092, a rounded pad on a lofted cap) has no geometry in tangency-labs/solidcore#1059, and its fork test is not public. In its place, a 10 mm cube was united with a 20 x 4 x 4 bar whose top edges pierce the cube's top face at a shallow angle: 32 placements, tilt -1, -0.1, -0.01 and -0.001 degrees, turn 10, 30, 60 and 85, roll 0 and 30 (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1059_grazing_edge_crossing_probe.rs`).
- **Result:** with `Kernel::fixes.boolean_vertex_by_best_crossing` the flag's branch is reached (vertices move by round-off), but no placement shows a material difference. The worst edge-end gap is at most 4.07e-10 with the flag off (tilt -0.001, turn 85) and identical with it on; vertices move by at most 1.2e-14.
- **Test:** `issue_1059_best_crossing_result_unchanged` (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1059_best_crossing_reaches_its_branch.rs`) asserts only that, for tilt -1 and turn 30, the topology, volume and vertices are unchanged within 1e-13; that the branch is reached there is shown by the probe (vertices move by round-off), not asserted. With the flag off the same union matches the C++ (`fixtures/boolean.api_brep/issue_1059_grazing_bar.json`); confirmed at c9c5979 (1.0.0) and against 1.0.1 (3fd5b95): both cases, `issue1059_grazing_bar_union_alpha-1.0_beta30` and `issue1059_grazing_bar_union_alpha-0.1_beta30`, pass.
- **Still open:** a geometry where the first crossing places the vertex visibly wrong (the fork reports 1.2e-4) and the fix corrects it. The fork's `grazing_edge_crossing` test (capi/tests/booleans.cpp) would give one once it is public.
