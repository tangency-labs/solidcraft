Updates: WR-633

#### WR-633 (update, 2026-11-24). `offset_extension_as_itself` is removed; an analytic base always continues as itself

An offset surface whose base is a cylinder, cone, sphere, torus or a surface of revolution of a circle arc now always extends that base as itself (`extend_as_itself` in `crates/solidcore/src/surface/offset_surface.rs`): the base's spans are kept and rational quadratic arc spans are joined at its ends round the axis, a sphere's, torus's or revolved arc's generator in arcs of its circle and a cone's line straight, up to closing the turn (WR-650). Every other base grows by upstream's G1R extension, from the base (WR-458). Removed: the field, its fixes doc, the goldens preset arm and the tests' flag lines and flag-off halves (tangency-labs/solidcraft#18921).

- **Tests:** `wr633_offset_of_a_cylinder_continues_as_itself_past_its_domain`, `wr633_43fd6b6_revolved_circle_continues_as_its_circle` (in `offset_surface.rs`); the integration tests `issue_3045_round_at_plate_arc_end` and `round_between_crossing_cylinders`.
- **Goldens:** against main, 438 rows move in 8 units (`blend.fillet_standard_solver_p1` 172, `surface.offset_surface` 123, `blend.fillet_standard_solver_p2` 100, `blend.fillet_executive_p3` 27, `blend.fillet_solver_p4` 10, `topology.brep_cutting` 4, `blend.fillet_intersector` 1, `primitive.api_trim_ops` 1). No row turns a success into a refusal. Each is replayed by a goldens test that certifies it, not by comparing it with main:
  - the solver, offset and cutting rows (`wr458_moved_rows` in each unit): every extension an offset holds after the case's own run is rebuilt bit for bit from its base (`g1r_cert::ExtensionWatch`: a fresh G1R evaluation from the base's NURB toward one corner and then the other, each step certified G1 and each knot-removed step within G1R's `MODEL_TOL` of its input; an analytic base checked as itself by `as_itself_cert`, its net exact rational arcs on the base's own circles);
  - the real-solid rows (`blend_fillet_executive_p3/wr458_certificates.rs`, `two_offset_chamfer.rs`): status and counts are main's, `check_brep` Level 2 reports nothing (two rows report exactly main's lines on main's body of the row), the body is one closed manifold solid, every blend definition re-certifies its net and the mass values lie in their certified bands.
  - rows certified by a closed form instead: `surface.offset_surface` gen0655 grows no extension (its base, a revolution of a circle, is evaluated as itself past its domain; the moved normal is the radial of the offset torus through its point, `the_wrapped_row_is_its_revolutions_closed_form`); the surface-surface mirror row is within its tolerance of its closed form, the mirrored complement: the radius-2 cylinder about x = 5, z = 0 between the planes z = 0 and x = 5 (a Bernstein bound on every Bezier patch of its net, 3.15e-3 against 0.01), once each section is mirrored once (WR-21288, fixed here: the extension from the base puts the end sections a knot over the others, which reaches upstream's second mirror); `topology.brep_cutting` cuts are open shells whose vertices lie on the kept side of the cutting plane and whose counts are main's; `primitive.api_trim_ops` gen0043 moves only the order of its vertices, each on a flat face of its rounded box; `blend.fillet_intersector` gen1001 moves only the law point's parameters, whose two points agree and lie on the plane within the solver's tolerance.
