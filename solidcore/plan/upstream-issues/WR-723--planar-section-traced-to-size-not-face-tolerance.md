#### WR-723. `SmBrep::CreatePlanarSectionCurves` with no tolerance traces each face's section to 1/1000 of its surface's size, not to the face's tolerance
- **Severity:** Wrong result (accuracy: section curves off their face by more than its tolerance)
- **Area:** Planar sections (surface intersection tolerance)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp`, `SmBrep::CreatePlanarSectionCurves`: the face's tolerance stands in for a null `cpdOpt3DApproximationTol` in the coincidence checks only; the null is passed on to `SmSurface::CreatePlanarSectionCurves` and `GlobalSurfaceIntersect`, where `SmSurfaceIntersector`'s constructor sets `m_dThisApproxTol3d` to 1/1000 of the surface's size (`(|P(min) P(mid)| + |P(mid) P(max)|) / 1000`), and the march (`TestSpanAccuracy`) meets that.
- **What happens:** on a face of a B-spline or procedural surface the section curves are traced, and stray from the face by up to the size default. The G^n rounds (r 2) of a 40 x 30 x 20 box with a chamfer, cut by the plane x = 20: the section leaves the round by 5.2e-3 and 3.8e-4 at a face tolerance of 1.6e-4 (32 and 2.3 times it), off the round's certified net by the same to 1e-11.
- **Trigger:** `SmApiIntersectBrepWithPlane`, or `CreatePlanarSectionCurves` without a tolerance, on a body with non-analytic faces.
- **How found:** solidcore #2451, while building #2388. Solidcore-native: no fork commit.
- **Confirmed:** in the Rust port (`crates/solidcore/tests/wr698_gn_round_is_its_definition.rs`, `a_plane_section_of_a_round_is_within_its_tolerance`). With the flag, the same sections are 4.6e-5 and 6.9e-5 off the round (0.29 and 0.43 times the face's tolerance, at 401 points along each).
- **Rust port (opt-in):** `Kernel::fixes.planar_section_face_tolerance` (`UpstreamFixes`, default `false`). `topology::brep::planar_section::face_planar_section_curves` (the per-face step of `create_planar_section_curves`) passes the face's tolerance on as the approximation tolerance when none is given.
