Updates: WR-304

#### WR-304 (update, 2026-10-31). A later boolean turns the slits into zero-area loops, which SMLib's check refuses (#575)
- **Trigger (issue #575):** an r 37 disc, z -3.6..0, built as the disc z -1.2..0 joined with the regions of a sketch with the chords y = 0 and y = 8 (each region extruded z -3.6..-1.2, the regions joined in turn), then joined with an annulus r 35..37, z -5.6..-3.6, under it. Test: `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_575_split_rim_join.rs`.
- **What happens:** joining the regions to the disc merges their wall faces into the disc's wall and removes the circle at z = -1.2 between them, but leaves the line at each chord end (z -3.6..-1.2) with the merged wall on both sides: a WR-304 slit, here a strut (one end on no other edge). The body is valid. Joining the annulus merges the two walls and removes the rim at z = -3.6, the struts' other ends; each strut is then a loop of its own two edge uses, of zero area. `SmTrimmingTools::CheckFace` refuses the wall: test #3, "Face Loop has bad orientation", and test #4, "Face Loop has zero area". The volume is right (16387.8039, the closed form, to 3e-12).
- **With `Kernel::fixes.boolean_cleanup_removes_slits`:** the struts are removed, and the result is a valid manifold solid of the same volume (5 faces, 6 edges, 4 vertices). This holds for the chords y = 0 and 8 and y = 32 and 35, and also when Body1 was built without the field and only the last join has it. No kernel change.
- **Differences from the issue:** the issue's Body1 comes from SolidCraft (3 faces, 5 edges, 5 vertices as SolidCraft counts them); this reconstruction with `SmApiCreateTaperExtrude` and `SmApiBooleanUnion` has 3 faces, 9 edges, 8 vertices (the three struts and the seam). The issue reports a single chord at y = 0 as valid; here it fails too (one strut at 180 degrees) and is valid with the field.
- **Confirmed:** no. The `boolean.api_brep` difftest operands (cylinders, boxes, booleans) do not build it: the same body as cylinders cut by boxes has no struts, and the C++ and the port both pass the check on it (compared locally, 4 cases). Comparing #575's input against the pinned C++ needs an extruded operand in that runner.
- **Rust port:** unchanged; the existing opt-in, default `false`. Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#575.
