#### WR-51 (update, 2026-11-16). Touching points are added after the degenerate-curve cleanup; three plane-first cases are known failing

- **Order:** `DoIntersection` removes degenerate curves that lie on other curves. It now adds the touching points left for a start point (`SurfaceIntersector::add_deferred_touching_points`) after that removal, not before it (`remove_covered_degenerate_curves_then_add_touching`). Each point is checked against the curves that remain. Before, a point whose only cover was a degenerate curve the removal then dropped was lost. Such a point lies within 5 tol of the curve that the degenerate curve lay on but is not on it, and is now reported; the test `a_touching_point_covered_only_by_a_removed_degenerate_curve_is_kept` builds one. A point that is added lies on none of the remaining curves, and the removal uses the same test, so the removal would not have dropped it either.
- **Known failing, refused, not blessed:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#17507 blessed a lone touching point, in place of 1001, for three `intersect.api_intersectors` cases: `ssi-revolution-plane-near-axis-parallel-swapped`, `ssi-revolution-plane-oblique-swapped` and `ssi0043`; `ssi0048` returned one too. The point alone is the wrong topology. With the plane first, the intersector takes its tolerance from the plane's 2.47e6 domain (3.49e3) and each case returns one touching point (deviation 0.500001, 0.2, 2.44 and 0.52). The answers: two arcs from the bottom rim to the top; one closed curve round the axis; one closed curve round the axis; one open arc with both ends on the top rim. The first three are proven from the plane and the profile's control points; for `ssi0048` the two ends are the closed-form roots on the top rim and the single arc is measured by counting sign changes along 3600 meridians. At the revolution's own tolerance (5.15e-3, 5.15e-3, 2.96e-2, 3.19e-3) the plane-first intersection finds them.
- **Refusal:** `intersect_surfaces` and `intersect_surfaces_within` now refuse such a result with `IntersectFailure::PointsWhereAPlaneCrosses { tolerance, surface_tolerance }` (`ErrorKind::NotWithinTolerance`): the result is touching points only, one operand is a plane, the other surface's box lies over the plane's domain, a 17 x 17 grid of the other surface has points strictly on both sides of the plane, and some point's deviation exceeds the other surface's own tolerance. A touching point within that tolerance is kept: `ssi-cone-plane-near-apex` (a crossing circle of radius 5e-8 at the apex, deviation 0) is not refused in either order. `surface_tolerance` is the tolerance that builds the answer.
- **Tests and listing:** the four cases are listed in `known_failing.json` under tangency-labs/solidcraft#17720 and have no golden line. `plane_first_pairs_refuse_touching_points_where_the_plane_crosses` asserts the refusal and the answer at `surface_tolerance`. `wr51_plane_first_pairs_report_their_crossing_curves` (ignored, known failing under #17720) replaces `wr51_plane_first_pairs_report_their_touching_point` and asserts the answer at the default tolerance. `a_plane_tangent_to_the_revolution_is_not_refused` and `a_touching_point_within_the_surface_tolerance_is_not_refused` cover the cases the refusal must keep.
