Updates: WR-455

#### WR-455 (addendum 2026-11-16): a contact at a curve's end always moves there; `curve_contacts_at_curve_ends` is retired

- **Status in the port:** `UpstreamFixes::curve_contacts_at_curve_ends` is gone. `Curve::global_curve_intersect` (`curve/curve/p8.rs`) always runs `move_contacts_to_curve_ends` after `sm_RefineCCSolutions`: a point solution moves to an end of either curve when that end lies within tolerance of the other curve and the curves are certified to stay within tolerance of each other over the whole stretch (`curves_stay_within`, below). After a move the solutions are sorted again on the first parameter (stable), which the later merge and cull passes assume; without a move nothing is reordered. Part (1), `offset_stitch_at_body_tolerance`, keeps its own flag.
- **Not for self intersections:** the move is skipped when the curve is intersected with itself. There the "other" curve is the same curve, so the stretch test compares the curve with itself and always passes; under the flag the move carried real self intersections onto the trivial diagonal, where they were dropped: a closed curve's seam (a full ellipse, closed Hermite curves) disappeared from `GlobalCurveSelfIntersect`, whose contract is that a closed curve always has one. The old default (flag off) never ran the move; the flagged path had this defect. Test: `self_intersection_keeps_a_closed_curves_seam` (goldens `curve_curve_p8`).
- **The stretch test is certified, not sampled:** `curves_stay_within` (`curve/curve/p8.rs`) walks a path that is monotone in both parameters from the solution to the end and its drop. Each piece is bounded by `(f0 + f1)/2 + L/2`, where `f` is the distance at the piece's ends and `L` sums each curve's speed bound times its parameter step. The speed bounds come from the curve's own data: a B-spline's derivative control points (rational: `2p (wmax/wmin) diam / h`), a Hermite curve's derivative Bernstein coefficients, a line's affine slope. Every bound is widened by `gamma_n` rounding terms and by a bound on the evaluation error. A piece that fails is halved, up to 4096 pieces. The move is refused when a node is certainly farther than the tolerance, when the budget runs out, or when no bound exists: other curve kinds, B-splines that are not continuous and clamped, and stretches that leave the knot range of a curve evaluated out of bounds. Because the path is monotone in both parameters, it covers both curves' stretches, so it bounds the distance measured on each curve. The sampled `curve_max_distance_between` is no longer used here, and the one-point-drop guard is gone: goldens `curve.curve_p8` gen2153 (`t1 = 1` crossing a closed curve's whole turn to 0) is refused by the certified test itself. Test: `one_point_drop_stretch_does_not_move_the_contact`.
- **A failure in the move leaves the solution in place:** an evaluation or solve that fails while the move tests an end (for example an interval end past the knots of a B-spline evaluated out of bounds) skips that end instead of failing the intersection, so the pass only ever refines solutions already found. Errors with no upstream status (cancellation, unported calls, port invariants) still propagate.
- **A contact is not reported twice:** a move that lands exactly on a contact another solution already names (a point, or an end of a range) drops the moved solution. In goldens `curve.curve_p8` `cov_gci_same_cubic_tiny_overlap_split_range` (one cubic on `[0, 0.5000001]` against itself on `[0.5, 1]`), the certified move took a point onto the end of the tiny overlap range. The later passes then turned the range into points at its ends, so the contact at `0.5000001` was reported twice. With the point dropped, the overlap is reported as the one range it is. Its ends are main's two points, and the curves are certified within tolerance along it. Test: `a_move_onto_a_named_contact_does_not_report_it_twice`.
- **Known limit:** the move may name an interval end that lies past a clamped curve's knots, where the curve is constant, as the fork does.
- **Certificate:** `contacts_at_curve_ends_are_certified` certifies every moved `curve.curve_p8` row: each solution is a contact, and each earlier solution is joined to a reported one by a stretch the curves keep within tolerance. It calls the library's `curves_stay_within`, and `BSplineCurve::trim_gap_intersection` (the intersection step of `trim_gap`) for the gap certificates, so the tests certify the production code, not a copy.
- **The mouse case:** with the move on, the goldens case `blend.fillet_executive_p1` `remove_topo_edges_mouse_two_struts` failed with `create_by_joining: segments not end to end`. The defect was in the consumer, `longest_drop_run` (WR-543 addendum of the same date), which now joins only pieces the join accepts.
- **Not retired here:** `simple_coincidence_crossing_is_point` (WR-647) keeps its flag. With it on by default, the issue1036 coincident skins' sections (parallel curves 2.6e-6 to 1.7e-5 apart at tolerance 2e-5) become single points, and the booleans return wrong bodies where main refuses (WR-473). It is retired after WR-473 is fixed.
- **Removed:** the upstream path, the field and its doc, the preset line in `crates/smlib/src/core.rs`, the `fixes.json` and `issue_2260` entries and the flag settings in the integration tests.
- **Tests:** `issue_855_offset_stitch_keeps_the_bodys_tolerance` no longer loops over the contact setting: the stitch fix still gives the closed form 4564.924657 and the default still refuses (1001).
