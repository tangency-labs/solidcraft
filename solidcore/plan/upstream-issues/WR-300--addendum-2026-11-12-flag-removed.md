Updates: WR-300

#### WR-300 (update, 2026-11-12). `fillet_retry_radius_as_first` is removed: variable rounds meet at their law radii by default

The fix is now unconditional, and the flag is gone. SolidCraft's preset already turned it on. The fix holds a variable round's radius at its end value only at a corner calculated as constant radius (`SmFilletCorner::IsCalcAsConstRad`). It applies in four places: in `CalcRailIntEdgeuse`'s retry rail search, in its search for the adjacent round (`pFilletGeom2`, where the fork holds at every corner too), in `RailRailIntersect` when `CalcRailIntRail` calls it, and in `CalcRailRailInterpolation`'s cubic rail interpolant.

- **Why it is the default:** upstream's hold puts the rails' meeting point on balls that the laws never give there. The trace takes that point as a fixed station, so the round's skin goes through a section that is not one of its balls. On the 10 box with its four top edges rounded 0.5 -> 2 (`TestSmVariableRadiusFillet`), the station on the edge along `y = 10` at `t = 2` was 0.5 from one face and 0.8 from the other, where the law gives 0.8. The station at `t = 8` was 2.0 and 1.7 from its faces, where the law gives 1.7. With the fix, the rails meet where both balls have the law's radius: at `x = y = 190 / 23` on the top face for the two rounds meeting at (10, 10, 10).
- **The adjacent round's search:** where the side edge is not square to the round's edge, the ball whose foot is on the side edge is not the end's. Two rounds 0.5 -> 2 on the top edges of a 10 box whose two side faces at (10, 10, 10) lean in 15 degrees fail with 1001 under the hold (the faces the rails enclose reach past the rails). Without it they build, and each side foot is the law ball's, in closed form (`a_round_meets_a_slanted_side_edge_on_its_law_ball`).
- **Results:** listed with their proofs in the PR that removes the flag.
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr300_variable_rounds_meet_at_law_radii.rs` runs on the default kernel. Its `the_rails_meet_where_both_balls_have_the_law_radius` checks the meeting vertex against the closed form `(190 / 23, 190 / 23, 10)`. The upstream half (`upstream_holds_the_end_radii_and_the_trace_fails`) is removed. The tests that set the flag drop the setting.
