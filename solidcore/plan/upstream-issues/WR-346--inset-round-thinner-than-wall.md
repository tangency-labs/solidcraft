#### WR-346. `SmOffsetExecutive::DoExtendedOffset` fails an inward shell across a round thinner than the wall
- **Severity:** Wrong result (the shell fails with `SM_ERR`)
- **Area:** Offset
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp`, `SmOffsetExecutive::DoExtendedOffset`: `if (sOffsetSurface == NULL) { SER(SM_ERR); }` after `FaceuseOffset`; `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetGeometryCreation.cpp`, `SmOffsetGeometryCreation::TrimExtendedEdge`, the neighbour classification at each end of the base edge use.
- **What happens:** the inward offset of a convex round of radius r <= the inset distance is degenerate, so `FaceuseOffset` gives no surface and the offset stops. Skipping the face is not enough: the faces beside it have no partner across it in `MateEdges` / `ExtendTrimEdges`, their trims run to the round's offset edge ends, and the offset of an edge along the round's end is left dangling.
- **Trigger:** a 30 x 20 x 10 box with its edges rounded r1 (or r0.5), shelled 2 inward with `SmApiShellBrepFull`, closed or open at the top: 1001.
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig on the SMLib fork (tangency-labs/solidcore#397, ladder case 09/006).
- **Confirmed:** on the fork's C++ build: all twelve edges or the four vertical ones rounded r1 and r0.5, shelled 2 closed and open at the top, match their closed-form volumes (the cavity is the box shrunk by the wall) and pass the level-2 check; r3 (thicker than the wall) shells to its rounded cavity as before.
- **Fix (fork commit a252570, "SMLib: an inward shell mates the walls across rounds thinner than it"):** with an inset and extended convex edges, a face with no offset is recorded as collapsed and gets no offset face; each rail of a collapsed four-sided round mates the offsets of the faces on either side (`MateEdges` with the original edge uses on both sides); `TrimExtendedEdge`, finding the face next to a vertex collapsed, trims to the extended offset of the face beyond it (`GetEdgeuseAcrossCollapsedFace`); the offset edge along a collapsed round's end, shrunk to a corner by the mating, is squeezed out; a collapsed face that cannot be bridged fails with `SM_ERR_DEGENERATE_SURFACE`.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.offset_collapsed_rounds_mated` (default `false`, upstream behaviour). `OffsetExecutive::offset_face_prefix` records the collapsed faces, `do_extended_offset` mates across them, skips them in the edge passes and squeezes the end edges (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p2.rs`); `OffsetExecutive::edgeuse_across_collapsed_face` and `mate_edges_across_with_marked` (`p1.rs`); `edgeuse::mate` (`edgeuse.rs`); `OffsetGeometryCreation::edgeuse_across_collapsed_face` and the neighbour classification (`collect_neighbour`) in `crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_geometry_creation.rs`, with the default-`false` trait methods `OffsetExecutive::has_collapsed_faces` / `is_collapsed_face`. Test `issue_397_inset_rounds_thinner_than_wall_collapse` (a block with r1 vertical rounds, swept from its outline, shelled closed 2 inward), `#[ignore]`d for now: the swept rounds are `SurfOfExtrusion` faces whose offsets reach the unported `SmOffsetSurface::CalculateBoundingBox` (`surface.surface_cache_p1`), so the fix is not yet exercised; it needs a fixture with analytic cylinder rounds. No difftest case compares the opt-in path. Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#397.
