#### WR-570. A knife edge (a cusp, tangent faces folding back on each other) is taken for a smooth edge and cannot be rounded
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmEdge.cpp:6825` and `:6831`, `SmEdge::GetBlendEdgeuse` (def. `:6746`); `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletStandardSolver.cpp:120`, the edge-use constructors of `SmConstantRadiusFS` (def. `:76`), `SmConstantDistanceFS` (def. `:1245`) and `SmVariableRadiusFS` (def. `:1888`); `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp:5892`, `SmFilletNx1Corner::CheckNx1Degeneracy` (def. `:5850`).
- **What happens:** an edge whose two faces are tangent along it is refused as smooth. `GetBlendEdgeuse` returns NULL when either face's normal is within 2 degrees of perpendicular to the other's binormal. The solvers' constructors cannot pick an offset side when a binormal is tangent to the other face (`SM_ERR`). The Nx1 corner's degeneracy test takes parallel side normals for faces running into each other. That is right when the faces continue each other (binormals pointing apart). But when the binormals point the same way, the faces fold back on each other in a cusp (a knife edge: a curved wall running out tangentially into a flat face). They part to second order, and the ball touching both lies back from the edge, so the edge can be rounded like any other.
- **Trigger:** rounding the edge where an arc wall of radius R meets a plane face tangentially, e.g. a plate whose corner is cut by a circle of radius 21 tangent to its back face, extruded, its tip edge rounded r 0.5 to 5.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork (tangency-labs/smlib b7ec55b, capi test `knife_edge_rounds`, SolidCraft #851).
- **Confirmed:** by the port's test below. By default (pinned behaviour) the round fails.
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@b7ec55b. `GetBlendEdgeuse` refuses tangent faces only when their binormals point apart. The three solver constructors orient their offsets by `sm_SectorSides`: the side from the first-order test where the faces are not tangent, and otherwise, for a cusp, the sign of `k(1-i) N(1-i).ni - ki Ni.ni` (the faces' normal curvatures along their binormals). Smooth edges and osculating faces still fail. `CheckNx1Degeneracy` takes only parallel, not anti-parallel, side normals for a degenerate corner.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. `Kernel::fixes.fillet_knife_edge_rounds` ports the fork's change in `topology::edge::blend_edgeuse`, `blend::fillet_standard_solver::p1::{sector_sides, constant_radius_from_edgeuse}`, `p2::{ConstantDistanceFs, VariableRadiusFs}::from_edgeuse` and `blend::fillet_corner::p1::check_nx1_degeneracy_status`.
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr_570_knife_edge_rounds.rs`. With the opt-in, the knife-edge part and its mirror image round at r 0.5, 2 and 5 to 7 faces, a manifold solid of the closed-form volume. Without it the round fails.
- **Difftest:** no change on the default path.
