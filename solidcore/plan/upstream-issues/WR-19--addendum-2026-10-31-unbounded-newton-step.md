Updates: WR-19

#### WR-19 (update, 2026-10-31). Opt-in unclamped Newton step without a solution interval (`#2150`)

- **Location:** `source/SMLib/inc/SmLocalSolve1d.h:290`-`300` (`SmLocalSolve1d::SmLocalSolve1d(SmEvalFunctionObject&)`), `source/SMLib/src/SmLocalSolve1d.cpp:971`-`983` (`SmLocalSolve1d::SolveIt`, def. `:751`), and `crates/solidcore/src/geom/local_solve1d.rs`.
- **Defect:** When `SmLocalSolve1d` is constructed without an interval (`m_bUseInterval = FALSE`), `m_vInterval` remains at its default `SmExtent1d()` state (`[1e20, -1e20]`, `GetLength() = -2e20`). `SolveIt` guards the initial guess clamp (`:785`), the failed-guess tweak (`:818`), and the post-step domain clamp/wrap (`:1017`) with `m_bUseInterval`, but evaluates `dStepLimit = m_vInterval.GetLength() * 0.08 + SM_EFF_ZERO` (`-1.6e19`) unconditionally (`:975`-`983`). Consequently `fabs(dDeltaT) > dStepLimit` is always true and every Newton step is replaced with `±1.584e19` (`-1.6e19 * 0.99`, negated when `dDeltaT < 0`) in the opposite direction of Newton. No other method on `SmLocalSolve1d` (`SolveOscillating`, `SolveByBisection`, `SolveByBrent`) reads `m_vInterval`.
- **Opt-in (`Kernel::fixes.local_solve1d_unbounded_newton_step`, `#2150`):** default `false` preserves pinned C++ v1.0.1 parity.
  - `LocalSolve1d::new_with_kernel(k: &Kernel)` constructs `LocalSolve1d::new()` with `failed_guess_no_sign_change = k.fixes.oscillating_skips_failed_guesses` and `unbounded_newton_step = k.fixes.local_solve1d_unbounded_newton_step`.
  - `LocalSolve1d::with_interval_and_kernel(k: &Kernel, interval: Extent1d, is_periodic: bool)` constructs `LocalSolve1d::with_interval(interval, is_periodic)` with both kernel flags applied.
  - `LocalSolve1d::set_unbounded_newton_step(&mut self, on: bool)` and `LocalSolve1d::unbounded_newton_step(&self) -> bool` set and inspect the flag directly.
  - When `unbounded_newton_step` is `true` and `use_interval` is `false`, `LocalSolve1d::solve_it` skips the `0.08 * interval.length() + EFF_ZERO` clamp so Newton steps follow `-f / f'` across the real line. When `use_interval` is `true` (including every internal `crates/solidcore` caller, all of which construct `LocalSolve1d::with_interval`), `self.use_interval || !self.unbounded_newton_step` remains `true` and Newton steps are clamped identically to upstream.
- **Relationship to `WR-253` (`oscillating_skips_failed_guesses`):** `WR-253` (`LocalSolve1d::set_failed_guess_no_sign_change`) returns a failed first evaluation's error status when solving without an interval instead of iterating from its leftover value, while `WR-19` (`LocalSolve1d::set_unbounded_newton_step`) prevents valid evaluations without an interval from stepping `±1.584e19` against Newton; `new_with_kernel` and `with_interval_and_kernel` forward both flags from `Kernel::fixes`.
- **Regression tests (`crates/solidcore/src/geom/local_solve1d.rs`):**
  - `wr19_default_without_interval_steps_against_newton`: verifies default `LocalSolve1d::new()` (and `new_with_kernel(&Kernel::default())`) steps to `-1.584e19` from `guess_t = 0.0` (`delta_t = +3.0 > 0`) and to `5.0 + 1.584e19` from `guess_t = 5.0` (`delta_t = -2.0 < 0`) on `f(t) = t - 3.0`, failing to converge.
  - `wr19_opt_in_unbounded_newton_converges_positive_and_negative_roots`: verifies 1-step linear convergence (`f(t) = t - 3.0`), quadratic convergence on positive and negative cubic roots (`t^3 - 27.0` and `t^3 + 125.0`), and unclamped large-scale convergence (`f(t) = t - 1.0e6`).
  - `wr19_bounded_interval_still_clamps_newton_step`: verifies `LocalSolve1d::with_interval_and_kernel` with `local_solve1d_unbounded_newton_step = true` on `[0.0, 10.0]` still clamps positive and negative Newton steps exceeding `0.8` to `0.792`, matching `LocalSolve1d::with_interval` bit-for-bit.
  - `wr19_flag_lookup_and_kernel_constructors`: verifies `UpstreamFixes` flag lookup (`flags()`, `flag_mut`) and forwarding of both `local_solve1d_unbounded_newton_step` and `oscillating_skips_failed_guesses` through `new_with_kernel` and `with_interval_and_kernel`.
