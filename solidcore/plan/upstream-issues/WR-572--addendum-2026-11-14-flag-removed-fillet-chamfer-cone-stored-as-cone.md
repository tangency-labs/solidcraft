Updates: WR-572

#### WR-572 (update, 2026-11-14). `fillet_chamfer_cone_stored_as_cone` is removed

With `fillet_chamfer_cone_on_arc` (WR-421), `CalcTorusFilletGeom` turns a chamfer's linear section round a circular edge into a cone. That revolution is now always stored as the `SmCone` it is: its NURBS form is passed through `CopyAndAddAnalytics`, and the cone is kept when that is what it gives. The fix is unconditional and the flag is gone. SolidCraft's preset already had it on, so the shipped result does not change. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::fillet_chamfer_cone_stored_as_cone`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json`, in the SolidCraft preset list of `issue_2260_gn_pipe_refined_range_end.rs` and in the fixture `fixtures/solidcraft/12435-chamfer-round-a-rim-with-a-rounded-notch.json`, the flag-off branch, and every test setting of it.

- **Results:** no goldens row moves (all 283 units pass). Two pinned test results move:
  - `issue_379_chamfer_hole_rim_closed`: the rims' chamfers are cones, so no faces on a surface of revolution remain (2 to 0). The test now asserts the closed form: two 45 degree cones, apexes on the hole's axis at z = 2.5 and z = 7.5.
  - `issue_398_shell_box_rounds_chamfer_fillet` (open-top shell, edges on analytics exact): the volume pin moves from 13701.734187900483 to 13701.734697389531 (measured with the revolution: 13701.734182305687). The closed form is 13701.734185297493. Both values pass the test's closed-form check at 1e-6, but the cone-stored value is 3.7e-8 above it where the revolution was 2.2e-10 below. The other two shells of the case move the same way inside their own closed-form checks (integrator converged at accuracy 1e-12: open top 6.7e-8 to 9.4e-8 above, closed 6.5e-8 to 8.6e-8). The part before the shell is the same to 1 ulp. The cause is the shell's intersection of each offset corner cone with the offset floor plane: the circle lies on the cone but 1.2e-6 below the plane (tracked in tangency-labs/solidcraft#16587), which costs the shells 3.6e-4 to 5.1e-4 mm^3.
- **Tests:** `wr_572_chamfer_rim_stored_as_cone` (the flag-off half and the default-off check removed), `issue_379_chamfer_hole_rim_closed` and `issue_398_shell_box_rounds_chamfer_fillet`.
