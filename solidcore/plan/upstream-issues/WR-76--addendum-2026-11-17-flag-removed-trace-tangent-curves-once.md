Updates: WR-76

#### WR-76 (update, 2026-11-17). `ssi_trace_tangent_curves_once` is removed

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, group G6). `SurfaceIntersector` (`intersect/surface_intersector/`) always keeps one start point per cluster of tangent boundary start points (`add_start_point`), removes the clusters at a finished trace's ends, runs `TraceCurve`'s "already traced" test on a first step that snapped onto another start point, and stops a near-tangent trace at the free end of a curve already found. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::ssi_trace_tangent_curves_once`, SolidCraft's preset line (which already turned it on), its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, and the test settings in `issue_1945_fork_1207_step4.rs`, `issue_2020_offset_mitred_below_depth.rs` and `wr650_tabbed_plate_mitred_rounds.rs`. `issue_392_tangent_twice` keeps its fixed-path test; its flag-off run (the upstream double trace) is removed.

Three defects of the opt-in path, found by the goldens when it became the default, are fixed with it:

- A better tangent start point that replaces a cluster's representative keeps the representative's direction. With the replacement's own direction, `TestPoint` snapped a trace from the contact band's edge onto the cluster after one step and the cluster went with that stub: the coaxial sphere/cylinder tangent circle was lost (`intersect.api_intersectors`). Only the sphere-first operand order loses it; the swapped order passes either way and is kept as a check.
- The stop at a found curve's free end: the "already traced" test needs the span's start, its end and three Hermite samples each within the tolerance of a found curve (any found curve, not necessarily the same one), and the shortened span's step gets up to four secant corrections until its end is within the tolerance of the found curve's end along the span. The first-order step left a 7e-4 gap between the two curves of an offset's tangent circle; it is now 1.2e-8.
- A trace stopped at its start point because its first span lies on a found curve no longer flushes that lone point as a touching point (`SurfaceIntersector::lone_start_on_found_curve`): two planes crossing along a line reported a touching point on the found segment. A trace back from the point that adds points still makes it a curve's end.

- **Results:** 17 goldens rows move, each closer to the true intersection: `intersect.surface_intersector_p4` 10 (the trace from a point on its found curve retraces less of it: in 9 rows no curve at all; in `gen0153` the curve left runs exactly from start point 0 to start point 2, the points the case adds to the curve itself with `add_curve`; the trace from point 3 adds none), `intersect.surface_intersector_p5` 6 (second traces of kept curves are dropped), `offset.offset_geometry_creation` 1 (a tangent circle's second curve no longer runs on over 31 degrees of the first, and ends on its start).
- **Tests:** `crates/goldens/tests/wr76_wr79_flag_retirement_moves.rs` replays the 17 rows from the committed inventory with per-row certificates, every comparison with its rounding bound: control-point hulls against the found segment and both planes (and no touching point, no new curve type), chord-line bounds of each dropped curve against a kept one, and Bernstein bounds of the offset curves against the circle through the earlier curve's start. In the same file `shortened_trace_ends_on_the_found_curve` (the offset curve's end) and `trace_keeps_a_curve_beside_the_found_segment` (`intersect.surface_intersector_p4` `gen1081`), and in `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_392_tangent_twice.rs` `coaxial_sphere_cylinder_tangent_circle` (and `_swapped`), fail with their fixes reverted.
