Updates: WR-697

#### WR-697 (update, 2026-11-02). A tangent chain run over an earlier round's end curve, where that round has the chain's radius, fails with status 1001
- **Severity:** Wrong result (failure)
- **Area:** Fillets (corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp`, `SmFilletSolver::ClassifyExactBlend` (the fork's; no class for the pair, so the round is traced) and the trace it falls back to; `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`, `sm_CreateUVCurve` (an end on a degenerate side of the surface).
- **What happens:** round one edge of a vertex R first, then a tangent chain of the same radius R that runs over that round's end curve. Along the end curve the chain's ball rolls on a face and on the earlier round, a cylinder or a torus of tube radius R. That round's offset by R collapses onto its spine, a line or a circle, and the other face's offset crosses the spine at one point C. Every ball touching both faces is then the one about C. There is no rolling: the piece along the end curve is the ball's triangle. It runs between the two end sections, which are great circles through the ball's contact P with the face, and the ball's contact circle with the earlier round. The fork traces the piece instead, and the trace fails (`c4_cusp_recovery`, then `SM_FILERR_SURF_COMPUTATION`, 1001). The same piece builds where the end arc lies in a plane square to the earlier round's axis: there the pair is coaxial, and the round about that axis has a tube-centre radius of 0.
- **Source:** solidcore #2403 (from tangency-labs/smlib#94). This is solidcore-native, with no fork commit.
- **Rust port:** under the same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner`:
  - **Tori are read.** `quadric_of_surface` reads a torus (analytic, or a NURB surface that `Torus::is_nurb_surface_torus` recognises), kept only when it lies on the surface. The torus gets no class of its own. Its offset by its tube radius is `QuadricKind::Circle`, as a cylinder's offset by its radius is its axis.
  - **The point spine.** `classify_ball_on_collapsed_offset` runs after the fork's classification leaves a constant round traced. Where one offset collapsed onto a line or circle and the other (a plane, sphere, cylinder or cone) crosses it transversally at the trace's centre, the spine is `BlendSpine::Point` about the crossing. The crossing is found by Newton's method on the curve's parameter against the other offset's implicit form, at most 50 steps.
  - **The triangle.** `FilletSolver::calc_ball_triangle_fillet_geom` builds that piece as the blend corner's Ball patch (`ball_triangle_patch`, degree (4, 2), with `SmBallPatch::Create`'s refusals). Its pole is P. Its third side is the contact circle's arc between the rails' ends, which the Joins placed. Its rails are its v = min and v = max iso curves.
  - **UV curves at the pole.** `sm_CreateUVCurve` takes an end that drops to one solution on a degenerate side of the surface (`degenerate_side_at`: the side's iso curve `IsDegenerate` at `SM_EFF_ZERO`) onto the iso curve through the other end, as its rule for an end with more than two solutions does. Without this, the global solve reports the pole once, anywhere along its side. The end sections at the Joins then got a diagonal UV curve, and an edge tolerance of 2.7 merged the wall's vertex into the front's ("Result Is Not A Closed Manifold Solid").
- **Triggers:** #851's knife-edge plate (the capi's `knife_edge_arc_part`) in two orders. `851-fillet-arc-then-top-front`: the arc's top edge R2, then the top front edge's chain (the edge, the arc round's end curve, the upright). `851-fillet-top-front-then-upright`: the top front edge R2, then the upright's chain (the upright, the first round's end curve, the arc's top edge).
- **Confirmed:** in solidcore, graded on geometry. Both orders give the shape of the three edges rounded together, with 10 faces and `check_brep` Level2 clean. The corner face is the ball about C = (62 1/6 - sqrt(525), -19, 8), radius 2: its surface and edges lie within 8.9e-13 of that sphere, and it is G1 to its neighbours within 4.0e-13. The volumes are 9524.343669157 (arc first) and 9524.343664766 (top front first), against 9524.343664763 for the three together (4.6e-10 and 3e-13 relative). SolidCraft's expected value is 9524.342896 to its grid's 5e-3. Without the flag both orders still fail, as in the fork. The flat end arc (`851-fillet-top-front-then-front-left-upright`) is unchanged, at its closed form to 1e-9.
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2403_chain_over_end_curve.rs`; the two orders fail without the change. Unit tests: `blend::fillet_exact::tests::a_torus_moved_in_by_its_tube_is_its_circle`, `a_collapsed_offset_crossed_by_the_other_is_the_ball`, and `blend::fillet_geom::p4::tests::uv_curve_from_a_pole_is_the_iso_curve_through_its_other_end` (flag on and off).
