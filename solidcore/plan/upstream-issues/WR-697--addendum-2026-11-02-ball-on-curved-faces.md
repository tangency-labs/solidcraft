Updates: WR-697

#### WR-697 (update, 2026-11-02). Three equal rounds at a vertex of planes, cylinders, cones and spheres meet in the old corner's patch, off the ball
- **Severity:** Wrong result (accuracy)
- **Area:** Fillets (corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletCorner::Create` (the three-solver case builds a 3x3 corner) and `SmFilletCorner::CreateCornerPatch`.
- **What happens:** where three constant circular rounds of one radius r and one convexity meet at a vertex whose faces are planes or natural quadrics (a cylinder, a cone, a sphere), the ball of radius r touching the three faces is centred where the faces' offsets by r meet. Each round's end section through that centre is still a great circle of the ball: the ball's centre runs square to both contact normals, so the section lies in the plane of the two normals through the centre. The pinned corner is a patch fitted between the rounds' ends, off the sphere (the fork measured seams 1.2e-6 to 4.5e-6 rad and the D prism's volume 2.2e-10 off Steiner's). The fork's Ball (WR-697's 2026-11-02 Ball addendum) took only three planes.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's smlib#110 (tangency-labs/smlib 7048946; its capi `ball` cases 9a5a8ce and 3969288). `SmBlendCorner::ClassifyBall` takes each face that is a plane or a natural quadric (`sm_BallFaceQuadric`, `SmBlendQuadric::FromSurface` at the face's UV mid point), a cylinder or sphere only when wider than the ball, the three normals at the vertex independent. `SmBlendCorner::BallCentre` solves the centre where two or three offsets are planes in closed form: the two offset planes' line meets the third offset, a plane (one root) or a quadric (a quadratic along the line, solved without cancellation, the root nearest the rounds' ball). Otherwise it uses Newton's method on the offsets' implicit forms, accepted only where Krawczyk's test proves the one root in a box of 1e-12 of the faces' size (`sm_BallCertifiedRoot`). The contacts are the centre moved back along each offset's normal. The patch is the same as between planes.
- **Rust port:** under the same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner`, in `crate::blend::blend_corner::ball` (`classify_ball`, which now takes the vertex as the fork's does; `ball_centre`; `ball_face_quadric`, `BallImplicit`, `ball_inverse`, `ball_certified_root`). The Newton loop is bounded at 50 steps, as in the fork. With the flag off nothing changes.
- **Triggers:** the D prism (the disc R 10 less x > 4, 10 tall, every edge r 2): four Balls on the convex cylinder, the volume Steiner's, 2247.518144467428. The D pocket (a 40 x 40 x 20 block less a D prism R 8, flat x = 24, floor z = 10; the floor's chord and arc and the uprights r 2): two Balls on the concave cylinder, the volume 30426.315667313200. The cone frustum (a cylinder R 10 from z 0 to 6 under a cone narrowing at 30 degrees to z 10, less x > 4, every edge r 1.5): six Balls, two of them (the flat, the cylinder and the cone, one plane) through the certified root.
- **Confirmed:** in the port. The fork at dda881f was built locally and run on the three triggers, with the bodies built from the same SMLib calls the port's test makes (`SmCircle::CreateCanonical` arcs in the frame of their start, `SmApiCreateLinearSweep`, the SM_API booleans), through `SmFilletExecutive` (the per-edge `SetFilletParameters`, `StitchAndOrient`, `SmApiBrepComputeVolume` at 1e-12). Fork and port give the same counts (14/24/12, 15/30/18, 20/36/18) and every corner face on its sphere about the closed-form centre: the same per-corner sampled deviations to the four digits printed, 2.7e-15 to 1.2e-14. The fork's volumes are 2247.51814446744, 30426.3156673132 and 2134.91230051228 (15 significant digits). The port's are 2247.518144467436, 30426.315667313160 and 2134.912300512333, the frustum's 2.4e-14 relative from the fork's. Values not compared bit for bit. The planar Ball triggers' volumes (the Ball addendum) are unchanged in the port.
