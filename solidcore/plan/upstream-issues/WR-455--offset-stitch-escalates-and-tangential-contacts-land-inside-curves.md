#### WR-455. A shell fails where an offset crosses another tangentially or barely: the offset's protective stitch escalates its tolerance, and a tangential curve contact is placed inside the curve
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Offset
- **Location** (at 3fd5b95): (1) `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp:1105`-`:1968`, `SmOffsetExecutive::DoExtendedOffset`: `SmStitch::StitchIntoSolid(pResult, ...)` at `:1816`, after the piecewise merge. (2) `source/SMLib/src/SmCurve.cpp:6178`-`:7847`, `SmCurve::GlobalCurveIntersect`: the point solutions after `sm_RefineCCSolutions` (calls at `:6242` and `:6670`).
- **What happens:** (1) `StitchIntoSolid` doubles its tolerance until no lamina edge is left (up to a 200th of the body's size). After a piecewise merge the lamina edges are the rims of the pieces the intersections cut off, which `MakeManifold` removes next; escalating, the stitch welds two of them (an arc and the chord between its ends) into one edge of a far larger tolerance and the shell comes apart. (2) Two curves that touch tangentially stay within the intersection tolerance of each other over a stretch, and where in it the point solution lands is set by noise; an offset's intersection curve that leaves a face's edge tangentially was found touching it 3.3e-4 of parameter inside its start, the stretch before became an edge whose two ends are one point, with a sliver face beside it, and `SmMerge::MakeFaceuse` fails (1001).
- **Trigger:** (1) a 40 x 40 x 10 plate with an r 2 through hole at (4.99, 20) shelled 1.5 inward open at the top: the hole's offset crosses the offset side 0.01 mm deep. (2) SolidCraft's ladder parts 09/011, 10/008, 11/004 (a round whose offset is tangent to the wall's offset), reported in tangency-labs/solidcore#855; not reproduced in the port.
- **How found:** gdb on SolidCraft's shell failures in the fork, tangency-labs/solidcore#855.
- **Confirmed:** (1) no in C++ (read at 3fd5b95 `SmOffsetExecutive.cpp:1816`); in the port the shell above is no manifold solid by default. (2) no (read at 3fd5b95 `SmCurve.cpp:6242`, `:6670`); the fork's run (09/011 solid, volume 15397.69983 mm3) is not the pinned C++.
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@601d804: (1) one `SmStitch` pass at the body's tolerance; (2) `sm_MoveContactsToCurveEnds`: a point solution moves to an end of either curve when that end lies within tolerance of the other curve and the curves stay within tolerance of each other over the whole stretch, measured on each curve.
- **Rust port:** `Kernel::fixes.offset_stitch_at_body_tolerance` (`OffsetExecutive::do_extended_offset`) and `Kernel::fixes.curve_contacts_at_curve_ends` (`move_contacts_to_curve_ends`, `Curve::global_curve_intersect`). Default off: upstream behaviour. Test `issue_855_offset_stitch_keeps_the_bodys_tolerance`: the plate above is no solid by default, and with the stitch fix a solid of volume 4564.924657 (analytic) to 1e-6, also with the contact fix on. The contact fix has no test that reaches its move: no small input was found whose point solution lands inside the tangent stretch.
