#### WR-510. `SmCone::IntersectWithPlane` takes a plane parallel to a cylinder's axis as tangent only when its crossing lines coincide
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a plane a hair off a cylinder wall it touches is cut in two lines microns apart, so a union with it fails or leaves a sliver face)
- **Area:** Surfaces / cone (cylinder)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmCone.cpp`, `SmCone::IntersectWithPlane`, the cylinder branch for a plane parallel to the axis: the tangent test `if(smos_Fabs(dDistPlaneToCenterBot) >= dBotRadius || (smos_Fabs(dDistPlaneToCenterBot) > dBotRadius-dTol && (dBotRadius*dBotRadius - dDistPlaneToCenterBot*dDistPlaneToCenterBot) < dTol*dTol))`.
- **What happens:** the plane counts as tangent only when its two crossing lines with the wall are within the tolerance of each other (`R^2 - d^2 < tol^2`). A plane turned a hair off a wall it touches dips in far less than the tolerance, but its two crossing lines lie each `sqrt(2 R depth)` from the point nearest the axis, so they are about `2 sqrt(2 R depth)` apart. For depth 1e-11 and R 3 each is 7.7e-6 from that point and the two are 1.5e-5 apart, well beyond `tol` 1e-6, so the cylinder is cut in two lines instead of touching along one.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcore#1525: a union of a box and a flange plate under it, both with upright corners rounded r 3, sharing planes, where the plate's planes are turned 1e-13 rad. The union fails or leaves a sliver face.
- **How found:** SolidCraft fork (tangency-labs/smlib#55), reported in tangency-labs/solidcore#1525.
- **Confirmed:** no. The pinned C++ is not built on this machine, so the fork's merged change is the evidence. The port reproduces the two-line result with the opt-in off: `issue1525_plane_within_tolerance_of_wall_is_tangent` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/cone/p3.rs`) gets two crossing lines for a plane 1e-11 inside a radius-3 wall with bare surfaces.
- **Fix (fork):** tangency-labs/smlib#55, merged with its second commit bd20f6e. That commit keeps the original test and adds a zone-tolerance disjunct: `|| smos_Fabs(dDistPlaneToCenterBot) > dBotRadius-dZoneTol`, where `dZoneTol` is the larger of the cone face's and the plane face's tolerance (0 for a surface with no face). So one tangent line is taken when the two crossing lines are within the approximation tolerance (as before), or when the plane lies within the zone tolerance of a bounded face's wall. A bare cylinder and plane keep their two lines (the fork's `sunk_cylinder` test, sunk 5e-7 to 5e-13 at tol 1e-6). The first commit, 7b1c0e8, used `if( smos_Fabs(dDistPlaneToCenterBot) > dBotRadius-dTol )` alone, which the issue text quotes; it was superseded by bd20f6e and is not what is ported.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.cylinder_plane_tangent_within_tolerance` (default `false`, upstream's behaviour) in `Cone::intersect_with_plane_owned` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/cone/p3.rs`), which takes the zone tolerances of the cone's face and the plane's face (`zone_tols`, `None` for no face, read only with the flag on). With the flag on the test is upstream's OR `|d| > R - zone_tol`; with the flag off it is upstream's test unchanged. Test: `issue1525_plane_within_tolerance_of_wall_is_tangent`, which asserts with bare surfaces and the flag on two crossing lines for a plane 1e-11 inside a radius-3 cylinder, one tangent line on the wall when a face zone tolerance of 1e-5 is given to the plane or to the cone (and two crossing lines with the flag off), two crossing lines for a plane at d = 2, and nothing at d = 4.
