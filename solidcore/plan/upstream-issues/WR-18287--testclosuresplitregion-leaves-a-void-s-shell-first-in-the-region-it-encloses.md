#### WR-18287. `TestClosureSplitRegion` leaves a void's shell first in the region the split encloses

- **Severity:** Wrong result (invalid topology)
- **Area:** Region nesting, `topology.brep_p6`
- **Location:** the end of `SmBrep::TestClosureSplitRegion` in `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp`: the shell nesting skips a shell whose region does not change ("might break 'the region's 1st shell is the outer shell' rule"), and `pOldRegion->OrderShells()` runs only when a shell moved; `SmRegion::OrderShells` (`SmRegion.cpp`) returns at once when the first shell is a face-use shell.
- **What happens:** SMLib takes the first shell of every bounded region as the one enclosing the others (`SmBrep::AssertValid` test 12). When the split leaves the side of the closed boundary that encloses its region (the inner shell) in the old region, and that region already held another shell listed ahead of it, nothing moves it forward. A solid opened into the infinite region by a blend holds its void's shell there before the stitch closes it again; when the split then gives the outside to the new region, the solid's region keeps the void's shell first.
- **Trigger:** a Ø20 x 20 mm cylinder with a Ø2 x 4 mm blind hole in its top face, a Ø20 x 5 mm cylinder joined on top (sealing the hole into a void), then a 2 mm chamfer on an outer rim. The fillet's stitch closes the body and splits the infinite region. Which side of the boundary the new region takes depends on the face use the split starts from: on solidcore's own operations the bottom rim, chamfered or rounded, gives it the outside (the defect), the top rim the inside (no defect); the issue's script through SolidCraft's commands builds the body differently and hits it on the top rim.
- **How found:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#18287 (stress corpus item S0433): the chamfered body fails its mapping to the portable B-rep with "a closed shell bounds two regions and encloses one".
- **Confirmed:** on the port, which follows the C++ here. A trace of every region list edit during the chamfer: before the stitch the infinite region holds the void's shell (3 face uses) ahead of the body's pieces; after the split the new region holds the outside (4 face uses) and becomes the infinite region, and the old region lists the void's shell, then the inner shell (4 face uses). With the inside in the new region, which starts with it, the order is right. Upstream's `OrderShells` would not repair it either: it returns at once when the first shell is a face-use shell, as the void's is.
- **Rust port:** unconditional. `split_closed` in `crates/solidcore/src/topology/brep/p6.rs` puts the inner shell first in its region after the nesting, reusing the split's own `IsInnerShell` classification (no further ray fire). Tests `a_round_on_a_solid_with_a_cavity_keeps_the_outer_shell_first` (`crates/solidcore/tests/fork_351c57c_stitch_nested_solid.rs`: the issue's body rounded with r 2 on its bottom rim, the cavity's shell first before the change) and, end to end through the export, SolidCraft's `a_blend_on_a_cap_that_seals_a_hole_keeps_the_void`.
