#### WR-707. A round that breaks through a thin wall builds a body that intersects itself
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletExecutive.cpp`, `SmFilletExecutive::CreateFilletBrep`: no check refuses a convex blend whose section between its rails crosses another face of the part (the fork's `SmFilletSolver::BandCutsThePart` at tangency-labs/smlib 5e8c9c1 counts such a crossing as material kept).
- **What happens:** A box shelled outward by 2 mm, open at the bottom ((-52, -32, 0)..(52, 32, 42) less (-50, -30, -1)..(50, 30, 40)), has its outer top edge along y = 32, z = 42 rounded with r 7. The ball's centre is at (25, 35) in (y, z), and the wall's inner corner (30, 40) lies sqrt 2 (r - 2) = 7.07 from it, so the round crosses the inner faces y = 30 and z = 40. The pinned port builds it anyway: 12 faces, the whole section (1 - pi/4) 49 104 removed, the round's surface running through the hollow without trimming the inner faces (#2299). The largest radius that stays inside the wall is 2 sqrt 2 / (sqrt 2 - 1) = 6.828427.
- **Trigger:** a convex round larger than the wall it rounds, on a thin-walled part (SolidCraft #1668).
- **How found:** SolidCraft's case `rounds_on_both_sides_of_a_wall_build_as_one_fillet`, reported as #2299.
- **Confirmed:** in the Rust port (pinned behaviour, every `UpstreamFixes` field on before this record). The pinned C++ was not run on this case. Fork 5e8c9c1's SMLib/SM_API, built locally and driven through `SetFilletParameters`, `CreateFilletCorners` and `DoFilleting`, segfaults inside `DoFilleting` for r7 and r6.8.
- **Rust fix (opt-in):** `UpstreamFixes::fillet_refuse_rounds_breaking_through`, with `fillet_gn_section_generator`. The 32 sections of `BandCutsThePart` are also judged for crossing a face of the part inside it (the faces at the blended edge's ends excepted). Such a blend is refused with `NoRoom` (`NoRoomReason::BreaksThrough`), naming the largest radius that stays inside, and that radius less the fit margin is the refusal's fit. Regression test `crates/solidcore/tests/wr707_round_breaking_through.rs`.
