Updates: WR-697

#### WR-697 (update, 2026-11-02). Three equal rounds at a vertex of three planes meet in a Coons patch or the analytic sphere, not the ball's exact rational patch
- **Severity:** Wrong result (accuracy)
- **Area:** Fillets (corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletCorner::Create` (the three-solver case builds a 3x3 corner, `SmFilletConvexNxNCorner` or `SmFilletConcaveNxNCorner`) and `SmFilletCorner::CreateCornerPatch` (`MakeAnalyticCornerSurface` fits an `SmSphere` where one fits, else a Coons patch).
- **What happens:** where three constant circular rounds of one radius r and one convexity meet at a vertex of three planes, the ball of radius r touching the three planes touches each round along that round's section through its centre, a great circle of the ball. The rounds' ends are where their rails cross, as the solver found them. The corner face is a Coons patch, or an `SmSphere` where one fits (the square box corner). The fork makes the corner's face exactly the ball's triangle between the three contacts, one rational patch on the sphere, G1 to every round, at square and oblique vertices alike, and ends each round on the ball's exact section.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's smlib#79 step 4c (tangency-labs/smlib 79d4763, a6243fa, f8e6709, 4241a1b): `SmBlendCorner::ClassifyBall`, `MakeBallTopology`, `PlaceBallContacts`, `BuildBallSurface` (`sm_BallTrianglePatch`) and `CreateBallPatch` (`source/SMLib/src/SmBlendCornerBall.cpp`); `TestAndConvertBigRadFillet` leaves a Ball's rounds alone. The fork's capi `ball` cases (c3f8c80, ce9efb4, 9c58248, f8059d4).
- **Rust port:** under the same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner`. A vertex with three blended edge uses among three is the blend corner's Ball (`crate::blend::blend_corner::ball`) when each is a constant circular round of one radius, all are convex or all concave, the three faces are distinct planes meeting at a point, and the caller has not set the vertex back (`FilletSession::add_set_back_vertex`, the fork's `AddSetBackVertex`). Its face is the fork's rational patch of degree (4, 2), a `SurfaceKind::BSpline` with the fork's net. The fork's f8e6709 also wraps that net in `SmBallPatch`, carrying the sphere as its definition (its own type, an exact offset and its database IO). That class follows in its own change.
- **Triggers (each with its closed-form volume):** a 40 x 30 x 20 box, the three edges at (40, 30, 20) rounded r 3: 23830.692428057893. A 40 x 30 x 20 block less a 20 x 10 x 11 box at (10, 10, 10), the pocket floor corner's three concave edges r 2: 22032.997059573161. A kite prism 10 tall (corner of 120 degrees), the three edges at the top of that corner r 2: 1713.318744345522. A corner of three planes none square to another (normals along (1, 0.2, 0), (0, 1, 0.2), (0.2, 0, 1)), its edges r 2: the body's volume less the closed-form removal, 13426.588615521434.
- **Confirmed:** in the port. The fork 5e8c9c1 was built locally and run on the four triggers, the bodies built as the port's test builds them, through `SmFilletExecutive` (`SetDoGlobalMerge(TRUE)`, the per-edge `SetFilletParameters`, `StitchAndOrient`, volume at 1e-12). Its volumes are 23830.692428057911, 22032.997059573168, 1713.318744345519 and 13426.588615521479, with the same face, edge and vertex counts (10/21/13, 15/33/21, 10/21/13, 10/21/13). The port prints 23830.692428057911, 22032.997059573165, 1713.318744345519 and 13426.588615521474. On the box, pocket and prism, the patch's net (control point [1][1] and its weight) and its point at (0.3, 0.7) agree with the fork's to all 17 digits printed. On the oblique corner they differ at about 1e-16, as do the body's planes before the round (for example, a normal's z of -0.196116135138184072 here and -0.196116135138184100 in the fork). That gap is in the boolean-built body, not the Ball. Not compared bit for bit. Without the flag, the box corner is the pinned 3x3 corner's `SmSphere`.
