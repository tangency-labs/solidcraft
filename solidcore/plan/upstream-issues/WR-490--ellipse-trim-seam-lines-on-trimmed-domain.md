#### WR-490. `sm_GetEllipseTrimIntervals` drops an ellipse whose only seam crossing lies in a trimmed-off part of the face
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Surfaces and volumes
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmCone.cpp`, `sm_GetEllipseTrimIntervals` (file static; def. `:5282` at `c9c5979`, `:5315` in NVIDIA usd-brep 1.0.1, `:5304` in the fork): the seam-line corners (`:5368` in 1.0.1) and the early return on fewer than two crossings (`:5532`). Reached from `SmCone::IntersectWithCone` (equal cylinders with crossing axes, `sm_AddEllipseIntervalsTrimmedToTwoCones`) with the faces' UV domains from `SmFace::FaceIntersect`.
- **What happens:** the function cuts the full ellipse at its crossings with the cone's boundaries and classifies the pieces. The end circles are taken at the cone's own height (`GetSTEPUVDomain().GetVMin()` plus `GetHeight()`), and the pieces are classified with `SmCone::STEPInversion`, which ignores the domain it is given (it uses `m_vAnalUVDomain`). Only the two seam lines are evaluated on the given domain's v range. A face trimmed along its axis (a cylinder already joined to another body) then loses the seam crossing that lies in its trimmed-off part; with no crossing left (`lCnt < 2`) the ellipse is returned as lying outside the cone, although the face holds part of it.
- **Trigger:** a cylinder C r 44.45 along x (-200..200) and two rods r 24.15 at +-45 deg in the XZ plane whose axes meet at (0, 0, 30), each 240 long from 40 behind that point; `(C u A) u B` with SMLib's own cylinders (`smlib_make_cylinder`, seam turned 0, 35 or 60 deg about the axis). Rod A's face in `C u A` starts at v = 36.29; its seam crosses the rods' ellipse in the plane x = 0 at v = 15.85. The half ellipse through (0, 0, 64.15) is dropped, B's side face gets no curve with A's, and the union is `C u A` exactly (2816296.79, status OK, check clean; the volume is 3137849.37 from slices, FreeCAD 3137858.71). The same joint with tubes (SolidCraft rig `booleans/steel-026-chs-k-joint-overlapped-braces` built from SMLib's cylinders) fails the braces' union (1001) and the chord's union (-1100), or with the seam at 90 deg makes a body 4592 mm3 too big that the check passes. `C u (A u B)` and `(C u B) u A` are right.
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig (steel-026 rebuilt from C API cylinders); traced from `SmMerge::ManifoldBoolean` (an open intersection loop) through `SmTopologyIntersector::IntersectTopology` (the face pair with no curve), `SmFace::FaceIntersect` and `SmCone::IntersectWithCone` to the trim intervals, with the crossings and the pieces' classes logged.
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the C++ build (the fork, tangency-labs/smlib@5af7070 with the WR-265 and WR-266 changes, x86-64 Linux, g++): evaluating the seam lines on the cone's whole height (the sweep still from the domain) makes all 4 seam angles of both joints right; fork tests `capi/tests/booleans.cpp` `equal_rods_join` and `equal_tubes_join` fail without it. The other caller, `SmCone::IntersectWithPlane`, passes the natural domain and does not change (`oblique_cone_cuts`). NVIDIA 1.0.1 has the same code.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.ellipse_trim_seam_lines_span_cone_height` (`UpstreamFixes`, default `false`, upstream behaviour). With it, `ellipse_trim_intervals` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/cone/p2.rs`) follows the fork's fix: the seam-line corners are evaluated on the cone's v range and the domain's sweep. Tests in the same file: `seam_crossing_below_a_trimmed_domain_keeps_the_ellipse` (field on; a cylinder trimmed to v >= 30, an ellipse that meets the seam only at z = 20) fails without the change, and `seam_crossing_below_a_trimmed_domain_drops_the_ellipse_by_default` (field off: the ellipse is returned as outside, as C++). The fix was first unconditional; it then changed 48 `surface.cone_p2` and 4 `surface.cone_p3` difftest cases against the unpatched `c9c5979`, so it is opt-in (as WR-469, #870). With the default, both units match C++. Callers that need the fix (SolidCraft) set the field.
