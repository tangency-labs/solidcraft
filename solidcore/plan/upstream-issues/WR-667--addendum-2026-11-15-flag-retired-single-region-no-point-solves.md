Updates: WR-667

- **Flag retired:** `Kernel::fixes.offset_keep_cavity_regions` is gone; its fixed path is the only path. For an inset solid offset, `do_extended_offset` (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p2.rs`) passes `make_manifold` only the finite regions `cavity::is_cavity_region` finds cavity, as before under the flag.
- **Change to the fixed path:** the cavity verdicts are asked only when the merge leaves two or more finite regions. With one, the selection cannot change which regions `make_manifold` keeps (a lone cavity region is every region, and with none cavity nothing is selected), so the body's point-solve cache and its three point solves per face are not built. The verdicts' own side effects on the merged body (the UV trim curves, gap and trimmed surface caches and any tolerance growth the live point classification leaves on it) are then not made either; under SolidCraft's preset, which ran the verdicts, that is the only difference for a single-region inset (measured: the goldens and the app suites). Before #17064, the swept bar of `tests/issue_1476_swept_bar_shell.rs`, read with every entity under one name, failed with `Unsupported` ("point-solve geometry owners must identify one edge or face each") building that cache, although its merge has one finite region; since #17064 the reader refuses such a body and the fixture names entities apart. A body whose geometry owners are ambiguous and whose merge leaves two or more finite regions is still refused that way: the point solve cannot tell its faces apart.
- **Why:** SolidCraft ran with the flag on (its `fixes()` preset); the kernel's default did not. With the flag on, no goldens row moves.
- **Tests:** `tests/fork_37af643_inset_keeps_cavity_regions.rs` runs on the default kernel and checks the frame with `check_brep` at every level; its flag-off test of the old path is deleted, and the 1.0-floor control is checked against its closed form 669. `tests/wr567_revolution_round_offset_collapses.rs`: without the collapse test, the disc open at the other end now shells to its closed form (to 1e-6, the fixture's tolerance) and passes `check_brep` at every level, where the inset that kept every finite region failed with 1001. `tests/issue_1476_swept_bar_shell.rs` `issue_1476_swept_bar_shells` (entities named apart) shells on the default kernel to the closed form; `issue_1476_swept_bar_shells_with_solidcraft_fixes_validates` (ignored like its slow siblings) also passes `check_brep` at every level under SolidCraft's set. On the default kernel that shell has one edge 4.6e-5 off its surfaces (tolerance 1e-5), with or without this change; the cavity selection does not run for it.
