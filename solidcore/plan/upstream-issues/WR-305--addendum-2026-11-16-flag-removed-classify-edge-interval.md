Updates: WR-305

#### WR-305 (update, 2026-11-16). `classify_edge_interval` is removed; curved pieces along an edge are certified

`CurveClassification::classify_edge_interval` (`intersect/curve_class/p3.rs`) now classifies a curved interval on a face edge by default, and only by a certificate: `curve_piece_runs_along_edge` drops both ends of the interval onto the edge, requires the curve to be tangent to the edge's curve at both drops (`tangent_to_edge`), and proves the curve over the interval and the edge's curve between the drops within the intersection tolerance of each other as wholes on control points (`curve_runs_along`). The fork's midpoint sample is gone; straight pairs keep the end-point certificate of the 2026-11-13 update. Every way the certificate can fail leaves the interval as the edge intersections left it, which is main's default result, so the change adds no refusal of an operation. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::classify_edge_interval`, its doc file and every test setting of it.

Without the tangency requirement the certificate accepted a short piece of a sphere/box-corner crossing arc that cuts across a corner to an edge's vertex (goldens `tess0033`, a Level 2 clean union on main): the piece lies within the band only because it is about as short as the band, and classifying it on the edge made the union fail to relate the operands' entities. With the tangency requirement `tess0033` is unchanged.

- **Results:** one golden row changes (goldens of the touched units against origin/main, 2026-11-20).
  - `boolean.topology_intersector_p2` `gen0433`: the proto vertex/edge/face tail goes from 8, 5, 0 to 5, 3, 0: two B-spline pieces are classified on their B-spline edges (tolerance 7.567e-3) instead of being left as separate edges.
  - `blend.feature_executive` `single_face_fillet1019_6`, which moved on the branch's earlier base (one edge's tolerance 1.37e-3 to 2.58e-3, through the lazy UV-trim refit of #18162), no longer moves: on the current base no curved piece of that case reaches the certificate (`curved_edge_runs` 0), and the row is identical to main's.
- **Tests:** the goldens harness's `gen0433_puts_two_certified_curved_pieces_on_their_edges` (replays the row: the new tail and two certified pieces), and the unit tests in `p3.rs` (a piece along a curved edge is certified; a crossing piece and a piece off the edge are left alone). `WorkCounters::curved_edge_runs` counts certified pieces as the tests' witness.
