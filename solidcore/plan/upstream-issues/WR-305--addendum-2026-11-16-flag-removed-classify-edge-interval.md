Updates: WR-305

#### WR-305 (update, 2026-11-16). `classify_edge_interval` is removed; curved pieces along an edge are certified

`CurveClassification::classify_edge_interval` (`intersect/curve_class/p3.rs`) now classifies a curved interval on a face edge by default, and only by a certificate: `curve_piece_runs_along_edge` drops both ends of the interval onto the edge, requires the curve to be tangent to the edge's curve at both drops (`tangent_to_edge_at_ends`), and proves the curve over the interval and the edge's curve between the drops within the intersection tolerance of each other as wholes on control points (`curve_runs_along`). The fork's midpoint sample is gone; straight pairs keep the end-point certificate of the 2026-11-13 update. Every way the certificate declines (a failed drop, two drops at one parameter, a crossing at either end, a bound not proved, an edge with no 3D curve) leaves the interval as the edge intersections left it, which is main's default result. An error from evaluating the curves or dropping a point onto the edge is not a decline: it propagates as the classification's error, as the same evaluations do elsewhere in it. The tangents at the interval's ends are read from inside the piece (on the curve, the span starting at its lower end and the span ending at its upper end; on the edge, the span from each drop towards the other), so a piece leaving or meeting a knot at an angle to the edge is not passed by a neighbouring span's direction. The same applies to the tangency check for an unclassified end (`ends_on_edge`), which shares the function. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::classify_edge_interval`, its doc file and every test setting of it.

Without the tangency requirement the certificate accepted a short piece of a sphere/box-corner crossing arc that cuts across a corner to an edge's vertex (goldens `tess0033`, a Level 2 clean union on main): the piece lies within the band only because it is about as short as the band, and classifying it on the edge made the union fail to relate the operands' entities. With the tangency requirement `tess0033` is unchanged.
