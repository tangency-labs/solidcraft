Updates: WR-305

#### WR-305 (update, 2026-11-16). `classify_edge_interval` is removed; curved pieces along an edge are certified

`CurveClassification::classify_edge_interval` (`intersect/curve_class/p3.rs`) now classifies a curved interval on a face edge by default, and only by a certificate: `curve_piece_runs_along_edge` drops both ends of the interval onto the edge, requires the curve to be tangent to the edge's curve at both drops (`tangent_to_edge`), and proves the curve over the interval and the edge's curve between the drops within the intersection tolerance of each other as wholes on control points (`curve_runs_along`). The fork's midpoint sample is gone; straight pairs keep the end-point certificate of the 2026-11-13 update. Every way the certificate can fail leaves the interval as the edge intersections left it, which is main's default result, so the change adds no refusal of an operation. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::classify_edge_interval`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entry in `fixes.json` and every test setting of it.

Without the tangency requirement the certificate accepted a short piece of a sphere/box-corner crossing arc that cuts across a corner to an edge's vertex (goldens `tess0033`, a Level 2 clean union on main): the piece lies within the band only because it is about as short as the band, and classifying it on the edge made the union fail to relate the operands' entities. With the tangency requirement `tess0033` is unchanged.

- **Results:** two golden rows change (goldens `--all` with the flag on against off on the branch's base).
  - `blend.feature_executive` `single_face_fillet1019_6`: one edge's tolerance and its two vertices', in the base and in the feature B-rep, go from 0.0013684680561596358 to 0.0025778854416825133. Graph and every geometry sample are identical. Two tiny curved pieces (a circle on an ellipse edge, a circle on a B-spline edge) are now classified on their edges, so stitching merges the edges and fits a new UV trim whose fit error sets the larger tolerance. The edge's measured gap to both its faces' surfaces is 2.65e-4 on main and on the branch, and Level 2 reports the same 14 findings on both: the new tolerance is looser than needed but valid.
  - `boolean.topology_intersector_p2` `gen0433`: the proto vertex/edge/face tail goes from 8, 5, 0 to 5, 3, 0: two B-spline pieces are classified on their B-spline edges (tolerance 7.567e-3) instead of being left as separate edges.
- **Tests:** `wr305_corner_round_defeature_edge_tolerance` (replays `single_face_fillet1019_6`, checks exactly that edge and its vertices moved, and bounds the edge curve's distance to the cylinder each of its faces lies on, by Bézier decomposition and convex-hull bounds with rounding counted, below the old tolerance; and that two curved pieces were certified), the goldens harness's `gen0433_puts_two_certified_curved_pieces_on_their_edges` (replays the row: the new tail and two certified pieces), and the unit tests in `p3.rs` (a piece along a curved edge is certified; a crossing piece and a piece off the edge are left alone). `WorkCounters::curved_edge_runs` counts certified pieces as the tests' witness.
