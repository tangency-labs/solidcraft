Updates: WR-685. Under the same flag, `fillet_gn_section_generator` (its refusals and their sizes, noted WR-696 in the code), the refusals that had no size through the fit name one, each decided from its own geometry (#2401, carried over from tangency-labs/smlib#91). Solidcore-native, no fork commit: the fork's construction ended at 18e96f1 with this item open, and its archived WIP was not used.

- **A polygon face taken whole** (`refuse_polygons_taken_whole`, with `fillet_refuse_face_consuming_blends`). A plane face bounded by straight edges, each blended with a constant ball, is taken whole once the least of its balls reaches `NoteFitOfPolygonsTakenWhole`'s bound, within the blended edge's tolerance. It is judged before the corners and the trace, which failed first on rails of no length. The refusal is `FaceConsumed`, naming the bound less the fit margin.
  - The fork's `chamfers_taking_a_square_top_refused` (a 10 cube's top chamfered 5) was `SM_ERR` "Fillet Face Creation Error". It is now refused naming 4.99, and 4.99 builds at the closed form.
  - A 10 x 6 top chamfered 3.5 named 0.094 (`BandsMeetAt` across a shared vertex, jyviko on smlib#91). It now names 2.99, which builds.
- **A collapsed offset** (`refuse_collapsed_offsets`, `NoRoomReason::OffsetCollapses`; `blend::fillet_exact::ball_zone_open_at_an_end`). A constant ball as wide as a face it rolls on has its offset of that face collapsed (`QuadricKind::Line` or `Point`), so its centre is fixed. It is refused where the plane through an end of the edge, square to it, misses the centre: the crossing of that plane with the collapsed axis does not lie on the other face's offset, within the solver's tolerance. The refusal names the face's radius less the fit margin.
  - A ball past that radius refused by `BallIsRegular` as not enclosed names it open (less the margin) where the zone at that radius is open, and closed (as before) elsewhere.
  - The fork's `middle_edge_of_an_arc_tangent_chain_r2` was `SM_ERR` in the solver's geometry. It is now refused naming 2 less 1000 x 4e-5. At 2.5 it names the same, where it named 2.
  - The tray (#416) and the cornered block's rim (#417) still build at their closed forms.
  - With `fillet_blend_corner`, an end of the edge at a vertex where another blended edge runs on closes the zone whatever the plane: the Join places that round's section through the centre, and #2403 builds the piece as the ball's triangle. Judged by the plane alone, SolidCraft's `851-fillet-top-front-then-upright` chain (the upright, the edge over the top front round's end curve and the arc's top edge, R2) was refused naming 1.96, though #2403 builds it at R2. SolidCraft's retry at 1.96 then traced for minutes. It builds at R2 again, and no size is noted (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2431_851_chain_after_top_front_round.rs`, on SolidCraft's dumped body).
  - Below 2 the middle edge still fails (unsized `CornersTakeWholeEdge`, a corner error at 1.99). That is the smooth-vertex End's refusal (fork 41a39ec, #2364), which names 0 for it.
- **The open ends of an overflow** (`note_fit_on_its_faces`). An overflow's open end that its end face does not cut (`CliffEndFaceNotPlanar`, `CliffEndNotCut`), and a trimmed blend that keeps no length where no corner takes any, name where the end's blend stays on its faces: `LargestSizeOnItsFaces`, open, as the overflow's other refusals do.
  - A 10 x 20 x 30 box with a concave cylindrical end, its top edge rounded 12 or 10.01, is refused naming 9.99, which builds.
- **The fit margin stays.** `LargestSizeOnItsFaces` samples the ball through each boundary edge and keeps the least. It does not say whether the rail runs along that edge at the bound (the bossed can's footprint, closed) or touches it at a point (open). Closing the margin for the first kind needs that distinction, and the bossed can builds at 4.3 only with #2415.
