#### WR-472. `SmFace::ComputePreciseProperties` holds volume and moments too tightly to converge (1.0.1)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Mass properties
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFace.cpp:6214` at `3fd5b95` (`const double dLength = smos_Max(smos_Sqrt(dEstimatedArea), dOriginDistance/10.0);`), `SmFace::ComputePreciseProperties` (def. `:6144`); new in SMLib v1.0.1.
- **What happens:** 1.0.1 gives the volume and each moment group its own error scale for the Runge-Kutta integration: the area's (`yscale[0] = dEstimatedArea/10`, about a thousandth of the face's squared size) times `dLength` per extra length dimension (powers 1, 2, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3). `dLength` is `sqrt(dEstimatedArea)`, a tenth of the face's size, so every extra power holds a component ten times tighter, relative to its own size, than the area. The volume is held 10x tighter and the third-power moments 1000x tighter. 1.0.1 also integrates each trim-curve knot span separately, starting every component from zero. At a relative accuracy the API documents as usable (`SmApiQueries.h`: values below 1e-8 "not recommended"), the bound on those components falls below roundoff. `RungeKutta5thOrderStep` then reduces the step until `SM_ARE_SAME(x+h, x)` or its `eps <= errmax * SM_EFF_ZERO` guard fails. `ComputePreciseProperties` returns `SM_ERR`, and `SmBrep::ComputePreciseProperties`, `SmApiBrepComputeVolume` and `SmApiBrepComputeMassProperties` fail with it. At `c9c5979` the volume and moments had no bound of their own (`SM_BIG_DOUBLE`); the Rust port at that pin measures the same bodies.
- **Trigger:** `SmBrep::ComputePreciseProperties` of `SmApiCreateSphere`'s body at relative accuracy 1e-8 (the SolidCraft fork's report, below). The Rust port at 1.0.1 shows the same failure on four SolidCraft volume cases at 1e-8: three of #3303's frustum face cuts and #451's block_crossing_inner_sphere_join (`fixtures/solidcraft/3303-inward-cut-on-cone.json`, `451-hollow-shell-cut.json`; `mass::integrator` status 1001). It also fails on a full sphere of radius 3 at 1e-10 (`crates/solidcore/tests/primitives_solid.rs`).
- **How found:** pin move to v1.0.1: SolidCraft volume cases and the primitives test that passed at `c9c5979` began failing in the integrator. Traced to the scale (read of `SmFace.cpp:6205`-`6262`; the Rust port matches it operation for operation).
- **Confirmed:** confirmed in C++ 1.0.1 on pole spheres at 1e-10, 1e-9 and 1e-8 and on the four SolidCraft bodies (3303-inward-cut-on-cone x3, 451 block_crossing_inner_sphere_join; volume status 1001); the SolidCraft fork hit and fixed the same failure (tangency-labs/smlib@073d4740)
- **Fix (opt-in, not the default):** `Kernel::fixes.precise_properties_body_length_scale` uses the fork's length, `smos_Max(10.0 * smos_Sqrt(dEstimatedArea), dOriginDistance)`. That is the body size, or the origin's distance when larger, so every component is held to the area's share of its own size. A zero estimate still gives zero scales and fails, as before.
