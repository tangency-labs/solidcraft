#### WR-540. `SmEdge::AssertValid` test 9 refuses the valid radial order of faces tangent along an edge
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a valid body refused: "Bad Sector Ang Sequence", offset -180, or a few 1e-6 degrees on tori)
- **Area:** Validity checks
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmEdge.cpp`, `SmEdge::AssertValid` (def. `:7478`), test 9 of the radial edge-use walk over manifold and spine edges: the faces round the edge are ordered by their binormals at the edge.
- **What happens:** A boolean keeps the tangency of a cylinder or torus resting on a planar face as an edge that four faces share: the plane's two sides and the curved face's two halves. They are in the right radial order, and the body is closed and only non-manifold there. Tangent faces leave the edge in the same direction, so their binormals tie, or differ by rounding either way round. Test 9 then reads the right order as out of sequence.
- **Trigger:** box (20, 5, 5)-(35, 20, 32.5) minus a z cylinder at (25, 12.5, 22.5) r 5 h 25, which rests on x = 20. These are the seeds of tangency-labs/solidcore#383 (1, 4, 32, 36, 39, 65, 74, 85, 86): boxes minus or with cylinders and tori tangent to a face.
- **How found:** tangency-labs/solidcore#383. The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@5533bbb ("the check orders faces round a tangent edge by where they go"), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run was made here. The Rust port's `radial_order` follows the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`).
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.edge_check_orders_tangent_faces`, `radial_order` first walks the sectors. If any two faces' unit binormals at the test parameter are within `SM_ANG_TOL_DEG` (dot above its cosine), every face is then measured by the direction from the edge to its point 20 edge tolerances into the face (`EvaluateBinormalStepOff`, `sector_direction`), square to the edge. A failed step or unitisation keeps the binormal. `tests/wr_540_tangent_cylinder_edge_order.rs` checks that, with the fix, SMLib's check passes the box minus the resting cylinder.
