#### WR-647 (update, 2026-10-31). Curves within tolerance that keep one side come nearest at a point (the fork's 04b42b4)

Updates: WR-647

- **Fork change:** tangency-labs/smlib@04b42b4, the cherry-pick of 73114e7, adds case 3e to `SmCurve::SimpleCoincidenceChecker` (`source/SMLib/src/SmCurve.cpp`), right after c2e3d9d's crossing case. Where the curves stay within tolerance over the interval, their separation does not change sides, and the largest gap between them (`CurveMaxDistanceBetween`) is above `SM_EFF_ZERO * (1 + max end coordinate)`, the curves are taken as approaching, not coinciding. The answer is one `SM_ST_SINGLE_VALUE` solution where they come nearest: `LocalCurveSolve(SM_SO_MINIMIZE)` from the two intervals' middles, at the coincidence tolerance, with `SM_ERR` when it finds nothing. Only curves whose separation vanishes to numeric zero keep the coincidence range.
- **Found by:** the fork-exactness check of the flags that port fork fixes. The opt-in `Kernel::fixes.simple_coincidence_crossing_is_point` ported c2e3d9d's crossing case only. A probe on two concentric 90 degree arcs, r 5 and r 5 + gap with gap 1e-6, 5e-6 and 1e-9, at tolerance 1e-5, gave one range over the whole arcs where 04b42b4 gives one point. The fork side of that probe re-implemented the fork's rule; it was not a fork run.
- **Rust port:** the same flag now also takes case 3e, in `Curve::simple_coincidence_checker` (`crates/solidcore/src/curve/curve/p6.rs`). It is placed where the fork has it: inside the flag's block, after the crossing test, gated on the gap the coincidence test already measured against the crossing block's scaled zero, then `local_curve_solve` with the same arguments. The start point is the same expression as the fork's: `SmExtent1d::Evaluate(0.5)` is `m_dMin + t * (m_dMax - m_dMin)` (`source/SMLib/src/SmExtent1d.cpp`, unchanged in the fork), the same as `Extent1d::evaluate`. The two intervals are built as the fork builds them, from the first and last drops. With the flag off nothing changes.
- **Test:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr_647_concentric_arcs_nearest_point.rs`, the concentric arcs above. Off: one range over the whole arcs. On: one point, the arcs `gap` apart there. The same arc twice: a range in both settings. The fork commit has no test of its own.
- **Callers that change:** every operation reaching the checker with two curves that lie within tolerance on one side of each other without touching (booleans, validation, fillets, sections) now gets a point there instead of a range, with the flag on. SolidCraft's preset turns this flag on, so its results can change in those cases.
