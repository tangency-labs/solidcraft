#### WR-674. A round whose edge runs on tangentially into an edge of its own convexity fails the corner, or (WR-303) leaves an open body
- **Severity:** Failure (status 1001); with the WR-303 opt-in, wrong result (an open body reported as success by `DoFilleting`)
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletNx1Corner::MakeCornerTopology`, which at a three-edge vertex of uniform convexity makes one end rib from each rail's end on its side edge to the other's; `SmFilletEdge::CalcFilletIntSideFace`, which refuses the rib when a rail end is `SM_FV_NO_INT_RAIL_EU`. The fork's `SmFilletNx1Corner::CalcCornerEdgeGeom` (tangency-labs/smlib@967f7ab, WR-303) deletes such a rib and sets the global merge.
- **What happens:** at each end of the filleted edge, a side edge continues it tangentially with its convexity (a plate outline's straight side running on from a rounded corner arc). The rail on the face the two share runs alongside that edge and never meets it, so its end is `SM_FV_NO_INT_RAIL_EU` and the corner fails (`SM_FILERR_VERTEX_PROBLEM`, "Corner Edge Computation Failure", 1001). With WR-303 the end rib is deleted for the global merge, but no face of the body cuts the round off there (the round continued lies along the continued edge, inside the material), so `DoFilleting` returns success with a body that is not closed: 9 faces, 6 non-manifold edges, nothing removed. `DoFillet`'s check of WR-212 then refuses it.
- **Trigger:** an R25 disc with a tab (sides x = +-4, R2 corners, top y = 280/9 + 2) extruded 2; round r 0.5 on one R2 corner arc of the top, picked alone (tangency-labs/solidcore#2176). The straight edges next to it build alone.
- **How found:** SolidCraft's `fillet_tabbed_plate_outer_loop_and_partial_selection_after_hole` (tangency-labs/solidcore#2176).
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the C++ build: the difftest cases `issue_2176_tabbed_plate_*` of `blend.fillet_executive_p3` run the plate (`fixtures/blend.fillet_executive_p3/issue_2176_tabbed_plate.smb`) through `SmApiFilletEdges` on both sides and agree; the corner arc fails there with 1001. The fork's `CalcCornerEdgeGeom` and `DoFillet` (replay/1.0.1) take the same global merge and do not refuse the open body.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With the opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_round_ends_at_tangent_continuation` (own design, not in the fork), `FilletCorner::drop_end_rib` takes a missed side edge that continues the filleted edge tangentially (`sm_TestG1Continuity` within the corner's tangency tolerance), is straight, and reaches farther from the vertex than the other rail's end, as an end left open at the round's section through the vertex. A short or curved continuation (WR-303's arc, past which the round runs onto another face) keeps the global merge. The executive then builds the round by subtraction, as a cliff overflow does: the fillet face is cut by the plane through the vertex normal to the filleted edge (`cut_fillet_at_open_end`), `close_section_ended_round` closes it with a face on each of the filleted edge's faces (along the rail, the section's line to the vertex and the filleted edge) and a flat face at each section, and the region is subtracted from the body. Only one round whose two ends are both such ends is built; any other combination, or a face whose section is not straight, is refused with `FilletErrorType::NoRoom` (`NoRoomReason::TangentContinuationEndUnsupported`). The plate's corner then removes (1 - pi/4) r^2 (pi/2) (2 - c), Pappus' volume of the round's section over the quarter turn, within 1e-8, 11 faces, check clean. Test: `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2176_round_of_arc_between_tangent_straights.rs`.
