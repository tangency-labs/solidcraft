#### WR-664. `TrimRailCurves` can trim a rail within the zone tolerance of its own knot, which leaves an end span with coincident control points
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blend (fillet geometry)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`, `SmFilletGeom::TrimRailCurves`. The start vertex's parameter comes from `DropPoint` and the end vertex's from `GlobalPointSolve`, and the rail is trimmed at those parameters as they are.
- **What happens:** the rail's knots are its marching points, and one of them can lie within the zone tolerance of the parameter a vertex drops to. That happens where the rail stands still at a face's boundary past its end (any point of the stationary stretch drops the vertex equally well), or where the section's other rail crosses its side edge there. Trimming at that parameter leaves an end span shorter than the zone tolerance with coincident control points. That is an internal point singularity of the rail and of the face curves trimmed with it, and SMLib's body check refuses it (Test #15).
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft rig fillets/1207 r0.5, where the drop lands 1e-8 before the knot at which the rail starts to move, and an oblique cut's r0.1 round, where the two are 6e-7 mm apart. Both come from the fork's commit message. The bodies themselves are not available here.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@7d79866 (`sm_SnapRailEndToKnot`).
- **Confirmed:** no. No C++ run was made, and no input of our own reproduces the near-knot drop yet.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_rail_trim_at_near_knot`, `trim_rail_curves` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_geom/p5.rs`, `snap_rail_end_to_knot`) does what the fork does. Each end parameter moves to the rail's next knot inside the trim (above the start, below the end) when the rail between the two is no longer than the target B-rep's zone tolerance (length accuracy `0.01` of it). This happens before the vertex use's UV point is set and the curves are trimmed.
  Test: `blend::fillet_geom::p5::tests::rail_end_snaps_to_a_knot_within_the_zone_tolerance`. On a degree-1 rail with knots 0, 1, 2 and zone tolerance 1e-6, a start at `1 - 1e-8` and an end at `1 + 1e-8` trim at the knot 1. Values 1e-3 away, a knot on the wrong side, and a knot at the trim's other end leave the parameter as it is.
