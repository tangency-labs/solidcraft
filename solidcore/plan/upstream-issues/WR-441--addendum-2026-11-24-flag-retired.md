Updates: WR-441

#### WR-441 (update, 2026-11-24). `bspline_trim_preserves_uv_spans` is removed; a UV trim keeps its short end spans

`BSplineCurve::trim` (upstream `SmBSplineCurve::Trim`, through `trim_with_zone_tol`) now always returns the exact restriction `crv_trim(curve, lo, hi)` of a dimension-2 (UV) curve. Upstream's `N_CrvRemoveDegenSegs` runs only on dimension-3 model curves. It measured UV span lengths against the 3D zone tolerance and reparameterised the curve, so it could move a trim by up to that tolerance in surface-parameter units. The exception from the 2026-11-01 addendum stays: an end span that collapses to a pole, the way check test #15 measures one, is still cleaned (#2129). Removed: the `Kernel::fixes` field and its doc file, the copies of the flag into the edge-zone kernels (`edgeuse` p3, `fillet_solver` p1 and p2), and the flag lines in seven integration tests. `trim_with_zone_tol` loses its unused kernel argument. The #2129 sweep of 64 seam placements now runs with the default kernel and checks clean.

- **Goldens:** 7 rows move in 5 units. `bstuv_trim_rows` (goldens lib) replays each row and checks that it reproduces the blessed row. Each row's certificate lives next to its runner and uses rounded-interval bounds:
  - `blend.fillet_executive_p1` `remove_topo_edges_mouse_two_struts` (`bstuv_mouse_two_struts_trims_lie_on_their_edges`). Every UV trim of the result is certified on its edge within the edge's tolerance (`trims_on_edges::certify_edgeuse`). The joined edge's trim (edge use 23) keeps its 77 spans; its bound is `1.05e-2` against a tolerance of `3.67`.
  - `blend.fillet_solver_p1` `trim_rail_mouse_rebuild_edge7` (`bstuv_trim_rail_keeps_its_rail_on_the_edge`). The rail's UV trim (48 control points) is certified on the trimmed rail over its whole interval and over each end span (`curve_surface_gap::trim_image_gap_bound`): `6.33e-4` against the edge's `1.26e-3`.
  - `offset.offset_geometry_creation` `gen0074` and `gen0400` (`bstuv_offset_moved_trims_lie_on_their_edges`). The moved trims, edge use 15 and edge use 23, are certified on their edges in each of the three written bodies: `2.56e-6` against `2e-5`, and `5.44e-4` against `4.36e-3`.
  - `surface.surface_p1` `sil0048` and `sil_adv_20_torus_f0` (`bstuv_silhouette_kept_spans_lie_on_their_curves`). The end spans of each moved UV curve are certified on its 3D silhouette curve within the trace tolerance, for example sil0048 curve 4's kept head span: `1.42e-6` against `8.23e-6`.
  - `surface.surface_p2` `ssi_adv_ssi_clasic_ruled_f0_f0` (`bstuv_intersection_kept_spans_lie_on_their_curves`). The end spans of curves 1 and 3 are certified on both surfaces, each bound below `5e-9` against `1e-3`.
- **The mouse row was wrong with upstream's cleanup.** It cut the joined edge's trim to 7 spans. The certificate did not hold: the bound was `17.5`, and points sampled on the trim lay `7.8` off the edge, past the `3.67` tolerance. This is measured, recorded when the flag was retired. The other rows held either way. Their kept spans are certified above, and the rest of each curve is the restriction upstream also built before its cleanup.
- **Two offset trims fail in both versions.** In `gen0074`, body 2's edge use 10 has a bound of `6.4` against `2e-5`. In `gen0400`, edge 2's edge use 6 has a bound of `7.4` against `2e-5`. Both fail identically with and without the cleanup, so this change does not cause them. They are recorded here only.
