#### WR-661 (update, 2026-11-17). `fillet_statuses_pass_on` is removed

The fillet executive, solvers, corners and geometry now always return the statuses SMLib drops and the fork passes on (tangency-labs/smlib 873c7c9, 66b02de, 9b7cd48). Propagation is the only path, and the flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, group G1): the field, every ignore branch at the sites (`passed_on` with the flag, and the helpers left unused), `TemporaryRadiusChange`'s `*_with_fixes` variants, SolidCraft's preset line, its `fixes.json` entry and every test setting. The pass-on unit tests keep their pass-on half.

One site keeps a result (not upstream, which ignores this status for every body): `finish_merged_fillet`'s `OrientTrimmedSurfaces`. When the stitched result still has lamina edges, the closed-solid orientation's "gathered faceuses from both sides of some face" assert (`SmBrep.cpp`:24796, ported as `topology::brep::p3::GATHERED_BOTH_SIDES`) fires on an open sheet that is orientable: walking a face use's neighbours crosses the sheet's boundary edges to its other side. The check fails before any face is flipped, and `StitchAndOrient` unifies an open body's normals afterwards. Every other orientation failure, and this one on a body without lamina edges, is passed on.
