Updates: WR-56

#### WR-56 (update, 2026-10-31). The fork's fix also tightens the foot test; ported under the same flag
- **Fork change:** tangency-labs/smlib@eaa998a (the same content as 0b4de8c, fork PR tangency-labs/smlib#70), "surface: a dropped point's foot from a guess is one the local solve converged to", is one commit with two changes to `sm_GoodDropResult`: the distance swap already ported (`WR-56--addendum-2026-10-31-opt-in-flag.md`), and a foot test at the local solve's own tight tolerance, `dTightTol = SM_EFF_ZERO * (1 + crPtToDrop.GetMaxDimension())` (`LocalPointSolve_1`'s), in place of upstream's `|Fu| + |Fv| < 0.0001` and the interior `|Fu| > 0.001`, `|Fv| > 0.001` refusals. Against upstream 3fd5b95 it replaces the fork's interim face-tolerance test (737c392, never ported), so the net change is upstream's constants to `dTightTol`.
- **Why it matters:** with only the swap, a local result that stalled short of the foot (inside upstream's 1e-4) is still taken, and the swap now accepts it more often (a result nearer than its guess). SolidCraft's case "fillet an edge tangent at one end into a cylinder" (step 14) is refused with the swap alone (Bad Gap, SmBrep #4, 4.7853e-4 > tol 3.3753e-4) and builds with eaa998a (10 faces, V 9399.383). That case is SolidCraft's and not reproduced here.
- **Rust port:** `Kernel::fixes.good_drop_result_distances_in_order` now ports both halves in `surface::surface::p7::good_drop_result`: they are one fork change, and SolidCraft holds the flag off until both are in. Off, the function is unchanged (upstream's 1e-4 / 1e-3).
- **Tests:** `surface::surface::p7::tests::wr_56_good_drop_result_takes_only_a_tightly_converged_foot` (a plane, an interior result with the point on the plane 1e-13, 1e-6, 5e-5, 5e-4 and 2e-3 off along `S_u`: off, all but 2e-3 are taken; on, only 1e-13). `wr_56_good_drop_result_compares_the_right_distances` now expects the opt-in to refuse its 5e-4-off interior result, and `issue_1852_good_drop_compares_the_actual_guess_distance` puts its result on the domain's `u = 0` side, where the tight test lets the distance comparison decide.
- **Difftest:** no change on the default path.
