Updates: WR-669

#### WR-669 (update, 2026-11-24). `fillet_exact_round_classes` is removed

Whether a constant round is exact is now always decided by one classification of its spine from its faces' natural quadrics (line, circle about a common axis, point, conic), at `CalcFilletGeom` and at `ReCalcFilletGeom`. Upstream's try-and-fall-through chain (`CanMakeAnalytic`, then the cylinder, torus and sphere builders, each gated on the edge's shape) and the cone/torus branch of `ReCalcFilletGeom` are deleted. One root fix came with the retirement: the class is read at one station, and a trace run on past a support's edge (WR-610) can end off that class's offset quadric (issue 880's one-half chamfer, an end 5.64 from the axis against an offset cylinder of 3.556). The exact build now requires both trace-end ball centres on the class's offset quadrics within the body's tolerance, and otherwise traces the round; a support's quadric is read with the uv clamped into its natural domain. A second root fix: the circle spine's start and end sections are turned about the class axis into the half-plane through their ball's centre. The solver's rail points stood a hair either side of it (2e-6 degrees on `box_with_large_fillet.smb` edge 4 r 0.1), so the revolved arc was a twisted generator and the round face failed `SmSurfOfRevolution` Test #10; both faces turn about that axis, so the turned points stay on their quadrics. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::fillet_exact_round_classes`, the preset line, its `fixes` entries and the try-chain (`c3_try_analytic`), with the WR-420 sphere fallback inside it (a point spine is a class of the one classification).

- **Goldens:** the four flags are retired together (#18913), so their moved rows are proved together: 177 rows in six units (`blend.fillet_executive_p3` 138, `topology.brep_cutting` 19, `primitive.api_trim_ops` 9, `blend.fillet_solver_p2` 6, `topology.api_projection` 3, `topology.api_files` 2). Each is checked by `crates/goldens/tests/issue_18913_moved_rows_are_proven.rs`, which replays it and certifies the result: a Level 2 check of the body, every analytic face and every line or conic edge on its closed-form support, and the mass properties against a certified enclosure or a closed form. All other units pass unchanged.
- **Defaults:** SolidCraft's preset already had the flag on, so the shipped result does not change. The comparison SMLib keeps upstream's path.
- **Tests:** in `issue_880_half_arc_rim_chamfer.rs`, `issue_18913_one_half_chamfers_at_other_offsets` (fails without the trace-end check, and without the domain clamp) and `issue_18913_other_half_chamfers_at_equal_setbacks` (the goldens' `issue-880-half-arc-rim-1-setbacks-1-1`; fails without the domain clamp with `QuadricReadbackMismatch`); `fork_099cf96_exact_class_cases`; `issue_18913_round_section_is_a_meridian` (`box_with_large_fillet.smb` edge 4 r 0.1; fails without the half-plane turn). No primitive input was found that fails without the turn: a cylinder's rim and boxes with a vertical edge rounded 2 to 9 and their top arc rounded 0.1 to 1 pass Level 2 either way, so the test reads the upstream file and runs where the upstream tree is present.
- **Refusals that changed:** four rows that main refused now build a body, each certified as above with its mass inside a certified enclosure: `issue-880-half-arc-rim-1-setbacks-1-1` and the `stubs4-delete-edge-sphere_diff_3cylinders` rows `e2-r0.1`, `e5-r0.1` and `e5-r0.5`.
