#### WR-751. `MergeIntoObject` splits an edge at the classification's parameter, not where the edge passes the classified point
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Booleans
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPointClass.cpp:951-955`, `SmPointClassification::MergeIntoObject` (the `SmEdge_TYPE` branch).
- **What happens:** a point classified to an edge is merged into it by `MakeVertexSplitEdge(pE, GetTParam(), ...)`, on the assumption the code states, `// note: sSrcPoint expected to = Edge->Evaluate(TParam)`. The point merged is the vertex definition's, the consensus of every intersection that met there; `TParam` is that of the one intersection that classified it to this edge. Where several surfaces meet at a grazing angle they fix the point only to an edge's accuracy over the angle's sine, so the two differ: the edge is split away from the point, the vertex then moves to cover both, and the edge ends that far from its vertex, past half the vertex's tolerance. The check refuses the result (`SmBrep` tests #0 and #2, `SmLoopuse` test #5).
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcore#2621: SolidCraft's M16 thread groove, trimmed at the plane z = 21 where a fillet's top circle lies, cut from the filleted boss. The groove flank meets that circle at about 5 degrees. The body's circle is split where it meets the flank, the tool's end-face/flank edge where it meets the circle, at its own parameter 2.93e-5 further along; the merged vertex is the body's, so the tool edge ends 2.9317e-5 from it (half-tolerance 2.29e-5), while its curve passes 2.7e-6 from the vertex. With the groove turned through 32 phases on the same body, 30 are refused (gaps 2.15e-5 to 3.06e-5), whether the vertex lies inside the tool edge's span or past its end.
- **How found:** SolidCraft rig (#2621), traced in the Rust port, which ports `MergeIntoObject` unchanged; the C++ source does the same.
- **Confirmed:** yes, by reading the pinned source (`SmPointClass.cpp:951`, its stated assumption) and on the port, which follows it line for line: splitting where the edge's curve passes nearest the classified point (`intersect::point_class::split_parameter`) passes all 32 phases and the M10, M12 and M20 grooves with their closed-form volumes (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2621_m16_groove.rs`). The split moves only for a point within the edge's tolerance of its curve: a point classified to an edge it is not on (an offset vertex 6.3e-2 from the edge, at its interval's end) keeps `TParam`; moved, it left a 1.2e-3 sliver and the face build failed with status 1001 (`fork_2e1bb8c_four_face_vertex_split`). The C++ build was not run on the #2621 operands. Every `boolean.*` difftest case still agrees with the C++ within 1e-9; one moves within it (`torus_guard_huge_scale_box_intersection`, 5.2e-15 relative: a split point 1.2e-10 off the classified point moves onto it). Integration pins that move, each to a nearer split: `wr490_equal_rods_and_tubes` seam 35 becomes a manifold solid within 1.2e-8 of its closed form (it was a body of volume -4592.38); the measured volume in `issue_1480_pleated_loft_union_edge_ends` moves 4.0e-9 relative; the flag-off control of `wr579_near_coincident_tube_ends_join` at gap 1e-5 loses one `SmBrep` gap pair and gains a second `SmEllipse` pair, WR-575's defect, which its flag clears.
