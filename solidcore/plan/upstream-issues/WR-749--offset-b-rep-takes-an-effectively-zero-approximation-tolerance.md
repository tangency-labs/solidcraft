#### WR-749. The solid and extended offsets set an effectively zero approximation tolerance as the offset B-rep's tolerance, and build a body that fails its own validity check
- **Severity:** Wrong result (an invalid B-rep: `SmBrep::ValidatePointers` test 0, "Tolerance less than effective zero"; or the crash UB-291)
- **Area:** Offsets and shells
- **Location:** at the pin `3fd5b95`: `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp:428` (`DoSolidOffset`) and `:1115` (`DoExtendedOffset`), `m_pOffsetBrep->SetTolerance(m_rGeometryCreation.GetThisApproxTol3d())`. The `SmZoneTol3d` argument takes `fabs` (`source/SMLib/inc/SmTol.h:147`-`:148`, `:167`-`:168`). So `SmBrep::SetTolerance`'s automatic tolerance for a nonpositive value (`SmBrep.cpp:26748`) only catches ±0. Any other value is stored as given, however small.
- **What happens:** the offset B-rep, and the result built in it, gets the approximation tolerance's magnitude as its tolerance. A nonzero magnitude at or below `SM_EFF_ZERO` (1e-12) is a tolerance no B-rep can hold: `ValidatePointers` requires `tolerance > SM_EFF_ZERO`, and double arithmetic leaves edges and vertices farther than that from their faces (7.7e-15 on the sphere of fuzz00167). On a cylinder (fuzz00087) the offset returned such a body. On a sphere (fuzz00167) the region search fails at that tolerance and the extended offset crashes (UB-291).
- **Trigger:** a solid or extended offset whose approximation tolerance is nonzero with magnitude ≤ 1e-12. Two ways in:
  - A member call with such a tolerance, e.g. -1e-300. `offset.offset_executive_p2` `fuzz00167` (a sphere; the C++ crashes, UB-291) and `fuzz00087` (a cylinder inset by 0.5 at B-rep tolerance 1e300; on main before #2737 the C++ result's tolerance was 1e-300, `values.result.geometry[0]`; since #2737 that case is refused first for its distance).
  - The static `OffsetBrep` and `ShellBrep`, which pass the source's own tolerance as the approximation tolerance (`:116`-`:118`, `:208`-`:220`). Cases `tiny_tolerance_sheet_exact_boundary` and `tiny_tolerance_sheet_above_boundary`: static shells of a plane sheet whose tolerance is 1e-15, by 2e-15 and just above. gdb shows `SetTolerance(1e-15)` on the offset B-rep, and the C++ returns a result. Here the source is already below `SM_EFF_ZERO`, and the result keeps that.
- **How found:** generated difftest cases (nightly fuzz00087 of 2026-10-31, fuzz00167 of 2026-11-04). The port's result for fuzz00167 at tolerance 1e-300 (before #2393) failed `check_brep` level 2 with test 0 and the edge/vertex tolerance tests 59 and 82 (gap 7.7e-15).
- **Confirmed:** yes. gdb on the release harness (2026-11-04): `fuzz00167` sets 1e-300 as the offset B-rep's tolerance (`m_sThisZoneTol3d` at `FindInfiniteRegion`), and `tiny_tolerance_sheet_exact_boundary` sets 1e-15 and returns a result.
- **Rust port:** since #2393 `OffsetExecutive::check_resolvable` (`offset/offset_executive/p2.rs`, called by the static offset, the solid offset and the extended offset before anything is built, after #2737's distance check) refuses a nonzero approximation tolerance at or below `EFF_ZERO` with `OffsetFailure::ToleranceUnresolved { tolerance }`, kind `InvalidInput`, naming the value. An exact zero still takes upstream's automatic tolerance. `tiny_tolerance_sheet_exact_boundary` and `tiny_tolerance_sheet_above_boundary` are parked with `cpp_defect = "WR-749"`, with ratchet waivers (both were passes). `tiny_tolerance_sheet_below_boundary` still passes: upstream refuses it before the offset (`SM_ERR_INVALID_INPUT`, distance below twice the tolerance). Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2393_extended_offset_tiny_approx_tol.rs`, and `offset_negative_approximation_tolerance.rs` (fuzz00087 with a resolvable distance).
