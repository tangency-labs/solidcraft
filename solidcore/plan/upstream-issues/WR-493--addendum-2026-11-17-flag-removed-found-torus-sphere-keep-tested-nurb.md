Updates: WR-493

#### WR-493 (update, 2026-11-17). `found_torus_sphere_keep_tested_nurb` is removed

A torus or sphere found in a NURBS now always keeps a copy of the tested NURB (`Torus::is_nurb_surface_torus`, `Sphere::is_nurb_surface_sphere`). The sphere's half-meridian test keeps upstream's `EFF_ZERO` plus the angles' rounding at the points' size (#15122). The fork's `360 * EFF_ZERO` is not taken: `tests/far_sphere_recognised.rs` holds the allowance to that rounding and no more. The polar-curve sweep interval of the 2026-10-31 addendum applies to every found sphere. The fix is now unconditional and the flag is gone. Removed: `kernel/fixes/found_torus_sphere_keep_tested_nurb.md`, the file the field `UpstreamFixes::found_torus_sphere_keep_tested_nurb` was generated from (so the field is gone), and every test setting of it.

- **Results:** goldens of the files whose spheres and tori are found while reading move: `corpus.parity`, `topology.*` on `sphere.smb`, `surf_sphere150.smb` and `Fillet/sphere_plane.smb`, `topology.api_files` writes, and fillets on `Fillet/box_w_slot.smb`, `Fillet/sphere_plane.smb` and `Fillet/sphere_diff_3cylinders.smb`. The read faces now carry the file's own NURB and keep the file's UV trims. Before, the trims were dropped and rebuilt on the refit surface with a grown tolerance. That rebuild hid an edge whose tolerance the healer set under its edge/face gap, and file trims that run off their edge between their ends (the read's end check, `sm_CheckUVTrimCurve`, compares only a trim's ends). Both are fixed separately, before this change: healed edge tolerances cover their gap, and the read raises the tolerance of an edge whose kept trim runs farther from it to a proved bound on that distance (`cover_kept_uv_trim_curves`, `topology/brep/p2.rs`).
- **Tests:** `tests/wr493_class_proofs.rs` checks four things on the default kernel:
  - every found surface is the file's NURB bit for bit, and the file's trims stay on it (the healer still rebuilds a trim that misses its edge, as on any surface);
  - the `ss_plane_sph` areas lie in the sphere face's certified area enclosure; they are closer than the earlier rows to the area the face's edge loop bounds (ARGUED: that reference is a quadrature, not an enclosure);
  - `Fillet/sphere_plane.smb` is closer to its closed-form area;
  - a point 1e-7 outside the sphere's pole classifies into the infinite region.

  `tests/wr493_corner_sphere_polar_domain.rs` checks the corner sphere's volume in closed form. The `surface.torus` and `surface.sphere` unit tests (`issue_1433_*`, `wr493_*`) run on the default kernel.
- **Farther:** the change is not closer everywhere. These moved results are farther from their references, each far inside its tolerance:
  - `blend.fillet_executive_p3` `stubs4-delete-edge-sphere_diff_3cylinders-e1-r0.5`: the round edge 37 ends 9.85e-7 from its end vertex where it ended 2.11e-8 from it (ARGUED: float evaluations with a margin above 1e5; tolerance 4e-5). Its mirror case, `-e4-r0.5`, moves the other way by the same amount (`goldens/src/wr493_rows.rs`), and the two cases swap their level 2 lines likewise (two `Test #15` lines on the round's B-spline curve, on `e1` on main and on `e4` here). The pair's end parameter takes one of the same two values in both builds (70.64830970 or 70.64830874); the retirement swaps which case gets which.
- **Level 2:** `goldens/src/wr493_rows.rs`'s `wr493_moved_bodies_pass_level_2` checks every upstream test file whose read finds a sphere (both read modes) and every moved fillet result: none reports a level 2 line beyond main's build, counted with repeats, but for that mirror pair, which is asserted to swap exactly.
  - `Fillet/sphere_plane.smb`: the file's trim of edge uses 8/9 strays farther from its edge at equal parameters than the trim rebuilt on the fitted NURB (same-parameter metric; ARGUED ranking margin; the read's kept-trim cover encloses its distance from the edge and raises the edge's tolerance to it, `goldens/src/refit/replay/trim_certificate.rs`).
