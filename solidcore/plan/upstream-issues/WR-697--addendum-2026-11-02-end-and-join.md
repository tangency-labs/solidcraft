Updates: WR-697

#### WR-697 (update, 2026-11-02). A blend's end at a vertex and the Join of two blends continuing each other are built by separate corner classes, and a square bar rounded on all four long edges loses a round
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Fillets (corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletCorner::Create` (a vertex where one blended edge ends builds `SmFilletNx1Corner`, `SmFilletOpenCorner` or `SmFilletDegenerateCorner`; two blended edges continuing each other alone at the vertex build `SmFillet2x2Corner`; one closed blended edge meeting itself builds `SmFillet1x1Corner`); `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`, `SmFilletVertex::CalcVertOnCrossSection` (the shared section is solved for the first blend only and copied to the second), `SmFilletVertex::CalcRailIntExtendedEdgeuse` (a mixed-convexity end's rail end is the end of the blend's intersection with the end face's surface, marched from the other rail's end) and `SmFilletGeom::TrimOriginalSurfaces` (the faces to keep are collected from the rail edge's primary edge use).
- **What happens:**
  - A 20 x 4 x 4 bar with its four long edges rounded r 2 in one call should be the rod of radius 2, 80 pi, 6 faces. It comes out 268.495559215389 with 7 faces, one round dropped. Each side face is used up by the two rounds beside it, so each rail is one edge of the fillet B-rep between two rounds' faces. The faces to keep are collected from that edge's primary edge use, which for one round is its neighbour's on both rails. That round's own face is never collected, and the merge deletes it.
  - At a mixed-convexity end the rail's end is the end point of a marched intersection. It is not the rail's own crossing with the end face's surface.
  - At a Join the section is solved for the first blend only, and the second blend's rail ends are copied from it.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's smlib#79 step 4d (tangency-labs/smlib 794946e, 3e26f76, 68e039c, 543d25a, 53c47b8; tests 4a58f3d, 34b220e, 565bcae). In the fork every vertex where one blended edge ends is the blend corner's End (`SmBlendCornerEnd.cpp`). Its three kinds are the three old classes' constructions, moved unchanged: runs out (`EndRunsOut`, the old degeneracy test), open (a lamina edge) and on the end face. Every G1 continuation of blends is the Join (`SmBlendCornerJoin.cpp`): one shared section, solved for each blend on its own faces in the plane through the vertex square to the first blend's edge (`SM_FV_JOIN_SECTION`, `CalcJoinSection`). Where one edge stands between the blends on a side, the section ends where the rails cross that edge. More than one edge is refused (`SM_FILERR_CORNER_UNSUPPORTED`). A mixed-convexity End's rail ends at the rail's crossing with the end face's surface, nearest the vertex (`CalcRailIntEndSurface`). `TrimOriginalSurfaces` takes the rail edge's edge use on the fillet's own face.
- **Rust port:** under the same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner`, with no new flag. An End is a corner of the old kind (`NByOne`, `Open` or `Degenerate`) whose `blend` is `BlendCornerEvent::End` with its `BlendEnd`. A Join is an `NByTwo` corner whose `blend` is `BlendCornerEvent::Join`, with one blended edge use (`FilletCorner::is_closed_join`) or two. The blend corner's type is `SM_FCR_BLEND`, which is `FilletCornerType::NByTwo` here, as 74e9beb made every test of `SM_FCR_N_x_2` one of `SM_FCR_BLEND`. So the tests the fork keys on `SM_FCR_BLEND` reach the End and the Join as they do there. The topology is still dispatched by the corner's construction. The tests the fork rewrote (`IsEndOnEndFace`, `IsClosedJoin`) are ported where pinned SMLib tests `SM_FCR_N_x_1` and `SM_FCR_1_x_1`. `SM_FV_JOIN_SECTION` has the code 14 here, after the pinned `SM_FV_UNKNOWN`.

The rail ends at (sqrt 396, 0, 8). Volume flag on 21407.602511807883, off 21407.602511915738. The closed form, the spandrel swept from the cylinder to x = 40, is 21407.602507916134.
