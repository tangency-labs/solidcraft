#### WR-380 (update, 2026-11-18). `geometric_g2_continuity` is removed; G2 is always geometric

`smgu_EvaluateCurveContinuity`'s G2 test is now only the geometric one: the two sides' Frenet curvature vectors, compared by length relative to the larger (`SM_EFF_ZERO_SQRT / 10 * (1 + max)`) and by the angle between their normals. Removed: the field, `ContinuityOptions`, `evaluate_curve_continuity_with` and the parametric path, and every test setting or skipping the flag. SolidCraft already ran with the flag on, so its results do not change; `Kernel::default` and its goldens do.

One defect of the opt-in test is fixed with it:
- **Curvatures within the gap of zero.** It compared the normals whenever both curvatures were above `SM_IS_ZERO`, so a straight join whose second derivatives are rounding noise (curvatures 1e-12 to 1e-9 pointing opposite ways, `nurbs.b_spline_curve_p1`'s curve0) was only G1. The normals are now compared only where both curvatures exceed the test's own length gap; otherwise the two curvature vectors must lie within that gap of each other.

`SmApiCreateSurfaceRevolution` (`create_surface_revolution`) sweeps its profile whole: upstream's `test_continuity` split the profile at every join that is not C1 and G2 and the API returned the first piece's surface, which the geometric test made visible on two arcs bending opposite ways (`sweep.api_surfaces` adv_rev_*).

- **What assumed parametric G2:** a capped skin splits its side surfaces into faces at the knots that are at most G1 (`create_faces_from_surface`, `topology/brep/p8.rs`). The issue_948 sections are curvature continuous at their knots, but their parameter speed jumps by up to 1.3e-5 relative there; the parametric test called hundreds of those knots G1 and split the skins into 342 and 274 faces, where the geometric test leaves 7 and 6. On those faces the difference needs the nearest-point half of WR-647 that stays behind `simple_coincidence_crossing_is_point`, which the test now turns on; without it the Boolean fails in `topology/brep/p6.rs`, as the pinned C++ does.
- **Results:** 68 goldens rows move in 7 units, each through a continuity verdict. Straight joins (curvatures 1e-20 to 1e-10) whose second derivatives differ along the tangent are G2, where they were G1 or C1: `curve.curve_cache` 4, `surface.surface_cache_p3` 5, `surface.surface_p1` 1, `surface.surface_p6` 14. Joins whose curvatures differ (20) or whose normals are mirrored (20) are not G2, where they were G1G2, C1G2, C1G2G3 or C1C2: `geom.geom_utility_p2` 40 of its 41 (one, gen1499, goes from C1 to C1G2: equal curvatures and normals), `topology.brep_p8` 1, and `primitive.primitive_creation_p2` 2, whose hostile partial torus (a circle of radius 1e-6 at 1e4 from the origin) is refused with SM_ERR (1001) instead of 1006.
- **Tests:** `geom::geom_utility::p2` (`issue_933_geometric_g2_continuity`, `continuity_levels`), `sweep::api_surfaces` (`revolution_and_skin_primitive_validate_and_construct`), `tests/issue_933_geometric_g2.rs`, `tests/issue_948_ruled_skin_difference.rs`.
