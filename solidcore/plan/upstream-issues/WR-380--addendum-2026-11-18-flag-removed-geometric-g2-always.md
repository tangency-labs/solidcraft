#### WR-380 (update, 2026-11-18). `geometric_g2_continuity` is removed; G2 is always geometric

`smgu_EvaluateCurveContinuity`'s G2 test is now only the geometric one: the two sides' Frenet curvature vectors, compared by length relative to the larger (`SM_EFF_ZERO_SQRT / 10 * (1 + max)`) and by the angle between their normals. Removed: the field, `ContinuityOptions`, `evaluate_curve_continuity_with` and the parametric path, and every test setting or skipping the flag. SolidCraft already ran with the flag on, so its results do not change; `Kernel::default` and its goldens do.

Two defects of the opt-in test are fixed with it:
- **Normals of zero curvatures.** It compared the normals whenever both curvatures were above `SM_IS_ZERO`, so a straight join whose second derivatives are rounding noise (curvatures 1e-12 to 1e-9 pointing opposite ways, `nurbs.b_spline_curve_p1`'s curve0) was only G1. The normals are now compared only where both curvatures exceed the test's own length gap.
- **C2 without G2.** The curvature gap (1e-7 relative) is tighter than the derivative gaps of C1 and C2 (1e-6 relative), and the curvature moves with the first derivative: a join with identical second derivatives of length 607, mostly tangential, and first derivatives 4e-8 apart passed C1 and C2 but missed the curvature gap and came out C1 (`geom.geom_utility_p2` gen0721). A C1 join within the C2 gap is now C2, and so G2, without the curvature test. Upstream tests C2 only after G2.

- **What assumed parametric G2:** a capped skin splits its side surfaces into faces at the knots that are at most G1 (`create_faces_from_surface`, `topology/brep/p8.rs`). The issue_948 sections are curvature continuous at their knots, but their parameter speed jumps by up to 1.3e-5 relative there; the parametric test called hundreds of those knots G1 and split the skins into 342 and 274 faces, where the geometric test leaves 7 and 6. On those faces the difference needs WR-647's crossing (`simple_coincidence_crossing_is_point`), which the test now turns on; without it the Boolean fails in `topology/brep/p6.rs`, as the pinned C++ does.
- **Results:** 69 goldens rows move in 8 units, each through a continuity verdict. Straight joins (curvatures 1e-20 to 1e-10) whose second derivatives differ along the tangent are G2, where they were G1 or C1: `curve.curve_cache` 4, `surface.surface_cache_p3` 5, `surface.surface_p1` 1, `surface.surface_p6` 14. Joins whose curvatures differ (19) or whose normals are mirrored (20) are not G2, where they were G1G2, C1G2 or C1G2G3: `geom.geom_utility_p2` 39 of its 40 (one, gen1499, goes from C1 to C1G2: equal curvatures and normals), `sweep.api_surfaces` 2 (a revolved profile is split at its inflection, so its domain halves), `topology.brep_p8` 1, and `primitive.primitive_creation_p2` 2, whose hostile partial torus (a circle of radius 1e-6 at 1e4 from the origin) is refused with SM_ERR (1001) instead of 1006.
- **Tests:** `geom::geom_utility::p2` (`issue_933_geometric_g2_continuity`, `continuity_levels`), `tests/issue_933_geometric_g2.rs`, `tests/issue_948_ruled_skin_difference.rs`.
