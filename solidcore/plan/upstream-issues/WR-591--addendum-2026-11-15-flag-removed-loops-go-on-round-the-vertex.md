Updates: WR-591

#### WR-591 (update, 2026-11-15). `loops_go_on_round_the_vertex` is removed; `OrderCurvesIntoLoops` always goes on round a shared vertex

`order_curves_into_loops` (`topology/trimming_tools.rs`) always computes the call's turn normal (`loops_turn_normal`) and goes on from the end next round the vertex (`next_round_the_vertex`). Removed: the field, SolidCraft's preset line, the goldens `enable_fix` arm, the rig's `fixes.json` entry, the flag-list test entry, and the tests' flag-off halves (`wr_591_off_the_input_order_decides`, `wr591_without_the_flag_the_capped_sweep_returns_an_open_skin`).

- **Defaults:** SolidCraft's preset had the flag on, so the app's results don't change. `Kernel::default()` changes: loops touching at a vertex close in every input order.
- **A collapsed curve runs forwards (tangency-labs/solidcraft#17033).** Retiring the flag first moved `primitive.primitive_creation_p2` `ub215_degenerate_face_top_line_*` (6 rows): a partial cone whose top line (`1e-9` to `9.9e-7` long) is shorter than the matching tolerance, so its two ends meet the side's and the axis's at the top vertex. `next_round_the_vertex` offered the top line's end as a leaving ray too; it came first counterclockwise, so the loop ran the top line backwards (orients `[Same, Opposite, Opposite, Same]`), and the sweep then refused the cone as axis inside face (1025), which is false. A curve collapsed to the vertex (it has a certified bounding box, `Curve::certified_bounding_box`, and the sphere enclosing that box lies within `tol_3d` of it; a curve without a certified box is not collapsed) now offers only its start, so it is run forwards (`[Same, Same, Opposite, Same]`, as upstream) and the cone is refused as upstream refuses it (1007, the degenerate swept face, UB-215). A closed curve through the vertex also has both ends there but is not collapsed, so both its ends stay candidates. No golden row moves. `issue_17033_collapsed_curve_at_the_vertex_runs_forwards` (`topology/trimming_tools.rs`) pins the orients and the 1007. `closed_curve_at_the_vertex_keeps_the_turn_order` pins a closed triangle touching a square's corner from inside: the triangle's backward ray comes next counterclockwise, so in all 96 input orders not starting with the triangle the result is one loop of five with the triangle run backwards. That is the same result as without the guard; a guard on the end points alone ran the triangle forwards.
- **Tests:** `wr_591_loops_touching_at_a_vertex.rs` and `wr591_touching_loops_sweep_any_order.rs` run on the default kernel; `wr_591_loops_apart_close_apart` checks two loops touching nowhere against their closed-form areas 60 and 2. `surface::surface::p2` `issue_892_touching_hole_is_the_plate_less_the_hole` (was `issue_892_only_join_touching_loops_changes_the_sweep`): with `join_touching_loops` off the WR-405 record's plate was 12 faces and volume 122, the touching hole's prism taken as material; going round the shared vertex already orders the eight lines into one loop, so both settings give 10 faces and `(60 - 1) * 2 = 118`, Level 2 clean.
