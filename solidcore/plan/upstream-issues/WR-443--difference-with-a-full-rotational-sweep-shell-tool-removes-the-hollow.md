#### WR-443. A difference with a hollow shell made by a full rotational sweep removes what lies in the hollow
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Booleans
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmMerge.cpp`, `SmMerge::ManifoldBoolean` (def. `:2510`): the tool's region on each side of a face, and the region `Point3DClassify` gives an unmarked face (`:3755`-`:3815`, `SM_BO_DIFFERENCE` deletes a face whose region is not a void). Reached from `SmApiBooleanDifference` (`source/SM_API/src/SmApiBrep.cpp:317`). The cause is the tool: WR-442's revolve result, where the inner sphere bounds a solid ball of its own instead of a void.
- **What happens:** the tool is the shell r 18.5 to 40 that WR-442's trigger builds (three regions: the infinite region and two solid balls, r 40 and r 18.5). The difference reads the inner ball as material, so it removes the part of the block inside r 18.5 and keeps the part between the spheres, the reverse of a hollow:
  - the 8 mm block centred on the origin, wholly inside the hollow, never meets the tool. `Point3DClassify` puts its faces in the r 18.5 ball's solid region and the difference deletes them all. The result has no shell and volume 0, not the block's 512.
  - the 12 x 8 x 8 block from x = 8 to 20, crossing the inner sphere: the inner sphere face has the infinite region on its outer side and the r 18.5 ball on its inner side. The block's part inside r 18.5 is deleted and the part outside is kept. Volume 114.6570903332565, which is 768 - 653.343 (the complement of the right 653.3429).
  - the block from x = 8 to 50, crossing both spheres: the result is the block minus the r 40 ball, one lump of 648.5533527329339. The piece in the hollow (653.343) is lost, so the total should be 1301.896 in two lumps.
- **Trigger:** `SmApiBooleanDifference(box, shell)` with `SmApiCreateBox((-4,-4,-4), 8, 8, 8)`, `SmApiCreateBox((8,-4,-4), 12, 8, 8)` or `SmApiCreateBox((8,-4,-4), 42, 8, 8)`, and the shell from WR-442's trigger (`SmApiCreateRotationalSweep` of the half-ring profile about the X axis, 360 degrees, capped). SolidCraft cases `451-hollow-shell-cut/block_inside_hollow_cut`, `block_crossing_inner_sphere_cut` and `block_crossing_both_spheres_cut` (`fixtures/solidcraft/451-hollow-shell-cut.json`).
- **How found:** SolidCraft case 451 (solidcore's SolidCraft runner), then an `SM_API` program against the pinned reference build that logs each result's regions, void flags, lump count and `SmApiBrepComputeVolume` (relative accuracy `1e-8`).
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the pinned `c9c5979` reference build (x86-64 Linux, g++, `-ffp-contract=off`): volumes 0 (no shell), 114.6570903332565 (1 lump) and 648.5533527329339 (1 lump). Control: the same three differences with the shell built as `SmApiBooleanDifference(sphere 40, sphere 18.5)` (a void region inside the solid) give 512 (1 lump), 653.3429080270938 (1 lump) and 1301.896261752286 (2 lumps), which are right.
- **Rust port:** the port gives the same three results to every printed digit (0, 114.65709033325655 and 648.553352732934, with the same lump counts) and gives 653.3429080270938 for the sphere-difference control. The booleans follow upstream. A fix belongs in the revolve (WR-442), so that the tool has a void region and is not two balls.
