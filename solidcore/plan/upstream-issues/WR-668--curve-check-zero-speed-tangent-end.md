#### WR-668. `SmBSplineCurve::AssertValid` reports a zero-speed tangent end as coincident control points and a vanishing radius of curvature
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a valid curve refused: test 3, and tests 13/14 where the radius is read)
- **Area:** Validity checks
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmBSplineCurve.cpp`, `SmBSplineCurve::AssertValid`: test 3 (the first two control points coincide) and tests 13/14 (a 2D or 3D radius of curvature below the tolerance).
- **What happens:** Where two faces touch at a vertex (a G2 blend's tangent end on a face tangent to its own), the curve where they meet leaves the vertex with curvature growing as `s^(-1/2)` in its length `s`. No regular parametrization has that. The curve is a B-spline in `t ~ sqrt(s)`, with zero speed at the vertex: its first two control points coincide and its second derivative runs along its tangent there. The check reports the coincident control points (test 3) and the radius, which vanishes as `|t - t_end|` (tests 13/14).
- **Trigger:** `C(t) = (t^2, t^3, 0)` on `[0, 1]` (`y = x^(3/2)`), the Bezier `(0,0,0) (0,0,0) (1/3,0,0) (1,1,0)` with a knot at `5e-5`, as the curve of an edge whose faces are tangent at `t = 0` (tangency-labs/solidcore#2082).
- **How found:** the fork's tangency-labs/smlib ecf1963 ("check: a curve's zero-speed end at a vertex where its edge's faces touch is no kink"), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run was made here. The Rust port follows the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`): test 3 fires on the trigger, and so does test 14 with `bspline_check_min_radius` (WR-382).
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.curve_check_zero_speed_tangent_end` an end is a zero-speed tangent end when all of these hold (`is_zero_speed_tangent_end`): its end control points coincide to the test's scaled zero; its second derivative is longer than that; the curve's end parameter is the edge's interval end within `SM_EFF_ZERO (1 + length)`; and at least two faces of the edge's uses have their normals parallel there, within `SM_CONTINUITY_ANGLE` of the first, at their UV trims' points. At such an end test 3 passes over the coincident first control points. Tests 13/14 pass over a radius solution below the tolerance within `tol / rate` of the end, where `rate = 2 |C''|^3 / |C'' x C'''|`, when the radius at that distance is the tolerance to within half of it (`zero_speed_radius_stretch`, on the mapped trim with WR-560). The port reads the first solution that is not passed over. Rust curves do not point back to their edge, so the edge and edge-use checks build an `EdgeEnds` context (the edge's interval, and the faces' tangency at each end) and pass it in. A curve checked on no edge keeps the upstream result. Regression tests: `nurbs::b_spline_curve::p3` `wr_668_*`, and `topology::brep::validation::assert_valid::edge` `wr_668_edge_ends_read_the_faces_tangency`.
