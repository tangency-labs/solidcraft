Updates: WR-685

#### WR-685 (update, 2026-11-01). A variable round's circle section is the great circle through its rails, not the balls' touch circle, and a round whose balls fold is built folded
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a variable round leaves the union of its balls: the box 20 x 30 x 20 with its edge y = 30, z = 20 rounded by the law 1 to 5 removes 44.353 against the balls' cone's 43.778; a law whose balls fold over one another is built to 726.56 removed against the union's 332.2)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet solver)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletStandardSolver.cpp`, `SmCircularCrossSectionFSG::CreateSurface` and `CreateSurfaceFromCurves` (the fork's `SmGnSectionFSG::CircleSurface` and `CircleSurfaceFromCurves`, its order-1 section since tangency-labs/smlib@58e073d = d01d7b0's 3aae1c4); `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCliffOverflow.cpp`, `sm_HoldsItsLaw` (fork only).
- **What happens:** each circle section is the circle about the ball's centre through the two rail points. Where the ball's radius varies along the centre line the ball touches the round along a smaller circle, square to the centre line and moved along it by `-r r'/|c'|` (the envelope's condition `<p - c, c'> = -r r'` on the ball). The great circle lies outside the envelope there. Where the touch circles move back along the centre line the envelope folds over itself, and the great circles, which never fold, build such a round anyway.
- **Trigger:** the fork's capi tests `section_variable_round_on_its_cone` (box above, closed form 11956.2224267359) and `section_variable_round_spline_folds` (box 10 x 20 x 30, its edge x = 10, z = 30, the cubic law 2, 2, 40, 3, 2 over the knots 0, 0, 0, 0, 0.5, 1, 1, 1, 1).
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib 6b346f6 → 5db8feb → fb5c060 → d622107 and 8bedf89 (= d01d7b0's d2a6125, f2ec002, e9560e3, 2714d37 and 6007a3d) (smlib#78). Tracking: tangency-labs/solidcore#2250.
