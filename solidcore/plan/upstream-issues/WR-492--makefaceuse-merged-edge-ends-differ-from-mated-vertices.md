#### WR-492. `SmMerge::MakeFaceuse` fails (`SM_ERR`) when a matched edge's ends are not the mates of the other edge's vertices; two mitred cylinders at a small turn do not unite
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Intersection
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmMerge.cpp:7066`-`7078` at `3fd5b95` (v1.0.1), `SmMerge::MakeFaceuse` (def. `:6833`), the `pV != pV2` branch: `pVBRel = GetBrepMate(pV)` must be `pVB` or `pVB2`, the two ends of the edge `MergeEdge` returned (`:7031`), else `SER(SM_ERR)` (`:7077`).
- **What happens:** `MergeEdge` returns the primary edge the intersector already mated to the other operand's edge. The loop's vertices were merged just before, and a vertex with no mate gets a new shell vertex at its own point. When the mated edge ends a small gap away from that point (a few vertex tolerances), neither end of the edge is the new vertex, and `MakeFaceuse` returns `SM_ERR` (1001) from `SmApiBooleanUnion`. The gap comes from the imprint: the other operand has two vertices 7.2e-5 apart (1e-5 tolerance) where its seam meets the mitre plane, and the primary one between them (3.6e-5 from each, its tolerance grown to 4.6e-5 to cover that) is mated to the first, not the second.
- **Trigger:** two cylinders of radius 2.75 through one point, their axes `turn` degrees apart (symmetric about the mitre plane's normal), each cut back with a half-space to that plane (`SmApiBooleanDifference`, the half-space an `SmApiCreateLinearSweep` square), then `SmApiBooleanUnion` of the two pieces, which only touch on the plane. The union fails for turns of 0.1, 0.25 and 0.3 degrees and succeeds for 0.4, 0.5, 1, 2, 5 and 90. SolidCraft's tube corners trigger it from a different pair: in a52's `lead-out-minus` (3 runs, 2 mitres) the curve-swept run joined to the next straight run fails at 5 degrees (the swept wall's cap boundary is a spline within 3.4e-4 of the exact ellipse of the other run); the same SM_ERR site, mated vertices 3.4e-4 from the edge's end. Rotating the profile seams onto one another does not help.
- **How found:** SolidCraft's corner union (`Upstream { operation: "boolean::merge::p2::make_faceuse", code: 1001 }` from its solidcore adapter), reduced in the Rust port, then rebuilt through SM_API.
- **Confirmed:** yes, with our own SM_API drivers on the C++ build of `3fd5b95` (aarch64 Linux, g++ 13, `-ffp-contract=off`): the cylinder pair returns 1001 at 0.1, 0.25 and 0.3 degrees and 1000 (success) at 0.4, 0.5, 1, 2, 5 and 90, the same thresholds as the Rust port; the a52 pair (run 1: `SmApiCreateCurveSweep` along the joined path, run 2: `SmApiCreateLinearSweep`, each cut at its mitre planes; the volumes agree with the Rust port to 1e-7: 560.933228236 against 560.9332282357797 for run 2) returns 1001 from the union.
- **Rust port:** same behaviour by default (`crates/solidcore/tests/wr492_mitred_cylinders.rs`). No fix yet. Taking the nearer end of the matched edge in `orientation_to_find` (within the edge's and the vertices' tolerances) removes the error but returns a wrong body for the cylinders (6 faces, 8 edges, 6 vertices, volume 285.10 (0.1 degrees) and 237.58 (0.25) against 522.68, not a manifold solid); mating the vertex with the edge's end before the loop's vertices are merged fails in `Relate` (1001), because that end is already mated to the other operand's neighbouring vertex. The fix has to remove the near-duplicate vertices from the imprint (a vertex clustering step before the faces are made), not choose between them in `MakeFaceuse`.
