#### WR-794. `sm_FixUVTrimCurves` moves a UV trim's end control point alone onto the vertex, which hooks the trim when the gap is not small against its end control leg
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a trim that bends back on itself; SMLib's own check then fails the body)
- **Area:** Topology, faces, UV trim curves
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFace.cpp:7864`-`7875` (`sm_FixUVTrimCurves`): the two `EditEndPoint` calls, each under the comment "no protection from introducing kinks".
- **What happens:** `EditEndPoint` sets the first or last control point to the vertex's UV point and leaves the others. The end control leg `l` becomes `l - d`, with `d` the move. When `|d|` is not small against `|l|` the leg turns by any angle, and the trim bends back within its end span.
- **Trigger:** a trim cache build on a face with a trim that ends beside its vertex by more than its end control leg. CADGenBench edit input 204 (ODC-BY 1.0, dataset revision f76f9655), read from STEP, has 16 such trims: cubics with knot multiplicities 4, 3, 3, 3, 4 whose first piece is 1.2e-4 long in UV (control legs about 3.9e-5), ending 1.04e-4 or 1.55e-4 beside the vertex's UV point. After the move the radius of curvature at the end is 7.2e-5 or 5.8e-5 on trims about 0.73 long. `SmBSplineCurve` test 13 (tolerance 7.4e-5) fails for each of them, so the check refuses every edit of the body. The file's 3D curve there has a radius of about 980.
- **How found:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#14257, tracing the 16 failures on input 204 to the corner fix. With the moves longer than the end leg skipped (an experiment), all 16 went and none came.
- **Confirmed:** no (read at `3fd5b95`, `SmFace.cpp:7864`-`7875`; the port made the same move and shows the hook).
- **Rust port:** `topology::face::p2::spread_end_move` moves the control points next to the end part of the way first. With `g` the gap and `d_i` the length of the control polygon from the end to control point `i`, the move stops at control point `j`: the second one when the end leg is at least `20 g` long, else the first with `d_j >= 20 g` at which the knots keep the curve tangent-continuous, else the far end. Control point `i < j` moves by `1 - d_i / d_j` of the gap. No point of the trim moves by more than `g`; a control leg before `j` turns by at most `asin(g / d_j)`; legs that were parallel stay parallel; a trim whose end leg is at least `20 g` long is edited as upstream. 20 is the ratio `sm_FixUVTrimCurves` already uses to call a gap small (it skips a gap above a twentieth of the face's UV domain). Tests `issue_14257_*` in `crates/solidcore/src/topology/face/p2.rs`.
- **Not covered:**
  - A trim whose whole control polygon is shorter than `20 g` is spread to its far end, and its legs turn by up to `asin(g / d)` with `d` the polygon's length.
  - A trim of degree 1 with an end leg of at least `20 g` is edited as upstream: its end leg turns at its first corner, by at most `asin(1/20)`.
  - `FixTrimLoopTinyParameterSpaceGaps` (`topology::trimming_tools::fix_loop_tiny_parameter_space_gaps`) moves two end control points alone in the same way, each by less than its `tol_2d`. No case that hooks a trim there is known; it is unchanged.
