Updates: WR-669

#### WR-669 (update, 2026-11-02). A round on a line spine is the cylinder about the exact spine, and keeps that frame
- **What changes:** with `Kernel::fixes.fillet_exact_round_classes`, the `Line` class carries its spine, the line its two offsets meet in. `sm_ClassifyBlendSpine` (`classify_blend_spine`) now gives it in closed form (`line_spine`). Two planes meet along `n1 x n2`. A plane parallel to a cylinder's axis meets it `sqrt(R^2 - h^2)` either side of the axis's foot on the plane. Two cylinders with parallel axes meet through their sections' circles' crossing. Of two lines, it takes the one nearer the classified centre. `CalcLineSpineFilletGeom` (`cylinder_fillet_geom_parts_of` with the class) projects the stations' centres onto that line and takes the rails' ends as their feet on the faces' quadrics (`BlendQuadric::foot`). It then builds the analytic cylinder on the frame it made (`class_cylinder`): the cone `IsNurbSurfaceCone` would recognise, without refitting its frame from the patch's end circles' `IsArc` fits. Without the class, the round is built as before.
- **Why:** on the fork's oblique Ball (capi `ball oblique_corner`), the round's axis ended up 2.17e-12 and 2.1e-12 off the corner ball's centre C on two of the three rounds. The fork read its 1.06e-12 as the refit (`CopyAndAddAnalytics` → `IsNurbSurfaceCone`). In the port, most of it came from the stations. The far station's centre, solved by Newton on the offsets, was 2e-12 off the spine, so the axis direction was 7.9e-14 off the edge over the 28-long extended spine. The refit then moved the direction a further 7e-15, which is 2e-13 at C.
- **How found:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#10850 and tangency-labs/smlib#97 (item 1); solidcore #2405. Solidcore-native: there is no fork commit, and the fork's `CalcLineSpineFilletGeom` at its last commit (18e96f1) still builds from the stations and recognises the patch.
- **Confirmed:** in the Rust port (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2405_blend_follow_ups.rs`, `the_exact_rounds_cylinder_is_on_its_exact_spine`). Each round's axis is now 1.7e-14, 5.2e-15 and 8.9e-15 from C (the frame's origin is 28 from C, at coordinates that round at 3.6e-15). Its direction is 1e-16 from `n_i x n_(i+1)` (it was 7.9e-14), and its radius is within 1.1e-14 of r. The fork's C++ was not run here.
