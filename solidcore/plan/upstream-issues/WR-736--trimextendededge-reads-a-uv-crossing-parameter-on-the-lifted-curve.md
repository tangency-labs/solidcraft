#### WR-736. `TrimExtendedEdge` reads a UV-curve crossing's parameter on the lifted 3D curve, so a thickened sheet's free corners land off each other and the merge fails or leaves slivers
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Offset
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetGeometryCreation.cpp:1102-1211`, `SmOffsetGeometryCreation::TrimExtendedEdge` (def. `:878`), the tangent-sector and lamina neighbours of the base edge use. A neighbour already mated gives its offset vertices, solved onto `p3DCurve` within `10 * m_dThisApproxTol3d` (`:1121`); otherwise `pTanUV->GlobalCurveIntersect` (`:1202`) intersects the base edge use's extended UV trim curve with the neighbour's, and the solutions (parameters of `pTanUV`, the UV curve) are evaluated on `p3DCurve` (`:1318-1323`), the extended UV curve lifted onto the extended offset surface by `ExtendTrimEdges` (`LiftCurve`, `:1546`).
- **What happens:** the lift does not keep the UV curve's parameterisation, so a UV-curve parameter read on the 3D curve is a point elsewhere along it. On a thickened sheet's free boundary the offset of a B-spline edge then ends up to 4.3e-5 (four times the tolerance, R20) along the curve from where its neighbour's offset ends at their shared corner. Where the two edges are tangent the crossing of their UV curves is also ill-conditioned (about sqrt(2 R eps) along the curve: 6e-6 at R20), and the neighbour's offset vertex solved onto the curve within ten tolerances returns a point up to that far along it. The side walls (`BuildSideWallOfEdgeuse`, ruled from each edge to its offset) then meet the offset face and each other at corners that disagree, and `PiecewiseMerge` either fails in `TopologyIntersector::Relate` (1001: a vertex already has a different mate), leaves a zero-width sliver that `ShrinkGeometry` refuses (`TrimWithDomain`, 1001), or splits two-edged slivers off the walls and returns a body that is no closed solid.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcore#2611 (SolidCraft #11744): the outer face (r 25) of a half annulus r 23..25 extruded z -40..40, cut below y = 1 for z < 0, below an arc of radius R from (z, y) = (0, 1) through 45 degrees and past the arc's end, copied as a sheet and thickened by 2 (`SmApiShellBrepFull`, create offset solid, extended convex edges, merged results). Its free boundary is two lines, two circular arcs and two B-spline intersection curves, each line tangent to its B-spline at z = 0. At the pinned defaults R20 builds a manifold body that `check_brep` level 2 rejects (two vertex-gap reports) and R12 a clean one. With every other opt-in fix on (`UpstreamFixes::all()` without this flag): inward R18, R20 and R40 fail in `Relate`, R14 and R30 in `TrimWithDomain`; outward R12 (11 faces) and R18 (10 faces) are no closed solid; over R5..R40 (step 1) and thicknesses +-0.1, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 5, 370 of 432 thickenings are not manifold solids clean at level 2.
- **How found:** SolidCraft report #11744, reduced to the repro above; the corner positions were traced in `trim_extended_edge` against the vertices' exact images on the r 25 + t cylinder.
- **Confirmed:** yes, against the pinned C++ on the R20 and R12 sheets thickened both ways (`fixtures/offset.offset_executive_p2/issue2611.json`, Rust-written ASCII `.smb` operands): the C++ gives the port's default results (4 cases pass, 56 exact fields and 21 values each, max_rel_err 0). The corner positions were measured in the port, whose `trim_extended_edge` follows both branches as upstream has them.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.offset_lamina_corner_at_vertex_image`, `trim_extended_edge` (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_geometry_creation.rs`) ends a lamina edge's offset, at a vertex whose next edge is lamina or a tangent sector, where the offset curve passes nearest the vertex's own image `V + sign d n(V)` (`offset_vertex_image`: the face's surface normal at the vertex, which is on the surface, so a well-conditioned solve), and takes neither the UV crossing nor the neighbour's snapped vertex. The image is exact by construction: the face's offset is `S + sign d n`, so the vertex's offset is its image whatever the neighbours are. It is the trim point upstream's crossing aims at (the crossing of the two edges' UV curves is the vertex's parameters on the face), found without a UV-curve parameter on the 3D curve and without intersecting tangent curves. Dropping the vertex itself onto the offset curve (the image is the nearest point of an exact offset curve to the vertex) was tried first: it amplifies the lifted curve's tolerance-level error by the thickness (1.25e-5 along the curve at t 3) and left 83 of 432 thickenings unclean. Solidcore-native, no fork commit.
  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2611_thicken_arc_trim.rs`. With the flag (on the pinned defaults, and with every opt-in fix) every sheet R5..R40 thickens by +-0.1..5 (432 of 432 in each mode) to a manifold solid of 8 faces, `check_brep` level 2 clean, within 3.7e-7 of the closed-form volume (2.3e-6 at t -0.1 on the pinned defaults), as do the 10, 60 and 80 degree arcs. Without it the R20 recipe fails inward with 1001 in `Relate` and R12 outward is no closed solid (11 faces). The flag alone also closes the earlier free-rim cases on its own: the #2174 caps (WR-673's, with that flag off; `issue_2174_cap_rim_two_edges.rs`, `wr736_alone_closes_the_gap`) and #1502's z5 cap of two half rims (WR-463's; `issue_1502_z5_cap_thicken.rs`) thicken to their closed forms, and the #2111 patch sheets offset without the Bad Gaps WR-705's tolerance update covers. It changes results that already built (#2174's outward body at the defaults, #2111's offsets), hence the flag.
