Updates: WR-710

#### WR-710 (update, 2026-11-02). The blend corner's Mixed corner builds the cusp corner the refusal guards
- **What changed:** with `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner` (WR-697) on, `FilletCorner::create` first asks whether the corner is the blend corner's Mixed corner (`blend::blend_corner::mixed::classify_mixed`; built in solidcore from smlib#82, #2360). A convex and a concave blend continuing each other G1 on a common plane, with the third edge at the vertex a straight tangent seam, is built as the Mixed corner instead of the 3x2 mixed-convexity corner, and `fillet_refuse_mixed_corner_crossing_rails` is not applied to it. #2256's peg, rounded r 2 or r 5 or chamfered d 2, then builds within its box, G1 across the corner, to the closed-form volume (`WR-697--addendum-2026-11-02-mixed-corner.md`).
- **The refusal is kept:** it still applies where the blend corner is off, and to any corner whose rails cross that the Mixed classification does not take (a common face that is not a plane, a third edge that is not a straight tangent seam). It is not retired.
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2256_peg_mixed_corner.rs` and `rounds_together_refused_by_wr710` in `crates/solidcore/tests/fork_9bebf34_tangent_end_cases.rs` now turn `fillet_blend_corner` off, so they keep covering the refusal and the pinned corner. `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2360_mixed_corner.rs` runs with every flag on, the refusal included, and the Mixed corner builds.
