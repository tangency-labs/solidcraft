#### WR-450. `SmBSplineSurface::CopyAndAddAnalytics` gives an analytic copy in another parameterization, so a split face's UV trims point off its edges (#387)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Surfaces and booleans
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmBSplineSurface.cpp`, `SmBSplineSurface::CopyAndAddAnalytics`, called by `SmBrep::MakeEdgeInFaceWithVU`.
- **What happens:** splitting a face copies its surface for the new face, as an analytic surface where the shape is one. The new face keeps the old face's UV domain and trim curves, but the analytic types build their NURBS form in their own parameterization (a circle as SMLib's two-span arcs) and match only the domain. For a NURBS surface parameterized otherwise, such as a rational half cylinder in four equal spans, the copy has the same shape at other parameters, so every kept trim points elsewhere on the surface (up to 5 mm off its edge). The edges take tolerances to cover it.
- **Trigger:** a box (-14,0,-14)-(14,20,14) minus a z cylinder at (0,50,-60), r 40, h 120, given as two rational quadratic half-cylinder faces of four spans each (weights 1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.5, 0.75, 1) with their UV trims. The result has edge and vertex tolerances of 0.78 mm against a body tolerance of 0.022 and fails `ValidatePointers`. The same box minus SMLib's analytic cylinder is valid.
- **How found:** SolidCraft routing its booleans through SMLib (tangency-labs/solidcore#387).
- **Confirmed:** yes, by the fork's capi test `rational_cylinder_cut` (tangency-labs/smlib@276b509). In the port, the recogniser takes the rational half cylinder as analytic and the copy is off its source by more than 1e-3 at the same parameters (unit test `rational_half_cylinder_copy_keeps_parameterization`); the full boolean is not run in Rust (the port has no flat-record import).
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@276b509 checks an analytic copy against the surface at a 7 x 7 grid of parameters (within `SM_ZONE_TOL_3D` or 1e-9 of the size) and keeps an exact copy where it differs.
- **Rust port:** `BSplineSurface::copy_and_add_analytics` behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.analytic_copy_keeps_parameterization` it runs the same grid check and returns an exact copy where the analytic differs.
