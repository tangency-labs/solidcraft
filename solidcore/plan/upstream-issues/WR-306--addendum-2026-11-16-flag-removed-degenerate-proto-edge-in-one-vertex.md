Updates: WR-306

#### WR-306 (update, 2026-11-16). `degenerate_proto_edge_in_one_vertex` is removed; the absorb test is a certified hull bound

The fork's fix (03e7bf1) took a proto edge from a proto vertex back to itself as degenerate below twice the proto vertex's containment radius, `max(m_dTol, capture distance)`, when both curve ends found one vertex definition. The capture distance is how far apart vertex definitions are merged (an absolute 0.01 in the Boolean, up to 9.9e6 in the generated proto-topology scripts), not the size of a point, so that bound dropped real curves: a radius-1e-3 circle on a box's top (the union came back as 2 solid regions), a length-2 line in a radius-10 capture ball, B-splines 1.7 and 14 long. It also replaced upstream's `2 * m_dTol` test instead of adding to it (`plan/findings/topology.proto_topology_p2--wr306-absorbs-small-closed-curves*.md`).

What is now the only path (`ProtoTopologyManager::resolve_proto_edge`, `curve_inside_one_vertex`):
- Upstream's test is kept as it is: a self-loop proto edge whose first curve is shorter than `2 * m_dTol` is degenerate.
- Past it, the proto edge is absorbed into the vertex only when both of its first curve's ends find one vertex definition **and** every definition's curve, over its own interval, lies within that definition's tolerance of its position. That is decided on a certified hull of each curve (`curve_piece_within`: a line's ends, or a B-spline or conic piece's control points, each distance grown by `gamma(8)` rounding plus twice the coordinates' error bound; a curve with no certain hull, or non-finite data, is never absorbed). The capture distance never decides it.
- The hull test is decided for every self-loop proto edge, below upstream's short test too. A curve that passes it marks the proto vertex, as the fork marked every speck it dropped; a short curve that fails it is dropped unmarked, as upstream drops it. `ImprintIntersections` then fits a marked vertex's final vertices to their edges and makes the pair's tolerances cover each other (the fork's fit, unchanged, now unconditional for marked proto vertices). That the curve lies within the definition's tolerance is proven; that the fitted vertex then stands for the curve is argued (the fit grows its tolerance to cover the edges' ends).

Removed: the field, SolidCraft's preset line (`crates/smlib/src/core.rs`), its entry in `fixes.json`, every test setting of it, and the flag-off tests (`issue_1058_flag_is_off_by_default`, `issue_1058_upstream_join_has_millimetre_tolerances`, and the flag loop of `boolean_union_of_crossing_creases_has_no_dangling_vertex_refs`).
