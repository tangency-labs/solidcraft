Updates: WR-407

#### WR-407 (update, 2026-11-15). `cover_refuses_folded_continuation` is removed

The fold rule is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, #17215). `Surface::cover_coincident_surface` (`surface/surface/p3.rs`) always refuses a cover that extends a B-spline-based surface past its natural domain onto a continuation whose unit normals turn back (dot product not positive) between neighbouring samples of the 21 x 21 grid over the domain the merge would use, whose normal vanishes at a sample past the natural domain, or where a sample cannot be evaluated. The refusal names its witness (`CoverCoincident::fold`, `ContinuationFold`). Upstream merges onto the continuation.

One change from the fork's rule (tangency-labs/smlib 1b6f5fa): the fork also refused any sample whose normal is not longer than `SM_EFF_ZERO_SQRT` times the normal at the centre of the natural domain. Past the natural domain this is kept, and a normal within its rounding bound refuses there too (`ContinuationFold::Vanishes`): the continuation may run into a pole or a cusp, and the merged face would take it on. This refusal is sampled and conservative: a sample just past one of the surface's own degenerate edges can fall below the cut-off and refuse too. For example, a hemisphere covered onto its continuation up to the opposite pole fails the union of solidcore-ops' `a_dome_1e_8_off_its_circle_joins_to_its_mirror`. Inside the natural domain (boundary included), the cut-off refused covers at the surface's own degenerate points, which the face already has. In generated case `gen2303` of `surface.surface_p3`, the normal is exactly zero at the corner (-0.938, 0) of the natural domain, and the cover is exact to 2.2e-12. There, a sample whose computed normal is no longer than the cross product's own rounding bound, `2 γ_4 |Du| |Dv|` (an exact zero included), has no direction and witnesses nothing. Above that bound the computed direction is within 90 degrees of the exact cross product of the computed derivatives. The next sample along its grid row and column is compared with the last sample before it that has a direction, so a reversal across the zero is still a fold. A short but non-zero normal (the evaluation replaces a vanishing first derivative by a short tangent along its limit direction) is compared by its direction like any other.

Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::cover_refuses_folded_continuation`, SolidCraft's preset line (`crates/smlib/src/core.rs`), the goldens SolidCraft fix-name arm, the `fixes` lists of `fixtures/solidcraft/434-two-overlapping-swept-arc-walls.json`, the entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, and the test settings in `issue_1945`, `issue_2020` and `wr650`.

- **Tests:** `surface/surface/p3.rs` `wr_407_degenerate_edge_of_the_domain_is_no_fold` (exactly zero normal on an edge, no fold), `wr_407_collapsed_edge_of_the_domain_is_no_fold` (short normal below the fork's cut-off, no fold), `wr_407_fold_through_a_zero_normal_is_still_a_fold` (the bridged pair is found), `wr_407_collapsed_edge_of_the_continuation_refuses` (a collapsed edge only the continuation has refuses with `Vanishes`) and `issue_434_default_refuses_the_fold_of_a_planar_sheet`. The goldens unit's `fold_witness_tests` replay `gen2303` (the cover is kept) and the six folding covers (each refused, with its reversed pair recomputed from the surface's derivatives). `tests/wr_407_swept_arc_walls_join_without_fold.rs` checks the default join: a manifold solid, clean at level 2, with the closed-form volume. Removed: `issue_434_cover_refuses_a_continuation_that_folds` (the same assertion as the default test now) and `wr_407_default_join_loses_the_folded_cover` (it pinned the upstream path).
- **Results:** six `surface.surface_p3` rows go from a cover to a refusal: gen0041, gen1448, gen1514, gen1567, gen2066 and gen2376. `gen2303` keeps its cover.
