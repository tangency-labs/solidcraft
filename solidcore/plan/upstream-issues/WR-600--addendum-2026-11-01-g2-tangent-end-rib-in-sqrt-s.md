Updates: WR-600

#### WR-600 (update, 2026-11-01). A G2 blend's end rib at a tangent end is fitted in t = sqrt(s) (fork cd47efd)

- **What happens:** with the tangent end of the trace (fork 856b54e, previous addendum), `sm_TangentEndRib` solves the end rib from the tangent end P outward at stations `s ~ t^2` for an odd contact order k. For a curvature-continuous section (k = 3) the rib leaves P as `a ~ s^(3/2)` along the seam, its curvature growing as `s^(-1/2)`. The three curves (3D, on the fillet surface, on the end surface) were still fitted in `s`, regular at P, so no fitted curve could follow the rib there. On the de3a8c9 peg, a G2 round of 2 on the front top edge gives an end rib whose first derivative at P is (-0.176, 0, 3.51), not zero.
- **Fix:** the SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@cd47efd, not merged upstream. For an odd order past 1 the curves are B-splines in `t`, with the zero-speed end at P. Their first derivative there is zero (the first two control points are set to coincide), and their second is the exact `2 (s1 - s0) dX/ds`, X the fillet surface along its section at P. On the end surface it is the uv derivative that maps to that, solved from the normal equations of its tangent plane. Both are end conditions of `CreateInterpolatingCurve` at the stations (0cc8cd4). The stations start as 8 uniform ones, and only the spans whose midpoint is off the exact rib are halved, at that midpoint. The chamfer (k = 1) and the round (k = 2) keep doubling their stations everywhere, as before.
- **Rust port:** under the same flag, `Kernel::fixes.fillet_nx1_tangent_side_edge_same_convexity`. Nothing is shipped, so the flag is extended and no new one is added. The change is in `tangent_end_rib` in `blend/fillet_geom/tangent_end_rib.rs`, which only that flag reaches (from `calc_fillet_int_side_face`, `blend/fillet_geom/p5.rs`). It calls `BSplineCurve::create_interpolating_curve_with_params` (#2080). Its new refusals (an end surface singular at P, the fillet section leaving the end surface's tangent plane, an interpolation whose second control point leaves P) are `SM_ERR`.
- **Results** on the peg, with the fork's profile from the previous addendum plus `bspline_check_min_radius` and WR-668's `curve_check_zero_speed_tangent_end`, for a G2 round (blend-curve section, continuity 2, thumbweight 1) of 2 on the front top edge:
  - the end rib has zero speed at P, and its second derivative lies along the seam;
  - `check_brep` at level 2 is clean;
  - the volume is 9395.419893021, against 9395.419890495 with the rib fitted in `s` and 9395.419872879 with WR-600's flag off.

  The fork's message gives 9393.307931047 against its quadrature's 9393.307936313. That matches a quintic G2 section whose control points are evenly spaced on the faces (2, 4/3, 2/3 from the edge). solidcore's blend-curve section removes less material on this part whether this change is on or off, so that gap is not the rib's.
- **Test:** `crates/solidcore/tests/fork_cd47efd_tangent_end_rib_sqrt_s.rs`.
