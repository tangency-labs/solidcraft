Updates: WR-345

#### WR-345 (update, 2026-10-29). Loops that only touch are refused as SM_ERR_OFFSET_WALLS_TOUCH (opt-in)

The fork's follow-up to the crossing-loop fix, tangency-labs/smlib a759622 ("SMLib: an offset whose walls would meet is refused as such"), is ported as a second opt-in, `Kernel::fixes.offset_walls_touch_refused` (default `false`), for tangency-labs/solidcore#1442.

- **What it changes:** `ResolveCrossingLoopsOfFace` refused loops that only touch with the status and reason of whichever crossing check failed first (`SM_ERR_BAD_INTERSECTIONS`, 1027, e.g. "Offset face loops leave a crossing more than one way"). With the opt-in, every contact between two loops is examined before any crossing is resolved or refused. Loops touch where they run along one another (a range solution), meet tangentially (one leaves the contact along the other, `SmVector3d::IsParallelTo` at `SM_ANG_TOL_DEG`), or come up to one another and go back on the same side (the directions each loop leaves the contact in, about the surface normal). Touching loops fail with the fork's new status `SM_ERR_OFFSET_WALLS_TOUCH` (1042; `solidcore::error::SM_ERR_OFFSET_WALLS_TOUCH`), surfaced as `SolidError::Upstream { code: 1042, .. }` whose `operation` is the fork's reason ("Offset face loops touch without crossing: the offset walls meet along a line"). A crossing at a loop vertex stays `SM_ERR_BAD_INTERSECTIONS` ("cross at a loop vertex").
- **Dependency:** the opt-in only changes `ResolveCrossingLoopsOfFace`, which runs only with `offset_resolve_crossing_loops`. Without that flag, `offset_walls_touch_refused` has no effect (test `issue_1442_walls_touch_needs_resolve_crossing_loops`).
- **Not ported here:** a759622's `SmBrep::LocalOperation` change (it returns the offset's status instead of `SM_ERR`). `SmBrep::LocalOperation` is being ported separately (tangency-labs/solidcore#1427).
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1442_offset_walls_touch.rs`. SolidCraft rig case shell/4711 (a 60 x 40 x 10 block less an r3 hole at (22.5, 7) and a 10 x 10 front notch, shelled 2 inward and open at z = 0) fails with 1042, and with 1027 without the opt-in. #854's plate with its hole at x = 5 (offset tangent to the side's) fails with 1042. Shell/4711 at 1.5 and 2.5 builds with and without the opt-in (6301.584938 and 9840.098443, against the fork's C++ 6301.584956 and 9840.098462). #854's plate at x = 4.5 builds with the same volume, bit for bit, with and without the opt-in.
- **Difftest:** no change on the default path (the routine does not run without `offset_resolve_crossing_loops`). The `offset.offset_executive_p2` runner uses the default kernel, so no case compares the opt-in path, as for WR-345.
