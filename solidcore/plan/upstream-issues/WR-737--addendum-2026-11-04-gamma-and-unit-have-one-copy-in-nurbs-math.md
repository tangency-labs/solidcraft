Updates: WR-737

#### WR-737 (update, 2026-11-04). `gamma` and `UNIT` have one copy in `nurbs::math`
- **What changed:** `step_within_rounding` and `eval::collapse` now import `crate::nurbs::math::{gamma, UNIT}`; the copies in `eval::collapse` are deleted. This follows [jyviko's decision](https://github.com/tangency-labs/solidcore/pull/2675#issuecomment-6629347476) to keep `nurbs::math::gamma` as the one copy and add `UNIT` beside it. It supersedes the shared-helper location stated in the [earlier addendum](https://github.com/tangency-labs/solidcore/blob/main/plan/upstream-issues/WR-737--addendum-2026-11-04-bound-calls-collapse-gamma-and-unit.md) and resolves its "Open, for the maintainers" point.
- **The value is unchanged, bit for bit.** `UNIT = f64::EPSILON * 0.5`, identical to `f64::EPSILON / 2.0`. The former `gamma` expressions, `n as f64 * f64::EPSILON / 2.0` and `n as f64 * (f64::EPSILON * 0.5)`, both scale by exact powers of two, without overflow or subnormal intermediates for any `usize`; both then use `nu / (1.0 - nu)`. `rounding_bounds_keep_the_previous_bits` in `crates/solidcore/src/nurbs/math.rs` checks the previous expressions against the shared implementation for 4290 values on 64-bit targets.
- **The record's figures stand.** The per-pair bound remains `(2 u + gamma_3) (|P0| + |P1|)`: 1.1e-15 at coordinates of 1, 1.1e-10 at 1e5, with the two criteria still parting at `|P0| + |P1| = 1801.44` mm.
