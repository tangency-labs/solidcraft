Updates: WR-564

#### WR-564 (update, 2026-11-15). `thicken_planar_side_walls` is removed

Thickening a sheet puts every planar side wall onto its plane (the fork's `sm_PutPlanarFaceOnItsPlane`, gated by `Surface::is_planar(SM_EFF_ZERO)` on the wall's control-point box) and deletes the old surface's seam it leaves as a slit. The fix is now unconditional and the flag is gone; SolidCraft already ran with it on. `ShellOptions::merge_sheet_side_walls` still decides only whether coplanar walls are merged into one face. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::thicken_planar_side_walls`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260_gn_pipe_refined_range_end.rs`, and every test setting of it.

- **Results:** 14 `offset.offset_executive_p2` goldens rows move, every one a sheet thickening. In 10 the only change is the walls' face kind (ruled B-spline to `SmPlane`); in the four cylinder-sheet rows the end rings also become planes with two loops and lose their two seam edges (loops 4 to 6, edges 8 to 6). The goldens test `offset_offset_executive_p2::tests::wr564_thickened_sheet_walls_are_planes_and_sound` replays all 14: each result is one sound body (`check_brep` level 2 clean, a manifold solid of positive volume). On the 10 plane and cylinder sheets every wall is a plane and the volume's certified enclosure (`enclose_volume`, refined to a width of at most 1e-7 of the volume) holds the closed form (plane sheets: area times thickness; cylinder sheets: `pi |(r + d)^2 - r^2| h`). On the 4 half pipes (radial thickenings of a region of the r 25 cylinder, of volume `((25 + d)^2 - 25^2) / 2` up to sign times the region's integral over z of its angular width) exactly four walls are planes, and a certified enclosure of that closed form (monotone bounds over 2^20 cells, widened for rounding; under 4e-8 wide) lies inside the volume's certified enclosure. The volume's enclosure is asked for 1e-6 of the volume and stops at its cell budget near 2e-6 (R 12) and 1e-4 (R 20), so the test accepts a width up to 2e-4: the body's volume is certified to the closed form within 2e-4 of it, slack set from the measured R 20 width, which suffices (argued) because a wall is replaced only where its control points lie within `SM_EFF_ZERO` of the new plane, so the replacement moves the volume by at most the replaced walls' area times `SM_EFF_ZERO`.
- **Tests:** `tests/issue_1599_thicken_planar_side_walls.rs` (the tube's volume against the closed form on the default kernel). Removed with the upstream path: `issue_1599_without_the_opt_in_rings_are_surfaces_of_revolution`.
