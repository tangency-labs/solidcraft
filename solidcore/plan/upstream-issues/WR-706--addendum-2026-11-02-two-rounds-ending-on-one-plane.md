Updates: WR-706

#### WR-706 (update, 2026-11-02). Two rounds whose section ends lie on one plane were refused

- **What happened:** with `fillet_section_end_trim_at_creases` on, the trim closes each section end with a flat face bounded by the round's section there, which `section_edge` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_cliff_overflow.rs`) found as the one lamina edge of the sheet on the end's section plane, searching the whole sheet. When another round of the same selection also ends on that plane, two lamina edges qualified and the trim refused (`NoRoomReason::TangentContinuationEndUnsupported`, 1001 in `trim_at_creases`).
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcore#2314: #2223's tabbed plate (R25 disc, tab sides x = +-4, R2 corners centred at (+-2, 280/9), top y = 280/9 + 2, extruded 2), rounded r 0.5 on both R2 corner arcs together, every `UpstreamFixes` flag on. Each arc runs on into the tab's side at (+-4, 280/9) with tangent +-y, so both have a section end on the plane y = 280/9. Each arc alone built (0.15913583445762924 and 0.15913584503823586 removed).
- **Fix (same flag, a gap in WR-706's own behaviour):** `section_edge` searches only the lamina edges of the end's own round's faces (the solver's fillet faces as `cut_fillet_at_open_end` left them); `FilletSession::trim_at_creases` keeps each section end's solver to pass them. No new flag: this is solidcore's own WR-706 code, not reachable with the flags off, where results are unchanged and match the pinned C++.
- **Result:** both arcs together remove 0.31827168065728983 against the closed form 0.31827167944383244 (two disjoint Pappus wedges, (1 - pi/4) r^2 (pi/2) (2 - c), c = r (10 - 3 pi) / (3 (4 - pi))), 14 faces, `check_brep` Level2 valid; with the rim as well, the rim's 7.958674274526857 plus both wedges to 1e-7, 15 faces. Each arc alone is unchanged. Checked against the closed form only (no fork comparison).
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2314_tab_corner_arcs.rs` (fails before the fix with the 1001); `issue_2223_section_end_trim_at_creases.rs` and `wr695_overflow_trim_at_creases.rs` still pass.
