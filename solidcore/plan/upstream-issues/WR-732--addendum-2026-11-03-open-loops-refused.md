Updates: WR-732

#### WR-732 (update, 2026-11-03). Open loops mid-edit are refused; loop closure reads the edges' ends through their uses
- **What changed (#2624):** under `UpstreamFixes::brep_ray_classifies_current_trims`, a face whose loops are open while its B-rep is edited is now `SolidError::FaceTrimsUnavailable` naming the face, like one whose trims fail to implant. The untrimmed-tree fallback for such faces (the original record's "keeps upstream's untrimmed tree") is removed.
- **Why the fallback existed:** refusing open faces had failed 15 fillet tests that turn every flag on (`issue_2223_section_end_trim_at_creases` 5, `issue_2364_alpha1_ends` 2, `wr696_fit_sizes` 3, `wr707_rounds_running_off_their_faces` 5). Their classifications are the region rays of a stitch's glue inside the fillet's finish (`glue_edges_general` -> `test_closure_split_region_attempt` -> `split_closed` -> `find_and_set_infinite_region` -> `ray_fire`), and the planar sections of `fillet_overflow::creases`. In every one, the face was not open: `loops_close` read each edge's cached end pair (`Edge::vertices`, port-only, refreshed only when a public operation returns), which mid-edit still named a vertex a merge had deleted, and counted that as an open loop.
- **Fix:** `loops_close` reads an edge's ends through its edge uses (the starts of the primary edge use and its mate, as `SmEdge::GetVertices` does). Those faces close and are classified against their current trims; the 15 tests pass with the refusal in place.
- **Result change, same flag:** with the flag on, a face mid-edit whose cached end pair is stale was answered against its untrimmed surface and is now answered against its trims. The bar into the post with every cached pair stale gave `[false, true, true, true, false, false]` and gives `[true, true, true, true, false, false]`, the answer with editing off. Flag off: unchanged.
- **Regression tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/brep_query_cache_lifetime.rs`: `an_open_loop_mid_edit_is_refused_naming_its_face`, `a_stale_edge_end_pair_mid_edit_still_closes_its_faces`.
