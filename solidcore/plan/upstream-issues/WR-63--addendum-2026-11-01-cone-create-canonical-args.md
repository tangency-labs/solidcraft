#### WR-63 (update). Opt-in preservation of `bInsideOut` and rejection of non-positive `dHeight` in `SmCone::CreateCanonical`

- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmCone.cpp:341-348`, `SmCone::CreateCanonical(crContext, crOrigin, dBotRadius, dSemiAngleDeg, rpNewCone, pOptHeight, bSwapUV, bInsideOut)` (def. `:308`); and `source/SMLib/src/SmCone.cpp:392-400`, `SmCone::CreateCanonical(crContext, crOrigin, dBotRadius, dTopRadius, dHeight, rpNewCone, bSwapUV, bInsideOut)` (def. `:375`). Both defects remain in the pinned `v1.0.1` (`3fd5b95`) source and are compared by difftest fixtures `canonical_semi_with_height_inside_is_mngc`, `canonical_height_zero_top_is_semi`, and `canonical_height_negative_top_is_semi` (`surface.cone_p1`).
- **Rust port:** `Kernel::fixes.cone_create_canonical_preserves_inside_out_and_checks_height` (off by default, #2207):
  - In `Cone::create_canonical_with_semi_angle` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/cone/p1.rs`), when `height` is `Some(h)`, passes `(inside_out, false)` for `(inside_out, make_nurb_gen_curve)` to `Cone::new_bounded` instead of upstream's `(false, inside_out)`, matching the bounded branch of `Cone::create_canonical` (`SmCone.cpp:414-416`).
  - In `Cone::create_canonical` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/cone/p1.rs`), when `height <= 0.0`, returns `Err(SolidError::InvalidInput { .. })` (`SM_ERR_INVALID_INPUT`) instead of calling `Cone::new_unbounded` with `top_radius` in place of `semi_angle_deg`.
- **Regression:** `wr63_semi_angle_with_height_preserves_inside_out_under_opt_in`, `wr63_two_radii_non_positive_height_rejected_under_opt_in`, and `wr63_valid_cones_and_flag_lookup_match_across_modes` in `crates/solidcore/src/surface/cone/p1.rs` verify default C++ parity vs opt-in behavior across `(swap_uv, inside_out)` combinations, NURBS orientation/evaluation, equivalence with the two-radius frustum constructor, rejection of zero and negative heights, and unchanged behavior for `None`-height and positive-height cones.
- **Scope:** Only the bounded `Some(h)` branch of `Cone::create_canonical_with_semi_angle` (when `inside_out` is `true`) and the `height <= 0.0` branch of `Cone::create_canonical` change under the opt-in flag. Default difftest inputs and tolerances are unchanged.
