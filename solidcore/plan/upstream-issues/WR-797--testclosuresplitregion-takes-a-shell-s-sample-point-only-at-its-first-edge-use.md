#### WR-797. `TestClosureSplitRegion` takes a shell's sample point only at its first edge use

- **Severity:** Wrong refusal (a valid operation fails)
- **Area:** Region nesting, `topology.brep_p6`
- **Location:** the shell nesting at the end of `SmBrep::TestClosureSplitRegion` in `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp`: `SER(pEU->EvaluateBinormalStepOff(pE->GetInterval().Evaluate(0.4567),dStepDist,sPnt,sBinVec))` with `pEU` the first edge use of `pShell->GetFirstFaceuse()` and `dStepDist = 20.0 * pE->GetTolerance()`.
- **What happens:** when a split closes a shell, every other shell of the old region is given its region by a ray fired at the inner shell from one point of it ("Now sPnt = point on the current shell"). The point is taken 20 edge tolerances into the shell's first face use from its first edge use. Where that face is narrower than the step at that edge, `EvaluateBinormalStepOff` fails (the step leaves the face's UV domain) and `SER` returns the failure, although every other face of the shell has such a point. The whole operation fails on the order the shell's faces are stored in.
- **Trigger:** a 10 mm cube whose edges around the first face use of each shell have a tolerance of 1 mm (a step of 20 mm across a 10 mm face), united with a 1 mm cube 5 m away: the union fails with status 1001 when the far cube's shell closes. Found on a body read from a STEP file: at the first edge use of its first face use the edge tolerance is 0.00217 mm, the step 0.0434 mm, and the step moves v by 0.061 on a face whose UV domain is 0.001 wide in v. Any body joined beside it, touching nothing, failed the same way.
- **How found:** a 1 mm box joined 5 m from an imported STEP body (CADGenBench edit input 250) failed with "the stepped-off point does not lie on the face".
- **Confirmed:** no (read at 3fd5b95 `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp:28704-28710`); the port, which followed the C++ here, fails on the trigger.
- **Rust port:** unconditional. `shell_point` in `crates/solidcore/src/topology/brep/p6.rs` takes the point at the first edge use of the shell, in stored order (face uses, then each one's edge uses), whose step of 20 edge tolerances stays in its face's UV domain. The first candidate is upstream's edge use, so a shell that had a point keeps the same one. A step that leaves the face is a value of `evaluate_binormal_step_off_or_left_face` (`topology/edgeuse/p2.rs`); every other error propagates. A shell with no such edge use is refused with the first edge use's error, as before. `nest_closed_shells_as_cavities` takes its points through the same function. Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr_797_far_union_beside_a_narrow_first_face.rs`.
