#### WR-697 (update, 2026-11-04). The End at a smooth vertex caps a blend that, run on, leaves the part
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Fillets (corners)
- **Location:** the fork's `source/SMLib/src/SmBlendCornerEnd.cpp`, `SmBlendCorner::EndAtSmoothVertex` (tangency-labs/smlib `41a39ec`, smlib#124, with smlib#130's `a763ac1` and `9822462`).
- **What happens:** a blended edge E that runs on tangentially at its vertex V into an unblended edge E' of its convexity along one of its faces is made the End at a smooth vertex: the blend is cut by the plane through V square to E, and a cap face meets the round and the part at 90 degrees. That is right where the blend run on past V would go on cutting (or filling) along E' (a rounded corner arc running on into a straight side, tangency-labs/solidcore#2176; one face round V, #453's rim). It is wrong where E' turns into the shared face away from E continued, so the part falls away from the blend run on: E' an earlier round's end arc, or the arc of a round between two faces of a corner. There the round runs on until it leaves the part, which the End on the end face (the pinned Nx1 corner's construction) builds, as before smlib#124. The fork's 9822462 avoids the cap only where the caller names the earlier round's faces (`AddEarlierPassFace`); a round made by another call, or a caller that names none, is still capped.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's rig and tests at its pin bump to 5d2635d36 (tangency-labs/solidcraft#11875, tangency-labs/solidcore#2656): ladder 07-fillets/012 (a variable round running into an upright's round: 9 faces, 47770.945948 against the closed form 47755.125926 and 8 faces), fillets/1207 (7957.723438 against 7957.505202), ladder 021, fillets/5158 and fillets/1080 in two passes, and SolidCraft's tests `fillet_takes_exactly_the_picked_edges` and `fillet_into_an_earlier_round`.
- **How found:** SolidCraft's bisection of its pin bump to #2364 (845bdb4e0).
- **Confirmed:** yes, in solidcore with SolidCraft's calls and fixes (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2656_blend_regressions.rs`); the fork has the same `EndAtSmoothVertex` test (#2364's port matched it).
- **Rust port:** under the same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner`, no new flag (it restores the results built before #2364). Solidcore-native, no fork commit. `end_at_smooth_vertex` takes no edge into which the blend run on leaves the part (`run_on_leaves_the_part`, `blend/blend_corner/end.rs`): with A the two edges' shared face and `d` A's binormal into A at V, each edge's height along `d` past V is `c2 x^2 + c3 x^3` from its derivatives there (E continued past V), taken at `x` the shorter of the two edges' chords from V against A's zone tolerance. Where the second orders' difference there exceeds both third orders it decides (E' turns away where its height exceeds E's); otherwise E''s own third order does (a curvature-continuous round's end curve leaves its face with almost no curvature, then turns; E's third order says how E turns into itself, not past V, so a curvature-continuous E meeting a straight E' keeps its cap: wr698's round on the reopened round). One face round V, E' along E continued, or E' straight past a curved E keep the cap. Values with SolidCraft's fixes: ladder 012 8 faces, 47755.125876 (closed form 47755.125926); 1207 8 faces, 7957.505246 (7957.505202); ladder 021 11 faces, 24 edges, 15 vertices, 47589.770574 (SolidCraft's golden 47589.770467); 5158 in two passes without `AddEarlierPassFace` 8 faces, 1681.903765 (1681.903758); 1080 the same 13 faces, 10198.718249 (the fork's two passes with `AddEarlierPassFace`); the curvature-continuous side round run into the front round refused, as before #2364, not capped. #2176's corner arcs and #453's rim keep their caps and values. Two earlier expectations move to the run-on: #2176's tab side and top (straight edges running on into the corner arcs, which turn into the top face) take off 0.0317159189 more past each such end, the closed form (the round's section cut by the arc's wall, integrated past the vertex), measured within 1.5e-6; and WR-512's knife-edge plate's arc round runs on into the front round's end arc, as the pinned C++ with the WR-512 line builds it (9531.3100142533 to 1e-8; refused without that line, as pinned), where the fork's End capped it at 9532.2987121741.
- **Not changed:** SolidCraft's every-block-cylinder-tangent-concave-arc-r2-partial-chain picks one of the two pieces the cylinder's seam splits the concave arc into; that piece's round is capped at the seam vertex, where the arc runs on between the same two faces (#453's construction).
