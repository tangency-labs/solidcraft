Updates: WR-671

#### WR-671 (update, 2026-11-16). `offset_edges_keep_source_tolerance` is removed

When `MapEdgeuses` matches an offset edge use to its original, the offset edge now always takes the original edge's tolerance and each of its vertices the tolerance of the original vertex it stands for (`keep_source_tolerances`, `crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p1.rs`). Tolerances only grow. The flag is gone. SolidCraft already ran with it on. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::offset_edges_keep_source_tolerance`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and the `issue_2260` list, the setting in `offset_faces.rs`'s `a_rebuilt_vertex_keeps_its_own_tolerance`, and the `keep` parameter of `tests/issue_2111_offset_patch_sheet_gaps.rs` (its flag-off half pinned the WR-671 defect itself).

One change made the routine fit to be the only path:

- **Ends are paired by their UV tangents, not their 3D tangents.** The matched uses may run either way, so the routine decides which offset vertex stands for which original end. It used to read that from the 3D tangents at the midpoint, and raised a `PortInvariant` when they were exactly square. `MapEdgeuses` matches in UV, and every offset face is trimmed by its original's UV curves. On an offset surface, and on a local operation's new surface whatever its own frame, a matched pair's UV tangents are one curve's, so their sign is the pairing. The 3D tangents are not: a local operation's surface turned in its own parameters carries the same UV curve another way in space. At a quarter turn the tangents are square and the operation raised a `PortInvariant`. Past it they point opposite ways, and the ends were paired to the wrong vertices. A pair whose UV tangents are not parallel within the angular tolerance (`|a·b| < cos(ang_tol)|a||b|`, 0.5 degrees) is not one curve and its image, so nothing is raised, as upstream raises nothing. A NaN, infinite or zero tangent is still a `PortInvariant`.
- **Tests:** `run_same_way_takes_parallel_tangents_only`: parallel within 0.5 degrees, either way, pairs; 0.6, 30, 90, 120, 150 and 179.4 degrees do not; NaN, infinite and zero tangents are `PortInvariant`. `a_turned_top_keeps_source_tolerances_on_its_images`: a box's top replaced by a plane tilted 2 degrees, its frame turned 0 and 30 degrees. With the top corner at the origin loosened to 1e-3, only that corner's image takes it. With the top edge on y = 0 loosened, its image takes it and the top edges at y = 10 stay thin. `a_quarter_turned_top_is_refused_typed_not_a_port_invariant`: at 90 degrees the operation is refused as `OffsetFailure::Unsound`, with the box unchanged, where the 3D pairing raised a `PortInvariant`. `issue_2111`'s `kept_source_tolerances_check_clean` now checks that no offset edge or vertex is tighter than the sheet's tightest. Before, it compared areas against the flag-off offset.
- **Observed separately:** a local operation given a surface turned in its own parameters builds the face where that surface carries the UV curves, so its result depends on the given surface's frame. At 30 degrees the top is the face's square turned about the corner at the origin; at 90 and 120 degrees the body is refused as unsound; at 180 degrees it fails check test #34. This is not caused by the pairing and is left as is here.
- **Results:** 14 golden rows move; every other unit matches main. (1) 7 `topology.brep_local_operation` rows (gen0004, gen0025, gen0043, gen0055, gen0065, gen0079, fc0006): only edge and vertex tolerances move, each grown to its source's (2e-5 here) and never shrunk. Every curve, UV trim and surface sample, every vertex point and the graph are byte-identical to main's, and each body's soundness (level 2 check, manifold, volume sign) is unchanged. (2) 3 `offset.offset_executive_p2` cylinder shells (`api_shell_cylinder_top_face`, `_top_extend_merge_in`, `_r5_top_open_tol_1e6`, B-rep tolerance 1e-6 under the cylinder's own 1e-5): the offset keeps 1e-5 and so builds the shell the cylinder at its default tolerance gives, 7 edges and 5 vertices (the rim split at an extra vertex) instead of 6 and 4. Both check clean and have the closed-form volume; test `wr_671_shell_at_a_tighter_brep_tolerance_keeps_the_cylinders_tolerances` (`issue_1453_shelled_cylinder_exact_edges.rs`) asserts 7/5, level 2 clean, no tolerance under the cylinder's, and the volume to 1e-9. The 1.7e-4 volume error #16963 reported no longer occurs (main's integrator fix, #16735). (3) 4 `blend.fillet_executive_p3` fillet-then-shell rows, area, volume and mass unchanged to 1e-9: in `real-box-fillet12-r3-shell-closed-issue-460` and `-open-top-issue-460`, 14 and 16 inner vertices move onto their exact integer corners (main had them up to 7.8e-6 and 1.3e-5 off), and the largest vertex tolerance falls from 6.3e-5 and 8.1e-5 to 4e-5, the filleted box's own. In `real-box-fillet4v-r3-shell-closed` and `real-box-fillet1v-r3-shell-closed`, positions move only at rounding (3.6e-15), and 4 edges go from 2e-5 to 8e-5 and their vertices from about 2.06e-5 to 8.06e-5. That is twice the kept source tolerance (4e-5), where main had twice the B-rep's 1e-5: a later step doubles these edges' tolerance whatever their base, and this change leaves it as it is.
