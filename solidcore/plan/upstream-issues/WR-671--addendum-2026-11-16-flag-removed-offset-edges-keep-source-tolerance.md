Updates: WR-671

#### WR-671 (update, 2026-11-16). `offset_edges_keep_source_tolerance` is removed

When `MapEdgeuses` matches an offset edge use to its original, the offset edge now always takes the original edge's tolerance and each of its vertices the tolerance of the original vertex it stands for (`keep_source_tolerances`, `crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p1.rs`). Tolerances only grow. The flag is gone. SolidCraft already ran with it on. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::offset_edges_keep_source_tolerance`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and the `issue_2260` list, the setting in `offset_faces.rs`'s `a_rebuilt_vertex_keeps_its_own_tolerance`, and the `keep` parameter of `tests/issue_2111_offset_patch_sheet_gaps.rs` (its flag-off half pinned the WR-671 defect itself).

One change made the routine fit to be the only path:

- **The edge is raised first, and ends are paired by where they lie in UV.** The matched uses may run either way, and when curves were split (`MapEdgeuses` then drops the offset use's midpoint onto the original's UV curve) the offset use is a piece of the original. The routine used to pair ends from the 3D tangents at the two midpoints. That raised a `PortInvariant` when they were exactly square (a local operation's surface turned a quarter turn in its own parameters), paired the wrong vertices past a quarter turn, and gave a piece's split vertex the far original end's tolerance. Now the edge always takes the original edge's tolerance, and each offset end, the use's UV point at normalized 0 or 1, takes the tolerance of each original end within the UV tolerance the two uses were matched with. Every offset face is trimmed by its original's UV curves on a surface that shares the original's parameters, so an end that stands for an original end lies on it in UV, whichever way the uses run and however the new surface carries them in space. A piece's split end lies on no original end and takes nothing beyond what the edge gives it. No tangent is read, so a zero or NaN tangent no longer aborts the offset. A NaN distance is near nothing.
- **Over all goldens (283 units, measured with temporary counters):** 11,936 matched pairs; 11,889 matched at the base UV tolerance, 47 at 10x to 1000x, 28 of those by the drop fallback. The old tangent test found no pair non-parallel and no zero or NaN tangent. Ends paired by UV agree with the tangent pairing in 11,552 pairs; 352 more are closed edges on one vertex, where both rules give that vertex both ends' tolerance. In the remaining 32 pairs, all pieces of split edges, the tangent rule gave a split vertex an original end's tolerance and the UV rule does not: 12 pieces touch neither original end and 20 touch one. No golden row moves from this.
- **Tests:** `a_split_piece_keeps_its_original_ends_tolerances_only` (a box edge split in half, run either way: each piece takes the edge's tolerance, its end on an original end that end's, and the split vertex only the edge's), `a_split_circle_piece_keeps_the_rims_tolerances` (a cylinder's closed rim split in half, on the side and on the cap) and `a_use_stopping_at_its_middle_keeps_source_tolerances` (a UV curve whose derivative is zero at its middle) each fail with the tangent pairing. `a_turned_top_keeps_source_tolerances_on_its_images`: a box's top replaced by a plane tilted 2 degrees, its frame turned 0 and 30 degrees. With the top corner at the origin loosened to 1e-3, only that corner's image takes it. With the top edge on y = 0 loosened, its image takes it and the top edges at y = 10 stay thin. `a_quarter_turned_top_is_refused_typed_not_a_port_invariant`: at 90 degrees the operation is refused as `OffsetFailure::Unsound`, with the box unchanged, where the 3D pairing raised a `PortInvariant`. `issue_2111`'s `each_offset_entity_keeps_its_own_sources_tolerance` loosens each of the sheet's 4 vertices and 4 edges to its own tolerance and asserts that each offset vertex and edge carries exactly its own source's, at all 6 distances. `kept_source_tolerances_check_clean` checks that no offset edge or vertex is tighter than the sheet's tightest. Before, it compared areas against the flag-off offset.
- **Observed separately:** a local operation given a surface turned in its own parameters builds the face where that surface carries the UV curves, so its result depends on the given surface's frame. At 30 degrees the top is the face's square turned about the corner at the origin; at 90 and 120 degrees the body is refused as unsound; at 180 degrees it fails check test #34. This is not caused by the pairing and is left as is here.
- **Results:** 14 golden rows move; every other unit matches main. (1) 7 `topology.brep_local_operation` rows (gen0004, gen0025, gen0043, gen0055, gen0065, gen0079, fc0006): only edge and vertex tolerances move, each grown to its source's (2e-5 here) and never shrunk. Every curve, UV trim and surface sample, every vertex point and the graph are byte-identical to main's, and each body's soundness (level 2 check, manifold, volume sign) is unchanged. (2) 3 `offset.offset_executive_p2` cylinder shells (`api_shell_cylinder_top_face`, `_top_extend_merge_in`, `_r5_top_open_tol_1e6`, B-rep tolerance 1e-6 under the cylinder's own 1e-5): the offset keeps 1e-5 and so builds the shell the cylinder at its default tolerance gives, 7 edges and 5 vertices (the rim split at an extra vertex) instead of 6 and 4. Both check clean and have the closed-form volume; test `wr_671_shell_at_a_tighter_brep_tolerance_keeps_the_cylinders_tolerances` (`issue_1453_shelled_cylinder_exact_edges.rs`) asserts 7/5, level 2 clean, no tolerance under the cylinder's, and the volume to 1e-9. The 1.7e-4 volume error #16963 reported no longer occurs (main's integrator fix, #16735). (3) 4 `blend.fillet_executive_p3` fillet-then-shell rows, area, volume and mass unchanged to 1e-9: in `real-box-fillet12-r3-shell-closed-issue-460` and `-open-top-issue-460`, 14 and 16 inner vertices move onto their exact integer corners (main had them up to 7.8e-6 and 1.3e-5 off), and the largest vertex tolerance falls from 6.3e-5 and 8.1e-5 to 4e-5, the filleted box's own. In `real-box-fillet4v-r3-shell-closed` and `real-box-fillet1v-r3-shell-closed`, positions move only at rounding (3.6e-15), and 4 edges go from 2e-5 to 8e-5 and their vertices from about 2.06e-5 to 8.06e-5. That is twice the kept source tolerance (4e-5), where main had twice the B-rep's 1e-5. The doubling is `ReplaceEdgeCurves` (`OffsetGeometryCreation::replace_edge_curves_in`; upstream `SmOffsetGeometryCreation.cpp:840`), reached from the edge-use offsets the shell builds (`edgeuse::build` via `extend_trim_edges`): it passes the edge's own tolerance to `SetUVTrimCurve` as the UV curve's distance from the surface, and `SetUVTrimCurve` raises the edge to `TOL_FACTOR` (2) times that distance. So each replaced edge goes to exactly twice whatever tolerance it has. A temporary trace of the fillet4v row found all 32 raises to 8e-5 from 4e-5 there; the boolean merge (`merge_edge_with`) then gives the merged edges the same 8e-5. That rule is upstream's and applies to every offset alike; this change leaves it as it is.
