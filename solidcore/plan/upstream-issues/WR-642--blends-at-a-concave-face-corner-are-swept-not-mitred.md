#### WR-642. Blends meeting at a face's concave corner are joined by a swept 3-sided face, not mitred
- **Severity:** Wrong result (shape)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletNx2Corner::MakeCornerTopology`, the mixed-convexity branch (`bMixedConvexity && !m_bBevel && dAngleDeg > 5.0`) of the 3-edge case.
- **What happens:** at a vertex of three edges where two filleted edges meet and the common face's corner between them is concave (a pocket's rim at its sharp corner, a boss's root at its sharp corner, the side edge not filleted), the corner is taken as one of mixed convexity: a 3-sided face is swept round the side edge, between ribs straight across from where the rails meet it. Users expect the blends mitred there, as at a corner of uniform convexity. A pocket's rim rounded r 2 on a 60 x 40 x 10 plate (30 x 20 pocket 5 deep) gives 19 faces, 20911.749794 where the mitred closed form is 15 faces, 20911.091414.
- **Trigger:** rounds or chamfers on a pocket's rim or a boss's root loop with sharp corners.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#960). The fork's tangency-labs/smlib@4f06e72 ("rounds meeting at a face's concave corner are mitred") records the owner's ruling of 2026-10-30: such rounds are mitred; a swept corner may become an option later. Its gdb run showed each pocket and boss corner reaching the 3-sided branch with `bMixedConvexity` 1, `m_bBevel` 0, `dAngleDeg` 90.
- **Confirmed:** the port reproduces the pinned behaviour (`3fd5b95`); no C++ run of the fork was made.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_mitre_concave_face_corners` (`blend/fillet_corner/topology.rs`, `make_nx2_corner_topology`): the mixed-convexity 3-sided branch is skipped, so the corner takes the bevel of a corner of uniform convexity (the face vertex is the rails' intersection, `m_bBevel` set); edges meeting at 5 degrees or less still blend.

  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr642_mitred_concave_face_corners.rs`, against the closed forms `V0 -/+ (L r^2 (1 - pi/4) + 4 r^3 (5/3 - pi/2))` (rounds) and `V0 - (50 d^2 + 4/3 d^3)` (the pocket's rim chamfered, L = 100): pocket chamfer d 3 20514.000000073 (closed form 20514), d 5.5 past the floor (a cliff overflow, WR-636) 19265.666666667 (19265.666667); pocket round r 2 15 faces 20911.091276 (20911.091414), r 5 11 faces 20415.560000 (20415.560239); boss root r 4 15 faces 24299.233388 (24299.233158).
