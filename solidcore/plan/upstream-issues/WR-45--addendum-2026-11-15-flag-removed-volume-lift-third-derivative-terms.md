Updates: WR-45

#### WR-45 (update, 2026-11-15). `volume_lift_third_derivative_terms` is removed

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, batch surface2). `lift_third_derivative_srf`, `lift_third_derivative_vol` and `concat_evals` (`surface/volume/p1.rs`) always use the exact multivariable chain-rule terms: `Dxzz` for the `x z^2` outer-derivative term of `Duuv`, `Duvv`, `Duuw`, `Duww`, `Dvvw` and `Dvww`, and `Fuw`/`Fw`, `Fvw`/`Fw` in the volume form's `Duww` and `Dvww`. The `_opt_in` variants and their `exact_terms` argument are gone; `Volume::evaluate` and `Volume::map`, the twist, bend and unbend volumes' `evaluate` and `SrfInVolume::evaluate` call the exact functions. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::volume_lift_third_derivative_terms`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in the kernel rig's `fixes.json` and in `issue_2260_gn_pipe_refined_range_end.rs`, and the upstream formulas.

- **Defaults:** SolidCraft's preset had the flag on, so the app's results don't change. `Kernel::default()` and kernel-free calls of the lift and concatenation functions change: third partials of maps compounded through a non-linear volume, and third partials of surfaces and curves mapped through one.
- **Goldens:** `surface.srf_in_volume` moves 49 rows (third partials of surfaces through twist, bend, unbend and transform chains) and `surface.volume_p1` 14: 2 maps through an oriented unbend volume (`map_unbend_or_n3`, `map_unbend_or_nocomp`, which moved with the flag) and 12 direct calls of the kernel-free lifts and `concat_evals` (`lift_f1_o3`, `lift_f2_o3`, `lift_f2_o3_axes`, `concat_n3` and 8 generated cases), which take the exact terms now that the pinned variants are gone. The srf_in_volume rows are checked against interval forward-mode AD enclosures of the true composite (the WR-46 retirement's harness, judging order 3 against the true partial instead of the pinned lift): all 1290 entries of the 49 rows lie within 7.1e-15 of the scale from the enclosure of the true partial, and the pinned formula's value of each of the 437 moved order-3 entries is disjoint from it (harness, data and output: https://gist.github.com/brett19/6c6256fbcfb2c8a9ca7b8276e829d3e6). The same harness proves the two map rows: every partial of order 3 or less of `Map` lies within 1.4e-16 of the scale from the enclosure of the true composite `Orient(Unbend(InvOrient(p)))`, and the pinned formula, which matches the base rows, is rejected. The goldens test `surface_volume_p1::chain_rule_composition_tests` replays every `Lift` and `Concat` case against the composition of truncated Taylor polynomials built from the same partials, an independent statement of the chain rule. Its tolerance is the derived floating-point bound `2 gamma_128 S / (1 - gamma_128)`, where `S` is the sum of the absolute values of the terms.
- **Tests:** the WR-45 unit tests in `surface/volume/p1.rs` run on the default kernel. Their assertions about upstream's formulas (the legacy values, the `Dxxz - Dxzz` error terms, the default-kernel mismatch) are removed; the exact values are still checked against central differences of the second partials, and `concat_evals` against the lift on full arrays.
