Updates: WR-459

#### WR-459 (update, 2026-11-14). `fillet_cleanup_keeps_target_faces` is removed

`DoFilleting`'s `BooleanPostProcess` removes none of the target's own edges (those it left whole), nor an edge between two of its faces or faces merged from them (`CleanupRelations::kept_edges` and `kept_faces`, filled in `blend/fillet_executive/session/finish.rs`); WR-602's slit check on those stays. That is the only path now, and the flag is gone. SolidCraft already ran with it on. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::fillet_cleanup_keeps_target_faces`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entry in `fixes.json`, its name in the SolidCraft profile list of `issue_2260_gn_pipe_refined_range_end.rs` and every test setting of it.

- **Which path went:** the upstream one, which checks every edge of the target after filleting and merges the target's own faces on coincident surfaces.
- **Results:** 23 golden rows of `blend.fillet_executive_p3` move, each attributed to this flag alone by a scratch build of the base with only its read site turned on. The four `stubs4-delete-edge-sphere_diff_3cylinders-e{1,4}-r{0.1,0.5}` rows change topology and volume. Measured at volume accuracy 1e-9 on the same input (`Fillet/sphere_diff_3cylinders.smb`, target volume 169497.3201417502), upstream's merge gives a body larger than the target after a round of r 0.1 on e1, e4, e9, e13 and e36 (169498.71, +1.39), while e8 and e10 on the same body remove 0.744, and the mirror pair e35 / e36 gives 169496.148 and 169498.291. With the keep the r 0.1 rounds remove material on every edge that builds, and e35 and e36 agree to 5e-11 relative.
- **Tests:** `issue_15059_fillet_cleanup_keeps_a_coplanar_split.rs`, `issue_423_sphere_cut_round_edge_check.rs` and `issue_423_fillet_cleanup_keeps_faces.rs` lose their flag-off halves; the last now compares a round on a target with the slit to the same round on the union's own body.
