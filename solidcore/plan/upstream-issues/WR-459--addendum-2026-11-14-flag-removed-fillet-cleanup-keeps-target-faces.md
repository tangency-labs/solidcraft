Updates: WR-459

#### WR-459 (update, 2026-11-14). `fillet_cleanup_keeps_target_faces` is removed

`DoFilleting`'s `BooleanPostProcess` removes none of the target's own edges (those it left whole), nor an edge between two of its faces or faces merged from them (`CleanupRelations::kept_edges` and `kept_faces`, filled in `blend/fillet_executive/session/finish.rs`); WR-602's slit check on those stays. That is the only path now, and the flag is gone. SolidCraft already ran with it on. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::fillet_cleanup_keeps_target_faces`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entry in `fixes.json`, its name in the SolidCraft profile list of `issue_2260_gn_pipe_refined_range_end.rs` and every test setting of it.

- **Which path went:** the upstream one, which checks every edge of the target after filleting and merges the target's own faces on coincident surfaces.
- **Results:** 36 golden rows of `blend.fillet_executive_p3` move, each attributed to this flag alone by a scratch build of main with only its read site turned on. In 32 of them only the per-edge count of stored UV trim curves moves (209 edges, from 2 to 1 or 0). A full dump of the records shows topology, counts, every vertex, curve and surface sample, and mass properties identical bit for bit. The most likely cause, not traced, is that upstream's clean-up builds an edge's trims while it tests the edge (`IsTopological`), and with the keep it does not test the target's own edges. The four `stubs4-delete-edge-sphere_diff_3cylinders-e{1,4}-r{0.1,0.5}` rows change topology. Measured at volume accuracy 1e-9 on `Fillet/sphere_diff_3cylinders.smb` (target 169499.45870155524, which itself fails the Level 2 check: face test 36 three times, 14033 tests 11, 13 and 15):
  - On e1, the round removes the same volume either way (0.74842236 at r 0.1, 11.35870 at r 0.5, equal to 8e-10 relative). The result keeps the target's edge (V/E/F 28/44/10 instead of 27/42/10). Its failed checks are the same.
  - On e4, which a half turn about y maps onto e1, the keep removes less: 0.43210 against 0.74842 at r 0.1, and 11.04238 against 11.35869 at r 0.5. The result has one more face. Without the keep, e1 and e4 agree to 9e-6, so the e4 results with the keep are wrong. This is an open defect of the keep path, not a proof of the new rows.
  - An earlier note here, that upstream's merge made these rounds add volume, compared against a target volume measured another way. It was wrong and is withdrawn.
- **Tests:** `issue_15059_fillet_cleanup_keeps_a_coplanar_split.rs`, `issue_423_sphere_cut_round_edge_check.rs` and `issue_423_fillet_cleanup_keeps_faces.rs` lose their flag-off halves; the last now compares a round on a target with the slit to the same round on the union's own body.
