Updates: WR-737

#### WR-737 (update, 2026-11-04). The bound calls `eval::collapse`'s `u` and `gamma_k`; `Hom` is not part of it
- **What changed:** `step_within_rounding` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/surface/assert_valid.rs`) carried its own `UNIT_ROUNDOFF` and `gamma`, because `eval::collapse` (solidcore#2629) was not on `main` when the record was written. #2629 is on `main` since 2026-11-04, so both copies are deleted and the bound reads `2.0 * collapse::UNIT * (l0 + l1) + collapse::gamma(3) * (l0 + l1)`.
- **The value is unchanged.** `collapse::UNIT` is `f64::EPSILON * 0.5`; the deleted `UNIT_ROUNDOFF` was `f64::EPSILON / 2.0`, the same `f64` to the bit. Both `gamma` bodies are `k u / (1 - k u)` over that `u`. So the record's figures stand: the per-pair bound is `(2 u + gamma_3) (|P0| + |P1|)`, 1.1e-15 at coordinates of 1 and 1.1e-10 at 1e5, and the two criteria still part at `|P0| + |P1| = 1801.44` mm.
- **`Hom` is not called, and this is the finished state.** The record's closing line ("the bound becomes a call of its shared `collapse::gamma` and `Hom`") holds for the `gamma` half only. A net read off a surface (`GetControlPointNet`) divides the stored homogeneous points, which are exact as stored, so `ex = ew = 0`, and the homogeneous half of `Hom::euclid`'s bound, `(sqrt(3) ex + |P| ew) / (|w| - ew)`, is zero for every point this pass sees; what remains is its `2 u |P|`, which is what the bound already adds per point. `Hom` is also private to `eval::collapse` (`crates/solidcore/src/eval/collapse.rs:154`), so it is not reachable from `surface::surface::assert_valid` at all.
- **Open, for the maintainers.** After this change the kernel still has two copies of `gamma`: `nurbs::math::gamma`, which `geom::certified` re-exports as "the kernel's one copy" (`30b733e88`), and `eval::collapse::gamma`, which `eval::collapse::UNIT` sits beside. `docs/layers.md` ("Two implementations of one job are a bug") wants one; which one survives is a maintainer decision (`docs/layers.md` section 5), so this change only removes the third copy, the one in `assert_valid`.
