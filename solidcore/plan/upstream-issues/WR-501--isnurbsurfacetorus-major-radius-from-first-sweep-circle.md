#### WR-501. `SmTorus::IsNurbSurfaceTorus` measures the major radius from the first sweep circle, not the torus origin
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a torus found in a NURB has the wrong major radius)
- **Area:** Surfaces / torus
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmTorus.cpp`, `SmTorus::IsNurbSurfaceTorus`: `sMajorVec = pOtherFrame->GetOriginRef() - pRefFrame->GetOriginRef()` is taken before `sTorusOrigin`, the torus origin (the minor circle's centre projected onto the common line), is computed.
- **What happens:** the major radius runs from the centre of the NURB's first sweep circle (its first constant-v iso curve). That centre is the torus origin only when the circle lies on the equator. A sweep circle at tube angle v sits r sin v along the axis from the origin, so the radius comes out sqrt(R^2 + (r sin v)^2) instead of R. A tube that starts at the bottom (v = -90 degrees) gives R 13.1934, r 1.25 as 13.2525.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcore#1470: a torus NURB, R 13.1934, r 1.25, about 80 mm from the origin, whose NURB runs once round its tube starting at -90 degrees.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (the fork, tangency-labs/smlib@6136a04), reported in tangency-labs/solidcore#1470.
- **Confirmed:** no. The pinned C++ is not built on this machine, so the fork's commit is the evidence. The port reproduces it with the opt-in off: `issue_1470_1471_tube_turn_from_bottom_is_found_with_its_radii` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/torus.rs`) finds 13.2524829 for R 13.1934.
- **Fix (fork):** tangency-labs/smlib@6136a04 computes the torus origin first, then measures the major radius from the torus origin to the minor circle's centre (`major_vec = other_frame.origin() - torus_origin`), and checks the positive radii after it.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.torus_major_radius_from_origin` (default `false`, upstream's behaviour) in `Torus::is_nurb_surface_torus` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/torus.rs`). With the flag on, the major vector used for the minor circle's orientation is the new one too, as the fork's variable replacement does; the inside-out test is unaffected, since the two vectors differ only along the torus axis, which drops out of `torus_z . (other_z x major_vec)`. Test: `issue_1470_1471_tube_turn_from_bottom_is_found_with_its_radii`, which asserts both radii with the flag on and upstream's 13.2525 with it off.
