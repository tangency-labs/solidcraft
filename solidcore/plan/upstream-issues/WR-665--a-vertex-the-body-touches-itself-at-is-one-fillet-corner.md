#### WR-665. Blends at a vertex the body touches itself at make one corner of edges from different fans
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a valid blend refused)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletCorner::Create` (the two-edge case), reached from `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletExecutive.cpp`, `SmFilletExecutive::SetFilletParameters` / `DoFilleting`.
- **What happens:** where the body touches itself at a point (two cubes sharing a corner; the up and down halves of squares drawn edge to edge, each missing a quadrant on opposite sides), the edge uses at the vertex form several fans of faces that share no edge. Corners are keyed by vertex and work on one fan of faces round it: `Create` walks one fan's faces but finds the blended edges of every fan, and builds an Nx2 corner of edges that share no face. On the cube [-10, 10]^3 less the octants [0, 10]^3 and [-10, 0]^3, chamfering the two concave edges on the line x = y = 0 (one in each fan) with leg 1 fails with status 1001 (fillet error 3, corner). The expected result is each chamfer ending on its own fan's end face, V = 6000 + 10.
- **Trigger:** blends of edges of more than one fan at a non-manifold vertex.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#960), rig `fillets/cov-chamfer-concave-edge-through-up-down-joined-squares`. The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@4ecfbab (`SmBrep::SplitVertexByFans`, `JoinVertexFans`) and @e418750 (`SetFilletParameters` splits each filleted edge's vertex into one vertex per fan, the manifold form of the same body; `DoFilleting` joins the vertices still at a split point after the blends, whether they built or not; `Create` builds no two-edge corner where an edge not blended there is a knife edge, `SmFilletSolver::IsKnifeEdge`).
- **Confirmed:** the port reproduces the failure on the pinned behaviour (`3fd5b95`); no C++ run of the fork was made.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_corner_per_vertex_fan`: `crate::topology::brep::vertex_fans` (`split_vertex_by_fans`, `join_vertex_fans`), the split in `FilletExecutive::set_fillet_parameters` (`FilletParametersAccess::parameters_split_blended_vertex_fans`, live on `FilletSession`), the join in `FilletSession::do_filleting`, the knife-edge test in `FilletCorner::create_with_solver_lookup` (`blend::fillet_solver::p5::is_knife_edge`, over a port of `SmEdgeuse::CalcSectorAngle`, `topology::edgeuse::p3::calc_sector_angle`, which no unit owned).

  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr665_corner_per_vertex_fan.rs`: the origin splits into two vertices of three edges, the same solid (6000), and joins back; a box corner stays one vertex; the two axis chamfers fail with code 3 without the flag and give 6010.000000000000 with it; one axis chamfer alone gives 6005 either way; a blend that fails after the split (leg 30) leaves the origin one vertex of six edges again; on WR-570's knife-edge part the knife edge is one and the two cap edges at its end make an Nx2 corner without the flag, none with it.
