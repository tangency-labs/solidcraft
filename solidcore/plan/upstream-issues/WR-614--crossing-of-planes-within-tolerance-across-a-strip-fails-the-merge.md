#### WR-614. The crossing line of two planes within tolerance of each other across a narrow strip fails the merge
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a boolean of valid bodies fails with `SM_ERR`)
- **Area:** Booleans
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmTopologyIntersector.cpp`, `SmTopologyIntersector::IntersectInsertRelate` (def. `:1092`), which resolves (`ResolveIntersections`, def. `:2251`) every face/face proto edge it found; `source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp`, `SmProtoVertex::GetFinalVertex` (def. `:2143`); the failure is reported by `SmMerge::MakeFaceuse` (`source/SMLib/src/SmMerge.cpp`, `SER(SM_ERR)` when the Brep mate of an edge use's vertex is neither end of the Brep edge, `:7077`).
- **What happens:** two planar faces crossing at a small angle meet in a line fixed only to `tol / sin(angle)`. Where an edge of one face lies on the other face within tolerance, parallel to the crossing, the faces are within tolerance of each other across the strip between them, yet the crossing still becomes an edge definition. In the rebuilt cubes below, `IntersectTopology` combines it into the proto edge of B's corner edge lying on A's face, about 1e-4 away: the imprint then ties the crossing on A's face to B's edge, whose end vertices are not the crossing's. In the issue's imported bodies, the crossing's end proto vertices (capture distance 0.01) hold two correctly classified vertex definitions, one on either side of it; `GetFinalVertex` picks, per body, the one nearest and connected to that body's source face, and `SetRelationships` relates vertices only within one definition. Either way `MakeFaceuse` finds the edge's vertex mates inconsistent and fails. ProtoFaces only detect coincident surfaces, never non-parallel planes coincident along a strip.
- **Trigger:** two 4 mm cubes, B turned 5.7 deg about z, whose faces (A's y = 20, B's F1) cross at x = 1.99995 and stay within 5e-6 of each other across x in [1.9999, 2], overlapping by about 1.0e-4 mm (the issue's imported bodies, intersection tolerance 5e-6): Intersect returns `SM_ERR` (1001); both bodies pass the B-rep check. Rebuilt from primitives at the default tolerances, A `[-2, 2] x [16, 20] x [0, 4]` and B turned atan(0.1) about z within 1e-5 of A's face across x in [1.9998, 2], the union fails with `SM_ERR` from `MakeFaceuse`. The same cubes within 5e-6 give WR-619 instead.
- **How found:** reported in tangency-labs/solidcore#1794 (SolidCraft, from the fork's batch-4 bodies). No fix in the fork (tangency-labs/smlib main and the batch branches) or upstream.
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the C++ build: `boolean.api_brep` fixture `fixtures/boolean.api_brep/wr614_coincident_strip.json`, cases `wr614_strip_union_d1e-05` (`SmApiBooleanUnion`) and `wr614_strip_measured_union_d1e-05` (`SmApiBooleanWithOptions`, post-processing on), fail with the same status on both sides.
- **Workaround:** none found. Post-processing does not help: `SmApiBooleanUnion` already merges with `bBooleanPostProcess` TRUE (the `SmMerge` constructor's default), so the plain and the options path are the same merge and both fail.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream. A rule that drops the crossing's edge definition was tried and dropped: it turned the `SM_ERR` into the region-less union of WR-619.
