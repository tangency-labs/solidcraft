#### WR-587. `SmCurve::ComputeCrvsNormal` reverses a concave loop's normal, so an outward draft leans inward
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Curves and sweeps
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmCurve.cpp:743`, `SmCurve::ComputeCrvsNormal`; called by `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateTaperExtrude` when deriving the profile normal.
- **What happens:** the sum of cross products of consecutive chords weights each turn by the lengths of its adjacent chords. Long chords meeting at reflex corners can outweigh the convex corners of a counterclockwise planar loop. The returned normal then points clockwise; the taper offsets toward the opposite side, exchanging inward and outward volumes.
- **Trigger:** the counterclockwise plus with vertices `(-0.5,-3.5), (0.5,-3.5), (0.5,-0.5), (3.5,-0.5), (3.5,0.5), (0.5,0.5), (0.5,3.5), (-0.5,3.5), (-0.5,0.5), (-3.5,0.5), (-3.5,-0.5), (-0.5,-0.5)` in the XY plane. Eight convex turns contribute +24 and four reflex turns -36, so the unit normal is `(0,0,-1)` even though the signed area is +13.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft in tangency-labs/solidcore#1776 (a drafted text's letter "t"); reproduced in the Rust port and in a standalone program calling the pinned C++ library's `SmCurve::ComputeCrvsNormal` on twelve actual `SmBSplineCurve::CreateLineSegment` curves.
- **Confirmed:** yes, the v1.0.1 C++ reference (`3fd5b95`, harness key `v1-3fd5b9548d19-726643fb17a52b7d`) returns `SM_SUCCESS` (1000) with normal `(0,0,-1)`. The previous `c9c5979` source contains the same weighting. Differential fixtures `issue1776_plus_normal_{ccw,cw,xz,translated}` retain default behavior for both kernels.
- **Rust port:** default behavior remains pinned C++ parity. Enable `Kernel::fixes.curve_loop_normal_from_area` to sum the sampled polygon's area vector as a triangle fan about its first point, as in the fix supplied in #1776. Two-point curves use their endpoints; other curves use six points at `j/5`, including both endpoints. An open chain closes by its chord. The single-linear-curve error, zero-area fallback to +Z, ignored evaluation statuses and propagation of `Unported` are retained. This uses constant auxiliary space and no recursion.
- **Regression:** `curve::curve::p3::tests` checks both plus orientations, translated/rotated planes, every starting corner with chord subdivision, curved Hermite loops, open chains and degeneracy. `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1776_concave_taper.rs` checks a closed manifold plus drafted 10 degrees in both directions against analytic volumes with linear-extension, curvature-keeping and round corners. No tolerances are loosened; result-changing behavior is opt-in under AGENTS.md section 4b.
