Updates: WR-613

#### WR-613 (update, 2026-11-17). `corner_patch_face_side_twist` is removed

The flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, #18640) and its fix becomes a guarded default. `FilletCorner::create_corner_patch_default_with` (`blend/fillet_corner/p3.rs`) always marks the corner patch's sides that lie on a face of the body. For a four-sided patch with a surface on every side, `corner_blend_4` (`nurbs/b_spline_surface_n_lib/p2.rs`) also builds the face-sided patch, in which `create_coons_srf` gives a corner between a face side and a blend side the blend side's twist, so the patch meets the face exactly. It takes that patch only where every side's seam is **proved** within the largest angle the patch that ships otherwise is proved to reach, so the largest angle over the sides is no larger (one side may read higher than that patch's same side); else, and on any error in the attempt other than an unported call or a cancellation, that patch is kept bit for bit. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::corner_patch_face_side_twist` (its file `src/kernel/fixes/corner_patch_face_side_twist.md`; SolidCraft took it through `UpstreamFixes::all`), its entry in `issue_2260`'s list, and the setting in `issue_1945`.

- **Why a guard:** a bicubic Coons patch has one twist per corner. Where the face side's and the blend side's cross-derivative twists differ, the face-sided patch moves the whole mismatch onto the blend side, whose angle then rises next to that corner. With the flag alone, 3 rows came out farther (#18479).
- **The rule (`face_sided_patch`).** The bar is the patch that ships otherwise (WR-547/WR-713's `make_coons_patch_tangent_to_sides` result, or the bare Coons patch). Its sides' largest proved lower bounds of the normal angle (`query::seam_bound::seam_angle_lower_bound`, the seam check's stations) give `limit`: the largest of them, capped at the seam check's tangent angle. A candidate is taken only where every side's seam, between the candidate and that side's surface along the side's 3D curve as `CreateCornerBlend` was given it, is proved at most `limit` over the whole side (`seam_bound::seam_angle_within`, at most `CERTIFY_PIECES` = 2048 piece bounds a side; past it the candidate is refused). So the candidate's largest angle along its sides is no larger than the bar's. Candidates, in order: the face-sided patch as built; then that patch refined to 8, 16, 32 and 64 spans each way, each with 0 to 3 halvings of the first span along each blend side toward each face-side/blend-side corner (`grade_corner_spans`), its side rows projected by `project_coons_side_rows` with the face sides.
- **The proof (`query::seam_bound`).** An enclosure of the largest acute normal angle along a seam, over the whole curve, not only at stations, in outward-rounded interval arithmetic: the feet of the curve on each surface lie near a correspondence (fact 1, narrowed to the nearest point by Krawczyk steps, 1b); the normal over a box of feet lies in a cone (fact 2); next to a side collapsed to a point the normal is enclosed on the fan's line (fact 3); and along a piece the two sides' normals are enclosed together, `n_1 − σ n_2` with its derivative, so long pieces close where the sides turn together (fact 4).
- **Not taken where a side has no surface** (a triangular corner patch, `corner3x2`, `stubs3`): such a side's seam is not between the patch and a surface the corner is given, so nothing could hold it. An earlier version of #18640 changed the bar there too (an exact apex, G1 to both neighbours); it moved the angle onto that free seam (up to 2.1e-2 rad against main's 3.7e-5 on `stubs3-union-inset-side-step-edges16-22-r1`), so it was dropped. Those rows keep main's patch bit for bit.
- **Tests:** in `nurbs::b_spline_surface_n_lib::p2::tests`: `wr_613_corner_point_goes_into_the_face_sides_plane`, `wr_613_corner_spans_are_graded_along_the_blend_side`, `wr_613_face_sided_patch_is_held_to_the_shipped_patchs_largest_angle`, `wr_613_a_candidate_past_the_certification_budget_is_refused`, `wr_613_an_error_in_the_face_sided_attempt_keeps_the_shipped_patch`; in `query::seam_bound::tests`: two planes, a leaning surface, a right angle, surfaces turning together in long pieces, a triangle up to its apex, the nearest-point narrowing. The goldens module `blend_fillet_executive_p3::end_to_end::wr613_certificates` checks each of the 24 rows with corner-patch sides on plane faces against main's figures (recorded at `30f970e49e` by its `record_main_figures`), one test per row.
- **Results:** 6 rows of `blend.fillet_executive_p3` move, with unchanged topology and counts: `curve-sih-box_diff_cyl-all-edges-r0.1`, `-r0.25`, `-r0.5`, `-r1.0`, `-step2` and `source-file-box_diff_cyl-all-edges`. Each is a valid closed manifold solid (Level 2). On each face-sided patch the face sides hold the tangency certificate (the next control row in the plane to 9.1e-13, the net on one side of it), and every seam of the patch is proved at most a bound below main's patch's largest proved lower bound over the same seams (for example 6.0834e-3 against 6.1026e-3 at r0.1, 8.3783e-3 against 8.3855e-3 at r1.0): its largest angle along its seams is no larger than main's patch's (PROVEN, `wr613_certificates`). The volume lies in its certified enclosure, a check of the mass integrator. The other 18 rows (`corner3x2` 12, `stubs3` 5, `stubs4-Heart-all-edges-r0.1`) keep main's patches, with volume, next-row distance and every seam's reading equal to main's bit for bit. `stubs4-Heart` is four-sided, but one side's proof does not close: the piece bound stops shrinking at about 1.52e-3 against a limit of 1.519e-3 while the proved lower bound there is 5.3e-5; its floor is the foot term near the r0.1 round (gap about 8.6e-7), not the piece.
- **Follow-ups:** a triangular corner whose free seam is held as well (an exact apex with the free seam in the rule); a nearest-point foot term in fact 4, which would let `stubs4-Heart` close; a second-order cone along the seam next to an apex (a WIP that moved the `corner3x2` face-side failure from t = 4.8e-7 to 8.1e-3 of the side at a minute a corner, not enough).
