Updates: WR-613

#### WR-613 (update, 2026-11-17). `corner_patch_face_side_twist` is removed

The flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, #18640) and its fix becomes a guarded default. `FilletCorner::create_corner_patch_default_with` (`blend/fillet_corner/p3.rs`) always marks the corner patch's sides that lie on a face of the body. For such a patch `corner_blend_4` (`nurbs/b_spline_surface_n_lib/p2.rs`) also builds the face-sided patch, in which `create_coons_srf` gives a corner between a face side and a blend side the blend side's twist, so the patch meets the face exactly. It takes that patch only where `face_sided_patch` shows it no worse than main's patch at every station of every side; otherwise main's patch is kept bit for bit. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::corner_patch_face_side_twist`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, and the setting in `issue_1945`.

- **Why a guard:** a bicubic Coons patch has one twist per corner. Where the face side's and the blend side's cross-derivative twists differ, the face-sided patch moves the whole mismatch onto the blend side, whose angle then rises next to that corner. With the flag alone, 3 rows came out farther (#18479), and on `corner3x2`, `stubs3` and `Heart` rows the blend side rose by up to 3.1e-7 rad next to the corner while its largest angle (at another corner) stayed the same. A per-side maximum hides such a rise, so the guard compares station by station.
- **The guard (`face_sided_patch`):** candidates, in order: the face-sided patch as built; then that patch refined to 8, 16, 32 and 64 spans each way (WR-547's `COONS_REFINEMENTS`), each with 0 to 3 halvings of the first span along each blend side toward each face-side/blend-side corner (`grade_corner_spans`), its side rows projected by `project_coons_side_rows` with the face sides (a control point next to exactly one face side and one blend side goes into the face side's plane alone). Every candidate and main's patch (WR-547/WR-713's `make_coons_patch_tangent_to_sides` result, or the bare Coons patch where it gives none) are measured at the same stations (`coons_side_frames`), each angle with its rounding bound (`coons_side_angle`). The first candidate whose enclosure at every station main certifies does not lie wholly above main's (`no_worse_at_every_station`) is taken. At most 17 candidates.
- **Tests:** in `nurbs::b_spline_surface_n_lib::p2::tests`, `wr_613_corner_point_goes_into_the_face_sides_plane` (the corner point goes into the marked side's plane, onto the mean plane with no marks or both, and the face move is an exact zero-sum transfer: the gap between the two sides' planes is unchanged by the mean move and changed by `(1 - c) d_f` by the face move), `wr_613_corner_spans_are_graded_along_the_blend_side` and `wr_613_face_sided_patch_is_no_worse_at_every_station` (equal within rounding passes; a rise of 1.35e-8 rad at one station, a station the candidate does not certify, or a NaN fails). The goldens module `blend_fillet_executive_p3::end_to_end::wr613_certificates` checks each of the 24 rows with corner-patch sides on plane faces against main's figures, one test per row.
- **Results:** 3 rows of `blend.fillet_executive_p3` move, with unchanged topology and counts: `curve-sih-box_diff_cyl-all-edges-r0.1`, `-step2` and `source-file-box_diff_cyl-all-edges`. Each is a valid closed manifold solid (Level 2). Each corner-patch side on a plane face holds the tangency certificate: the boundary row and the next row of control points lie in the face's plane to 9.1e-13 (measured 4.4e-15; main 3.8e-11), and the net lies on one side of it. Every seam of those patches is CLOSER or EQUAL, with the rounding of each reading taken at its own angle: the seams' largest normal angles drop from 2.1e-3 to 6.8e-4 rad, from 4.3e-3 to 6.2e-4 and from 6.1e-3 to 2.8e-4 (MEASURED), and the seams at 0 stay at 0. The volume lies in its certified enclosure, which checks the mass integrator, not which body is closer (PROVEN, `wr613_certificates`).
  - 21 rows keep main's patch, with volume, next-row distance and every seam's angle equal to main's bit for bit (PROVEN, `wr613_certificates`). No candidate clears every station on them: `box_diff_cyl` r0.25, r0.5 and r1.0 (3), `corner3x2` (12), `stubs3` (5) and `stubs4-Heart` (1). On the last 18, the face side and the blend side need different twists at the corner. Meeting both needs a corner with twist compatibility (a Gregory-type patch), which is follow-up work.
