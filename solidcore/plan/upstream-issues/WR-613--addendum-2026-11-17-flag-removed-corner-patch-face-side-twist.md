Updates: WR-613

#### WR-613 (update, 2026-11-17). `corner_patch_face_side_twist` is removed

The flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, #18640) and its fix becomes a guarded default. `FilletCorner::create_corner_patch_default_with` (`blend/fillet_corner/p3.rs`) always marks the corner patch's sides that lie on a face of the body. For such a patch `corner_blend_4` (`nurbs/b_spline_surface_n_lib/p2.rs`) also builds the face-sided patch, in which `create_coons_srf` gives a corner between a face side and a blend side the blend side's twist, so the patch meets the face exactly. It takes that patch only where `face_sided_patch` reads every side's seam no farther from tangent than on the patch that ships otherwise; otherwise main's patch is kept bit for bit. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::corner_patch_face_side_twist`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, and the setting in `issue_1945`.

- **Why a guard:** a bicubic Coons patch has one twist per corner. Where the face side's and the blend side's cross-derivative twists differ, the face-sided patch moves the whole mismatch onto the blend side, whose angle then rises next to that corner. With the flag alone, 3 rows came out farther (#18479).
- **The guard (`face_sided_patch`) reads seams with the seam check's own evaluator.** `query::continuity::seam_normal_angle` is the one function both the guard and the certificates call: `continuity_over_surfaces` over `SEAM_PARTS` (257 even stations over the whole side and 129 over each end's 1/32), the metric `min(θ, π - θ)` of the normal angle, at least 257 stations, every angle finite. The comparison is `seam_angle_farther` (farther by more than the two readings' rounding, `seam_angle_rounding`), the test the certificates apply. The guard reads each side between the patch and the side's surface along the side's 3D curve as `CreateCornerBlend` was given it, which is the curve the body's edge runs along. It reads the candidate and the patch that ships otherwise (WR-547/WR-713's `make_coons_patch_tangent_to_sides` result, or the bare Coons patch where that gives none). It takes the first candidate that is not farther on any side, and refuses one where either patch reads nothing on a side with a surface. Candidates, in order: the face-sided patch as built; then that patch refined to 8, 16, 32 and 64 spans each way (WR-547's `COONS_REFINEMENTS`), each with 0 to 3 halvings of the first span along each blend side toward each face-side/blend-side corner (`grade_corner_spans`), its side rows projected by `project_coons_side_rows` with the face sides. At most 17 candidates.
- **Why the given curve and not the boundary the patch interpolates:** that boundary is `create_non_rational_curve`'s approximation of the given curve. Read along it, `corner3x2`'s blend side read 5.92e-3 rad on both patches, which hid the candidate's rise next to the corner. The body's edge, along the given curve, reads main's 3.836e-3 and the candidate's 3.2e-7 above it. With the approximation, 17 rows (`corner3x2` 12, `stubs3` 5) came out FARTHER on one seam each. With the given curve the guard refuses those candidates, and those rows keep main's patch bit for bit (MEASURED, #18640).
- **Tests:** in `nurbs::b_spline_surface_n_lib::p2::tests`:
  - `wr_613_corner_point_goes_into_the_face_sides_plane`: the corner point goes into the marked side's plane, or onto the mean plane with no marks or both; the face move is an exact zero-sum transfer (the gap between the two sides' planes is unchanged by the mean move and changed by `(1 - c) d_f` by the face move).
  - `wr_613_corner_spans_are_graded_along_the_blend_side`.
  - `wr_613_face_sided_patch_is_no_farther_than_the_fallback`: equal within rounding passes; a rise of 1.35e-8 rad fails; #16729's crease corner, whose blend side reads below the fallback's, passes; a side either patch reads nothing on fails; a NaN fails.

  The goldens module `blend_fillet_executive_p3::end_to_end::wr613_certificates` checks each of the 24 rows with corner-patch sides on plane faces against main's figures, one test per row. Its seam reading is `query::continuity::edge_seam_normal_angle`, the same `seam_normal_angle` along the body's edge.
- **Results:** 7 rows of `blend.fillet_executive_p3` move, with unchanged topology and counts: `curve-sih-box_diff_cyl-all-edges-r0.1`, `-r0.25`, `-r0.5`, `-r1.0`, `-step2`, `source-file-box_diff_cyl-all-edges` and `stubs4-Heart-all-edges-r0.1`. Each is a valid closed manifold solid (Level 2). Each corner-patch side on a plane face holds the tangency certificate: the boundary row and the next row of control points lie in the face's plane to 9.1e-13, and the net lies on one side of it. Every seam of those patches is CLOSER or EQUAL against main's, with the rounding of each reading taken at its own angle, and none is FARTHER (MEASURED; the comparison is PROVEN by `wr613_certificates`). The volume lies in its certified enclosure, which checks the mass integrator, not which body is closer.
  - 17 rows keep main's patch, with volume, next-row distance and every seam's angle equal to main's bit for bit (PROVEN, `wr613_certificates`): `corner3x2` (12) and `stubs3` (5). On them, the face side and the blend side need different twists at the corner. Meeting both needs a corner with twist compatibility (a Gregory-type patch), which is follow-up work.
