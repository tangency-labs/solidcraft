Updates: WR-613

#### WR-613 (update, 2026-11-17). `corner_patch_face_side_twist` is removed

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, #18640). `FilletCorner::create_corner_patch_default_with` (`blend/fillet_corner/p3.rs`) always marks the corner patch's sides that lie on a face of the body, and `create_coons_srf` gives a corner between a face side and a blend side the blend side's twist. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::corner_patch_face_side_twist`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, and the setting in `issue_1945`.

- **Interaction fixed first:** with the flag alone, 3 rows came out farther (#18479): the corner patches of `curve-sih-box_diff_cyl-all-edges-r0.25`, `-r0.5` and `-r1.0` crossed the plane face they meet by twice main's amount. Those patches are refined by WR-547's `make_coons_patch_tangent_to_sides`, whose `project_coons_side_rows` moved the control point next to two sides onto the mean of the two sides' tangent planes. With the blend side's twist at that corner, the blend side's plane there stands off the face's by twice as much as with the mean twist, so the mean plane pulled the point twice as far off the face. `project_coons_side_rows` now takes the face sides too and moves a point next to exactly one face side and one blend side into the face side's plane alone; a point next to two sides of one kind still goes onto their mean plane. The rule is the same as the twist's: the patch meets the body's face exactly and the blend side takes the miss. The unrefined patch (r 0.1) needed no change.
- **Tests:** `nurbs::b_spline_surface_n_lib::p2::tests::wr_613_corner_point_goes_into_the_face_sides_plane` builds a bicubic patch next to a plane face side and a tilted blend side: the corner point goes into the marked side's plane (either side), and onto the mean plane with no marks or both. The goldens module `blend_fillet_executive_p3::end_to_end::wr613_certificates` replays each moved row (below).
- **Results:** 24 rows of `blend.fillet_executive_p3` move, all with unchanged topology and counts. Every one is a valid closed manifold solid (Level 2), its corner patches tangent to the plane faces they meet all along the shared side (the boundary row and the next row of control points lie in the face's plane to 1e-12) and not crossing them (the net lies on one side), and its volume inside a certified enclosure (PROVEN, `wr613_certificates`, one test per row). Before the change those sides missed tangency by up to 6.2e-3 rad (`corner3x2`, `stubs3`), 1.7e-3 (`Heart`) and 2e-9 (`box_diff_cyl`), and the refined `box_diff_cyl` r0.25/r0.5/r1.0 patches crossed plane face 11 (MEASURED).
  - `box_diff_cyl` (6 rows): the blend sides' miss is unchanged (MEASURED), so every side is as close or closer: CLOSER.
  - `corner3x2` (12), `stubs3` (5), `stubs4-Heart` (1): the face side is exact, and the blend side takes the corner's whole twist mismatch, its largest kink going from 4.9e-3 to 9.8e-3 rad (`corner3x2`/`stubs3`) and from 6.8e-4 to 3.5e-3 rad (`Heart`; MEASURED), still within the 1 degree that WR-547's refinement targets. A bicubic Coons patch with one twist per corner cannot meet two sides whose cross-derivative twists differ; this is the trade WR-613 chooses, putting the exact join on the body's face and the miss on the toleranced join between blend surfaces (which already meet only to G0 within 1e-5).
