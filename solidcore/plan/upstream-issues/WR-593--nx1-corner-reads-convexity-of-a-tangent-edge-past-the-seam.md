#### WR-593. An Nx1 fillet corner reads a convexity off a tangent edge past the tangent side edge, so a residue fails the round (status 1001)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `SmFilletNx1Corner::MakeCornerTopology` in `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, in the tangent side-edge skip. The skip is the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@d5ebcb3 code and is not in pinned v1.0.1; the Rust port carries it behind WR-457.
- **What happens:** at each end of the round, the skip passes the tangent side edge round the vertex and takes the edge it reaches as the corner's edge only when `same_convexity_edges_round_vertex()` finds that edge has the filleted edge's convexity. Here the edge reached is itself G1: its faces' normals agree along it, and `IsConvexRadialSector()` on it reads only the sign of rounding in the geometry. With exact dimensions both ends read "same" and the round builds. With a residue of about 1e-10 in the bar's outline, one end reads "opposite" and leaves no corner edge. The multi-neighbour fallback then fails with `SmFilletNx1Corner` fillet error 3 (corner type 8), status 1001.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's test-peg part, rig case `fillets/report-fillet-concave-arc-bar-top-into-cylinder-solver-residues`. A stadium bar's top meets a cylinder of the bar's end radius standing on its round end, and the bar's flat sides run tangent into the cylinder. The concave arc where the top meets the cylinder's wall is rounded r 2, and the sketch solver leaves residues of about 1e-10 mm in the outline.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft. The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@1ff7353 ("fillets: a tangent edge past a tangent side edge is the round's corner edge"), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** yes, in the Rust port with WR-457 on: a bar x 0..20 with radius 5 and height 10, a cylinder 10..20 about x = 20, and the start of the bar's first side line 1e-10 off fails with 1001 (`crates/solidcore/tests/wr_593_nx1_tangent_corner_edge.rs`). This is fork-only code, so the pinned C++ cannot confirm it.
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@1ff7353. An edge reached past the tangent side edge that is itself tangential at the vertex (`sm_CheckTangentialEdgeuseAtVertex`) has no convexity, so it is never of the opposite one. It is taken as the corner's edge, as the exact geometry already has it at both ends.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. `Kernel::fixes.fillet_nx1_tangent_corner_edge`, together with WR-457's `Kernel::fixes.fillet_nx1_tangent_side_edge`, ports the fork's change in `FilletCorner::make_nx1_corner_topology`: the tangential check comes first, and `same_convexity_edges_round_vertex` is the alternative. The new field does nothing without WR-457, since the change lives inside that skip. With both on, the residue case builds the round with Pappus' volume over the half turn, and the exact case is bit-identical with the field on or off.
