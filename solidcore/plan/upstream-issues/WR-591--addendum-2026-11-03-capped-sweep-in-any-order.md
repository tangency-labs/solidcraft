Updates: WR-591

#### WR-591 (addendum 2026-11-03). Through the capped linear sweep the fix makes the cap in every order, but in 9 of 24 orders the closed body gets no solid region

- **Operation level.** The record's 24 orders (the 10 x 6 outline `(0,0) (10,0) (10,6) (0,6) (0,3)` and the triangle `(0,3) (2,3.5) (2,2.5)` as eight line segments: none, the outline or the hole reversed, each of the 8 rotations of the list), swept by the public `create_linear_sweep(curves, +z, 2.0, cap_ends = true)`. Its caps go through `Surface::create_plane_with_3d_curves` and so through `order_curves_into_loops` (#2423). `Kernel::fixes.join_touching_loops` (WR-405) is on in every run, as SolidCraft runs it; only `loops_go_on_round_the_vertex` is toggled. Volume and area by `brep_volume` and `brep_area` at `1e-6`, the check by `check_brep` `Level2`.
- **Prior art:** the fork has this case in one order: tangency-labs/smlib@1f2b0c4, capi test `capi/tests/sweeps.cpp` "extrude plate notched at a corner" (`smlib_extrude` of the same plate and hole, 2 up), which checks volume 118, a clean level 2 check, 10 faces of one loop each and two regions. The 24 orders here add the other 23.
- **Without the fix (the default):** the cap plane is refused in 15 of the 24 orders (`order_curves_into_loops` statuses 1007 and 1001): variant 0 rotations 5-7, variant 1 rotations 4-7, variant 2 all. The capped sweep still returns `Ok`: the eight side faces as an open skin (8 faces, 16 lamina edges), one void region, area 74.246, volume 0, and the `Level2` check reports nothing. In the other 9 orders the plane is one loop of all eight lines.
- **With the fix:** the cap plane is one loop of all eight lines in all 24 orders (`outer_loop_counts = [1]`, `loop_counts = [8]`, enclosed area 59 either way round), and the sweep builds 10 faces, 14 vertices and 24 edges, every edge manifold, area 192.246 (2 x 59 caps and the sides). In 15 orders that is the plate less the hole: two regions, a manifold solid, volume 118.0 (within `4e-14`), `Level2` clean.
- **A second defect, after the loop ordering.** In the other 9 orders, rotations 4, 6 and 7 of every variant, the same closed shell gets **one void region and no solid one**: `is_manifold_solid` is false and `brep_volume` gives 0. `check_brep` `Level2` reports no failure, so the check does not see it. Variant 0 rotation 4 builds the same body without the fix too (its plane is already right there). The cap plane is the same one loop as in the orders that work, and the shell's edges are all manifold. So the order of the loop's curves through the corner (0, 3), which the cap's boundary passes twice, decides whether the shell gets a solid region. The likely place is the region building after the sweep's stitch (`PrimitiveCreation::create_linear_sweep`, `crates/solidcore/src/primitive/primitive_creation/p1/primitives.rs`). The cause is not traced and no fix is proposed here.
- **Reachable only with fork flags.** A loop through a vertex twice comes from `join_touching_loops`, which is not in SMLib v1.0.1. With both flags off the hole is a second outer loop (the WR-405 addendum of 2026-11-02), so this was not compared against the pinned C++. The SMLib fork was not built.
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr591_touching_loops_sweep_any_order.rs`: `wr591_without_the_flag_the_capped_sweep_returns_an_open_skin` (the default's defect, pinned while the flag is opt-in), `wr591_on_cap_plane_is_one_loop_in_every_order`, `wr591_on_sweep_is_the_plate_less_the_hole` (15 orders), and `wr591_on_no_solid_region_known_failing` (the 9 orders, failing once any comes out right).
