#### WR-785. The fillet trace drops a skinning failure, and the round fails later as a corner error
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends / fillets
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp:2093-2467`, `SmFilletSolver::CalcFilletGeom`'s `while (!bFoundAllPoints)` loop, which ignores the status of `DoPointIntersection`; `SmFilletIntersector::FlushCurve`, which sets `SM_FS_SURF_SKINNING_FAILURE` and fails when the fillet surface generator's `CreateSurface` fails.
- **What happens:** `FlushCurve` fills the points' curve parameters before it skins the fillet surface. When the skinning fails (for example, `CreateSkinnedSurface`: "the boundary continuity cannot be met"), the trace finds no missed point and ends with success, so `CalcFilletGeom` returns `SM_SUCCESS` with no fillet surface for that blend. The corner's second pass then finds the rail end unprocessed, and the fillet fails as "Corner Edge Computation Failure" (status 1001), or with `SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER` (1006) where a later stage finds the fillet surface missing, instead of the blend's "Fillet Surface Computation Failure".
- **Trigger:** golden `blend.fillet_executive_p3` case `sweep4-native-crash-sphere_diff_3cylinders-e30-r0.1` with #13891's seam-angle change.
- **How found:** the golden above, tangency-labs/solidcraft#14807.
- **Confirmed:** in the port, by `blend::fillet_solver::p4::tests_c4::trace_fails_with_the_skinning_failure_instead_of_recovering` and `only_a_skinning_failure_ends_the_trace`, and at the executive by `blend::fillet_executive::p3::tests::a_skinning_failure_keeps_its_error_in_the_note_and_as_the_cause`.
- **Rust port:** fixed unconditionally (no flag): `c4_trace` returns the `DoPointIntersection` error when the solver's status is `SurfSkinningFailure` (`c4_skinning_failure` in `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_solver/p4.rs`). The executive then notes the blend's surface failure with what failed: `SM_FILERR_EDGE_PROBLEM`, status `SM_FS_SURF_SKINNING_FAILURE` (not upstream's `SM_FS_SURF_TRACING_FAILURE`), and the comment "Fillet Surface Computation Failure: " followed by the trace's error (`FilletExecutive::record_skinning_failure` in `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_executive/p3.rs`). The executive's `SM_ERR` keeps the trace's error as its cause. Every other `DoPointIntersection` failure only sets the status, as upstream does, and every other failure of a solver's geometry is noted as upstream notes it. The golden case above now fails with `SM_ERR` (1001), the status of the skinning failure, instead of `SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER` (1006), which a later stage returned when it found the blend's fillet surface missing; its topology is unchanged.
