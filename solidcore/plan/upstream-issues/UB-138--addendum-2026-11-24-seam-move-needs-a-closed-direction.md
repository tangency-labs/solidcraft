Updates: UB-138

#### UB-138 (update, 2026-11-24). The Rust port drops a seam move in a direction the surface does not close in
- **Trigger seen:** the `topology.face_p1` rows whose face properties report a crossed or near-miss seam in a direction the surface is not periodic in (the 37 UB-137 rows on `vase_rev.smb`, which is periodic in U but open in V, and 25 more `op9`/`p5` rows on other files). Upstream trusts the properties: it splits the surface there and `JoinSurface`s the open ends. The join does not fail, so the unchecked status is not what goes wrong. The joined surface is up to 11.8 away from the original on `vase_rev.smb`, so the face's edges no longer lie on it. The seam split that follows then merges interval ends onto edge points up to 4.19 away and grows the vertex tolerances to match (#21368). Test #82's limit is 2L = 0.02.
- **Rust port:** `move_seam` now handles a crossed or near-miss seam in a direction only when that direction is closed by one of `HasSeamProblem`'s own tests. The first is the face properties' closure: closed (not False) at C1 or better, the healer's measure to its cross-section tolerance. The second is `Surface::is_periodic` over the surface's natural UV domain. Where both say the surface is open there is no seam to move, so that direction is a no-op and not a refusal. A healer-computed property that calls a direction closed is still honoured. The trigger rows carry made-up face properties: some (`guard_*_c01`, `c11`) say `closed_v = 1` with an undefined closure continuity, so they fail the C1 test. `crates/goldens` `topology_face_p1::tests::seam_move_rows_keep_the_tolerances_the_geometry_needs` replays every moved row and checks four things: the op succeeds; no new kind of `check_brep` failure appears; every edge end lies within its vertex tolerance; and no tolerance exceeds the largest of its old value, the body tolerance and the end gap plus a rounding allowance.
