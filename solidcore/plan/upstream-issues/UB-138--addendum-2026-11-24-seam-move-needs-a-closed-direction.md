Updates: UB-138

#### UB-138 (update, 2026-11-24). The Rust port drops a seam move in a direction the surface does not close in
- **Trigger seen:** the `topology.face_p1` rows whose face properties report a crossed or near-miss seam in a direction the surface is not periodic in (the 37 UB-137 rows on `vase_rev.smb`, which is periodic in U but open in V, and 25 more `op9`/`p5` rows on other files). Upstream trusts the properties: it splits the surface there and `JoinSurface`s the open ends. The join does not fail, so the unchecked status is not what goes wrong. The joined surface is up to 11.8 away from the original on `vase_rev.smb`, so the face's edges no longer lie on it. The seam split that follows then merges interval ends onto edge points up to 4.19 away and grows the vertex tolerances to match (#21368). Test #82's limit is 2L = 0.02.
- **Rust port:** `move_seam` now handles a crossed or near-miss seam in a direction only when `Surface::is_periodic` holds for that direction over the surface's natural UV domain. That is the same test `HasSeamProblem` makes when it gets no face properties. Where the surface is not closed there is no seam to move, so that direction is a no-op and not a refusal. `crates/goldens` `topology_face_p1::tests::seam_move_rows_keep_the_tolerances_the_geometry_needs` replays every moved row. It checks that every edge end lies within its vertex tolerance, that no tolerance exceeds what the geometry needs, and that no new `check_brep` failure appears.
