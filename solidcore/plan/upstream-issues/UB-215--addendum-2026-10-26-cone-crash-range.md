Updates: UB-215

#### UB-215 (addendum, 2026-10-26). Exact crash range of the partial cone
- **Severity:** Crash / undefined behaviour
- **Area:** Topology
- **Location:** as UB-215; the lower bound comes from `SmTopologySweep::SweepEdgeHigh`'s degenerate-surface test (`pNewFaceSurf->IsDegenerateCurve() || pNewFaceSurf->IsDegeneratePoint()`, `SM_ERR_INVALID_INPUT`), which runs before `MakeFaceWithCurves`.
- **What happens:** `CreateCone` over a turn below `359.99999` degrees sweeps each profile line (side, base radius, axis, top radius). The axis line sweeps to no face. Any other line of length `L` sweeps to a face whose surface has `|Su| = L` at every sample, so `SmSurface::IsDegenerateCurve` (3D tolerance `SM_ZONE_TOL_3D / 10 = 1e-6`, or the scaled zero when that is larger) refuses the face for `L` up to `1e-6`. From just above `1e-6` up to the zone tolerance (`1e-5`), `MakeFaceWithCurves` drops both copies of the line and builds the face anyway (instrumented native build: status `SM_SUCCESS` with every slot of `s3DCurves` cleared), and the `pOrigEdgeCurve->GetOwner()` call reads the freed curve.
- **Trigger:** unit size, 90 and 359 degrees: `1007` (`SM_ERR_INVALID_INPUT`) up to `L = 1e-6` exactly, a segfault from `1e-6 + 2e-19` (bisected). Origin `(1000, -700, 300)`: `1007` up to `1.0010000437e-6`, a segfault above it (the scaled zero). Frame of `ub215_partial_cone_apex_top_line_1e-6` (origin about 360 away, 359 degrees): a segfault at exactly `1e-6`, `1007` at `9.99999e-7`, so rounding decides at the boundary. Top, base and side lines behave alike (`9.9e-7` gives `1007`, `1.01e-6` and `2e-6` segfault). A short axis line alone gives `1025`. Small turns also refuse lines in the band: with `L = 1.1e-6`, `3e-6` and `9e-6` the face is refused while `2 L sin(turn / 2) <= 1e-6` (bisected to 6 digits; `1007` from `0.01` degrees), but a turn of `1e-9` degrees segfaults for `L = 2e-6` and `3e-6`. At origins around `1e5` a `2e-6` line gives `1007` (the scaled zero passes the band).
- **Torus:** the minor-circle boundary depends on the major radius and position (major `0.01`: `1007` up to minor radius `1.537e-7`, a segfault from `2e-7`; origin `(1000, -700, 300)`: up to `2.125e-7`; major `0`: `1007` up to at least `5e-7`); minor radius `1.59e-6` returns `1001` without a crash.
- **How found:** native probes of the `primitive.primitive_creation_p2` shim (one process per input), with a scratch build of `SmTopologySweep.cpp` that prints the swept curves, the degenerate tests and the `MakeFaceWithCurves` status. 300 random partial cones with every non-axis line below `0.9999e-6` ran without a crash.
- **Confirmed:** yes. The FFI guard now refuses cone lines from `0.9999e-6` to `1e-5` (non-axis only); fixtures `ub215_degenerate_faces.json` compare 16 inputs below it.
