Updates: WR-345

#### WR-345 (update, 2026-10-31). An offset face's loops winding the other way under an opening are kept as the cavity's floor

The fork's tangency-labs/smlib@1b016ca ("shell: an offset face's loops winding the other way under an opening are the cavity's floor") extends `ResolveCrossingLoopsOfFace`. It is ported under the same opt-in, `Kernel::fixes.offset_resolve_crossing_loops`, in `crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/crossing_loops.rs`.

- **What it changes:** a stretch also notes whether it bounds a region the loops wind round once the other way (`bFloor`). That is a hole's stretch outside the outer loop and every other hole, or the outer loop's stretch inside exactly one hole. Such stretches are chained into closed loops. Unclosed chains are dropped. For each loop the face is rebuilt on a copy of the surface with `SM_OT_OPPOSITE` (the cavity's face, reversed) when `floor_is_cavity` (`sm_FloorIsCavity`) holds. The test point is the mean of the stretches' middles, dropped on the surface and stepped `1e-3` of the wall off it to the side opposite the face's own cavity side. That point must be inside the body (`Point3DClassify`, a non-void region). It must also be on the cavity side of every other offset face whose wall it lies within: the face's foot is inside it and less than the wall away. A face's cavity side is opposite the body's outward normal at the original face it came from (`outward_of_body`, `sm_OutwardOfBody`). Faces with no original face are not looked at. Any other such region is dropped as before.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft rig case shell/616, a lid shelled 0.5 open at a rib's outer side, which stands 0.15 in from the skirt's outer wall. Without this the cavity was left open there (`SM_ERR`, 1001).
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/fork_1b016ca_cavity_floor_under_opening.rs`. A 20 x 20 x 5 block with a rib x 0.15..3.15, y 2..18, z 5..8 is shelled 0.5 inward open at x = 0.15. It now shells to the closed form 587.5, with and without `offset_walls_touch_refused`. Before this change it failed with 1001. The rib 0.6 in has no reverse-wound region and shells to the same volume.
- **Difftest:** no change on the default path. The routine runs only with the opt-in.
