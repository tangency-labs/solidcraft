Updates: WR-533

#### WR-533 (update, 2026-11-15). `boolean_splits_definition_across_split` is removed

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, batch topology-1). `ProtoTopologyManager::update_edges` (`topology/proto_topology/p2.rs`) always splits a coincident edge definition that runs across the split point (both ends farther than the cross-section tolerance from it, the point dropped onto the definition's curve within that tolerance strictly inside its interval), with its proto edge (`proto_edge_split`), and classifies the upper part in its turn. Upstream re-points the whole definition to the piece under its midpoint, whose vertices are not its ends, and the imprint finds no vertex for them (`SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER`). Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::boolean_splits_definition_across_split`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, the test settings in `issue_1945_fork_1207_step4.rs`, `issue_2020_offset_mitred_below_depth.rs` and `wr650_tabbed_plate_mitred_rounds.rs`, the flag loop of `update_edges_splits_a_definition_across_the_split_point` (its upstream half) and of `wr_533_lip_swept_flush_round_a_corner_joins_with_either_setting` (now `wr_533_lip_swept_flush_round_a_corner_joins`).

- **Tests:** `update_edges_splits_a_definition_across_the_split_point` (`topology/proto_topology/p2.rs`) and both tests of `tests/wr_533_lip_swept_flush_round_a_corner.rs` run on the default kernel. `tests/wr579_near_coincident_tube_ends_join.rs` asserted that the union failed without `proto_edge_splits_at_captured_vertices`; that union now joins too, so the test asserts, for both settings of that flag, one manifold solid with a clean level-2 check and a volume strictly between the larger tube's and both together. Goldens tests: `topology_proto_topology_p2::tests::wr533_update_edges_rows` replays the `UpdateEdges` op five moved proto-topology rows end at, on the state it starts from; `offset_offset_executive_p2::tests::wr533_overlap_offsets_are_refused` asserts the refusal of the five moved offset rows, and the ignored `wr533_overlap_offsets_fail_the_gap_check_on_main` records main's side (an edge/edge gap over a thousand times the check's tolerance, not manifold) when run on main.
- **Results:** 12 goldens rows move: `boolean.api_brep` `issue1517_tube_ends_union_{checked,measured}` (1001 becomes a union whose level-2 check is clean), five `offset.offset_executive_p2` rows `overlap_pc_extrudeHoles1_f{0,2,3,4,5}_k0.36_out_rnd` (an unsound body becomes a 1001 refusal) and five `topology.proto_topology_p2` rows (gen0904, gen0913, gen0914, gen0976, `upstream_planar_union_crossings_1e-3`). gen1321 no longer moves since #16608: its split point lies outside `tol_3d` of the crossing definition's ends but within ten times that definition's own tolerance, so neither the definition nor (since #16608) its proto edge splits, and the definition is re-pointed whole as upstream does (tracked in #17628). The PR gives the proof of each.
