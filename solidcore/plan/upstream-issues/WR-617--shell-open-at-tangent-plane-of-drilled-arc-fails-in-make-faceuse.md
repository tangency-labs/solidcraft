#### WR-617. `SmApiShellBrepFull` fails with `SM_ERR` on a drilled plate opened at the face tangent to its arc side
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a valid shell request fails)
- **Area:** Offset
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp`, `SmOffsetExecutive::ShellBrep`; the merge of the offset faces fails with `SM_ERR` in `SmMerge::MakeFaceuse` (`source/SMLib/src/SmMerge.cpp`). The exit taken is not yet traced.
- **What happens:** a 2-inward shell with extended convex edges and merged results returns `SM_ERR` (1001) and no result. The same plate opened at its top face shells to a closed solid.
- **Trigger:** the #1528 drilled plate. It is a linear sweep 27.5 along +z of the profile (0, 0)-(42.5, 0)-(42.5, 20)-(0, 20) plus the half circle r 10 centred (0, 10), less `SmApiCreateCylinder((-10, 12.5, 12.5), r 5, h 17.5)` turned 90 degrees about +y. That is a blind hole along x opening through the arc side; inset by 2, the hole's tube (r 7) passes out through the arc's offset (r 8). The plate is shelled -2, open at its face y = 20, which the arc meets tangentially at (0, 20). This happens with and without `bCreateOffsetSolid`, through `SmApiShellBrepFull` and through a bare `SmOffsetExecutive::ShellBrep`.
- **How found:** tangency-labs/solidcore#1795: SolidCraft's `smlib_shell` reports 1001 on its own plate. Reconstructed in the port as `fixtures/offset.offset_executive_p2/issue1795.json`.
- **Confirmed:** on the pinned C++ build (v1.0.1, 3fd5b95). The difftest cases `issue1795_plate_api_shell_2_y20`, `_y20_offset_solid`, `issue1795_plate_static_shell_2_y20` and `_y20_offset_solid` return `status 1001` with no result on the C++ side, and the port returns the same.
- **Rust port:** matches upstream (`SM_ERR` from `boolean::merge::p2::make_faceuse`). Test `shell_open_at_y20_fails_as_upstream` in `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1795_shell_hole_through_arc.rs`. Finding `plan/findings/offset.offset_executive_p2--1795-y20-shell-fails-in-both.md`.
