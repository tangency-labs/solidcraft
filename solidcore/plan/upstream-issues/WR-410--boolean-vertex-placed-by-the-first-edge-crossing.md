#### WR-410. A boolean vertex takes its position from the first intersection curve to reach it, even one that grazes the edge
- **Severity:** Wrong result (an edge of the result starts off its vertex; SmBrep test #61)
- **Area:** Booleans / proto topology
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp`, `SmProtoTopologyManager::FindOrCreateProtoEdge` → `FindOrCreateProtoVertex`. Where an edge of one B-rep meets a face of the other, the intersection curves of that face with both faces of the edge end at the same point, and each finds it by crossing the edge. The `SmVertexDefinition` keeps the position of the first curve to arrive.
- **What happens:** a curve that crosses the edge at a grazing angle fixes the point along the edge only to its gap divided by the sine of that angle. In SolidCraft rig crashes/4092 (a rounded root pad joined onto a lofted cap), the wall∩round curve runs along the edge at 1.1 degrees, with its end 1.4e-6 off the round. That put the vertex 7.6e-5 along the edge, and the rib∩round curve, crossing at 28 degrees, then started 1.2e-4 from it: `ValidateGeometry` fails, test #61.
- **Trigger:** a boolean where two intersection curves end at one edge/face crossing, the first of them nearly parallel to the edge.
- **How found:** reported by jyviko (tangency-labs/solidcore#1059, with a correction in the comments). Fixed in the SolidCraft fork ("an edge/face vertex is placed by the crossing that fixes it best"; "a crossing re-places a vertex only when its points meet within tolerance").
- **Confirmed:** no (read at c9c5979 `source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp:4725`, `:3992`, `:4067`; reported and fixed in the SolidCraft fork, not independently reproduced)
- **Fix:** `FindOrCreateProtoEdge` passes each end's crossing angle (the smaller of the two intervals' sines when both ends lie on edges) to `SmProtoVertex::AddCrossing`. `SmVertexDefinition::PlaceByCrossing` takes a later crossing's point parameters (an edge's t, a face's uv), position and gap when its bound on the misplacement along the edge (gap over sine) is smaller. A curve parallel to the edge within the angular tolerance never places a vertex, and a crossing whose two points lie farther apart than both source objects' tolerances never re-places it. The proto vertex moves by the definition's displacement over its definition count.
- **Rust port:** `crates/solidcore/src/topology/proto_topology/p1/proto.rs` (`VertexDefinition::crossing_sine`, `VertexDefinition::place_by_crossing`, `crossing_error`, `ProtoTopologyManager::add_crossing`) and `crates/solidcore/src/topology/proto_topology/p3.rs` (`edge_crossing_sine`, `crossing_sine`, called in `find_or_create_proto_edge`). Opt-in `Kernel::fixes.boolean_vertex_by_best_crossing`; it is off by default, which keeps upstream's first-come placement, so the boolean and proto-topology difftest units keep matching the C++. Test: `issue_1059_a_steeper_crossing_places_the_vertex_definition` in `p3.rs` (unit level: the rig's lofted bodies are not reproduced).
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#1059.
