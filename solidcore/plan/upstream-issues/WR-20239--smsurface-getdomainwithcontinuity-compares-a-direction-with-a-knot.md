#### WR-20239. `SmSurface::GetDomainWithContinuity` compares the direction vector with the first and last knot values, so off the domain its answer depends on the vector's size

- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Surfaces (`GetDomainWithContinuity`, and `LiftCurve` through it)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmSurface.cpp`, `SmSurface::GetDomainWithContinuity` (def. `:12048`): the first and last knot tests of the U loop (`if (i == 0 && sVec.x < dKnots[i])`, and the matching `i == n-1` test with `>`) and of the V loop (`if (j == 0 && sVec.y < dKnots[j])`, and its `j == n-1` test).
- **What happens:** at a first or last knot whose continuity is below the minimum asked for, the routine compares a component of the direction vector `sVec` with that knot's parameter value. A direction is not a parameter value.
  - It probably meant to compare the point `crUV`, or to test the direction's sign at the boundary.
  - As written, when the start point lies off the domain, whether the domain's bound moves to that knot depends on the length of the direction vector against the knot coordinates, not on the surface.
  - Scaling the same direction can change the answer.
- **Trigger:** a start point outside the surface's knot range, or a direction component on the other side of a boundary knot's value. Examples: in 13 off-domain `surface.surface_p1` `DomCont` cases the answer changes when the direction is scaled by 1e-3, 1 and 1e3 (0009, 0011, 0049, 0061, 0062, 0068, 0071, 0073, 0082, 0089, 0090, 0093, 0095). In 17 more the vector's sign already decides the comparison and the answer is a box of zero width at status 0 (0002, 0004, 0006, 0021, 0023, 0028, 0029, 0035, 0043, 0051, 0057, 0063, 0067, 0079, 0081, 0086, 0088). These 30 are known failing under #20239, with no golden line. Not every off-domain case is an instance: in 0000, 0026, 0044 and 0058 no knot is below the requested continuity, so the comparison is never reached and the answer is the full natural domain.
  - In `LiftCurve` the vector is the UV curve's derivative, not normalised. So the domain of a segment that starts off the surface depends on the curve's parameterisation speed.
  - `offset.offset_geometry_creation` gen0266's lift is refused (status 1001) only because both bounds collapse for its derivative. A faster parameterisation of the same segment would be lifted onto a curve off the surface again.
- **How found:** review of #20054, which made the routine read a surface's own continuity arrays under every cache history. Before #20054, the empty arrays of a cache without a tree hid the comparison in most of these cases.
- **Confirmed:** by reading. The C++ and the Rust port (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/surface/p1.rs`, `domain_with_continuity_using`: `i == 0 && vec.x < ki`, `i == n - 1 && vec.x > ki`, and the same for `v`) do the same thing. For the cases above, the values equal the formula worked by hand on the surfaces' filled arrays. In the cases #20054 blesses (start points on the domain) the first and last knots are the natural bounds, so the comparison does not move their answers.
- **Fix:** not made. The ported behaviour is kept, and #20054 does not change it. The intended test, and what the routine should return for a start point off the domain, are the open questions of https://github.com/tangency-labs/solidcraft/issues/20239.
- **Rust port:** ported as upstream, deviation not taken.
