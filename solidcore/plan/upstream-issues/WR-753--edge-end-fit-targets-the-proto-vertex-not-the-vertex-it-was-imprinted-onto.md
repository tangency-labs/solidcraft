#### WR-753. `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint`'s end fit (WR-452, WR-453, WR-498) aims at the proto vertex's position when that proto vertex was imprinted onto a vertex the B-rep already had, which sits elsewhere
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a rim edge ends 1.45e-5 to 3e-5 short of its vertex: loops out of tolerance, the ops crate's `NotSolid`; SolidCraft's Subtract fails its check) 
- **Area:** Booleans / proto topology
- **Location:** `crates/solidcore/src/topology/proto_topology/p2/imprint_ends.rs`, `fit_edge_def_ends_to_proto_vertices`; the fork's end-fit block at the top of `SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint` (tangency-labs/smlib b43876f, a8caccd, ffaf4e8, bec8a0b). Not in upstream SMLib, which has no end fit.
- **What happens:** WR-498 sends an end to its proto vertex's position when every vertex definition of that proto vertex resolved to the edge's own final vertex in each B-rep, assuming the vertex sits there. A proto vertex whose definition was imprinted onto a vertex the B-rep already had (`imprint_vertex_definition`'s merge within tolerance) keeps the mean of its definitions' points; the B-rep's vertex is not moved. When one definition is an ill-conditioned hit, the mean lies off the vertex by half the hit's error, and the fitted end stops there.
- **Trigger:** a subtract whose tool has a cylindrical face coaxial with the wall it cuts and a hair (1e-10 to 6e-10) outside it, meeting the wall at a torus/cylinder tangent junction, where the wall's rim meets fillets tangentially: the intersection curve along the rim hits the fillet's rim arc tangentially, the hit lands 2.9e-5 (1e-10) to 5.8e-5 (4e-10) along the rim from the exact corner, the proto vertex's position is the mean of the hit and the corner, and the rim edge is fitted to that mean: 1.45e-5 to 2.9e-5 short of the corner vertex, over the 2e-5 loop tolerance. Without the imprint flags the edge keeps the raw hit parameter (2.9e-5 off), as upstream. SolidCraft: tangency-labs/solidcraft#13085 (a half-ring cup with a lug, a revolve cut of 36 degrees whose profile corner the sketch solver left 4e-10 off the wall; tangency-labs/solidcore#3150).
- **How found:** SolidCraft triage of #13085; the ops-crate test `crates/solidcore-ops/tests/boolean_cut_tool_wall_a_hair_off_coincident.rs` (committed first, failing), probes of the proto vertex position and the final vertices at the fit.
- **Confirmed:** yes, in the port: with SolidCraft's fix set the 4e-10 cut fails its check with the gaps the issue reports (5.9e-5 and 2.9e-5), and with this flag the 2e-10, 4e-10 and 6e-10 cuts pass at 28 and 36 degrees. Not confirmed against the C++ fork (no fork build here); the fork at bec8a0b carries the same WR-498 rule.
- **Fix:** `Kernel::fixes.imprint_fits_edge_end_to_final_vertex` (opt-in, off by default): the fit's target is the end's final vertex whenever its final vertices in the two B-reps lie within the edge definition's tolerance of each other; the proto vertex's position only when they do not and the proto vertex is one vertex in each B-rep (WR-498's rule), else the end is left alone. The edge is made between those final vertices, so that is where its curve must end.
- **Not fixed here:** from 1e-9 outside, the rulings where the tool cylinder meets the lug fillets (two cylinders internally tangent within the offset) come out of the surface/surface intersection beside the true tangent line (9.1e-5 at 1e-9), which no end fit can move, and `merge::p2::make_faceuse` fails; a second cause, recorded on tangency-labs/solidcore#3150.
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#3150 for offsets up to 6e-10 (the reporter's 4e-10).
