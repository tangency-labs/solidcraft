#### WR-784. `SmLoopuse::IsClosed3d` ignores a widened shared vertex's tolerance
- **Severity:** Wrong result (reachable only with the WR-93 correction; the pinned C++ never reports a loop open)
- **Area:** Topology
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmLoopuse.cpp`, `SmLoopuse::IsClosed3d`: the end-to-end tolerance is `GetXSectTol3d(ThisEdgeuse, NextEdgeuse)` only.
- **What happens:** The gap between an edge use's curve end and its CCW neighbour's curve start is measured against the sum of the two edges' zone tolerances. The vertex the two ends share is not looked at. Two curve ends that each lie inside a widened vertex's tolerance can be farther apart than their edges' tolerances allow. `SmTrimmingTools::CheckLoop` has the same omission, recorded and corrected as WR-381. With the pinned sign (WR-93) `IsClosed3d` never reports an open loop, so the omission cannot show; once gaps are reported (`loop_use_reports_open_gaps`), `SmLoopuse` Test #5 refuses a loop whose only fault is a gap its vertex covers. `SmBrep::ValidateAndUpdateTolerances` itself produces such bodies: it widens a vertex to twice its vertex/edge gap and leaves the edges as they are.
- **Trigger:** on the port, a square loop on a plane with one corner's curve ends 1e-3 apart, edge zones 1e-4 each (cross-section tolerance 2e-4) and that corner's vertex zone 2e-3: reported open at 2e-4. Also every solid of an imported STEP file whose vertices were widened by `ValidateAndUpdateTolerances` over gaps larger than its edges' tolerances.
- **How found:** a STEP file written by a mainstream CAD system at its default settings; after SMLib's own tolerance update widened its vertices, 22 loops of one solid failed `SmLoopuse` Test #5 against the edges' 1e-4.
- **Confirmed:** on the port and by reading the C++ (the tolerance line). The pinned C++ cannot show it (WR-93).
- **Fix in solidcore:** where gaps are reported (`loop_use_reports_open_gaps`, WR-93), the limit is the larger of the edges' cross-section tolerance and the shared vertex's zone tolerance, as WR-381 does in `CheckLoop`. Without WR-93 nothing changes, bit for bit. Test `is_closed_3d_takes_the_shared_vertex_tolerance` (`crates/solidcore/src/topology/loopuse.rs`).
- **Not covered:** two ends on different vertices keep the edges' tolerance.
