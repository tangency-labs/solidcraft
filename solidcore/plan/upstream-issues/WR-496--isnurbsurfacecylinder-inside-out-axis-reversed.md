#### WR-496. `SmCylinder::IsNurbSurfaceCylinder` reverses an inside-out NURB's axis
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a cylinder whose STEP evaluation is mirrored in height against its own NURB: `SmCone::AssertValid` #3 and `SmSurfOfRevolution` #8/#9/#10 fail, and edges miss the surface, `SmBrep` #4 Bad Gap).
- **Area:** Surfaces (cones and cylinders)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmCylinder.cpp`, `SmCylinder::IsNurbSurfaceCylinder` (def. `:233`): the Z axis is `centre(top-knot iso) - centre(bottom-knot iso)` and the origin the bottom-knot circle; when the iso circles turn the other way about that Z it sets `bInsideOut = TRUE` ("Do some correction here", `:320`) and keeps the frame.
- **What happens:** an inside-out NURB (its linear direction against the circles' right-hand axis) comes back as a cylinder with Z reversed, from the wrong end. `SmCone::IsNurbSurfaceCone` handles the same NURB correctly: Z against the linear direction, origin at the top-knot circle.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcore#1463: `SmCylinder(origin 0, X, Y, r 8, 0..360, h 22, inside out)`, its NURB made and recognised: origin z = 22, Z = -Z, test #3 = -7.861 (the made cylinder's +7.861), STEP vs NURB 11.68. A flat-format round trip of any inside-out cylinder (SolidCraft's `AnalyticOfNurbs`) hits it.
- **How found:** SolidCraft rig cases (sweeps/726, sweeps/4008, sweeps/1866), issue #1463.
- **Confirmed:** by reading the pinned source; the Rust port matches it (difftest `surface.cone_p2` cases `issue1463_cyl_*`, inside-out NURBs with the fix off). The fork (tangency-labs/smlib 5afca53) has the same code.
- **Fix:** for an inside-out NURB, build the frame as `IsNurbSurfaceCone` does (origin the top-knot circle, Z its negation).
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.nurb_cylinder_inside_out_frame` (default `false`, upstream's frame) in `Cone::is_nurb_surface_cylinder`, `crates/solidcore/src/surface/cone/p2.rs`. Test `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1463_revolve_inside_out_cylinder.rs`. Separately, the opt-in `fixes.revolve_analytic_profile_faces` (WR-448) no longer makes inside-out cylinders or cones: like tangency-labs/smlib 9853e54's `SmRotationalSweepGeometry::AnalyticSurface`, a line falling along the axis is built from its low end. Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#1463.
