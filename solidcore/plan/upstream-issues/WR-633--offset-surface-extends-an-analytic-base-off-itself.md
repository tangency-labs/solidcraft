#### WR-633. `SmOffsetSurface` extends a cylinder, cone, sphere or torus base past its domain by a tangent ruled extension, which leaves the surface at once
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Surfaces (offset surface)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetSurface.cpp`, `sm_ExtendSurface` (def. `:463`): the base's NURB is extended with `NL_G1R` (`:517`), a tangent ruled span along the boundary, for every kind of base surface.
- **What happens:** an analytic base (a cylinder, cone, sphere or torus) is not carried on past its domain: the extension follows the boundary's tangent. An offset (4) of a half cylinder (r 5) evaluated 45 degrees past its arc's end lies 6.3 from the axis instead of 9; a cylinder R25 continued 0.44 past its face's end is 0.0036 off the circle. A fillet traced on that continuation (a mitre's rails meeting past a concave corner, a rail leaving its face across a cliff, #960) is built that far off along its whole length, and the mitre's curve then finds no fillet surface through its ends (1001).
- **Trigger:** any offset surface evaluated past the domain of an analytic base with extension allowed; in SolidCraft, the six top outline edges of a tabbed plate rounded r 0.5 (its R25 arc walls are `SmCylinder`s).
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#960). The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@de0a6b8 (branch `replay/1.0.1`, not an ancestor of rev `68fb9162`), which is not merged upstream: `sm_ExtendAsItself`, called from the fork's `sm_ExtendSurface` (the WR-458 extension from the base) for a base that `IsKindOf` `SmCone` (cylinders too), `SmSphere` or `SmTorus`. Each end span's rational polynomial is carried on (NLib's C-infinity extension, `N_SrfExtendByParamDist` with `NL_CMAX`) piece by piece, the old end made a knot of full multiplicity and each piece's weights rescaled so its far weight is the old end's, since one long rational span needs a weight outside `[NL_WMIN, NL_WMAX]`.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run of the fork was made. The pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`) has the tangent extension; the Rust port reproduces it with the flag off (the regression test below measures 6.3 against 9 at 45 degrees).
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.offset_extension_as_itself` and `Kernel::fixes.offset_extension_from_base` (the fork's change sits in that extension; without it the flag does nothing), `extend_from_base` hands such a base to `extend_as_itself` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/offset_surface.rs`), a port of `sm_ExtendAsItself` with its 4096-piece bound per side.

  Tests: `surface::offset_surface::tests::wr633_offset_of_a_cylinder_continues_as_itself_past_its_domain` (an offset 4 of a half cylinder r 5, evaluated 0.07 to 3 domains of turn past both ends: more than 1e-3 off radius 9 without the flag, within 1e-9 of it with it).
