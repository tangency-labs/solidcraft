#### WR-588. `SmFace::CalculateTightBoundingBox` misses a trimmed face's own extreme when its surface peaks outside the trim
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a trimmed face's tight box is too small)
- **Area:** Faces / tight bounding box
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFace.cpp`, `SmFace::CalculateTightBoundingBox`: one `GlobalPointSolve(SM_SO_3D_SIGNED_DIRECTED_MAXIMIZE)` per direction over the face's whole UV domain, its solution kept only when it classifies inside the face.
- **What happens:** that solve gives the surface's global extreme over the domain (`SmCoreTypes.h`: all answers within tolerance of the global value, not every local one). The point is kept only if it lies inside the face. Where the surface peaks outside the trim, the face's own extreme inside it is never looked for, and the box comes from the edges alone.
- **Trigger:** a bicubic height field that peaks at z = 50 at a corner outside its square trim and rises to exactly 4 at the middle of the trimmed face: the tight box's z maximum comes out 0 (expected 4). Found through a crowned n-sided fill (SolidCraft) whose height field is built over its loop's box: it was boxed at its rim, 12.53, with its middle at 16.0.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (the fork, tangency-labs/smlib@9d44c1b), reported in tangency-labs/solidcore#1799.
- **Confirmed:** no. The pinned C++ is not built on this machine, so the fork's commit is the evidence. The port reproduces it with the opt-in off: `tight_box_of_a_trimmed_face_without_the_fix_is_upstreams` (`crates/solidcore/src/query/face/p3.rs`) gives z maximum 0.
- **Fix (fork):** tangency-labs/smlib@9d44c1b decides each direction over cells of the UV domain (`sm_FaceDirectedMax`), from the vertices' and edges' extreme. A cell is settled when its greatest value is not above the best found, when the point taking it is inside the face, when it lies wholly outside the face (its least value above the edges' value), or when the value is monotone over it. Otherwise the cell is halved. A cell left at depth 16 or after 4096 cells counts with its own greatest value, so the box is never smaller than the face.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.face_tight_box_searches_trim` (default `false`, upstream's behaviour) in `calculate_tight_bounding_box_into` (`crates/solidcore/src/query/face/p3.rs`), through `face_directed_max`. Tests: `tight_box_finds_a_trimmed_faces_peak_when_the_surface_peaks_outside` (flag on: z maximum 4), `tight_box_of_a_trimmed_face_without_the_fix_is_upstreams` (flag off: z maximum 0), and `tight_box_with_the_peak_inside_the_trim_agrees_with_upstream` (both give 4 when the surface's peak is inside the trim).
