Updates: WR-697

#### WR-697 (update, 2026-11-02). The Ball's face carries its sphere: an `SmBallPatch` with an exact offset
- **Severity:** Wrong result (accuracy); a shell of a body with Ball corners fails
- **Area:** Fillets (corners), offsets and shells, file IO
- **Location:** as WR-697's Ball addendum (`WR-697--addendum-2026-11-02-ball-corner.md`): pinned SMLib has no Ball. With the Ball's face a plain `SmBSplineSurface`, its offset is the generic B-spline offset (`SmSurface::CreateOffsetSurface`), approximate and with no collapse test (`SmSurface::OffsetCollapses`).
- **What happens:** the fork's f8e6709 records the Ball's face as an `SmBallPatch` (`source/SMLib/inc/SmBallPatch.h`, `source/SMLib/src/SmBallPatch.cpp`; `SmBallPatch_TYPE`, `SURF_BASE_TYPE + 42`): an `SmBSplineSurface` carrying the centre and radius of the sphere its net is exactly on. Its offset by d is the net scaled about the centre by (R + s d) / R, weights unchanged (s = +1 where its normal points away from the centre); `OffsetCollapses` where R + s d is at most the tolerance; `Transform` moves the centre and radius with the net (rigid or uniform scale only); `WriteToDB` writes the centre and radius, then the net, and `SmSurface::ReadFromDB` dispatches the type. Without it, the fork's capi shell of a 30 x 20 x 10 box with all twelve edges rounded r 3, shelled 2 in, fails.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's smlib#79 step 4c follow-up f8e6709 and its capi shell case 5eb43a1. efb8007 adds `SmBallPatch::IsOnSphere`, which certifies a net on a sphere from the Bernstein coefficients of |X - C W|^2 - R^2 W^2 over its Bezier pieces. The fork's SMLib has no caller for it; only its capi import calls it. b439d73 is a TODO comment only: the shell puts an exact Ball offset on a tangent-extended B-spline, which no longer carries the sphere. The port keeps that behaviour.
- **Rust port:** under the same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner`. `crate::surface::ball_patch::BallPatch` is `SurfaceKind::BallPatch`, built on #2274's `ProceduralBlend` pattern. Every arm that takes an `SmBSplineSurface` by `SM_CAST_PTR` or `IsKindOf` takes the net. The overridden virtuals (`Create` with its refusals, `operator==`, `Copy`, `NormalSide`, `CreateOffsetSurface`, `OffsetCollapses`, `Transform`, `WriteToDB`, `ReadFromDB`, `IsKindOf`) have their own arms. `SplitAt` and `CreateExtendedSurface` give plain B-splines of the net, as the fork's inherited bodies do. The reader reads the type only with the flag; without it the type is refused, as the pinned reader refuses it. `SmDatabaseIO::IsValidType` is a debug-build assertion and is not ported for any type. `IsOnSphere` is ported as `BallPatch::is_on_sphere`, with no caller, as in the fork's SMLib. The Ball's face (`crate::blend::blend_corner::ball`) is now a `BallPatch`.
- **Triggers:** the box corner of WR-697's Ball addendum (a 40 x 30 x 20 box, the three edges at (40, 30, 20) rounded r 3), its face offset by -2, 2 and -2.999; the fork's capi shell case 5eb43a1.
- **Confirmed:** in the port, and against a local build of the fork at b5bb747, which contains f8e6709. A driver linked against that build rounded the box corner with `SmApiFilletEdgesPerEdge` and printed, with `%.17g`, the face's `SmBallPatch` centre and radius, its 5 x 3 net with weights, and its `CreateOffsetSurface` at -2, 2 and -2.999. All 258 printed values equal the port's: radii 1, 5 and 0.00099999999999988987, with the scaled nets. `OffsetCollapses` is true at -3 and false at -2 in both. efb8007's `SmBallPatch.cpp` was compiled and linked against that build. Its `IsOnSphere` gives the same five answers as the port's `is_on_sphere`: the face's net on r 3 is true; on r 3 + 1e-9 or with the centre moved 1e-9 it is false; the net cut by knots at 0.3 and 0.7 of u is true on r 3 and false on 3 (1 + 1e-9). The shell case was checked against the fork's quoted numbers and closed forms: the shell builds, and at each of the 8 corners there is a face on the sphere of radius 3 about its ball's centre and one on the concentric sphere of radius 1. Each surface over its face's UV box is within 1.2e-14 of its sphere; the fork quotes 1.1e-14. Without the ball patch (the face a plain B-spline) the port's shell fails, as the fork reports for its own.
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr697_ball_patch.rs`, also `crates/solidcore/tests/wr697_ball_corner.rs`, which now checks the face's class, centre and radius.
