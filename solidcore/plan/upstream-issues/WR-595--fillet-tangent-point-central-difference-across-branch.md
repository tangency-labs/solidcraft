#### WR-595. `ComputePointValues` takes a central difference across a tangent point, where the intersection curve does not run on through
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blend (fillet solver)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp`, `SmFilletSolver::ComputePointValues` (def. `:3544`): `if ( bFoundForw && bFoundBack )` takes the central difference of the forward and backward steps (`:3664` at 1.0.1) for a tangent point too.
- **What happens:** at a point where the offset surfaces are tangent (`SM_IP_TANGENT_POINT`) the intersection curve ends or branches; it does not run on through the point. The curve tangent there is set by finite differences of a step forward (along the previous point's tangent) and a step back. Where the surfaces touch exactly the step back finds nothing and the forward difference is used. Where they touch only to within the model's accuracy (a sketch with 1e-10 residues), the step back lands on the near-tangent crossing beyond the point and the central difference averages the curve with that branch: in SolidCraft's case 18 degrees off the curve, so every step after the point failed the angle test and was halved, the trace creeping on in steps down to 8e-10: 109 rail control points instead of 25, coincident rows and an internal pole, which `smlib_brep_check` refused with 11 failed tests.
- **Trigger:** a round whose centre line is traced from a tangent point of the offset surfaces where both the forward and the backward step find an intersection (SolidCraft: a bar meeting a cylinder, the concave arc where the bar's top meets the cylinder rounded r 2, then the bar's front top edge rounded r 2 with its tangent chain). Upstream's `Fillet/nurb_cone.smb` edge 5 at r 0.5 reaches the same branch.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (test-peg part with sketch residues). The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@f8fd83e, which is not merged upstream and has no test.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run was made of the fix. The Rust port follows the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`) with the flag off; the difftest cases that reach the branch (`stubs4-degenerate-*` of `blend.fillet_executive_p3`, e.g. `stubs4-degenerate-nurb_cone-e5-r0.5`) agree with the C++.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_tangent_point_one_sided_step`, a tangent point takes the forward difference whenever the forward step found the intersection, and the backward difference only when it did not (`blend/fillet_solver/p4.rs`, `c6_compute_point_values`). On `Fillet/nurb_cone.smb` the flag also turns edge 8's r 0.5 round, which upstream builds, into a failure; it stays opt-in.

  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr595_tangent_point_one_sided_step.rs` (`Fillet/nurb_cone.smb` edge 5 at r 0.5: the rails have 310 control points with the flag off, 286 with it on; the volume agrees to 1e-7).
