#### WR-706. A round ended at its sections (WR-674) is built only alone
- **Severity:** Wrong result (refusal)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet executive)
- **Location:** not upstream: solidcore's WR-674 (`fixes.fillet_round_ends_at_tangent_continuation`, #2185), whose section-ended round is closed into the region it removes and subtracted (`close_section_ended_round`, `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_cliff_overflow.rs`; `subtract_removal_region`, `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_executive/session/cliff_overflow.rs`).
- **What happens:** the subtraction builds only one round both of whose ends are section ends. Any other selection with a section end is refused with `FilletErrorType::NoRoom` (`NoRoomReason::TangentContinuationEndUnsupported`; `fillet_edges` returns `Upstream { code: 1001 }`), even when the other rounds are far away.
- **Trigger:** #2223: an R25 disc with a tab (sides x = +-4, R2 corners, top y = 280/9 + 2), extruded 2, rounded r 0.5 on the top rim arc and the tab's R2 corner arc at x > 0 together, with every `UpstreamFixes` flag on. Each edge rounds alone (7.958674274526857 and 0.15913583445853874 removed).
- **How found:** SolidCraft (`fillet_tabbed_plate_outer_loop_and_partial_selection_after_hole`), reported in #2223.
- **Confirmed:** in solidcore (the regression test below). Not reachable in the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`), which fails the corner arc alone (`SM_FILERR_VERTEX_PROBLEM`, 1001). The fork at tangency-labs/smlib 5e8c9c1 (local build, `SmApiFilletEdges` r 0.5) leaves the corner arc unrounded: rim alone 7.9586734663816969 removed, corner arc alone -3.3e-9, both 7.9586734967351731 (10 faces). So the fork is known to differ here, and the fix is checked against the closed form.
- **Rust port:** `Kernel::fixes.fillet_section_end_trim_at_creases`, effective only with both `fillet_overflow_trim_at_creases` (WR-695) and `fillet_round_ends_at_tangent_continuation` (WR-674); own design, not in the fork. A round with a section end goes to the crease trim (`FilletSession::trim_at_creases`) instead of the subtraction: each section end is cut by the section's plane and closed by the section's flat face (`section_end_face`, the face the subtraction builds), and a side of that face that lies along an edge of the body (the vertical seam where the corner's cylinder runs on tangentially into the flat side) is sewn to that edge's piece, which the rail's imprint cuts off, instead of being imprinted. Off: the subtraction, as before.

  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2223_section_end_trim_at_creases.rs`: the rim and corner arc together (either pick order) remove the rim's 7.958674274526857 plus the corner arc's Pappus wedge (1 - pi/4) r^2 (pi/2) (2 - c), c = r (10 - 3 pi) / (3 (4 - pi)), = 0.159135839721916, to 1e-7 (8.117810109458333 against 8.117810114248773), 12 faces, `check_brep` Level2 valid; each edge alone unchanged (9 and 11 faces); with the flag off the pair is still refused with 1001.
