Updates: WR-408

`SmTorus::IsNurbSurfaceTorus` (`Torus::is_nurb_surface_torus`) now measures its signed generator interval with the zero tolerance `SM_EFF_ZERO_DEG`, as the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@40344bf (tangency-labs/solidcore#1471). It took `SM_EFF_ZERO` (1e-12 in the port) before. A generator whose tube closes on itself has its two ends carrying the rounding of their coordinates (about 1e-12 degrees for a torus 80 mm out), so they differ by more than 1e-12 degrees and the interval came out a sliver of about 1e-11 degrees instead of a full turn. The same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.torus_signed_gen_curve_interval` covers this; no new flag, and it is off by default. Test: `issue_1470_1471_tube_turn_from_bottom_is_found_with_its_radii` in the same file, with that flag and `torus_major_radius_from_origin` on; it fails on the sliver before the change.
