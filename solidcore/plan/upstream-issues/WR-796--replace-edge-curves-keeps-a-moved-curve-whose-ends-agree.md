#### WR-796. `SmOffsetGeometryCreation::ReplaceEdgeCurves` keeps an edge's old curve when only its ends agree with the new one
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a body with an edge in two versions: not a manifold solid, or refused by the check)
- **Area:** Offset / local operation
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetGeometryCreation.cpp:732-754` (`SmOffsetGeometryCreation::ReplaceEdgeCurves`, the two "low work" branches: the edge use's curve and the new 3D curve have start and end points within the edge tolerance, in either order, so the new curve is deleted and the old one kept). The port: `crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_geometry_creation.rs`, `OffsetGeometryCreation::replace_edge_curves_in`.
- **What happens:** ends that agree are taken for "no change". They prove nothing about the curve between them. Three ways it bites:
  - a face turned about a hinge through both ends of one of its edges (a draft): the ends stay where they are while the edge between them moves onto the turned face's section with its neighbour;
  - a closed edge (start = end at its seam): the ends agree trivially whenever the seam point stays, while the curve moves;
  - a thickened sheet whose two faces meet in a seam tangent at its ends that bends between them: the seam's offset curve, lifted from one face, ends where the other face's own offset of the seam ends, but leaves it in between.
  The kept old curve lies off one of its faces, so the edge comes out in two versions: the result is not a manifold solid, or the merge glues them at their gap and the check refuses it.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#16727: a ring between a cylinder and a cone about an axis 2 past the hinge, its flat bottom drafted 3° about a plane through both ends of its inner arc (refused: the local operation's body fails check test #59). tangency-labs/solidcraft#15513: a surface loft between two fit-point splines, one face per span, thickened by 250; its seams bend by up to 0.048° between tangent ends, and the two offsets of a seam stay up to 0.21 apart (refused: not a closed solid). The goldens' `torus_edgeuse_offset_extend` (upstream's `torus.smb`, offset 0.1 with extension): two closed circles whose seam points stay while each circle lies 10 to 20 off its new curve.
- **How found:** SolidCraft triage of #16727 (a draft refused on a real part, reduced to the ring) and of #15513 (a thicken refused on a real loft); the generated cases and the torus fixture from the goldens moving with the fix.
- **Confirmed:** yes, in the port: the kernel cases `shell/15513-thicken-three-span-loft-sheet-out-250`, `-in-250` and `shell/15513-thicken-two-span-loft-sheet-bent-seam-250` are refused without the fix. Not run against the C++.
- **Fix:** always on (no flag): ends that agree say "no change" only where the old curve lies on the new one between them: its points a quarter, a half and three quarters along drop onto the new curve within the edge tolerance (`curve_lies_on`). With the ends these are five points, which determine a line or a conic, so for a plane face's edges the test is exact; for a freeform edge it is a check at the edge tolerance. Otherwise the new curve replaces the old one, as for an edge whose ends moved. The goldens this moves (16 cases of `offset.offset_geometry_creation`) were blessed with their justification.
