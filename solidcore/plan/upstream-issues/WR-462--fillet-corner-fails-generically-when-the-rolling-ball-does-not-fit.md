#### WR-462. A round whose rolling ball does not fit the faces it rolls on, run up into a corner, fails in `CalcRailIntEdgeuse` with the generic `SM_ERR` (#1172)
- **Severity:** Poor status
- **Area:** Fillets
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletExecutive.cpp:2071` (at 3fd5b95) (`CreateFilletBrep`, the `CalcCornerVertGeom` loop, which runs before the `CalcFilletGeom` loop at :2209); `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp:1739` (`SmFilletVertex::CalcRailIntEdgeuse`).
- **What happens:** a blend whose ball is larger than a face's radius of curvature has a rail that turns back on itself, so the round cannot exist. SMLib finds out only in the corners, which run before the rails are built and look for a rail end that is not there; the fillet fails with `SM_ERR` ("Vertex Computation Failure" in the fork's run) instead of reporting that the round does not fit.
- **Trigger:** a 60 x 40 x 20 box, its top edge at y = 40 rounded r5, its top edge at x = 60 rounded r3, then the vertical edge at x = 60, y = 40 with the two edges tangent to it up into the corner (the side's arc on the r5 round, the seam between the rounds): r3.1 and up cannot fit (the ball curves less than the r5 round around it; no ball over r3 fits the r3 round).
- **How found:** SolidCraft ladder 07-fillets/016 (tangency-labs/solidcore#1172).
- **Confirmed:** no (read at 3fd5b95 SmFilletExecutive.cpp:2071, SmFilletGeom.cpp:1739; reported in the SolidCraft fork, whose fix is not public). The port's unit test `a_ball_that_does_not_fit_is_no_room_before_the_corners` shows the corners fail with status 1001 and no `NoRoom` code, which is not a C++ run.
- **Fix:** the fork's `SmFilletSolver::BallFitsItsFaces` runs in `CreateFilletBrep` before the corners (diff `2c5baae..e5696ca` in #1172; the commits are not on a public branch of tangency-labs/smlib). It solves nine sections along each blend's edge and refuses when `det M` or `trace M` of the face's offset stretch `M = I - rS` is below -1e-6.
- **Rust port:** with `Kernel::fixes.fillet_refuse_ball_not_fitting` the executive runs `ball_fits_its_faces` on each solver before the corners and records `FilletErrorType::NoRoom` (8); the call still returns the executive's status 1001, as for the other refusals.
- **Note:** the pin has no `NO_ROOM` code: its `SmFilletErrorType` ends at `SM_FILERR_BAD_MERGE` (`SmFilletGeom.h:56-63`). `NoRoom = 8` is an additive, not-upstream variant that only the opt-ins record. At the pin the impossible round is refused (`SM_ERR` 1001 from the corners), so the defect is the uninformative status, not a wrong body. The check refuses only what it can solve for (a section whose drop or solve fails is skipped), and its nine sections at `(j + 0.5) / 9` do not test the ends of the edge.
