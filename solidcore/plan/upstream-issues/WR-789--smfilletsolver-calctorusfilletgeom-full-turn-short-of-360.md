#### WR-789. `SmFilletSolver::CalcTorusFilletGeom` sweeps a round along a whole closed edge a hair short of 360 degrees when its end section measures just short of the wrap
- **Severity:** Failed operation
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp:1006`-`:1011` (`SmFilletSolver::CalcTorusFilletGeom`, def. `:860`): `CCWAngleBetween(sVec, sEndVec, dSpanAng)` measures the turn from the round's start section to its end section, and only `dSpanAng < SM_ANG_TOL_RAD` is taken as a whole turn (`dSpanAng += 2*SM_PI`, clamped to 360 degrees later).
- **What happens:** along a whole closed edge the round's start and end sections both lie at the edge's seam, but they are solved apart, so the two directions differ by a hair (the solver's convergence, about 1.6e-9 mm on the trigger below). When the end direction lies a hair past the start one, the turn is just above 0, becomes 2 pi plus that hair and is clamped to exactly 360 degrees. When it lies a hair before it, the turn is just short of 2 pi and stays so: the revolution's sweep is a few 1e-9 degrees short of 360, while the sweep circle its polar converter is rebuilt from is closed at 360. `SmSurfOfRevolution::AssertValid` Test #7 (the polar curve's analytic and NURB intervals must match the surface's, tolerance 1e-12 x (1 + 360) degrees) then refuses the round. Which way it falls depends on the part, so one of two mirror-image edges builds and the other fails.
- **Trigger:** a ring (radii 17.5 and 7.5, z -10 to 10, extruded up from z = -10) joined by a tab (x 10 to 60, y -10 to 10, z -5 to 5), the tab's edges at the ring and its long edges rounded r 2. A round r 2 (or r 0.14) on the ring's outer circle at z = -10 sweeps [0, 359.999999994606] degrees and fails Test #7; the same round at z = 10 sweeps [0, 360] and builds.
- **How found:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#12175 (and #15311): a fillet on one of two mirror-image circular edges failed with this check.
- **Confirmed:** on the port, which follows these lines one for one (`solidcore/crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_solver/p2.rs`, `torus_fillet_geom_parts_of`): the sweep and polar intervals above were printed from `has_same_parameterization` at SolidCraft 0fea5059. Not run against the C++ build.
- **Rust port:** fixed: a round along a whole closed edge whose end section is its start one (its rail points within the solver's 3D tolerance) sweeps exactly 360 degrees.
