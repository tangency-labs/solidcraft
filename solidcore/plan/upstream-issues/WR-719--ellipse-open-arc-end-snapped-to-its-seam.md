#### WR-719. `SmPolarConversion::SetUpPolarConversion` snaps an open ellipse arc ending just short of its seam to 360
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Curves
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmEllipse.cpp`, `SmPolarConversion::SetUpPolarConversion` (the end angles of an arc shorter than 360 degrees, found by `SmEllipse::STEPInversion` over a temporary full circle `[0, 360]`).
- **What happens:** `SmEllipse::Trim` cuts the NURB (B-spline subdivision: the same curve over the shorter interval), sets the STEP domain from the cut's ends, then rebuilds the polar converter. The set-up takes the arc's end angles with `STEPInversion` over a full circle. For a closed curve `STEPInversion` takes a point at the seam's end (within the zone tolerance / 100 and 1e-4 degrees of 360) to be the seam's start, 0, and the set-up then adds 360. On an open arc that ends 1.17e-6 degrees short of 360 that rule makes the end angle exactly 360, so the STEP domain disagrees with the NURB by the gap: 1.45e-8 at radius 0.71, failing `SmEllipse`'s own validity test 1 (each STEP angle evaluating where its NURBS parameter does, to 100 `SM_EFF_ZERO` of the part's size).
- **Trigger:** a round's End ellipse on a sloped basin wall (radii 0.5 and sqrt(0.5), 313.53 to 360 degrees) trimmed 2.57e-8 of its NURBS parameter short of its end by the trace (solidcraft#1080; the fork's capi test `scenario_ellipse_trimmed_just_short_of_its_seam`, tangency-labs/smlib 4e0d0e8).
- **How found:** by the fork (tangency-labs/smlib cbeb14b, smlib#82 and #97), gating the Mixed corner's trace rule.
- **Confirmed:** in solidcore: without the fix the trimmed arc's STEP domain ends at 360.000000000000 against its end's angle 359.999998837308, with STEP and NURBS points 1.435e-8 apart; with it the domain ends at 359.999998837307 and the points are 7.585e-11 apart. These are the values the fork's cbeb14b quotes for its build.
- **Fix:** the seam rule stays for a curve whose NURB is closed (`IsClosed` over its natural interval). An open arc's ends take their own polar angles, `atan2(rx / ry * y, x)` of their local coordinates in `[0, 2 pi)`, in degrees, as `STEPInversion` computes them before the seam rule (tangency-labs/smlib cbeb14b).
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.ellipse_open_arc_keeps_end_angle` (port in progress), in `curve::ellipse::p2::set_up_polar_conversion` (unit `curve.ellipse_p2`). Off: the pinned set-up. Test: `curve::ellipse::p1::tests::open_arc_trimmed_short_of_its_seam_keeps_its_end_angle` (flag off: snapped to 360; flag on: the end's own angle within 1e-9, points within 1e-10).
