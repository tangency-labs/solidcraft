#### WR-785. `SmOffsetGeometryCreation::ReplaceEdgeCurves` keeps an edge's old curve when only its ends agree with the new one
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a body with an edge in two versions: not a manifold solid, or refused by the check)
- **Area:** Offset / local operation
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetGeometryCreation.cpp:732-754` (`SmOffsetGeometryCreation::ReplaceEdgeCurves`, the two "low work" branches: the edge use's curve and the new 3D curve have start and end points within the edge tolerance, in either order, so the new curve is deleted and the old one kept). The port: `crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_geometry_creation.rs`, `OffsetGeometryCreation::replace_edge_curves`.
- **What happens:** ends that agree are taken for "no change". They prove nothing about the curve between them. Two ways it bites:
  - a face turned about a hinge through both ends of one of its edges (a draft): the ends stay where they are while the edge between them moves onto the turned face's section with its neighbour;
  - a closed edge (start = end at its seam): the ends agree trivially whenever the seam point stays, while the curve moves.
  The kept old curve lies off one of its faces, so the edge comes out in two versions: the result is not a manifold solid, or the merge glues them at their gap and the check refuses it.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#16727: a ring between a cylinder and a cone about an axis 2 past the hinge, its flat bottom drafted 3° about a plane through both ends of its inner arc (refused: the local operation's body fails check test #59). Upstream's own `Fillet/tilt_cyl_on_box.smb`, solid offset 11.0492 of face 1 (the goldens' `pe_Fillet_tilt_cyl_on_box_f1_d0_in_ext` / `_out_ext`): the circle at the tilted cylinder's foot is a closed edge whose seam stays while the circle moves by more than a thousand times the edge tolerance; the result has the circle twice, once on each face, and is not a manifold solid.
- **How found:** SolidCraft triage of #16727 (a draft refused on a real part, reduced to the ring); the tilt fixture from the goldens moving with the fix.
- **Confirmed:** yes, in the port (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1061_moved_boss_top_extends_cylinder.rs`, `mod hinge_through_an_edges_ends`, the cone ring refused without the fix; the goldens' tilt fixture non-manifold on `main`). Not run against the C++.
- **Fix:** always on (no flag): ends that agree say "no change" only where the old curve lies on the new one between them: its points a quarter, a half and three quarters along drop onto the new curve within the edge tolerance (`curve_lies_on`). With the ends these are five points, which determine a line or a conic, so for a plane face's edges the test is exact; for a freeform edge it is a check at the edge tolerance. Otherwise the new curve replaces the old one, as for an edge whose ends moved. The goldens this moves (17 cases in `offset.offset_executive_p2` and `offset.offset_geometry_creation`) were blessed with their justification.
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcraft#16727.
