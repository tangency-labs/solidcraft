#### WR-713. Inside-out and negative-angle unbounded `SmCone`s are parameterised unlike their NURB: `CreateIsoCircleFromPoint` builds the circle far from the point or none
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Surfaces and volumes
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmCone.cpp`: the unbounded cone constructor `SmCone::SmCone(Origin, XAxis, YAxis, BotRadius, SemiAngleDeg, bSwapUV, bInsideOut)` (def. `:64`, the `dAngleRad > SM_EFF_ZERO` test at `:90` and the domain at `:128`); `SmCone::ConvertUVFromSTEPToNURBS` (def. `:1799`, the unbounded branch) and `SmCone::ConvertUVFromNURBSToSTEP` (def. `:1934`, the unbounded branch at `:1978`); seen through `SmCone::CreateIsoCircleFromPoint` (def. `:1620`).
- **What happens:** two independent defects of the unbounded cone (`CreateCanonical` with no height, or with a height `<= 0`).
  - **Inside out.** `ConvertUVFromSTEPToNURBS` maps an unbounded cone's STEP height `h` to the NURB height `-h` when inside out, and `ConvertUVFromNURBSToSTEP` back. The cone's NURB is not over the negated heights: it runs over the same interval `[apex, SM_INFINITE_PARAMETER]`, reversed (NURB `v = apex` is the top). So every NURB parameter the cone hands out or reads names another height: `CreateIsoCircleFromPoint` inverts the point to STEP height 1, converts it to NURB `v = -1`, and the NURB's iso curve there is at height `apex + SM_INFINITE_PARAMETER + 1`. `EvaluatePoint` at NURB parameters (through the STEP conversion) and the NURB's own derivatives disagree in the same way.
  - **Negative semi-angle.** The constructor rotates the generator line by the semi-angle only when it is positive, so a negative semi-angle leaves the generator parallel to the axis (a cylinder of the base radius, with speed `1 / cos(semi-angle)`), while `GetRadius` and `GetApex` use the semi-angle. The domain is then `[apex parameter, SM_INFINITE_PARAMETER]`, with the apex above the origin, so it holds none of the cone's points below the apex, its base circle included, and `CreateIsoCircleFromPoint` returns no circle for them. The constructor and `CreateCanonical` document a semi-angle in `[0, 90)` and assert it in debug builds (`:79`, `:325`), but release builds accept a negative one, and the bounded constructor handles it.
- **Trigger:** placement at the origin, Z axis, the point `(0.6 r, 0.8 r, 1)` with `r = GetRadius(1)`, tolerance 1e-3 (#2313):
  - bottom radius 1, semi-angle 10, inside out: upstream's circle is at height 1234562.33, radius 217687.65; the circle through the point is at height 1, radius 1.17633.
  - bottom radius 2, semi-angle −15, inside out or not: upstream returns no circle; the cone's circle at height 1 has radius 1.73205.
- **How found:** SolidCraft's check of `cone_iso_circle_at_axial_height` (WR-704) on unbounded cones (#2313). The results are the same with that flag on and off.
- **Confirmed:** against the pinned C++ build: the `surface.cone_p1` difftest fixtures `fixtures/surface.cone_p1/issue_2313.json` (both cones, inside out or not, swapped or not, at height 1, next to the apex and 1e4 from it) agree exactly with the C++ with the flag off, including the circle at 1234562.33 and the missing circles. The fork (tangency-labs/smlib `5e8c9c1`) has the same constructor and conversions.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.cone_unbounded_parameterized_as_nurb`, off by default. With it on:
  - the unbounded constructor rotates the generator for `|semi-angle| > SM_EFF_ZERO`, and a cone of negative semi-angle runs from `-SM_INFINITE_PARAMETER` to its apex;
  - both conversions map an inside-out unbounded cone's height linearly onto the NURB's reversed interval, as for a bounded cone, except where negation already maps the STEP interval onto the NURB's (an interval symmetric about 0, the infinite cylinder's), which keeps upstream's negation.
  The iso circle through a point on the cone is then on the cone at the point's axial height to 1e-9 relative, next to the apex and 1e4 from it, on both placements tested (one tilted), with or without `cone_iso_circle_at_axial_height`, and with the parameters swapped or not. Bounded cones are unchanged.
- **What the flag changes:** with it forced on for every call, the cases that differ from the pinned C++ are all on unbounded cones that are inside out (not cylinders) or of negative semi-angle: 2,437 of 20,736 in `surface.cone_p1` (1,563 of them iso circles), 33 of 1,713 in `surface.cone_p2` and 823 of 17,590 in `surface.surface_p2` (which reruns `surface.cone_p1` inputs). Of the 3,293, 1,332 are on inside-out cones of positive semi-angle, 1,472 on negative-angle cones that are not inside out and 489 on both. No case outside those classes changes beyond 1e-9 (an inside-out infinite cylinder did before the symmetric-interval exception); `surface.cone_p3`, `surface.sphere`, `surface.surf_of_revolution_p3`, `surface.surf_of_extrusion_p2` and `intersect.api_intersectors` are unchanged. The ops that move are the iso circle, the build state (domains, NURB points), the inversion, the parameter conversions, transforms and evaluations of those cones: by design, since their heights and NURB parameters are what the fix changes.
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2313_unbounded_cone_iso_circle.rs`.
