#### WR-445. A fillet corner setback is measured from the far vertex on an edge whose primary edge use runs against its curve
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`, `SmFilletVertex::CalcSetBackVert` (about `:3336`): `SmExtent1d sIvl = pCurve->GetNaturalInterval()`, then `if (pE->GetVertex() == cpVertex)` walks `dSetBackDist` from `sIvl.GetMin()`, else walks a reversed copy of the curve.
- **What happens:** the direction is chosen with `GetVertex()` (the primary edge use's vertex), but the walk is over the curve's natural interval. Where the primary edge use runs against the curve, `GetVertex()` is the curve's end vertex, so the setback point is placed from the far end of the edge, whichever end is filleted. The curve's natural interval also need not be the edge's own interval.
- **Trigger:** a box 60 x 40 x 20 with r5 rounds on the three edges at the corner (60, 40, 20) and a setback of 8 on that corner (`SmFilletCorner::SetSetBackDist(8)` between `CreateFilletCorners` and `DoFilleting`): the x-edge round ends at x = 8 instead of x = 52. The result has 15 faces, a bounding box maximum of 60.255 and a volume of 47241, outside the convexity bounds [47271.976, 47384.893], and the six section-end vertices such as (52, 35, 20) and (55, 32, 20) are missing.
- **How found:** SolidCraft's setback corner test through the fork's `smlib_blend_setback` (tangency-labs/solidcore#1169).
- **Confirmed:** yes (reported with the fix and the capi test `corner_setback` in #1169).
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib walks the edge's own interval (`GetInterval()`) from the end `GetStartVertex()` marks, with a negative arc length from the other end.
- **Rust port:** `set_back_frame` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_geom/p2.rs`, used by `FilletBrep::calc_set_back_vert`) behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_setback_from_start_vertex` it walks the edge's own interval from the end the filleted vertex marks, as the fork does. Test `a_setback_is_measured_from_the_filleted_vertex_with_the_fix`: every end of every edge of a box, where upstream is right on the edges whose primary edge use runs with the curve and measures from the far vertex on those that run against it. Test `a_setback_walks_the_edges_own_interval_with_the_fix`: the box's edges on lines running 10 past both ends, each edge keeping the middle part as its interval, where upstream starts outside the edge from either end.
