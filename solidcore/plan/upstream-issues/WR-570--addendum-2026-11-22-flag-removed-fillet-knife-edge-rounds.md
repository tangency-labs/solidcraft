Updates: WR-570

#### WR-570 (update, 2026-11-22). `fillet_knife_edge_rounds` is removed; a sheet's two sides are not a cusp

The knife-edge path is now the only one. `SmEdge::GetBlendEdgeuse`'s port (`topology::edge::blend_edgeuse`) refuses tangent faces only when their binormals point apart. The constant radius, constant distance and variable radius solvers orient their offsets by `sector_sides`. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::fillet_knife_edge_rounds`, its doc file, the solvers' upstream orientation path (`edgeuse_orientations`, `edgeuse_frame`), every test setting of the flag, the flag from the `fixes` lists of `fixtures/solidcraft/851-knife-edge-blends.json`, and the test half that pinned upstream's refusal (`wr_570_without_the_opt_in_the_knife_edge_is_refused`).

**The Nx1 degeneracy test (not the fork's).** The fork's change to `SmFilletNx1Corner::CheckNx1Degeneracy` (tangency-labs/smlib b7ec55b) takes every pair of anti-parallel side normals for a cusp, so an ordinary corner. Two face uses of one face (a sheet's two sides) also have anti-parallel normals, at every point of any surface, by the definition of a face use. Their parting is zero to every order: a ball of radius r touching both sides at a point P has its centre at P + r n and at P - r n, so r = 0. No ball lies back from the vertex, and the corner stays degenerate, as upstream answers. `check_nx1_degeneracy_status` (`blend/fillet_corner/p1.rs`) therefore takes anti-parallel normals for a cusp only when the two side edge uses lie on different faces, or on one face through one face use (a periodic face whose seam is a real edge). For two face uses of one face its result is upstream's, bit for bit. `sector_sides` already refuses the same pair (the curvature difference is zero: "the faces osculate").
