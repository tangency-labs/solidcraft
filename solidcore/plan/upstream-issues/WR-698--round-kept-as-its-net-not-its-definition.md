#### WR-698. A round between NURBS faces keeps only its B-spline approximation, not its definition
- **Severity:** Wrong result (geometry off the rolling ball beyond the body's tolerance)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet executive)
- **Location:** the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`) builds every constant and variable-radius round between NURBS faces as a traced B-spline net (`SmFilletSurfaceGenerator`), and its file reader refuses `SURF_BASE_TYPE + 41`. The fork adds `SmProceduralBlend` (`SmProceduralBlend_TYPE`) with its certified net (`SmBlendNetScope`), tangency-labs/smlib@014f66f to 729f893, smlib#79 step 7, merged at 5e8c9c1.
- **What happens:** the round's surface is the traced net alone: nothing records the faces the ball rolls on, its radius or radius law, or the section, and the net carries no bound on its distance from the rolling ball. Where the trace is coarse the face lies off the ball by far more than the body's tolerance (on the goldens' `sweep5-nurb_box1-all-r0.5`, up to 2.5e-2 on an r 0.5 round), and its area and volume move with it. The fork keeps the round as its definition, which evaluates exactly, and uses a net certified within a stated bound for the surface cache and tessellation.
- **Trigger:** any round between NURBS faces, e.g. the goldens unit `blend.fillet_executive_p3` (38 rows).
- **How found:** SolidCraft, tangency-labs/smlib#79 (the generic blend's step 7), solidcore #2273.
- **Confirmed:** the fork at 5e8c9c1 builds the definition and the pinned C++ the net; solidcore's port (#2273, #2486) checked against the fork's records (`wr698_*` tests).
- **Rust port:** `crates/solidcore/src/surface/procedural_blend/` (`ProceduralBlend`, the certified net, the exact classes and the chamfer by its definition, which is not the fork's, #2486). It was the opt-in `UpstreamFixes::fillet_round_as_definition` until the 2026-11-16 addendum removed the flag. This record was first cited in that flag's docs; the file was added with that addendum, because the ID had no record of its own.
