#### WR-265. `ManifoldBoolean` leaves a void's shell first in the solid region when a union seals the void
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Topology, attributes, solvers
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmMerge.cpp`, `SmMerge::ManifoldBoolean`: step 6 (`:4715`, `m_vTI.DeleteFaces`) and step 8 (`:4905`-`4915`). The region merge is `SmBrep::DeleteFace` (`source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp:7072`), which moves the deleted region's shells to the surviving one with `PostInsert` (`:7186`). The check that refuses the result is `SmBrep::AssertValid` test 12 (`SmBrep.cpp:35020`).
- **What happens:** `SmRegion` takes its first shell as the one that encloses it, and test 12 asks every other shell to lie inside it (`SmShell::IsInnerShellOf`). In a union that seals a void, `MakeFaceuse` (step 5) splits off a region bounded by the cavity, and step 6 merges the operand's solid region into it: the cavity's shell stays first and the outer shell is appended after it. Step 8 calls `FindAndSetInfiniteRegion()` with its header default `SM_VS_INFINITEREGION_SWAP` (only the void flags are swapped), or not at all when the infinite region is current, so the order is never put right. The geometry and the void flags are correct; only the shell order is wrong, and test 12 refuses the body.
- **Trigger:** a cube 100 less the box 10..90 x 10..90 x 10..110 (an open cup), united with the box 0..100 x 0..100 x 90..100 (a lid): one solid with a sealed 80^3 void, volume 488000; either operand order. A cube with a void made by a difference, and unions, differences and intersections of a body that already has a void, keep the right order. SolidCraft rig `booleans/steel-022-chs-48-through-chs-60-cross` (CHS 48.3x4 through CHS 60.3x4, a void where the bores cross) and `steel-026-chs-k-joint-overlapped-braces` (test 12 and, separately, an edge/vertex gap) on tangency-labs/solidcraft branch `kernel-smlib`.
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig; traced with an `SM_API` program against the fork's C++ build, logging the solid region's shells after steps 5 to 8.
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the C++ build (the fork at tangency-labs/smlib@5af7070, x86-64 Linux, g++): test 12 fails, and putting the outer shell first (`SmRegion::SetList`) makes the check pass with nothing else changed. The pinned `c9c5979` has the same steps 6 and 8.
- **Rust port:** `put_outer_shells_first` (`crates/solidcore/src/boolean/merge/p4.rs`) follows the fork's fix (documented deviation): after step 8, a Union, Difference or Intersection whose result is a manifold solid with more than two regions, the infinite region first, runs `set_region_is_void_flags_for_nested_solids`, which makes each nested region's outer shell its list head (its void flags on a manifold solid are step 7's). The regression test `union_sealing_a_void_puts_the_outer_shell_first` cannot exercise it yet: the whole Boolean stops at `SmFace::GetBrep` (unit `topology.face_p4`, unported).
