Updates: WR-49

#### WR-49 (update, 2026-11-15). `volume_representational_end_continuities` is removed

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246, batch surface2). `BSplineVolume::calculate_continuities_simple` (`surface/b_spline_volume_n_lib.rs`) with `calc_geometric == false` always sets both end knots to `ContinuityType::Discontinuous` and takes the representational continuity (`degree - multiplicity`) and the lowest continuity over the interior knots only, so a single-span volume has no discontinuity (`CInfinity`). Upstream left the last knot `Undefined`, took knot `0` as `C0` and so reported discontinuities on every clamped volume. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::volume_representational_end_continuities`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in the kernel rig's `fixes.json` and in `issue_2260_gn_pipe_refined_range_end.rs`, and the upstream branch.

- **Defaults:** SolidCraft's preset had the flag on, so the app's results don't change. `Kernel::default()` changes for representational continuities of B-spline volumes and for `has_discontinuities(..., false)` of chains holding one.
- **Goldens:** `surface.b_spline_volume_n_lib` moves 721 rows and `surface.volume_p2` 10, all representational continuity or `HasDiscontinuities` cases. The goldens tests `surface_b_spline_volume_n_lib::representational_continuity_tests` and `surface_volume_p2::representational_continuity_tests` replay every such case as the runner calls it, assert the whole stored record against the closed form, and assert that each moved row is replayed. Distinct knots and multiplicities come from `BSplineVolume::knots` (the tolerance grouping WR-49 does not touch) in the first test and from the raw knot vectors in the second. The interior class `C^(degree - multiplicity)` saturates at `C0` for a multiplicity above the degree, as upstream's representational convention does. That case is really discontinuous, but WR-49 does not change the interior values.
- **Tests:** the WR-49 unit tests in `surface/b_spline_volume_n_lib.rs` run on the default kernel; their default-kernel halves, which pinned upstream's `C0` / `Undefined` ends, are removed, and `wr49_volume_wrapper_and_flag_lookup` is now `wr49_volume_wrapper` without the flag lookup. `representational_continuities` and `has_discontinuities` now expect the closed form (ends discontinuous; a single span has no discontinuity).
