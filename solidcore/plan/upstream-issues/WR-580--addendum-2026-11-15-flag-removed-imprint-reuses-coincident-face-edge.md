Updates: WR-580

#### WR-580 (update, 2026-11-15). `imprint_reuses_coincident_face_edge` is removed, and the edge is taken on an enclosure

The fix is the default and its flag is gone. `ProtoTopologyManager::imprint_edge_definition` (`topology/proto_topology/p2.rs`) always takes an edge Brep2's face already has between the definition's two Brep2 vertices along the definition's curve as the definition's edge there (`along_face_edge`). Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::imprint_reuses_coincident_face_edge`, its off path, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, and the test settings.

The decision is no longer sampled. The fork (and the opt-in port) took the edge on `SmTools::FindCoincidentEdge`: its mid point dropped onto the curve and the curve/edge distance at 30 samples. Now an edge qualifies when it joins the two vertices (`SmVertex::GetCommonEdges`), is an edge of the face (`SmEdge::IsConnectedToFace`), and its curve over its interval and the definition's curve over its interval are proved within the larger of the face's and the definition's tolerance of each other as wholes, in both directions (`curve_runs_along`, `intersect/curve_class/p3.rs`). The proof cuts the two curves into pieces paired end to end; each pair is bounded on its certified control points (`Curve::piece_hull_points`): each piece lies within its control points' distance from its chord and its chord within that distance of it, and the two chords lie within their end gaps of each other, every rounding included (`pieces_apart_bound`, the bound `piece_within_band` already used). A pair not proved is halved, the curve at its middle parameter and the edge at the drop of that point (the drop only chooses the cut: any cut covers both curves). A failed drop, a piece with no certified hull, or more than 1024 pairs proves nothing, and the definition is imprinted as upstream does. `ProtoBrep::face_coincident_edge` stays for the topology intersector's `edges_coincide`, which proves its pairs with `coincidence_bound`.

The edge taken carries the bound it was proved under. The edge it stands for would have been made with the larger of the face's and the definition's tolerance; the taken edge's tolerance (with its vertices') rises to that same bound (`ProtoBrep::raise_edge_tolerance`, `SmEdge::SetTolerance` growing only), so the relation to Brep1's edge, which raises both to the larger of their own tolerances, never mates two edges farther apart than both tolerances (the CodeRabbit point on #17629).

Literature the bound follows: the convex hull property of a B-spline piece's control points (Piegl and Tiller, *The NURBS Book*, 2nd ed., 1997, the strong convex hull property of B-spline curves), which puts each piece within its control points' distance from its chord; subdivision of the curves until that bound is small enough (Lane and Riesenfeld, "A theoretical development for the computer generation and display of piecewise polynomial surfaces", IEEE TPAMI 2(1), 1980), here by halving the parameter range; and the rounding factor `gamma(3)` of Higham (*Accuracy and Stability of Numerical Algorithms*, 2nd ed., 2002, §3.1), which makes the computed bound an upper bound in floating point.
