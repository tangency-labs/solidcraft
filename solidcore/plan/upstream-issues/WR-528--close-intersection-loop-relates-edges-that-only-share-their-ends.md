#### WR-528. `SmTopologyIntersector::CloseIntersectionLoop` relates two different edges that only share their end vertices
- **Severity:** Wrong result (an edge 37.9 off its face, a UV trim of two points)
- **Area:** Booleans
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmTopologyIntersector.cpp`, `SmTopologyIntersector::CloseIntersectionLoop` (def. `:6108`), the sole-common-edge branch (`:6185`-`:6230`).
- **What happens:** When an unmated edge of one body runs between two vertices that are mated to vertices of the other body, and exactly one edge of the other body connects those mates, the function drops the edge's midpoint onto that edge (`DropPoint`, `SM_SO_MINIMIZE`). If the drop succeeds, it relates the two edges as one. A closest-point drop always succeeds, so any two edges between the same pair of vertices get related, however far apart they run.
- **Trigger:** a crescent prism (the r 6 disc centred on the rim less the r 10 disc) standing on top of a cylinder r 10, its corners on the rim and its r 10 arc through the cylinder's seam vertex, united with the cylinder (#2177). The rim splits at the crescent's corners, and the rim's long arc is the sole edge between them. That arc is related to the crescent's r 6 arc, and the r 6 wall is built on the rim's circle. The fork's own trigger, the prism standing against the cylinder's wall, joins cleanly at the pin.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft. The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@e881cdb ("boolean: closing an intersection loop relates two edges only when one lies on the other"), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** yes, by the C++ build (#2177). A crescent prism standing on top of a cylinder's rim (cylinder r 10, z 0..10; the crescent skinned between its r 10 and r 6 arcs, z 10..15, its corners on the rim and the r 10 arc through the seam vertex (10, 0, 10)) united with the cylinder fails SMLib's level-2 check (`SmBrep` #59 and #82, offset 38.157568) and `SmApiBrepComputeVolume` with 1001 on the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`, usd-brep v1.0.1) and identically in the port: `fixtures/boolean.api_brep/issue_2177_crescent_on_top_of_rim.json` compares both. The crescent standing against the cylinder's wall (#1615's first trigger) did not reproduce.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.close_loop_relates_only_coincident_edges` (`TopologyIntersector::close_intersection_loop`), the edges are related only when the drop distance is at most `max(2 * dMaxDist, the two edges' tolerances)`; otherwise the function goes on to the coincidence intersection as before. `tests/wr_528_crescent_on_rim_join.rs` joins the prism with the cylinder, once across the cylinder's seam and once clear of it. It checks that the result passes the level-2 check and has the exact volume. `tests/issue_2177_crescent_on_top_of_rim.rs` joins the prism on top of the rim (#2177): with the field it passes the level-2 check, every UV trim has at least order points and the volume is exact; without it the check is not clean and the volume fails.
