#### WR-602. The fillet clean-up keeps a slit: an input edge left with one face on both sides
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Fillets and blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmMerge.cpp`, `SmMerge::BooleanPostProcess` (def. `:827`), the edge loop before `IsTopological` (`:881`), as the SolidCraft fork changed it for WR-459 (`m_bPostProcessKeepsFilletInput`, tangency-labs/smlib@1ccd461 and 9ddf4a8). The pinned upstream has no such keep. This record is about that keep, which the Rust port has as `Kernel::fixes.fillet_cleanup_keeps_target_faces`.
- **What happens:** with the keep, the clean-up after `DoFilleting` skips every edge marked as the fillet's input and every edge whose faces are all input faces. A round can take away the face on one side of an input edge, so that the same face lies on both its sides. That edge is then a slit inside one face, and the clean-up keeps it because it is an input edge, even though removing it would merge no faces.
- **Trigger:** the SolidCraft test-peg part (one wall face per extruded circle), with the bar's front top edge rounded r 2 to r 5. The round runs into the full-turn wall at the bar's tangent point. The top of the old edge between the front face and the wall (x 41.65, z 7 to 10 for r 3) loses the front face to the round and keeps the wall on both sides. The result has 23 edges and 15 vertices where 22 and 14 are expected.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft. The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@cac11ea ("fillets: the clean-up removes a slit, an input edge with one face on both sides").
- **Confirmed:** yes, in the Rust port. With `fillet_cleanup_keeps_target_faces` on and every edge and face of a body kept, `boolean_post_process` leaves a slit in place.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream with the fork's keep by default. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_cleanup_removes_input_slits`, an edge in `CleanupRelations::kept_edges` that has exactly one face (`GetFaces`) is checked like any other edge, and the test for an edge between kept faces needs at least two faces (the fork's `sKeptFaces.GetSize() > 1`). `IsTopological` then removes the slit, as it does any edge with one face on both sides that is not a seam. `crates/solidcore/tests/fork_cac11ea_fillet_cleanup_removes_input_slits.rs` makes the slit of #1120 (WR-304: a disc extruded from two half arcs joined to a cylinder of the same radius) and runs the clean-up with every edge and face kept. With the flag on, the slit goes, the wall's seam stays, and the volume is unchanged. With the flag off, nothing is removed.
