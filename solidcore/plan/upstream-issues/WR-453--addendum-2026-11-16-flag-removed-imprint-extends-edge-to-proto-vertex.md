Updates: WR-453

#### WR-453 (update, 2026-11-16). `imprint_extends_edge_to_proto_vertex` is removed

`SmEdgeDefinition::Imprint`'s end fit continues an intersection edge's 3d and UV curves to a proto vertex that lies a few tolerances past the curve's end (fork a8caccd, with ffaf4e8's own-vertex correction, WR-498, and bec8a0b's kind-keeping continuation, WR-601). Where no continuation applies, the end is still trimmed to the curve's point nearest the vertex (WR-452). That is now the only path, and the flag is gone (#14246 group B). Removed: the field, SolidCraft's preset line, its entry in `fixes.json`, its arm in the goldens' `enable_fix` and every test setting of it.

- **Results:** three `blend.feature_executive` rows move, `fillet_each_fillet1019`, `fillet_group_fillet1019_rb23` and `single_face_fillet1019_3`, all on `Fillet/fillet1019.smb`. Only the defeatured base body's digests (`base_*`: graph, geometry, record) change; each row's status and feature bodies are as before, and no other goldens unit moves. The 2026-11-12 finding (`plan/findings/general--b21a-imprint-trim-retired-extension-kept-2026-11-12.md`) kept the flag because the extension then made `rb23` refuse (`NotEnclosed` at edge 14); that refusal is gone at this main, and `rb23` builds the same body with and without the extension (below).
