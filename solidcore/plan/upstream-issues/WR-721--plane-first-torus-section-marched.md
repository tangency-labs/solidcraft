#### WR-721. `SmPlane::GlobalSurfaceIntersect` has no torus case, so a plane meets a torus by marching when the plane comes first
- **Severity:** Wrong result (accuracy: exact circles replaced by fits)
- **Area:** Surface intersection (analytic special cases)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPlane.cpp`, `SmPlane::GlobalSurfaceIntersect`. The switch on the other surface's type has `SmPlane_TYPE`, `SmCone_TYPE`/`SmCylinder_TYPE`, `SmSphere_TYPE`, `SmSurfOfExtrusion_TYPE` and `SmSurfOfRevolution_TYPE`, but no `SmTorus_TYPE`, so a torus takes the default branch, the general `SmSurface::GlobalSurfaceIntersect`. Also `SmSurfOfRevolution::IntersectWithPlane` (the plane through the axis): it rotates the generator's `SmBSplineCurve` part into the plane, so a torus's meridian comes back as a plain rational B-spline, not a circle.
- **What happens:** with the torus first, `SmTorus::GlobalSurfaceIntersect` takes `SmSurfOfRevolution::IntersectWithPlane` with the torus's iso circles: a plane square to the axis gives its circles, a plane through it the meridians, both exact. With the plane first (a Boolean whose primary body owns the plane face, the fillet's global merge), the same section is traced and fitted. A torus R 10, r 3 cut by its equatorial plane gives circles 1.36e-6 off; cut by a plane through its axis, meridians 3.46e-6 off.
- **Trigger:** `boolean_intersection(box, torus)` with a box face on z = 0 or on x = 0 through the torus's axis. `boolean_intersection(torus, box)` gives the exact circles.
- **How found:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#10850 and tangency-labs/smlib#97 (item 2: the merge builds plane∩torus edges by intersection); solidcore #2405. Solidcore-native: the fork at its last commit (18e96f1) has no torus case in `SmPlane::GlobalSurfaceIntersect` either. Plane∩cylinder is exact in both orders already (`SmCone::IntersectWithPlane`: lines, circle or ellipse by the angle); the item's 4.2e-6 on a cylinder end did not reproduce.
- **Confirmed:** in the Rust port (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2405_blend_follow_ups.rs`, `a_plane_meets_a_torus_in_its_exact_circles_in_either_order`). With the flag, both orders give two circles (ellipse kind) within 3.6e-15 (square to the axis) and 2.2e-15 (through it). Without it, the box-first sections are B-splines 1.36e-6 and 3.46e-6 off.
- **Rust port (opt-in):** `Kernel::fixes.plane_first_torus_section_exact` (`UpstreamFixes`, default `false`).
  - `surface::plane::p2::global_surface_intersect_into` dispatches a torus to `Torus::intersect_with_plane_owned_into`, the orders and UV curves swapped as for a surface of revolution. It still needs the general intersection where the torus's own case does (an oblique plane, a plane parallel to the axis off it).
  - `SurfOfRevolution::intersect_with_plane_owned_using_into` rotates a circle generator as the circle it is, so a meridian keeps its kind.
