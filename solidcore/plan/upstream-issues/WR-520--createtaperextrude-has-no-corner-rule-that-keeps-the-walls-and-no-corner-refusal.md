#### WR-520. `CreateTaperExtrude` has no corner rule that keeps the walls' own surfaces, and reports nothing about a corner it cannot close
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Primitive creation / taper extrude
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp`, `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateTaperExtrude`; `source/SM_API/src/SmApiBrep.cpp`, `SmApiCreateTaperExtrude` (only `bFilletCorner`, mapped to `SM_OC_FILLET_CORNER` or `SM_OC_LINEAR_EXTENSION`).
- **What happens:** both corner rules close a corner's gap with separate corner faces ruled from offset extension curves, so a line's wall never continues as its own plane nor an arc's as its own cone. A corner whose walls meet across a neighbouring wall before the far end is not detected: the draft builds whatever solid the offset method leaves (a ridge with no far cap) and returns success.
- **Trigger:** `SmApiCreateTaperExtrude` of the strip (0,0)-(20,0)-(20,2)-(0,2), height 10, draft 10 degrees inward, both caps, no fillet: a 5-face closed solid (the long walls meet in a ridge at height 1 / tan 10 = 5.67) where a drafted extrusion of height 10 does not exist.
- **How found:** reported from SolidCraft (tangency-labs/solidcore#1449); fixed in the SolidCraft fork (tangency-labs/smlib 54148db, "draft corners that keep the curves' curvature").
- **Confirmed:** by the port's regression test (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1449_taper_corner_rules.rs`, SMLib's 5 faces without the fix); the fork's corner rule is not in the pinned C++, so there is no difftest comparison.
- **Fix:** a third corner type, `SM_OC_CURVE_EXTENSION`: for a profile of lines and circular arcs with a sharp corner each wall continues as itself until it meets its neighbour, along their exact seam (a conic, with exact pcurves on both walls), and a corner that cannot be closed is refused (`SmTaperCornerRefusal`: apart, not exact, wall closes) with the corner's point. Without a sharp corner it is `SM_OC_LINEAR_EXTENSION`; the offsetters (`CreateOffsetsOfManyCurves`) refuse it.
- **Rust port:** `OffsetCornerType::CurveExtension` (`curve::composite_curve`), `primitive/primitive_creation/p2/taper_walls.rs` (`curvature_keeping_walls`), `PrimitiveCreation::create_taper_extrude_with_refusal`, and `sweep::api_brep::create_taper_extrude_with_corner` returning `TaperExtrude::Refused(CornerRefusal)`. No `Kernel::fixes` field: the rule is a new argument value, so no existing call changes result (`create_taper_extrude(.., fillet_corner)` and `create_draft_sweep` never pass it). Tests: `curve_extension_refuses_the_strip`, `a_drafted_rectangle_has_six_faces_with_the_fix`, `a_half_disc_keeps_its_cone_or_gets_tangent_planes` (volume against the exact section integral), `a_free_form_corner_is_not_exact`.
- Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#1449 (with WR-521).
