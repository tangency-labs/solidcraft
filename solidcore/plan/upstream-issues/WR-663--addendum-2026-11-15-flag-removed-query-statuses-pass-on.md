Updates: WR-663

#### WR-663 (update, 2026-11-15). `query_statuses_pass_on` is removed
The loop, vertex, edge use, face, curve and surface queries now always pass on the statuses SMLib drops, as the fork does (tangency-labs/smlib 7a35f33, 6997962, 666242b, 8843967, 8311f8e, 21dcf69). The classifying queries answer only from what they computed. Propagation is the only path, and the flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246).

Removed:
- the field, SolidCraft's preset line, its entry in `fixes.json` and in `issue_2260`'s list;
- every ignore branch at the sites, and the per-file `pass` wrappers, `point_or_pass_on`, `unitize_or_pass_on`, `ignored_or_soft` and the private helpers that existed only for the flag;
- the control-point bound shortcut of `AssertValid`'s property analyses (`nurbs/b_spline_curve/analysis.rs`, #2568). With the statuses passed on, a search that fails must run so that it reports its failure, which no bound on the curve's speed or radius predicts. The analyses' memo stays.

Tests that compared the flag off and on keep the passed-on half. Tests that pinned a dropped status are re-pinned to the fork's verdict (listed in tangency-labs/solidcraft#16957).

Port corrections made with the removal (each where the flag-on path diverged from the fork or from upstream's own intent):
- **`ComputeCrvsNormal`'s written normal.** `Unitize` writes `(0, 0, 1)` into a zero vector before it returns `SM_ERR_INVALID_INPUT`, and the fork's `SER( crCrvsNormal.Unitize() )` returns with it. The flag-on path returned only the error, so callers that ignore the status (or test `== SM_ERR`, as `CreateCurveSweep` does) read an undefined or zero normal. `compute_crvs_normal_written` hands back the normal as upstream leaves it: zero first, the partial sum after a failed evaluation, `(0, 0, 1)` after a failed `Unitize`. `CreateCurveSweep` keeps it unless the status is `SM_ERR`; `PositionProfileCurves` (unchecked), the taper, the composite chamfer, the analytic rotational sweep and the planar path read it.
- **`SmCPGlobalSolver::Solve`'s closed-curve end continuity.** The fork's `SER` there fails every closed curve that cannot give three derivatives (a conic, a tangent field), so nothing could be dropped onto one. A continuity that cannot be evaluated is no evidence of one: the ends stay discontinuous, upstream's initial value. This deviates from the fork, which is wrong here.
- **`IsLineInParamDomain`'s simple-map copy.** Upstream copies the map with `SmVolume sSimpleVolume(*this, TRUE)`, which slices the derived map off: the plain `SmVolume` cannot evaluate, so the compounded check ran on nothing (with the statuses dropped, `HasPlaneCrossings` read that as in the domain; passed on, as outside it, and the domain-restricted inverses through a bend or unbend then returned points outside the domain as success). The port copies the simple map with its kind, as `IsCurveInParamDomain`'s virtual `Copy(TRUE)` does.

- **`IsClosed` decides closure from the end positions.** The fork returns `FALSE` when anything along a sample cannot be evaluated, which made a cone through its apex open and reported a false seam. Only an end with no position leaves a surface not shown closed. The position is the one the evaluation writes even when its status fails: a cone's NURB position at the apex, where its STEP parameters cannot be found. Where only the derivatives cannot be evaluated (the ends', the continuity or the middle), the ends decide closure and the continuity is Discontinuous, the rule `SmCPGlobalSolver::Solve` follows; a short iso line without a Frenet frame still shows the surface open, as the fork.
- **`LocalSurfaceIntersect` with a zero plane normal.** Upstream's `Unitize` turns a given zero normal into `(0, 0, 1)`, an arbitrary plane; passed on, the solve failed. A zero given normal is treated as none, so the plane runs across both surfaces' normals. An answer is still kept only within the tolerance.
- **The coincidence check's degenerate samples.** A boundary or drop sample where either surface cannot be differentiated (a degenerate corner) shows no coincidence, and the check goes on to its other samples. At such a corner the whole-surface check's seam direction test leaves the direction guess unchanged. The fork failed the whole check instead.
- **`SmCurve`'s point-drop boundary culling with a zero vector.** The angle between a boundary tangent (or the signed direction) and the gap is taken only when both have length. Without one there is no outside direction, so the answer is kept, as upstream's ignored `AngleBetween` leaves it; the fork's `SER` failed the whole drop, and with it 18 curve classifications through degenerate edge ends.
- **`LocalSurfaceSolve` checks a guess its equations cannot evaluate.** Upstream's solver reports such a guess as found; with the normal statuses passed on, that handed back the unchecked guess of a solve on a surface without normal. The guess is kept only for a minimum whose two surface points there (the position written even where the status fails, as at a cone's apex) coincide by the equations' own small-gap test (`SsNormalizeEnfo`: `|gap|^2 < 1000 EFF_ZERO (1 + max |p1|)`): a zero gap is the least distance. Whether a guess solves any other operation (a normalization's parallel normals, a directed minimum's tangents) cannot be checked without the normal that failed, so it is no solution. This makes `oscillating_skips_failed_guesses`'s first-evaluation rule (WR-20) unconditional at this call site, and adds the point check that rule lacks.
- **`HasDogLegNearPole` skips a control point without angles.** The fork ends the whole scan with no dog-leg when one control point's polygon angle cannot be measured (a repeated control point). Such a point is no evidence either way, so it is skipped and later points are still tested.
- **`GetKnots`' status is passed on** where the fork's `SER` does so: `FindParameterAtArcLength`'s span lookup and `CurveMaxDistanceBetween`'s sample count. Before, the port took the knots and dropped a failing classification status, against its own comment.
- **WR-407 without the fold rule.** The default join's volume no longer differs from the closed form by more than 1000 mm3, but the level-2 check reports the merged face's B-spline net doubling back (`SmBSplineSurface::Test #5 Bad NonMonotonic CPts`); the default-join test now pins that fold. `cover_refuses_folded_continuation` stays a flag: its retirement is blocked by tangency-labs/solidcraft#17215 (the fold check refuses a vanishing normal at a domain corner).

- **Results:** goldens blessed on main and with this change, compared row by row: 793 rows move in 24 units, each listed with its site and verdict in tangency-labs/solidcraft#16957.
