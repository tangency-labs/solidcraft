#### WR-344 (update, 2026-10-30). An edge whose mate failed is left to the merge

The fix assumes that with extended convex edges `EdgeuseOffset` mated the offsets of every manifold edge without a cap face. It does not when `MateEdges` fails, which `EdgeuseOffset` only reports ("Problem Extending Convex Edge - Will be Fixed Later"): on the #1528 drilled plate shelled 2 inward open at its top (tangency-labs/solidcore#1795), the hole's tube (r 7 inset) passes out through the arc's offset (r 8), the two extended offsets meet along one open curve that `TrimExtendedEdge` cannot trim for the closed hole/arc edge, and the edge is left unmated. Taking that pair as mated anyway, the merge never intersects it and the shell fails with `SM_ERR` (1001) in `make_faceuse`; the default path (upstream) intersects it and shells the plate (pinned C++ 1.0.1 agrees, `offset.offset_executive_p2` case `issue1795_plate_api_shell_2_top`).

The port's `Kernel::fixes.offset_extended_edges_mated` now leaves such an edge to the merge: `EdgeuseOffset` records the original edge uses whose `MateEdges` failed (`OffsetExecutive::note_unmated_edgeuse`), and `piecewise_merge` relates the pair only when none of the edge's uses is recorded (else the upstream `IsTangentSector` test decides). This departs from the fork's 85bbfaf, which still returns 1001 here (SolidCraft's `smlib_shell`); every edge whose mate succeeds is handled as before. Test `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1795_extended_edges_mated.rs` (the plate under the flag, `create_offset_solid` as `smlib_shell` passes it: a closed manifold solid of the default shell's volume).
