Updates: WR-703

#### WR-703 (addendum 2026-11-02): a round just under a boss's footprint failed when keep features swept the boss's walls into the sheet

- **Severity:** Failure (a round that builds in exact geometry fails with a bare `SM_ERR`)
- **Area:** Blends
- **Status in the port:** the same flag, `UpstreamFixes::fillet_keep_features`, off by default. Nothing changes with it off: the pinned behaviour, which difftest gates, never sweeps a wall. Not the fork's: the fork's `SmFilletExecutive::KeepFeaturesInBands` (eb883ad, unchanged at 18e96f1) has the same defect; its capi test `scenario_round_just_under_a_boss_footprint` holds it as known failing ("status 1001 with no reason"). Solidcore-native, no fork commit.
- **Location:** `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_executive/session/keep_features.rs`, `keep_features_on`, step 1: a boss's walls continued down from its footprint were built by `PrimitiveCreation::create_linear_sweep` in the sheet of fillet faces itself.
- **What happened:** `CreateLinearSweep` ends with `StitchAndOrient` of the body it builds in. In the sheet that body also holds the fillet faces, whose rails are laminar, so the stitch's second pass ran at twenty times the first (2 x 4e-5 x 20 = 1.6e-3) and glued any rail lying within that of a wall's edge to it. The bossed can (a cylinder r7 h48.2, a boss r2.7 on its top) with its top rim rounded 4.299: the rail on the top, a circle of radius 2.701, was glued to the wall's top edge, the footprint at 2.7 (edge tolerance 0.002). The band does not reach the boss (no crease), so the walls were deleted again and the rail went with them; `SetFilletBrepEdgeMap` found no edge for it (`FindFilletBrepEdge`, `SM_ERR`). Rounds 0.003 or more under the bound were clear of the 1.6e-3 and built.
- **Fix:** `sweep_walls`: the walls are swept in a body of their own, at the sheet's tolerance, so the sweep stitches only them, and copied into the sheet with the topology between them (`CopyFaces` through the merge). The rest of the step is unchanged.
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2404_build_near_size_bound.rs`: the bossed can rounded 4.299 and 4.2999 builds one solid of six faces, `check_brep` level 2 clean, its volume the closed form pi 49 48.2 + pi 2.7^2 2 - 2 pi (7 - r (10 - 3 pi) / (12 - 3 pi)) (1 - pi/4) r^2 within 1e-8 (found within 6e-10); at the bound, 4.3, the round takes the whole top and meets the boss's wall (five faces, the same volume form). Without the fix the three fail with `SM_ERR` from `FindFilletBrepEdge`; with the flag off they build as before. The keep-features tests (`fork_eb883ad_keep_features.rs`, whose volumes are the fork's bit for bit, and `issue_2370_keep_features_band_clear_of_boss.rs`) are unchanged.
- **Not fixed here:** within one edge tolerance (1e-5) of the bound, on either side (4.29999, 4.30001), the round builds a solid `check_brep` flags ("Face Near missing Seam") with its volume 3.9e-3 off, with or without the flag; 4.30001 is not refused. The fit stays the bound less the shared margin (`FitMargin`, a thousand of the edge's tolerances): `LargestSizeOnItsFaces` does not tell a footprint the rail runs along (closed: the round takes the face) from one it touches at a point (open), so the margin is left to #2401.
