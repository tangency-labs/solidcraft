#### WR-776. `sm_CreateAnalyticRotationalSweep` makes a line skew to the axis the cone through its end circles
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a face up to millimetres off the surface the line sweeps).
- **Area:** Surfaces and volumes
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp`, `sm_CreateAnalyticRotationalSweep`, the line branch neither parallel nor perpendicular to the axis. It takes the distances of the line's ends from the axis as the cone's bottom and top radii, and their axial positions as its height. It never checks that the line and the axis lie in one plane.
- **What happens:** a line skew to the axis sweeps a hyperboloid of one sheet. Its radius about the axis is the square root of a quadratic in the axial coordinate, not linear in it. The cone through the end circles matches it only at the ends. Between them the cone falls inside the hyperboloid.
- **Trigger:** a whole turn of the one-curve profile [600, 0, 0] to [700, 0, 200] about the axis through [500, -50, 0] along [40, 80, 300]. The cone is up to 3.85 mm inside the hyperboloid, about 44 % of the way along the line, and the display mesh of the result is 3.80 mm off. The same line about an axis in its plane ([500, 0, 0] along [40, 0, 300]) is exact.
- **How found:** an application's revolve about a picked axis, checked against the distance from the axis that every point of an exact surface of revolution keeps.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run here; the port shows it, and the closed form gives 3.85 mm.
- **Rust port:** `primitive_creation::p1::analytic_rotational_sweep` returns `SM_ERR` before any edit for such a line, when `coplanar_with_axis` (their common perpendicular at most the B-rep's tolerance, or parallel lines) does not hold. The caller, the whole-turn `create_rotational_sweep`, then gives the line its `SurfOfRevolution`, or with `revolve_analytic_profile_faces` its `analytic_revolution` first, which already refuses a curve outside one half-plane through the axis. Not behind a flag: the default result changes, and only for skew lines. Test: `p3::sweeps::tests::a_line_skew_to_the_axis_revolves_onto_its_hyperboloid`, run with the fix both off and on. It checks every sampled surface point's distance from the axis against the profile point's at the same axial coordinate, from the numbers alone.
- **Related, open:** the perpendicular branch, in both `sm_CreateAnalyticRotationalSweep` and the whole turn's per-curve rule, makes the flat ring between the ends' radii. For a skew line square to the axis whose nearest point to the axis lies between its ends, the ring's inner radius should be that nearest distance. Its surface of revolution would fold over itself there, so refusing it is not the fix. Not changed here.
