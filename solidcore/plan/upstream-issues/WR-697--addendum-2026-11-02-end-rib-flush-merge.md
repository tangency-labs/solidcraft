Updates: WR-697

#### WR-697 (update, 2026-11-02). With the End's rail end on the end face's surface, a traced end rib's merged last span leaves its UV curve 4.1e-5 off its 3D curve (Bad Gap)
- **Solidcore-native:** fork 3e26f76 has the same defect (b5bb747 and 18e96f1, same offset 4.1077428e-05). The fix is solidcore's. There is no fork commit for it.
- **Severity:** Wrong result (an invalid body: check_brep `SmBrep::Test #4 Bad Gap`)
- **Area:** Fillets (corners); surface/surface intersection
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmSurfaceIntersector.cpp`, `SmSurfaceIntersector::FlushCurve` (pinned :5828, fork 18e96f1 :6035). A last span shorter than a tenth of the span before it is merged into that span (`dTDeltaFinal/dTDelta < 0.1`: the next-to-last point is skipped). `TraceCurve` tested each traced span with `TestSpanAccuracy`: the 3D Hermite span and both UV Hermite spans mapped onto their surfaces agree within `m_dThisApproxTol3d` at five interior parameters. Nothing tests the merged span, which is longer than either of them. Reached here through the fork's 3e26f76: at a mixed-convexity End the rail ends on the end face's surface (`CalcRailIntEndSurface`). The end rib then has no curve yet, and `CalcFilletIntSideFace` traces it (`sm_SrfSrfIntersection`, `sm_TwoPntsIntersection`, `DoPointIntersection`). Before 3e26f76, `CalcRailIntExtendedEdgeuse` had given the rib its curve, so no rib was traced.
- **What happens:** SolidCraft's `fillet_tabbed_plate_outer_loop_and_partial_selection_after_hole`. The plate is #2176's (an R 25 disc 2 thick with a tab x = +-4, R 2 tab corners), with a hole r 1.5 at (0, 262/9) and its top rim already rounded r 0.5. The tab's run on the top (its two sides, two corners and top) is rounded r 0.5 in one call. Each side round ends at a mixed-convexity End on the R 25 wall. At x > 0 the rib is traced from the top rail's end (3.5, sqrt 612.75, 2) to the side rail's end (4, sqrt 609, 1.5). The trace's points are at curve parameters 0, 0.1586, 0.3151, 0.4698, 0.6228, 0.7741 and 0.7867. The last span (0.0126) is under a tenth of the one before (0.1513), so the point at 0.7741 is dropped. In the merged span the round's UV curve, mapped onto the round, lies up to 4.1077e-5 from the 3D curve (edge tolerance 4e-5). The other spans peak at 3.5e-5 to 3.7e-5. At x < 0 the rib is traced from the side end, the steps are shorter, and the gap is 7.9e-6.
- **Fix (under `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner`, no new flag):** `FlushCurve` merges the short last span only when the merged span passes the trace's own span test (`SurfaceIntersector::span_keeps_accuracy`: the same tangent-turn and five-point distance tests as `test_span_accuracy`). Otherwise both spans stay as traced. `crates/solidcore/src/intersect/surface_intersector/p1.rs` (the test) and `p2.rs` (the merge). With the flag on, every traced intersection is affected, not only fillet ribs. Flag off is the pinned code.

That test's pinned figure, 0.375912317213533, came from an invalid body. With the fix the body is clean and the round removes 0.375912307117687. The test now checks each body.
  - The peg chamfers (#2307's `issue_2307_peg_chamfers`) are unchanged by the fix: 9432.066057580230 against the fork's 9432.066057685654.
