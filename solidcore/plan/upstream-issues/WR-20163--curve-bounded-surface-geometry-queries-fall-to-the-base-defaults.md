#### WR-20163. A curve-bounded surface answers its geometry queries with the base-class defaults, not its basis's

- **Severity:** Wrong result (queries refused with `SM_ERR`, and callers that need them refuse or fall back)
- **Area:** Surfaces (curve-bounded surface)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/inc/SmCurveBoundedSurface.h` (the class overrides only `GetRootSurface`, `GetNaturalUVDomain`, the evaluators, `operator==`, `WriteToDB` and `AssertValid`); the base bodies it inherits in `source/SMLib/src/SmSurface.cpp` (`GetKnots`, `GetDegree`, `GetNumberControlPoints`, `GetMaxAnalyticDomain`, `IsBilinear`, `IsParallelToVector`, `CreateIsoParametricCurve` and the others below). solidcore: `crates/solidcore/src/surface/surface/` (the `SurfaceKind::CurveBounded` arms and guards), `Surface::geometry`.
- **What happens:** every evaluator of the class passes `uv` unchanged to its basis surface, so the surface is its basis over the same parameters. The queries it does not override run the `SmSurface` bodies: `GetKnots` clears its output and fails, so continuities, `GetDomainWithContinuity`, `LiftCurve` and the surface cache tree fail on the surface (the tree then leaves later solves with no cache, `SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER`); degrees, control-point counts and the analytic domain are the base defaults; `SM_CAST_PTR(SmBSplineSurface, ..)` fails, so the point solvers' control-net guesses, the boundary-plane test of the surface intersector, the cache tree's Bézier patches and the gap sampling at knot spans treat it as general geometry.
- **Trigger:** any curve-bounded surface, for example a face's surface read from an `.smb` file.
- **How found:** SolidCraft issue 20163 (a lift across a crease of a curve-bounded surface's basis failing on its knots).
- **Confirmed:** yes, in the port, which ran the base bodies: `surface::curve_bounded_surface::tests` (`knots_are_the_basis_knots`, `continuities_and_continuous_domains_are_the_basis_ones`, `a_curve_across_the_crease_lifts_as_on_the_basis`) fail without the change. The pinned C++ was not run.
- **Rust port:** a query whose answer is a function of the basis geometry over its parameters answers the basis's: knots, degrees, control-point counts, the analytic and STEP domains and their conversions, STEP inversion, iso curves, point drop, plane, line and surface intersection, coincidence, the bilinear, planar, closed, parallel, seam, singularity and degenerate point and curve tests, the surface's bounding boxes (upstream's `CalculateBoundingBox` is the box of the whole surface's cache tree, not of the bounded region), the out-of-bounds evaluation switch, and the kind tests that pick a B-spline code path (`Surface::geometry`). The basis takes the surface's owner, owning face and zone tolerance. Constructors and edits (copy, mirror, offset, trim, split, reverse, reparameterise, swap, transform) are refused for the kind as before, and `operator==`, `WriteToDB` and `AssertValid`'s boundary checks still read the boundary curves.
- **Evidence:** in the 24 golden units that build a curve-bounded surface, every changed value equals, bit for bit, the same query on the bare basis surface (a run with the basis built in its place), except values that record the curve-bounded object itself (its type code, its boundary reports) and steps after an edit the kind refuses. `surface::curve_bounded_surface::tests::geometry_queries_answer_the_basis_s` keeps that comparison as a unit test over several bases.
- **Consequences recorded elsewhere:** the surface now meets the basis's own defects, and the rows whose new values are not proved are known failing:
  - `heal.gap` `gen0373`, `gen0398` and `gen0102` drop to feet not certified the nearest points (tangency-labs/solidcraft#17773).
  - `heal.gap` `gen0036` and `gen0276` drop to curves, which no certificate covers.
  - `heal.gap` `gen0061`, `gen0242`, `gen0281` and `gen0307` meet the B-spline gap sampling's refusal of a sample domain that spans no knot interval (`SmGap.cpp:1764-1767`).
  - `surface.surface_p1` `domhist0052` and `domhist0079` start off the natural domain (tangency-labs/solidcraft#20239).
  - `surface.surface_point_solve_p3` `matrix_0222`, `matrix_0289`, `matrix_0627` and `matrix_1156` report maxima that in-domain points beat (tangency-labs/solidcraft#13684), and `matrix_0370` a local minimum a neighbour beats (tangency-labs/solidcraft#17773).
  - `surface.surface_p1` `domhist0070` reads the continuities of a cache without a tree (tangency-labs/solidcraft#20054).
  - UB-273's generic continuity failure is gone (its addendum of 2026-11-21).
