#### WR-740. The point/curve closest-point Newton step stalls near a centre of curvature, so no closest point on an arc is found from near its centre
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Curves
- **Location:** `SmFindPCExtremaEFO::Evaluate`, the function `SmCurve::LocalPointSolve` solves for `SM_SO_MINIMIZE` (and so every `GlobalPointSolve` of a B-spline curve through its leaf spans): the second order term of `F' = C'.C' + (C - P).C''` is limited to `-0.95 C'.C'` wherever it is lower ([B52], "so that it cannot change the sign of the step").
- **What happens:** near a centre of curvature the true `F'` is small and positive: for a point `rho` from the centre of an arc of radius `R`, `F' = C'.C' rho / R` at the closest point. The limit raises it to `0.05 C'.C'`, so every step is `(rho / R) / 0.05` of Newton's: the iteration converges linearly at the rate `1 - 20 rho / R` and runs out of its 100 iterations from within about `R / 100` of the centre. `LocalPointSolve` returns no answer, and the curve/point global solver, which gives up on a span when its first guess fails, finds no closest point on the arc at all, though it is unique (the arc's point at the test point's angle). On the R5 quarter arc of a rounded box's bottom loop: found from 0.05 and 0.1 off the centre, nothing from 0.02, 0.01, 1e-3, 1e-4 or 1e-6.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcore#2546 (SolidCraft #9522): a 30 x 30 x 30 box with its vertical edges rounded R5, its bottom loop chamfered d = 4.99 with `fillet_chamfer_cone_on_arc`. The corner where an arc's chamfer meets a line's (`SM_FV_RAIL_X_EDGEUSE`) starts its search for where the rail crosses the round's seam with `LoadInitialValues`, which drops the ball's centre, 5 - d = 0.01 from the round's axis, onto the filleted arc; the drop finds nothing, `LoadInitialValues` fails, and the fillet ends with a bare status 1001 from `CreateFilletCorners`.
- **How found:** the #2546 case, traced to `cr_initial_body`'s edge drop, then reproduced on the loop's B-spline alone.
- **Confirmed:** yes, against the pinned C++ in the difftest (CI run 41788948221): `curve.curve_p3` `circle_pc_extrema` (`smCurveTest.cpp:455`, a point exactly at a unit circle's centre, the gap square to the tangent, where the true `F'` is 0): the C++ gives `F' = 2.1663`, its limit `0.05 C'.C'`; the port gives 5.7e-14, 0 to rounding. `heal.face_props` `gen0010`: a surface of revolution whose V seam is a circle of radius 692.585, the face's loop vertices about 1 from its axis; the C++ limit finds none of the six vertices' closest points on that circle (the port's upstream limit, run on the same seam, finds none either), so its seam classification lacks them and reports the missing seam in U only; the port finds each at the closed-form distance `sqrt((R - rho)^2 + dz^2)` (698.4447 for the first, `rho` 0.59994, `dz` 94.7714) and reports it in both directions. Both parked on this record.
- **Rust port:** unconditional (no flag: solidcore has detached from SMLib, owner 2026-11-04, #2647). `FindPcExtremaEfo::evaluate` (`crates/solidcore/src/curve/curve/p3.rs`), when minimising, takes the full `F'` where it is positive and the gap is within the limit's own angle of the normal (`|cos(gap, C')| < 0.05`), bounded below by `|cos(gap, C')| C'.C'` so that a step never moves the foot along the curve farther than the gap is long; everywhere else `F'` is upstream's. Near the answer the step is Newton's (on a circle, from angle `a` off it, `-tan(a)` whatever `rho` is), far from it upstream's damping. Taking the full `F'` wherever it is positive was tried first: from far off the normal, where it is tiny, a step ran to its span's end, and on a 1e-5 cubic (`query.api_queries` `kind3_scale1e-05_op2`) the all-closest-points query gained a point that is no stationary point (gap 9 degrees off the normal). Maximisation keeps upstream's limit. Solidcore-native, no fork commit. It also changes the last digits of closest points that upstream found slowly (within `R / 20` of a centre); the gate's changed results are listed in #2639.
