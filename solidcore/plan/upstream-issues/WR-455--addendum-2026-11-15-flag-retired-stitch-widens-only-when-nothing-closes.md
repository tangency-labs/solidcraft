Updates: WR-455

- **Flag retired:** `Kernel::fixes.offset_stitch_at_body_tolerance` is gone. `OffsetExecutive::do_extended_offset` (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p2.rs`) always stitches the merged offset first, in one pass at the body's tolerance, as the fork's fix does. It then runs upstream's widening `StitchIntoSolid` only when both of these hold: the offset is to be a solid (an offset solid is asked for, or the body is a manifold solid), and that pass closes no finite region, so `MakeManifold` would keep nothing. An offset sheet is never widened. `curve_contacts_at_curve_ends` stays a flag.
- **Why the widening stays for that case:** with the one pass alone (the former opt-in), a sheet thickened by a few times the body's tolerance stayed open, because its two sides' seams are farther apart than the body's tolerance. The golden `shell_boundary_exactly_two_tol` went from a manifold slab to an open body. Such an offset closes no region at the body's tolerance, so the stitch widens as upstream's does and closes it. When the one pass does close a region, the lamina edges left are on open shells that `MakeManifold` deletes, and widening would weld them (in #855's plate, into a vertex whose tolerance no clearance excuses).
- **Known limits:** (1) At 3 and 10 times the body's tolerance, the widened slab joins its side walls' edges without raising their tolerance over the gap (`check_brep` Test #4), as upstream's widening always did. At 100 times the slab is valid. (2) An offset whose one pass closes some region while another part of the body stays open across a gap wider than the body's tolerance now keeps only what closed, as SolidCraft's preset already did; upstream's widening would have closed it. No goldens row, rig case or test reaches this.
- **Tests:** `issue_855_offset_stitch_keeps_the_bodys_tolerance` (`p2.rs`) runs on the default kernel, where the plate shells to its closed form 4564.924657 to 1e-6 and passes `check_brep` at every level. Before, the default refused it with 1001. `tests/issue_1599_thicken_planar_side_walls.rs` `thin_thickened_sheet_is_a_closed_slab` covers a 2 x 3 sheet thickened by 3, 10 and 100 times the body's tolerance: a six-face manifold slab of volume 6 t to 1e-6, valid at every level at 100 times. It fails under the former opt-in.
