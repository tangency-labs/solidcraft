Updates: WR-455

- **Flag retired:** `Kernel::fixes.offset_stitch_at_body_tolerance` is gone. `OffsetExecutive::do_extended_offset` (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p2.rs`, `stitch_merged_offset`) always stitches the merged offset first, in one pass at the body's tolerance, as the fork's fix does. When the offset is to be a solid (an offset solid is asked for, or the body is a manifold solid) and that pass leaves it not closed and manifold, one of two things happens:
  - If the pass closes no finite region, upstream's widening `StitchIntoSolid` runs, because nothing would survive `MakeManifold`.
  - Otherwise the widening is tried on copies whose face surfaces have cache slots of their own, holding a copy of the body's (`Brep::clone_for_trial`), with the kernel context's notify callbacks lent out so that no application sees the trial. It then runs on the body itself, with its notifications delivered, only if all of these hold for what `MakeManifold` leaves of the widened copy (every finite region kept): it is closed and manifold; it keeps every face that `MakeManifold` leaves of the one pass, and more faces than that; and for each class of level-0 `check_brep` failure (checked as a stitched body; class = reporting type, list, test and owner type) it has no more failures than the one pass's kept body, leaving out every list 2 Test #4 edge-use failure (edge/surface gaps, the kind upstream's widening leaves on its welds). A vertex whose tolerance its clearance does not excuse (the weld #855 made) is counted. Failures are counted by class, not keyed by owner, because a stitch merges vertices and edges and the survivor keeps one of their ids. When `MakeManifold` keeps nothing closed of the one pass, every failure counts. If any of these fails, the body stays as the one pass left it. The run on the body is compared with the trial's (face, edge and vertex ids, closed and manifold). If it differs or fails, it is rolled back (`Brep::with_rollback`) and the offset fails with `PortInvariant`, since listeners have already seen the rolled-back edits. The trial starts from the body's cache state and the stitch is deterministic, so this does not happen in the tests.
  A B-rep without an infinite region counts as closing no region, as before the trial was added.
  An offset sheet is never widened. `curve_contacts_at_curve_ends` stays a flag.
- **Why the widening stays for that case:** with the one pass alone (the former opt-in), a sheet thickened by a few times the body's tolerance stayed open, because its two sides' seams are farther apart than the body's tolerance. The golden `shell_boundary_exactly_two_tol` went from a manifold slab to an open body. Such an offset closes no region at the body's tolerance, so the stitch widens as upstream's does and closes it. When the one pass does close a region, the lamina edges left are on open shells that `MakeManifold` deletes, and widening would weld them (in #855's plate, into a vertex whose tolerance no clearance excuses).
- **Known limits:** (1) At 3 and 10 times the body's tolerance, the widened slab joins its side walls' edges without raising their tolerance over the gap (`check_brep` Test #4). Upstream's widening always did this, and main's default kernel does it too; the follow-up is the stitch raising edge tolerance over its welds. At 100 times the tolerance the slab is valid. (2) Trying the widening costs one widening stitch on a copy (and a second on the body when it is kept), three copies (a fourth, the snapshot, when it is kept), two `MakeManifold` runs and two level-0 `check_brep` runs on copies. This happens only for a would-be solid whose one pass closes a region but leaves the body not closed and manifold. (3) A widening that welds cut-off pieces into an extra closed region is refused only when that adds level-0 failures other than weld edge gaps, or drops a face the one pass kept. A spurious closed region that passes that test and keeps them all is kept. (4) Because failures are counted by class, a widening that clears one failure and makes another of the same class is accepted. (5) The weld edge gaps excused in the trial are bounded only by the widening's own tolerance, at most a 200th of the body's size, as upstream's.
- **Tests:** `issue_855_offset_stitch_keeps_the_bodys_tolerance` (`p2.rs`) runs on the default kernel, where the plate shells to its closed form 4564.924657 to 1e-6 and passes `check_brep` at every level. Before, the default refused it with 1001. The plate reaches the trial (the one pass closes its inner region and leaves the rims open) and the trial refuses the widening, which would make upstream's weld and the 1001 again. `tests/issue_1599_thicken_planar_side_walls.rs` `thin_thickened_sheet_is_a_closed_slab` covers a 2 x 3 sheet thickened by 3, 10 and 100 times the body's tolerance: a six-face manifold slab of volume 6 t to 1e-6, valid at every level at 100 times. It fails under the former opt-in. `merged_offset_widens_when_only_widening_closes_the_body` (`p2.rs`) covers a small closed box beside a box whose top is raised 3 times the tolerance off its walls: both are kept, volume 100 (10 + gap) + 1 to 1e-9. It fails without the trial (only the small box is kept); as a sheet's offset nothing is widened.
