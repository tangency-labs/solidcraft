Updates: WR-455

- **Flag retired:** `Kernel::fixes.offset_stitch_at_body_tolerance` is gone. `OffsetExecutive::do_extended_offset` (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p2.rs`) always stitches the merged offset first in one pass at the body's tolerance, as the fork's fix does. It then runs upstream's widening `StitchIntoSolid` only when that pass closes no finite region, because only then would `MakeManifold` keep nothing. `curve_contacts_at_curve_ends` stays a flag.
- **Why the widening stays for that case:** with the one pass alone (the former opt-in), a sheet thickened by a few times the body's tolerance stayed open. Its two sides' seams are farther apart than the body's tolerance. The golden `shell_boundary_exactly_two_tol` went from a manifold slab to an open body. Such an offset closes no region at the body's tolerance, so the stitch widens as upstream's does and closes it. When the one pass closes a region, the lamina edges left are the rims of pieces `MakeManifold` removes, and widening would weld them (here, into a vertex whose tolerance no clearance excuses).
- **Tests:** `issue_855_offset_stitch_keeps_the_bodys_tolerance` (`p2.rs`) runs on the default kernel. The plate shells to its closed form 4564.924657 to 1e-6 and passes `check_brep` at every level. Before, the default refused it with 1001. `tests/issue_1599_thicken_planar_side_walls.rs` `thin_thickened_sheet_is_a_closed_slab`: a 2 x 3 sheet thickened by 3, 10 and 100 times the body's tolerance is a six-face manifold slab of volume 6 t to 1e-6. It fails with the widening removed. The `offset.offset_executive_p2` goldens are unchanged, `shell_boundary_*_two_tol` included.
