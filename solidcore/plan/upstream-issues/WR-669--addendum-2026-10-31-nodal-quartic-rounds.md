Updates: WR-669

#### WR-669 (update, 2026-10-31). A round between faces touching at one point is exact on their nodal quartic
- **What changes:** with `Kernel::fixes.fillet_exact_round_classes`, the `NodalQuartic` class (two cylinders or cones, or one and a sphere, touching at one point the same way round) is no longer traced. `calc_rational_spine_fillet_geom` builds it as it builds a conic. The offsets meet in a quartic with a node where they touch (`sm_NodalQuartic`). Each line from the node along a direction of the cone the two quadrics share there meets the quartic once more. The lines through one of the cone's rays (a branch's tangent) sweep it rationally, so the spine is a rational quartic in that sweep's parameter. It runs from the trace's first point to its last, on the arc that keeps off the node. The candidate arcs are both sweep directions, and for an end at the node both branch tangents. Each is checked at 63 interior samples and both ends for G and W not changing sign. The spine is cut into 16 segments (`sm_PowerToBernstein`), extended as far as the node and the point at infinity allow, and the round is built on it as on a conic. Two cylinders give degrees (12, 2). A trace that ends at the node (the round fading out there) stays traced. The same commit changes the conic span: it now runs in the trace's order, an open ellipse the way that passes the edge's middle and a closed one the way the edge runs. A hyperbola or parabola trace that runs against the edge is no longer `SM_ERR`.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@dda3b5d. Tracking: tangency-labs/solidcore#960.
- **Confirmed:** in the Rust port (`crates/solidcore/tests/wr669_exact_nodal_quartic_round.rs`). The fork's C++ was not run here. The test uses an r3 pipe up z and an r1 pipe along +x from x = 1/2, its axis at y = 2, touching the first inside at (0, 3, 0). Their concave edge is rounded r1/2.
  - The result is one rational (12, 2) round face. Its rails lie on their pipes, and every sampled point is at radius 1/2 from the closed-form spine, both within 1e-9 (the fork's exact-round criterion, from tangency-labs/smlib@95ce0c5). The round's area matches a Gauss-Legendre quadrature of the canal surface between the rails and the end disc's plane to 1e-9.
  - Without the flag, or with the arm traced, the fillet fails (`SM_ERR` 1001 in the session's finish).
- **Numerics:** the fork's formula in doubles loses about 1e-10 on the spine toward the trace's last point. There W cancels by about 1e4, because that point is the second point of a short line from the node. The round is 9e-10 off the ball at that end and 1e-12 at the first. Running the same formula in 60-digit arithmetic gives 1e-14, and in doubles it reproduces the port's value. The port keeps the fork's operation order.
- **Deviations:** see the header of `blend/fillet_exact/rational.rs`. `sm_NodalQuartic` returns its outputs instead of writing out-params.
