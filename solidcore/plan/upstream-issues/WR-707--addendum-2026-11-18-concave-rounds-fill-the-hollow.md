Updates: WR-707

#### WR-707 (addendum 2026-11-18): a hollow a concave round of the same blend fills is no break-through; the refusal is always on

- **Severity:** Wrong result (the fixed path refused sound geometry)
- **Area:** Blends
- **Status in the port:** the flag `UpstreamFixes::fillet_refuse_rounds_breaking_through` is retired. A convex blend whose section breaks through a wall into the hollow behind is always refused (`NoRoomReason::BreaksThrough`, with its fit). Built instead, it leaves a self-intersecting body: on #2299's shelled box the round R7 alone takes the whole section, 38458.389, through the 2 mm roof and wall.
- **Location:** `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_executive/session/band_cuts.rs`, `ConcaveFill` and `section_breaks_through`; `session.rs`, `creation_band_breaks_through`.
- **What happened:** the test judged the outer round against the body before the concave rounds of the same blend add their material. On #1668's box, R7 on the outer top edge together with the inner roof/wall edge (the rig's `1668-fillet-both-sides-of-thin-wall-r7`) was refused ("largest radius that fits is 6.828 mm"), though the inside round fills the hollow's corner that the outer round runs over.
- **What changes:** the concave rounds of the same blend along a straight edge between two planes, of constant circular section, count as material. Each fills a prism along its edge: the hollow's corner between the two faces, out to the lines from the ball's centre through the rails, outside the ball. A piece of the section that leaves the part counts as kept when it lies in such a prism, by the body's tolerance, as a bound over the whole piece in closed form (along the arc a linear function is least at an end or at one angle). Every other concave round adds nothing, so the test only narrows: what it now passes was refused before, and nothing it refused before passes unless a fill covers it.
- **Closed form:** both R7 build: the outer round takes (1 - pi/4) 49 per length along 104 and the inner one adds it along 100, every point the outer round takes inside the hollow lying in what the inner one adds (it is farther than 7 from both ball centres, (25, 35) and (23, 33)): 39552 - 4 (1 - pi/4) 49 = 39509.938040026.
- **Regression evidence:** `solidcore-ops/tests/blend_hollow.rs`, `an_inside_round_holds_the_wall_an_outer_round_runs_over` (ignored until now) builds the closed form to the ops tolerance; it fails with the fills left out. `fixtures/solidcraft/1668-round-both-sides-of-wall.json` gains `round_7_both_sides_of_wall`. The other WR-707 tests are unchanged, now on the default kernel.
