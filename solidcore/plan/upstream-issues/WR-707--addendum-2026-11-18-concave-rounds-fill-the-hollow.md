Updates: WR-707

#### WR-707 (addendum 2026-11-18): a hollow a concave round of the same blend fills is no break-through; the refusal is always on

- **Severity:** Wrong result (the fixed path refused sound geometry)
- **Area:** Blends
- **Status in the port:** the flag `UpstreamFixes::fillet_refuse_rounds_breaking_through` is retired, so the check always runs. A convex blend whose section it finds breaking through a wall into the hollow behind is refused (`NoRoomReason::BreaksThrough`, with its fit): over the whole edge where the band is certified, at the judge's stations elsewhere (see "Between the stations"). Built instead, it leaves a body that removes what is not material: on #2299's shelled box the round R7 alone takes the whole section through the 2 mm roof and wall, volume 38458.389, less than any valid result (part of what it removes is hollow), though `check(All)` passes it.
- **Location:** `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_executive/session/band_cuts.rs`, `ConcaveFill` and `section_breaks_through`; `session.rs`, `creation_band_breaks_through`.
- **What happened:** the test judged the outer round against the body before the concave rounds of the same blend add their material. On #1668's box, R7 on the outer top edge together with the inner roof/wall edge (the rig's `1668-fillet-both-sides-of-thin-wall-r7`) was refused ("largest radius that fits is 6.828 mm"), though the inside round fills the hollow's corner that the outer round runs over.
- **What changes:** the concave rounds of the same blend along a straight edge between two planes, of constant circular section, count as material. Each fills a prism along its edge: the hollow's corner between the two faces, out to the lines from the ball's centre through the rails, outside the ball. A piece of the section that leaves the part counts as kept when it lies in such a prism, by the body's tolerance, as a bound over the whole piece in closed form (along the arc a linear function is least at an end or at one angle). A round counts only where it adds its whole section along its whole edge and the hollow ends where the round does: each end vertex of its edge has exactly one other face, a plane square to the edge (|n·dir| off by at most the tolerance over the section's legs) whose line edges stay clear of the section's corner triangle and whose material lies beyond the end (its centroid classifies inside the face), so the prism stops at a wall and the hollow cannot run on past the round. It also counts: not in the blend corner's Roll-over second pass (its concave overflow across the cliff is accepted and trimmed flush with the cliff), and not where an end of its edge is a set-back vertex or meets the edge of another blend of the same fillet (a corner or set-back patch replaces the round there, and the corners are made after this judgement, so there is no shape to bound against yet). Elsewhere a concave round whose rail leaves its face is refused (`ConcaveOverflow`), so a fillet that builds has the round's rails on their faces. A section the round's solve cannot find counts no fill. Every other concave round adds nothing. A fill is used at a station only when it is parallel to the convex round's band and the whole stretch of the band holding the station lies within the fill's span.
- **Closed form:** both R7 build: the outer round takes (1 - pi/4) 49 per length along 104 and the inner one adds it along 100, every point the outer round takes inside the hollow lying in what the inner one adds (it is farther than 7 from both ball centres, (25, 35) and (23, 33)): 39552 - 4 (1 - pi/4) 49 = 39509.938040026.
- **Regression evidence:** `solidcore-ops/tests/blend_hollow.rs`, `an_inside_round_holds_the_wall_an_outer_round_runs_over` (ignored until now) builds the closed form to the ops tolerance; it fails with the fills left out. `fixtures/solidcraft/1668-round-both-sides-of-wall.json` gains `round_7_both_sides_of_wall`. `an_inside_round_too_small_to_fill_the_hollow_is_still_refused` (R8 outside, R1 inside: the outer arc passes 0.929 from the inside round's centre, inside its ball) is still refused, with the fit (3 sqrt2 - 1) / (sqrt2 - 1) = 7.828 that counts the fill; `an_inside_round_meeting_another_round_is_no_fill` (the inside roof edge at the end rounded too) is refused with the outer round's own fit, 4 + 2 sqrt2. The other WR-707 tests are unchanged, now without the flag (their helper sets `UpstreamFixes::all()`, every remaining fix on).
- **Between the stations:** along a straight convex edge between two planes, the section sweeps a band (a cylinder piece, or a chord strip). Where every edge and face of the part that is not certainly clear of the band (beyond its span, off the arc's cylinder, behind the chord between its rails, or off the chord's strip, bounded over each one's box) is a line or a plane, the stations at which the section's pieces against the part can change are computed in closed form: the line edges crossing the band, and the folds of each plane over the arc (the extremes of a + b cos θ + c sin θ and the rail angles). The judge adds a station inside every stretch between two such events, so each stretch is judged at a point of it and the decision holds over the whole edge, with or without fills. A curved edge or face that is not certainly clear of the band, an edge with no curve or a face with no surface, or a NaN leaves the band uncertified: then no fill counts and the judge runs at its 32 stations alone, as it does on every convex edge that is not a band. There its finding of no break-through is decided at those stations, not over the whole edge. Fills are built only when the judge alone breaks through.
- **Regression evidence (ends):** `a_hollow_running_on_past_the_inside_round_is_refused` (the cavity steps out to a 1.5 mm wall at x 48.6, so the inside round stops short of the hollow's end) is refused BreaksThrough with the outer round's fit against the stepped corner, 3.5 + sqrt6; it built between stations before. `an_inside_round_ending_on_an_oblique_face_is_no_fill` (the hollow ends on a face 5.7 degrees off square) is refused with 4 + 2 sqrt2. `an_inside_round_is_no_fill_in_a_roll_over_pass_or_at_a_set_back_end` covers the Roll-over and set-back exclusions. `a_curved_face_clear_of_the_band_leaves_its_stretches_judged` (the stepped hollow with a blind hole in its roof 5.5 from the arc, R6.5 alone) is refused with 3.5 + sqrt6; it built (volume -38579.469) while any curved face in the band's box left the band uncertified. `an_inside_round_skew_to_the_outer_one_is_no_fill` (the hollow turned 0.02 rad) is refused.
