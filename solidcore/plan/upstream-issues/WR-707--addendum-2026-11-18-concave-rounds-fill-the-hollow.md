Updates: WR-707

#### WR-707 (addendum 2026-11-18): a hollow a concave round of the same blend fills is no break-through; the refusal is always on

- **Severity:** Wrong result (the fixed path refused sound geometry)
- **Area:** Blends
- **Status in the port:** the flag `UpstreamFixes::fillet_refuse_rounds_breaking_through` is retired. A convex blend whose section breaks through a wall into the hollow behind is always refused (`NoRoomReason::BreaksThrough`, with its fit). Built instead, it leaves a self-intersecting body: on #2299's shelled box the round R7 alone takes the whole section, 38458.389, through the 2 mm roof and wall.
- **Location:** `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_executive/session/band_cuts.rs`, `ConcaveFill` and `section_breaks_through`; `session.rs`, `creation_band_breaks_through`.
- **What happened:** the test judged the outer round against the body before the concave rounds of the same blend add their material. On #1668's box, R7 on the outer top edge together with the inner roof/wall edge (the rig's `1668-fillet-both-sides-of-thin-wall-r7`) was refused ("largest radius that fits is 6.828 mm"), though the inside round fills the hollow's corner that the outer round runs over.
- **What changes:** the concave rounds of the same blend along a straight edge between two planes, of constant circular section, count as material. Each fills a prism along its edge: the hollow's corner between the two faces, out to the lines from the ball's centre through the rails, outside the ball. A piece of the section that leaves the part counts as kept when it lies in such a prism, by the body's tolerance, as a bound over the whole piece in closed form (along the arc a linear function is least at an end or at one angle). A round counts only where it adds its whole section along its whole edge: not in the blend corner's Roll-over second pass (its concave overflow across the cliff is accepted and trimmed flush with the cliff), and not where an end of its edge is a set-back vertex or meets the edge of another blend of the same fillet (a corner or set-back patch replaces the round there, and the corners are made after this judgement, so there is no shape to bound against yet). Elsewhere a concave round whose rail leaves its face is refused (`ConcaveOverflow`), so a fillet that builds has the round's rails on their faces. A section the round's solve cannot find counts no fill. Every other concave round adds nothing, so the test only narrows: what it now passes was refused before, and nothing it refused before passes unless a fill covers it.
- **Closed form:** both R7 build: the outer round takes (1 - pi/4) 49 per length along 104 and the inner one adds it along 100, every point the outer round takes inside the hollow lying in what the inner one adds (it is farther than 7 from both ball centres, (25, 35) and (23, 33)): 39552 - 4 (1 - pi/4) 49 = 39509.938040026.
- **Regression evidence:** `solidcore-ops/tests/blend_hollow.rs`, `an_inside_round_holds_the_wall_an_outer_round_runs_over` (ignored until now) builds the closed form to the ops tolerance; it fails with the fills left out. `fixtures/solidcraft/1668-round-both-sides-of-wall.json` gains `round_7_both_sides_of_wall`. `an_inside_round_too_small_to_fill_the_hollow_is_still_refused` (R8 outside, R1 inside: the outer arc passes 0.929 from the inside round's centre, inside its ball) is still refused, with the fit (3 sqrt2 - 1) / (sqrt2 - 1) = 7.828 that counts the fill; `an_inside_round_meeting_another_round_is_no_fill` (the inside roof edge at the end rounded too) is refused with the outer round's own fit, 4 + 2 sqrt2. The other WR-707 tests are unchanged, now on the default kernel.
- **Known limit (pre-existing, §7.6):** the break-through judge itself runs at 32 stations along the edge (`STEPS`), not as a bound along the edge. The fill's pass-over is a closed-form bound over each piece, but it sits inside that sampled judge. With the refusal now always on, the judge only adds refusals relative to SolidCraft's old profile, which had it off.
