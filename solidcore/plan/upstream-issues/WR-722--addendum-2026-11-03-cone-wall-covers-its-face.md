#### WR-722 (update, 2026-11-03). `SmBrep::ShrinkGeometry` trims a drafted cone wall padded past its face

The record says `SmBrep::ShrinkGeometry` doesn't trim the cone wall. That holds only while the wall's padding stays within a thousandth of the face's size. The draft's walls cover their seams' reach plus a pad (5% of the reach and 1e-3 rad), so the cone's domain is wider than its face. `ShrinkGeometry` takes the face's domain for the surface's within a thousandth of its diagonal, and trims the surface where it is wider. A trim removes every trim curve, and `StitchAndOrient` projects each edge's curve again. With the flag on, a seam that crosses a span end of its cone then loses its exact pcurve (#2497).

- **Trigger:** a 240 degree segment of R 8 from -90 degrees, closed by its chord, drafted 30 degrees over 10 with `OffsetCornerType::CurveExtension` and `keep_exact_pcurves`. The face's v range is [0.0182, 0.9818] of the cone's [0, 1], and the snapping tolerance is 0.0100, so the cone is trimmed. The seams' pcurves come back as cubic fits, 3.17e-6 off their edges, in place of the cut quartics. Drafted 20 degrees, the range is [0.0059, 0.9941] (tolerance 0.0100) and the pcurves are kept.
- **Rust port (opt-in, same flag):** with `keep_exact_pcurves`, an arc's wall on its cone covers just its face, from the least angle of its seams to the greatest (`taper_walls::cone_face_angles`). Each seam's extreme angles are exact: at its ends, or where `Q x Q'` about the axis vanishes for its homogeneous point `Q(s)` less its height and the centre, a quadratic's roots (`seam_angles`). `ShrinkGeometry` then finds the face's domain to be its surface's and keeps the pcurves. Without the flag, the padded reach is kept.
- **Confirmed:** `crates/solidcore/tests/fork_3ae7bfa_drafted_cone_wall.rs`, `a_seam_cut_at_a_span_end_keeps_its_exact_pcurve`. At 20 and 30 degrees, every pcurve on the cone face lifts onto its edge within 1e-12. At 30 degrees, each seam's pcurve has one interior knot, and its lifted point there is on a knot of the cone's v.
