Updates: WR-196

#### WR-196 (update, 2026-10-27). Confirmed by difftest: a `BooleanLists` surface list always fails with `SM_ERR`; the Rust port no longer defers it
- **Confirmed: yes**, `boolean.merge_p3` fixture `fixtures/boolean.merge_p3/surface-operands.json` (PR #1357), 12 of its 15 cases: whichever surfaces (`SmPlane`, `SmSphere`), list position, operation (an invalid one too) or options, an input list that holds surfaces and no B-rep ends the call with `SM_ERR` (1001): list 1 before list 2 is read, and both before the operation is checked. The path on the base-sliced copy `new (*pContext) SmSurface(*pOrig)`, which has no geometry: `GetNaturalUVDomain` (base default: `ERR(SM_ERR)`, the zero extent) passes `CreateFaceInRegionFromSurface`'s containment check (the zero domain within the zero domain); `CreateNaturalUVTrimCurves` calls the base `CreateIsoParametricCurve` for the minimum-`u` side, whose `IsSingularity` fails its `SER(EvaluateSimple(...))` at the base `Evaluate` and, being an `SmBoolean` function, returns that `SM_ERR` as a nonzero "singular"; the singular branch's `SER(EvaluatePoint(sUV, sPnt))` (base default `SM_ERR`) then returns through every `SER` to `BooleanLists`. The copy and the list's new empty `SmBrep` leak (their `SmObjDelete` guards are never reached). A list that has B-reps never reads its surfaces (the fixture's other 3 cases compare real Boolean results with surfaces beside the B-reps). So `IsKindOf(SmPlane_TYPE)` is never evaluated on a copy, and the planar 2D path (`Boolean2D`) cannot be reached through `BooleanLists`.
- **Rust port (PR #1357):** `boolean::merge::p3::list_operand` models the sliced copy (`BaseSlicedSurface`: the zero natural domain, the `SM_ERR` of its face creation) instead of deferring to `surface.surface_p1`. `Kernel::fixes.boolean_lists_virtual_surface_copies` (off by default) copies with the virtual `Copy`, which makes a face per surface and recognises a plane list as planar (the function's documented intent).
