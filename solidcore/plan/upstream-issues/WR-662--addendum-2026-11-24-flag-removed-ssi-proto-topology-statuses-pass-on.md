#### WR-662 (update, 2026-11-24). `ssi_proto_topology_statuses_pass_on` is removed

The surface intersector's and the proto topology's statuses that SMLib drops are now passed on as the fork does (bcc6f01), unconditionally: a failed step is the caller's error instead of a result read from unset values. The flag is gone. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::ssi_proto_topology_statuses_pass_on`, its page `kernel/fixes/ssi_proto_topology_statuses_pass_on.md`, the helpers `passed_on` and `ignore_or_pass_on` and every test setting of it (tangency-labs/solidcraft#18927, together with WR-73's `proto_vertex_miss_is_false`). Two places are not a plain pass-on:

- **A vertex definition's classification with no parameters** (an interval's midpoint after a split, a region). `FindObjPoint3d` fails on it upstream and the unset point is read. `resolve_vertex_definition` now takes such a side's object point as the definition's position (`definition_object_point` in `topology::proto_topology::p1::proto`): `sm_FindPositionFromPCs` took the position from the other side, so the side gives no evidence against it. Passing that failure on refused valid definitions the library itself makes.
- **`resolve_overlapping_proto_edges`** fails after it has taken an overlap group's proto edges and edge definitions out of the intersection (the `find_interval` of a face curve, "the parameter is before the start"). Main carried on with the half-resolved intersection and built an unsound body; the boolean now refuses with `BooleanFailure::OverlapGroupNotReplaced` (`SM_ERR`, 1001). A failure before the deletions start is passed on as before.
