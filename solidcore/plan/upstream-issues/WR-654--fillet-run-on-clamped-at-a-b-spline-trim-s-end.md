#### WR-654. With WR-610's run-on, `RailEdgeuseIntersect` cannot run a side edge on past its ends when its UV trim is a B-spline, which clamps to its natural interval
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blend (fillet solver)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp`, `SmFilletSolver::RailEdgeuseIntersect` (def. `:3262`), with `SmBSplineCurve::Evaluate` (def. `source/SMLib/src/SmBSplineCurve.cpp:2997`), which clamps a parameter outside the natural interval to it unless out-of-bounds evaluation is on (`:3052`-`3056`). The pinned C++ bounds the side edge use's parameter to its own interval (`SmFilletSolver.cpp:3344`), so it never evaluates the trim outside it. The defect is in the fork's widening of that interval (WR-610, tangency-labs/smlib@6e77631), which assumes the trim continues along its line as an `SmLine` does.
- **What happens:** `SmFace::SwapUV` (`SmFace.cpp:13983`) mirrors each line UV trim with `SmBSplineCurve::CreateMirrorCurve` (`:14032`), so some faces of a box from `create_box` carry exact degree-1 `SmBSplineCurve` trims with out-of-bounds evaluation off (the copy keeps it off, `SmBSplineCurve.cpp:201`, and nothing turns it on), and the others carry `SmLine`s. The run-on tests the trim with `IsLinear`, true for both, and widens the side edge use's interval past its ends. On a line trim the solve then finds a rail end past the edge's end; on a B-spline trim every evaluation past the end gives the end point, and the solve does not converge there. Where a side edge's two edge uses mix the two kinds, the rail rolling on one wall finds its end past the edge and the rail on the other wall finds none. At a chamfer's corner, the bevel case of `SmFilletNx2Corner::AdjustTopology` then evaluates the side edge's curve at the found parameter (-0.5) and at the other rail's parameter, left at 0 by the failed solve, finds the points 0.5 apart, splits the vertex and puts an edge between two coincident points, whose face intersection fails (1001) and the fillet fails with error 3, "Fillet Corner Error: Corner Edge Computation Failure". Line trims on both walls work; B-spline trims on both leave both rails without an end there and happen not to split.
- **Trigger:** #1981. A pocket 5 deep (box (0,0,0) 60 x 40 x 10 minus box (15,10,5) 30 x 20 x 6) chamfered round its rim, leg 5.5, on the Boolean's direct body with SolidCraft's fillet flags and `keep_operand_uv_trims` (WR-361): the Boolean copies the cut box's trims exactly onto the pocket's vertical edges, B-splines on the walls x = 15 and y = 30 and lines on the walls x = 45 and y = 10, and two of the four rim corners mix them. Without `keep_operand_uv_trims` those edge uses get lines (made later by `Plane::drop_curve`) and the chamfer succeeds. Without a Boolean: a round r 9 or r 10 on the long edge (0,5) of a 5 x 5 x 30 box with the same flags fails with error 4, where the other three long edges round.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (#1981). Traced by giving the pocket's vertical edge uses chosen trim kinds before the chamfer: lines and B-splines mixed fail (also with no Boolean copy), all lines or the B-splines with out-of-bounds evaluation on succeed.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run of the fork was made, and the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`) has no run-on at all. In the Rust port, with the issue's flags, the pocket fails with error 3 and the box edge (0,5) r 10 with error 4.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default, and as before with WR-610's run-on alone. With `Kernel::fixes.fillet_trace_reaches_rails` and `Kernel::fixes.fillet_run_on_bspline_trims`, `rail_edgeuse_intersect` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_solver/p2.rs`) turns on out-of-bounds evaluation of its local copy of a B-spline trim when `run_side_edge_on_along_its_line` widened its interval (`continue_bspline_trim_past_its_ends`); the edge use's stored trim is not touched. It is its own field because it also changes results without `keep_operand_uv_trims`: the box edge (0,5) rounds (r 9 and r 10), and r 9 on the edges (0,0) and (5,5), whose widened side edges include B-spline trims, moves their volumes by 4e-14 and 9e-14 relative. The Boolean's trim copy (WR-361) is exact and unchanged.

  Tests: `blend::fillet_solver::p2::tests::wr654_widened_bspline_trim_runs_on_along_its_line` (a degree-1 B-spline trim clamps past its ends, runs on along its line once continued, and a line trim is left as it is). `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1981_pocket_chamfer_operand_trims.rs`: the pocket with the issue's flags and the fix gives `21000 - (50 d^2 + 4/3 d^3)` (19265.666666667) to 1e-8 relative and still fails with error 3 without the fix; without `keep_operand_uv_trims` the fix leaves the volume bit for bit; the box edge (0,5) r 10 fails with error 4 without the fix and with it gives the closed-form volume (30 times the part of the 5 x 5 section inside the round's circle, 236.360057721), as its mirror image (5,0) does without it, both to 1e-9 relative.
