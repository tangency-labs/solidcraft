#### WR-714. A round on a round by its definition is not built as its definition, and a definition's partials at one of its stations always take the side after it
- **Severity:** Wrong result (accuracy)
- **Area:** Fillets (sections)
- **Location:** the SolidCraft fork's `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSection.cpp` at 5e8c9c1 (step 7, WR-698): `SmGnSectionFSG::CircleSurface` (a face whose base surface is an `SmProceduralBlend` takes the old round), `SmGnSectionFSG::Build` (a face that is a blend by its definition is refused: "a blend of order 2 and up on it cannot be certified until its definition is enclosed"), `sm_GnEnclose` (a face's partials over a box only from its own NURBS pieces), `sm_SurfacePartials` (a definition evaluated with `FromLeft` TRUE whatever side is asked) and `SmProceduralBlend::EvaluateDefinition` (its partials at a station always the ones after it). The pinned SMLib has no blend by its definition: every round is a skin of its sections.
- **What happens:** a round of one radius on a face that is itself a round of one radius between NURBS faces (a definition, WR-698) is built as before: the circular generator's skin through the solver's sections, with no certified bound against the ball's circles. A definition is only C1 at its stations, where a foot crosses a knot line of a face under it (its curvature jumps there), and its higher partials there are those of the side after the station even where the caller asks for the side before it. So a round rolling across such a station would take the wrong side's series for the span that ends there.
- **Trigger:** the fork's capi case `round_on_a_definition_is_its_definition`: the twisted wedge (the block 60 x 30 x 50 under the fork's cubic `twisted_top`), its front edge rounded r 3 (a definition), the block cut by the plane x + 0.6 y + 0.6 z = 55 through that round's twist, and the cut's tangent chain of three edges rounded r 1. Without the change the round on the round is not a definition (fork 7959c0e and solidcore with the flag off). With it, it is one definition two deep: 45 spans, its sections off the ball's circle by 4.7e-14, its net at 0.309 of its certified bound, and its rail crosses the inner round's stations 11 times, each at a station of its own.
- **How found:** the fork's tangency-labs/smlib#101 (7959c0e, the test; c5b6bb7, the change; merged into `replay/1.0.1` at 852dd8c). Tracking: tangency-labs/solidcore#2298.
- **Confirmed:** against the fork built locally at 852dd8c and at 7959c0e (SMLib, SM_API and the capi, with the capi's flags: `-O3 -ffp-contract=off`, `SM_RAND_SEED=20240101`; macOS, Apple clang), through the capi test and a local driver that is not committed:
  - The inner round (the first round, r 3) is bit-identical: 194 spans, bound 3.9774904608037319e-06, its stations the same.
  - `EvaluateDefinition` of the inner round at every 7th knot of its net along s, at t = 0, 0.37 and 1, partials to order (2, 2), on both sides: 168 evaluations, 4536 values, bit-identical. 33 of the 84 (s, t) pairs differ between the two sides.
  - `sm_DefinitionPartialsOverBox` on 378 boxes of the inner round (every 9th piece of its net, orders 2, 4 and 6, a box and a point each): 18522 intervals, bit-identical.
  - End to end, the round on the round has the fork's figures: 45 spans, 11 crossings, none missed, sections off the circle by 4.73e-14 (fork 4.06e-14), the net at 0.309 of its bound (0.309). Its stations agree with the fork's to about 1e-14 and its certified bound to 3e-8 relative (1.52118474509e-05 against 1.52118479241e-05). Its inputs already differ before this change: the r 1 round beside it, which rolls on no definition, has the bound 3.91159952058e-06 here and 3.91159953810e-06 in the fork, with the flag on or off. So the round on the round is not compared bit for bit end to end.
  - The definition evaluations and station solves are not the fork's: 181076 and 122690 here, 135809 and 101687 in the fork (its test quotes 137720 and 102941 from its CI). The gap is there before the change: with the flag off, solidcore takes 134718 and 71986 where fork 7959c0e takes 88937 and 54775, none of it in the round on the round's `Build`. The change adds about the same on both sides (+46358 evaluations here, +46872 in the fork), and the `Build` of the round on the round takes 35488 here, 36376 in the fork.
- **Rust port:** on by default (the opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_round_on_round_as_definition` it shipped behind was retired with #2570, after #2647); a no-op without `fillet_round_as_definition` and `fillet_gn_section_generator`. With them:
  - the circle generator's gate (`CircularCrossSectionFsg::definition_surface`) takes a base face that is a definition when the ball is of one radius and the face is a round of one radius between NURBS faces (`definition_enclosable`);
  - the certificate (`GnSectionFsg::certify`) encloses such a face's definition over its boxes (`blend::fillet_section::definition_box`: the inner feet by Krawczyk's operator on the faces' polynomial pieces, then the arc in interval arithmetic, in the centred form where one order more is held), for an order-1 round of one radius only; a law or G^n round on it is refused, as before;
  - `EvaluateDefinition` takes the side it is asked for at one of its stations (`ProceduralBlend::evaluate_definition_sided`, the side from the evaluators' `FromLeft` flags), a foot on a knot line taking the piece it moves into, and the station memo keys a one-sided station by its side.

  The refactors that change no value are not under the flag: `sm_GnEncloseOver` (the span's pieces and guesses given, the centre's residual evaluated once), `sm_LeaderSeriesOver`'s piece, and `sm_T2` and `sm_TV` over `double` or `sm_Ivl`. With the flag off nothing changes: step 7 as #2274 ports it (the fork's 5e8c9c1).
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/fork_7959c0e_round_on_a_round.rs` (the fork's case and figures with the flag; the round on the round built as before without it).
