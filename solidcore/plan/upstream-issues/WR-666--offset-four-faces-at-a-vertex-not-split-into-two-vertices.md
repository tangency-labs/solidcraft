#### WR-666. An offset leaves a vertex of four faces whose offset planes do not meet in one point as one vertex, so a shell of a floor falling to a drain fails or comes out wrong (#2052)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Offsets and shells
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/inc/SmOffsetGeometryCreation.h:69` (the constructor's `m_dTangencyTolRadians(2.0*SM_PI/180.0)`) and `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetGeometryCreation.cpp`, `SmOffsetGeometryCreation::TrimExtendedEdge`: the near-tangent test on each adjacent face's cut (`:1294`) and the choice of the cut nearest the base vertex (`:1303`-`:1350`), at 3fd5b95.
- **What happens:** where four faces meet at a vertex (the bottom corners of a floor of four facets falling to a drain, two walls and two facets, and the drain itself, four facets), the four offset planes in general do not meet in one point: the offset vertex is two vertices joined by a new edge between two faces that did not meet before. Three things keep the offset from building it:
  - faces meeting at up to 2 degrees are taken as tangent (`IsTangentSector` with the creator's tolerance), so facets 1.2 degrees apart are left to meet by extending their trims instead of being intersected;
  - a cut where an edge's offset crosses a neighbour's offset surface within 5 degrees of tangent is dropped even where the curve passes through the surface (the walls' offset edges cross the facets' offsets 0.6 degrees off them);
  - the edge's offset is cut by the offsets of the two faces on either side of its end, and the cut nearest the old vertex is taken. Where every edge at the vertex is convex (or every one concave) the edge must end at the cut it reaches first coming from its other end: past it, it has left the cavity (or entered another face's side).
  The loops of the faces round the vertex then have gaps off their surfaces.
- **Trigger:** a trough 60 x 40 x 20 (walls from z = 0 to the top at z = 20) whose floor is four facets, each through one bottom edge and the drain (30, 20, -20 tan 1 deg), shelled 2 inward open at the top (`SmApiShellBrepFull(b, -2, TRUE, TRUE, TRUE, [top])`): refused, 1001 in `SmMerge::MakeFaceuse`. An 80 x 60 x 20 slab with that floor's pocket cut 10 deep over [10, 70] x [10, 50], shelled 3 inward open at the top: a manifold solid of volume 43302.668 against the closed form 43522.321.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft kernel regression rig (shell/cov-shell-trough-whose-floor-falls-to-a-drain, shell/cov-shell-pocket-whose-floor-falls-to-a-drain); fixed in the SolidCraft fork, tangency-labs/smlib@2e1bb8c.
- **Confirmed:** by reading the pinned source; the port follows it (the regression test's default-fixes cases above). The C++ reference was not run on these bodies.
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@2e1bb8c: tangency tolerance half a degree; a candidate within 5 degrees of tangent is kept where the curve lies on either side of the surface 1e-3 of its interval before and after the point (each side farther than `10 * SM_EFF_ZERO * (1 + |point|)`); and where exactly two adjacent faces gave cuts and every edge at the base vertex is convex (or every one concave; a lamina, tangent or shell-less edge counts as neither), each face's cut nearest the vertex is found, and when they are apart (more than ten times the approximation tolerance) the edge ends at the one nearer, along the curve, to where the base edge's middle drops onto it.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.offset_four_face_vertex_split` together with `offset_closes_loop_gaps` (WR-578, the fork's `CloseLoopGaps`, which closes the loop gaps the split leaves; the new flag has no effect without it), `OffsetExecutive::use_creator_tangency_tol` takes half a degree and `trim_extended_edge` makes the two other changes (`first_cut_from_far_end`). `tests/fork_2e1bb8c_four_face_vertex_split.rs`: the trough shells to 11718.162842036698 (17 faces) and the pocket to 43522.32137406869 (28 faces), each the closed form (the cavity as the faces' half-spaces moved by the thickness, integrated apart from the kernel) to 1e-15 relative; `offset_closes_loop_gaps` alone refuses both (1001).
- **Related:** WR-657 (`offset_splits_corner_vertices`) splits a convex corner of exactly four plane faces after the edge offsets, inside `CloseLoopGaps`; it is not needed here (with it on as well the trough comes out the same).
