Updates: WR-729

#### WR-729 (update, 2026-11-16). `face_swap_uv_transposes_trims` is removed; `SwapUV` transposes the UV trims exactly

`swap_uv` (`topology/face/p1.rs`) always transposes each trim by swapping its NURB control points' `x` and `y` (`BSplineCurve::create_transposed_curve`). The mirror through the rounded `I - 2nn^T` matrix is gone. Removed: the field, SolidCraft's preset line, the rig's `fixes.json` entry, the flag-list entry in `solidcore-ops/tests/blend.rs`, the two `= false` overrides in `blend_refusal.rs`, the `all_fixes` exclusion in `fork_smlib104_setback_corner.rs`, and the flag-off test of `issue_2479_pcurve_ends_in_domain.rs`, which pinned the ends past the domain.

- **Defaults:** SolidCraft's preset had the flag on, so the app's results don't change. On `Kernel::default()` every face that `StitchAndOrient` or another reversal swaps changes: its trims' coordinates move by up to an ulp, back onto the domain bounds.
- **Tests:** `swap_uv_transposes_every_trim_bit_for_bit` swaps every face of a box and a cylinder. After one swap, each mate's trim holds the old control points with `u` and `v` exchanged, bit for bit. The weights stay bit for bit, and a 2D trim is made 3D at `z = 0` as the mirror makes it. After a second swap, every trim is back bit for bit. The 54 trims checked include 3 rational ones (the cylinder's circles) and one ellipse, which takes the conic arm. `every_pcurve_ends_in_its_face_domain` now runs the scan on the default kernel. `fork_smlib104_setback_corner.rs`'s `all_fixes` now includes the transpose. Its pinned volumes still hold within `PIN_TOL`.
- **Goldens:** 298 stored rows in 8 units move. The full arrays of base and branch were compared entry by entry:
  - **Gap to zero** (`topology.assert_valid` 70, `heal.heal_data_p1` 23, `p2` 47, `p3` 16). Two kinds of entry change. One is a vertex/edge/face gap: it shrinks from at most 1e-15 to 0 or the record's undefined value. In the `m6` rows, which move a vertex by exactly 1e-3, it shrinks from `1e-3 + 5.6e-16` to `1e-3 - 1.1e-16`. The other is the id of the use that holds the largest gap, in a row where some gap changed. A script checked this on every row.
  - **Written files** (`topology.api_files` 91, the `write_bytes` files). The ASCII and binary files keep the same tokens and layout. All 2791 differing numbers were measured: each moves by at most `4.4e-15 * max(|x|, 1)`, for example `-4.440892098500626e-15` becomes `0e0`.
  - **Same B-rep, other list order** (`topology.brep_cutting` 10, `primitive.api_trim_ops` 6, and the 6 `blend.fillet_executive_p3` rows whose `result_topology` moved). An order-free description matches exactly: surface and curve types, vertex keys at 1e-6, and every edge use's face and orientation. Matched vertices move by at most 1.4e-14. For the cutting and fillet rows, `check_brep` Level 2 is clean on both sides. Within each list the keys are unique, so the matching is a permutation of the old list. For the cutting rows, the sorted value arrays agree within 1.3e-14. For `api_trim_ops`, the parsed vertex, edge and face records agree within 2.9e-14 after sorting. The fillet's inputs (the box unions) are identical in topology and list order. Only the round's construction lists its entities in another order. These six rows' mass entries also move, by at most 2.9e-10 relative.
  - **Mass estimates** (the other 25 `blend.fillet_executive_p3` rows, and the mass entries of the rows above). The runners ask the integrator for an accuracy of 1e-4, which is an estimate and not a bound. Each entry moves by at most 3.8e-10 relative to the largest entry of its kind in the row. The kinds are area, volume and mass; the centroid's coordinates; the moments and products. Relative to themselves, entries near zero move more. The worst is a product of 2.7e-8 in two `curve-sih-box_diff_cyl` rows, which moves by 2.3e-13 (8.7e-6 of itself). Five rows round one rim of a cylinder, and Pappus gives their exact area and volume: the four `issue920-cylinder-rim-edge2` rows and `source-cylinder-rim-edge2`. On both sides these rows lie within 1.7e-7 of exact, and they move by at most 5.8e-16 relative.
  - **Tighter tolerance** (`corner3x2-union-overhang-corner-edges6-12-r1`). Two round vertices keep the default 4e-5 tolerance where the mirror grew it to 1.04e-4. `check_brep` Level 2 is clean at the smaller tolerance. The vertex `(0, 1, 9)` is now 1.1e-16 from its exact position, against 1.8e-15 before.
  - **Shell offsets** (3 `issue_460_shell` rows). The shell's fitted offset surfaces move by at most 1.6e-5. The closed shells' areas stay within 1.9e-7 (12 rounded edges) and 5.1e-9 (4 vertical edges) of the closed forms `1952 + 376π` and `3168 + 92π`. The open-top shell's area moves by 1.1e-8 relative. All three volumes move by at most 1.5e-12 relative.
