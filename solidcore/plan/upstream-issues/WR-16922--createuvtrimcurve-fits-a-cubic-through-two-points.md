#### WR-16922. `CreateUVTrimCurve` fits a cubic through two or three dropped points, a curve with fewer control points than degree + 1

- **Severity:** Wrong result (invalid geometry: the body's file cannot be read back)
- **Area:** UV trim curves, `topology.edgeuse_p3`; curve fitting, `nlib.crv_fit_p3`
- **Location:** the point-drop recovery at the end of `SmEdgeUse::CreateUVTrimCurve` calls `SmBSplineCurve::InterpolatePoints` with degree 3 on however many of its 101 samples drop onto the face at distinct UV points; with parameters given, that is `N_FitCrvKnotsAndTangents` without end tangents, whose `N_FitCalcKnotVector` fills the `n + p + 2` knots it has room for and never checks `p <= n` (`N_FitCrvInterp` does).
- **What happens:** two dropped points give a curve of degree 3 with 2 control points and the knots `u0 u0 u0 u0 u1 u1`; three give 3 control points. The curve is kept as the edge use's UV trim. `SmBSplineCurve::WriteToDB` writes it as it is, and `SmBSplineCurve::ReadFromDB` refuses it ("control point count is fewer than degree + 1"), so the body's `.smb` file does not read back.
- **Trigger:** a box [0,10]^3 united (post-process only) with a 10 x 10 x 20 box turned 45 degrees about z, then x, whose crease crosses the first box's edge y = z = 10 at depth 1e-5 (`boolean.api_brep` `crossing_creases_union_d1e-05`). Classifying the other body's faces steps off a 1.4e-5 edge; its UV trim is made by the point drop, and only two samples are farther apart than the UV tolerance.
- **How found:** writing every body the goldens measure as `.smb` (`--dump-smb`) and reading it back: this body was the one of `boolean.api_brep`'s 34 that did not read.
- **Confirmed:** on the port, which follows the C++ here: a backtrace from the curve's constructor during the case runs `create_uv_trim_curve`, `interpolate_points`, `interp_knots_and_tangents` and `interp_given_params` with 2 points at degree 3.
- **Rust port:** unconditional. `create_uv_trim_curve` (`crates/solidcore/src/topology/edgeuse/p3.rs`) fits degree 3 or as high as the points carry (two points give the line through them at their parameters), and `interp_knots_and_tangents` (`crates/solidcore/src/nurbs/crv_fit/p3.rs`) refuses a fit with fewer control points than degree + 1; every call upstream refuses keeps its error. In `nlib.crv_fit_p3` three generated cases (two points at degree 4, no tangent) are refused where they returned such a curve. Tests `bodies_round_trip_with_their_own_identities` and `bodies_round_trip_with_a_callers_identities` (`crates/solidcore/tests/brep_file_round_trip.rs`, the union above) and `knots_and_tangents_refuses_fewer_points_than_the_degree_needs`.
