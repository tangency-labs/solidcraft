Updates: WR-605

#### WR-605 (update, 2026-11-17). `fillet_removal_takes_ring_fillets` is removed

`delete_fillet_face` now always takes a face that `RingFilletRemoval::is_ring` accepts (two closed tangent manifold rails and at most one seam) through the ring removal of tangency-labs/smlib 52bfef0. The fix is unconditional and the flag is gone; SolidCraft already ran with it on. `delete_fillet_face` now asks `is_ring` on a copy standing for the B-rep (`Brep::clone_standing_for_self`): the ring test reads the rails' tangency, which may make UV trim curves, and before this a face that is no ring (`nurb_fillet.smb` face 7, two closed manifold rails that are not tangent) reached the four-sided removal with new trims, so its refusal left different UV trims than main's. The answer is unchanged, and `is_ring`'s other callers (the ring removal itself and the offset) are as before. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::fillet_removal_takes_ring_fillets`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entry in `fixes.json`, `issue_2260`'s flag list, the flag-off branch, and every test setting of it.

- **Results:** two `blend.fillet_removal` goldens rows move from a refusal (status 1001) to a removal: `nurb_fillet_face18` and `defillet_face8`. Each result is one closed manifold solid with no Level 2 report and one face fewer. Main's refusal left `nurb_fillet_face18` with 4 Level 2 reports. `nurb_fillet_face7` is still refused and its row does not move.
- **Tests:** `tests/wr605_ring_fillet_fixtures.rs` covers the two removals on the upstream fixtures. `ring_test_tests::a_face_that_is_no_ring_is_refused_as_the_four_sided_removal_refuses_it` in the goldens crate checks that face 7's refusal leaves the sampled geometry bit for bit as the four-sided removal's refusal does (it fails without the copy). `tests/fork_52bfef0_ring_fillet_removal.rs` still covers the boss's foot and rim rounds. Removed with the flag: `ring_fillet_removal_is_off_by_default` and `upstream_refuses_a_ring_fillet` (the removal failing without it).
