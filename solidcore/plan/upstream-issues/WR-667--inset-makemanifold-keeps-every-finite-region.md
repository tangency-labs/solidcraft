#### WR-667. An inset's `MakeManifold` keeps every finite region, so a floor no thicker than the wall leaves a sliver region and no manifold cavity
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Offset
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp`, `SmOffsetExecutive::DoExtendedOffset`, the `pResult->MakeManifold()` call after the stitch. Its comment says "Here we will need to select regions to keep and send in to make manifold."; it passes no list, so every finite region is kept.
- **What happens:** where the body between two faces is no thicker than the wall (a 0.5 floor between a recess from the top and a ring cut from the bottom, shelled 0.5), the offsets of the two faces cross and the merge closes the region between them. That region touches both pieces of the cavity along edges. `MakeManifold` keeps it with the cavity, so the result is not a manifold solid. The shell fails with `SM_ERR` (1001), or with the default fixes returns a body that is not a manifold solid.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft rig case shell/545, a frame shelled 0.5 open at the ring cut's outer wall. Rebuilt here as a 20 x 20 x 5 block less a recess (5..15)^2 x 3..5 and a square ring (2..18)^2 less (7..13)^2, z 0..2.5, shelled 0.5 inward open at x = 18.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@37af643 (`sm_IsCavityRegion`, an upstream defect at 3fd5b95 by its commit message).
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run. The Rust port at parity returns no manifold solid for the rebuilt frame.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.offset_keep_cavity_regions`, for an inset solid offset (`inset` and `SolidOffset`), `do_extended_offset` (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p2.rs`) passes `make_manifold` only the finite regions that are cavity (`offset/offset_executive/cavity.rs`, `is_cavity_region`). This is skipped when no region is cavity or every one is. A region is cavity when at least one of its faces has the body's boundary the wall's thickness behind it and none has it that far ahead. The test point is an internal point of the face. The normal is the normal of the face use that bounds the region. Each test is a `BrepPointSolve` against the body, hit within 10 times the body's tolerance. Faces that lie on the body are skipped.
  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/fork_37af643_inset_keeps_cavity_regions.rs`. With the flag the rebuilt frame shells to the closed form 638.75, as the default fixes and with WR-345/WR-455/WR-344 on. Without the flag it is not a manifold solid. A 1.0 floor shells bit-for-bit the same with and without the flag.
