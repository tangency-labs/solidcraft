Updates: WR-77

#### WR-77 (update, 2026-11-16). `ssi_split_curves_at_singular_points` is removed

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246). `SmSurfaceIntersector::DoIntersection` (`intersect/surface_intersector/p5.rs`) always ends with the fork's split at singular points (`surface_intersector/singular_splits.rs`) for every intersection that is not a self intersection. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::ssi_split_curves_at_singular_points`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, and every test setting of it.

- **Interaction fixed first:** with the split always on, the thickened `HoodCorner` sheet of `tests/issue_1502_thicken_rim_halves.rs` came out with an edge tolerance of 9.3e-5 (the test's bound is 4e-5). The split cuts the sheet's crease curve at a node where the offset surfaces are tangent; the offset's `join_through_tangent_points` (`offset/offset_geometry_creation.rs`) joins the two pieces back, and `BSplineCurve::create_by_joining` leaves a full-multiplicity knot at the joint. The edge's trim starts 6.3e-8 before that knot, at the node's offset vertex, and the UV trim dropped from the curve onto an offset face then fits its span `[2.3105, 5.117]` to 3.46e-5 instead of 7.25e-8. Two pieces cut from one curve meet smoothly, so the joint knot is exactly removable. `remove_exact_joint_knot` now removes every copy of it when Piegl and Tiller's knot removal (A5.8, `BSplineCurve::remove_one_knot`, all copies or none) succeeds within `EFF_ZERO (1 + max |coordinate|)`, and the joined curve's deviation grows by the bound on that removal (copies times tolerance, rounded up). Pieces traced apart keep their knot: their joint is a true break. The hood edge's tolerance is back under 4e-5.
- **Tests:** `tests/issue_2110_tangent_cylinders_union.rs` keeps its five split cases (now on the default kernel); its four upstream-behaviour cases are removed. `tests/fork_099cf96_exact_class_cases.rs` drops the flag for the pipes case, and its "the pipes join only with WR-77" check becomes "the pipes join". The unit test `a_joint_inside_a_smooth_curve_loses_its_knot_and_a_corner_keeps_it` covers the knot removal.
- **Results:** 16 goldens rows move, all of them among the 17 that #17570's leave-one-out run attributed to this flag, each a set of curves cut where the normals are parallel, or a body built from them: `surface.surface_p2` (11 rows), `topology.brep_planar_section` `upstream_torus_a_plane{2,4}` and `boolean.api_brep` `issue2110_tangent_cylinders_union_{a135_b225,checked_a0_b0,checked_a0_b90}`. The pull request's class table gives the check of each row. The 17th, `offset.offset_executive_p2` `issue1818_square_to_circle_loft_api_shell_1_top` (4 more edges in that run), stays bit-identical to main on this head (likely because the offset now joins the pieces through the tangent point again, #17210; not bisected).
