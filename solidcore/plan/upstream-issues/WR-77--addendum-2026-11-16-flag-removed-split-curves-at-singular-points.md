Updates: WR-77

#### WR-77 (update, 2026-11-16). `ssi_split_curves_at_singular_points` is removed

The fix is the default and its flag is gone (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246). `SmSurfaceIntersector::DoIntersection` (`intersect/surface_intersector/p5.rs`) always ends with the fork's split at singular points (`surface_intersector/singular_splits.rs`) for every intersection that is not a self intersection. Removed: the field `UpstreamFixes::ssi_split_curves_at_singular_points`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and `issue_2260`'s list, and every test setting of it.

- **Interaction fixed first:** with the split always on, the thickened `HoodCorner` sheet of `tests/issue_1502_thicken_rim_halves.rs` came out with an edge tolerance of 9.3e-5 (the test's bound is 4e-5). The split cuts the sheet's crease curve at a node where the offset surfaces are tangent; the offset's `join_through_tangent_points` (`offset/offset_geometry_creation.rs`) joins the two pieces back, and `BSplineCurve::create_by_joining` leaves a full-multiplicity knot at the joint. The edge's trim starts 6.3e-8 before that knot, at the node's offset vertex, and the UV trim dropped from the curve onto an offset face then fits its span `[2.3105, 5.117]` to 3.46e-5 instead of 7.25e-8. Two pieces cut from one curve meet smoothly, so the joint knot is exactly removable. `remove_exact_joint_knot` now removes the copies of it that Piegl and Tiller's knot removal (A5.8) takes out within `EFF_ZERO (1 + max |coordinate|)`, through `BSplineCurve::remove_knot_certified`: a removal is kept only when reinserting the removed copies (Boehm, in rounded `Ivl` arithmetic) gives back the old knot vector and control points within copies times tolerance, which bounds the move at every parameter. A partial removal is kept, so a cut on an existing knot loses only the copies the joint can drop; rational curves are left alone. The joined curve's deviation grows by that certified bound (rounded up). The decision rests on the curve's shape at the joint, not on where the pieces came from: pieces at a true corner keep their knot, smooth ones (including traced-apart collinear pieces) lose it. The hood edge's tolerance is back under 4e-5.
- **Tests:** `tests/issue_2110_tangent_cylinders_union.rs` keeps its five split cases (now on the default kernel); its four upstream-behaviour cases are removed. `tests/fork_099cf96_exact_class_cases.rs` drops the flag for the pipes case, and its "the pipes join only with WR-77" check becomes "the pipes join". The unit tests `a_joint_inside_a_smooth_curve_loses_its_knot_and_a_corner_keeps_it`, `a_cut_on_an_existing_knot_loses_only_the_copies_the_join_added`, `a_knot_removal_is_kept_only_within_its_certified_bound` and `the_removal_certificate_bounds_the_move_and_sees_a_moved_point` cover the knot removal and its certificate.
- **Results:** 16 goldens rows move, all of them among the 17 that #17570's leave-one-out run attributed to this flag, each a set of curves cut where the normals are parallel, or a body built from them: `surface.surface_p2` (11 rows), `topology.brep_planar_section` `upstream_torus_a_plane{2,4}` and `boolean.api_brep` `issue2110_tangent_cylinders_union_{a135_b225,checked_a0_b0,checked_a0_b90}`. The pull request's class table gives the check of each row. The 17th, `offset.offset_executive_p2` `issue1818_square_to_circle_loft_api_shell_1_top` (4 more edges in that run), stays bit-identical to main on this head and does not depend on this split: with `split_curves_at_singular_points` disabled outright, all of `offset.offset_executive_p2` stays bit-identical (while the 11 `surface_p2` rows move back), and with the joint-knot removal disabled no goldens row moves. Its attribution to this flag in the all-flags run came from another path (both experiments MEASURED on the pull request's head). Each piece's deviation is certified against its parent (`piece_deviation` in `intersect/surface_intersector/singular_splits.rs`): it grows by the bound on the move of `Trim`'s end-span cleanup (the two end pieces of `ssi_adv_ssi_clasic_ruled_f0_f0`, by 4.44e-6 and 1.59e-6) and stays the parent's for the other 182 pieces, whose certified move is at most `EFF_ZERO (1 + scale)` (a move that small, the split's rounding or a cleanup of an end span below that size, is not recorded). The deviation is the 3D curve's; the UV curves' move is not recorded, as for #17210's joins.
