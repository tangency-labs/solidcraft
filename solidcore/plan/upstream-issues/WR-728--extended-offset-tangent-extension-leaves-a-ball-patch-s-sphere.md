#### WR-728. The extended offset extends a ball patch by a tangent extension that leaves its sphere, and the shell's inner corners lose their definition
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a shell's inner Ball corner face is a plain B-spline instead of a ball patch on its sphere: exact over the face, but no longer carrying the sphere, so a later offset of the shell is not exact)
- **Area:** Offsets and shells
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp`, `SmOffsetExecutive::GetCreateExtendedSurface` (def. `:2237`): every surface is extended with `CreateExtendedSurface(.., SM_CT_G1R, ..)`. The ball patch is the SolidCraft fork's `SmBallPatch` (tangency-labs/smlib@f8e6709), which inherits `SmBSplineSurface::CreateExtendedSurface`: its net extended, a plain B-spline.
- **What happens:** the shell puts each offset face on its extended surface (`ExtendResultFaces`) and puts back the face's own surface only when the face's trims lie inside that surface's domain to `SM_EFF_ZERO`. A box rounded r 3 on every edge (eight Ball corners) shelled 2 in: the inner corners' trims overshoot their patches' domain by 4.8e-14 (corner (27, 3, 3)) and 1.6e-12 to 2.4e-11 (the other seven), rounding in their UV curves. Seven of the eight inner corners therefore keep the tangent extension, a plain B-spline that leaves the sphere at once (it is exact over the face only); one keeps its ball patch. The 8 outer corners are ball patches.
- **Trigger:** a shell of a solid whose Ball corners (`fillet_blend_corner`) are ball patches (solidcore #2484, item 4 of #2405).
- **How found:** SolidCraft; the fork notes it at tangency-labs/smlib@b439d73 (no code change there).
- **Confirmed:** in solidcore, `tests/issue_2484_shell_keeps_ball_patches.rs` (`without_the_fix_seven_inner_corners_lose_their_sphere`); no C++ run was made here.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.offset_extends_ball_patches_as_themselves`, `OffsetExecutive::get_create_extended_surface` extends a ball patch as itself (`extend_ball_patch_as_itself`), in the manner of WR-532's cones:
  - The Ball's patch is the sphere's triangle with its pole on a side collapsed to a point, found by the extension's own singularity test at the domain's corners. That side is not extended.
  - The net is carried on with `SM_CT_CINFINITY`, the polynomial continuation of the homogeneous net, so `|X - C W|^2 = R^2 W^2` holds on the extension too: it stays on the sphere and comes back a ball patch with the same centre and radius (`SmBallPatch::Create` refuses a weight that is not positive).
  - Around the pole, each side by at most a quarter of the turn the third side's great circle leaves open (WR-532's bound on a cone's turn); away from the pole, by at most half the arc from the third side's farthest point to the pole's antipode, where the great circles through the pole meet again.
  - A ball patch without a singular side (none is made today) is extended as its net, as before.

  `tests/issue_2484_shell_keeps_ball_patches.rs` shells a 30 x 20 x 10 box rounded r 3 all round, 2 mm in. All 8 inner corners are ball patches with their outer corner's centre and radius 1 (to 1e-12), each net on its sphere over its whole extended domain (1e-12, and certified by `IsOnSphere`). The volume matches Steiner's closed form (outer rounded box less the box rounded r 1 about the same inner box) to 8.3e-15 relative, and `check_brep` (level 2) finds nothing.
