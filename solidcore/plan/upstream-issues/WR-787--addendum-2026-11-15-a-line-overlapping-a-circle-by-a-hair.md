#### WR-787 (update, 2026-11-15). A line overlapping a circle by a hair crosses it at two points far apart

- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmGeomUtility.cpp`, `smgu_LineCoPlanarCircleIntersect`: a line nearer the centre than the radius by δ gives two crossings `2√(2rδ)` apart, merged into one only when they are closer than `dTolerance`. `SmEllipse::IntersectWithLine` calls it with ten times its intersection tolerance.
- **What happens:** the line stays within δ of the circle between the two crossings, as two circles overlapping by δ do, but a large enough radius puts the crossings past the band: for δ = 5.8e-10 at radius 10.5667 they lie 2.2e-4 apart, past a band of 2e-4. A flat side a hair inside a round tangent to it is then cut at a second point that bounds a strip thinner than the tolerance, and the result is not a solid.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#14599: a cut whose profile has a round tangent to its straight side, through a 0.3 mm rib whose side lies along that straight side, 5.8e-10 inside the round at the rib's back face.
- **Confirmed:** no. The pinned C++ is not built on this machine. The port reproduces it on main: `a_traced_cut_whose_round_is_tangent_to_a_thin_ribs_side_removes_its_tool` (`crates/solidcore-ops/tests/boolean.rs`) fails without the fix, not a solid.
- **Rust port:** `line_coplanar_circle_intersect_distinct` (`crates/solidcore/src/geom/geom_utility/p1.rs`) takes a contact tolerance as `circle_coplanar_circle_intersect_in_contact` does: an overlap below it is tangent at the chord's midpoint, unless the whole circle lies within it of the line. `Ellipse::intersect_with_line` and `Line::intersect_with_ellipse` take it from `analytic_curve_intersect` for a line in the circle's plane, and put the touching point at an end of the arc or the line that lies on the stretch between the two crossings (an arc that starts on the line there, as in `an_arc_grazing_a_coplanar_box_face_joins`, touches it at its start), else at the chord's midpoint; so the edge classifier (`intersect/curve_class/p9.rs`), which passes `contact_tol_3d` there, decides a line and a circle as it decides two circles; the curve/surface classifier (`classify_curve_surface_xsect_point_with`, `surface/surface/p4.rs`) passes it for its line against an osculating circle when it has an edge and a face. Every other caller passes zero, upstream's. A line tilted out of the circle's plane, and the line through two circles' planes in `Ellipse::intersect_with_ellipse`, keep zero: they meet the circle where they pierce its plane, at one of the crossings, not at the chord's midpoint. Tests: `lines_overlapping_a_circle_below_the_contact_tolerance_touch` (unit, `geom_utility/p1.rs`), `a_line_a_hair_inside_a_circle_touches_it_only_in_its_plane` (unit, `curve/ellipse/p2.rs`).
