#### WR-597. A 3x2 mixed-convexity corner reads its fillets' roles off a tangent side edge's rounding (status 1001)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFillet3x2MixedConvexityCorner::MakeCornerTopology`, in the role choice the fork added in tangency-labs/smlib@1255993 (WR-524; not in pinned v1.0.1, carried by the Rust port behind `fillet_3x2_mixed_concave_side_edge`).
- **What happens:** the corner chooses which fillet's rail crosses the unfilleted edge (`pCrossEU`) and which crosses its extension (`pExtEU`) from `IsConvexRadialSector(5)` on that edge. When the edge's faces meet tangentially at the vertex, the edge is G1 and the call reads only the sign of the geometry's rounding. In the reported case it sent the concave round's rail on the cylinder wall (z = 17) to cross the side edge (z 0..15). `CalcRailIntEdgeuse` found no intersection and left that corner vertex at the origin, so `CreateCornerBlend` failed (fillet error 3, corner type 5, status 1001).
- **Trigger:** a block 40 x 20 x 15 joined to a cylinder R 10 x 30 that is as wide as it, so the block's sides run tangent into the cylinder's wall. The block's long top edge (convex) and the concave arc where its top meets the wall above it are rounded r 2 together. At the vertex where those two edges meet, the third edge is the line where the block's side runs tangent into the cylinder.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft. The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@c17b745 ("fillets: a 3x2 mixed corner's tangent side edge takes its roles from the faces' corners"), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** partly. In the Rust port the branch is reached at (40, -10, 15) of that body. `IsConvexRadialSector` on the tangent side edge reads concave there, and the fork's face-corner rule reverses the roles (`cross_uses_convex_fillet` test in `blend/fillet_corner/topology/mixed_3x2.rs`). The whole round still fails with 1001 with either role choice, for a reason this record does not cover. This is fork-only code, so the pinned C++ cannot confirm it.
- **Fix:** tangency-labs/smlib@c17b745. For an unfilleted edge that is tangential at the vertex (`sm_CheckTangentialEdgeuseAtVertex`), `pCrossEU` is the filleted edge whose face shared with that edge turns less than half a turn between them at the vertex (`! sm_CheckFaceComplimentaryCornerConcavity(vertex, pConvexEdgeuse, pCurrEU, NULL)`), so that its rail on that face runs into the edge. Edges with a convexity keep the convexity rule.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. `Kernel::fixes.fillet_3x2_tangent_side_edge_roles`, together with WR-524's `Kernel::fixes.fillet_3x2_mixed_concave_side_edge`, ports the change in `cross_uses_convex_fillet`, which `FilletCorner::make_3x2_mixed_corner_topology` calls. `IsConvexRadialSector` is still evaluated first, as the fork does.
