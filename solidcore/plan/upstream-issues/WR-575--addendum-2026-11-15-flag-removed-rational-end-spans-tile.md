#### WR-575 (update, 2026-11-15). `bspline_trim_keeps_rational_end_spans` is removed; pieces of a small curve no longer overlap

`BSplineCurve::trim` keeps a rational curve's short end spans, and the flag is gone: the field, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and in `issue_2260`'s preset list, and every test setting of it. Tests that ran it off and on keep the fixed half.

The knot snap of the 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-02 addenda (a model curve's trim end within a pole of an interior knot ends at that knot) is corrected first. It took the first interior knot, in ascending order, within the zone tolerance mapped to parameter space whose piece to the end was a pole. On a curve no larger than the zone tolerance (`intersect.curve_class_p5` gen0231, gen0403, gen0533 and gen0598: a circle of radius 1e-6, its NURB knots at the quarters, split at 0, 0.2, 0.4, ...) that window covers most of the curve and every span is a pole, so 0.2 moved to 0.25 and 0.4 to 0.25. The piece [0.2, 0.4] then had both ends at one knot, the guard kept its requested ends, and the pieces came out [0, 0.25], [0.2, 0.4], [0.25, 0.5], [0.5, 0.75], [0.75, 1]: neighbours overlapped.

Now the snap looks only at the ends of the knot span holding the trim end (no other knot can bound the end span the trim makes), the nearer first, and never inside a knot span whose control points all lie within the zone tolerance of its first (a control-point bound: the span lies in a ball of that radius). Such a span is no larger than the zone tolerance, the scale at which the trim already calls an end span short, so cutting it makes no new short span, and moving the end to its knot would move it by a large part of the span. The small circle's pieces keep their requested intervals, as upstream's do. The end still depends only on its parameter, the curve and the tolerances, never on which side is kept, so #2424's split pieces still meet at the knot. One case is unchanged: a piece whose two ends both lie within a pole of one knot (shorter than twice the pole tolerance, 2e-6) keeps its requested ends while its neighbours move to the knot, so it overlaps them by less than that.

- **Results:** `nurbs.b_spline_curve_n_lib` changes in its 12 WR-575 trim cases (`issue1529_r16_*`, `issue1114_dim2_*`, `issue1114_dim3_*`, notify and silent modes): the short end span is kept and both spans are exact arcs of the circle, where upstream's single stretched span is not the circle: for `issue1529_r16_head_notify` its middle point is 5.3e-5 (`r^2`-scaled) off its end tangent, its middle weight 1.3e-7 off the half-angle cosine, and the curve up to 8.2e-7 off the circle. No other golden row changes, and the four `curve_class_p5` rows keep main's values.
