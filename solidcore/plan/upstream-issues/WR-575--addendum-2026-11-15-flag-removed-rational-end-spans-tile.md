#### WR-575 (update, 2026-11-15). `bspline_trim_keeps_rational_end_spans` is removed; pieces of a small curve no longer overlap

`BSplineCurve::trim` keeps a rational curve's short end spans, and the flag is gone: the field, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and in `issue_2260`'s preset list, and every test setting of it. Tests that ran it off and on keep the fixed half.

The knot snap of the 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-02 addenda (a model curve's trim end within a pole of an interior knot ends at that knot) is corrected first. It took the first interior knot, in ascending order, within the zone tolerance mapped to parameter space whose piece to the end was a pole. On a curve no larger than the zone tolerance (`intersect.curve_class_p5` gen0231, gen0403, gen0533 and gen0598: a circle of radius 1e-6, its NURB knots at the quarters, split at 0, 0.2, 0.4, ...) that window covers most of the curve and every span is a pole, so 0.2 moved to 0.25 and 0.4 to 0.25. The piece [0.2, 0.4] then had both ends at one knot, the guard kept its requested ends, and the pieces came out [0, 0.25], [0.2, 0.4], [0.25, 0.5], [0.5, 0.75], [0.75, 1]: neighbours overlapped.

Now the snap looks only at the ends of the knot span holding the trim end (no other knot can bound the end span the trim makes), the nearer first, and never on a curve that is degenerate at the zone tolerance (`is_ncrv_degenerate`, the measure of `SmBSplineCurve::IsDegenerate`: the control points' box is point-sized). Such a curve is a point to the kernel at that tolerance, and every split of it is at the pole scale, so moving ends to knots would move them by large parts of the curve and make pieces overlap. A pole end span the trim then leaves is cleaned as upstream cleans it, so check test #15 (which skips only curves degenerate at its own 1e-6 pole tolerance) is still met. A larger curve snaps as before, short knot spans included (`wr_575_split_inside_a_short_knot_span_meets_at_the_knot_on_the_circle`). The small circle's pieces keep their requested intervals, as upstream's do. The end still depends only on its parameter, the curve and the tolerances, never on which side is kept, so #2424's split pieces still meet at the knot. One case is unchanged: a piece whose two ends both lie within a pole of one knot (shorter than twice the pole tolerance, 2e-6) keeps its requested ends while its neighbours move to the knot, so it overlaps them by less than that.

- **Results:** `nurbs.b_spline_curve_n_lib` changes in its 12 WR-575 trim cases (`issue1529_r16_*`, `issue1114_dim2_*`, `issue1114_dim3_*`, notify and silent modes): the short end span is kept and both spans are exact arcs of the circle, where upstream's single stretched span is not the circle: for `issue1529_r16_head_notify` its middle point is 5.3e-5 (`r^2`-scaled) off its end tangent, its middle weight 1.3e-7 off the half-angle cosine, and the curve up to 8.2e-7 off the circle. `boolean.api_brep` `issue1517_tube_ends_union_measured` also changes: a tube-end circle of that union split just beside its knot now ends at the knot on the circle instead of having its pole end span stretched (volume 1032433.15104 to 1032433.13648, one cut cap 402.134846 to 402.135972). Both values lie in the closed-form windows `wr533_tube_ends_union_measures_lie_in_their_closed_forms` asserts. No other golden row changes. The rows the old snap moved besides these keep main's values, which keep the requested interval: the four `curve_class_p5` rows, `sweep.trans_sweep_geometry` gen0209, gen0239 and gen0472 (circles of radius about 1e-6 swept over [0.2, 0.8], which the old snap made [0.25, 0.75]), `surface.cone_p1` gen0824 and `surface.surface_p2` common_cone_gen0824 (a cone's iso circle over [0.0749, 1], which the old snap started at the knot 0.25, dropping 64 degrees), and `blend.feature_executive` multi_feature_extrusion.
