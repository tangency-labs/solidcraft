#### WR-756. `MergeIntoObject` keeps a vertex where it stood when its tolerance ball holds the other point, so the vertex can lie more than XSectTol3d off one of its faces and `SmVertex::AssertValid` test 1 fails
- **Severity:** Invalid result (a valid-volume body that fails `check(All)`: `SmVertex::Test #1 Bad Vertex Gap`, plus `SmBrep::Test #1/#3 Bad Gap` where the vertex tolerance was set to twice the gap)
- **Area:** Boolean (point classification merge, vertex placement)
- **Location:** at the pin `3fd5b95`: `source/SMLib/src/SmPointClass.cpp:1040-1059` (`SmPointClassification::MergeIntoObject`, the "use VertexPt location and ZoneTol3d" branch), against `source/SMLib/src/SmVertex.cpp:1720-1801` (`IsWithinXSectTol3dOfFaces`) and `SmVertex.cpp:2745-2751` (`AssertValid` test 1).
- **What happens:** when the vertex's zone tolerance covers the source point's neighbourhood (`vtol >= srcTol + gap`) the vertex keeps its position. Its ball then holds the other operand's point, but the vertex itself lies `gap` off that operand's face. `AssertValid` test 1 requires every vertex/face gap below `XSectTol3d(brep)` whatever the vertex's own tolerance, and nothing after the merge moves the vertex to its faces' corner (`CalcCornerFromFaces` is only read by the check). `ValidateAndUpdateTolerances` and `SetUVTrimCurve` grow the vertex tolerance to twice the gap, which satisfies the edge-use tests (gap at most half the tolerance, equal to it at round-off) but not test 1.
- **Trigger:** two 40 x 30 x 10 boxes with R2 vertical fillets, the second (an extruded rounded rectangle) shifted by dx = 1e-2 along x, A - B: the tangent edge of A's fillet ends 2.5e-5 (dx^2 / 4) from B's fillet; the vertex has tolerance 4e-5 from the fillet's own build, covers the point (1e-5 + 2.5e-5), keeps its place, and is 2.5e-5 off B's fillet against XSectTol3d 2e-5.
- **How found:** issue #3090 (follow-up of #3012).
- **Confirmed:** by reading the C++ at the pin (the lines above) and by the Rust port, which reproduces the branch line by line (`merge_vertex_neighborhood`): the merge inputs printed for the four vertices are `src_tol 1e-5, vertex_tol 4e-5, gap 2.5e-5`, the covered branch. Not run against the C++ (no C++ build here).
- **Rust port:** after the boolean's cleanup, `place_vertices_on_face_corners` (`boolean/merge/p1.rs`) puts each intersection vertex whose tolerance is past XSectTol3d and whose gap to a face is past it at the corner its faces define (`calc_corner_from_faces`: the midpoint of two parallel faces that part), when the move is at most half the vertex's tolerance (its edge curves keep ending at the old point, and the invariants ask vertex/edge gaps of half the tolerance at most) and every face gap at the corner is under XSectTol3d. Otherwise, and when the faces define no corner, the vertex stays. Test: `crates/solidcore-ops/tests/issue_3012_filleted_boxes_apart.rs` (dx = 1e-2, both orders; the four tangent-end vertices below XSectTol3d off each face, tolerance 5e-5) and unit tests of the move decision in `p1.rs`.
