#### WR-569. StitchIntoSolid leaves the region inside a nested closed shell solid
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a cavity counted as material; with a twist, SmBrep check Test #12 "an inner shell not inside its region's outer shell")
- **Area:** Healing / stitching
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmStitch.cpp`, `SmStitch::StitchIntoSolid` (def. `:2659`), where it reports a solid (`rbProducesASolid = TRUE`, `:2717`).
- **What happens:** The stitch passes find the infinite region only (`SM_VS_INFINITEREGION_SWAP`, `DoStitching`'s region nesting). When the stitched faces close into a shell nested inside another, the region inside the inner shell stays solid, and the shells stay in the regions where the gluing put them. A holed profile swept round a closed path then measures as if it had no hole. Twisted a half turn about the path, it fails the check.
- **Trigger:** stitching into a solid the faces of a closed body with a closed cavity: a holed profile swept round a closed path (SolidCraft issue_2005), or a cube's faces together with those of a smaller cube inside it.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft. The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@351c57c ("stitch: a stitched solid is a nested solid, its cavities void"; smlib#63). It is not merged upstream. The fix makes the stitched solid a nested solid model (`FindAndSetInfiniteRegion(NULL, SM_VS_NESTEDSOLID)` with editing disabled).
- **Confirmed:** yes, C++ build. The `heal.stitch_p2` fixture `wr569_cube_with_cavity_solid` (`fixtures/heal.stitch_p2/wr569-nested-cavity.json`) stitches the twelve loose faces into a solid on both sides (status 0, solid 1, three regions) with void flags `[0, 0, 1]`: only the infinite region is a void, the cavity stays solid.
- **Rust port:** behaves as before by default. With `Kernel::fixes.stitch_solid_nests_cavities`, `stitch_into_solid` (`heal/stitch/p2.rs`) runs `find_and_set_infinite_region(.., SetRegionVoidType::NestedSolid, ..)` with editing disabled once the stitch leaves no lamina edge and more than one region, as the fork does. `stitched_solid_cavity_is_a_void` (same file) stitches the twelve loose faces of a cube of side 10 and a cube cavity of side 4. It checks two voids (the infinite region and the cavity) with the fix and one without.
