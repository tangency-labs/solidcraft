Updates: WR-654

#### WR-654 (update, 2026-11-15). `fillet_run_on_bspline_trims` is removed

Wherever WR-610's run-on (`fillet_trace_reaches_rails`) widens a side edge's interval, `rail_edgeuse_intersect` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_solver/p2.rs`) now always turns on out-of-bounds evaluation of its local copy of the edge's B-spline UV trim (`continue_bspline_trim_past_its_ends`). The trim runs on along its line as a line trim does. The run-on itself is still behind `fillet_trace_reaches_rails`, so a kernel without that flag is unchanged. Removed: the field, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `kernel_cases/fixes.json` and `issue_2260_gn_pipe_refined_range_end.rs`, its entry in the two fix lists of `fixtures/solidcraft/190-round-as-tall-as-its-wall.json`, and every test setting of it.

- **Results:** no goldens case changes (the run-on is off on the default kernel). Every test that sets `fillet_trace_reaches_rails` now takes this path too, including those that did not set this flag, and they pass unchanged. One consequence: the WR-630 addendum of 2026-11-01 says the #1467 back-edge case of `wr631_cliff_overflow_construction` is not reached without WR-654's flag, and it is now reached.
- **Tests:** `issue_1981_pocket_chamfer_operand_trims` drops its flag parameter. The bar edge (0,5)'s flag-off refusal (error 4) and the bit-for-bit comparison of the pocket chamfer with and without the flag are removed. Both closed-form volumes are still checked. `fork_856b54e_tangent_end`, `fork_cd47efd_tangent_end_rib_sqrt_s`, `issue_1467_rail_end_past_side_edge_end`, `wr610_779cb9b_rail_meets_seam_line_past_its_end`, `wr631_a307480_end_section_off_its_end_face`, `wr636_db4a655_closed_rim_past_its_wall` and the SolidCraft test `core::blend_tests::pocket_chamfer_on_a_boolean_body` keep their assertions without setting it.
