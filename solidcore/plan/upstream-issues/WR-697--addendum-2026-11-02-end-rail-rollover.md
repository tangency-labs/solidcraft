Updates: WR-697

#### WR-697 (update, 2026-11-02). A round's rail that rolls over a tangent edge before the round ends at a smooth vertex
- **Severity:** Failure (status 1001, refused with `SM_FILERR_NO_ROOM` and no size)
- **Area:** Fillets (corners, rollover)
- **Location:** the fork's `source/SMLib/src/SmBlendCornerEnd.cpp`, `SmBlendCorner::CalcEndCapEdges` (refuses a rail end off its side face, at 18e96f1); `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletSolver.cpp`, `sm_FindRolloverSideEdge` (a tangent side edge of the fillet's convexity is a crash, so the last segment off the face is never rolled over) and `SmFilletSolver::ProcessRollOver` (only `SM_FV_RAIL_X_EDGEUSE` corner vertices are adjusted); `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`, `SmFilletGeom::ReCalcFilletGeom` (an `SM_FV_RAIL_X_EXTENDED_EDGEUSE` rail end gives the trace no point).
- **What happens:** SolidCraft #851's plate with its concave R21 corner wall, its top front edge rounded R2, then the middle edge between that round and the wall rounded alone at r (tangency-labs/solidcore#2406, from tangency-labs/smlib#98). The middle edge ends at two smooth vertices. At the top one, V, the rail on the R2 round reaches the round's tangent edge T with the top face at Q, short of the End's plane P, for every r > 0 (the ball's centre is at the angle asin(2 / (21 + r)) about the wall's axis, short of V's asin(2 / 21)). The ball rolls over T onto the top face. The fork refuses it: "a rail rolling over into an end is not built".
- **How found:** the fork's capi `scenario_middle_edge_of_an_arc_tangent_chain_r0_25` (18e96f1, ops_kernel.cpp) gave the input and the fork's refusal. Solidcore-native, no fork commit: the fork has no equivalent (smlib#98 open; the refusal is still at 18e96f1).
- **Rust fix:** under the same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner`, with no new flag.
  - `blend::blend_corner::end::end_awaits_rollover`: an End at a smooth vertex whose rail ends on P off its side face F, where F is a face of the blended edge and F's other edge at V is tangent (`sm_TestManifoldTangentRollover`), builds its edges once the rails' rollover has run. That is the adjusted corner's pass. Its edges are not built in the first pass, on F's surface, which does not run on past T. This holds only while the rails are still to be classified (classification on, no cliff overflow). Any other rail end off its face is refused as the fork refuses it.
  - `sm_FindRolloverSideEdge` (`fillet_solver::p2`, `rolls_over_tangent_edge`): a tangent side edge whose faces' normals agree is rolled over (`SM_FG_TANGENT_ROLLOVER`), whatever its convexity. The faces' offsets continue each other across the edge's offset. A tangent edge's convexity does not say which way the side face, run on past it, leaves the face beyond. Here (a convex R2 round into a plane) the round run on past T stays under the top face, so no trim of the top face can end it. **This broadens the flag:** a tangent side edge of the fillet's convexity was a crash (trim) before.
  - `ProcessRollOver` (`fillet_solver::p5::recovery`): a first or last piece that ends on an End at a smooth vertex adjusts that corner (`Recovery::retraced_end`). The End's two rail ends are marked unprocessed and put on their rails' faces (the top face, after the rollover), and the cap's edges at them get those faces. After the piece's trace and before the rails' trim, both rail ends are found again as the rails' crossings with P (`CalcRailIntEndSurface`). The adjusted corner's pass then builds the end rib on the rolled piece and the cap's edge on the top face (G ∩ P).
  - The piece before the rollover keeps its first trace when `Kernel::fixes.fillet_round_as_definition` is also on and that trace is a `ProceduralBlend` (`Recovery::keeps_round`: nothing on it changed but the rails' split at Q). It is not traced again. Its definition, certified net and rails stay as they are, its cross sections are computed as `ReCalcFilletGeom`'s tail computes them (`FilletBrep::calc_cross_sections`), and its rails are trimmed at Q. The piece after the rollover is the same ball's definition on its new support: a `ProceduralBlend` on the wall and the top face, the face beyond T. Without `fillet_round_as_definition`, the piece before is traced again, as `ProcessRollOver` does, through the End's section in P (`Recovery::end_sections`: `sm_FindFilletTsectPnt` in the plane, on the offsets as they are then, through `FilletBrep::re_calc_fillet_geom_through`). The fork's first trace runs through the edge's end the same way (`fillet_through_extended_rail_end`).
  - The End's rib (`SmFilletGeom::CalcFilletIntSideFace`, `fillet_geom::p5`) on an order-1 definition and a plane end face, with `fillet_round_as_definition` on, is the definition's section in the plane (`definition_section_in_plane`). That section is a rational quadratic arc through its two feet, with the middle control point and weight of the ball's circle through them. It is used only if, within the approximation tolerance, the feet are the rib's ends, the section at t = 0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1 lies in the plane and the arc's middle is the definition's. Otherwise the rib is the surfaces' intersection, as before.
  - `calc_end_cap_edges`: a rail end that lies on the side face's own edge at V, where that edge lies in P up to V, makes that cap edge the edge's piece (`SM_FE_ON_EDGE`), not a second curve laid on it. This is the bottom End of the same round: the rail end lies on the round's tangent edge with the front face, exactly on the cap plane z = 8, for every r.
  - Close to r 2 the ball on the R2 round does not reach P before the rollover. At P its foot there would sit asin(2r / (21 (2 - r))) past T: 42 degrees at r 1.75, 59 at 1.8, and 90 at r = 42/23 (1.826). Beyond 42/23 there is no section in P on the R2 round at all. The trace runs on the R2 round only 45 degrees past T, its extended domain. Two things carry such an End to the rollover, at an End at a smooth vertex whose blended edge has a face F with its other edge at V tangent to the face beyond (`end_past_tangent_edge`, `end_can_await_rollover`):
    - The trace's section at the edge's end (smlib#82's `c2_trace_to_edge_ends`) is skipped when it cannot be solved there, and the trace runs on to its extended domains (`c2_end_past_tangent_edge`). Before, the trace failed (1001, `Fillet Surface Computation Failure`; r 1.96).
    - A rail end that its traced rail does not reach on P (`CalcRailIntEndSurface` failing, `SM_FIL_SURF_INT_FAILURE`) takes the rail's traced end nearer V for now (`place_rolled_rail_end`). This is done only while the rails are still to be classified. Before, the corner's vertices failed (1001, `Vertex Computation Failure`; r 1.8 to 1.95).
    - The rollover then traces the rest on the top face as at smaller r. Its End section is solved from the middle of the two rail ends, dropped on P (`Recovery::end_sections`), and the rail ends are found on P again.
