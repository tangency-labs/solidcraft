Updates: WR-707

#### WR-707 (addendum 2026-11-03): a void between islands of material is not a hollow behind a wall

- **Severity:** Wrong result (in the port's opt-in fix: a valid blend refused)
- **Area:** Blends
- **Status in the port:** the same flag, `UpstreamFixes::fillet_refuse_rounds_breaking_through`, off by default. Nothing changes with it off.
- **Location:** `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_executive/session/band_cuts.rs`, `section_breaks_through`.
- **What happened:** the fork's capi test `scenario_round_over_a_thin_rib_not_a_cut` (tangency-labs/smlib@02b6103) rounds the top front edge of a bridged 20 x 4 x 10 block r9, with a rib 0.05 thick across the slot along a chord of the section arc. Over the slot the section runs void (its front rail in the slot), rib, void, rib, void (its top rail past the 4 deep top). The void between the rib's two crossings lies between two crossings of the rib's faces, which share no edge with the blended faces, so the test read it as a hollow behind a wall and refused the round (`NoRoomReason::BreaksThrough`, fit 8.557140). With only this flag off the round builds: one lump, `check_brep` clean, 401.198953617955 against the closed form 401.198953621465.
- **What changes:** a void piece that meets the earlier conditions breaks through only where the material beside it runs to a rail: the material between the void and the blended face is a wall of the part the blend rounds, and the round would cut through it. Where the material on both sides is an island (void on either side of it along the section), the round runs past it and is trimmed to its faces, as `trim_at_creases` trims the sheet at every face it crosses. The change only narrows the test: a section that breaks through now broke through before.
- **Limits:** a void between an island and a wall is still refused, also where it is not a hollow. Measured: the same rib in a slot that ends under the bridge (block 10 deep) is refused, fit 8.176952, though without the flag it builds 1517.346847697292 against the closed form 1517.346847698631. Refining the rule by the wall's inner face (a face the blend reaches, such as a cliff face, is no wall) would build that case, but it also lets through a fin standing behind the cliff, which `trim_at_creases` fails on with a bare 1001 (with WR-730 off, with and without this flag); so it is not done here.
- **With `fillet_round_ends_on_outline` (WR-730):** the round of the rib case ends on the body's outline instead (501.307311043297, one lump, `check_brep` clean), on main and with this change alike; the change acts only without that flag. With WR-730 on, every case named here gives the same result with and without this change: the three hollows and the rib in the deeper slot stay refused (the hollows behind the cliff now with fit 2.255035), and the fin behind the cliff builds, ending on the outline.
- **Regression evidence:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr707_rounds_running_off_their_faces.rs`. `a_round_past_a_thin_rib_across_its_slot_builds` (WR-730 off) fails without this change and passes with it (1e-9 relative); its one round face is its definition (an exact round, bound 0). `hollows_behind_a_wall_stay_refused`: a closed cavity and a pocket open from below behind the cliff (r9, fit 4.990015) and a closed cavity under the top face (r3, fit 1.337738) stay refused, with WR-730 on and off. The other WR-707 tests are unchanged and pass.
