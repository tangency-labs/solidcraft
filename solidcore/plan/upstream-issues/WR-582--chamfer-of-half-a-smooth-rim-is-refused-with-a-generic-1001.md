#### WR-582. A chamfer of one edge of a smooth (tangent) rim chain is refused with a generic 1001 after its rail runs off its face
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a refusal with the wrong form: the generic status, no clear reason)
- **Area:** Blends (fillets and chamfers)
- **Location:** `SmFilletExecutive::CreateFilletBrep` (`source/SMLib/src/SmFilletExecutive.cpp`), reported by `DoFilleting` as the generic 1001. Depending on the setbacks the failure is in `CalcFilletGeom` ("Fillet Surface Computation Failure", error info code 4) or, after rails that run past the edge's ends and off the face, in `CalcCornerEdges` ("Corner Edge Computation Failure", code 3).
- **What happens:** the rim a flat cut leaves on SolidCraft's tilted cylinder is two half-ellipse edges that meet tangentially (G1) at the wall's seam vertices. Chamfering the whole chain (both edges in one executive) builds a closed solid on both sides: faces 4 → 6, for offsets 1/1, 1.4437/1.4437 and 1/2. Chamfering one half edge alone, which ends where the rim continues tangentially into the unchamfered half, is refused with 1001, and the body is left unchanged. The refusal itself is reasonable (a chamfer that stops at a tangent continuation is not a case the executive handles). The defect is its form: no status says that only part of a tangent chain was picked. The rail is traced past the half edge's ends (in one case over twice the edge's length, -0.85 turns about the rim against the edge's -0.5, and off the cut face) until a later stage fails with the executive's generic status.
- **Trigger:** a cylinder of radius 5 built as SolidCraft builds it: two half-circle arcs from 0.6131 rad (35.128°), swept 40 along the axis (1,0,2)/√5 from (0,0,-10), cut by the box z ≥ 10. Chamfer one of the two rim edges at z = 10 with `SmConstantRadiusFS` and a second radius (offsets from the setbacks), a linear cross-section generator and bevel corners (tangency-labs/solidcore#880). The same with both rim edges builds.
- **Note:** The issue reported only the 1/2 chamfer of one half edge, refused by the fork with NO_ROOM at its `RailsFitTheirFaces` check (port: opt-in `fillet_refuse_rails_leaving_faces`). Pinned 1.0.1 has no such check and fails later with 1001. The fork's C API grew a picked edge into its tangent chain until fork commit fc43974 ("blends take exactly the given edges"; its message: "a caller that wants a chain selects it"). From then on SolidCraft's pick of one rim half chamfers only that half, which is the case here. None of the existing side-edge opt-ins changes the half-edge result: WR-457 (`fillet_nx1_tangent_side_edge`), WR-457 with WR-499 (`fillet_nx1_opposite_continuation_side_edge`), WR-468 (`fillet_through_extended_rail_end`), WR-301 (`fillet_rail_rim_on_face`), and all four together.
- **How found:** SolidCraft's `too_large_or_unsupported_is_refused` (#880). Reproduced by rebuilding the body with the port's API and the C++ shim, then staged with a diagnostic of `CreateFilletBrep`'s stages and the rails.
- **Confirmed:** yes, C++ build, `blend.fillet_executive_p3`. The half edges (`issue-880-half-arc-rim-{0,1}-setbacks-{1-2,1-1}`) are refused with 1001 on both sides, bit-identical, with the body unchanged (the same mass properties in every case), the 1/1 control included. The whole chain (`issue-880-half-arc-rim-whole-offsets-{1-1,1.4437-1.4437,1-2}`) agrees on both sides, and the port's test `issue_880_half_arc_rim_chamfer.rs` asserts it builds (a manifold solid, 4 faces to 6, for all three offset pairs) and that each half edge alone is refused with 1001. A single closed-ellipse rim (`issue-880-tilted-cylinder-rim-*`) also chamfers on both sides.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream. No fix is planned: a caller chamfers the whole tangent chain. A clearer refusal status for a partial tangent chain is a possible low-priority opt-in.
