#### WR-493. A torus or sphere found in a NURBS does not keep that NURBS; a half meridian measured to its rounding is refused
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Surfaces (tori and spheres)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmTorus.cpp`, `SmTorus::IsNurbSurfaceTorus` (after `Reparameterize`); `source/SMLib/src/SmSphere.cpp`, `SmSphere::IsNurbSurfaceSphere` (the `sAnglesU/V.GetLength() <= 180.0 + SM_EFF_ZERO` orientation tests, and after `Reparameterize`).
- **What happens:** both tests build the analytic surface from the circles fitted to the tested NURBS and keep that surface's own NURB, whose weights and control points differ from the tested ones by ~1e-11 (a fillet torus R 2.85, r 0.2, 90 mm out). A face trimmed in the tested NURBS's parameters then sits on a slightly different surface, and an exact same-NURBS check (the C API's flat import, `FACE_NURBS_FORM`) refuses it. `SmCylinder::IsNurbSurfaceCylinder` already keeps a copy of the tested NURB. The sphere test also measures a pole-to-pole meridian in degrees (180.0000000000024 on a radius 2 lune 500 mm out) against `180 + SM_EFF_ZERO` and takes it for more than a half circle; `RebuildSTEPFromNURBParameters` already allows `360 * SM_EFF_ZERO` there ([B279]).
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcore#1433: the NURBS form of a torus or sphere face given to the recognisers (e.g. `Torus::create_canonical` R 10, r 3, then `IsNurbSurfaceTorus` on its NURB).
- **How found:** SolidCraft flat round-trip import (the fork's capi `flat_roundtrip` tests).
- **Confirmed:** on the fork's C++ build (tangency-labs/smlib@7ea6a96). The pinned C++ keeps the fitted NURB, which the `surface.torus` `issue1433_torus_*` difftest fixtures compare with the fix off.
- **Fix (fork):** after `Reparameterize`, `SetFromGwNurb(0, pTestSurface->GetGwNurbPointer())` (a copy of the tested NURB, then `RebuildSTEPFromNURBParameters`, status ignored); the sphere's half-meridian tolerance is `360 * SM_EFF_ZERO`.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.found_torus_sphere_keep_tested_nurb` (default `false`, upstream's behaviour) in `Torus::is_nurb_surface_torus` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/torus.rs`) and `Sphere::is_nurb_surface_sphere` (`crates/solidcore/src/surface/sphere.rs`), both parts under the one flag. Tests: `issue_1433_torus_found_in_its_nurb_keeps_it`, `torus_recognition_partial_scaled_and_non_matching`, `wr493_sphere_found_in_its_nurb_keeps_it`.
