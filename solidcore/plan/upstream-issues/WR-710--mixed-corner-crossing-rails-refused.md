#### WR-710. A 3x2 mixed-convexity corner whose rails cross before it is built off its faces
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blend (fillet corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp:1378`, `SmFilletCorner::Create` (def. `:1129`), case 3 of two filleted edge uses: `SmFillet3x2MixedConvexityCorner` (its `CreateCornerPatch`, `:4623`).
- **What happens:** at a vertex of three edges where two filleted edges of differing convexity leave the vertex along one tangent, in the same sense, `Create` still builds the 3x2 mixed-convexity corner. The two edges' common face closes to a cusp at the vertex (its corner angle between them is zero), so the two rails, each set the blend's size into that face, cross each other before the corner. The convex blend's rail runs past the concave edge, and the concave blend's rail runs past the convex edge. The corner is then built from the rail crossing far back along both edges: for #2256 the crossing is at (32, 2, 10), 8 from the vertex at r 2. Its three-sided Coons patch stands out of the faces. `check_brep` passes it at Level2.
- **Trigger:** #2256: the tangent peg (a bar 40 x 16 x 10 with a half-disc R8 end about (40, 8), and a disc R8 about the same centre from z = 10 to 20, joined). The front face y = 0 runs tangent into the cylinder along x = 40, so the front top edge (convex) and the concave arc where the bar's top meets the cylinder both leave (40, 0, 10) along -x. SolidCraft's STEP-reimported body, with `fillet_edges` r 2 on both edges and every `UpstreamFixes` flag on, gives volume 9402.870799856591 and tight box min y -6.544244339992678e-3 (the corner patch). The native peg at r 2 builds within the box, but its seam at (32, 2, 10) is G0 by SolidCraft's continuity check (the normals across it differ by 180 degrees). At r 5 it stands 1.561e-3 out. The fork at tangency-labs/smlib a87c502 marks both failing, at 0.0374 and 0.128 out of the front face (`TANGENT_END_CASES` in its capi tests).
- **How found:** SolidCraft (`fillets/report-peg-step-front-and-concave-rounds-together-r2`), reported in #2256.
- **Confirmed:** in solidcore, where the opt-in flags reach the corner. With no flags the pinned C++ path fails the round with 1001 earlier, so the body is not reachable in the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`).
- **Rust port:** `Kernel::fixes.fillet_refuse_mixed_corner_crossing_rails` (off by default). This is a refusal only, not a fix of the geometry. `FilletCorner::create` returns `SolidError::Unsupported` with the reason `blend::fillet_corner::p3::MIXED_CORNER_RAILS_CROSS`, and `fillet_edges` passes that error on. The test is geometric. The corner must be a 3x2 mixed-convexity one, and the two filleted edges' tangents, taken pointing away from the vertex, must lie within the solvers' tangency tolerance of each other (`mixed_corner_rails_cross`). Two edges of one tangent that leave the vertex in opposite senses, where one edge runs on smoothly into the other, are not this case. A construction for the corner is separate work (#2308).

  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2256_peg_mixed_corner.rs` covers the reimported peg. With the flag on, the fillet is refused, with the front and concave edges alone and with the back edge too. With the flag off, the body is built as before. `rounds_together_refused_by_wr710` in `crates/solidcore/tests/fork_9bebf34_tangent_end_cases.rs` covers the native peg at r 2 and r 5, both refused.
