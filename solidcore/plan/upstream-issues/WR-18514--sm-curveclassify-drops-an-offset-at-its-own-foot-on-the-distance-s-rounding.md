#### WR-18514. `sm_CurveClassify` drops an offset at its own foot on the distance's rounding

- **Severity:** Wrong result (a valid taper extrusion is not a closed solid)
- **Area:** Curve offsets, `curve.composite_curve_p1` (`sm_CurveClassify`, called by `IntersectAndTrimOffsets`)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmCompositeCurve.cpp:2124` `double dCutoffDist = dOffsetDistance * ( 1.0 - SM_EFF_ZERO );` and `:2179` `if(dThisDist < dCutoffDist )`, with `dThisDist` the `GlobalPointSolve` distance from the trimmed offset's mid point to each original curve.
- **What happens:** a trimmed offset is discarded when its mid point's solved distance to any original curve is below the cutoff. The mid point of an offset lies the offset distance from its own curve, at its foot, so for its own curve the test compares two equal numbers. The computed distance carries the rounding of evaluating both points, which grows with the coordinates, while the margin `SM_EFF_ZERO` (1e-12) is relative to the offset distance. With a small offset far from the origin the rounding passes the margin, and whether a piece survives depends on the last bits of its distance. The comment above the cutoff ("plenty big enough to cover numerical noise") holds only where the offset is not small next to the coordinates.
- **Trigger:** a rounded rectangle 222.97 x 82.97 mm (corners r 8.98 about (+-102.5, +-32.5)) drafted 0.5 degrees inward (`SmApiCreateTaperExtrude`) to 2 mm: the offset is 0.0175 mm and the corner arcs about (102.5, -32.5) and (-102.5, 32.5) solve to d - 2.1e-14, below the cutoff d - 1.75e-14. Their offsets are dropped, the extrusion falls back to the surface intersection method and builds 9 faces, not a closed solid. At 1.99 and 2.05 mm every piece lands above the cutoff.
- **How found:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#18514 (the issue's script fails at 2, 2.01 and 2.5 mm and builds beside them); measured in the port with prints in `intersect_and_trim_offsets` and `curve_classify_cached`.
- **Confirmed:** no (read at 3fd5b95); the port, which followed the C++ here, fails on the trigger.
- **Rust port:** unconditional. `curve_classify_cached` (`crates/solidcore/src/curve/composite_curve/p1.rs`, `proved_closer`) evaluates the solve's nearest point on the original curve again and discards the piece only where the distance to it, widened by its rounding (`certified::distance`) and twice that point's coordinate error bound (`Curve::point_error_bound`), stays below the same cutoff. The margin is never narrower than upstream's. A curve kind with no point error bound keeps upstream's test. Tests: `sweep::extrude::tests::a_rounded_rectangle_drafted_slightly_inward_keeps_every_corner_wall`, `curve::composite_curve::p1::tests::a_point_is_closer_to_a_curve_only_where_its_rounding_proves_it`.
- **Related:** `check_bow_ties` (`curve/composite_curve/p2.rs`, `sm_CheckBowTies`) tests an open chain's offset ends against the same cutoff; not changed here.
