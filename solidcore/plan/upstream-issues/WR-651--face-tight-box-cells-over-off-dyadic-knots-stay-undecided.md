#### WR-651. The trimmed-face tight box search (WR-588) leaves cells over a knot off the domain's dyadic points undecided, so the box overshoots
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a trimmed face's tight box is too large; opt-in path only)
- **Area:** Faces / tight bounding box
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFace.cpp`, `sm_FaceDirectedMax` (the SolidCraft fork's, tangency-labs/smlib@beb6061, ported for WR-588): an undecided cell is halved at its middle. Not in upstream SMLib, which has no such search; this completes WR-588's fix.
- **What happens:** `sm_CellMonotone` reads a rational patch's monotonicity only over one knot span. Halved at middles alone, a cell over a knot that is not a dyadic point of the face's UV domain never comes to lie within one span, so every such cell along the trim stays undecided down to the depth limit (16) or the cell limit (4096) and counts with its own greatest value.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's threads/1250: an M10x1.5 thread groove cut from a shaft of radius 5 and length 12 boxes to z 12.46875 (expected 12); the screw sweep's walls are rational quadratic with a knot every eighth of a turn. In the port: the 270-degree rational cylinder of radius 1 (knots at u = 1/3, 2/3) trimmed to 0 <= z <= 0.3 boxes to z 0.30859375 with the search on (expected 0.3).
- **How found:** SolidCraft (the fork, tangency-labs/smlib@326de73); 15 SolidCraft thread cases overshoot without it.
- **Confirmed:** in the port (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1799_tight_box_divides_at_knots.rs` fails with z max 0.30859375 when the knot division is removed). The pinned C++ has no such search, so there is nothing upstream to confirm.
- **Fix (fork):** tangency-labs/smlib@326de73 divides a cell each way at the surface's knot strictly inside it (by more than `CopySubPatch`'s 1e-8 snap) nearest its middle, at its middle only where it holds none (`sm_CellDivision`), the knots read by `GetKnots` when the surface is a kind of `SmBSplineSurface`.
- **Rust port:** under the same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.face_tight_box_searches_trim` (WR-588; default `false`, upstream's behaviour): `cell_division` in `face_directed_max` (`crates/solidcore/src/query/face/p3.rs`). It is the rest of the fork's fix for one defect, and the flag has not yet been turned on by any caller. Tests: `trimmed_rational_cylinder_box_is_exact_with_the_search` (flag on: box (-1, -1, 0) to (1, 1, 0.3)) and `trimmed_rational_cylinder_box_without_the_search_is_the_edges` (flag off).
