#### WR-23428. A surface point minimize reports a saddle of the distance as the nearest point
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Surfaces and volumes
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmSurfacePointSolve.cpp`, `SmSurface::LocalPointSolve_1` (`:5683`-`:5968`: the converged guess taken at `:5804`, the result at `:5937`-`:5947`) and `SmSurfPtGlobalSolver::Solve` (its maximum test `IsLocalMax` at `:2995` and `:3066`, defined at `:2761`). Reached from `SmSurface::DropPoint` and every caller of the global point solve.
- **What happens:** the local solve's Newton step looks for a zero of the distance's gradient, and it stops at any stationary point: a minimum, a maximum or a saddle. A converged point is reported as found, and for a minimize so is one that did not converge. The global solve rejects only a local maximum, and `IsLocalMax` checks the curvature along the u and v parameter lines alone, so a saddle whose descent runs across them passes. The drop then returns the saddle as the nearest point.
- **Trigger:** a coil's wall swept from a circle of radius 1 about (10, 0, 30) in the plane z = 30, 6 turns at pitch 3 down the axis (tolerance 3e-4), and the point of that circle at its own parameter 0.524124633791162 (it lies on the wall's first station). Near the circle's inner point the circle runs almost along the screw motion, the wall's partials are 3 degrees apart, and the distance has a saddle 0.0137 away at uv (0.020930, 0.491576): its Hessian of the squared distance has determinant -54.5. The drop without a guess, or seeded with the foot of the point at parameter 0.5, returns that saddle. The result check measures an edge on the circle 0.0137 off the wall and refuses the coil cut (tangency-labs/solidcore#3446).
- **How found:** tracing the check's edge/face gap of that cut (`calc_max_edge_face_gap`, its gap function's drops) to the local solve's iterations.
- **Confirmed:** yes, the Rust port at d795d991ca returns the saddle for that point (test `issue_3446_a_point_of_a_walls_start_station_drops_onto_it` in `crates/solidcore/tests/screw_sweep.rs`), and for the point (0, 0, 1) over the bilinear saddle z = 2xy started at its centre (unit test `a_minimize_started_at_a_saddle_goes_on_to_the_nearest_point` in `crates/solidcore/src/surface/surface_point_solve/p3.rs`).
- **Fix:** `SurfPtSolveStepper::leave_saddle` (`p3.rs`): a minimize converged to a point where the Hessian of the squared distance has a negative eigenvalue moves to the least distance along that eigenvalue's eigenvector and iterates on, ending no farther than the nearest point a move reached. Always on.
- **Not fixed here:** a maximize (`LocalPointSolve_0`) converged to a saddle, and `IsLocalMin` and `IsLocalMax`, which still test the parameter lines only.
