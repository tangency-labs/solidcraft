#### WR-606. `SmFilletRemoval` approximates side-edge and face continuations, with no way to refuse them
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a removal that should be exact is approximated: a fitted curve and naturally extended surfaces replace the analytic geometry)
- **Area:** Blends / fillet removal
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletRemoval.cpp`, `SmFilletRemoval::FindSideEdgeExtensions` (def. `:581`; the side edge's run-on is `PointBasedSurfaceIntersect` at `:760`) and `SmFilletRemoval::DoRemoval` (the faces grown with `ExpandBoundarySpan`, `:1150`).
- **What happens:** a side edge that stops short of the new corner runs on along a marched, fitted intersection of its end and tangent faces, and the tangent and end faces grow past their surfaces' domains with a natural extension. Both are approximations except on lines and planes, and the class has no mode that refuses them. Its preparation also swaps or clears a side edge's UV trims before the B-rep is edited, so a removal that fails later leaves them behind (the next operation then meets broken loops, `SM_ERR_BAD_INTERSECTIONS`).
- **Trigger:** any four-sided fillet whose side edge stops short of the sharp corner on a curved edge, or whose new corner lies outside a non-planar face's surface domain. The port's tests use the upstream fillet fixtures (the `blend.fillet_removal` set), where fillets between B-spline faces have new corners outside a surface's natural domain.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (the fork, tangency-labs/smlib 5b0534b), for the followed blends of a face move (#1060).
- **Confirmed:** no. The pinned C++ has no exact mode, so the fork's commit is the evidence.
- **Fix (fork):** tangency-labs/smlib 5b0534b adds `SetExactOnly` (off by default, so `DeleteFilletFace` is unchanged) with `NotExact` and `GetSharpEdge`. A side edge too short to reach the corner runs on along its own line (its end span continued, `CreateExtendedCurve` C-infinity; the corner must lie on it), installed in `DoRemoval`. A curved side edge, or a non-planar face whose new corner lies outside its surface's natural domain (with `SM_EFF_ZERO_SQRT * (1 + max dimension)` slack), fails the removal with `NotExact`, and so does a corner of more than three edges. Nothing changes before `DoRemoval`, which makes the side edges' UV trims anew from their curves.
- **Rust port:** `FilletRemoval::set_exact_only` / `not_exact` / `sharp_edge` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_removal.rs`), off by default; `delete_fillet_face` never sets it. Tests: `exact_only_is_off_by_default`, `exact_only_removal_on_lines_and_planes_is_upstreams` (the rounded cube comes back sharp, the same as upstream's removal), and, in the `blend.fillet_removal` runner (`crates/difftest/src/units/blend_fillet_removal.rs`, `exact_only_tests`), `exact_only_refuses_inexact_removals_and_agrees_with_the_rest`: every face of the 18 upstream fillet fixtures, each refusal with `not_exact` leaving the solid unchanged, each exact removal giving the default removal's face and edge counts, at least one refusal.
