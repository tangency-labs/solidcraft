#### WR-46 (update, 2026-10-31). Opt-in correction of `EvaluateSimple`'s `Duw` y component

- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmTwistVolume.cpp:1045`, `SmTwistVolume::EvaluateSimple`; Rust `crates/solidcore/src/surface/twist_volume.rs`, `TwistVolume::evaluate_simple_impl`.
- **Evidence:** The pinned C++ source sets `Duw.y` to `per * -cos`, while `Evaluate` at line 861 uses `per * cos` along the binormal. For an identity-frame twist with period 4 at `(0.7, -0.4, 0)`, the Rust port returns -1.5707963267948966; the central difference of `Du` in `w` with step 1e-6 gives +1.5707963267942506. The new regression failed before the correction. This numerical reproduction is Rust-only; it does not newly confirm the defect through a C++ execution.
- **Rust opt-in:** `Kernel::fixes.twist_simple_duw_sign`, default `false`. `CrvInVolume::evaluate` and `SrfInVolume::evaluate` copy the kernel fixes into `EvalOptions`; volume dispatch reads the flag and corrects only simple `Duw.y` to `per * cos`. Options survive orientation and compounding, including analytic volumes' next maps. Kernel-free calls keep pinned behaviour unless the caller supplies fixes in its options. The extra public `evaluate_simple_with_kernel` method was removed.
- **Production reachability:** `SmVolume.cpp` calls virtual `EvaluateSimple` from `Evaluate` (3212/3230), `Map` (3713/3732), and simple point/first-derivative/directional-derivative helpers (6533/6556/6636). Other calls at 4223 and 7701 only request first derivatives. Rust's twist overrides `Evaluate` with the already-correct direct formula, but inherits the base `Map` path through `Volume::evaluate_simple`; curve/surface lifts in in-space and compounded next maps reach it with second and third derivatives.
- **Regression:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr46_twist_simple_duw.rs` compares mapped mixed derivatives against central differences at zero and nonzero `w`, for both handednesses and orders 2/3; checks default parity and unchanged other entries; and evaluates a real `CrvInVolume` directly in in-space and through a parameter-space compounded map. The mapped surface also checks its mixed derivative through `SrfInVolume::evaluate`, with an oriented twist. These cases require incorrect second derivatives with the flag off and agreement with finite differences with it on.
