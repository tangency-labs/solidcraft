#### WR-722. A face made from exact pcurves loses them: the callers drop them, and `MakeFaceWithCurves`'s keep step refuses them on a face of opposite orientation
- **Severity:** Wrong result (accuracy: exact pcurves replaced by fits)
- **Area:** Topology (face construction and copy)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp`:
  - `SmBrep::MakeFaceWithCurves`. Its keep step (`pbOptCrvOnSurf` TRUE) calls `rpNewFace->CreateUVTrimCurves(FALSE, &sNewUVCurves, &crCurveOrientations, ...)` with the orientations as given, along the loop.
  - `SmBrep::CopyFaces`, the branch without the merge. It takes the face's curves and trims (`CreateCurvesFromFace`) and passes them to `MakeFaceWithCurves` without `pbOptCrvOnSurf`.
  - The fork's draft walls (`SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp`, `sm_CreateCurvatureKeepingWalls`, tangency-labs/smlib 54148db and e8f23e0) make each wall with `MakeFaceWithCurves(..., &s3D, &sUV, sOrient, ..., SM_OT_OPPOSITE, ...)` without `pbOptCrvOnSurf` either.
- **What happens:** a face's exact pcurves are dropped at three points, and each edge's curve is projected again later.
  1. The walls' own `MakeFaceWithCurves` drops the exact pcurves their construction made and checked: the seams' affine images on a plane wall and the rational quartics on a cone wall.
  2. Asked to keep them, `CreateUVTrimCurves` matches each given curve to an edge use with `sm_FindBestUVCurve`. That compares the lifted tangent, flipped where the given orientation differs from the primary edge use's, with the edge's tangent, flipped where the edge use's orientation differs from the primary's. `CreateUVTrimCurves` walks the upward face use. On a face made with its surface's orientation opposite (`SM_OT_OPPOSITE`), that face use's edge uses run against the loop the orientations are given along. So the tangent test fails at the exact hit (distance 1e-15) and `sm_FindBestUVCurve` refuses every given curve. The keep step then projects each edge's curve again (`GetOrCreateUVTrimCurve`). Fillets, the only upstream callers that keep their pcurves, always pass `SM_OT_SAME`, so the defect is latent there.
  3. The draft builds its walls in a scratch B-rep and copies them into the target with `CopyFaces(faces, target, NULL, FALSE)`, which drops the trims again.
- **Trigger:** `SmApiCreateTaperExtrude` with the curvature-keeping corners (`OffsetCornerType::CurveExtension`): a half disc of radius 20, its diameter on the x axis, drafted 10 degrees in over a height of 10. The arc's wall is on the cone `rho = 20 - z tan 10`, and its edges are on it to 1e-13. Its two seam pcurves come back 2.37e-6 off their edges. For 2. alone: a face `MakeFaceWithCurves` makes with `SM_OT_OPPOSITE` and `pbOptCrvOnSurf` TRUE from a clockwise loop of a cylinder sub-domain's sides with their iso lines. Every edge use ends up with a projection, up to 1.8e-15 off its iso line, instead of the iso line it was given.
- **How found:** tangency-labs/solidcraft#10850 and tangency-labs/smlib#97 (item 5: `MakeFaceWithCurves` callers outside fillets don't keep their exact pcurves); solidcore #2405. The draft wall's seam pcurves were traced stage by stage (wall face, `StitchFaces`, `CopyFaces`, `SmBrep::ShrinkGeometry`, `StitchAndOrient`). `SmBrep::ShrinkGeometry` doesn't trim the cone wall. It trims the plane walls and removes their trims; WR-700 covers only `SmFace::ShrinkGeometry`. The other callers in the survey already end with exact pcurves or differ only by rounding: the sweep's edge faces (`SweepEdgeHigh`) end with their exact iso lines anyway, and `CreateFaceInRegionFromSurface`'s projections are within 1.8e-15 of its iso lines. Solidcore-native: the fork at its last commit (18e96f1) has none of the three changes.
- **Confirmed:** in the Rust port (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2405_blend_follow_ups.rs`).
  - `a_draft_cone_wall_keeps_its_exact_seam_pcurves`: with the flag, the cone wall's four pcurves lift onto their edges within 2.7e-13 (the seams 1.2e-13). Without it, the seams' are 2.37e-6 off. Each of the three changes is needed.
  - `an_opposite_face_keeps_the_pcurves_it_is_given`: with the flag, every edge use keeps its given iso line, exactly on its side of the domain. Without it, the projections replace them.
- **Rust port (opt-in):** `Kernel::fixes.keep_exact_pcurves` (`UpstreamFixes`, default `false`).
  - `PrimitiveCreationAccess::face_with_curves` (the draft walls) passes `pbOptCrvOnSurf`.
  - `topology::brep::p7`'s keep step flips the orientations for a face made with `OrientType::Opposite`.
  - `copy_faces_named` (`CopyFaces` without the merge) passes `pbOptCrvOnSurf` with the face's own trims.
