#### WR-695. A cliff overflow is subtracted from the body instead of trimmed at its creases
- **Severity:** Wrong result (refusal or wrong topology)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet executive)
- **Location:** the fork's `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCliffOverflow.cpp` (`CloseRemovalRegion` and the subtraction, deleted by tangency-labs/smlib 5c275e6), replaced by `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletOverflow.cpp` (`SmFilletExecutive::TrimAtCreases`), tangency-labs/smlib#79 step 6, merged at 5e8c9c1.
- **What happens:** a blend that runs past the faces it rolls on (WR-631's cliff overflow) was closed into the region the blends remove and that region subtracted from the body. The closing has to guess the region's sides past the cliffs (WR-635, WR-636), and where the blend's creases and rails enclose no part of the body the subtraction still builds something. The fork instead trims the built sheet in place: each sheet face is cut along its creases with the body's faces, kept only where it lies inside the body, the body's faces cut back along the same creases, and the pieces sewn at the sheet's own accuracy. A blend whose kept faces reach every face of the body is refused (`SM_FILERR_BAD_MERGE`); a crease across a tangent edge of a face the blend rolls on, a blend whose corners take its whole edge, and a rail that runs past its face into material are `SM_FILERR_NO_ROOM`.
- **Trigger:** any cliff overflow, e.g. a stepped bar's inner step edge chamfered 4 (subtracted to 1700 - 160; the fork refuses it, every face reached), and SolidCraft's rig cases 5181, 4055 and 477 (fork bc8c44d, 8832ead).
- **How found:** SolidCraft, tangency-labs/smlib#79 (the generic blend's step 6), commits 99e57b8, 668fe9f, bacd0bb, 5c275e6, 2d4b154, bc8c44d, de5df14, 8832ead.
- **Confirmed:** a local build of the fork at 5e8c9c1 run on the regression test's bodies (`SmFilletExecutive` on the same edge, no global merge, volume at 1e-7): the same refusal for the chamfer, and the volumes and face counts the test asserts. Not reachable in the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`), which has no cliff overflow.
- **Rust port:** `Kernel::fixes.fillet_overflow_trim_at_creases` (with `fillet_cliff_overflow`; off keeps the subtraction byte for byte): `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_overflow.rs` (the file's free functions) and `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_executive/session/trim_at_creases.rs` (`TrimAtCreases`). A round with a WR-674 section end (not in the fork at 5e8c9c1) is still subtracted. The three fit notes step 5 adds inside `TrimAtCreases` (tangency-labs/smlib a3d6d81, 49f393d) are marked `FIT(#2278)` for that port to wire.

  Tests: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr695_overflow_trim_at_creases.rs` (seven bodies, flag on against the fork's volumes to 1e-8 relative and its face counts, flag off against the subtraction; the every-face chamfer refused only with the flag).
