#### WR-531. The extended offset leaves a cone's apex open: a box with a blind drilled hole does not shell
- **Severity:** Wrong result (the shell is left open at the apex)
- **Area:** Offsets and shells
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp`, `SmOffsetExecutive::DoExtendedOffset`: it builds no vertex offsets, unlike the unextended offset, which calls `BuildOffsetOfVertex` for every vertex.
- **What happens:** The extended offset (shells, and offsets with extended convex edges) extends offset faces until they meet and builds no vertex offsets. Consider a vertex where one face closes on itself to a point: a cone's apex whose seam is its only edge, as at the tip of a drilled hole. It has no neighbouring offsets to meet, and the cone's offset ends on the circle the apex's normals sweep out (radius `d cos(59 deg)` for a 118 degree drill tip). The cavity is left open there and the offset is not a closed solid. `SmOffsetExecutive::ShellBrep` hands such an offset back as a success when no offset solid is asked for (`SmOffsetExecutive.cpp:255-262`, `return SM_SUCCESS;   // was SM_ERR: ??`); the fork's shell fails (1001) only because its WR-454 check (`shell_empty_cavity_fails`, tangency-labs/smlib@fbe43bb) refuses that open offset. Flat-bottomed holes and through holes shell fine.
- **Trigger:** a box with a blind drilled hole shelled open at a side or at the top (SolidCraft robustness `shell_after_a_drilled_hole`).
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft. The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@d5dadaa ("shell: the apex of a cone (a drilled hole's tip) is offset as a sphere"), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run was made here. The Rust port's `do_extended_offset` follows the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`).
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.offset_point_vertex_caps`, `OffsetExecutive::do_extended_offset` acts after the collapsed-face squeeze and before the excess-face removal, as the fork places it. For each vertex of the original with exactly one edge, it builds the vertex offset (`build_offset_of_vertex`). Where that gives a surface, it makes a face of the whole surface (`make_cap_face` with no curves) and relates it to the vertex. `tests/wr_531_shell_drilled_hole_tip.rs` shells a box with an r 3 blind hole 2 mm inward, open at a side and, separately, at the top. With the fix each result is a closed solid with one sphere face, with and without WR-454's check; its level-2 check is clean. Without the fix the result is not a closed solid, and with WR-454's check the shell fails.
