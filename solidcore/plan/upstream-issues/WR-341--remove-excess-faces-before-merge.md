#### WR-341. `SmOffsetExecutive::DoExtendedOffset` removes "excess" offset faces before the merge, while they are untrimmed
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a closed shell fails with `SM_ERR`; a whole offset gives a body of volume 0)
- **Area:** Offset
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetExecutive.cpp`, `SmOffsetExecutive::DoExtendedOffset`: `SER(RemoveExcessFaces(m_pOffsetBrep))` before `PiecewiseMerge`, and `if (!m_bCreateOffsetSolid) SER(RemoveExcessFaces(pResult))` after it.
- **What happens:** for closed shells and whole offsets (no open faces), `RemoveExcessFaces` deletes every face with an edge midpoint nearer the body than 0.95 × the offset distance. Before the merge an offset face is not trimmed yet: its loop runs out to where its neighbours' offsets meet it, past where other offsets cut it, so a face that bounds the offset can have an edge well inside the excess, and it is taken. The merged offset then encloses no region and `MakeManifold` has nothing to keep. Open shells skip the function, so they pass.
- **Trigger:** the ladder 09/011 part shelled closed 1.5 inward: 8 edges at 1.00 to 1.42 from the part flag 5 faces (the wall, the back wall, the round, the chamfer and the window arch); 1001. `SmApiOffsetBrepFull(-1.5)`: volume 0. A plate whose hole offset crosses a side, shelled closed: 1001.
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig on the SMLib fork (tangency-labs/solidcore#977, root cause of #865); gdb on the fork's C++ build.
- **Confirmed:** on the fork's C++ build. With the fix, closed plates whose hole offset crosses one side or both match their closed forms to 1e-7; the 09/011 part shelled closed gives 18041.1012; its whole offset is a solid of 34965.8345, the part less the closed shell to 2.5e-8.
- **Fix (fork commit "SMLib: excess offset faces are found after the merge"):** the pre-merge call is dropped; the post-merge call (where each merged face lies wholly on the offset or wholly in the excess) also runs for a solid input whatever `m_bCreateOffsetSolid` says (a sheet's side walls run from the sheet and would be taken for excess; a solid has none).
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.offset_excess_faces_after_merge` (default `false`, upstream behaviour), read in `OffsetExecutive::do_extended_offset` (`crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p2.rs`). Test `issue_977_closed_shell_excess_faces_after_merge` (a 30 x 20 x 6 plate with a hole 1 from a side, shelled closed 1.5 inward; both runs also set `offset_resolve_crossing_loops`, WR-345, since the hole's offset crosses the side's). No difftest case compares the opt-in path (the `offset.offset_executive_p2` runner uses the default kernel). Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#977.
