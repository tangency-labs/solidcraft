Updates: WR-587

#### WR-587 (update, 2026-11-23). `curve_loop_normal_from_area` is removed; a closed loop's normal is its area vector, an open chain's of no area its plane's normal

`SmCurve::ComputeCrvsNormal` (`curve::curve::p3::compute_crvs_normal` and `compute_crvs_normal_written`) now answers the unit area vector of the sampled polygon, summed as a fan about its first sample, whenever that vector's direction is certain past its derived rounding bound. Otherwise an open chain whose samples span a plane (to the kernel's angular zero) answers that plane's normal, on the side the area takes when its component along the normal is certain, else on the side of the chain's summed turns when that sum is certain, else of the first turn past the angular zero (the cancelling Z path, exactly zero area, answers `-Z` that way). A closed loop of zero area, collinear samples, or an area within its rounding of zero refuses with `SM_ERR_INVALID_INPUT` and `(0, 0, 1)` written. The fork's other statuses stay: a failed sample evaluation is the call's status and a single linear curve keeps its `SM_ERR`. Samples whose sums overflow are scaled by a power of two first. Upstream's weighted turns are gone as the answer, and so is the flag. Removed: the field, its `kernel/fixes` doc, the tests' flag lines and the default-path half of `issue_1776_concave_taper` (the draft that leaned the wrong way).

- **Tests:** `curve::curve::p3::tests` (the plus in both orientations and planes, every starting corner, curved loops, open chains, zero areas, the single line, failed evaluations) and `issue_1776_concave_taper` (the plus drafted both ways at the analytic volumes) now run on the only path. In the goldens crate, `units::curve_curve_p3::tests::wr587_every_normal_row_is_the_area_normal` certifies every committed `Normal` row of `curve.curve_p3` against the exact area vector of its samples (expansion arithmetic, with a derived bound on the kernel's double sum), and `wr587_replay::every_moved_row_moved_only_through_the_loop_normal` replays each moved row.
- **Goldens:** against main `769bea6b0b`, 243 rows move in 3 units: `curve.curve_p3` 222, `primitive.api_curve_ops` 19, `primitive.primitive_creation_p1` 2. With main's turns substituted for every loop normal call (a test seam, feature `wr57-replay`, goldens tests only), each row hashes to main's line. Without the substitution it is the committed line, and every call's own answer is certified (as the area normal, or as the plane normal of an open chain). By class: `curve_p3` 169 succeed on the same side as main's normal, 52 reversed, and 1 newly answered; `api_curve_ops` 10 same side, 9 reversed; `primitive_creation_p1`'s 2 rows make 4 calls that refuse as on main (2 exact zero areas, 2 single lines; only the status's message moves). The newly answered row is `curve_p3` gen1544, status 1007 -> 0: main's weighted turns cancel to exactly zero there, while the exact area vector of its samples is certainly nonzero, and the area certificate proves the answer is its direction.
