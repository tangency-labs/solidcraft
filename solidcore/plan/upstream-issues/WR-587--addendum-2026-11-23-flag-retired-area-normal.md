Updates: WR-587

#### WR-587 (update, 2026-11-23). `curve_loop_normal_from_area` is removed; a loop's normal is always its area vector

`SmCurve::ComputeCrvsNormal` (`curve::curve::p3::compute_crvs_normal` and `compute_crvs_normal_written`) now always answers the unit area vector of the sampled polygon, summed as a fan about its first sample, with the fork's statuses (a failed sample evaluation is the call's status; a zero area is `SM_ERR_INVALID_INPUT` with `(0, 0, 1)` written; a single linear curve keeps its `SM_ERR`). Upstream's weighted turns are gone, and so is the flag. Removed: the field, its `kernel/fixes` doc, the tests' flag lines and the default-path half of `issue_1776_concave_taper` (the draft that leaned the wrong way).

- **Tests:** `curve::curve::p3::tests` (the plus in both orientations and planes, every starting corner, curved loops, open chains, zero areas, the single line, failed evaluations) and `issue_1776_concave_taper` (the plus drafted both ways at the analytic volumes) now run on the only path. In the goldens crate, `units::curve_curve_p3::tests::wr587_every_normal_row_is_the_area_normal` certifies every committed `Normal` row of `curve.curve_p3` against the exact area vector of its samples (expansion arithmetic, with a derived bound on the kernel's double sum), and `wr587_replay::every_moved_row_moved_only_through_the_loop_normal` replays each moved row.
- **Goldens:** against main `769bea6b0b`, 245 rows move in 4 units: `curve.curve_p3` 222, `primitive.api_curve_ops` 19, `primitive.primitive_creation_p1` 2, `primitive.primitive_creation_p2` 2. With main's turns substituted for every loop normal call (a test seam, feature `wr57-replay`, goldens tests only), each row hashes to main's line. Without the substitution it is the committed line, and every call's own answer is certified as the area normal. The calls by class are: 179 succeed on the same side as main's normal, 61 succeed reversed, 1 now answers where main's turns cancelled to zero, 2 now refuse an exact zero area (a line run back over itself; the taper refused those rows on main as well), and 4 refuse as on main (no curves or a single line; only the status's message moves). No valid input becomes a refusal.
