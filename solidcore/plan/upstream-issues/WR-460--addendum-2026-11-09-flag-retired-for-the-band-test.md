#### WR-460 (update, 2026-11-09). `fillet_refuse_crossing_rails` retired; WR-542's band test refuses these rounds

- **What changed:** the opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_refuse_crossing_rails` and its check (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_executive/session/crossing_rails.rs`, called from `session/finish.rs` after `CreateFilletBrep`) are deleted. The defect itself (the pinned SMLib builds an open body) is unchanged: `Kernel::default()` still builds it (`issue_1073_upstream_builds_an_open_body`).
- **Why:** it tested what WR-542 (`fixes.fillet_refuse_overlapping_bands`, the fork's `BandsClearOtherRails`) tests, a rail inside the band another round takes off a shared face, but later (after the fillet's B-rep was built), and it refused with a bare `Upstream { code: 1001 }` that names no size. WR-542 runs right after the trace and refuses with `NoRoomReason::BandsOverlap` and the largest radius at which the bands stay apart. Two band tests that disagree (the twisted disc of the generic blend's collapsed-offset case, where WR-460 refused 1.9 and 1.9586 that WR-542 passed) left the user a refusal with no fit.
- **Coverage now:** with SolidCraft's fix list (`tests/kernel_cases/fixes.json`), the post's four vertical edges at r = 3.03, 3.0625, 3.5 and 4 are refused `BandsOverlap`, fit 3 (to 1e-9), and the fit builds a closed solid (`issue_1073_rounds_past_the_face_are_refused`). The rounds WR-460 once refused wrongly (`wr460_untrimmed_rails.rs`, `issue_2050_crossing_rails_query_no_solution.rs`) build with the band test on, bit for bit as without it. The SolidCraft cases `fixtures/solidcraft/199-*` and `871-*` that asked for the flag ask for `fillet_refuse_overlapping_bands` instead.
- **Twisted disc:** with the side rounds their definitions (sc#13774), its top round at 1.9 and 1.9586 builds, `check_brep` L2 clean and manifold; the crossing-rails check was still reached at 1.9586 and passed.
