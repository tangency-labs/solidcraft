Updates: WR-647

#### WR-647 (addendum 2026-11-17): contacts seen through the tolerance are decided on merged stretches; `simple_coincidence_crossing_is_point` is retired

- **Status in the port:** `UpstreamFixes::simple_coincidence_crossing_is_point` is gone, with its doc, the preset line in `crates/smlib/src/core.rs`, its `fixes.json` entry and the flag settings in the integration tests. `Curve::simple_coincidence_checker` (`curve/curve/p6.rs`) returns upstream's coincidence range again in every case. The flag's per-span-pair rules (c2e3d9d's crossing case and 04b42b4's case 3e) are not kept.
- **Why the flag could not be retired as it was:** one Bezier span pair cannot tell a grazing crossing from tolerance-scale fit noise. With the flag on, the #1036 coincident skins (a section curve that is a fitted copy of the edge, 2.6e-6 to 1.7e-5 off at tolerance 2e-5, changing sides at random) came apart into points, and the Booleans gave wrong bodies (A - B empty, A ∩ B all of A, A ∪ B failing).
- **The rule now:** `Curve::contacts_seen_through_tolerance` (`curve/curve/p8.rs`) runs after the ranges are merged: in `GlobalCurveIntersect` before `classify_ranges` and at the single-leaf exit, and in `GlobalCurveSolve` on the parallel projected path. Each stretch (ranges that overlap or meet) is judged as a whole:
  - **Crossing:** the separations at the stretch's two ends point to opposite sides and the curves part at both ends (each end lies inside both curves). The answer is the one crossing `local_curve_intersect` finds strictly inside the stretch, nearer than every range end (#1979's ring lip).
  - **Touch:** the stretch is all the solutions and the curves part at one or both ends. The touch is the least `Minimize` result started from every range end. It is taken for one point only when (a) it is nearer than where the curves part, (b) the curves **meet** there: the evaluated distance is at most `SM_EFF_ZERO * (1 + max coordinate)`, after a `SignedDirectedMinimize` refinement across the tangent when `Minimize` stalls short of a flat tangency, (c) it is not on a fold of a curve against itself, and (d) it is **isolated**: with `κ` the across-tangent part of the difference of the two curvature vectors there, the lift `κ x² / 2` reaches the tolerance within both stretch ends' tangent reach `x` (the curves cannot stay within tolerance past the stretch), and exceeds numeric zero (an exact overlap, `κ = 0`, stays a range).
  - Everything else keeps upstream's ranges: a coincidence running to a curve end (#1036, concentric arcs, an edge against its fitted copy), curves within tolerance that never meet, several touches, and crossings the solver does not find.
- **Never-meeting curves are ranges (the reviewer's probe):** a curve within 4e-6 of a line over x in [-0.875, 0.875] at tolerance 1e-5, leaving on the same side, is a coincidence at that tolerance, not a touch. Upstream returns the range; so does the port. Test: `wr_647_a_curve_lying_within_tolerance_on_part_of_a_line_stays_a_range`.
- **Perspective and rotation projections keep their ranges:** their projected derivatives are upstream's secant approximations, and the solver stops short of the contact (goldens `curve.curve_p9` gen0088 found 8.6e-5 where the true contact is 7.9e-9).
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr_647_concentric_arcs_nearest_point.rs` (crossing, touch, flat touch, never-meeting and exact-overlap cases, coincidence to a curve end), `issue_1979_ring_lip_crossing_not_coincidence.rs` (default kernel, every offset valid), `issue_1036_coincident_skin_sides.rs` (no flag), and goldens `curve_curve_p8::tests::range_end_tail_and_its_head_are_one_crossing` plus `moved_contacts_lie_in_their_base_ranges`.
- **Moved golden rows:** nine, each a range (or two that meet) that becomes the one point inside it where the curves pass each other or meet, nearer than base's range ends: `curve.curve_p8` gen0288 (7.1e-9), gen0940 (9.3e-10), gen1137 (9.1e-11), gen1319 (3.2e-14), gen2141 (5.72e-4, two curves passing each other 5.72e-4 apart at tolerance 1e-2), gen2160 and `range_end_preserves_noncollapsed_tail` (4e-15), gen2904 (7.0e-16); `intersect.topology_solver_p1` gen2408 (a line and a Hermite curve at tolerance 100 that meet, 5.3e-13 apart, inside base's range whose ends are 45 and 42 apart). Certified by `curve_curve_p8::tests::moved_contacts_lie_in_their_base_ranges` and `intersect_topology_solver_p1::tests::wr_647_gen2408_meets_inside_its_base_range`: for the `curve_p8` rows the separations at the base ends point to opposite sides; for every row the point lies inside the base stretch, its evaluated gap is within the stated bound and below both base end gaps, and no stencil neighbour is nearer. Rows that an earlier version of this change moved and that are back at base: `surface.plane_p2` gen0007 and `curve.curve_p8` polyline_touching_vertex (curves that touch without the isolation or meeting test passing keep their range).
- **Convention change:** goldens `range_end_refinement_keeps_the_noncollapsed_tail` pinned the base answer (three solutions, the tail kept as a range). On its inputs the tail and its head are one stretch the curves cross inside (gap < 1e-12 at the crossing, > 0.1 at both old far ends), so the answer is now the crossing and that test fails. The #527 range-end check it was written for stays covered by `issue_527_refine_cc_range_end` in `curve/curve/p1.rs`.
- **Known limits:** the touch is the least of local minima started from every range end, not a global minimisation. The isolation test uses the curvature at the touch only; a stretch whose curvature changes strongly between the touch and its ends is judged by the curvature at the touch. Straight pieces have no curvature, so a touch between them (goldens polyline_touching_vertex, two polylines touching at a vertex) is not certified isolated and keeps upstream's range.
