#### WR-579. `SmProtoEdge::Resolve` keeps an intersection edge whole past a third vertex definition of its own proto vertex, and the union of two tubes a hair apart end to end fails
- **Severity:** Wrong result (the boolean fails on valid input)
- **Area:** Booleans
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmProtoTopology.cpp`, `SmProtoEdge::Resolve`: the branch for a proto edge whose start and end proto vertex are the same and whose curve is longer than `2*m_dTol`. When the curve's ends find two different vertex definitions of that proto vertex, nothing is done; the other definitions of the proto vertex are not looked at.
- **What happens:** a proto vertex collects the intersection points within its capture distance (0.01 mm), and `Resolve` makes one vertex of the definitions within a few tolerances of each other and separate vertices of the rest. A proto edge whose curve runs from one of its definitions to another is kept as one edge between those two vertices, but a third definition of the same proto vertex lying on the curve between them becomes a vertex of its own face with the edge running past it. The merge's classification then meets a vertex that is not its edge's, and the boolean fails.
- **Trigger:** a bicycle frame's seat tube (r 16, from (0, 0, 300) towards (-150, 0, 820)) ending 3.7e-4 short of where the top tube (r 16, (-150, 0, 820) to (600, 0, 860)) starts, united. Where the walls touch, the seat's end circle runs within tolerance of the top tube's wall for a stretch and crosses it there too; the crossing and the stretch's ends fall in one proto vertex as three definitions. The fork reports failures for gaps from 1e-4 to 1e-3.
- **How found:** reported by SolidCraft (its bench frame; tangency-labs/solidcore#1517). The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@9eec159 ("boolean: an intersection curve inside one ProtoVertex is split at the vertices on it"), which is not merged upstream.
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the C++ build: the `boolean.api_brep` fixture `issue_1517_tube_ends.json` (`checked` and `measured` unions at the default fixes) gives the same failed status on both sides (CI run 41068103436, both cases pass with the status the only compared value), and the port's union fails there with 1001 in `SmMerge::MakeFaceuse` (`wr579_near_coincident_tube_ends_join`, fix off). With the fix the union is one manifold solid; its check still reports `SmEllipse` test 2 on an 8.6e-5 ellipse piece, the separate defect #1518, as the fork's commit notes.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.proto_edge_splits_at_captured_vertices`, `ProtoTopologyManager::resolve_proto_edge` splits such a proto edge at each other definition of the proto vertex that `DropPoint` finds on the curve (within the larger of the proto edge's raw tolerance and the definition's, inside the interval, farther than twice that from both ends), from the far end back, then resolves the proto vertex, as the fork does. `crates/solidcore/tests/wr579_near_coincident_tube_ends_join.rs` builds the tubes as rotated `SmApiCreateCylinder`s (the fork uses its C API's extrude) and checks that the union fails without the fix and is one sound solid with it.
