#### WR-51 (update, 2026-11-16). `point_on_start_point_squared_tolerance` is removed

`SurfaceIntersector::is_point_on_start_point` (upstream `IsPointOnStartPoint`) compares each surface point's distance with the tolerance, so a point is on a start point within a real distance of `this_approx_tol_3d` (upstream compares the squared distance with the tolerance: radius `sqrt(tol)`). This is now the only path, and the flag is gone. Removed: the field, the kernel argument of `is_point_on_start_point`, SolidCraft's preset line, its entries in `fixes.json` and the issue 2260 flag list, and the pinned half of `is_point_on_start_point_accepts_a_real_distance`.

- **Defect found on the fixed path:** upstream's `FindBoundaryStartPoints` drops a touching point that lies on a start point for good, assuming the curve traced from that start point covers it. Above a tolerance of 1 the real-distance test reaches farther than upstream's (radius `tol`, not `sqrt(tol)`). In goldens `intersect.surface_intersector_p5` gen0236 (two plane patches 9.9 apart at their corners, tolerance 2785) the corner touching point then matched a start point that traced no curve, and the intersection came back empty. The same loss happens on upstream's own radius, and when the traced curve leaves the point behind. Now the touching point is kept aside, and at the end of `DoIntersection` it is added when its two surface points are closer than the tolerance and no curve found covers it (`SurfaceIntersector::add_deferred_touching_points`).
- **Results:** `intersect.surface_intersector_p1` 21 cases (one `IsPointOnStartPoint` answer each); `boolean.api_brep` `issue1519_bench_tubes_union_measured` (one answer flips: a touching point 2.77e-4 from a start point at tolerance 5e-6 is no longer merged into it; a 3.06e-13 sliver face's area moves by a relative 9e-8); and 13 cases that had no curve and now report the dropped touching point: `intersect.surface_intersector_p5` gen0133, gen0247, gen0351 and gen0840, `intersect.api_intersectors` `ssi-revolution-plane-near-axis-parallel-swapped`, `ssi-revolution-plane-oblique-swapped` and `ssi0043` (which were 1001), and `surface.surf_of_extrusion_p2` `global_extrusion_skew`, `global_bspline`, gen0463, gen0581, gen0626 and gen1018. gen0236 keeps its touching point.
- **Tests:** `intersect::surface_intersector::p1::tests::is_point_on_start_point_accepts_a_real_distance`, `intersect::surface_intersector::p3::tests::deferred_touching_points_are_added_only_as_uncovered_contacts`, `intersect::surface_intersector::p5::tests::a_touching_point_no_curve_covers_is_kept`; goldens `intersect_surface_intersector_p1::wr51_tests`, and the `wr51_*` certificate tests of the `intersect_surface_intersector_p5`, `intersect_api_intersectors` and `surface_surf_of_extrusion_p2` runners.
