#### WR-727. `IsTopologicalVertex` counts only edges, so the boolean cleanup joins a seam through a point contact and moves the other face's vertex loop to the seam's far end
- **Severity:** Wrong result (invalid B-rep: `Bad Vertex Gap`)
- **Area:** Booleans (merge cleanup)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmVertex.cpp:716-741`, `SmVertex::IsTopologicalVertex`; `source/SMLib/src/SmMerge.cpp:1235-1255`, `SmMerge::BooleanPostProcess` (def. `:827`); `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp:7865-7866`, `SmBrep::DeleteTopologicalVertex` (def. `:7625`). `SmBrep::RemoveTopologicalEdgesAndVertices` (`SmBrep.cpp:26269-26285`) and the tessellator (`SmTess.cpp:4111`, `:4289-4335`) use the same predicate. In Rust, `crates/solidcore/src/topology/vertex.rs` (`is_topological_vertex`), `crates/solidcore/src/boolean/merge/p1.rs` (`boolean_post_process_with_notifications`) and `crates/solidcore/src/topology/brep/p5.rs` (`delete_topological_vertex_with_notifications`).
- **What happens:** where two solids touch at one point that lies inside an edge of one of them, the imprint splits that edge at the contact (`MergeIntoObject`, `MakeVertexSplitEdge`) and glues the split vertex to the vertex loop it made in the other solid's face. The contact vertex then has the two halves of the edge and a vertex loop in the other face. `IsTopologicalVertex` asks only for exactly two edges that continue each other (the check of the vertex's uses, `sVUs.GetSize() != 4`, is commented out "for wire edges"), so the vertex counts as removable. `BooleanPostProcess` deletes it with `DeleteTopologicalVertex`, whose `SqueezeEdge(pEdgeToDelete, pSurvVertex)` merges it into the far vertex of the shorter half. The other face's vertex loop goes with it. That vertex is a pole of the sphere or the end of the seam, away from the face: `SmVertex` check test 1 (`Bad Vertex Gap`) refuses the body. The volume is right, and the contact is lost from the topology. Joining two edges at a vertex (Mäntylä's `KEV`, *An Introduction to Solid Modeling*, 1988) is only defined for a vertex that only those two edges use.
- **Trigger:** `SmApiBooleanUnion` of a sphere centre (2.5, 15, 12.5), radius 7.5, and the box (10, 0, 0)-(15, 17.5, 15). They touch at (10, 15, 12.5), on the sphere's seam meridian, and the loop goes to the south pole (2.5, 15, 5), 7.5 off the box face. Other cases with the same cause: two spheres touching on the seam of one (gap 2.36), a sphere touching a cylinder on its seam line (2.83), a sphere touching a torus on its seam meridian (0.66), and a box touching a torus on its outer seam circle (7.38). Contacts off every seam, at a pole, or at the torus's seam vertex (four edges) are kept and check clean.
- **How found:** SolidCraft's boolean property test (`boolean_props identity_on_grid`), solidcore #2520. Traced in the Rust port: the split at t = 0.5 of the seam, the glue, then `delete_topological_vertex` from `boolean_post_process` on the contact vertex, which has six uses (four edge uses, two loop uses). The C++ (pinned v1.0.1 libraries, a small `SmApiBooleanUnion` driver) gives the same body: status `SM_SUCCESS`, 7 faces, volume 3079.645937984, one Level2 check failure; the off-seam control (centre (12.5, -7.5, 7.5)) checks clean there too.
- **Confirmed:** yes, in the C++ (above) and in the Rust port with the pinned behaviour, `crates/solidcore/tests/boolean_api.rs` `point_contact_unions_check_clean` fails on its six seam-edge cases without the flag.
- **Rust port (opt-in):** `Kernel::fixes.topological_vertex_has_only_edge_uses` (`UpstreamFixes`, default `false`). With it, `is_topological_vertex` returns false for a vertex with a use that is not an edge use (a vertex loop in another face, a shell vertex). The contact vertex stays, splitting the seam, with the other face's vertex loop on it. This covers every caller: `boolean_post_process`, `remove_topological_with_notifications` and `topological_vertices`. Regression: `point_contact_unions_check_clean` (the issue's union in both orders and the variants above, at the analytic volume, all Level2 clean) and `point_contacts_off_seam_edges_check_clean_by_default` (the controls, unchanged without the flag).
