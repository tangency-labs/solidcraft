#### WR-18212. `sm_AssertTestFace34` reports two edges that only share a vertex as partially coincident, depending on the tolerance
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Topology
- **Location:** `sm_AssertTestFace34` (`SmFace.cpp`) fails a face when `SmCurve::GlobalCoincidenceChecker` (`SmCurve.cpp:19950`) returns any range for an edge pair; the checker returns any two drops more than `SM_EFF_ZERO` apart in parameter, after the gap check, as a range.
- **What happens:** two edges that leave a shared vertex at an angle stay within the tolerance of each other for a short way, about `tol / sin(angle)`. When one of them is split there (closed or half closed, e.g. a hook at its start), a split piece that lies inside that way without reaching the vertex has its own drops on the other curve and a gap under the tolerance, so the checker returns it as a range and test 34 reports the edges as partially coincident. Whether a piece falls inside depends on where the splits land, so a smaller tolerance can fail the test where a larger one passes.
- **Trigger:** edges e21 (a line) and e24 (a C0 cubic B-spline with a 1e-5 hook at its start) of face 9 of the base body of `blend.feature_executive` case `single_face_fillet1019_6`, which meet at about 38 degrees: ranges at `tol` 1.4e-3, 1.4897e-3, 2.5e-3, 4e-3 and 1e-2, none at 1e-3, 2e-3 or 2.889e-3 (SolidCraft issue #18212).
- **How found:** `check_brep` face test 34 on the goldens case above (SolidCraft issue #18212).
- **Confirmed:** in the port, which follows `GlobalCoincidenceChecker` and `sm_AssertTestFace34` step for step here: the test `face34_edges_that_only_share_a_vertex_are_not_partially_coincident` (`crates/solidcore/src/topology/brep/validation/assert_valid.rs`) fails without the port's deviation. The C++ was not run on these curves.
- **Rust port:** test 34 does not count a range that is one point at the tolerance on either edge (tight box diagonal under the tolerance; `topology/brep/validation/assert_valid/face.rs`, module deviations). This covers edges meeting at about 24 degrees or more. `GlobalCoincidenceChecker` keeps upstream's answer: other callers rely on those ranges (changing it there moved 70 goldens rows in seven units).
