#### WR-576. `CreateLinearSweep` takes a curve within the B-rep's tolerance of a circle for that circle
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Sweeps
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp:1339` (v1.0.1, `3fd5b95`), `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateLinearSweep` (curve overload, def. `:1197`): `IsArc(8, dTol, ...)` with `dTol` the B-rep's tolerance; the same test at `:1672` in `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateLinearSweepSimple` (def. `:1554`).
- **What happens:** a profile curve that `IsArc` finds within the B-rep's tolerance of a circle, over a whole turn, is swept as the `SmCylinder` of its fitted circle, not as itself. The body is the fitted circle's, not the sweep of the curve, and the curve's own edge lies off that cylinder by up to the tolerance. In the SolidCraft fork, where every arc swept square to its plane becomes a cylinder (#1477, WR-565), a text outline's quadratic Bezier 4.3e-9 off its fitted circle becomes one, and SMLib's check refuses the body (`SmSurfOfRevolution` test #9, issue #1507).
- **Trigger:** the exact rational circle of radius 0.4 about (0.5, 0.5, -1) stretched by 5e-7 along x (an ellipse about 2e-7 off its fitted circle), swept 1 along +Z with `SmApiCreateLinearSweep`: an `SmCylinder` and two `SmPlane`s, whose volume is 4.7e-8 of itself off the ellipse's.
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression rig (an extruded text outline), issue #1507.
- **Confirmed:** no. The pinned source tests the arc to `dTol` (`:1339`). In the Rust port the case above gives the cylinder and the volume error; there the stitched edge is the cylinder's circle and `smlib_brep_check` passes. The check failure is the issue's, on the fork.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.linear_sweep_arc_to_cylinder_zero` (`UpstreamFixes`, default `false`: upstream's test to the B-rep's tolerance) in `PrimitiveCreation::create_linear_sweep` (`crates/solidcore/src/primitive/primitive_creation/p1/primitives.rs`, helper `arc_zero`). With it, the arc test is to the zero an `SmCylinder`'s own check holds its generating curve to, `SM_EFF_ZERO * 1000 * (1 + the largest coordinate of the curve's ends)`, as the fork's `ArcZero` (tangency-labs/smlib fd59111, ported from #1507's description and the diff it quotes). Only a curve that is a circle becomes a cylinder; any other is swept as itself (`SmSurfOfExtrusion`). With #1495 (WR-565), the partial arc's cylinder comes from the same `is_arc` call, so this zero applies to it too. `CreateLinearSweepSimple` has no Rust port. Every difftest runs with the field off. Test `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_1507_linear_sweep_arc_zero.rs`: the near circle above (field off: the fitted cylinder, volume off; on: its extrusion, exact volume, `smlib_brep_check` clean), an exact rational and an analytic circle (a cylinder either way), and two text-outline letters of lines and quadratic Beziers in z = -1 swept 1 along +Z (valid, volume equal to the outline's exact area by Green's theorem). Fixes tangency-labs/solidcore#1507.
