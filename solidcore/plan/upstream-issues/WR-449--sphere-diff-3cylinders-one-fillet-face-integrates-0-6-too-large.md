#### WR-449. `Fillet/sphere_diff_3cylinders.smb`: one of six symmetric fillet faces integrates 0.6 too much area and 2.1 too much volume
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Mass properties
- **Location:** the trim of edge 30 that `SmFace::CreateOrientedTrimBoundary` makes for face 7 when `SmFace::ComputePreciseProperties` (`source/SMLib/src/SmFace.cpp`) integrates it: `SmFace::CreateUVTrimCurves` -> `SmEdgeuse::GetOrCreateUVTrimCurve` -> `SmEdgeuse::CreateUVTrimCurve` (`source/SMLib/src/SmEdgeuse.cpp`, `DropCurve` / `DropAndTrimCurve`), with `sm_FixUVTrimCurves` (`SmFace.cpp`) called from `CreateUVTrimCurves`. Which of these steps yields the single cubic span is not yet pinned.
- **What happens:** the six cone fillet faces (faces 2-7 in read order) are symmetric pairs about the origin. At accuracy 1e-9, with each face's own box midpoint as the area origin and the B-rep box midpoint (the origin) and area estimate of `SmBrep::ComputePreciseProperties` for the volume:

  Faces 0-1 (spheres) and 2-6 agree to the last bit. Face 7 alone differs: C++ is 0.601 larger in area and 2.141 larger in volume term, and both C++ values stand apart from the five symmetric siblings (area 3158.6906-3158.6959, volume term 34760.369-34760.372), which the port's face 7 matches. This one face is the whole of `corpus.parity`'s disagreement on the file: B-rep area 38236.6055 (C++) vs 38236.0045 (port), volume 169497.2899 vs 169499.4301 (1.26e-5 relative). The C++ face 7 value does not move with accuracy (B-rep area 38236.6109 at 1e-6, 38236.6055 at 1e-10).
- **Independent check:** a fine inscribed mesh of the port's B-rep (solidcore-mesh, relative chord 4e-6, 2e-6 and 1e-6, Richardson-extrapolated) gives 3158.6966 for faces 2, 3 and 5, against the integrals 3158.6911-3158.6959, which supports the siblings' value; the mesher did not mesh faces 4, 6 and 7 at that run (since fixed in solidcore-mesh), so face 7 itself is judged by symmetry and the trims, not by the mesh.
- **Cause (input to the integral):** the file carries no UV trim for most edge uses; both sides make them when the face is measured (gdb on the C++ child: no trim is made or changed during `ReadFromFile`; every `CreateUVTrimCurve` call comes from `ComputePreciseProperties`). The trim boundary face 7 is integrated over differs on one curve, curve 2: edge 30's, from (0.37738, 0), on the surface's v-seam, to (0.41329, 0.125) in UV. The port drops a degree-3 trim with 46 control points; C++ makes a degree-3 curve with 4 control points (one cubic span) over the same interval [0.49999995785315, 0.74999998663581] with the same first and last control points. This is a Rust/C++ divergence inside the ported on-demand trim creation (`CreateUVTrimCurve` / `DropCurve` / `sm_FixUVTrimCurves`); the diverging step is not yet pinned. Every other curve of face 7 and every curve of face 6 (a sibling, the control) match exactly (largest control point difference 0). The face integrated over C++'s single span gains 0.60 of area and 2.14 of volume term and stands apart from its siblings; over the port's 46-point drop it matches them. The port runs with the default `Kernel` (upstream's `sm_FixUVTrimCurves` path, `UpstreamFixes::uv_trim_corner_end_points_only` off).
