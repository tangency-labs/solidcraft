#### WR-780. `SmOffsetSurface::SwapUV` keeps the periodic flags and singular sides of the unswapped directions
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Surfaces (offset surfaces)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetSurface.cpp:1762`, `SmOffsetSurface::SwapUV`. It swaps `m_pExtendedSurface` and `m_pSurface` and negates `m_dOffsetDistance`, and leaves `m_bIsPeriodicOnU`, `m_bIsPeriodicOnV` and `m_lSingularities` as they were. `sm_ExtendSurface` (the same file) wraps a parameter by those flags and clamps it to the singular sides.
- **What happens:** after a swap, the flags describe the old directions. The extension then wraps the direction that is not periodic and extends the one that is, or clamps the wrong sides. An offset of a surface of revolution, swapped so that its angle is `v`, evaluated past the angle domain, is extended tangentially in the closed direction instead of wrapped.
- **Trigger:** goldens `surface.offset_surface` gen0354. An offset (d = 0.7557) of a surface of revolution about z, whose generator starts on the axis; the script swaps the offset's UV, then evaluates at (1.0, 4.5695), past the angle domain [0, 1]. Before the swap the state record shows periodic u = 1, v = 0 (the angle is u). After it, still u = 1, v = 0, with the angle now v. On the Rust port's main the point was 43.3 off the exact offset surface; with WR-725 retired it was on the surface but at the seam, 4.27 from the periodic image.
- **How found:** checking the rows the `closed_smooth_pole_tangent_limit` retirement moved (tangency-labs/solidcraft#14246; swarm probes 14246-pole-x1b and -probe2). Filed as tangency-labs/solidcraft#15559.
- **Confirmed:** read in the pinned source (`SwapUV` touches neither field) and measured in the Rust port, which kept upstream's behaviour: the state records of gen0354 before and after its swap.
- **Rust port:** `OffsetSurface::swap_uv` (`surface/offset_surface.rs`) swaps `is_periodic_on_u`/`v` and maps the singular sides (`SS_UMIN` with `SS_VMIN`, `SS_UMAX` with `SS_VMAX`, other bits kept) after the base surface's swap succeeds. `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_15559_offset_swap_uv_periodic_wrap.rs` checks an offset surface of revolution, swapped, past its angle domain against the closed form (the generator's parallel at the offset distance, turned to the rational circle's angle of the wrapped parameter).
