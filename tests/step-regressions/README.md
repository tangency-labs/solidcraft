# STEP regressions

STEP files that once broke an import, kept so the import stays fixed. Each is written by SolidCraft from
public commands, or entity by entity from formulas; none comes from another program.

| File | Made by | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rod_doubled_edge.step` | SolidCraft main (`edf57cec`), the commands of #4598: a Ø40 × 60 rod, a Ø8 cross bore cut 15 deep, the rod moved 100 mm, exported | The bore's rim exported as a B-spline of 1 399 control points running back and forth across the bore: before #4598 a moved edge's tolerance grew with the move. The triangulator's assertion lost the body on import (#4031, #4598). |
| `tiny_rounds_coarse_tolerance.step` | Written entity by entity from formulas | Three faces of radius 0.05 mm (a pin's quarter round, a corner ball, a quarter torus round a rim) in a file of 0.1 mm uncertainty, as an exporter writing a coarse tolerance gives them. A collapse test that took a surface thinner than the tolerance for a pole refused all three, and an enclosure lost 887 faces of its connectors. |
| `cylinder_spline_rims.step` | Written entity by entity from formulas | A cylinder of radius 10 and height 5 whose rims are closed rational cubic B-splines of two pieces, each an exact half circle, and no seam edge. A piece reaching half a turn about the axis was taken to reach every angle, so the wall had no angle left for its seam and was left out of the part. |
| `cylinder_rim_off_its_wall.step` | Written entity by entity from formulas | A cylinder of radius 10 and height 5 whose upper rim is a circle of radius 10.0002, 0.2 µm outside the wall, in a file stating 0.01 µm, the rims' vertices half a turn apart and no seam edge. The seam added from one rim to the other ends 0.2 µm off the cylinder; held to the file's uncertainty it was refused, and the wall was left out of the part. |
| `thread_crest_band.step` | Written entity by entity from formulas | One cylinder face of radius 3 from a circle at z = 3 down to a helical groove a quarter turn wide that winds two turns up from z = 0, its helices cubic B-splines (a thread's crest as a cap screw's major-diameter cylinder is written). The face's loop round the groove reaches every angle more than once, so no angle was clear for the seam joining it to the circle, and the face was left out (#16930). |
| `block_edge_off_its_face.step` | Written entity by entity from formulas (`lifted_bezier_block(2e-3)` in `crates/kernel/src/step_in/tests.rs`, which checks the file is its output) | A 20 × 30 × 10 mm block whose 12 edges are straight cubic Béziers, one of them split 1 µm from its start, with that edge's curve lifted 2 µm off the bottom face. The reader gives the two edges on it the gap as their tolerance; SMLib takes the part, but its check refuses those tolerances. The import said nothing, and every boolean on the part was refused later (#17689). |
| `torus_patch_and_rest.step` | SolidCraft 0.30.1, `solid.torus` (R 12, r 2), exported | The torus as two faces on their own TOROIDAL_SURFACEs sharing one loop: a patch a third of the way round and half way round the tube, and the rest of the torus round it, whose loop runs clockwise. The rest was read as the patch turned over: the solid came in closed with no volume (#17930). |
| `torus_patch_and_rest_revolved.step` | SolidCraft 0.30.1, `torus_patch_and_rest.step` inserted and exported again | The same torus with both faces on SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTIONs of a 9-point rational circle. The rest face got no pcurves ("flat arrays") and the torus came in open (#17929). |
| `hole_round_revolved.step` | SolidCraft 0.30.1, a 30 × 30 × 10 box less a Ø14 cylinder, its top hole edge rounded at 1 mm, exported, inserted and exported again | The round on a SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION of a rational circle. Its face got no pcurves and the block came in open (#17929). |
| `net_steps_back_within_tolerance.step` | Written entity by entity from formulas (a tetrahedron) | A tetrahedron A(0,0,0) B(10,0,0) C(0,10,0) D(0,0,10), uncertainty 1e-3 mm, whose face A B D is a flat spline of degree 1 by 3 whose two control columns each step back 0.5 µm along their side (as an exporter's refinement leaves them). SMLib's check failed it on "Bad NonMonotonic CPts" and refused every operation on it. |
| `swept_tube_last_wall.step` | SolidCraft 0.46.2, the commands of #21379 (a circle of radius 5 swept along a 38-point 3D spline), exported; cut down to the last wall, its end cap and a plane cap on its other rim | The wall is a rational spline closed round u whose seam edge is its first control column. Projecting the seam's middle put it 5.5e-8 short of the knots' end, the wall was started anew there, its rims crossed the new start, and the seam completion left the wall out: the tube came in open, with no mass (#21379). |
| `rod_cross_hole_rim_off_curve_start.step` | Written entity by entity from formulas | A rod of radius 15 from z = −50 to 50 with a through hole of radius 10 along x, its rims closed C1 cubic B-splines through the exact intersection. The exit rim is one closed edge whose vertex is half a turn round while its curve starts 1 rad round. It came in as one closed body of 875.18 mm³ for 61818.82, the only sign a "needs repair" warning (#20551). |
| `bent_strip_micrometre_cuts.step` | SolidCraft main (`c45c50e2c`), the commands of #22579: a strip bent round radii 0.6 and 0.7 mm, cut above and below its bend's axis by two slanted cuts, exported | The cuts' sides cross 7e-5 mm inside the strip, leaving edges 6.955e-5 mm long, and the file states the strip's largest tolerance, 9.17e-5 mm. | `rounded_disc_cut_vertices_off.step` | Written entity by entity from formulas | A disc of radius 10 and height 3, its top edge rounded at 3, cut off at x = 8.5; the cut meets the round in a closed oval (a C1 cubic B-spline through the exact intersection) whose edge has its two vertices 1e-7 mm off it. It came in as one closed body of 1164.29 mm³ for 1722.08, the only sign a "needs repair" warning (#20551). |
